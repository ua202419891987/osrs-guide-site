1. The Biggest Change: Sailing Skill（最大变化：航海技能）

If you haven't played since early 2025 or earlier, the single biggest change waiting for you is Sailing（航海）—— Old School RuneScape's 24th skill（第 24 项技能）, officially released on November 19, 2025（2025 年 11 月 19 日正式发布）after three years of community-driven design.

Sailing is a Utility skill（实用技能）that gives you command of your own ship. You can travel across the seas of Gielinor, discover new islands, fight sea monsters, and access exclusive resources. It weaves into almost every other skill in the game.

🎯 What You Need to Know as a Returning Player（作为回归玩家你需要知道的）

Question（问题）Answer（答案）
How do I start?（如何开始？）Complete The Pandemonium（潘德蒙尼姆）quest at Port Sarim docks. No requirements needed — starts you off with a Raft and unlocks Sailing.
What are the 4 training methods?（4 种训练方式？）Port Tasks (steady XP)（港口任务，稳定经验）, Shipwreck Salvaging (AFK at level 15)（沉船打捞，15 级挂机）, Sea Charting (exploration)（海图绘制，探索）, Barracuda Trials (fastest XP at level 30)（剑鱼试炼，30 级最快经验）.
What resources does it unlock?（解锁哪些资源？）New trees (Jatoba at 40 WC, Ironwood at 80, Rosewood at 92)（新树木：40 级伐木 Jatoba、80 级 Ironwood、92 级 Rosewood）, new ores (Lead at 25 Mining, Rubium at 48, Nickel at 74)（新矿石：25 级采矿 Lead、48 级 Rubium、74 级 Nickel）, new fish (Marlin heals 24 HP)（新鱼：枪鱼回复 24 点生命）.
New Slayer monsters?（新屠戮怪物？）Gryphons (51 Slayer)（狮鹫，51 屠戮）, Aquanites (78)（水精灵，78）, Frost Dragons (87)（霜龙，87）—— plus Shellbane Gryphon boss.
Is Sailing required?（航海是必须的吗？）It's optional but highly recommended. Unlocks transportation, equipment (Belle's Folly, Aquanite Hopper, new blowpipes), and hybrid training.
⛵ Pro Tip: Your First Sailing Steps（专业技巧：你的第一步航海）

Don't delay starting Sailing.（别拖延开始航海。）Even if you only do the Pandemonium quest for now (takes ~20 minutes), you'll unlock level 6 and the Raft. From there, Port Tasks at Port Sarim give steady XP while you re-learn the game. The passive wind mechanic gives 150-300 bonus XP every 60 seconds — click the wind icon when it appears.

🛠️ June 2026 Sailing Updates（2026 年 6 月航海更新）

In the June 17, 2026（2026 年 6 月 17 日）game update, Jagex made several quality-of-life changes:

  • Bounty Task rewards（赏金任务奖励）now scale based on your contribution to kills
  • Cannon convenience（火炮便利性）—— picking up loot no longer interrupts cannon firing（拾取战利品不再打断火炮射击）
  • Item value adjustments（物品价值调整）—— Dragon Keel Parts, Cannon Barrel, and other Sailing items had their GE values updated for Death's Coffer calculations（龙龙骨部件、火炮枪管等航海物品的大交易所价值已更新，用于死亡钱箱计算）

2. Leagues VI: Demonic Pacts（联盟 VI：恶魔契约）

Leagues VI: Demonic Pacts（联盟 VI：恶魔契约）launched in April 2026（2026 年 4 月）and is the most recent League season. If you missed earlier Leagues (Twisted, Trailblazer, Reloaded, Raging Echoes), this one introduced a unique Demonic Pacts mechanic — special buffs and challenges tied to demonic-themed content.

🎮 Key Features（主要特性）

  • Demonic Pacts（恶魔契约）—— temporary power boosts with trade-offs（带权衡的临时增益）
  • New relic system（新遗物系统）—— choose your path through demonic-themed upgrades（在恶魔主题强化中选择你的路线）
  • Exclusive rewards（专属奖励）—— Soulreaper Axe (o) cosmetic, Demonic Pacts Throne Scroll, and ornament kits for Trident of the Swamp, Trident of the Seas, and Iban Staff（灵魂收割斧（o）外观、恶魔契约王座卷轴，以及沼泽三叉戟、海洋三叉戟、Iban 法杖的装饰套件）
  • Slayer Helmet cosmetic（屠戮头盔外观）—— unlockable through league tasks (1,000 Slayer points)（通过联盟任务解锁，需 1000 屠戮点）

📋 Rewards Adjusted in June 2026 Update（2026 年 6 月更新中调整的奖励）

The June 17 patch made several visual and functional tweaks to League rewards:

  • Soulreaper Axe (o)（灵魂收割斧）—— axe head made larger to match its powerful design（斧头放大以匹配强力设计）
  • Demonic Pacts Throne Scroll（恶魔契约王座卷轴）—— now requires 0 Construction（现需 0 级建造，原为 99）to apply
  • Toxic Trident (e) (o)（剧毒三叉戟）—— color darkened for better differentiation（颜色加深以便区分）
  • Trident of the Seas (o)（海洋三叉戟）—— icon rotated to match base version（图标旋转以匹配基础版）
  • Dual-wield league trophies（双持联盟奖杯）and Storm cruiser's coat —— no longer create "tail" effect behind character（不再在角色身后产生"尾迹"特效）
  • Trident decoration（三叉戟装饰）—— now works both ways (use staff on pack OR pack on staff)（现在双向可用）
💡 League Still Active?（联盟还在进行吗？）

Leagues VI: Demonic Pacts was still running as of June 2026. If you haven't claimed your rewards yet, check the League interface — rewards earned during the league are permanent and usable on your main account even after the league ends.

3. Bank Tags & Inventory Management（银行标签与背包管理）

Released on June 17, 2026（2026 年 6 月 17 日）, Bank Tags are now available on mobile and the Official Client（手机与官方客户端）. This feature lets you organize your bank into up to 20 custom categories（最多 20 个自定义分类）—— no more scrolling through 800 items to find your gear setup.

🏷️ How Bank Tags Work（银行标签如何运作）

Creating a Tag（创建标签）

Click the + button（加号按钮）in the top-left of your bank interface. Type a name (up to 25 alphanumeric characters). Choose an item as your tag icon via the search box.

Adding Items（添加物品）

Drag items from your main bank onto the tag icon on the left sidebar. They remain in your main bank — the tag is just a filtered view.

Organizing（整理）

Drag items freely within a tag to reorder. You can even leave gaps or blank rows for visual separation. Use Shift + drag（Shift + 拖拽）to quickly duplicate a tag.

Feature（功能）Details（详情）
Max tags（最大标签数）20 custom categories（20 个自定义分类）
Tag name limit（标签名上限）25 alphanumeric characters（25 个字母数字字符）
Compatibility（兼容性）Mobile, Official Client, and existing third-party client tags unaffected（手机、官方客户端，现有第三方客户端标签不受影响）
Potion Storage（药水存储）Separate from tag system — potions need to be in main bank to appear in tags（独立于标签系统，药水需在主银行才会显示在标签中）
Main bank（主银行）Completely unaffected — tags are just an alternative view（完全不受影响，标签只是替代视图）
💡 Returning Player Bank Tip（回归玩家银行技巧）

Create your first 3 tags immediately: "Melee Gear"（近战装备）, "Ranged Gear"（远程装备）, and "Mage Gear"（魔法装备）. Add your equipment, ammo, and potions to each. This alone will save you hours of bank sorting every week.

4. Trouver System Rework (Wilderness Death Changes)（Trouver 系统重做（荒野死亡改动））

One of the most impactful changes for anyone who ventures into the Wilderness. The Trouver Parchment system was completely overhauled on June 17, 2026（2026 年 6 月 17 日）. The goal was simple: reduce the risk of permanently losing key untradeable items（降低永久丢失关键不可交易物品的风险）.

📋 Three Categories of Untradeables（不可交易物品的三类）

Category（类别）Examples（示例）Death Below 20 Wildy（20 级荒野以下死亡）Death Above 20 Wildy（20 级荒野以上死亡）
No combat bonus（无战斗加成）Graceful outfit（优雅套装）Never lost（永不丢失）Never lost（永不丢失）
Combat bonus (mid-tier)（战斗加成·中阶）Fire cape（火之法袍）, Ava's assembler（Ava 装配器）, Defenders（防御者）, Void Knight gear（虚空骑士装备）, Fighter hat（战士帽）, Halos（光环）, Penance gear（忏悔装备）Damaged — repair at Perdu（损坏——在 Perdu 处修复）Damaged — repair at Perdu（损坏——在 Perdu 处修复）
High-tier (Trouver required)（高阶·需 Trouver）Infernal cape（地狱法袍）, Imbued heart（附魔之心）, Tarn's locket（Tarn 吊坠）Damaged — repair normally（损坏——正常修复）Mangled — costs 500K GP to repair（严重损坏——修复需 500K GP）

🛡️ Key Changes from Old System（相比旧系统的关键改动）

  • Graceful never lost anymore（优雅套装不再丢失）—— even if you die at 50+ Wilderness（即便在 50+ 荒野死亡）
  • Fire capes, defenders, void（火之法袍、防御者、虚空）—— now only get damaged, never lost（现在仅会损坏，永不丢失）
  • Trouver Parchment is now permanent（Trouver 羊皮纸现在永久）—— apply once and it stays forever（使用一次即永久生效）
  • Ornament kits（装饰套件）—— PKers can still take tradeable ornament kits, you keep the damaged base item（PKer 仍可夺取可交易装饰套件，你保留损坏的基础物品）
  • Warning system coming（警告系统即将推出）—— Jagex is developing a warning when you enter the Wildy with high-risk untradeables（Jagex 正在开发：携带高风险不可交易物品进入荒野时发出警告）
⚠️ What This Means for You（这对你意味着什么）

If you were afraid of taking good gear into the Wilderness before, this update changes everything. Your Fire cape, Ava's assembler, and Defenders are safe from permanent loss（可免遭永久丢失）. Equip them with confidence — the worst that happens is a repair bill. For Infernal and other high-tier items, use a Trouver Parchment once and it's permanent protection.

5. New Slayer Content: Frost Dragons（新屠戮内容：霜龙）

Frost Dragons were added as a new Slayer monster accessible at level 87 Sailing（需 87 级航海）. In the June 17, 2026 update, two new Slayer unlocks（屠戮解锁）were added (100 Slayer Points each):

Unlock（解锁）Effect（效果）Cost（花费）
Chance of Heavy Frost（重霜几率）Increases Frost Dragon task weight from 5 to 8 (for Nieve and Duradel)（将霜龙任务权重从 5 提升至 8，适用 Nieve 与 Duradel）100 Slayer Points（100 屠戮点）
I See Dragon（我见龙）Increases task length to 180-240 Frost Dragons（将任务长度提升至 180–240 只霜龙）100 Slayer Points（100 屠戮点）

Both unlocks can be toggled off at any time (no refund). The Frost Dragon task now offers a viable alternative to other high-level Slayer tasks, especially valuable for Ironmen collecting Frost Dragon bones and Dragon Sheets.

PvP has seen several updates since early 2026:

⚔️ PvP Quality Improvements (Starting March 2026)（PvP 质量改进（自 2026 年 3 月起））

Ongoing balance changes and quality-of-life improvements for PvP content across the game, including:

  • Vesta's Spear (bh)（维斯塔之矛）—— fixed a bug where switching weapons after the special attack didn't block ranged damage（修复特殊攻击后切换武器不阻挡远程伤害的 bug）
  • Emir's Arena（埃米尔竞技场）—— now allows Ancient Warriors' weapons（现允许远古战士武器）
  • Last Man Standing（最后幸存者）—— major equipment overhaul (Dragon Crossbow, Dragon Claws, Berserker ring (i) added to Max/Med loadouts)（大型装备改版：巨龙十字弩、龙爪、狂暴者戒指（i）加入 Max/Med 配置）
  • LMS defaults（LMS 默认）—— new player presets now include 3 Saradomin brews, 2 Sanfew serums, 10 sharks; default spellbook set to Ancient Magicks（新玩家预设现含 3 瓶萨拉多明酿造、2 瓶 Sanfew 血清、10 条鲨鱼；默认法书设为远古魔法）

🏆 Deadman Events（Deadman 活动）

  • Deadman: Annihilation（Deadman：湮灭）(January 30 - February 20, 2026) —— Season 2 of the revived Deadman Mode（复兴 Deadman 模式第 2 季）
  • Deadman All Stars Season 3（Deadman 全明星第 3 季）(June 2026) —— Top players compete in the seasonal finale（顶尖玩家在季终赛中角逐）
💡 PvP Tip for Returning Players（回归玩家的 PvP 技巧）

If you haven't tried LMS (Last Man Standing) before, now is a great time. The June 17 update added Dragon Claws and Dragon Crossbow to the loot pool, and new players get improved default presets. It's a zero-risk way to learn PvP mechanics.

7. Grand Exchange Improvements（大交易所改进）

On June 15, 2026（2026 年 6 月 15 日）, Jagex published a developer blog outlining planned improvements to the Grand Exchange. The key proposal being discussed is transactions beyond max cash stack（超过最大现金堆的交易）—— currently capped at 2,147,483,647 GP (Integer MAX_VALUE). This would allow trading high-value items like Twisted Bow (now ~1.55B GP) and 3rd Age items more efficiently.

💡 What This Means（这意味着什么）

Currently, items worth over max cash must be traded manually (player-to-player with trust involved). If the GE cap is raised, it would make trading mega-rare items safer and more liquid. As of June 2026, this is still being developed — no release date confirmed.

8. Jagex Accounts & Security Changes（Jagex 账号与安全改动）

On June 12, 2026（2026 年 6 月 12 日）, Jagex announced the full transition to Jagex Accounts（全面过渡至 Jagex 账号）. This is a mandatory migration that improves account security with:

  • Two-factor authentication (2FA) built in（内置两步验证（2FA））
  • Single login for all your OSRS and RS3 characters（OSRS 与 RS3 角色统一登录）
  • Improved password recovery system（改进的密码找回系统）
  • Linked email verification（关联邮箱验证）

If you haven't migrated yet, you'll be prompted to do so on your next login. The process takes about 5 minutes — have your original account email ready.

9. Quick Catch-Up Roadmap (1 Week Plan)（快速追赶路线图（1 周计划））

Follow this plan to get up to speed fast（按此计划快速赶上进度）:

📅 Day 1 — Account & Security Check（第 1 天 — 账号与安全核查）
Migrate to Jagex Account (5 min). Check your bank — what items do you still have? What might have changed value? Check the GE for your old gear.

📅 Day 3 — Organize Your Bank（第 3 天 — 整理银行）
Use the new Bank Tags（银行标签）to set up 3-5 tags for your gear setups. Set up your new tag system — it'll save you hours of frustration.
📅 Day 4 — Wilderness & Trouver（第 4 天 — 荒野与 Trouver）
Review the new Trouver system. If you have an Infernal cape or other high-tier untradeables, apply a Trouver Parchment for permanent protection. Try the new Wilderness content with confidence.
📅 Day 5 — Slayer & Combat（第 5 天 — 屠戮与战斗）
Check your Slayer master. If you have 87 Sailing, try Frost Dragons. Review your gear against current prices (see our Gear Inflation Guide for what's trending).
📅 Day 6-7 — Explore & Settle In（第 6–7 天 — 探索并安顿）
Try the things you missed most. Check out the Sailing training methods. Browse the GE for new items. Join a clan. The game may have changed, but the core OSRS experience is as good as ever.

10. Returning Player FAQs（回归玩家常见问题）

I quit before Sailing was announced. What did I miss?（我在航海公布前就退坑了。我错过了什么？）

Everything. Sailing is OSRS's 24th skill, released November 19, 2025. It's a utility skill that adds ship travel, new islands, 4 training methods, new resources (Jatoba logs, Lead ore, Marlin fish), 4 new Slayer monsters, and multiple new equipment pieces (Belle's Folly, Aquanite Hopper, Ironwood/Rosewood blowpipes). Start at Port Sarim with the Pandemonium quest.

Is my old gear still good?（我的旧装备还能用吗？）

Mostly yes, but check current prices. The OSRS economy has seen inflation — Twisted Bow is over 1.5B GP, Scythe of Vitur and Tumeken's Shadow are in the hundreds of millions. If you own any mega-rares, they've likely appreciated. Check our Gear Inflation Guide for detailed price trends.

What's the deal with Bank Tags?（银行标签是怎么回事？）

Bank Tags are a new organization feature released June 17, 2026 for mobile and Official Client. You can create up to 20 custom tags to filter your bank items. Drag items onto a tag icon to create a filtered view. Your main bank is unaffected — tags are just alternative views.

Can I still lose my Fire Cape in the Wilderness?（我在荒野还会丢失火之法袍吗？）

No. The Trouver System Rework (June 17, 2026) changed this. Fire capes, Ava's assemblers, Defenders, Void Knight gear, and Halos now only get damaged on death — they are never permanently lost. You pay Perdu a small fee to repair them.

What happened to Leagues VI: Demonic Pacts?（联盟 VI：恶魔契约怎么了？）

It launched in April 2026 and was still active as of June 2026. The June 17 update adjusted several cosmetic rewards (Soulreaper Axe visual, Trident colors, Throne Scroll requirements). If you participated, check the reward interface for your unlocks.

Is there a new Slayer monster I should know about?（有我该了解的新屠戮怪物吗？）

Frost Dragons (87 Sailing) are the newest addition. Two new Slayer unlocks were added in June 2026: "Chance of Heavy Frost" (increases task weight) and "I See Dragon" (increases task length), each costing 100 Slayer points.

Do I need a Jagex Account now?（现在我需要 Jagex 账号吗？）

Yes. Jagex announced full transition to Jagex Accounts on June 12, 2026. It's mandatory for continued access. The migration takes ~5 minutes and adds 2FA, linked email verification, and unified login for all your characters.

What's the best way to make money as a returning player?（作为回归玩家，最佳赚钱方式是什么？）

Your best money maker depends on your stats. If you have access to bossing, Vorkath (~3-4M GP/h) and Zulrah (~2-3.5M GP/h) remain strong. For no-requirement methods, check our Money Making Zero Requirements guide. The GE Improvements blog also hints at future trading enhancements for high-value items.
