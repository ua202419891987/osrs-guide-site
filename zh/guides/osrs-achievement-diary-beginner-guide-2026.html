🏆 OSRS Achievement Diary Guide 2026

Achievement Diaries (also called "Diaries") are sets of tasks in each region of Gielinor. Completing them gives permanent rewards: unlock teleports, extra GP from NPCs, useful items, and even stat boosts. This guide covers Easy and Medium diaries — the ones you should complete first for maximum benefit.

🏆 1. What Are Achievement Diaries? (Why Do Them)（成就日记是什么？为何要做）

Each region in OSRS has an Achievement Diary（成就日记）— a list of 10–20 tasks（10–20 个任务） you can complete to earn a reward（奖励）. Tasks are simple things like:

  • "Kill 5 cows in Lumbridge"（在卢姆布里奇杀 5 头牛）
  • "Travel from Varrock to Falador via the shuttle"（经班车从瓦洛克前往法拉多）
  • "Pickpocket a H.A.M. member"（偷窃 H.A.M. 成员）
  • "Complete a lap of the Barbarian Agility Course"（完成一圈野蛮人敏捷课程）

Once you complete all tasks in a diary, talk to the Diary NPC（日记 NPC，通常位于中心位置） to claim your reward. Rewards include:

🎁 Why Achievement Diaries Are Worth Doing（为何值得做成就日记）

  • Teleport unlocks:（传送解锁）Some diaries unlock useful teleports (e.g., Ardougne Diary → Kandarin head teleport / 坎达林头像传送)
  • GP bonuses:（GP 加成）NPCs give you extra GP when you show them your completed diary (e.g., 20% more GP from H.A.M. loot / H.A.M. 战利品多 20% GP)
  • Item unlocks:（道具解锁）Free useful items (explorer ring / 探险者戒指, lumbridge ring / 卢姆布里奇戒指, etc.)
  • Stat boosts:（属性加成）Some rewards give temporary stat boosts (e.g., Explorer ring → 27 Super energy doses per day / 每天 27 份超级能量)
  • Access unlocks:（权限解锁）Some areas/features require a completed diary (e.g., Fremennik Diary → access to some areas)

The best part: Most diary tasks are things you'll do anyway（多数日记任务你本来就会做）(killing cows, traveling, etc.). You can complete many diaries "passively" while playing normally.

💡 Achievement Diaries = Free Permanent Upgrades（成就日记 = 免费永久强化）

Unlike quest rewards (one-time XP), diary rewards are permanent buffs（永久增益） that help you every day. An extra 20% GP from all sources adds up to hundreds of thousands of GP per week（每周数十万 GP）. That's why diaries are a top priority for efficient players.

📊 2. Achievement Diary Difficulty Tiers（成就日记难度分级）

There are 4 difficulty tiers（4 个难度层级） per region:

Tier（等级）Task Count（任务数量）Requirements（要求）Time to Complete（完成时间）Reward Value（奖励价值）
Easy（简单）10–15None–40 stats~1 hour（约 1 小时）Low (but useful)（低，但实用）
Medium（中级）15–2040–65 stats~3 hours（约 3 小时）Medium (very useful)（中，非常实用）
Hard（困难）15–2065–90 stats~8 hours（约 8 小时）High (game-changing)（高，改变玩法）
Elite（精英）10–1590+ stats~15 hours（约 15 小时）Very High (end-game)（极高，末期）

For beginners, focus on Easy and Medium diaries only.（新手只需专注简单与中级日记。）Hard and Elite require high stats and expensive gear. Easy + Medium diaries give 80% of the useful rewards with 20% of the effort.

🎯 3. Which Diaries to Complete First (Priority Order)（优先完成哪些日记：优先级顺序）

Not all diaries are equal. Here's the priority order for beginners（并非所有日记都等价，以下是新手的优先级顺序）:

🏅 Priority 1: Lumbridge & Draynor (Easy + Medium)（优先级 1：卢姆布里奇与德雷诺尔）

Why first:（为何第一）Gives the Explorer's Ring（探险者戒指，用于跑步能量 + 炼金） and Lumbridge Ring（卢姆布里奇戒指，传送至卢姆布里奇）. Tasks are very easy (all in starting area). Complete this in your first week of playing.（建议游戏第一周完成。）

🥈 Priority 2: Varrock (Easy + Medium)（优先级 2：瓦洛克）

Why second:（为何第二）Gives Varrock Armor（瓦洛克盔甲，双倍矿石锻造） and Varrock teleport（瓦洛克传送，非常实用）. Tasks are in Varrock (easy to reach). Complete after 30 Combat.（战斗等级 30 后完成。）

🥉 Priority 3: Falador (Easy + Medium)（优先级 3：法拉多）

Why third:（为何第三）Gives Falador Shield（法拉多盾牌，传送至法拉多 + 祈祷恢复） and Falador teleport（法拉多传送）. Tasks are in Falador/Draynor area. Complete after 40 Combat.（战斗等级 40 后完成。）

Priority 4: Ardougne/Kandarin (Easy + Medium)（优先级 4：阿杜格恩/坎达林）

Why fourth:（为何第四）Gives Kandarin Head（坎达林头像，传送至阿杜格恩） and Ardougne cloak（阿杜格恩斗篷，补丁双倍草药）. Very useful for mid-game. Complete after 60 Combat.（战斗等级 60 后完成。）

💡 Do Diaries WHILE Leveling Stats（练级时顺手做日记）

Don't specifically "grind" diaries. Instead, look at the diary tasks for your current region（查看当前区域的日记任务）, and try to complete them while you're already there. Example: If you're training Combat in Edgeville Dungeon (near Varrock), check the Varrock Diary tasks and see if you can complete any.

📋 6. How to Track Your Diary Progress（如何追踪日记进度）

You can track diary progress in two ways（你可以用两种方式追踪日记进度）:

① In-Game Quest List (Official)（游戏内任务列表（官方））

Press Alt + Q（或点击游戏界面中的任务列表图标）. Click the "Diaries"（日记）tab. Select a region (e.g., "Lumbridge & Draynor") to see all tasks. Completed tasks show a green checkmark.（已完成显示绿色对勾。）In-progress tasks show yellow.（进行中显示黄色。）Locked tasks show red.（未解锁显示红色。）

② RuneLite Diary Plugin (Recommended)（RuneLite 日记插件（推荐））

If you use RuneLite, the Achievement Diary plugin（成就日记插件） is much better than the in-game tracker. It shows:

  • All tasks for all diaries in a clean list（所有日记的全部任务，列表清晰）
  • Exactly where to go (map markers)（确切位置，地图标记）
  • One-click task filtering (show only incomplete tasks)（一键筛选，仅显示未完成任务）
  • Task requirements (what stats/quests you need)（任务要求：所需属性/任务）

How to Enable the Diary Plugin (RuneLite)（如何启用日记插件（RuneLite））

Step 1:（步骤 1）Open RuneLite settings (wrench icon).（打开 RuneLite 设置（扳手图标）。）

Step 2:（步骤 2）Click "Plugins"（插件）on the left sidebar.

Step 3:（步骤 3）Search for "Achievement Diary"（成就日记）in the plugin list.

Step 4:（步骤 4）Click the toggle switch（开关）to enable it (turns green).

Step 5:（步骤 5）The diary overlay appears on your game screen. Click any task to see details.

❓ 8. FAQ（常见问题）

Can F2P players complete Achievement Diaries?（F2P 玩家能完成成就日记吗？）

Yes, but only partially.（可以，但只能部分完成。）F2P players can complete some Easy diary tasks (the ones in F2P areas), but many tasks require membership. The diary reward is also members-only. Get membership to get full value from diaries.（购买会员以充分发挥日记价值。）

Do I need to complete diaries in order (Easy → Medium → Hard → Elite)?（需要按顺序完成日记吗（简单 → 中级 → 困难 → 精英）？）

No.（不需要。）You can complete them in any order. However, Higher tiers have higher stat requirements, so it's natural to do Easy first, then Medium, etc. You can work on multiple diaries simultaneously（你可以同时进行多个日记）(e.g., do Lumbridge Easy tasks while training in that area, then switch to Varrock Easy tasks when you're in Varrock).

What happens if I lose my diary reward item?（如果弄丢日记奖励道具会怎样？）

If you lose a diary reward item (e.g., Explorer's Ring), you can get it back for free（免费找回）by talking to the Diary NPC（日记 NPC）again. No need to re-complete the diary. This is very useful — you can safely drop/lose the item and get a free replacement.（非常实用——可放心丢弃/丢失道具并获得免费替换。）

How long does it take to complete all Easy + Medium diaries?（完成所有简单 + 中级日记要多久？）

About 20–30 hours（约 20–30 小时）total (spread across many play sessions). But you don't need to "grind" them — complete tasks passively while playing normally. After 2–3 weeks of playing, you'll have most Easy diaries done without specifically trying.（游玩 2–3 周后，多数简单日记会在不经意间完成。）

Are Achievement Diary rewards worth the effort?（成就日记奖励值得投入吗？）

100% yes.（绝对值得。）Diary rewards are permanent passive bonuses（永久被动加成）that help you every single day. The Explorer's Ring alone saves you hundreds of thousands of GP on run energy and alchs（仅在跑步能量与炼金上就省下数十万 GP）. Completing diaries is one of the highest-ROI activities in OSRS.
