💡 The Bottom Line（核心结论）

You CAN reach high combat levels as F2P（你作为免费玩家也能练到高战斗等级）. Many players have maxed combat stats without ever paying. However, membership gives you 10x more content variety (10 倍的内容多样性), faster XP rates across most skills, access to money-making methods that are literally impossible in F2P（免费版根本做不到的赚钱法）, and the ability to progress beyond "grinding the same 5 monsters forever." Think of F2P as a demo version that lets you learn the basics; membership is the full game.

2. When Should You Get Membership?（什么时候该开会员？）

This is the #1 question every F2P player asks（这是每个免费玩家问得最多的问题）. Here's the honest answer based on thousands of accounts:

① The Three Sweet Spots（三个最佳时机）

② Sweet Spot A: After Completing All F2P Quests (~Level 35–40 Combat)（做完所有免费任务后）

If you've completed Cook's Assistant, Sheep Shearer, Imp Catcher, Rune Mysteries, Restless Ghost, Witch's Potion, Romeo Juliet, Vampire Slayer, Goblin Diplomacy, Pirate's Treasure, Prince Ali Rescue, Ernest the Chicken, Black Knight's Fortress, Dragon Slayer I, and Shield of Arrav... you've exhausted most of what F2P offers（你已经把免费版能玩的都玩完了）. Now is the perfect time to get membership.

Estimated time（预计耗时）: 30–50 hours of play
Combat level at this point（此时战斗等级）: ~40–55
Your bank value（银行资产）: ~500K–2M GP from quest rewards and basic money making

③ Sweet Spot B: When You Hit Level 60+ Combat (Most Popular)（战斗到 60+ 级，最常见）

By level 60+ combat, you'll have trained enough in F2P to understand game mechanics well（你已经足够理解游戏机制）. You've probably made some GP, know how the Grand Exchange works, and have experienced the frustration of being locked out of 85% of the game world. This is when most players subscribe（大多数玩家此时订阅）.

Why this timing works（为什么这个时机合适）: You have enough game knowledge to actually benefit from member content. Getting membership at level 10 combat means you won't be able to use most of it anyway.

④ Sweet Spot C: Pay for 1 Month Only, Do Key Quests, Then Go Back F2P（只买一个月，做关键任务后再回免费）

A popular strategy among budget-conscious players（精打细算玩家的常用策略）: buy just ONE month of membership, rush through the highest-value member-only quests and unlocks, then let membership expire. The benefits (quest rewards, unlocked areas) stay with your account permanently even after going back to F2P.

What to accomplish in 1 month（一个月里要做的事）: Complete Tree Gnome Village, Horror from the Deep, The Grand Tree, Desert Treasure I (for Ancient Magicks), Monkey Madness I (for Dragon Scimitar), Recipe for Disaster (for Barrows Gloves), Fairy Tale II (for Fairy Rings). These quests give permanent benefits that transform your F2P experience.

⏰ My Recommendation（我的建议）

Get membership once you hit level 50+ combat（战斗到 50+ 级再开会员）. At this point you've learned the basics, earned enough to appreciate member content, and have enough stats to actually use what membership unlocks. If you can't afford it yet, stay F2P until you've saved up 2M GP (enough to cover 2 months via bonds（够买两张 Bond）).

💰 Can You Afford Membership Just From F2P?（只靠免费版能负担会员吗？）

Yes! Here's the math: As a F2P player, you can make roughly 150K–300K GP/hour doing basic methods like killing Hill Giants, collecting cowhides, or running essence（打 Hill Giants、收牛皮、跑 essence 等基础方法）. In about 15–25 hours, you'll earn enough for one bond (14 days of membership). That means if you grind for a weekend, you get two weeks of membership essentially for "free."

4. OSRS Bond Guide: Step-by-Step Purchase Process（Bond 指南：分步购买流程）

⑦ Method 1: Buying a Bond with Real Money (Cheapest)（方法1：用真钱买 Bond，最便宜）

📋 Steps（步骤）:

  1. Login to the OSRS website (oldschool.runescape.com) with your account credentials
  2. Go to "Account" → "Manage Membership"
  3. Select "Subscribe with Bond" OR "Subscribe directly"
  4. Choose your duration (1/3/6/12 months)
  5. Complete payment via credit card, PayPal, or other supported method
  6. Membership activates immediately — no need to re-login

⑧ Method 2: Buying a Bond with In-Game Gold (Popular)（方法2：用游戏金币买 Bond，最流行）

📋 Steps（步骤）:

  1. Open the Grand Exchange (GE) interface anywhere in-game
  2. Search for "Old School bond"
  3. Click the highest available buy offer (current market price)
  4. Wait for the purchase to complete (usually instant or minutes)
  5. Open your inventory and right-click the bond → "Redeem"
  6. Choose "14 days of membership"
  7. Confirm — membership activates immediately!

⑨ Method 3: Buying from Another Player (Sometimes Cheaper)（方法3：向其他玩家买，有时更便宜）

In the Grand Exchange, you can also place a lower-than-market-price offer and wait for someone desperate to sell their bond quickly（挂一个低于市价的买单，等着急出手的玩家）. This can save you 100K–200K GP per bond but may take hours or days to fill.

💡 Pro Tip: Buy During Updates（专业提示：更新时买）

When a major OSRS update drops, many players rush back and sell bonds they've been holding（大更新时很多玩家回流抛售手中 Bond）. This temporarily floods the market and drives prices down by 100K–400K GP per bond. If you're planning to buy a bond soon, check the news feed and time your purchase around update days!

6. Your First 24 Hours as a Member — Action Plan（会员头 24 小时行动清单）

So you just got membership. Don't waste time wondering where to start（刚开会员，别浪费时间纠结从哪开始）. Follow this optimized plan:

🕐 ① Hour 0–1: Immediate Setup (Do This First!)（第 0–1 小时：立即设置）

  1. Claim your 7-day membership loyalty reward — Open the Extras tab → Claim reward (free items for new members)
  2. Buy a Dwarf Multi-cannon（买矮人多管炮） from any dwarf cannon seller (Nulodion, Grand Exchange) — ~600K GP setup cost
  3. Set your respawn to Lumbridge（重生点设为 Lumbridge） if not already (talk to Father Aereck)
  4. Check your spellbook（检查法术书） — If you haven't done Rune Mysteries, do it NOW (unlocks basic rune crafting and Magic Guild)

🕐 ② Hour 1–4: High-Impact Quests (Permanent Benefits)（第 1–4 小时：高收益任务）

Do these THREE quests immediately — each takes 15–30 minutes and gives permanent game-changing benefits:

  1. The Grand Tree (~30 min) → Spirit Tree teleports everywhere
  2. Tree Gnome Village (~20 min) → Spirit Tree access + agility course
  3. Vampire Slayer was F2P but now do Monkey Madness I prep quests first

🕐 ③ Hour 4–8: Money Making Upgrade（第 4–8 小时：升级赚钱法）

With membership, switch to these better money-making methods（会员后改用这些更优赚钱法）:

  1. Start Slayer（开始屠戮） — Visit any Slayer Master (Turael in Burthrope is easiest). Even Turael tasks earn 10x more than F2P grinding
  2. Try Barrows — With 43 Prayer (Protect prayers) and 50+ combat, you can attempt Barrows for 500K–1.5M per hour
  3. Nature Running via Abyss — If you did Rune Mysteries, run nature runes through the Abyss. 300K–500K/hour, zero risk

🕐 ④ Hour 8–16: Core Skill Unlocks（第 8–16 小时：核心技能解锁）

  1. Get 37 Agility (Gnome Stronghold course) → Unlocks graceful outfit (run forever basically)
  2. Start Construction → Build a Chapel with Gilded Altar (350% prayer XP)
  3. Start Farming → Plant your first herb/trees at any patch (they grow while offline!)
  4. Unlock Fairy Tales Part I → Access to Fairy Ring network (massive travel shortcut)

🕐 ⑤ Hour 16–24: First Boss Attempt + Long-Term Prep（第 16–24 小时：首次打 Boss + 长期准备）

  1. Try your first Barrows run — Use Protect Prayers (you read Stage 0.11!) against each brother's attack style
  2. Start Desert Treasure I → Takes several hours total but unlocks Ancient Magics (game-changing magic)
  3. Set up daily farm runs — Herb patches, tree patches, bird house runs (passive income while you sleep)

At the extreme end, dedicated F2P players reach 99 in multiple combat stats purely through PvP (wilderness bounty hunter) or grinding the same monsters forever. Some enjoy the challenge. 8. Membership FAQs（会员常见问题）

Is membership worth it for casual players who only play 2–3 hours per week?（每周只玩 2–3 小时的休闲玩家值得开吗？）

Absolutely yes — arguably MORE worthwhile for casual players（绝对值得，对休闲玩家更值）. Why? Because membership lets you make progress faster during limited playtime. A casual player spending 2 hours doing Slayer with membership gains more XP and GP than 5 hours of F2P grinding. Plus, your membership doesn't expire when you're not playing — those 14 days last whether you log in daily or once a week. So buy a bond, play when you can, and don't feel pressured to "get your money's worth" by grinding.

Can I pay for membership entirely with in-game gold?（能完全用游戏金币开会员吗？）

Yes, this is extremely common（非常常见）. Many players never spend real money on membership — they earn enough GP to buy bonds indefinitely. Once you're making 500K+/hr (achievable within weeks of getting membership), you only need to grind about 9–10 hours per month to fund another bond. For active players, this is easily sustainable.

What happens to my member items when membership expires?（会员到期后我的会员物品会怎样？）

Member items stay in your bank — you don't lose them. But you cannot wear them, use them, or equip them. They become visually greyed out in your inventory/worn equipment. As soon as you reactivate membership, everything becomes usable again instantly. Quest rewards, skill levels, and unlocked areas remain permanently yours.

Should I buy 1 month or use a bond?（买一个月订阅还是用 Bond？）

It depends on your situation（取决于你的情况）:

  • Use real money subscription if: You want the cheapest rate ($8.29/month with annual sub), you don't have much GP, you prefer set-and-forget auto-renewal.
  • Use bonds if: You have plenty of GP (2M+), you want flexibility (no recurring charge), you're under 18 and can't pay online, you enjoy earning your membership in-game.

Is there a free trial for membership?（有免费试用吗？）

Jagex occasionally offers free 3-day or 7-day trials (usually around major updates or holidays). Check the OSRS website homepage or in-game news feed. These are rare but worth claiming when available. Even 3 days is enough to complete 5–10 key member quests that give permanent benefits.
