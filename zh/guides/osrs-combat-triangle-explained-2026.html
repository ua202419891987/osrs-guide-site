OSRS 战斗三角详解 2026（新手必读）

OSRS 的战斗不只是「点怪、打伤害」。它有一套类似石头剪刀布的「战斗三角」系统，决定哪种风格克制哪种。在练级前搞懂它，能帮你避开代价高昂的错误。

⚔️ OSRS Combat Triangle Explained 2026 — Melee, Ranged & Magic for Beginners

OSRS combat isn't just 'click monster, deal damage.' There's a rock-paper-scissors system called the Combat Triangle that determines which style beats which. Understanding this before you train will save you from making expensive mistakes.

📅 Novice（新手） — Part of our Weekly Beginner Series · 9 min read

⏱️ Quick Summary — 30-Second Read（快速摘要 — 30 秒速读）

  • 📌 Melee beats Ranged, Ranged beats Magic, Magic beats Melee（近战克远程，远程克魔法，魔法克近战） — like Rock-Paper-Scissors.
  • 📌 Start with Melee（从近战起步） — no ammo, no runes, simplest for new players.
  • 📌 Train Attack → Strength → Defence first（优先练攻击→力量→防御） — reach 40/40/40 for Rune gear.
  • 📌 The triangle matters most in PvP（三角在 PvP 中最关键） — vs NPCs, use the monster's weakness.

1. What Is the Combat Triangle?（什么是战斗三角？）

The Combat Triangle is the core system that determines which combat style has the advantage over others. It works like Rock-Paper-Scissors:

⚔️ The Three-Way Relationship（三方关系）

  • 🗡️ Melee beats 🏹 Ranged (Melee has an advantage against ranged users)
  • 🏹 Ranged beats ✨ Magic (Ranged has an advantage against mages)
  • ✨ Magic beats 🗡️ Melee (Magic has an advantage against melee users)

What "advantage" means in practice（「优势」实际含义）: If you use a style that has the advantage, you'll hit more often and take less damage. If you're at a disadvantage, you'll miss more and get hit harder.

💡 Important: The Triangle Only Matters in PvP and Some PvM（重要：三角仅在 PvP 与部分 PvM 中重要）

Against most NPCs (monsters), the combat triangle doesn't matter much. NPCs have fixed weaknesses. A general rule: Melee for most things, Magic for monsters with high defence, Ranged for safety (attack from distance). The triangle matters most when fighting other players.

2. Melee — The Starting Point for Everyone（近战 — 每个人的起点）

Melee is fighting up close with swords, axes, maces, or fists. Every new player starts with melee because it's the simplest and requires no ammunition (unlike ranged) or runes (unlike magic).

The 3 Melee Stats（近战的 3 项属性）

Skill（技能）What It Does（作用）How to Train（如何训练）
AttackDetermines which weapons you can wield and your accuracy (hit rate)Combat training with "Accurate" style
StrengthDetermines your max hit (how hard you hit)Combat training with "Aggressive" style
DefenceDetermines how often you avoid damageCombat training with "Defensive" style

Melee Combat Styles (Weapon Types)（近战战斗风格（武器类型））

Weapon Type（武器类型）Attack Types (Styles)（攻击类型）Best For（适用）
Sword / ScimitarSlash (most common)General training, fast attacks
AxeSlash / CrushStrength training (slow but hard hits)
Mace / MaulCrushAgainst monsters with slash defence weakness
Spear / HailberdStab / SlashSafe spotting (attacks from 2 tiles away)

🛡️ Beginner Melee Gear (F2P)（新手近战装备（免费服））

  • Levels 1-10: Bronze → Iron → Steel equipment (Tutorial Island gives you iron gear)
  • Levels 10-30: Black / Mithril equipment (buy from general stores or other players)
  • Levels 30-50: Adamant / Rune equipment (Rune is the best F2P gear!)
  • Weapon: Scimitar is the best F2P melee weapon (fast, good damage)

3. Ranged — Attack From a Distance（远程 — 远距离攻击）

Ranged lets you attack monsters from a distance using bows, crossbows, or thrown weapons. The big advantage: you can often attack without taking damage (the monster can't reach you).

The 2 Ranged Stats（远程的 2 项属性）

Skill（技能）What It Does（作用）
RangedDetermines your accuracy and what bows/crossbows you can use
Defence(Same as melee) Helps you take less damage

Ranged Ammunition — You Need These!（远程弹药 — 你必须有！）

Unlike melee (which needs no ammo), ranged requires arrows (for bows) or bolts (for crossbows). If you run out, you can't attack.

  • Bows（弓）: Shortbow (fast, less accurate) / Longbow (slower, more accurate) / Composite bow (members)
  • Crossbows（弩）: Slower but hit harder. Need bolts.
  • Ammunition costs（弹药花费）: Bronze arrows are cheap. Higher-tier arrows get expensive fast.
💰 Ranged Is More Expensive Than Melee（远程比近战更烧钱）

You'll constantly spend money on arrows/bolts. As a beginner with little gold, melee is cheaper. Consider ranged only after you have a steady income source.

4. Magic — Spells, Runes, and Ancient Magicks（魔法 — 法术、符文与远古魔法）

Magic uses spells that consume runes (consumable items). Magic is extremely powerful for certain tasks (high-damage spells, teleportation, utility spells) but requires constant rune purchases.

The 2 Magic Stats（魔法的 2 项属性）

Skill（技能）What It Does（作用）
MagicDetermines spell accuracy, what spells you can cast, and magic damage
Defence(Same as above)

Important Magic Concepts（重要魔法概念）

  • Spellbook（法术书）: You start with the Standard spellbook. Later you can unlock Ancient Magicks (powerful multi-target spells) and Lunar spellbook (utility spells).
  • Runes（符文）: Each spell consumes specific runes. You need to buy them from the Varrock rune shop or other players.
  • Auto-casting（自动施法）: Set a spell in your spellbook → it automatically uses that spell when you attack with the "Magic" combat style.
  • Magic weapons（魔法武器）: Staves (like the Air Staff) provide infinite runes of one type. Save you money!
⚠️ Magic Is Expensive for Beginners（魔法对新手很贵）

Every magic attack consumes runes. As a new player with 0 gold, magic is the most expensive combat style to maintain. Most players train Magic last (or use Magic only for teleporting).

5. How the Triangle Actually Works (Damage Bonuses)（战斗三角实际如何运作（伤害加成））

Here's the math (simplified) of how the combat triangle affects your damage:

Your Style（你的风格）Opponent Style（对手风格）Effect（效果）
MeleeRanged+10% accuracy, -5% damage taken
RangedMagic+10% accuracy, -5% damage taken
MagicMelee+10% accuracy, -5% damage taken
If neither has advantage: normal accuracy

For NPCs (monsters)（针对怪物）: Most monsters have a specific weakness (shown in the combat interface when you right-click them → "Show Combat Info"). Hit them with their weakness style for best results.

🔍 How to Check an NPC's Weakness（如何查看怪物弱点）

  1. Right-click the NPC → "Show Combat Info"
  2. Look at the Weakness line
  3. Equip the corresponding style and attack!

Example（例子）: Most dragons are weak to Stab (use a spear or stab weapon). Giants are weak to Crush (use a maul or mace).

6. Which Combat Style Should YOU Train First?（你该先练哪种战斗风格？）

As a complete beginner, here's my recommendation:

🥇 Start With Melee — Here's Why（从近战起步 — 原因如下）

  1. No ammo cost（无需弹药）: You never run out of "ammunition" with melee
  2. Simple（简单）: Walk up to monster, click it, done
  3. High HP XP（生命值经验高）: Melee training also gives you Hitpoints XP (you take damage while training)
  4. Best F2P gear is accessible（最佳免费服装备易得）: Rune armor (level 40 Defence) is cheap and effective
  5. Foundation for everything（一切的基础）: Most PvM content expects decent melee stats

7. Understanding Your Combat Stats (The Combat Level Formula)（了解你的战斗属性（战斗等级公式））

Your Combat Level determines which monsters you can fight and which players you get matched with in PvP. Here's the formula (simplified):

⚔️ Combat Level Formula（战斗等级公式）

Combat Level =

Base: (Defence + Hitpoints + Prayer/2 + Magic/2 + Range/2) / 4

Plus: Max(Attack+Strength, 2×Magic, 2×Range) / 4

(Don't worry — you don't need to calculate this manually. The game shows your combat level in the skill tab.)

What Combat Level Means in Practice（战斗等级的实际含义）

  • Level 3: Fresh account. Can't fight much. Tutorial Island gets you to about level 10.
  • Level 30-40: Can wear Rune armor. Can fight most F2P monsters comfortably.
  • Level 60-70: Mid-game. Can do most member quests. Ready for early bossing.
  • Level 100+: High-level content. Endgame bossing, raids, PvP.

🛡️ Always Upgrade Your Weapon First（永远先升级武器）

A better weapon gives you more damage than better armor does. As a rule: upgrade weapon → then upgrade armor. A Rune scimitar with Iron armor is better than an Iron sword with Rune armor.

9. Common Combat Mistakes to Avoid（常见战斗错误需避免）

⚠️ Top 5 Combat Mistakes Beginners Make（新手最常犯的 5 个战斗错误）
  1. Never changing attack style（从不切换攻击风格）: You've been training "Accurate" (Attack XP) for 20 levels and wonder why your Strength is still level 1. Check your combat tab!
  2. Not bringing food（不带食物）: You run out of food in the middle of a fight and die, losing all your items. Always bring more food than you think you need.
  3. Not checking NPC weakness（不查怪物弱点）: Hitting a monster with the wrong style (e.g., slashing a stab-weak monster) means you miss 50% of your hits. Check Weakness first!
  4. Spending all gold on gear too early（过早把钱全花在装备上）: You buy Rune armor at level 35 Defence (can't wear it!) or buy gear before having a money-making method. Build income first!
  5. AFK combat without checking（挂机战斗不查看）: OSRS has an AFK logout timer (about 5 minutes). You walk away and come back to a logged-out character. Set a timer!

10. Your First Combat Training Plan（你的首个战斗训练计划）

Here's your exact step-by-step combat training plan for your first week:

📅 Week 1 Combat Plan (F2P)（第一周战斗计划（免费服））

  1. Day 1（第 1 天）: Finish Tutorial Island → kill cows near Lumbridge for Attack/Strength (use "Accurate" for Attack, "Aggressive" for Strength)
  2. Day 2-3（第 2-3 天）: Reach 20 Attack + 20 Strength → upgrade to Steel scimitar → kill Al-Kharid warriors (bring food!)
  3. Day 4-5（第 4-5 天）: Reach 30 Attack + 30 Strength → Mithril scimitar → kill Moss Giants (Edgeville Dungeon, bring lots of food)
  4. Day 6-7（第 6-7 天）: Reach 40 Attack + 40 Strength + 40 Defence → Rune gear! → You're now ready for Member content
