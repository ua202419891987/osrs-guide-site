OSRS DPS Calculator

Max hit, hit chance and DPS for Melee, Ranged and Magic — with the parts other calculators skip: special attacks, multi-hit weapons and poison damage, all folded into one honest effective DPS number.

Formulas open-source in js/dps-engine.js
💡 Every calculator agrees on a plain whip swing. They stop agreeing the moment you throw a spec. This one simulates the whole kill — special attack energy, defence drain, multi-hit weapons and damage over time — so the number you see is the number you get in game.

What This Tool Does

The OSRS DPS Calculator turns your stats, gear bonuses and a target monster into four numbers that actually matter: your max hit, your hit chance, your damage per second, and your time to kill. It supports all three combat styles with their own separate formulas — Melee and Ranged use the effective-strength and equipment-bonus maths, while Magic uses the spell's base max hit plus your additive magic damage percentage. Prayers, potions, Void sets, attack stances, Slayer helmet (i), Salve amulet and Dragon hunter gear are all handled as proper multipliers rather than being bolted on at the end.

Why our numbers differ from other calculators

Four popular OSRS DPS calculators all share the same blind spot: they model a single, repeated, ordinary attack. Real combat is not that. You open a Bandos kill with a Dragon warhammer, land a 30% defence drain, and every swing for the rest of that kill is more accurate. You bring Dragon claws and land a four-hit cascade where the first successful accuracy roll decides the damage of the next three. You swing a Scythe of Vitur at a 5x5 boss and it hits three times, at 100%, 50% and 25% of your max. You run a Toxic blowpipe and venom quietly adds 6, 8, 10, 12 damage in the background every eighteen seconds. None of that shows up in a "hit chance times max hit over two" formula, which is why players keep saying their real kill times do not match the calculator.

This tool models those cases explicitly. It walks the kill forward tick by tick, spends special attack energy as it regenerates (10% per 30 seconds, or every 15 seconds with a Lightbearer), applies defence drains to the target so later attacks become more accurate, and adds poison or venom on top. The result is an effective DPS that includes your specs, not a paper DPS that pretends you never pressed the spec bar.

The second thing we add is money. A setup that wins on DPS can still be the wrong answer if it burns eight thousand GP per shot in ruby dragon bolts. The premium GP-per-damage panel converts ammunition, runes, potions and food into a single "GP per point of damage" figure and a supply cost per kill, so you can compare setups the way your bank account experiences them. As far as we know, no other OSRS DPS calculator does this.

Honest limits. We do not model enchanted bolt procs, Twisted bow's magic-level scaling, raid-specific invocations, or player-versus-player mechanics. Where an item stat or special attack value is community-reported rather than something we can verify, the tool marks it with a small verify tag and you can type over it. Every input on this page is editable for exactly that reason.

Data source & disclaimer. Combat formulas follow the OSRS Wiki "Damage per second" pages and are written out in plain JavaScript with comments in tools/dps-calculator/js/dps-engine.js — read it and check our working. Item stats, monster defence values and special attack multipliers live in js/data.js; anything we could not verify with confidence is tagged verify and every field on this page is editable. OSRS Wiki is the authority — if you spot a discrepancy, please tell us and we will fix it.
