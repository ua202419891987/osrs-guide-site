What This Tool Does

The OSRS Profit Finder is a free calculator that matches your account to the most suitable money making methods in Old School RuneScape. Instead of trusting wiki averages that get inflated by rare drops, the tool ranks methods by their conservative low-end GP/hr — the amount you can realistically expect on an unlucky day — so you can plan around a reliable floor rather than a fantasy number.

To use it, enter three things: your combat level range, your starting capital, and your preferred playstyle (PvM, skilling, merchant, or AFK). Tick the Ironman box if you play an Ironman account; the tool then hides merchant and trade-only methods and flags content that is difficult or impossible to obtain without trading. Within seconds you get three personalized methods, each showing its low-end GP/hr, risk level, required stats, and a link to a full step-by-step guide.

All figures are sourced from the OSRS Wiki GP/hr tables and cross-checked against community-tested rates. The tool is completely free, contains no paywall, and requires no sign-up. It is built to give new and returning players a fast, honest answer to the question: "What should my account actually be doing to make money right now?"
