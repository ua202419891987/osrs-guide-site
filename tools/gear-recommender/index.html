OSRS Gear Recommender

Free tool. Enter your combat level and style — get the best gear for your level, the total GE cost, a requirement check, and your exact next upgrade. No paywall, no sign-up.

💡 Gear is pulled from the OSRS Wiki / best-in-slot data and matched to your current level. Prices are GE reference estimates (updated 2026-07). Each slot shows its upgrade path so you always know what to buy next.

What This Tool Does

The OSRS Gear Recommender is a free tool that tells you the best equipment to use at your current combat level, for Melee, Ranged, or Magic. Instead of overwhelming you with every best-in-slot item at once, it shows the optimal gear for the tier you are actually in and, just as importantly, exactly what to buy or grind next.

Enter your combat level and chosen combat style, then tick the Ironman box if you play an Ironman account. The tool pulls gear from the OSRS Wiki and best-in-slot datasets, sorts it into level tiers, and renders your full 10-slot setup: head, cape, amulet, weapon, shield, body, legs, gloves, boots, and ring. Each slot shows its Grand Exchange price estimate, membership requirement, stat prerequisites, how to obtain it, and the upgrade path to the next tier.

The summary panel adds up your total GE cost, lists the levels you still need, and flags untradeable pieces an Ironman must earn themselves. Prices are GE reference estimates updated for 2026. The tool is free, has no paywall, and needs no sign-up — built to answer one question fast: "What gear should I be using right now, and what's my next upgrade?"
