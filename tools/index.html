Why these seven tools exist

Old School RuneScape is a game of long commitments. A Slayer grind is measured in days, a collection log page in weeks, and a gear upgrade in tens or hundreds of millions of GP. The cost of a bad decision is not a wasted click — it is a wasted month. Yet the information needed to make those decisions is scattered across wiki tables, five-year-old forum posts, and YouTube videos whose GP/hr numbers were true for a patch that no longer exists.

Each tool here targets one decision that players consistently get wrong. The Profit Finder exists because most money-making lists rank methods by best-case averages inflated by rare drops, which is useless if you want to know what a bad night looks like. The Gear Recommender exists because "best in slot" advice ignores that almost nobody has 2B to spend. The DPS Calculator exists because the honest answer to "is this weapon worth it" is a number, not an opinion. The Drop Rate Lookup exists because human intuition about probability is catastrophically bad and being 3x the drop rate dry is far more common than it feels. The GP and Supply Tracker exists because gross loot is not profit once you subtract Saradomin brews, Blood runes and death costs. The Bank Layout Library exists because an unusable bank quietly taxes every session you play. And the Collection Log Advisor exists because no tool anywhere would tell you which page to open next.

All of them run entirely in your browser. There is no account system, no launcher, no plugin and no server storing your data — your inputs, saved presets and log progress live in your own browser's local storage and never leave your machine. That design choice is why the free tier can be genuinely generous rather than a demo: serving a static page costs almost nothing, so the free version is not a trap, it is the product.

The Premium tier exists for a narrower reason. The features behind it — full rankings, exports, saved builds, session history, confidence intervals — are the ones people use when they are planning seriously rather than checking a single number. If that is you, $1.9 a month keeps the site running without ads dictating the content. If it is not you, the free tier is not going anywhere.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to use the tools?

No. There is no registration, no email and no login. Every tool works the moment the page loads, and anything you save — bank presets, gear builds, collection log progress — is written to your own browser's local storage.

Does one subscription cover all the tools?

Yes. Premium is a single site-wide plan. Subscribing from the DPS Calculator unlocks the Collection Log Advisor, the GP Tracker and everything else in the table above, plus any premium tool added later. There is no per-tool pricing and no higher tier.

How do I cancel?

Cancel directly in the PayPal or Stripe dashboard you subscribed through — the same place you manage every other subscription with that provider. There is no cancellation form here and nothing to email. Access continues until the end of the period you already paid for, then the account quietly reverts to the free tier with nothing lost.

Can Ironman accounts use these tools?

Yes, and several are built with Ironman in mind. The Profit Finder hides merchanting and trade-only methods when you tick the Ironman box, the Gear Recommender can be pointed at obtainable-only upgrades, and the Collection Log Advisor has a dedicated "Ironman essentials" ranking mode that weights drops you cannot simply buy off the Grand Exchange.

Where does the data come from?

Drop rates, requirements and mechanics come from the OSRS Wiki and are cross-checked against community-tested rates before being added. GP figures are deliberately conservative low-end estimates rather than best-case averages. Where a rate or weighting could not be verified with confidence, the content is left out entirely rather than filled in with a guess — the Collection Log Advisor names the sources it has excluded for exactly this reason.

Does using these tools break Jagex's rules?

No. Every tool on this site is a web-based calculator or lookup. Nothing interacts with the game client, reads game memory, injects into or overlays the client, or automates any part of gameplay. You read a number on a web page and then play the game yourself — which is the same category as a wiki calculator or a spreadsheet, and is consistent with Jagex's third-party tool policy. There is no plugin, no download and no executable of any kind.

How accurate are the GP/hr numbers?

They are intentionally conservative. Most published GP/hr figures assume perfect efficiency and average out rare drops that you may never see, which makes them a poor basis for planning. The tools here quote a realistic floor instead, so a good session beats the estimate rather than disappointing you against it. Item values still move with the Grand Exchange, so treat any GP figure as a well-informed approximation, not a quote.

What happens to my saved data if I stop paying?

Nothing is deleted. Saved presets, builds and log progress live in your browser, not on a server, so they survive a cancellation. You simply return to the free limits — for example the Bank Layout Library keeps showing your presets but caps new saves at three again.
