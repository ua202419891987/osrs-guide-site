What This Tool Does

The OSRS Profit Calculator answers a question that almost every other Old School RuneScape tool quietly dodges: after you pay for supplies, how much GP did that hour actually earn you? Loot trackers, wiki money-making tables and most GP/hr calculators report gross revenue — the raw value of what dropped. They do not subtract the Super restores you drank, the Saradomin brews you burned through, the dragon arrows you never picked back up, the blowpipe scales that vanished, the Barrows teleport tablets, or the gear you lost to a death. On high-tier bosses those supplies routinely eat 20–45% of your gross loot, which means the number most players quote for their favourite boss is not the number that lands in their bank.

This tracker closes that gap. Pick one of 18 presets — Vorkath, Zulrah, Alchemical Hydra, Cerberus, the four God Wars bosses, Demonic gorillas, Grotesque Guardians, Vardorvis, Rune dragons, Barrows, Chambers of Xeric, a generic Slayer task, Wintertodt, Green dragons, or a blank custom sheet — then tell it how many kills you did and how long it took. It loads live Grand Exchange prices straight from the official OSRS Wiki price API, prices every single supply line at what you would actually pay to instant-buy it, and shows you the total bill, the cost per kill, and the cost per hour. Every consumption figure on the sheet is an editable input, because your setup is not the wiki's setup: swap a blowpipe for a bowfa, delete the rune rows if you use a Sanguinesti staff, or add whatever the preset missed.

The preset consumption rates are converted from the OSRS Wiki money-making guide tables and from verifiable in-game mechanics, and each activity tells you which of the two it came from. They are community averages, not laws of physics — a lucky Hydra trip and an unlucky one can differ by 200k in supplies alone. Adjust them, and the numbers become yours. Add your loot total from RuneLite's Loot Tracker and the premium net-profit panel gives you the figure that actually matters: net GP per hour, profit margin, and the break-even loot value every single kill has to beat before you earn a coin.

Read this before you trust a number. Three assumptions in this tool can be wrong for you:
· Supply consumption. Every preset quantity is a community average converted from the OSRS Wiki money-making guide or derived from published game mechanics. Your gear, prayer book, kill speed and how often you tank a hit will all move these numbers. Edit them.
· Loot value. The tool never invents your revenue. The community ranges shown next to the loot field are deliberately wide sanity checks, not predictions — a single rare drop can swing a session by tens of millions. Paste your real RuneLite Loot Tracker total for an honest answer.
· Prices. Live prices are instant-buy (high) values from the OSRS Wiki API. Thin-volume items can print odd instant-buy prices for a few minutes. If the API is unreachable the tool falls back to a built-in August 2026 snapshot and says so at the top of the page.
Why "net" is the only number worth quoting. Two players can both say "Vorkath is 1.4M/hr". The one killing it with a Dragon hunter crossbow, Extended super antifires and Divine ranging potions is paying several hundred thousand GP an hour for that rate. The one on a Dragon hunter lance with Super combats is paying far less. Same gross, very different bank. That difference is exactly what this page exists to expose.
