What This Tool Does

The OSRS Drop Rate Lookup answers two questions that get asked on r/2007scape every single day: "what is the drop rate for this item?" and "I am X kills dry — is that normal, or is something wrong?" The first question needs a searchable table; the second needs actual probability maths. This tool does both in one page, and unlike a RuneLite plugin it works on a phone, on iOS, and on a work laptop where you cannot install anything.

The lookup half contains 35 of the most frequently searched drops in Old School RuneScape, covering the popular solo bosses (Zulrah, Vorkath, Cerberus, Kraken, Abyssal Sire, Alchemical Hydra), all four God Wars Dungeon generals, the Dagannoth Kings, high-demand Slayer drops such as the abyssal whip, black mask and zenyte shard, plus the Dragon Warhammer from Lizardman Shamans. Each row lists the source, the item, the published drop rate as a clean fraction, and an approximate Grand Exchange value band so you can see at a glance whether a grind is worth the time.

The dry calculator half is the part players actually come back for. Enter your kill count and the tool applies the binomial "no success" formula — the probability of still having nothing after k independent rolls is (1 − 1/rate) raised to the power of k. From that single number it derives everything else that matters: the percentage chance you should still be empty-handed, the median number of kills (the point where half of all players have the drop), how far past the average you currently are, and a plain-English verdict telling you whether your streak is unremarkable, genuinely unlucky, or the kind of thing worth screenshotting. It also shows the kill counts needed to reach a 50%, 90% and 99% chance, which is far more useful for planning a grind than the raw drop rate alone.

The lookup, the search, and the dry calculator are permanently free. Two extras — expected GP value per kill, and a side-by-side comparison of several drop targets — are part of OSRS Guru Premium.
