OSRS Bank Layout Library

12 battle-tested bank tab templates for Ironman, PvM, Skiller, Slayer and Raids accounts — each one shows what goes in every tab and why. Edit any template, save your own, and copy it as plain text to share in Discord.

Free · unlimited browsing
💡 A bank layout is not decoration — it is seconds saved per trip. The templates below are built around the one rule that actually matters: the stuff you grab every single trip lives closest to the tab you open first.

What This Tool Does

The OSRS Bank Layout Library is a free reference tool that turns the endless "how should I organise my bank?" question into 12 concrete, copy-able templates. Old School RuneScape gives every account a main bank tab plus nine numbered tabs, but the game offers no guidance at all on how to use them. Most players end up with one gigantic unsorted tab, then lose ten to fifteen seconds every single banking trip hunting for a stamina potion or the right teleport. Over a few hundred hours of Slayer or bossing, that adds up to entire wasted play sessions.

Each template in this library was built around a specific playstyle — Ironman all-rounder, boss rusher, Skiller chasing max cape, brand-new account, Raids preparation, Slayer grinder, AFK player, Grand Exchange merchant, quest cape runner, Group Ironman shared bank, Wilderness PKer, and endgame maxed main. For every tab you get three things: a short name you can copy straight into a RuneLite bank tag, the categories of items that belong there, and a plain-English explanation of why that grouping saves you time. The visual grid shows the whole layout at a glance so you can judge whether it fits how you actually play before committing to a re-sort.

You can also build your own layout from scratch or start from any template, rename sections, add or remove tabs, reorder them with the up and down buttons, and save the result to your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere — saved layouts live in your own browser's local storage. Finally, the one-click text export produces a clean plain-text checklist that is perfect for pasting into a clan Discord, a Reddit comment, or your own notes app while you re-sort in game. The whole library is free with no sign-up.
