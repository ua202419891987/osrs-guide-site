What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide information to site owners about how the site is being used.

How We Use Cookies

OSRS Guru uses cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

  • Essential cookies: Required for basic functionality of the site, such as remembering your preferences and keeping the site secure.
  • Analytics cookies: We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors use our site — which pages are popular, how long visitors stay, and where they come from. This helps us improve our content. These cookies collect information in an anonymous, aggregated form.
  • Advertising cookies: We display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to build a profile of your interests and show you more relevant ads on this site and across the web. This is known as personalized advertising.
  • Functional cookies: Used to remember choices you make (such as language selection or dismissed notifications) to provide a more personalized experience.

Cookies Set by Third Parties

Some cookies on our site are set by third-party services we use. These third parties may use cookies to collect information about your online activity over time and across different websites:

  • Google Analytics (GA4): Analytical cookies with names like _ga, _ga_*, and _gid. These help us measure site traffic and usage patterns. Data retention follows Google Analytics' settings.
  • Google AdSense: Advertising cookies such as _gads, IDE, and NID used to serve and measure ads.
  • Payment platforms (Payhip): If you purchase a digital product, the payment processor may set cookies to process your transaction securely.

We do not control these third-party cookies. Please review the privacy and cookie policies of these providers for more information about how they use data.

How to Control Cookies

You have several options to control or disable cookies:

  • Browser settings: Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies through their settings menu. You can find instructions in your browser's help section. Note that disabling essential cookies may affect how the site functions.
  • Google Ads Settings: You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.
  • Google Analytics opt-out: You can prevent Google Analytics from collecting data about you by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Cookie consent tools: Where required by law (such as in the EU/EEA or UK), we may display a cookie consent banner that lets you choose which cookies to allow.
