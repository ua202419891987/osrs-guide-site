🏆 Maxed in OSRS? 15 Goals to Chase After the Max Cape (2026)

Updated August 19, 2026  |  19 min read  |  By Yanchun Wu

You finally hit 99 in every skill and the max cape is on your back. Now what? OSRS does not end at maxing — it just changes shape. This guide lays out 15 concrete, long-term goals that give a maxed account real purpose, from Combat Achievements and the Collection Log to pets, boss records, Ironman challenges, clans, and speedrunning.

📅 Updated: August 2026 — Endgame Goal Guide · 16 min read
🛡️ Level: Maxed (99s / Quest Cape)
🆕 NEW

The max cape is a beginning, not an ending. With every skill at 99 and the Quest Point Cape in your bank, you have the freedom to chase the parts of the game that are actually hard: the grinds that punish RNG, the tasks that punish execution, and the content that punishes patience. Start with our Max Account Training Guide if you are still closing out a few 99s, then use the Combat Achievements Guide as your first post-max milestone tracker.

💡 Quick Verdict

If you only pick one goal, make it the Combat Achievements Grandmaster tier — it is the clearest "skill check" in the game and touches almost every boss. After that, the Collection Log and pets are the two grinds that will genuinely occupy you for years. Everything else on this list (speedruns, clans, diaries, the Completionist Cape) is flavor you can layer on top without burning out.

1. 🎯 Welcome to the Endgame — What "Maxed" Really Means

Most players imagine maxing as a finish line. In practice it is a gear shift. The early game is about getting capable; the endgame is about getting complete. The XP treadmill stops being the point and is replaced by a web of side objectives that are far more personal and far more punishing.

① The Two Flavors of "Done"

There are two milestones people call "maxed":

  • Maxed stats: all 23 skills at 99. This gives the max cape and unlocks the highest-tier methods everywhere.
  • Quest cape: all quest points (currently 299+ QP). This is usually done long before 99s but is worth naming because it gates content like the Completionist Cape.

Neither is "the end." The real endgame is the intersection of Combat Achievements, the Collection Log, pets, diaries, and personal records — and that is a decade-long commitment for most players.

② Why the Post-Max Game Is Better

Without XP pressure you can finally optimize for fun. You are no longer forced into the most efficient training method; you can chase the rarest drop, the hardest solo kill, or the fastest recorded speedrun. The account becomes a canvas. The 15 goals below are the most popular canvases players actually paint on.

③ How to Avoid Burnout

The biggest post-max mistake is treating it like a second XP grind. Pick one or two long goals (pets, Collection Log) and several short ones (a diary tier, a CA task batch) so you always have a win within reach. Variety is the only sustainable endgame.

2. ⚔️ Combat Achievements (Tiered Tasks)

Combat Achievements (CAs) are the single best-structured endgame system Jagex has shipped. They are grouped into tiers, each a checklist of combat feats — from "kill X with Y prayer" to "solo Zulrah under a time limit." Completing a tier grants a permanent perk (extra drop rate, Skilling Backpack slots, etc.).

① The Tier Ladder

Tier Typical Tasks Reward Perk Effort
EasyBasic boss KC, simple mechanics+5% Superior Slayer drop chanceLow (days)
MediumGear swaps, mid-tier solos+10% Superior Slayer drop chanceMedium (weeks)
HardClean solos, some speed tasksBank space + Skilling BackpackHigh (months)
EliteStrict speed/solo challengesMore bank space, decoratorVery High
MasterTop-tier executionExtra ornamental capeExtreme
GrandmasterThe hardest feats in the gameGM cape + permanent perksLegendary

② Why CAs Are the Best First Goal

They force you to actually learn boss mechanics instead of brute-forcing them with max stats. A maxed account with no CA progress is, frankly, a tourist. Working toward Grandmaster makes you a competent PvMer and naturally feeds your pet and log grinds because you will be killing everything anyway.

③ How to Approach It

  • Start with Easy and Medium — most unlock just by doing content you enjoy.
  • Use the in-game CA tracker; it filters by boss and by tier.
  • Batch similar tasks (e.g., all "no food" challenges) in one session.
  • Our Combat Achievements Advanced Guide and Master Guide break down the brutal GM tasks specifically.

3. 📒 The Collection Log (Completion)

The Collection Log (CL) tracks every unique item you have obtained across bosses, minigames, clues, and skills. It is the closest thing OSRS has to a "completion percentage," and for many maxed players it becomes the main game.

① What Counts

The log is split into sections: Bosses, Clues, Minigames, Skills, Seasonal, and more. Each entry is a specific item — a boss pet, a unique weapon, a holiday scarf. Some are common; others are 1/5,000 drops you will hate.

② Effort vs Reward

CL Section Time to Meaningful Progress Pain Level
Clue ScrollsMonths of consistent casketsMedium (RNG, but steady)
BossesYears of KCHigh (rares are cruel)
MinigamesWeeksLow–Medium
Skills (skillcapes, pets)MonthsMedium
Seasonal / HolidayOne event per yearLow (but slow to finish)

③ Strategy

Do not chase the whole log at once — pick one boss or one clue tier and commit. The log rewards consistency more than intensity. Many players pair CL grinding with pet hunting (the next section) since the drops overlap.

4. 🐾 Pets — The Grind Within the Grind

Almost every boss and several skilling activities have a pet drop. Pets are the ultimate flex because they are rare, visible, and permanent. They are also the definition of a burnout grind.

① The Most-Wanted Pets

Pet Source Drop Rate Why Players Want It
Baby ChinchompaHunting chinchompas1/2,500Easy first pet, skilling
Pet Chaos ElementalChaos Elemental1/350Iconic, early Wilderness pet

② How to Survive the Grind

  • Stack pet hunts with money-making — kill profitable bosses and the pet is a bonus.
  • Track your rate with a drop counter plugin so you know you are "due."
  • Accept that 3x the quoted rate is normal. RNG is not fair, it is random.
  • Pair with CAs and the Collection Log so one grind serves three goals.

③ The "Pet Gap" Mindset

Many maxed players measure progress by "pets obtained vs pets available." It is a clean, motivating scoreboard. Set a target (e.g., "all boss pets") and chip away. The Slayer Guide helps you find pet-bearing tasks efficiently once you are deep into the log.

5. 👑 Boss Records & Kill-Count Milestones

If pets are about rarity, records are about skill. Post-max players love quantifiable mastery: fastest solo, highest solo kill count, or "all bosses at GM CA."

① Kill-Count Milestones

Round numbers feel good. 1,000 Zulrah, 5,000 Vorkath, 10,000 Corrupted Gauntlet — these become personal trophies. They also quietly build your pet and log odds.

② Speed & Solo Challenges

  • Personal bests: time your cleanest solo of each boss.
  • Deathless streaks: how many Vorkath kills without a single hit taken?
  • Theatre of Blood / Tombs of Amascut: high-level raid records and no-death runs.
  • Inferno / Colosseum: the pinnacle solo PvM achievements.

③ Why Records Matter

Records turn repetition into improvement. Every maxed account can do the content; few can do it fast, clean, and consistently. That gap is the entire appeal.

6. ⛏️ Ironman / Hardcore Challenges

If your maxed account is a normal main, the Ironman modes are a whole second game hiding inside OSRS. Many maxed mains start an Ironman (or Hardcore Ironman, or Ultimate Ironman) precisely because the self-sufficient restriction makes the post-max grind meaningful again.

① Why Ironman After Maxing

On a main, "maxed" means you can buy anything. On an Ironman, "maxed" means you gathered everything yourself — a totally different achievement. The restriction re-creates the scarcity that made early game fun.

② The Challenge Ladders

Mode Rule Appeal
IronmanNo trading with other playersSelf-sufficient mastery
Hardcore IronmanOne lifeHigh-stakes tension
Ultimate IronmanNo bankExtreme logistics puzzle
Group IronmanTrade only within groupShared progression with friends

③ Getting Started

If this appeals to you, our Ironman Beginner Guide is the right on-ramp. The twist is that as a maxed main you already know the content — you just have to re-earn it the hard way, which is exactly the point.

7. 🏰 Clans, Social & Community Goals

OSRS is more fun with people. The clan system, group bosses, and community events give a maxed account a social reason to log in.

① Clan Goals

  • Clan capital: build and upgrade a shared clan hall.
  • Clan wars / events: organized PvP and minigame nights.
  • Group bosses: Nex, raids, and Corp with a coordinated team.
  • Clan achievements: collective KC and skill milestones.

② Teaching & Content

Many maxed players become teachers — running learner raids, making guides, or streaming. Passing knowledge is its own reward and keeps the game fresh. Community goals (seasonal leagues, holiday events) also reset the "what's the point" feeling annually.

③ Why It Works

Social accountability is the strongest anti-quit force in OSRS. A clan that expects you on Tuesday night will keep you playing long after the XP is gone.

8. ⏱️ Speedrunning & Questing Records

If optimization is your joy, speedrunning is the purest form of it. The quest speedrun community races from Tutorial Island to specific milestones (Quest Cape, "all quests," Fire Cape) as fast as possible.

① Common Speedrun Categories

  • Tutorial Island → Quest Cape: the classic "how fast can you be useful" race.
  • Any% to Fire Cape / Inferno: pure PvM execution speed.
  • League speedruns: seasonal mode records.
  • Skill-specific: fastest 1–99 in a skill (usually with heavy GP).

② Why Maxed Players Love It

You already have the knowledge; speedrunning tests how cleanly you can execute it. It is also endlessly replayable — every run can be a personal best. The Max Account Guide covers the route knowledge you need.

③ Getting Into It

Start by timing your own "first Fire Cape" or "Quest Cape from scratch" on a fresh account. Compare to world records. The gap is humbling and addictive. The speedrunning community shares routes, world-optimized bank presets, and even tick-perfect movement guides, so you are never figuring it out alone. Most speedrunners record every attempt and study the deaths — a single missed click on a low-level monster can cost minutes, and minutes are the entire margin at the top. If you enjoy optimization more than rewards, speedrunning may become your main game after maxing, and it pairs naturally with the Combat Achievements speed tasks you are already chasing.

9. 💰 Money & Wealth Goals

Even a maxed account can chase numbers. GP is a scoreboard, and some players treat "billions earned" as the real endgame.

① Wealth Milestones

  • First billion: a classic flex.
  • Buy every tradeable: owning one of each item is a quirky completion goal.
  • Fund a friend: some maxed players enjoy bankrolling newbies.
  • Flip to a target: mercantile goals via the Grand Exchange.

② High-GP Content

10. 🎽 The Completionist Cape

The Completionist Cape (Comp Cape) is the true "I finished the game" item. It requires maxed stats, the Quest Cape, all Achievement Diaries at least medium (often elite), maxed CAs or near, a huge slice of the Collection Log, and a stack of minigame and reputation requirements.

① What It Demands

Requirement Scope
All 99s + Quest CapeBase entry
Achievement DiariesAll medium (elite for trim)
Collection LogLarge portion of entries
MinigamesBarbarian Assault, Tears of Guthix, etc.
ReputationsKourend, Shayzien, etc.
MiscPlayer-owned house, friendly, etc.

② Why It's the Ultimate Goal

It forces you to touch every part of the game — skilling, bossing, clues, minigames, diaries. If you want one target that justifies doing everything else on this list, the Comp Cape is it. It naturally aggregates your CA, log, diary, and pet progress into a single wearable trophy.

11. 🎲 Minigames & Distractions

Not every goal needs to be epic. OSRS is full of smaller loops that are genuinely fun and feed bigger goals.

① The Useful Minigames

  • Barbarian Assault: Penance pets and a solid CL section.
  • Tears of Guthix: a free XP fountain — slow but free 99s.
  • Guardians of the Rift: fast Runecrafting + a pet.
  • Pest Control / Tempoross / Wintertodt: skillXP and rewards.
  • Soul Wars: quick low-effort XP.

② Why Bother

These are the "palate cleansers" between brutal grinds. They are social, low-stress, and often hand you progress on the Collection Log or a pet without the intensity of bossing. Seasonal content deserves a special mention: Leagues reinvent the game every time with a fresh region lock and a tier of relics that bend the normal rules, and a maxed main's knowledge lets you exploit those relics better than any new player. The Trailblazer, Twisted, and later League formats each became their own mini-endgame for thousands of players. Treat leagues as a once-a-year reset button that makes a maxed account feel brand new again, and you will never run out of things to do.

12. 🏗️ Achievement Diaries & Construction

Achievement Diaries are region-based checklists (easy/medium/hard/elite) that grant useful perks (extra teleports, resource boosts, discounted services). They are a perfect "background goal."

① Diary Perks Worth Having

  • Kandarin: extra charges on the skills necklace.
  • Desert: a free teleport and doubled pyramid plunder rewards.
  • Western: faster gnome gliders and a red chinchompa teleport.
  • Kourend: combat buffs and a handy teleport.

② Construction as an Endgame

Building a maxed Player-Owned House — ornate pool, all portals, a chapel with gilded altar, a superior garden — is a long, expensive project that pays off every single day through faster restoration and teleports. It is quiet, controllable progress, which is exactly what you want between RNG grinds.

13. 🔥 PvP & Wilderness Goals

For some maxed players the Wilderness is the real game. PvP and Wilderness PvM offer risk, reward, and adrenaline that safe content cannot.

① Wilderness Goals

  • PKing: build a risk account and hunt in hotspots.
  • Wilderness bosses: Vet'ion, Venenatis, Callisto, Artio for rare loot.
  • Revenants: high-risk, high-reward PvM.
  • Wilderness Diary: unlocks useful shortcuts and teleports.

② Why Risk Matters

The threat of losing your stack makes every kill meaningful. Maxed players who find safe bossing stale often rediscover the game in the Wilderness, where a single mistake costs real GP.

14. 📊 Account Building (Alts, UIM, HCIM)

The final meta-goal: build more accounts. A maxed main often becomes a "main" in the literal sense — the hub of a fleet.

① Useful Alts

15. ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do first after getting the max cape?

Start the Combat Achievements ladder, beginning with Easy and Medium. It is the most structured content available to a maxed account and improves your bossing skill, which feeds every other goal on this list.

How long does the Completionist Cape take?

For a fresh maxed account with nothing else done, commonly one to three years of casual play, or significantly less if you already grinded pets and the log along the way. Treating it as the aggregate of your other goals is the efficient path.

Are pets worth the grind?

Only if you enjoy the underlying content. Pet hunting multiplies the time you spend on a boss by 5–10x versus normal money farming. If you dislike the boss, the pet will break you. If you love it, the pet is the cherry on top.

Is the Collection Log or Combat Achievements more valuable?

Combat Achievements give permanent gameplay perks (drop rate, bank space) that help every other goal. The Collection Log is prestige-only. Do CAs first for the tangible benefit, then chase the log for completion.

Should a maxed main start an Ironman?

If you miss the sense of earned progression, yes. An Ironman re-introduces scarcity and makes the same content feel new. Our Ironman Beginner Guide is the right place to plan it. Many players keep a maxed main and an Ironman side by side.

What is the fastest meaningful post-max win?

Easy Combat Achievements and the medium Achievement Diaries. Both are reachable in days to weeks, give real perks, and build momentum before you commit to multi-year grinds.

How do I avoid quitting after maxing?

Set one long goal (pets or log) and several short ones (a diary tier, a CA batch, a speedrun PB). Always have a win within a session. Join a clan for social accountability. Variety, not intensity, is what sustains the endgame.

Is PvP worth it for a PvM-focused maxed main?

It is a different skill set and a different thrill. If safe bossing feels stale, the Wilderness and PKing reintroduce stakes. Start small in low-risk worlds and learn the mechanics before risking a real stack.

Can I make money meaningful again at max?

Yes — shift from "need GP" to "scoreboard GP." Billionaire goals, owning one of every item, or flipping on the GE are common. High-tier content (raids, top bosses) also earns far more per hour once you are geared and skilled.

What ties all 15 goals together?

The Completionist Cape. It is the aggregate trophy that requires slices of almost everything else here — CAs, diaries, log, minigames, reps. Many players informally treat "working toward Comp" as the umbrella goal that organizes the other fourteen.
