SEO for OSRS websites is different from normal niches. This guide covers exactly how to rank your OSRS guide site on Google in 2026, including keyword research, on-page SEO, and backlink strategies that actually work.

Why OSRS SEO is Easier in 2026

  • Less competition: Most OSRS sites are outdated (2020-2023 content)
  • High-value keywords: OSRS players search for specific guides (high intent)
  • Google loves fresh content: OSRS updates = fresh content opportunities
  • Low-competition long-tail: "OSRS how to X for beginners" has almost no competition

Step 1: Keyword Research (The Foundation)

Free Tools That Work

  • Google Search Console: See what you already rank for (free)
  • Ubersuggest (free tier): 3 free searches/day
  • AnswerThePublic: Shows what players are asking (free)
  • Google Autosuggest: Type "OSRS how to..." and see suggestions

Step 2: On-Page SEO (Technical Stuff Made Easy)

Title Tags (Most Important)

  • Format: "OSRS [Topic] Guide 2026 - [Benefit]"
  • Example: "OSRS How to Get Dragon Defender 2026 - Step by Step"
  • Length: Keep under 60 characters

Meta Descriptions

  • Format: "[Topic] guide for OSRS. Step-by-step with requirements, gear setup, and GP/hr."
  • Length: 150-160 characters

Header Structure (H1, H2, H3)

  • H1: Only ONE per page (the page title)
  • H2: Main sections (Requirements, Strategy, Loot)
  • H3: Sub-sections within H2

Step 3: Content Strategy (What to Write)

Content That Ranks in 2026

  1. Update old guides: Add "2026" to title + update numbers
  2. Write for beginners: "OSRS how to X for beginners" = low competition
  3. Target update searches: "OSRS [update] guide" = traffic spike
  4. Create comparison pages: "Best X for Y in 2026"

Content Length That Ranks

Content TypeRecommended Length

Comprehensive guide1,500-2,500 words
Category page500-800 words

Step 4: Technical SEO (Site Structure)

Site Speed (PageSpeed Insights)

  • Compress images: Use WebP format (70% smaller)
  • Minify CSS/JS: Remove spaces/comments
  • Use a CDN: Cloudflare is free and fast

Mobile-Friendly (Essential in 2026)

  • Responsive design: Your site must work on phones
  • Font size: Minimum 16px for mobile
  • Button size: Minimum 44x44px (easy to tap)

URL Structure

  • Good: osrsguru.com/guides/osrs-how-to-get-dragon-defender-2026.html
  • Bad: osrsguru.com/p=123

Step 5: Backlink Strategy (Off-Page SEO)

Free Backlinks for OSRS Sites

  1. Reddit: Post guides in r/ironscape, r/OSRS (value-first format)
  2. Twitter/X: Share guides with #OSRS hashtag
  3. YouTube: Make Shorts, link to your site in description
  4. Discord: Share in OSRS Discord servrs (check rules)

What NOT to Do (Black Hat SEO)

  • Don't buy backlinks: Google penalizes this
  • Don't use PBNs: Private blog networks = ban risk
  • Don't copy content: Duplicate content = no rankings

Common SEO Mistakes (2026)

  • Not updating old content: Google prefers fresh content — update annually
  • Ignoring mobile: 60%+ OSRS players use mobile — if your site sucks on mobile, you lose
  • Targeting only high-volume keywords: "OSRS guide" is impossible to rank for — target long-tail instead
  • Not using Google Search Console: It's free and tells you exactly what to fix
