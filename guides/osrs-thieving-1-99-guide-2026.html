1. 💰 Overview & Why Train Thieving in 2026

Thieving is unique: it is one of the only skills where higher levels directly mean more GP per hour, not just faster XP. A maxed Thieving account can print 3–4M GP/hr pickpocketing Elves or Vyres, while a low-level grinder makes almost nothing. That dual nature — speed and money — is why Thieving is a top priority for every Ironman and main alike. In 2026, after the 2024 success-rate buffs and the Varlamore citizen updates, the leveling curve is smoother than ever.

🎯 The Three Reasons to Rush It

  • It pays you back: Unlike most buyable skills, Thieving generates GP at high levels — Ardougne Knights alone can net ~15M over a 55–99 grind.
  • Quest access: 53 Thieving unlocks the Ancient Magicks spellbook; many quests gate behind mid Thieving.
  • Flexible intensity: Pick blackjacking for speed, or Ardougne Knights for a semi-AFK, profitable session.
💡 The One Rule That Wins Thieving

Every high-XP Thieving method lives or dies on the fail/stun rate. Cut fails with the Ardougne Diary, Dodgy necklace, Gloves of silence, and Shadow Veil, and your "attempts per minute" (and therefore XP/hr) roughly doubles. Gear up before you grind.

💡 Diary Beats Gloves

The Ardougne Diary effect does not stack with Gloves of silence, so once you have the Medium (or Hard) diary, the gloves are redundant for success rate. Keep the gloves only if you are pre-diary. The real stack is Diary + Dodgy necklace + Shadow Veil.

4. 🥊 Blackjacking — The Mid-Game Engine

Blackjacking is the click-intensive backbone of any "fastest 99" Thieving route. After The Feud (which opens Pollnivneach), you lure a Bandit or Menaphite Thug into a house, knock them out, pickpocket twice while they are stunned, then repeat. It is the highest XP available from level 45 up to the endgame.

Phase 1 — Bandits (45–65)

From level 45 you can blackjack Bearded Bandits in Pollnivneach. Expect ~100K–150K XP/hr while learning the lure. Bring food (Saradomin brews or cakes) to extend trips.

Phase 2 — Menaphite Thugs (65–91)

At 65 switch to Menaphite Thugs for ~200K–260K XP/hr. This is where most of your 99 comes from on the fast route — roughly 30–35 hours of focused clicking.

Phase 3 — Lure & reset

Use the house geometry to keep the NPC trapped, knock out on the correct game tick, and double-pickpocket. Mistimed knocks waste the stun — practice the timing before committing.

💡 Blackjacking Has No Profit

Blackjacking itself earns almost no GP — you are buying XP with clicks, not with money. If you want profit alongside speed, the Ardougne Knights route is the better trade at the cost of a little XP/hr.

5. 🔺 Pyramid Plunder — Endgame XP

From level 91, Pyramid Plunder in Sophanem becomes the top raw XP method (up to ~270K XP/hr). You need Icthlarin's Little Helper to enter the city. It is less click-intensive than blackjacking and loops nicely with a Pharaoh's Sceptre (teleports straight to the pyramid).

Step 1 — Enter & rush the rooms

You have a 5-minute timer. Skip the early rooms and head for rooms 7–8, looting the golden chest and urns. Use stamina potions and antipoison for traps.

Step 2 — Bank artefacts

Banking gems lowers your XP to ~240K/hr but can net ~300K GP/hr from artefact value. Decide based on whether you need GP or just levels.

Step 3 — Reset with the Sceptre

Recharge the Pharaoh's Sceptre at the obelisk and teleport back. A tight loop is ~15–20 hours from 91 to 99.

💡 91 Is the Gate

Pyramid Plunder XP scales hard with level — ~200K/hr at 91, climbing toward 250K–270K at 99. Don't rush into it before 91; blackjacking is faster below that.

8. ⚠️ Common Mistakes

❌ Mistake 1: "I'll just pickpocket without the diary."

Big loss. The Ardougne Diary's +10% success roughly doubles your attempts per minute. Do the Medium (and ideally Hard) diary before any serious pickpocketing grind.

❌ Mistake 2: "Stand anywhere at the stalls."

Stalls have a guard line of sight. Stand where the nearby guard can't see you, or you get stunned constantly and your rate collapses.

❌ Mistake 3: "Blackjacking without food."

You take damage from failed knocks. Bring Saradomin brews or cakes (or the Thieving Guild's healing) to extend trips — banking mid-grind kills your XP/hr.

❌ Mistake 4: "No Dodgy necklace / Shadow Veil."

These cut your stun time dramatically and stack. Running without them is leaving free XP on the table on every fail.

❌ Mistake 5: "Pyramid Plunder before 91."

Below 91 the room rewards and XP are weak; blackjacking is faster. Hit 91 first, then switch.

9. ❓ FAQ

What is the fastest way to 99 Thieving?

The fastest published route is Men/Women → stalls → Blackjacking Menaphite Thugs (260K XP/hr) → Rogues' Castle chests or Pyramid Plunder (270K+ XP/hr), totalling roughly 50–60 hours.

Can I make money with Thieving?

Yes — it is the best moneymaking skill. Ardougne Knights can net ~15M GP over 55–99; endgame Vyres and Elves earn 3–4M GP/hr. You need the Hard Diary (Knights) or quest unlocks (Vyres/Elves).

Do I need the Ardougne Diary?

Strongly recommended. The Medium gives +10% success in Ardougne (Knights); the Hard gives +10% everywhere and, combined with level 94+, means you never fail on Ardougne Knights. It is the single biggest Thieving upgrade.

What level do I need for Pyramid Plunder?

You can enter at level 21, but it only becomes the top XP method at level 91. Below that, blackjacking is faster.

Is Thieving worth rushing on a new account?

Very much so — 53 Thieving unlocks Ancient Magicks, and a profitable Knights grind funds your other skills. Most players treat 55–99 Thieving as a money printer, not just a level.

10. 🏁 Final Tips & 1-99 Optimal Path

  • Fastest path: 1–5 Men/Women → 5–25 Bakery stall → 25–50 Fruit stalls → 50–55 Wealthy citizens → 55–65 Ardy Knights → 65–91 Blackjacking Thugs → 91–99 Pyramid Plunder (~50–60h).
  • Profitable path: Get the Hard Ardougne Diary, then Ardougne Knights 55–99 in a Rogues outfit — semi-AFK and up to ~15M GP.
  • Gear first: Diary + Dodgy necklace + Rogues outfit before you grind; Gloves of silence only if pre-diary.
  • Food & pots: brews/cakes for blackjacking; stamina + antipoison for Pyramid Plunder.
  • Shadow Veil: if you have 47 Magic + A Kingdom Divided, the +15% no-stun stacks with Dodgy necklace for a real boost.
  • Next skill: pair Thieving XP with Crafting (bank the gems you steal) and fund Mining or Magic with the profit.
🤝 You've Got the Pickpocket Down

The lesson — stack every small success-rate bonus you can — applies to almost every grinder. Build the habit of "gear up before you grind" and every skill gets faster.
