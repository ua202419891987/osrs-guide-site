OSRS Mad Angel Boss Guide 2026 — Mechanics, Gear & Hallowfell Drop

Published September 12, 2026  |  14 min read  |  By Yanchun Wu

The Mad Angel is the golem boss waiting at the top of Ardeaglais cathedral on Wyrmscraig, and she is the best mid-game teacher in the game right now. She has only three special attacks, but each one punishes the wrong reaction: you dodge the cleave sideways for a guaranteed max hit, you stand on the shadow to bounce the light back at her, and you prayer-flick the smite on the exact tick. This guide covers the Fallen From Grace unlock, the golembane setup, every mechanic tick-by-tick, the 350 HP enrage phase, and the Hallowfell farm that makes her worth revisiting.

⚔️ Level: 75+ Attack / 70 Prayer
📅 Stage 2.1 — Part of the Wyrmscraig Boss Series · 14 min read · Published September 2026

⏱️ Quick Summary — 30-Second Read

  • 📌 Unlock: finish Fallen From Grace on Wyrmscraig, then return to Ardeaglais for the repeatable fight
  • 📌 Style: melee with a golembane weapon — a granite hammer outperforms far more expensive gear here
  • 📌 Cleave: dodge to her side (not behind) to bank a guaranteed max hit on your next attack
  • 📌 Light ball: stand on the shadow tile to bounce it back — 18–22 damage to her, three times per cast
  • 📌 Smite: Protect from Magic flicked on the right tick fully negates it and guarantees your next max hit
  • 📌 Enrage at 350 HP (~45%) — she opens with all three specials back to back, so stay high HP

1. 🗡️ Why the Mad Angel Is the Best Mid-Game Boss of 2026

Most mid-game bosses ask for gear. The Mad Angel asks for reactions. She arrived with the Wyrmscraig update and sits in Ardeaglais, the cathedral on the island's southern side. Jagex positioned her as the natural step after a Fire Cape: a fight you can start with a modest melee setup, but one where sloppy prayer switches and lazy positioning get you killed.

That design is exactly why she is worth your time. Every one of her three specials has a correct answer that rewards you instead of merely sparing you. Dodge the cleave to her side and your next hit is 100% accurate with a raised minimum. Bounce her light ball and you deal 18–22 damage to her per bounce. Time the smite prayer perfectly and your next attack is a guaranteed maximum hit. In other words, good mechanics here translate directly into faster kills — which is the opposite of how most entry bosses feel.

💡 Why This Matters More in 2026

With Sailing content pulling players toward Wyrmscraig anyway, the Mad Angel is the most efficient way to convert a Sailing trip into real PvM practice. If you have been putting off learning prayer flicking and tile-based dodging, she is a far gentler classroom than raids or the deep endgame bosses.

There is also a genuine payout at the end of the learning curve. Her unique drop, the two-handed sword Hallowfell, is the strongest melee weapon in the game against three stacked targets — which makes her farm valuable long after you stop needing the practice.

2. 🔓 Unlock Requirements & Getting to Ardeaglais

You cannot simply walk up and fight her. The repeatable version unlocks only after you complete the Wyrmscraig questline quest Fallen From Grace. During that quest you fight a deliberately weakened version of the golem; finishing it opens the full encounter at the cathedral.

📜 Quest Requirements for Fallen From Grace

  • 62 Sailing
  • 60 Crafting
  • 47 Runecraft
  • 53 Mining

Because Sailing is required, treat this boss as a natural reward for pushing your Sailing level rather than a detour. If you are still climbing toward 62, the fastest path is laid out in our Sailing 1–99 guide.

Getting There Fast

🧭 Three Reliable Routes

  • Ardeaglais teleport — the fastest option once you own it. It drops you at the boss, which is why it is also a common drop from her.
  • Slayer ring — teleports to Wyrmscraig Cavern. Interact with the golem to the north and climb the stairs, or exit west and run to the cathedral.
  • Necklace of Passage — run west and use the agility shortcut to the lair's front door. The shortcut needs 54 Agility and you must first mine the rock blocking its end.

💡 Bank Early, Bank Often

The cathedral is a short run from a bank route but not instant. Set your respawn close, keep a teleport tablet in the bank, and do not carry your entire cash stack into a fight you are still learning. The fight is cheap to repeat — deaths do not have to be.

3. 🛡️ Gear & Setup: Golembane Weapons and Prayer Minimums

The Mad Angel's single most important property is her golembane weakness. Any golembane weapon gets a large accuracy and damage boost against her, which means the humble granite hammer is genuinely competitive with gear costing hundreds of millions. Do not skip this — it is the difference between a smooth fight and a war of attrition.

⚙️ Recommended Setup

  • Attack: 75+ recommended. Below that, accuracy suffers badly even with golembane.
  • Prayer: 43 minimum for Protect from Melee and Protect from Magic. 70 Prayer for Piety is a large upgrade if you have it.
  • Defence: 70 recommended for comfortable enrage survival.
  • Weapon: granite hammer (budget and excellent), or any high-tier crush weapon you already own.
  • Food: your best available food plus one emergency combo. She hits up to 31 with melee before prayer.
  • Potions: super combat or divine super combat, plus enough prayer restoration for a full trip.
  • Optional: thralls for passive damage and a Book of the Dead if you are on the Arceuus spellbook.

Because accuracy is effectively boosted whenever you dodge a cleave perfectly and max hits are guaranteed after a perfect smite, lower-tier weapons perform far better here than their price suggests. At lower levels it is genuinely worthwhile to swap between two crush weapons to make the most of those guaranteed-hit windows rather than camping one expensive weapon.

💡 Prayer Is Your Real Armour

Protect from Melee cuts her standard attack from a 31 max hit down to 5. Protect from Magic halves the light-ball explosion and the smite. This fight is won by keeping a prayer overhead at all times and switching on cue — not by stacking defence bonuses.

4. ⚡ The Three Special Attacks Explained

She attacks with melee by default and performs a special after every three standard attacks. There are only three specials, and each has a distinct visual tell. Learn the tells and the fight becomes a rhythm game.

① Sword Cleave — Dodge to Her Side, Not Behind

She stands still and draws her sword back to either her left or her right. After a few seconds she cleaves the area toward where you were standing, plus the side she drew from. She repeats the cleave four times in the normal phase and six times during enrage.

You can dodge by running behind her, but that gives you nothing extra. The real answer is to run to her side opposite the sword. If you dodge to the side successfully, your next attack is 100% accurate with its minimum hit raised to 50% of your maximum. That is a huge, free damage window — save a special attack for it.

💡 Do Not Let Her Hug a Wall

If she performs a cleave while backed against a wall, the tiles you need to dodge onto may be unreachable. You can still earn the buff by clicking inside her 3x3 area on the middle tile toward the successful dodge direction — but it is cleaner to fight her in open space. Pull her away from walls before the cleave lands.

② Exploding Light — Stand ON the Shadow

She launches a ball of light toward a destination tile, marked by a looming shadow on the floor. If it reaches that tile it explodes and deals 30+ damage to the entire arena. The counter-intuitive answer: stand on the shadow tile. Doing so bounces the ball back at her for 18–22 damage, and she bounces it three times per cast if you keep catching it.

This is the mechanic that trips up players from other bosses, where a shadow on the floor means "move." Here it means "move onto it." She keeps attacking normally throughout, so keep your Protect from Melee up while you reposition.

③ Lightning Smite — Prayer Flick on the Tick

She draws her sword to her chest and charges a lightning attack. Simply holding Protect from Magic reduces the damage by roughly half, but the correct play is to flick the prayer on the exact tick the lightning strikes — which fully negates the damage and guarantees a maximum hit on your next attack.

Two reliable cues for the timing: activate the prayer about 4 ticks (2.4 seconds) after the charge begins, when the blue crackling first becomes visible, or react when her wings flap slightly after being fully extended. During enrage she performs three strikes in a row — the second lands 4 ticks after the first and the third just 2 ticks after that, so you need a rhythm rather than a single click.

💡 The Low-Effort Alternative

If precise timing is not clicking yet, 1-tick prayer flicking Protect from Magic for the duration of the animation works for both the single strike and the enrage triple. It is far more click-intensive but does not require you to hit an exact tick, which makes it a solid crutch while you learn the visual cues.

5. 🔥 The Enrage Phase at 350 HP: What Actually Changes

When she drops to 350 Hitpoints (roughly 45%), she enters the enrage phase at the next available opportunity. The tell is a "...!" message overhead. Everything you learned still applies — it just arrives faster and in a fixed opening sequence.

⚠️ What Changes in Enrage

  • Special attacks are performed more quickly, shrinking your reaction window.
  • She opens the phase by using all three specials consecutively — be ready immediately.
  • Sword cleave repeats six times instead of four.
  • Lightning smite becomes a three-strike sequence with a 4-tick then 2-tick gap.

The single most common cause of a failed enrage is entering it at low HP. Eat before she crosses 350, not after — the opening triple-special leaves you no safe window to heal if you are already sitting at 40%. At the same time, do not panic-eat after every small hit: eating costs attack ticks, and the movement-based mechanics give you natural gaps to heal without sacrificing much damage.

💡 Enrage Is Where the Achievements Live

Several of the Mad Angel Combat Achievement tasks require you to land consecutive enhanced attacks during enrage. If you want those, treat the enrage phase as the main event rather than something to survive — see our dedicated Mad Angel Combat Achievements guide for the full task list and the cleanest ways to earn them.

6. 💰 Loot Table: Hallowfell, Granite Dust and Aggy

The Mad Angel is a mid-level boss, so her table is not a jackpot — but it contains one genuinely best-in-slot item and a very useful consumable, which is more than most entry bosses can say.

🎁 Notable Drops

  • Hallowfell — the headline unique. A two-handed sword requiring 75 Attack that cleaves up to two additional enemies in a front-facing cone for 50% of your max hit. Against three targets it is the strongest melee weapon in the game.
  • Granite dust — used to make granite cannonballs for your dwarf cannon. A steady, sellable supply.
  • Jar of Light — a collection-log cosmetic jar.
  • Aggy — her pet, and typically the first entry players tick off in her collection log.
  • Ardeaglais teleport — a direct teleport back to the boss, which dramatically speeds up your kill volume once you have one.
  • Standard supplies — rune kiteshield, rune full helm and assorted runes to keep the trip profitable.

Hallowfell is the reason to keep coming back. Its cleave makes it the weapon of choice for multi-target Slayer tasks and any content where enemies stack in front of you. Once you own one, it also becomes the natural weapon to use at the Mad Angel herself, since the fight rewards accuracy windows rather than raw max hit.

💡 One Known Bug to Watch

Players have reported that graves and death piles can occasionally appear at the Wyrmscraig dock or another dock instead of the boss lobby. If you die, check your world map before assuming your items are at the cathedral — running to the wrong dock wastes a trip.

7. 🧠 9 Tips, Tricks & Common Mistakes

✅ Do This

  • Bring a golembane weapon. The weakness is large enough that it beats much more expensive alternatives.
  • Pull her away from walls before the cleave so your dodge tiles are always reachable.
  • Save a special attack for a perfect side-dodge — the guaranteed accuracy and raised minimum make it hit far harder than a random spec.
  • Keep Protect from Melee up at all times and only swap for the smite — the standard attack drops to a 5 max hit under prayer.
  • Eat before she hits 350 HP so you enter enrage at high health.
  • Practise 1-tick Protect from Magic flicking as a fallback for the triple-strike smite.
  • Use the Ardeaglais teleport once you have one to cut travel time between kills.

❌ Avoid This

  • Running away from the shadow tile — you are supposed to stand on it. Fleeing just eats a 30+ arena-wide explosion.
  • Dodging the cleave by hiding behind her every time — you survive, but you forfeit the free max-hit window.
  • Panic-eating through enrage — it costs attack ticks and drags the fight out, which increases the number of specials you have to handle.
  • Attempting it below 75 Attack — accuracy is the bottleneck, and golembane cannot fully compensate for a weak weapon tier.

💡 The Fastest Way to Learn

Do not try to learn every mechanic at once. Spend your first two or three kills ignoring damage and just watching the tells: sword position for the cleave, shadow tile for the light ball, and the wing flap for the smite. Once you can name the attack before it lands, add damage optimisation.

8. ❓ Mad Angel Boss FAQs

Q: How do I unlock the Mad Angel?

Complete the Fallen From Grace quest on Wyrmscraig, then return to Ardeaglais cathedral. The quest features a weakened version of the fight; the repeatable version becomes available afterwards.

Q: What levels do I need for Fallen From Grace?

62 Sailing, 60 Crafting, 47 Runecraft and 53 Mining.

Q: What combat stats should I have for the Mad Angel?

Around 75 Attack is the practical minimum, with 43 Prayer for Protect from Melee and Protect from Magic. 70 Prayer for Piety and 70 Defence make the enrage phase much more forgiving.

Q: What is the Mad Angel weak to?

Golembane weapons. A granite hammer is the standard budget answer and performs well above its price because of the weakness.

Q: How do I dodge the sword cleave?

Run to her side opposite the sword she drew back, or behind her. Dodging to the side is strictly better because it grants 100% accuracy and raises your next attack's minimum hit to 50% of your max.

Q: What do I do with the exploding light ball?

Stand on the shadow tile it is targeting. The ball bounces back and deals 18–22 damage to her, and she can be bounced three times per cast.

Q: When does the Mad Angel enrage?

At 350 Hitpoints, roughly 45% of her health. She signals it with a "...!" message overhead and opens the phase by using all three special attacks consecutively.

Q: What is the Mad Angel's main drop?

Hallowfell, a two-handed sword requiring 75 Attack that cleaves up to two extra enemies in front of you for 50% of your max hit. She also drops granite dust, a Jar of Light, the Aggy pet and Ardeaglais teleports.

Q: Is the Mad Angel worth farming for money?

She is a mid-level boss, so her hourly GP is modest. She is worth farming for the Hallowfell unique and the granite dust, and she is an excellent place to build the mechanics you will need at harder bosses.

Q: Can I fight the Mad Angel on a Slayer task?

No. She is a standalone boss encounter rather than a Slayer monster, so bring your best general-purpose melee setup instead of a task-specific one.
