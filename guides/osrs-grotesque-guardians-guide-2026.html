1. Unlock & Access (75 Construction)

The Grotesque Guardians — the twin sentinels Dusk and Dawn — are fought at the very top of the Slayer Tower, inside the chamber known as the Tower of Voices. Unlike most early-to-mid boss content, this fight is gated behind a hard skill requirement instead of a quest. To so much as step into the arena you need 75 Construction to rebuild the collapsed roof, and you must be on a Grotesque Guardian Slayer task (which itself requires 75 Slayer). This combination is what keeps the boss comfortably in the intermediate bracket: you are expected to already understand prayer flicking, gear switches, and basic PvM awareness before you ever arrive.

The roof repair is a one-time investment. When you first climb to the top floor you will find the chamber open to the sky with rubble blocking the actual boss platform. Speak to the roof and, provided your Construction is at least 75, you will be prompted to rebuild it. The materials are modest — you supply 25 noted Limestone bricks (or pay a small coin fee if you prefer), which costs only a few thousand gold from the Grand Exchange. Once the roof is back in place the boss is permanently available; you never pay again. Many players treat this as a milestone unlock, similar to building a ornate fountain or a gilded altar, because it signals you now have access to one of the most consistent mid-tier money-makers in the game.

🎯 Exact Requirements Checklist

  • 75 Construction — to repair the Tower of Voices roof (one-time, ~25 Limestone bricks).
  • 75 Slayer — the minimum level to be assigned Grotesque Guardians.
  • An active Grotesque Guardian task — assigned by Konar, Nieve, Steve, Duradel, or theaccordingly higher-tier masters.
  • Combat stats — realistically 70+ Attack/Strength/Defence and 70+ Prayer for comfortable trips.
  • Access to the Slayer Tower — teleport via Slayer ring, fairy ring (code CKS), or the Slayer Tower teleport.

Getting there is straightforward. The Slayer Tower sits west of Canifis in Morytania. From the ground floor you climb the stairs; if you have 60 Agility there is a shortcut crevice on the outside of the tower that drops you near the top, saving a climb. Inside, ascend to the top floor and walk to the cracked roof section. If you are not on a Grotesque Guardian task, the roof will not let you through and you will simply be told you lack the assignment — so always double-check your task before making the trip. A common mistake for first-timers is showing up on a regular gargoyle task; gargoyles and Grotesque Guardians are related lore-wise, but the boss only activates during the dedicated guardian assignment.

💡 Tip: Use a Slayer Ring

Enchant a ring of the elements into a Slayer ring (or buy one from the points store) and teleport directly to the Slayer Tower. It saves far more time over a long session than running from Canifis, and the teleport lands you right at the door.

Before you commit to the boss, make sure your account is actually ready for sustained PvM. If your combat fundamentals are shaky — you are not comfortable with protection prayers, you panic-switch, or you are unsure which weapon is best for your level — it is worth brushing up first. Our Combat Training Beginner Guide covers the attack styles, weapon choices, and accuracy math you will rely on here, and the New Player Boss Survival Guide explains the positioning and prayer habits that keep intermediate players alive at every mid-game boss.

2. Dusk Mechanics

Dusk is the larger of the two guardians and the one you will spend most of the fight meleeing. He is a slow, heavy stone construct that hits with powerful melee attacks and has a very high defence against slashing and stabbing weapons. His single most important property is his weakness to Crush: bring a crush weapon or you will watch your hitsplat bounce for single digits while he steadily wears you down. Players who arrive with a whip or a rapier consistently report miserable kill times precisely because those styles are resisted; the fight only becomes smooth once you switch to a proper crush setup.

Dusk's base attack is a standard melee swipe that can be fully blocked by Protect from Melee. The danger comes from his two special attacks. The first is a Rock Throw: Dusk hurls a boulder in a straight line across the arena. A red telegraph tile shows where it will land; if you are standing on that tile when it connects you take a large hit (often 20+ and capable of comboing into a quick death if your health is low). The counter is simple — step off the highlighted tile the moment it appears. The second special is a Ground Stomp shockwave: Dusk slams the floor and a ring of damage expands outward. You avoid it by either standing adjacent to him (inside the safe ring) or by hopping one tile away as the animation plays. Both specials are telegraphed well in advance, which is why the fight rewards calm positioning over reaction speed.

🎯 Dusk's Attack Patterns

  • Melee swipe — blocked by Protect from Melee; the bread-and-butter damage source.
  • Rock Throw — red line/tile telegraph; step off it to avoid a heavy hit.
  • Ground Stomp — expanding shockwave; stay tight to him or step away on the animation.
  • Statue Harden — periodically turns to stone and takes reduced damage until hit with crush again.

A subtle but crucial mechanic is Dusk's enrage. While Dawn is alive, Dusk fights at his normal pace. The instant Dawn dies, Dusk lets out a roar and becomes enraged: his attacks speed up, his Rock Throws come more frequently, and his max hit climbs noticeably. This is the core reason the "separate kill" strategy works so well — you deliberately remove Dawn first so that when Dusk enrages he is the only threat left, letting you simplify your prayer and positioning into a clean one-on-one. Fighting both at once is far more punishing because you must juggle Dawn's ranged/magic barrages while also dodging Dusk's stomps.

There is also a defensive quirk worth knowing: Dusk will occasionally harden into a statue, becoming briefly immune to non-crush damage and greatly reducing all incoming hits. If you are on a crush weapon this barely matters, but if you tried to range him during a switch you would waste the window. Keep your crush weapon equipped and you will break him out of the statue form almost instantly. In practice, intermediate players should simply stay on crush the entire Dusk phase and never bother ranging him — the boss is a melee duel, not a ranging encounter.

💡 Tip: Pre-aim the Stomp Safe Tile

Because the Ground Stomp is a ring, the single safest tile is the one immediately next to Dusk. If you fight him with melee you are already standing there, so the stomp often misses you entirely. Meleeing Dusk is not just optimal for DPS — it is also the safest position for his shockwave.

Defensively, Dusk's enraged melee can hit in the high 20s to low 30s. With 70+ Defence and good food (sharks or better), you will easily out-sustain his single-target melee while Protect from Melee is active. The only real damage spikes are his specials, and both are avoidable with awareness. This is why the boss is rated intermediate rather than hard — the ceiling is low once you internalize two telegraphs.

3. Dawn Mechanics

Dawn is the smaller, faster guardian and the more annoying of the pair because she refuses to stay on the ground. She spends most of the fight airborne, attacking with a mix of Ranged and Magic projectiles that you block with the appropriate protection prayer. Her defining weakness is Thrown weapons, with Stab also performing well because she is off the ground and harder for slashing weapons to connect with. The practical takeaway: if you intend to damage her efficiently you want dragon knives, a dragon thrownaxe, or a strong stab weapon rather than a scimitar or whip.

Dawn's standard attacks are ranged and magic bolts that are fully prevented by Protect from Ranged and Protect from Magic respectively. The trick is that she swaps styles, so you must watch her animation: a glowing ranged shot means switch to Ranged prayer, a shimmering magical orb means switch to Magic prayer. Good players flick between the two protections to fully negate her damage, which is the skill that separates a clean Dawn kill from a food-draining one. If you are not confident flicking yet, the Prayer Training Beginner Guide walks through the timing and the "one-tick prayer swap" habit that makes bosses like this survivable.

🎯 Dawn's Attack Patterns

  • Ranged shot — blocked by Protect from Ranged; telegraphed by a bow-like animation.
  • Magic orb — blocked by Protect from Magic; telegraphed by a glowing cast.
  • Dive Bomb — she flies up, a shadow appears on your tile, step away or take a big hit and knockback.
  • Wind Blast AoE — a short-range cone of wind; keep distance or sidestep.

Dawn's signature special is the Dive Bomb. She ascends, a dark shadow appears on the tile you are currently standing on, and after a brief delay she slams down there. Getting caught deals heavy damage and knocks you back a tile, which can throw off yourPrayer timing and even push you into Dusk's stomp radius. The fix is the same as Dusk's Rock Throw: move one tile the instant you see the shadow. Because Dawn's dive targets your current position, simply walking away negates it completely. A useful habit is to keep moving in a small circle during the Dawn phase so the shadow almost never lands where you end up.

Her second special is a Wind Blast: Dawn beats her wings and unleashes a cone of cutting wind. This is most dangerous when you are meleeing Dusk and Dawn happens to be lined up behind you, because the cone can clip you while you are busy dodging Dusk. The tell is a wind-up animation where she rears back; sidestep perpendicular to her facing and the blast whiffs. Neither of Dawn's specials is lethal on its own, but combined with Dusk they create the chaos that makes fighting both simultaneously risky — another reason to isolate Dawn and delete her early.

💡 Tip: Keep Dawn in Your Camera

Because both guardians attack from different angles, keep your camera angled so you can see Dawn's dive shadow without losing sight of Dusk. Most deaths at this boss happen when a player tunnels on Dusk's stomp and eats a dive bomb they never saw land.

When Dawn drops below roughly half health she becomes more aggressive, diving more often and swapping ranged/magic faster. This is the window where flicking matters most. If you have burst her down with thrown weapons you will usually end the Dawn phase before she enrages Dusk too hard, which keeps the whole encounter controlled. Remember: Dawn dying triggers Dusk's enrage, so the moment her health bar empties, immediately re-centre your attention on Dusk and swap to your crush melee setup if you were ranging her.

4. Recommended Gear & Supplies

Gear for the Grotesque Guardians is built around two truths: Dusk wants crush, Dawn wants thrown/stab. The cleanest intermediate setup therefore carries a strong crush melee weapon as your primary and a thrown weapon as your Dawn burst tool, swapping as the fight progresses. You do not need best-in-slot gear to be profitable here — a mid-tier void or blessed setup with a decent crush weapon already clears kills comfortably — but the right weapon choice matters far more than raw stats.

For the Dusk phase, the gold-standard crush options are the Bandos godsword, Osmuten's fang, or the Inquisitor's mace (the latter pairs with the Inquisitor's armour for a sizeable crush bonus). Budget players do fine with a Saradomin sword or even a Leaf-bladed battleaxe (which is also a stab option for Dawn). On the Dawn side, dragon knives (e) or a dragon thrownaxe deliver excellent DPS for the short burst you need; alternatively a strong stab weapon like a Zamorakian hasta or Abyssal dagger works if you prefer to stay melee.

🎯 Sample Intermediate Loadout

  • Head — Slayer helmet (i) or Neitiznot faceguard (crush bonus).
  • Body — Bandos chestplate or Obsidian platebody (with castle wars brace).
  • Legs — Bandos tassets or Torag's / Verac's plateskirt.
  • Weapon (Dusk) — Bandos godsword / Osmuten's fang (crush).
  • Weapon (Dawn) — Dragon knives (e) or dragon thrownaxe (thrown).
  • Prayer — Holy wrap or Proselyte for prayer bonus if flicking.

Your inventory should balance sustain and profit. Bring sharks or better (anglerfish are ideal for the higher heal), a super combat potion (or divine super combat) for the Dusk melee phase, a ranged potion if you burst Dawn with knives, and a prayer potion or two plus your best prayer restoration. Leave room for a teleport, a few antipoison (not strictly needed here but cheap insurance), and the rest as food. Because the boss has no poison and no overhead-damage mechanics beyond the two specials, you do not need special anti-venom or extended antifire — keep the loadout simple.

💡 Tip: One Inventory Template

A reliable loadout is: 1 teleport, 1 super combat, 1 ranged potion, 2 prayer potions, and the remaining 19 slots as anglerfish. That gives you a long, forgiving trip and keeps your profit-per-hour high because you rarely bank.

Do not underestimate the Slayer helmet (i) here. Because you are on a guardian task, the imbued slayer helmet gives a noticeable damage and accuracy boost against both bosses, which shortens every kill and lowers your food use. If you have not yet made one, the Combat Training Beginner Guide and the New Player Boss Survival Guide both cover how task bonuses and defensive gear choices compound into faster, safer kills. Prayer management deserves a separate mention: flicking Dawn and protecting Dusk drains prayer steadily, so a ring of the gods (i) or Proselyte armour stretches your potions and lets you stay longer per trip.

5. Kill Strategy — Separate Kill Plan

The defining strategic choice at the Grotesque Guardians is whether to fight both bosses at once or to separate them and kill one at a time. For intermediate players the separate-kill plan is strongly recommended because it removes the chaotic overlap of Dawn's barrages and Dusk's stomps. The plan is simple in concept: burst Dawn down first with your thrown weapon while flicking her ranged/magic, then turn and finish the now-enraged but isolated Dusk with crush melee. You trade a short, predictable Dawn phase for a longer but much safer Dusk duel.

Step one on entering the arena: turn on Protect from Ranged (Dawn usually opens with a ranged shot) and immediately start bursting Dawn with dragon knives. Keep moving in a small circle so her Dive Bomb shadow never lands on you, and flick to Protect from Magic the moment you see her cast change. Because Dawn is airborne and weak to thrown, your knives delete her health fast — typically within a few accurate volleys. Ignore Dusk entirely during this phase except to avoid his Rock Throw telegraph if it lines up with your path; you are not meleeing him yet, so his melee cannot reach you while you are at knife range.

🎯 Step-by-Step Separate Kill

  • Enter — Protect from Ranged up, position mid-range from Dawn.
  • Phase 1 (Dawn) — burst with thrown weapons, circle to dodge dives, flick Ranged/Magic.
  • Kill Dawn — the moment she dies, Dusk enrages; switch to crush melee.
  • Phase 2 (Dusk) — Protect from Melee, stand adjacent, dodge Rock Throw + Stomp.
  • Finish — crush him down; collect drops; rebank or continue the task.

Step two begins the instant Dawn's health bar empties. Dusk enrages — his attacks speed up — but he is now the only threat. Swap to your crush weapon, walk straight up to him, and switch your prayer to Protect from Melee. Because you are standing adjacent, his Ground Stomp ring misses you, and his Rock Throw is the only thing to watch: when the red tile appears, step off it and step back. With a super combat potion active and crush gear on, Dusk dies quickly, and the enrage actually works in your favour because an aggressive boss dies faster than a passive one once you are safely praying against him.

The alternative — fighting both at once — is viable for advanced players who want to race the clock, but it demands near-constant prayer switching between Melee (Dusk), Ranged (Dawn), and Magic (Dawn) while dodging two specials from two directions. For an intermediate session focused on steady profit, the separate-kill plan is strictly better: it cuts food costs, reduces deaths, and makes each kill a repeatable routine you can run for an hour without mental fatigue. If you are still building that confidence, our New Player Boss Survival Guide drills the exact prayer-flick and positioning routines this fight rewards.

💡 Tip: Pre-set Your Weapon Swap

Bind your crush weapon and your thrown weapon to adjacent action-bar slots (or your function keys) so the Dawn-to-Dusk switch is instant. The one or two ticks you save at the enrage moment keep Dusk's first enraged hit from landing before your prayer is up.

One more routine worth adopting: between kills, re-pot and re-prayer at the bank or a nearby altar rather than pushing a half-empty inventory. The guardian task usually grants several kills, and the Slayer Tower's closeness to a bank (or a Player-owned-house altar if you teleport out and back) means topping up is cheap. Players who stretch a degraded inventory tend to die on the last kill of a trip and lose the whole haul — a mistake that turns a profitable hour into a net loss. Keep trips clean, keep food topped, and the separate-kill plan will carry you through an entire task with minimal risk.

6. Profit & Drops

The Grotesque Guardians are a genuinely strong mid-game money-maker, and their appeal is the combination of a steady alchable floor with a lottery-style big drop. The headline unique is the Granite maul, a powerful crush weapon that sells for several million gold and appears often enough to keep trips exciting. Beyond that, the bosses drop a stack of Granite body and Granite legs pieces, various seeds, and the rare Black tourmaline core, a valuable material used in high-end crafting that commands a solid price on the Grand Exchange. Even on a dry streak with no uniques, the alchables alone usually cover your supplies and pay for the limestone you burned on the roof.

A typical intermediate runner achieves roughly 20 to 30 kills per hour using the separate-kill plan, depending on gear and how cleanly they flick. The guaranteed alchable value per kill — granite bodies, bolts, herbs, and seeds — lands in the low-to-mid tens of thousands of gold, which scales to a reliable 300k to 700k gp per hour floor. On top of that sits the chance at a Granite maul (the big-ticket unique) and the rare Black tourmaline core, which can swing an otherwise average hour into a multi-million-gp session. Because the boss is low-risk once learned, the effective hourly is among the best for the 75-Slayer / 75-Construction bracket.

🎯 What You Can Expect to Farm

  • Granite maul — the headline unique; high-value crush weapon.
  • Granite body / legs — frequent alchables and slayer-friendly gear.
  • Black tourmaline core — rare, valuable material for advanced crafting.
  • Seeds & herbs — consistent low-value drops that add up per trip.
  • Coins & alchables — the dependable floor that pays your costs.

To maximise profit you should alch the granite drops in place (or note them with a rune pouch + magic notepaper if you prefer to bank and sell in bulk) and keep your trips long by minimising deaths. The single biggest profit leak at this boss is dying — a single death can cost you a Granite maul drop and a full inventory of loot, wiping out hours of gains. Treat every kill as if it could drop the maul, keep your health topped, and never greed a kill on low food. If you want a broader picture of how this boss compares to other mid-tier money makers, the New Player Boss Survival Guide frames the risk-versus-reward mindset that keeps your net worth climbing.

💡 Tip: Track Your Dry Streaks

The Granite maul is rare, not guaranteed. Log your kills so you know your odds are eventually in your favour — chasing a unique after a bad streak is fine, but set a budget so a dry spell never pushes you into risky, food-starved kills that cost more than the drop is worth.

Finally, remember that the guardian task is finite — once your Slayer assignment ends, the boss stops spawning until your next task. Plan your profit sessions around a fresh task, block or skip unwanted assignments to keep guardians coming, and use the Combat Training Beginner Guide to keep your stats climbing so future tasks go even faster. With the roof repaired, the gear sorted, and the separate-kill routine drilled, the Grotesque Guardians become one of the most relaxing and rewarding boss loops available to an intermediate account in 2026.

7. Grotesque Guardians FAQs

Do I really need 75 Construction, or just 75 Slayer?

You need both. The 75 Slayer lets you be assigned the Grotesque Guardian task, but without 75 Construction you cannot repair the Tower of Voices roof and the boss arena stays unreachable. The Construction requirement is a one-time gate — repair it once with about 25 Limestone bricks and the roof is permanently fixed for all future tasks.

Why is my damage so low on Dusk?

Dusk is heavily resistant to slash and stab and is weak to Crush. If you show up with a whip, scimitar, or rapier you will see tiny hitsplats and painfully long kills. Switch to a crush weapon such as a Bandos godsword, Osmuten's fang, or Saradomin sword and your damage will jump dramatically. Crush is non-negotiable for an efficient Dusk phase.

Should I kill Dawn or Dusk first?

For intermediate players the separate-kill plan — burst Dawn first with thrown weapons, then finish the enraged Dusk with crush — is the safest and most consistent. Killing Dawn first removes her ranged/magic barrages so you face Dusk one-on-one. Fighting both simultaneously is faster but demands constant prayer switching and is best left for advanced runners.

What prayer should I use against Dawn?

Dawn attacks with both Ranged and Magic and swaps between them, so you should flick between Protect from Ranged and Protect from Magic based on her animation. If you are not comfortable flicking, keep Protect from Ranged up as a default and eat the occasional magic hit, but learning the flick (covered in our Prayer Training guide) will save huge amounts of food over a session.

How much profit can I make per hour?

An intermediate runner doing 20–30 kills per hour typically earns a 300k–700k gp floor from alchables and seeds, plus the chance at the Granite maul (multi-million gp) and the rare Black tourmaline core. The boss is one of the best risk-adjusted money makers in the 75-Slayer bracket because deaths are easily avoided once the mechanics are learned.

Can I farm the bosses without a Slayer task?

No. The Grotesque Guardians only spawn while you are on an active Grotesque Guardian Slayer assignment. If your task ends, the roof will not let you through until your next guardian task. Plan your profit sessions around a fresh assignment and use block/skip lists to keep guardians coming reliably.
