1. 🌀 The Great Brain Robbery — Windmill & Mechanism Puzzle

This quest (the sequel to Creature of Fenkenstrain and Recipe for Disaster's pirate segment) has two puzzle-style steps that stop players cold: the crate smuggling logic in Canifis, and the windmill basement "blow the door" mechanic on Harmony Island. Both are easy once you know the trick.

① The Crate & Ring of Charos Trap (Canifis)

The stuck point: you talk to Rufus in the Canifis meat store and he assumes you are shipping cats. You must build 10 fake wooden cats and a crate with a false bottom, then fill it. The catch is that the Ring of Charos(a) must stay equipped the entire time — take it off and Rufus realises the cats are wooden.

🛠️ Exact Fix

  • Wear the Ring of Charos(a) before speaking to Rufus — and never remove it until the crate is shipped.
  • Collect 6 crate parts + a wolf whistle from Rufus (keep 2 free inventory slots).
  • Make 10 wooden cats: 10 planks + 10 fur (regular/bear/grey wolf — NOT kebbit or larupia fur) at a Clockmaker's bench in a POH.
  • Build the crate next to Dr. Fenkenstrain, add the false bottom, fill it with the 10 cats.
  • Blow the wolf whistle, then use the shipping order on the crate.
💡 Mistake-Proof Tip

Do not drop or bank the wolf whistle — you need it to summon Rufus for the cutscene. Also, the false bottom uses extra nails (bring ~50+ nails total; bronze/iron bend, so bring spares). If you fumble the cats, you can re-craft them, but you cannot re-do the conversation without the ring on.

② The Windmill Basement Keg Puzzle (Harmony Island)

The stuck point: after the brain-swap items are requested, players run to the monastery door and have no idea how to open it. The door is blown open with a keg of gunpowder + fuse taken from the shipwreck.

StepWhereWhat to grab
1Northern shipwreckClimb ladder → pick up tinderbox
2Beside the cannonsTake the keg
3Locker (climb down)Take the fuse
4Monastery doorUse keg + fuse on door, then light the fuse
💡 Mistake-Proof Tip

You need a hammer and diving gear (fishbowl helmet + apparatus) for the surrounding sections. If the door won't open, you usually forgot the fuse from the locker — it is a separate step from the keg. After the explosion, kill 4 Sorebones for the cranial clamp, brain tongs, 3 bell jars and 30 skull staples.

③ The Barrelchest Fight (Prayer Disabled Mechanic)

This is not a maze, but it confuses people: Barrelchest disables your prayer and drains points with each hit. The fix is to re-enable Protect prayers after every attack lands. Keep your fishbowl helmet + diving apparatus equipped the whole fight or you cannot enter.

2. ⚖️ Hazeel Cult — The Choice and the Five Valves

Hazeel Cult is a short novice quest famous for two confusion points: which side to pick when talking to Clivet, and the five sewer valves you must turn before riding the raft. Both routes give the same quest point reward, so pick based on the cosmetic you want.

① The Choice — Ceril Side vs Hazeel Side

The stuck point: players refuse Clivet too early, or agree without understanding the consequence. On the Ceril (good) side you must ask "What do you mean?" before refusing — skip that line and you may never get the armour from Alomone. On the Hazeel (evil) side you agree, poison the Carnillean food range, then fetch a scroll.

SideWhat you doExtra rewardCombat?
Ceril (good)Refuse Clivet, kill Alomone, expose butlerCarnillean armour (drop-trick for 2)Yes — Alomone lvl 13
Hazeel (evil)Agree, poison food, recover scrollMark of Hazeel (cosmetic)No
💡 Mistake-Proof Tip

Ceril's side is quicker but needs a fight. The fake "Quest Complete!" screen on the Ceril side is not the real end — after turning in the armour, go upstairs and search the cupboard in the eastern room for the Mark of Hazeel + poison, then show Ceril to finish properly.

② The Five Valves (The Real Stuck Point)

Both sides require the same five valves turned to reach the cult hideout. The order and directions are fixed:

ValveLocationTurn
1Across the bridge south of the Carnillean houseRight
2Behind the Carnillean houseRight
3North of the cave (east of clock tower)Left ⚠️
4South of Ardougne Zoo's penguin cageRight
5South-east of valve 4, past Brother CedricRight

🚰 Why players get stuck

Valve 3 is the trap: almost everyone turns it right like the others, but it must be left. If the water never drains and the raft won't move, re-check Valve 3 first. Turn all five, then enter the cave, board the raft, and head south to Alomone.

③ Hazeel Side — The Secret Wall Scroll

If you picked Hazeel's side, after the poison step you fetch a scroll from the Carnillean basement: search the crates for a key, go upstairs, knock on the north wall of the north-west room, climb the ladder, and open the chest. Take the scroll back to Alomone to finish.

3. 🔩 Creature of Fenkenstrain — Body Parts & the Lightning Conductor

This quest is a chain of "go fetch, then assemble" logic steps. The two confusing bits are (a) gathering the scattered body parts in the right order, and (b) building the lightning conductor with canes + a broom. Get the order wrong and you will be running back and forth.

① Body Part Assembly Chain

The stuck point: players know they need a head, brain, torso, arms and legs, but not where each comes from or in what sequence.

PartSourceKey requirement
Decapitated headDig graves in Haunted Woods (gardener ghost directs you)Ghostspeak amulet equipped
Pickled brainBuy/take from the table in Canifis pub (50 GP or Telekinetic Grab)—
Torso / Arms / LegsExperiment cave: use star amulet on east tombstone, kill Experiment (lvl 51), dig 3 gravesStar amulet (marble + obsidian combined)
💡 Mistake-Proof Tip

Combine the marble amulet + obsidian amulet (read the two bookcases in the castle first) into the star amulet before pushing the eastern memorial. The Haunted Woods is full of vampyres — bring a holy symbol and food, and dig at the grave the gardener ghost points you to, not random graves.

② The Lightning Conductor Build

The stuck point: the "extended brush" step. You need 3 canes + 1 garden brush + 3 bronze wire. Use the brush on one cane (1 wire), then the other two canes on the extended brush (2 more wire). Then use it on the west fireplace to get the conductor mould, smelt a silver bar at any furnace into a conductor, and repair the lightning conductor.

🔧 Build order

  1. Get shed key from gardener ghost → search cupboard for garden brush.
  2. Take 3 canes from the pile by the shed.
  3. Brush + cane (1 bronze wire) → extended brush.
  4. Other 2 canes on extended brush (2 bronze wire).
  5. Extended brush on west fireplace → conductor mould.
  6. Silver bar at furnace → conductor (lightning rod).
  7. Repair the conductor (southern ladder, top of castle).

③ Claiming the Ring of Charos

After repairing the conductor, talk to the doctor, then climb the northern ladder and speak to Fenkenstrain's monster. Go back down and pickpocket Dr. Fenkenstrain for the Ring of Charos (needs 25 Thieving). This ring is what you later use in The Great Brain Robbery — so the two quests connect.

4. 🐒 Monkey Madness I & II — Sliding Puzzle & Prison Maze

Monkey Madness I is legendary for its reinitialisation sliding puzzle, and both games share the Ape Atoll prison maze. Monkey Madness II's puzzle is the gnome glider / temple sequence. Here is how to clear each.

① Monkey Madness I — The Reinitialisation Sliding Puzzle

The stuck point: in the underground hangar, click the panel south-east of Daero to open a 4×4-ish sliding puzzle (same style as Treasure Trail puzzle boxes). The fix is to solve it row by row, top to bottom, then finish the last two rows as columns.

🧩 Sliding Puzzle Method

  • Place tiles 1, 2, 3 in the top-left corner first (leave tile 4/5 to shuffle together).
  • Slide 4 to top-right, then 5 under it; rotate them into place to finish row 1.
  • Repeat for rows 2 and 3, never disturbing completed rows.
  • For the bottom two rows, work in 2-tile columns: place 16 & 21, then 17 & 22, then 18 & 23, then finish 19/20/24.
💡 Give Up? Pay Glough

If you cannot solve it, find Glough in the tree house south-east of the Grand Tree and pay him 200,000 GP. He leaves the puzzle with a single tile out of place — talk to Daero to return, then slide that one tile. Do not log out or teleport while solving: the puzzle resets.

② The Ape Atoll Prison Maze

The stuck point: after arriving on Ape Atoll you get shot and jailed. The fix is to watch the guard patrol pattern, pick the cell door lock, and escape while staying at least 2 squares from guards. Note: bearded guards cannot be walked through — go around them.

RuleWhy it matters
Stay 2+ squares from guards, even in the cellGetting spotted sends you back to jail
Watch the pattern, then pick the lockGuards move on a loop you can time
Bearded guards block pathsWalk around, never into them
If spotted, log out / hop worldsAvoids the lengthy re-jail route

③ Monkey Madness II — The Temple Puzzle

MM2's confusing step is the Ape Atoll temple: you must obtain the monkey greegree parts (monkey dentures, m'amulet mould, gold bar → Zooknock makes an enchanted bar) and navigate the temple without stepping on the wrong floor tiles. Keep Protect from Melee on, follow the marked path, and use the greegree to appear as a monkey so guards ignore you. The "puzzle" is really route memorisation — use the in-game map and stay on the dark brown floor.

5. 🌿 While Guthix Sleeps — Weight Room & Guthixian Temple

This grandmaster quest has two genuine logic puzzles: a weight/statue room where "tickits" = kilograms, and the Guthixian Temple where eight statues must each be given the correct potion. Both stop players who skip the clues.

① The Weight Room (Movario's Base)

The stuck point: a thermometer shows your weight in kilograms; notes say holding 17 coal (plus armour) weighs 38 "tickits". The trick: tickits = kilograms. You must put weights on the statue equal to your entry weight shown on the thermometer, then match it exactly on the way out.

💡 Mistake-Proof Tip

Remember your weight when you enter (check the thermometer). On exit, if you are heavier than when you entered, add the difference to the statue; if lighter, remove that much. The door opens automatically only when the dial stops dropping and your weight precisely matches. Runite platebody = 9, mithril crossbow = 5, fire battlestaff = 2 (example weights).

② The Guthixian Temple Statue Puzzle

The stuck point: eight statues each show a keyword; you must make the matching potion using ingredients dropped by druid spirits (use a druid pouch on them). Then place the resulting dolmen on the central Stone Table to open the huge door.

KeywordIngredientsKeywordIngredients
AttackClean guam + Eye of newtPrayerClean ranarr + Snape grass
StrengthClean tarromin + Limpwurt rootRangingClean dwarf weed + Wine of Zamorak
DefenceClean cadantine + White berriesMagicClean lantadyme + Potato cactus
CombatClean harralander + Goat horn dustBalanceClean harralander + Red spiders' eggs + Garlic + Silver dust

⚠️ Do not lose your druid pouch

If the druid pouch empties completely it disappears. Refill it by blooming your Silver sickle (b) near dead vines for flowers. An ouroboros pouch never depletes. Always keep 2+ free inventory slots for the flowers and dolmens. A mort myre fungus from the druids must be used on the right statue immediately or dropped, or you lose it.

③ The Stone of Jas / Balance Elemental

Not a maze, but the climax: the Balance Elemental uses all three combat styles and drains your stats. Bring at least two combat styles and a full Super Restore. Always carry your lit sapphire lantern. After the fight, search the mysterious stone twice for the cutscene that boosts combat stats to 255 (except Prayer).

6. 🔗 One Small Favour & Devious Minds — The Delivery Chains

Both quests are "favour chains" where you carry an item through a fixed loop of NPCs. The confusion is purely order — skip a step and the next NPC won't talk to you.

① One Small Favour — The Two Loops

The stuck point: the quest is one big loop across Kandarin, Asgarnia and Misthalin. The canonical order (start at Yanni in Shilo Village):

#NPC / PlaceAction
1Jungle forester (south of Shilo)Get blunt hatchet
2Captain Shanks (Lady of the Waves)Delivery handshake
3Brian (Port Sarim axe shop)He takes hatchet to sharpen
4Aggie (Draynor)She looks for Jimmy
5Johanhus (H.A.M. hideout)Release Jimmy the Chisel
6Seth Groats (Lumbridge)Chicken cages handover
7Horvik (Varrock) → ApothecaryTincture + salts + pot lid
8Tassie (Barbarian Village) → Hammerspike (Dwarven Mine)Pot lid, then stop the bully (fight 3 gang members)
9Arhein (Catherby) → Phantuwti (Seers) → weather vane fixReport + roof repair (36 Agility)
10Wizard Cromperty → Slagilith (Goblin Cave)Animate rock scroll, kill lvl 63 Slagilith
11Back to Yanni (Shilo)Red mahogany logs → quest complete
💡 Mistake-Proof Tip

The Slagilith fight (lvl 63, hits up to 26) is the only real combat. The weather vane repair needs a bronze, iron and steel bar at an anvil. Use fairy rings (CKR, DIS, etc.) and lodestones to hop between loops — most "I'm stuck" cases are just doing step 9 before step 4.

② Devious Minds — The Smuggle Chain

The stuck point: you make a "bow-sword" then must sneak an orb onto Entrana inside a large pouch — but Entrana bans weapons, armour and normal pouches, so you must go via the Abyss → Law altar.

🗡️ Step-by-step

  1. Talk to the monk by Paterdomus (River Salve); he wants a bow-sword.
  2. Use mithril 2h sword on Doric's whetstone → slender blade; add bowstring → bow-sword.
  3. Give bow-sword to monk → he gives you an orb.
  4. Put orb in a large/colossal pouch; travel via the Abyss Law rift to Entrana.
  5. Use the pouch on the church altar (you lose the pouch); watch cutscene.
  6. Return to the monk — he is dead. Go to Entrana's High Priest, then report to Sir Tiffy Cashien in Falador Park.
💡 Mistake-Proof Tip

Do not take weapons/armour into the Abyss approach if you can avoid it, but you do need to enter through the Law portal — bank everything else first. Your large pouch is NOT returned (unless you used a colossal pouch). The monk's death is scripted, not a bug — just keep going to Sir Tiffy.

③ Anti-Frustration: Travel Smart

Both chains span the whole map. Unlock the lodestone network, carry a ring of kinship or gold for charters, and use fairy rings. Bank between the Kandarin loop and the Asgarnia loop in One Small Favour so you are not carrying 5 pigeon cages and a pot lid everywhere.

7. 🍰 Recipe for Disaster, Puzzle Boxes & Light Mazes

The final section covers the puzzle-style steps in Recipe for Disaster subquests, the universal Treasure Trails puzzle box method, and the classic OSRS light maze (Temple of Light).

① RFD — Sir Amik Varze (Crème Brûlée Supreme)

The stuck point: the recipe has several "fetch then combine" steps and a windmill that must not be the Taverley one. Build order:

ComponentSourceNote
Milky mixtureCream on bucket of milkBuy both from the chest
CornflourSweetcorn in a non-Taverley windmill hopperTaverley windmill does NOT work
Vanilla podKharazi Jungle (SW of the pool)Need machete + hatchet
Evil chicken's eggZanaris Evil Chicken shrine → kill chickenAlso kill a black dragon for the token
CinnamonGrind a dramen branch with pestle & mortarEntrana Dungeon
FlambéWear ice gloves, rub token, ask Fairy DragonThen use on Sir Amik
💡 Mistake-Proof Tip

The single most common failure is using the Taverley windmill — it will not grind the sweetcorn. Use any other windmill (e.g. Zanaris or the one near Rimmington). Equip ice gloves before rubbing the dragon token or you cannot handle the flambéed brulee.

② RFD — Freeing the Lumbridge Guide (Quiz Puzzle)

The stuck point: Wizard Traiborn gives three quizzes (egg, milk, flour). You must answer 3 correctly each. The flour test is a memory test — screenshot or note the 10 items shown, because closing with ESC resets that portion.

🧠 Sample answers players miss

  • "Gold ring is to 5 as holy symbol is to…" → 16 (craft level).
  • "Glass vial is to 33 as glass orb is to…" → 46.
  • "Combat level of goblins near Lumbridge" → 2.
  • "Bribe at Al Kharid toll gate" → 10 GP.
  • Flour memory: "Which item began with 'm'?" → Mind Talisman.

③ Treasure Trails Puzzle Box Method

The stuck point: players scramble tiles randomly. The reliable method (works for every box — Troll Castle, Gnome child, Zulrah, Theatre of Blood, etc.):

PhaseWhat to do
Rows 1–3Solve left-to-right, top-to-bottom; never disturb a finished row
Bottom rowsSwitch to 2-tile columns: place 16&21, 17&22, 18&23, then 19/20/24
Stuck?You likely swapped two similar-looking tiles — compare to the Hint icon image (bottom-left)
ResetLog out (or destroy & re-request) to get a fresh scramble
💡 Mistake-Proof Tip

The Hint icon (bottom-left of the box) shows the solved image — keep it open on a second monitor. After solving, close the interface and re-talk the NPC. If you close early it re-scrambles. In the Wilderness the puzzle box is kept over the clue if you die.

④ The Light Maze — Temple of Light (Mourning's End Part II)

The stuck point: you redirect a light beam through pillars using mirrors and coloured crystals to open colour-coded doors. Rule: a door opens when hit by the beam of its opposite colour on the wheel (blue door → yellow beam; cyan door → red beam; etc.).

DoorBeam neededHow
BlueYellowMirror north → west → south, dye yellow, mirror east
PinkCyan (dyed) then yellowCyan beam north, yellow added east
Final (Death Altar)Red (yellow + blue crystals cancel)Split beams, dye, combine through magenta pillar

🔦 Core technique

  • Start at the crystal collector: mirror → beam north, then chain mirrors pillar by pillar.
  • Add a coloured crystal on a pillar to dye the beam the opposite of the door you want.
  • For multi-floor doors, send the beam up/down via ladders and rotate the fractured crystal so the wide side faces correctly.
  • Pull the collector lever to reset between doors (except the final one — don't reset it!).

8. 🧩 Quest Puzzle FAQs

What's the easiest confusing quest puzzle to skip with money?

The Monkey Madness I reinitialisation puzzle can be skipped by paying Glough 200,000 GP — he leaves one tile out of place, which you slide to finish. No other quest on this list has a direct pay-to-skip, but most (Hazeel valves, RFD quizzes) just need the right order, not skill.

My Hazeel Cult raft won't move — what did I do wrong?

You turned Valve 3 to the right. It must be left. Revisit the valve north of the cave (east of the clock tower) and turn it left; the water will drain and the raft becomes usable. All other valves are right.

Why won't the Taverley windmill grind my sweetcorn for Sir Amik Varze?

It is intentionally disabled for this subquest. Use any other windmill (Zanaris, Rimmington, or the one north of Falador). Put sweetcorn in the hopper, operate the controls, then use a pot on the flour bin.

I lost my druid pouch in While Guthix Sleeps — am I stuck?

If it fully emptied it disappears, but you can refill a new one: bloom your Silver sickle (b) near dead vines for flowers, then use flowers on the pouch. An ouroboros pouch never depletes and is the safest option. You can also just note each statue's keyword and teleport out to buy ingredients instead of using druid spirits.

Does logging out during a sliding puzzle reset it?

Yes. Both the Monkey Madness I reinitialisation puzzle and Treasure Trails puzzle boxes re-scramble (or lose progress) if you log out before completing. Finish the puzzle, then close the interface and re-talk the NPC. Teleporting away also resets the Monkey Madness puzzle.

How do I know which colour beam opens a Temple of Light door?

Use the colour wheel: the door opens from the beam of the colour directly opposite it. Blue door → yellow beam; cyan door → red beam (made by combining yellow + blue through a magenta pillar); pink door → green beam. Dye the beam with the matching coloured crystal on a pillar.

One Small Favour NPC won't talk to me — am I soft-locked?

Almost never. You are doing steps out of order. The chain is a fixed loop: Yanni → forester → Captain Shanks → Brian → Aggie → Johanhus → Seth Groats → Horvik/Apothecary → Tassie/Hammerspike → Arhein/Phantuwti → Cromperty/Slagilith → back to Yanni. Re-read the last dialogue; the next NPC is always the one the current NPC points you to.

Do I get my large pouch back after Devious Minds?

No — a normal large pouch is consumed when you use it on the Entrana altar (a colossal pouch survives). You must obtain another from the Abyss or buy one from Wizard Korvak in the Runecrafting Guild for 25,000 GP. Plan for this if you still need it for Runecrafting.

What's the most forgotten item in The Great Brain Robbery crate step?

The Ring of Charos(a) being unequipped, and the wolf whistle being dropped/banked. Keep the ring on the whole time and hold the whistle until after the shipping-order cutscene. Also bring plenty of spare nails — bronze and iron nails bend.

Are these puzzles required for quest-cape or quest-order progression?

Yes — most are on the optimal quest path. Monkey Madness I/II, While Guthix Sleeps, Recipe for Disaster, and One Small Favour gate later content (Desert Treasure II, SOTE-adjacent reqs, etc.). If you are following a quest order, see our Optimal Quest Order guide for where they fit.
