OSRS Membership Refund & Chargeback Ban 2026

Here's the hard truth most players learn too late: disputing an OSRS membership charge with your bank — a "chargeback" — gets the account permanently banned in the vast majority of cases. And cancelling your subscription does not refund you. This guide is the only place that lays out exactly why, what the real refund paths are (especially through the App Store and Google Play), and a damage-control template if you've already hit dispute.

🔥 Exclusive Pain-Point Troubleshooting (What Other Guides Skip)

Type "OSRS refund" into any forum and you'll find vague advice like "just cancel, it's fine" or "open a ticket." Neither addresses the two real money-losing traps: (1) players who cancelled thinking it refunded a charge that already posted, and (2) players who disputed through their bank and woke up to a permanent ban with no warning. This guide is built around those two traps, with the exact store-refund steps and a copy-paste ticket template. Start with the 5-step diagnosis above.

1. 🚫 Chargeback = Near-Automatic Permanent Ban

This is the single most expensive mistake an OSRS player can make with their wallet, and almost nobody warns you at the checkout. A chargeback is when you go around Jagex and ask your bank or card issuer to forcibly reverse a membership payment. To Jagex's systems, that reads as "someone took goods and then clawed the money back" — i.e. fraud. The automated response is a permanent ban on the account tied to that payment.

The Membership Guide 2026 covers how to subscribe safely; this section is about what happens when you try to undo it the wrong way.

① What a Chargeback Actually Is

When you buy membership, the money flows: your card → payment processor (e.g. Xsolla / PayPal / Apple / Google) → Jagex. A "refund" is Jagex or the store voluntarily returning it. A "chargeback" is your bank yanking it back after the fact, usually with a fraud/dispute code attached. The moment that reverse hits, Jagex sees an unpaid-for membership and a dispute flag.

② Why Jagex Treats It as Theft

  • You received the membership (game time, benefits) — that's value delivered.
  • A chargeback takes the money back after delivery, which the system logs as "goods taken, payment reversed."
  • Jagex's Terms of Service classify this as a breach and, because it's financially abusive, the standard enforcement is account closure.
  • Chargeback abuse is also a real industry-wide problem for live-service games, so the policy is intentionally strict to deter it.

③ Can It Ever Be Reversed?

ScenarioLikelihood of BanCan It Be Lifted?
Chargeback on an active membership ~99% permanent ban Only if you reverse the dispute AND repay — see §6
Chargeback reversed by you within days (before Jagex processes) Lower, but not zero Possible if caught pre-ban; contact support immediately
Bank did it "automatically" (fraud alert on old charge) Still treated as chargeback Same as above — you must undo it
Legitimate refund via Jagex/store (no bank dispute) No ban N/A — this is the safe path
⚠️ Linked Accounts Get Caught Too

Jagex often flags accounts that share payment details, IP, or identity. A chargeback on one account can ripple into a chain review of others on the same billing profile. If you run alts, read the linked-account chain-ban guide before you do anything.

2. 🔁 Cancelling Subscription ≠ Refund

The most common misunderstanding we see: a player cancels membership, assumes the recent charge is refunded, then is shocked when the money never returns. Cancelling only stops future billing. It does not reach back and refund a payment that already went through.

① What Cancelling Actually Does

Stops the next renewal

If you're on a recurring plan, cancellation means you won't be billed again at the next cycle. You keep the membership you already paid for until that cycle ends.

Preserves access until expiry

Your member benefits stay active through the paid period. Cancelling early does not cut you off mid-term.

② What Cancelling Does NOT Do

❌ It is not a refund

  • It does not return the money from the charge that already posted.
  • It does not prorate or "give back" unused days in most cases.
  • It does not undo a chargeback (that's a separate, far worse action).

③ Cancel vs. Refund vs. Chargeback — Know the Difference

ActionStops Future Billing?Returns Past Money?Risk to Account
CancelYesNoNone
Refund (via Jagex/store)Yes (ends membership)Only if approvedNone, if legitimate
Chargeback (via bank)Yes (payment reversed)Yes (forced)Permanent ban — very high

If you only want to stop being billed again, cancelling is correct and safe — see the dedicated Cancel Membership & Refund guide for the exact buttons. If you want money back, cancellation alone will not get it; you need a refund request through the right channel (next sections).

3. 📱 Bought via App Store / Google Play? You Can't Refund Through Jagex

This is the second big trap. If you subscribed to OSRS membership inside the iOS app (Apple App Store) or the Android app (Google Play), Jagex never directly received your card payment — Apple or Google did. That means Jagex cannot issue the refund, and contacting Jagex support about it will just bounce you back to the store. The refund must be requested from the store that took your money.

① Apple App Store Refund Flow

Step 1 — Open Apple's "Report a Problem" page

Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with the Apple ID that made the purchase.

Step 2 — Find the OSRS membership charge

Locate the Old School RuneScape membership transaction in your purchase list.

Step 3 — Choose "Request a refund" and a reason

Pick the closest reason (e.g. "accidental purchase," "unauthorized"). Apple reviews and decides; not all requests are granted, especially for consumed/used membership time.

Step 4 — Wait for Apple's decision

If approved, Apple returns the funds. Jagex is not involved and your account is not banned, because this is a store refund, not a chargeback.

② Google Play Refund Flow

Step 1 — Open play.google.com

Sign in with the Google account used for the purchase, then go to Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history.

Step 2 — Select the OSRS membership order

Find the Old School RuneScape subscription/one-time charge and choose Request a refund.

Step 3 — Submit and wait

Google (or the developer, depending on policy) reviews. Recent accidental purchases are more likely to be approved than used-up membership.

③ Critical Warning for Store Purchasers

🚫 Do NOT Chargeback the Store Charge

Even though Apple/Google took your money, a chargeback filed with your card bank still hits Jagex's system as a reversed membership payment — and still risks a permanent ban on your OSRS account. Always use the store's own refund request, never a bank dispute, for App Store / Google Play purchases. The Mobile Membership guide explains where your money actually went.

4. 🔒 "My Account Is Already Banned — Will I Get a Refund?"

Short answer: almost never. A ban (for chargeback, RWT, macroing, or any other reason) terminates the account and the services tied to it. Jagex does not refund membership on a banned account, because the ban is the consequence of a rule breach, not a billing error.

① The Banned-Account Refund Policy

  • A ban ends your access; unused membership time is forfeited — it is not convertible to cash.
  • Refund requests tied to a banned account are normally declined outright.
  • If the ban was itself caused by a chargeback, you're in the worst position: no refund and no account.

② Chargeback on a Banned Account Makes It Worse

If your account is already banned for another reason and you then chargeback the membership, you've now added a payment dispute on top of the sanction. This does not resurrect the account and can block you from creating or recovering related accounts on the same billing identity. For recovery from a non-payment ban (e.g. a mistaken macro flag), the Account Recovery guide and the chain-ban guide are the right reads — but a chargeback-driven ban is intentionally the hardest to lift.

5. ✅ Legitimate Refund Paths (When You Might Actually Get One)

Not every refund is a ban-worthy chargeback. There are narrow, legitimate cases where Jagex or the store may return your money — and these go through the proper channel, never your bank.

① Accidental Double Purchase

If you were billed twice for the same membership in one transaction cycle (a true duplicate, not two separate intentional buys), open a support ticket with both transaction IDs. Duplicate charges are the most likely to be refunded or credited.

② Unauthorized / Minor Purchases

If a child or someone else used your account

Jagex may consider refunds for clearly unauthorized use, especially involving a minor, but you must prove it wasn't you. Gather dates, the cardholder name, and any context. Going through the store's refund route (§3) is usually faster than a Jagex ticket for app purchases.

③ Jagex Support Refund Window

For direct (non-store) purchases, Jagex support handles refunds case-by-case and typically only for clear errors (duplicate, technical failure where membership wasn't delivered). A "I changed my mind after using it for a week" request will be declined. The Membership Worth It guide helps you decide before buying so you don't end up here.

6. 🆘 Already Did a Chargeback? Damage Control

If the chargeback already went through and your account is banned (or you fear it will be), act fast. The only realistic path to lifting a chargeback ban is to reverse the dispute with your bank AND repay Jagex — proving you're not trying to keep the game time for free.

① Can the Ban Be Lifted?

Sometimes, yes — but only if you fully cooperate: cancel the dispute with the bank, let the original payment settle, and then work with Jagex support to resolve the outstanding balance. There is no guarantee; Jagex's policy leans toward permanent closure, so speed and completeness matter.

② The Repayment / Reversal Path

Step 1 — Contact your bank and withdraw the dispute

Call or use your banking app to cancel the chargeback. Get confirmation that the reversal is processing.

Step 2 — Gather your proof

Save the bank's dispute-withdrawal confirmation, the original transaction ID, and your account display name.

Step 3 — Open a Jagex ticket explaining the reversal

State clearly that you withdrew the chargeback, the payment will settle, and you request a review of the ban. Do not argue — cooperate.

③ Copy-Paste Ticket Template

📋 Support Ticket — Chargeback Reversed, Request Ban Review

Subject: Chargeback withdrawn — request review of account ban Display name: [character name] Email on account: [email] Original membership transaction ID: [TXID] Date of charge: [date] Bank dispute reference: [ref] Issue: I filed a chargeback with my bank for the above membership. I have now CONTACTED MY BANK AND WITHDRAWN THE DISPUTE. The payment should settle normally. I do not wish to keep membership without paying. Request: Please review and lift the associated ban now that the chargeback is reversed. I have proof of the withdrawal on request. I understand chargebacks violate the Terms and apologize for the process error.

⏳ One Ticket, Then Wait

Open a single, complete ticket with your withdrawal proof attached. Do not spam or open multiple tickets — that slows the review. If the bank can't withdraw the dispute (some are irreversible once finalized), say so honestly; Jagex may still consider repayment options, but the odds drop sharply.

7. 🛡️ Prevention & Safer Alternatives

The cleanest way to never face a refund-vs-ban dilemma is to avoid the traps up front. A few habits save both your money and your account.

🎯 The Money-Safe Checklist

  1. Know your purchase channel: web/Jagex vs. App Store vs. Google Play — refunds go to whoever took your money.
  2. Cancel to stop future billing; never assume it refunds past charges.
  3. Never file a bank chargeback for membership. Use the store or Jagex refund route instead.
  4. Keep transaction IDs for every membership buy — they're your proof for any legit dispute.
  5. Consider bonds for flexible, non-recurring membership you control — see below.

Many players prefer bonds (bought in-game or via the Bond vs. Subscription breakdown) because there's no recurring charge to accidentally dispute, and the Cheapest Membership guide shows how to stretch costs. Whatever you pick, the rule is simple: if you want your money back, ask the seller — never your bank.

8. 💡 Refund & Ban FAQs

Will cancelling my subscription get me banned?

No. Cancelling is a normal account action and carries zero ban risk. The ban risk is exclusively tied to chargebacks (bank disputes), not to stopping a renewal.

My bank auto-reversed an old OSRS charge as "fraud." Is that still a chargeback?

Yes. If your bank reversed the payment for any reason, Jagex's system sees an unpaid membership and may ban the account. Contact your bank immediately to withdraw the reversal and then follow the §6 damage-control steps.

Can I get a refund if I simply didn't enjoy membership?

Almost never. "Changed my mind" isn't a valid refund reason once membership time has been used. Your options are to cancel (stop future billing) or, for app purchases, try the store's goodwill refund — but approval is not guaranteed.

I bought through the App Store and Apple refunded me. Am I safe?

Yes — a refund processed by Apple through its own "Report a Problem" system is a store refund, not a chargeback, so it does not trigger a ban. The ban only comes from a bank-initiated dispute, even if the underlying purchase was via the App Store.

How long do I have to request a store refund?

Apple and Google each set their own windows (often a short window after purchase, with stricter rules for consumed/used content). The faster you request it, the better. Don't wait weeks and then escalate to a bank dispute out of frustration — that's exactly how bans happen.
