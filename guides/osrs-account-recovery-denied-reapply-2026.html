OSRS Account Recovery Denied? How to Re-Apply and Actually Get Approved in 2026

You filled out the recovery form five, maybe ten times. Each time it comes back "denied" — sometimes in seconds, before a human ever looks at it. This guide is the post-mortem and the fix: why Jagex's auto-filter is rejecting you, what "not the original creator" really means, and the exact creation-era evidence that flips a denial into an approval.

⏱️ 30-Second Diagnostic — Which Denial Are You Getting?

  • 📌 Denied in under 10 seconds? That's the auto-filter, not a human. You triggered a mismatch on email, date, or password — fix the field, not the story.
  • 📌 "We could not verify you are the original creator" means your answers don't match the account's creation record. Oldest password + creation window are the two heaviest signals.
  • 📌 "Evidence too weak / not enough information" means you left optional fields blank. Fill every field with anything truthful you remember.
  • 📌 You reused a VPN / shared IP? Submitting from a country that isn't the account's creation country is a top auto-reject cause.
  • 📌 Stop re-submitting blindly. Each identical denial burns trust. Gather proof first, then send one strong re-apply.

1. 🔍 Why Recovery Keeps Getting Denied

Before you fire off attempt #11, understand the two completely different ways a recovery fails. Most players blame "Jagex hates me" when the real cause is a predictable filter they keep tripping. The account recovery after a hack walkthrough covers the happy path; this guide is for when that path keeps saying no.

Failure TypeHow FastWhat Triggered ItFix
Auto-filter rejection Seconds (1-9s) A hard mismatch: wrong recovery email, impossible creation date, impossible password age, or a flagged VPN/IP. Correct the offending field. No human ever read your ticket.
"Not the original creator" Hours–days A human or model compared your answers to the account's creation record and found them inconsistent. Supply older, more specific creation-era proof (see checklist).
"Insufficient evidence" Hours–days You left optional fields empty and the few answers you gave were weak or generic. Fill every field; add transaction IDs, old emails, ISPs.
Locked / cooldown loop Immediate Too many rapid submissions from one IP trained the filter to ignore you. Wait 24-48h, switch to the creation-country network, then re-apply once.
💡 The Core Insight

A "denied" is not a verdict on whether the account is yours — it is a verdict on whether your form proved it. Two players can own equally legitimate accounts, and the one who submits better evidence wins. Your job in this guide is to become the better-evidenced player.

② "Not the Original Creator" — What Jagex Actually Checks

When a recovery reviewer (or the automated model) says you are not the original creator, they are comparing your answers against the account's stored birth record: the email it was created on, the rough creation date, the earliest password hash, and the country/ISP at creation. If your recovery form contradicts those stored facts, you lose — even if you've played the account for a decade. Inheritance, account buying, and "my brother made it" are the three most common ways players genuinely cannot answer these questions, and they are the hardest cases to win.

2. 📋 The Creation-Era Evidence Checklist (Your Real Moat)

This is the part competitors skip. Anyone can tell you "be honest." Few show you the exact evidence hierarchy Jagex weights. Gather as many of these as you can before re-applying. The more rows you can fill, the higher your approval odds.

EvidenceWeightHow to Find It
Oldest password you ever used Very high A password manager history, a saved browser password from 2012, or a pattern you reused only on this account. Even a partial/close password helps.
Creation date & year High Old forum posts, the welcome email, a "happy 10-year" login message, or a credit-card statement from when you first paid.
Creation ISP / connection High Old ISP billing emails, a university network name, or the country you lived in when you made the account.
Original registration email High The inbox you signed up with — even if you later changed it. Find the 2008-2014 "welcome to RuneScape" message.
Transaction / membership records Medium-high Bank or PayPal statements showing Jagex/RuneScape charges, with dates and amounts.
Old linked emails / recovered emails Medium Any email that ever received a Jagex security or newsletter message.
First characters / early milestones Low-medium Names of your first account, early quest completions, or the first item you ever traded.

③ Build a "Proof File" Before You Re-Apply

🗂️ One Document, Every Clue

  • Open a notes file titled ACCOUNT-PROOF-[displayname].
  • List every password you have ever used on the account, oldest first. Don't self-censor — submit the closest match.
  • Write the creation year and any narrower window (e.g. "summer 2013, right after EoC").
  • Dig through old email inboxes for anything from Jagex, even spam folders and deleted items.
  • Screenshot any bank/PayPal charge and note the exact date.
  • Note the country and ISP you were on at creation — this matters for the VPN section below.
⚠️ Don't Guess the Creation Date Downward

A very common auto-reject is claiming a creation year that is later than the account's real creation. If the account was made in 2009 and you write 2014 "to be safe," the filter sees a lie and denies instantly. When unsure, give the earliest year you can defend, not the safest-looking one.

3. 🚫 5 Actions That Stop Instant (Second) Rejections

If your denials come back in seconds, you are failing the auto-filter, not the human review. These five moves remove the most common automatic triggers. Pair this with the full account security checklist so you don't get re-hacked after recovery.

① Use the original registration email as the recovery contact

The single biggest instant-reject is listing a recovery email that was never associated with the account. Put the first email you signed up with, not the one you use today.

② Submit from the account's creation country/network

A login or recovery from a foreign IP — especially a VPN exit node — is treated as a red flag. If the account was made in Germany, recover from a German connection (see Section 5).

③ Enter the oldest password you can recall, not your current one

The filter compares against historical password hashes. A password you set in 2015 beats the one you set last month, because it proves tenure.

④ Never leave optional fields blank

"I don't know" is fine; a blank box looks like a weak claim. Fill every field with your best truthful memory, even approximate dates.

⑤ Wait between attempts

Five submissions in an hour tell the system you are guessing. Space attempts by at least 24 hours, and only re-submit after you've added new evidence.

🔑 The 5-Second Rule

If a denial arrives faster than you could read your own form, it was automatic. Do not change your story — change the field that mismatched. Re-reading the same wrong answers ten times is the #1 reason people hit 10 denials.

4. 🔄 Re-Applying Done Right: A Scene-by-Scene Walkthrough

A strong re-apply is not a louder re-submit — it is a better-documented one. Here is the sequence that turns a denial into an approval.

④ Step 1 — Cool Down (24-48h)

Stop submitting. Rapid rejections train the filter to auto-ignore your IP. Use the wait to assemble your proof file from Section 2.

④ Step 2 — Match the Creation Country

Disable any VPN/proxy. If you're traveling or abroad, either wait until you're on the creation-country network or use a residential connection from that country. A mismatch here is an instant deny.

④ Step 3 — Open the Recovery Form Fresh

Go to the official account recovery page through a bookmark — never via an email link. Start clean; don't copy-paste your last denied answers blindly.

④ Step 4 — Fill Every Field With Proof, Not Guesses

Lead with your oldest password, your creation year, and the original email. In the "anything else" box, paste a condensed version of your proof file: transaction dates, old ISPs, linked emails.

④ Step 5 — Submit Once, Then Wait

Send a single, well-evidenced request. If denied again, the response will often hint at the weak point — use it to refine, wait another day, and submit once more. For the broader appeals process, see the post-hack recovery guide.

💡 One Strong Ticket Beats Ten Weak Ones

Reviewers can see your submission history. A pattern of ten near-identical denials reads as "guessing," while one detailed, evidence-rich re-apply reads as "legitimate owner who finally found their records." Quality of submissions > quantity, every time.

5. 🌍 When to Drop the VPN and Submit From the Creation Country

This is the silent killer of recoveries. Players using a VPN for privacy — or living abroad from where they made the account — get auto-denied because the IP country doesn't match the creation country. The anti-scam guide explains why Jagex weights location so heavily; here's how to work with it.

SituationRecommended Action
You're in the creation country now Disable VPN, use home Wi-Fi, submit. This is the ideal case.
You live abroad but have family/friends in the creation country Ask them to submit the recovery from their residential connection (with your correct answers). Never pay a stranger to do this.
You're traveling and the local IP differs Wait until you're back on the creation-country network, or use a trusted residential connection from that country — not a datacenter VPN.
You always used a VPN for OSRS The creation record may still show your real ISP. Submit from that real ISP; the VPN was likely never the "registered" location.
⚠️ VPN + Recovery = Auto-Suspect

Datacenter VPN IPs (the cheap ones) are shared by thousands of bots and recoveries. Jagex's filter flags them aggressively. If you must use a VPN, it must be a residential exit in the creation country — but the safest path is always a plain home connection from that country.

6. 📝 The Re-Apply Ticket Template (Copy-Paste)

Use this as the "additional information" block. Replace brackets with your real proof. Concise, factual, evidence-led tickets outperform emotional ones.

📄 Re-Apply Statement

Display name: [name] Oldest password I recall: [password or closest guess] Account created: [year / season], from [country] on ISP [name] Original sign-up email: [email] Transactions: Jagex charge on [date], $[amount], via [PayPal/card] Linked emails that received Jagex mail: [list] Notes: I previously submitted on [dates] and was denied; I have since located my original welcome email / bank statement confirming [fact].

💡 Lead With the Newest Evidence

Reviewers scan for what's new since your last denial. If you found an old welcome email or a 2013 bank charge, put that fact first. It signals "this owner did the work and found proof," which is exactly what flips a denial.

7. 💡 Recovery Denial FAQs

How many times can I re-apply before I'm permanently blocked?

There is no fixed "10 strikes and you're out" number published, but rapid, identical submissions absolutely increase auto-rejection risk and can trigger a cooldown. The strategy is not to maximize attempts — it's to maximize evidence per attempt. One strong re-apply after a 24-48h wait beats ten blind ones.

My recovery is denied in seconds — is a human even reading it?

No. A sub-10-second denial is the automated filter rejecting on a hard mismatch (email, creation date, password age, or flagged IP). Fix the mismatched field rather than rewriting your explanation. A human only gets involved after the auto-filter passes your form through.

What if I bought the account and can't answer creation questions?

This is the hardest case. Account buying breaks Jagex rules, and recovery is designed to return accounts to the original creator. If you cannot prove original creation, approval is unlikely through standard recovery. Focus on any evidence tying you to long-term legitimate play, but manage expectations — the system is built to favor the founder.

Does using a VPN during recovery get me banned?

A VPN alone won't ban you, but it will frequently auto-reject recovery because the exit IP's country won't match the account's creation record. For recovery specifically, submit from the creation-country residential connection. You can resume VPN use for normal play afterward.

I found an old welcome email — does that guarantee approval?

It's one of the strongest single pieces of evidence because it proves the original registration email and date. It doesn't guarantee approval by itself, but combined with your oldest password and a creation-country submission, it moves you from "weak" to "strong" very fast.

Should I contact Jagex Support directly instead of the form?

The recovery form is the official, tracked channel for ownership disputes — support tickets about "recover my account" are routed back to it. Use the form with strong evidence. If you have a separate security issue (e.g. a ban you believe is wrongful), then a support ticket is appropriate, but recovery itself goes through the form.

🎯 Final Tips — Stop the Denial Loop

If you remember nothing else: a denial is a verdict on your form, not your ownership. Build the proof file, match the creation country, lead with your oldest password, fill every field, and submit once after a cooldown. Then lock it down so you never need recovery again.
