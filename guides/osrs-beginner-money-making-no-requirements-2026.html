2. 🪙 The Methods (Zero & Near-Zero Stats)

② Cowhides — Collect & Tan (0 requirements)

Cows around Lumbridge and Al Kharid drop cowhides that anyone can pick up for free. Take them to Ellis the tanner in Al Kharid, who has no skill or quest requirement — he tans plain cowhides into leather for a nominal fee. Sell the leather, or (with a little Crafting) turn it into profit. Collecting ~1,000–1,400 hides per hour is realistic with world-hopping; tan and sell for a steady, zero-risk income.

③ Flax → Bowstrings (10 Crafting)

At 10 Crafting you can spin flax into bowstrings at any spinning wheel. The fastest setup uses Bales of flax (from Varlamore) plus an upgraded Bow string spool, reaching about 2,000 bowstrings/hour. The OSRS Wiki lists this at 188,160 GP/hr after tax (input 5,840 GP of flax → output 194,000 GP). Without the spool, expect closer to ~1,400/hr. At 76 Magic the Spin Flax Lunar spell pushes this to ~190,800 GP/hr.

④ Mining Iron Ore (15 Mining)

Fifteen Mining unlocks iron rocks. The Mining Guild (60+ Mining) has clustered iron spots next to a bank chest for ~162K GP/hr, but even outside it, power-mining or banking iron is solid early gold. The OSRS Wiki lists Mining iron ore at 161,845 GP/hr (15+ Mining). It also trains Mining, feeding into Smithing and later Blast Furnace profits.

⑤ Pure Collecting — Feathers, Ashes, Seaweed (0 requirements)

Chickens drop feathers (free, always in demand for fletching), the Edgeville/Harmony spirits drop ashes, and seaweed can be collected from shorelines. These are slow per-hour, but they are genuinely zero-requirement and a good habit while you train other stats. Use them as filler, not your main income.

💡 Pick flax yourself for more profit

Buying flax on the GE eats into margin. Picking your own at Lletya (Mourning's End Part I) or buying Bales of flax cuts input cost to near-zero, pushing your effective GP/hr well above the Wiki's buy-flax baseline.

6. ⚠️ Common Mistakes

❌ Mistake 1: Selling cowhides raw instead of tanning.

Raw cowhides are worth far less than tanned leather. Ellis in Al Kharid tans them with no requirements — always tan before selling.

❌ Mistake 2: Buying flax on the GE at peak price.

Flax margins live or die on the buy price. Pick your own or use Bales of flax; buying at a high GE price can erase most of the ~188K/hr figure.

❌ Mistake 3: Ignoring the 2% Grand Exchange tax.

Every sale pays a 2% commission. The Wiki's "after tax" numbers already include it — your own manual maths must too, or you will overestimate profit.

❌ Mistake 4: Quitting because early rates feel slow.

These are starter methods, not end-game income. Use them to bootstrap into iron mining, then Blast Furnace and members skilling. The goal is momentum, not millions on day one.

7. ❓ FAQ

Can a level-3 account really make money?

Yes. Collecting cowhides, feathers, and ashes needs no stats at all, and 10 Crafting (a few minutes of spin/tan training) unlocks flax spinning. These fund your first real progress.

What is the single best no-requirement method?

For pure zero-stat accounts, cowhides (collect + tan at Ellis, no quest) are safest. For "near-zero" (10 Crafting), flax → bowstrings at ~188K GP/hr after tax is the cleanest, most consistent earner.

Do I need membership for any of these?

No — all work on F2P. Only the Spin Flax spell is members-only; the manual spinning wheel method is free-to-play.

How fast can I afford a bond?

At ~150–190K GP/hr combined, a few focused sessions clear the ~14M GP bond cost. Pair cowhides with flax and avoid buying materials at peak.

Is any of this in the Wilderness?

No. Every method here is safe — no PvP risk, no item loss. Perfect for brand-new accounts building confidence.

8. 🏁 Final Tips

  • Start free: Cowhides + collecting cost nothing; never buy materials you can gather.
  • Get 10 Crafting fast: Flax spinning is the highest, safest near-zero GP/hr in the game.
  • Bank the tax: Always calculate the 2% GE commission so your estimates stay honest.
  • Re-check prices: GE values shift daily — verify before bulk buys or sells.
