Four Ways Crimson Desert Gets Stuck — and Which Are Fixed

"I can't interact with anything" is one of the most searched Crimson Desert complaints, and it is actually several different bugs wearing the same coat. Telling them apart is the whole game, because three of them are already fixed and one is not.

Here is the split, straight from Pearl Abyss's own documentation:

  • Interactions with NPCs, mounts and certain objects stop working — listed on the official Known Issues list, with restarting the game given as the remedy. This one is still open.
  • The character becomes unresponsive after using a shop — listed as fixed in patch 1.18.02, released 17 August 2026.
  • The camp resources screen shows comrade count as 0 — also fixed in 1.18.02.
  • Silver is reset to 0 after completing "A Secret Deal" on some saves — also fixed in 1.18.02.

The current client is version 1.000.484 (2.00.00, released 25 August 2026), which is newer than 1.18.02. That means if you are fully updated, three of the four should already be gone. Most people still hitting the shop freeze or the silver loss are running an outdated client, and the fix is simply to update.

The one-line version: update first. If the problem survives being on the current client, you are most likely looking at the interaction bug, which is the one still officially open — and section 3 tells you what to try before you resign yourself to a restart.

Before You Restart — Try These 5 Steps First

Restarting fixes the interaction bug, and Pearl Abyss's own note points there. But a restart costs you everything since your last save, and there are cheaper things to try first. Run these in order — each takes seconds.

1

Test whether it is the interaction or the whole game

Try a different action type: swing your weapon, open the map, sprint. If those work and only interactions are dead, this is the interaction bug. If nothing responds at all, you have a freeze, not an interaction problem, and a restart is unavoidable.

2

Move away and come back

Interaction prompts in open-world games are proximity and angle dependent. Walk well out of range, turn around, then approach again from a different direction. This clears a surprising share of "dead NPC" cases where the prompt simply failed to establish.

3

Swap characters or dismount and remount

If the failed interaction involves a mount, dismount fully, move away, and summon it again. Where the game lets you switch between characters, switching away and back re-initialises the interaction target and often restores the prompt.

4

Change area, or reload the area

Cross into a different zone and come back, or enter and leave an interior. A full game restart reloads everything; a local area reload reloads only what needs it, and usually costs you nothing.

5

Save deliberately, then restart

If you have reached this point, restart — but make it a controlled one. Save manually first if the game allows it where you are, and note your position so you can tell whether the reload put you back further than expected.

When a restart is genuinely the answer: the officially listed interaction defect names restarting as the remedy, so do not feel you are giving up by doing it. The point of these five steps is to avoid paying that cost for the cases that were never the bug in the first place.

Interactions With NPCs, Mounts and Objects Stop Working

This is the one still on the official list, and it is the most disruptive of the four because it can strand you mid-quest.

How to recognise it

  • It is selective. Some interactions work and others do not. You might be able to loot a chest but not speak to the quest NPC standing next to it. A total input freeze is a different failure.
  • Nothing appears to be wrong visually. The NPC is there, correctly animated, not stuck in geometry. The game simply declines to engage.
  • It is not tied to one quest step. If an interaction is dead because a quest is not at the right stage, that is progression logic, not this bug. Check whether you have actually reached the point where that interaction should be available before assuming the worst — our Quest Softlock Fix covers the cases where quest state, not a bug, is the blocker.
  • A restart reliably clears it, at least temporarily. That reproducibility is the signature of the listed defect.

Managing it rather than fixing it

Since there is no player-side fix, the practical goal is reducing how much a restart costs you:

Save before quest-critical interactions

Get in the habit of saving before you walk up to a quest NPC or turn in an objective. If the interaction is dead, you lose nothing by restarting.

Do not stack interactions

If you notice one interaction has gone dead, stop and sort it out rather than continuing. Pushing on and hitting a second dead interaction means a restart that rolls back further.

Try the checklist every time

Section 3's first four steps resolve a meaningful share of cases. Run them before each restart rather than going straight to the fifth step.

Note that separate from this bug, Pearl Abyss's Known Issues list also covers cases where a quest-required NPC cannot ride a legendary mount, and where Greymane faction quests cannot progress while playing as Damiane or Oongka. If your problem is specifically "the quest will not advance," check those before assuming a general interaction failure. Our Known Bugs and Fixes page tracks the full set.

The Three Fixes That Shipped in Patch 1.18.02

Patch 1.18.02 landed on 17 August 2026 and closed three separate "stuck" complaints. If you are on the current client, all three are behind you. If you are reading this because you are still experiencing one of them, check your version first — that is the entire fix.

Character unresponsive after using a shop

Symptom: you buy or sell, close the shop interface, and your character stops responding. Movement and actions do nothing, and you are left to force-close the client.

Why it mattered: it hit right at the point where players spend and organise resources, and because the natural response is to quit out, people lost whatever they had done since their last save — including the purchase that triggered it.

Status: fixed in 1.18.02. If this still happens to you on client 1.000.484, it is a different failure and worth reporting with reproduction steps.

Camp resources showing 0 comrades

Symptom: the camp resources interface reports your comrade count as zero even though you have comrades assigned. It is a display defect — the underlying value was not the problem — which is exactly why it was so confusing: nothing else in the game behaved as though your comrades were gone.

Status: fixed in 1.18.02. Any current guide telling you to rebuild your camp over this is working from pre-patch information.

Silver reset after "A Secret Deal"

Symptom: after completing the quest "A Secret Deal", silver is reduced to zero. Per the patch record this affected some saves rather than all, which is why reports were inconsistent and why some players insisted the bug did not exist.

Status: fixed in 1.18.02.

Already lost silver to the old bug? Updating prevents it from happening again, but it will not retroactively restore currency lost before the patch. If the loss happened on a save you care about, check whether you have an earlier save or a cloud backup you can roll back to — our Save Recovery Guide covers what is worth trying.

Evasion Control "Hold" — the Old Dodge Key Still Fires

This one is not a freeze, but it belongs in this group because it produces the same frustrated reaction: the game is not doing what I told it to.

Pearl Abyss's Known Issues list notes that when Evasion Control is set to "Hold", the default dodge key from before rebinding continues to work. You rebind dodge to a key you like, keep Evasion Control on Hold, and both keys trigger the evade — including the old one you thought you had freed up. If you then bound something else to that old key, you get two actions from one press.

How to confirm it

  • Press the old default dodge key on its own. If your character evades, the old binding is still live.
  • Press your new key. If that also evades, you have confirmed both are active.
  • Toggle Evasion Control away from Hold and retest. The behaviour is tied to that mode.

What to do about it

Rebind with the conflict in mind

Open the controls menu and set your dodge binding deliberately, then test every key you expect to be free. Treat any key that still triggers an evade as occupied, because it is. The exact menu path varies by client version, so navigate by setting name.

Avoid parking important actions on the old key

Until this is resolved, do not bind anything you cannot afford to trigger accidentally to the previous default dodge key. Put throwaway or low-stakes actions there instead.

Check the both-keys case on any new scheme

Whenever you redo your controls, verify each binding individually rather than assuming the menu reflects reality. That habit catches this class of problem generally, not just this instance.

For the full control layout and rebinding walkthrough, see our Controls and Keybindings Guide. If you are fighting a binding the game refuses to accept at all, our WASD Rebind Bypass page covers that separate issue.

Confirm Your Client Version and Report Reproducible Cases

Most of this page collapses into one action: be on the current client. Version 1.000.484 (2.00.00) shipped on 25 August 2026 and is newer than the 1.18.02 patch that fixed three of the four problems above.

1

Find your version number

The client version is displayed in the game's options or main menu. The exact location varies by platform and build, so look for a version string rather than following a memorised path.

2

Update if you are below 1.000.484

Enable automatic updates if you have not already. Running behind is the single most common reason players still hit bugs that are months old.

3

Retest the exact scenario

Go back to the specific NPC, shop or quest result that failed and try again. Confirm, do not assume.

4

Report what survives

If the problem persists on the current client, report it with your platform, version, and the shortest sequence that reproduces it. "Interactions stop working after about an hour" is far less actionable than "after dismounting inside a camp, the stable master cannot be spoken to until restart."

One more consideration specific to 2026: Pearl Abyss indicated at Gamescom that the scope and pace of free updates will not remain as consistent as it has been, with resources moving toward paid DLC and additional platforms. That is not a reason to stop reporting bugs, but it is a reason to learn the workarounds in section 4 rather than assuming a fix is weeks away.

FAQ

Why can't I interact with NPCs in Crimson Desert?

It may be the officially listed interaction defect, where interactions with NPCs, mounts and certain objects stop working and restarting is the given remedy. Rule out simpler causes first with the 5-step checklist in section 3.

Is the interaction bug fixed?

No. It remains on Pearl Abyss's Known Issues list as of client 1.000.484 (2.00.00, 25 August 2026). The restart advice in the official note is still current.

Is the shop freeze fixed?

Yes — fixed in patch 1.18.02, released 17 August 2026. If it still happens on the current client, you have a different problem.

My camp says 0 comrades but I have comrades. Is that a real bug?

It was a display defect, fixed in 1.18.02. Updating resolves it. Nothing was actually wrong with your camp.

I lost my silver after A Secret Deal. Can I get it back?

The defect that caused it was fixed in 1.18.02, but the patch does not retroactively restore currency already lost. Check for an earlier save or cloud backup — see our Save Recovery Guide.

Does the silver bug affect every save?

No. The patch record specifies some saves, which is why reports were inconsistent and why some players never saw it.

Why does my old dodge key still work after rebinding?

It is an officially listed issue: with Evasion Control set to Hold, the default dodge key from before rebinding remains active. Rebind deliberately and test each key.

How do I know which of the four problems I have?

Use the master table in section 2. The key question is whether input as a whole is dead or only interactions are.

Will restarting lose my progress?

You will return to your last save. Save manually before restarting where the game allows it, and note your position first.

Should I reinstall the game?

Almost never for these. Three of the four are fixed by updating; the fourth is a confirmed defect with a known workaround. Reinstalling addresses neither.
