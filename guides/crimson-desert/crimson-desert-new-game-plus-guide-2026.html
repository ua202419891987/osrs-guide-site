How New Game Plus Unlocks

Per the official 2.00.00 patch notes (client version 1.000.484, released August 25, 2026), Crimson Desert added a New Game Plus mode that carries over currency after you complete the main story. The condition is explicit in the patch notes and it is worth reading carefully: this is tied to completing the main story, not to reaching the endgame region, not to hitting a level threshold, and not to finishing side content.

The unlock rule in one line: finish the main story campaign → NG+ becomes available → your carried currency and your Abyss Link progress come with you. Side quests are not stated as a requirement.

This phrasing matters because Crimson Desert is a long game with a great deal of optional content, and players have been asking whether they need to 100% the map to qualify. Based on the official wording, they do not. Whether optional completion adds anything to the carryover is not stated — see section 3.

One thing to check before you commit: because the 2.0 update resets skill progression on first launch, your Abyss Link state at the moment you roll credits is the state you bring into NG+. Finish the main story with a build you would be happy to start again with, not an empty tree.

What Officially Carries Over

We are going to be strict here. The official 2.00.00 patch notes name exactly two categories:

  • Abyss Link — the new skill resource system introduced in the same patch.
  • Currency — carried over into the new run.

That is the whole list as far as official confirmation goes. Everything else you may have read elsewhere is either community inference or invention.

Do not plan around unlisted carryovers. If a guide tells you your gear, materials or map progress transfers, that claim is not in the official notes. Spend or convert anything valuable before you roll credits rather than assuming it travels.

The logic behind carrying Abyss Link and currency specifically is coherent: Abyss Link is the roster-level progression thread introduced in 2.0, and currency is the universal resource. Both survive a story reset in a way that gear tied to chapter rewards cannot.

What We Cannot Confirm Yet

NG+ shipped five days before this guide was written. The community has not finished mapping it, and we are not going to guess on your behalf. Open questions as of August 31, 2026:

1. Does difficulty scale in NG+?

The official notes do not mention an NG+ difficulty multiplier, enemy scaling, or a new difficulty tier. Whether enemies hit harder or have more HP is unverified.

2. Does NG+ add exclusive content?

No NG+-exclusive items, bosses or endings are listed in the official notes. Players have not reported any. Treat "NG+ has unique rewards" as unconfirmed.

3. Can you run NG+ more than once?

Whether the mode can be chained (NG++ and beyond) is not stated. Assume a single rollover until confirmed.

4. Does carrying Abyss Link interact with the skill wipe?

The 2.0 wipe reset skill progress and refunded Abyss Artifacts in full. How Abyss Link carryover interacts with a rebuilt tree is not documented.

5. Is there an achievement or trophy tied to it?

Not mentioned in the official notes. See our Achievements Guide for what is currently documented.

We will update this section as reliable community data appears. If you finish a run and can answer any of the five, that information is genuinely scarce right now.

What to Do First in NG+

Assuming you have cleared the main story and rolled into NG+, here is the order that makes the carryover pay off.

1

Spend your carried currency deliberately

Currency is one of only two confirmed carryovers, which makes it your single biggest NG+ advantage. Convert it into whatever the early game is short of — materials, upgrades, consumables — rather than sitting on it.

2

Check your Abyss Link state before buying anything

Open the skill menu on each character. Since 2.0, HP, stamina and spirit are no longer shared between Kliff, Damiane and Oongka (official), so each character's menu must be read separately.

3

Re-establish survivability before damage

Early NG+ content is still lethal and your pools are per-character now. Pool nodes first, damage second. Full ordering in our 2.0 Skill Reset Rebuild Guide.

4

Do not skip the tutorial region

Even with a strong carryover, NG+ is a fresh story run. The early chapters are where you re-establish your economy.

5

Route differently the second time

The main value of a second run is seeing the content you skipped. If you are going to rerun, rerun differently.

If you have not yet finished the story, the endgame work you should be doing instead is covered in our Endgame Guide and the Post-Game Guide.

NG+ and the 2.0 Skill Wipe

These two systems arrived in the same patch and interact in a way that catches people out. Per the official notes, all existing skill progress was reset on your first launch after updating, with spent Abyss Artifacts refunded in full. If you were mid-campaign on August 25, you woke up to an empty tree.

The consequence for NG+ planning: your Abyss Link state going into NG+ is whatever you rebuilt after that wipe. If you rolled credits on an empty or half-finished tree, you carried a half-finished Abyss Link into your second run.

Practical advice: before you finish the main story, spend the time to rebuild your main character properly. It costs you an hour now and it is the difference between a strong NG+ start and a weak one. Rebuild order is in our Best Skills to Unlock First guide.

There is also good news buried in the patch: skill progression is now reset per character. In an NG+ context that means you can rebuild Damiane for a forced encounter without touching Kliff — useful, because players report that the Chapter 8 Lucian Bastier fight forces Damiane while most artifact investment usually sits on Kliff.

Is NG+ Worth Starting?

Honest answer: it depends entirely on whether you still have unfinished business in your current save.

Your situationVerdictReason
Finished story, skipped most side contentStay in your saveNG+ only carries Abyss Link and currency; it does not help you see what you skipped
Finished story, did nearly everythingStart NG+Little left to lose and you keep your economy
Mid-campaignWaitNG+ requires the full main story
Burned out on the endgame grindStart NG+A fresh story with a carried economy removes most of the early friction
Chasing achievementsFinish current save firstNo NG+-exclusive achievements are documented

The strongest argument for NG+ is specific: Crimson Desert's early and mid game have real friction — limited healing (food only, no potions), punishing boss checks, and a gear curve that punishes falling behind. Starting a second run with carried currency removes most of that friction and lets you actually enjoy the story at pace.

The strongest argument against: players report that enemies do not respawn and cleared outposts stay permanently empty, which leaves long-running saves with little to fight. If your complaint is "there is nothing left to do," NG+ solves it by resetting the story — but it also resets your world state, so anything unconfirmed on the carryover list is gone.

Who Should Skip It

Three groups should not roll into NG+ yet.

1. Players mid-collection

Only Abyss Link and currency are confirmed to carry. If you are partway through collectibles, cards or treasure maps, finish those first — our Collectibles Guide covers what is still outstanding.

2. Players still on unconfirmed carryovers

If your plan depends on keeping gear, materials or map progress, you are planning around something the patch notes never said. Verify in-game or finish the content in your current save.

3. Players who have not rebuilt after the wipe

Rolling into NG+ with a half-rebuilt tree wastes the one advantage the mode gives you. Rebuild first — see the rebuild guide.

Longevity note: Pearl Abyss signalled at Gamescom 2026 that free updates will slow as resources shift to paid DLC, so 2.0 is likely the stable state of the game for months. NG+ is not going anywhere — there is no rush.

Once you do start, the most useful companion reading is our Abyss Link System Guide for exactly what the carried resource is, and our patch notes analysis for everything else 2.0 changed.

FAQ

How do I unlock New Game Plus in Crimson Desert?

Per the official 2.00.00 patch notes, NG+ becomes available after you complete the main story. Side content is not stated as a requirement.

What carries over into NG+?

Officially, Abyss Link and currency. Both are named in the 2.00.00 patch notes. No other category is confirmed.

Do my weapons and armour carry over?

Not stated in the official notes. The community is still verifying — do not assume gear transfers.

Does NG+ get harder?

The official notes do not mention a difficulty scale, enemy HP increase or new tier. This is unverified.

Can I do NG+ more than once?

Chaining NG+ into NG++ is not stated in the official notes. Assume a single rollover until confirmed.

Does the 2.0 skill wipe affect NG+?

Your Abyss Link state going into NG+ is whatever you rebuilt after the wipe. Rebuild your main character before rolling credits.

No NG+-exclusive items, bosses or achievements are listed in the official patch notes, and none have been corroborated by the community.

Is NG+ worth it if I am burned out?
