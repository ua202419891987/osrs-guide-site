Tempoross is a skilling boss that gives dual XP in Fishing + Firemaking. This 2026 guide covers the best Tempoross strategy, reward shop unlocks, and loot table.

What is Tempoross?

  • Tempoross is a Fishing/Firemaking skilling boss released in 2021
  • Dual XP: You get Fishing XP (catching fish) + Firemaking XP (burning them to damage Tempoross)
  • Location: Sea of Souls (south of the Fishing Guild, use Spirit Tree or Fairy Ring (code: AIQ))
  • Requirements: 35 Fishing (recommended 65+ for best XP rates)

💰 Best money making: ~150-350K GP/hr (depending on Fishing level) + Tempoross reward shop unlocks.

Requirements & Unlocks

Skill Requirements

  • 35 Fishing (minimum to catch raw fish for Tempoross)
  • 35 Firemaking (to burn fish and damage Tempoross)
  • 65 Fishing (recommended for Raw Anglerfish — best catch rate)

Quest Requirements

  • None! Tempoross is quest-locked (you just need to talk to the Spirit Angler to start)

Tempoross Fight Walkthrough

Phase 1: Filling the Cauldron (Fishing Phase)

  • Enter the Sea of Souls (talk to the Spirit Angler to start a run)
  • Catch raw fish from the Fishing spots (3 spots around the arena)
  • Each fish caught goes into the Cauldron (fills up Tempoross's heat meter)
  • XP per fish: ~70-150 Fishing XP per catch (scales with Fishing level)

Phase 2: Burning Fish (Firemaking Phase)

  • Once the Cauldron is full, burn the raw fish on the Fire of Souls (center of arena)
  • Each fish burned deals damage to Tempoross (20-40 damage per fish, scales with Firemaking level)
  • XP per fish: ~120-200 Firemaking XP per burn
  • Goal: Burn enough fish to deplete Tempoross's HP bar (~250 HP per kill)

Phase 3: Loot & Repeat

  • When Tempoross's HP reaches 0, loot the reward pool (3 items max per kill)
  • Reward currency: Tempoross Points (spent at the Spirit Angler's reward shop)
  • Restart: Talk to the Spirit Angler again to start another run (instant respawn)

Best Tempoross Strategy (2026)

Gear Setup

  • Fishing: Bring your best Fishing rod (Stronghold rod is best, but normal rod works fine)
  • Inventory: 20-24 raw fish (catch them during the fight, don't bring from outside) + 1-2 food (Lobster/Shark, in case you get hit by Tempoross's attacks)
  • Recommended: Fish Barrel (unlocked from Tempoross reward shop, stores up to 50 raw fish)

Optimized Route (Fastest Kills)

  1. Start fight → catch fish from all 3 fishing spots (don't stay at one spot)
  2. Fill Cauldron to 100% → start burning fish immediately
  3. Alternate between catching more fish and burning them (keep Cauldron full at all times)
  4. Kill Tempoross in ~2-3 mins per run (with 65+ Fishing)

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I AFK Tempoross?

No. Tempoross requires active clicking (catching + burning fish). However, the Attas crystal (Farming) can extend the time between clicks to ~30 seconds.

What's the Fastest Way to 99 Fishing?

Tempoross (65-99) is fastest (40-55K XP/hr). Alternative: Dark crabs (82 Fishing, 35K XP/hr, profitable) or Monkfish (62 Fishing, 30K XP/hr, profitable).

Is Tempoross Members-Only?

Yes. You need Membership (Bond) to access Tempoross. F2P players can only fish Lobsters (40 Fishing) or Swordfish (50 Fishing).
