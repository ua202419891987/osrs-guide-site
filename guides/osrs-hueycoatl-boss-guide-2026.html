1. What Is the Hueycoatl

The Hueycoatl (pronounced HWAY-koh-at-el) is a draconic boss coiled around the Darkfrost mountain in Varlamore. She is one of the most accessible "serious" money makers in the game because her HP does not scale with group size — a busy mass world lets a low-level player tag segments, stay alive, and still roll for the unique drop table based on personal damage dealt.

① Why Huey is a standout boss

🎯 The appeal

  • Beginner-friendly: No hard stat requirement; you get a warning below 85 combat / 43 Prayer but can still enter.
  • Flexible group size: Solo to 20 players — bring friends or hop a mass world for free.
  • Strong drops: The Dragon hunter wand and Tome of earth are genuinely useful, high-value uniques.
  • Forgiving: Dying sends your grave to the base camp; you are never "locked out."
💡 Ironman note

Ironmen are eligible for drops when fought in a group, which makes Hueycoatl one of the best early group bosses for an Ironman account building toward end-game gear.

🤝 Your first real group-PvM lesson

Hueycoatl is also the gentlest on-ramp into group PvM culture in the game. Because the fight never punishes low damage dealers, mass worlds are where countless players first learn boss habits under pressure: switching protection prayers against the Rain Fire projectiles, repositioning off glowing lightning symbols, and coordinating the three-pillar brazier buff with strangers. Those are exactly the habits every later solo boss expects, so a handful of Huey sessions both funds your upgrades and sharpens your fundamentals before you graduate to instanced content. Because her HP does not scale with group size, even a heavily out-geared player contributes meaningful damage on a mass world, which is precisely why she remains one of the most popular entry bosses years after release.

2. How to Access the Darkfrost & Start the Fight

Reaching the Hueycoatl is quick once Varlamore is open. Here is the exact path.

② Requirements

NPCSpeak to Taala at the Darkfrost base camp to unlock the fight.
MembersVarlamore is members-only content.
Recommended85+ combat, 43 Prayer for protection prayers, 70+ for Piety, 76+ for Thralls.

③ Getting to the base camp

Fastest routes

  • Pendant of Ates: Teleport to the Darkfrost after activating the Statue of Ates just west of the base camp (requires The Heart of Darkness for the pendant).
  • Quetzal Transport: Use the Quetzal network to Quetzacalli Gorge, then run north-east through the gate and north-west around the mountain.
  • Meeting flag: At the base camp, the meeting flag next to the bank buffalo lets you create a private instance for 50,000 GP (25,000 with hard CA).
💡 Find a team

The in-game "Huey Host" chat channel is the easiest way to find small teams. World 420 is unofficially popular for mass kills if you just want to tag along and roll drops.

3. Fight Phases — Body, Head, Tail & Final Stand

The Hueycoatl is fought in four phases. Understanding the flow is the difference between a clean kill and a frustrating one.

④ Phase 1 — Body segments (no attack)

You start by damaging five exposed body segments (250 HP each) at the mountain's base. They do not attack — just DPS them down while avoiding glowing symbols and flicking protection prayers. Tip: entering after phase 1 is cleared drastically reduces your contribution, so start early. Each segment has a 1x1 hitbox that can be awkward to click; use shift + "Tag-All" (RuneLite) or "Mark object" to reveal hitboxes.

⑤ Phase 2 — Head & braziers

Once the path opens, attack the head (2,500 HP) directly. A seer charges three pillars tied to protection prayers: Blue = Protect from Magic, Red = Protect from Melee, Green = Protect from Missiles. In groups of three or more, balancing these prayers lets the seer light the braziers for a +30% team damage bonus that persists as long as the braziers stay lit.

⑥ Phase 3 — Shielded tail

At half health she shields and drops into a tail phase. The tail has a per-hit damage cap of 4 (or 9 if your crush attack bonus is your highest), making this the longest phase for small teams. Break the tail to drop the shield and resume the head damage. Each tail shift triggers a slam shockwave of ~25 damage if not evaded.

⑦ Phase 4 — Final stand

With the shield gone, she resumes standard attacks (glowing symbols + prayer projectiles) at increased frequency, now also using the tail-slam shockwave. Push her down for the kill.

🎯 Total damage check

4,050 total HP (5×250 body + 2,500 head + 300 tail). Because HP does not scale with players, every extra DPS shortens the kill for the whole group.

4. Core Mechanics: Glowing Symbols, Prayer Projectiles & Braziers

Two attacks define the entire fight. Master these and Huey becomes routine.

⑧ Glowing symbols (lightning)

Glowing floor symbols appear near players. After 4 ticks, lightning strikes any player still standing on them for up to 16 damage. Simply step off the marked tiles before they discharge.

⑨ Rain Fire — prayer projectiles

Projectile colourPrayer to useDamage if correctly prayed
Red (melee, smoking tail end)Protect from Melee0–2
Green (ranged, no animation)Protect from Missiles0–2
Blue (magic, spinning)Protect from Magic0–2
Any, if prayed wrong—Up to 14

Correctly flicking the right protection prayer reduces the hit to 0–2; ignoring it deals up to 14. This is why 43 Prayer is the practical floor.

⚠️ Drop qualification thresholds

To qualify for drops you must deal a minimum of ~75 damage to the head or tail. If you deal less than ~25 damage to the body, your rewards are reduced by 95% (and the pet chance drops with it). Always contribute to the body phase.

5. Gear Setups — Earth Magic, Ranged & Crush Melee

Huey is 60% weak to Earth spells and vulnerable to dragonbane across all parts, so three styles all work. Pick the one matching your bank.

⑩ Magic (best DPS)

🌍 Earth spell build

  • Weapon: Dragon hunter wand (magic dragonbane) — a direct Huey drop and her best-in-slot here.
  • Book: Tome of earth (also a Huey drop) boosting Earth spells.
  • Spell: Any Earth spell; pair with Thralls (76 Magic) for free extra damage.
  • Armour: Ancestral / Virtus, Magus ring.

⑪ Ranged (dragonbane)

🏹 Dragonbane ranged

Use a Dragon hunter crossbow or dragonbane bolts with your best ranged armour. Strong and lower-cost than a full magic setup, and dragonbane keeps DPS competitive.

⑫ Melee (crush)

⚔️ Crush matters for the tail

A Dragon hunter lance (or Scythe of Vitur) is ideal — crush raises the tail's damage cap from 4 to 9, dramatically shortening phase 3. Inquisitor's set amplifies crush further. For the tail phase specifically, a fast multi-hit weapon helps break it quickly.

💡 Inventory baseline

Divine super combat (or super combat) potion, prayer potions / restores (+ Holy wrench if owned), food (sharks or anglerfish), and a spec weapon for the tail. Bring thrall runes + Book of the Dead if using magic.

6. Drops, Profit & Drop Rates

Rewards are a lump split by personal damage; the MVP (most damage) gets a 10% share boost. Multiple players independently roll uniques from one kill.

⑬ Unique table

There is roughly a 1/18 chance per kill at the unique table, scaled by your personal contribution and individual to each player — so even taggers can hit it.

⑭ Profit

💰 Money making

  • Solo: around 1.8 million GP/hr (wiki estimates range about 1.8–2.0 million) at around 7 kills/hr.
  • Mass world: Lower per-kill effort; you trade raw GP for safety and a shot at uniques.
  • Big bones: Dropped only for the MVP — a small bonus.

7. Solo vs Mass World — Which Should You Pick

⑮ Choose solo / private if…

Solo or private instance

  • You have solid DPS and want maximum GP/hr.
  • You can comfortably handle all four phases alone (or with 1–2 friends).
  • You want full control of the fight pace and drop qualification.
  • Cost: 50,000 GP per private instance (25,000 with hard CA).

⑯ Choose a mass world if…

Mass world (free, safe, beginner-friendly)

  • You are new, low-level, or just learning the mechanics.
  • You want to roll uniques by tagging without the pressure of solo survival.
  • You don't mind lower per-hour GP in exchange for near-zero risk.
  • Just hop World 420 or use the Huey Host channel and follow the crowd.

8. Hueycoatl FAQs

Q: What are the requirements to fight the Hueycoatl?

Complete the short quest Children of the Sun and speak to Taala at the Darkfrost base camp. There is no hard combat gate, though you get a warning below 85 combat and 43 Prayer.

Q: What is the Hueycoatl weak to?

She is 60% weak to Earth spells, and her body, head, and tail are all vulnerable to dragonbane weapons. Crush attack bonus also raises the tail's damage cap, speeding phase 3.

Q: How much HP does the Hueycoatl have?

4,050 total: five 250-HP body segments, a 2,500-HP head, and a 300-HP tail. Critically, this total does not scale with the number of players, which is why mass worlds are so efficient.

Q: How do I get the +30% damage bonus?

In groups of three or more, balance the three protection prayers (Blue = Magic, Red = Melee, Green = Missiles) so the seer lights the braziers. The bonus stays active as long as the braziers remain lit.

Q: What are the best drops?

The headline uniques are the Dragon hunter wand (magic dragonbane weapon) and the Tome of earth (Earth spell boost), plus Hueycoatl hide for armour. There is roughly a 1/18 unique roll per kill, scaled by your contribution.

Q: Is the Hueycoatl good money for Ironmen?

Yes. Ironmen are eligible for drops in group fights, making Huey one of the best early group bosses for an Ironman building toward end-game magic gear.

Q: Should I solo or join a mass world?

Solo (or a private instance) maximizes GP/hr if you have the DPS and survivability. Mass worlds are free, safe, and beginner-friendly — ideal for learning or just rolling for uniques by tagging.

Q: What happens if I die to the Hueycoatl?

Your grave appears at the base camp. The fight itself is a safe death outside the Wilderness, so you keep your items — only the lost time matters. (Entering via a Wilderness route would change that, but the Darkfrost is not in the Wilderness.)
