2. New Quest: Fallen From Grace

Confirmed Details

Name: Fallen From Grace (confirmed)

Skill requirements: 62 Sailing + 60 Crafting + 47 Runecraft + 53 Mining

Story: Begins in the village of Auchrie, where a disappearance has left locals fearful of outsiders. You investigate the mystery of the Mad Angel and the truth behind the island's disappearances. (Source: Jagex blog)

Rewards: Unlocks Mortimer (Slayer Master), Golem Crafting, Goat Hunting, and repeatable access to The Mad Angel at the Cathedral. Also grants new teleports (necklace of passage & slayer ring) per the polled rewards.

Speculation — Difficulty & Length: Based on similar island-unlock quests (Client of Kourend, The Fremennik Exiles), we expect Medium-to-Experienced difficulty and roughly 30–60 minutes of playtime. Full walkthrough and puzzle solutions will be published after July 29. This is not yet confirmed.

3. New Skilling Activities

3.1 Goat Hunting (Hunter)

Confirmed

Jagex confirmed Goat Hunting is a new Hunter activity on Wyrmscraig for players between 60 and 80 Hunter.

Requirements: 60 Hunter + completion of the Sheep Herder quest.

How it works (confirmed): Lure unruly mountain goats into a pit — drive them in with a cattle prod, or use Telekinetic Grab / Dark Lure (also grants a little Magic XP).

Confirmed drops: Goat Horns (a supply of Goat Horn Dust for Combat Potions) and Goat Fur (used to clothe Golems in Golem Crafting). A pet, Mr McGroot, is also a polled reward. (Source: Jagex blog & Unique Rewards blog)

Speculation — XP Rates & Best Method: Exact XP rates and the most efficient way to herd goats are not published. We expect it to be a strong 60–80 Hunter method; full rates and a step-by-step loop will be added after July 29.

3.2 Golem Crafting

Confirmed

Jagex confirmed Golem Crafting is a new Crafting method — an on-demand alternative to Glassblowing, with gameplay similar to Vale Totems.

Requires: Completion of Fallen From Grace, plus Hunter Furs as a core material (stock up before launch!). Higher-quality furs need a higher Crafting level to use.

Headline reward: The Jeweller's Chisel (untradeable) — a chance to cut two gems at once, with guaranteed success when cutting Opals (from 62), Jades (from 71) and Topaz (from 76), plus a 20% better success rate on those three. (Source: Jagex blog & Unique Rewards blog, Jul 6 2026)

Style: Built for both relaxed and active play. Pay attention to your clicks and you can beat Glassblowing XP; a relaxed approach is similar to or slightly below Glassblowing.

Speculation — Exact Process & XP Rates: The full step-by-step crafting loop (mining Sunstones, carving, clothing with furs) and exact XP rates are not yet published in final form. A complete Golem Crafting guide will replace this box after July 29.

4. Mortimer — New Slayer Master

Confirmed

Name: Mortimer

Location: Wyrmscraig Caverns (accessed after partial completion of Fallen From Grace)

Unlock: Reachable during the quest, not only after it

How it works: Instead of one assigned task, you choose between 2–3 tasks, each with its own modifier (a twist that makes every choice interesting).

Significance: Old School's first new Slayer Master in around seven years. (Source: Jagex blog)

Speculation — Task List, Levels & Points: Mortimer's exact Slayer level requirement, full task table (which monsters, weights) and how modifiers interact with points are not yet published. We expect a fresh, choice-driven alternative to Duradel/Konar. A full Mortimer breakdown with task weights and modifier details will be added after July 29.

5. The Mad Angel — New Solo Boss

Confirmed

Name: The Mad Angel

Type: Repeatable, mid-level PvM encounter (great next step after a Fire Cape)

Location: The Cathedral on Wyrmscraig (after completing Fallen From Grace)

Unlock: Completing the quest unlocks the repeatable version you first meet during the story

Signature drop — Hallowfell: A two-handed sword needing 75 Attack. On a hit it cleaves up to 2 additional adjacent enemies (the three tiles in front and two tiles beside you) for up to half your max hit — strong on packed Slayer tasks, weaker than dedicated singles weapons vs a lone target. (Source: Jagex blog)

Speculation — Fight Strategy & Drop Rates: The Mad Angel's attack styles, defences, and exact drop rates (including the Hallowfell rate) are not published. We expect a Fire-Cape-tier fight; full mechanics, gear setups and a drop-rate table will replace this box after July 29.

6. Preparation — What You Can Do Now (Confirmed Methods)

Everything in this section uses current, released game mechanics — you can do all of it today. None of it is speculation.

6.1 Get 62 Sailing (Hard Gate)

62 Sailing is mandatory just to dock. If you're short, start now — Sailing is already released, so train it with live methods:

StepWhat to do (current game)
1–10Complete the Sailing tutorial and early intro voyages
10–50Port tasks / trade routes from accessible harbours; tick off Sailing-friendly quests
50–62Barracuda Trials and higher-tier voyages (see our Sailing 1–62 guide for the fastest current route)

Don't skip this: you also need 62 Sailing for the Fallen From Grace quest itself.

6.2 Hit 60 Crafting

Confirmed quest requirement. Reliable current routes:

  • Quests (fast, free XP): Family Crest, The Fremennik Exiles, Making Friends with My Arm, Rum Deal, Recipe for Disaster (Lumbridge Guide sub-quest), Enlightened Journey.
  • Active training: Glassblowing (blowing glass), cutting gems, or dragonhide bodies once you're higher.
  • Goal: Reach 60 so the quest is unlocked on day one.

6.3 Hit 47 Runecraft

Confirmed quest requirement. Current best routes:

  • Guardians of the Rift (unlocked at 27 RC) — the fastest, most popular modern method; great AFK-ish and no bank running.
  • Ourania (ZMI) Altar — strong all-rounder from ~45 RC; use a pure-essence stash and a teleport.
  • Classic altars (e.g., Nature, Cosmics) if you prefer traditional running.

6.4 Hit 53 Mining

Confirmed quest requirement. Current best routes:

  • Motherlode Mine (from 30 Mining) — excellent AFK, no banking runs, good for 30→53+.
  • 3-tick granite (Desert Quarry / quarry) for faster, more active training.
  • Volcanic Mine for a different pace once 50+.

6.5 Complete Sheep Herder (for Goat Hunting)

Goat Hunting needs completion of the Sheep Herder quest (low requirements — a short, member-only quest with no skill levels needed). Do it now so you can start Goat Hunting immediately on July 29.

6.6 Stockpile Hunter Furs (for Golem Crafting)

Golem Crafting requires Hunter Furs, and Jagex has confirmed it's reviewing shop stock so Hunter remains the primary source. Bank them now while they're cheap:

  • Kebbit furs — Sabre-toothed kebbit, Barb-tailed kebbit, Polar kebbit (Polar kebbit fur).
  • Bear fur (from Arctic bears) and other Hunter-sourced furs.
  • Higher-quality furs will need a higher Crafting level to use, so gather a spread of qualities.
Speculation — How many? Exact fur quantities per Golem are not published. Banking a few hundred kebbit/bear furs is a safe, low-risk prep. Final numbers land after July 29.

6.7 Combat Readiness (Optional but Smart)

The Mad Angel is pitched at Fire-Cape level. If you don't have a Fire Cape yet, consider working toward it — it's the stated difficulty benchmark. This is advice, not a hard gate.
