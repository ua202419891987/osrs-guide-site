OSRS Best Melee Gear Progression 2026 — From Rune Scimitar to Scythe of Vitur

🆕 NEW
Published October 9, 2026  |  23 min read  |  By Yanchun Wu

Melee is the most balanced combat style to gear because the upgrades are spread across many affordable slots — but that also makes the upgrade order easy to get wrong. Spend on Bandos when you should be saving for a Scythe, and you stall your DPS for months. This 2026 progression walks every tier from the rune scimitar to the Scythe of Vitur, with the exact order that maximizes damage per gold and the crush versus slash switches that separate good melee accounts from great ones.

1. Why Melee Gear Order Matters

Melee damage comes from your weapon's attack and strength bonuses plus a stack of smaller armor bonuses. Unlike ranged and magic, where one weapon dominates, melee spreads its power across the weapon, the amulet, the boots, the gloves, and the defender. That means a smart upgrade order lets you reach near-bis DPS through a series of cheap, bite-sized purchases instead of one giant sink.

This guide ranks upgrades by damage per gold so you never park millions in a marginal slot while a game-changing one waits.

① What "progression" means here

🎯 The mental model

  • Weapon sets the floor: Your scimitar or Scythe defines your base; armor raises it afterward.
  • Bandos is the body: Bandos chest + tassets is bis for the entire mid and late game until Torva.
  • Torture + Primordial: The amulet and boots are the best non-weapon damage items per gold.
  • Ironman-aware: The best melee gear is self-obtainable, so you can reach near-bis without the Grand Exchange.
💡 The Scythe is a group-boss weapon

The Scythe of Vitur's special attack hits three times and is devastating against high-health bosses like the Theatre of Blood and Tombs of Amascut. Against a single small target a cheap weapon with the right armor often matches it. Buy the Scythe when you are doing group end-game content, not before.

2. Early Game (1–70 Attack): Scimitar and the First Real Weapon

Early melee is about unlocking the dragon scimitar and getting 70 Attack and Strength. You train, quest, and do easy bosses; you are not chasing DPS yet.

② The starter loadout

③ The first real weapon: Dragon Scimitar

✅ Your first power spike

The Dragon scimitar (from Monkey Madness) is the best pre-70 weapon and carries you through the entire early game and most mid-game Slayer. Pair it with a Fighter torso, Dragon boots, and an Amulet of strength and you have a strong, near-free melee kit that bosses effectively until Bandos.

⚠️ Don't over-spend on a whip early

The Abyssal whip is faster but only marginally stronger than the dragon scimitar for most content, and it lacks a strength bonus. The dragon scimitar is the smarter early buy; move to Bandos and the Scythe before you worry about the whip.

3. Mid Game (70–80): Bandos, Fighter Torso, and the Core

The mid game is where melee gets good. One purchase defines it: Bandos. Buy the chest and tassets before anything else — they are the bis melee body until Torva and they last for hundreds of hours.

④ The mid-game pillars

⚔️ The loadout that carries you for the whole mid game

  • Bandos chestplate + tassets: The bis melee body. Drops from General Graardor (God Wars Dungeon) or is tradeable.
  • Fighter torso: Free chest alternative that nearly matches Bandos strength if you cannot afford the chest yet.
  • Dragon boots: A strong, cheap strength boot that holds until Primordial.
  • Berserker ring: A strong strength ring; imbue it later for a clear bonus.
  • Fire cape: The best mid-game cape and a big strength boost over the obsidian cape.

⑤ Supporting slots

🛡️ Fill these alongside the pillars

Amulet of torture is the bis strength amulet and a top-three upgrade overall. Primordial boots replace Dragon boots as bis. Ferocious gloves beat Barrows gloves for strength. Avernic defender replaces the Dragon defender as the bis off-hand. With Bandos + torture + primordial + berserker ring (i) + fire cape you are already near the top of the melee ladder.

💡 BIS-adjacent before BIS

You do not need Torva or the Scythe to clear most of the game. The Bandos + torture + primordial core handles Zulrah, Vorkath, Muspah, the Moons of Peril, and the majority of Slayer comfortably. Save Torva and the Scythe for when you are doing group end-game raids.

4. Late Game (80–90): Torture, Primordial, Ferocious, Avernic

Once you have Bandos, the late game is about completing the jewelry, boots, gloves, and defender. Each is affordable and worth more than it costs.

⑥ The late-game damage items

BootsPrimordial bootsBis melee boots; a clear strength upgrade.
GlovesFerocious glovesBis strength gloves; better than Barrows.
Off-handAvernic defenderBis defender; a solid strength and defence boost.

⑦ The free DPS: Piety

✅ The highest value "purchase" is a prayer

Piety (unlocked at 70 Prayer) adds a large strength and defence bonus that outperforms most armor swaps and costs nothing but a few quests. If you have not unlocked it, do so before buying another gear piece — it is the most efficient melee DPS upgrade available.

5. End-Game BIS: Scythe of Vitur and the Full Max Setup

The full max melee setup is the strongest melee kit in the game and is built from high-level PvM. Here is the complete bis list (all-round, slash/stab focused).

⑧ The max melee loadout

👑 Best-in-slot melee (all-round)

  • Weapon: Scythe of Vitur (bis group-boss DPS) or a cheaper weapon for small targets.
  • Body: Torva platebody.
  • Legs: Torva platelegs.
  • Boots: Primordial boots.
  • Necklace: Amulet of torture.
  • Ring: Berserker ring (i).
  • Cape: Infernal cape.
  • Off-hand: Avernic defender.
  • Gloves: Ferocious gloves.
  • Head: Slayer helmet (i) on-task, or Dwarven helmet / Neitiznot for general use.
  • Prayer: Piety active.
💡 Torva vs Inquisitor's

Torva is the all-round bis melee body. For content where the enemy is weak to crush (many end-game bosses), the Inquisitor's armour set and Inquisitor's mace out-damage Torva. Carry both and switch by attack style — that is what max melee players do.

6. Crush vs Slash vs Stab: When to Switch

Melee has three attack styles, and using the wrong one can cut your accuracy and damage dramatically. Learning to switch is the difference between a good melee account and a great one.

⑨ The style cheat-sheet

🎯 Match the style to the enemy

  • Slash: Most common; the scimitar, Scythe, and Torva favour it. Use it against the majority of enemies.
  • Crush: Against crush-weak bosses (ToB, CoX guardians, many raid mobs) switch to Inquisitor's armour + Inquisitor's mace for a large gain.
  • Stab: Strong against a handful of specific enemies (e.g., certain daggers and the Zamorakian hasta scenarios); niche but worth knowing.
⚠️ Style matters more than gear tier

A Bandos-wearing player using the wrong attack style can underperform a naked player using the right one. Always check the enemy's defensive weakness (the OSRS wiki lists it) before committing to a long boss grind.

7. Budget Swaps and Ironman Self-Sufficiency

You do not need the full bis to be effective. These swaps keep you competitive for a fraction of the cost, and most are obtainable without the Grand Exchange.

⑩ Cheaper but strong

💰 Stretch your gold

  • Fighter torso instead of Bandos chest — nearly the same strength for free.
  • Obsidian platelegs instead of Bandos tassets early.
  • Amulet of fury / glory instead of torture until you can afford it.
  • Dragon boots instead of Primordial for a long time.
  • Barrows gloves instead of Ferocious; Dragon defender instead of Avernic early.
⚠️ Ironman note

Every core melee piece is self-obtainable: Bandos (General Graardor), Primordial (Cerberus / CoX), torture (CoX), Ferocious (CoX), Avernic (CoX), and the Scythe (CoX / ToB) are all drop-based. An Ironman can assemble a near-bis melee kit without the Grand Exchange, which makes melee a strong first max-combat style for self-sufficient accounts.

8. The Exact Upgrade Order (Spend-Smart Path)

If you have a fixed budget, buy in this order. Each step is the highest damage-per-gold upgrade available at that point.

⑪ The list

🎯 Your melee upgrade roadmap

→ Step 1: Dragon scimitar + Fighter torso + Dragon boots (bossing start).
→ Step 2: Unlock Piety (70 Prayer) — free DPS.
→ Step 3: Bandos chest + tassets.
→ Step 4: Amulet of torture, then Berserker ring (i), then Primordial boots.
→ Step 5: Ferocious gloves + Avernic defender + Infernal cape.
→ Step 6: Inquisitor's set (for crush content).
→ Step 7: Scythe of Vitur + Torva (the final, biggest spikes).
💡 Why the Scythe is last

It is the biggest upgrade, but it is also the most expensive and the least necessary for the content that funds it. You will earn the Scythe faster by bossing efficiently with the steps above than by saving for it from scratch. Buy the cheap DPS first, let it pay for the expensive DPS.

10. Common Melee Mistakes That Drain Your Gold

⑫ Bandos before the Fighter Torso

The Torso is free from Barbarian Assault and gives almost the same strength bonus. Buying Bandos first wastes ~45M for a marginal gain.

⑬ Scythe before you can wield it

The Scythe needs 75 Attack and 75 Defence. Until then a whip, Blade of Saeldor, or even a chaotic weapon outperforms it. Do not buy the Scythe at 70 stats.

⑭ Wrong strength order

Players chase big armor before the cheap wins: Amulet of Fury → Torture, and a regular Berserker Ring → Berserker (i). Those two rings/ammies beat a chestplate for DPS per gold.

⑮ Overlooking Void

Void Melee is exceptional at low stats and is essentially free. Many players buy Bandos while their Void set would already be the better choice at their level.

⑯ Forgetting Piety

Piety is a free ~20% melee boost from prayer. It out-values most single gear pieces and costs only 70 Prayer.

⑰ The Inquisitor's trap

Inquisitor's is only best-in-slot for crush style. Against slash or stab bosses it is worse than Bandos. Do not wear it everywhere.

11. Melee Gear Progression FAQs

Q: Is the Scythe of Vitur worth it if I already have Bandos and a whip?

For group end-game bosses, yes — its three-hit special attack is unmatched. Against a single small target a well-geared Bandos setup with a cheaper weapon is competitive, so the Scythe is most valuable once you are raiding.

Q: What is the single best first upgrade after the dragon scimitar?

Bandos chest + tassets. They are the bis melee body until Torva and they last for the entire mid and late game. Buy them before any jewelry or boots.

Q: Do I need Piety to raid?

Practically yes. Piety is expected at every high-level raid and it adds more DPS than most gear swaps. Unlock it (70 Prayer, a few quests) before you start chasing bis armor.

Q: Bandos or Torva?

Bandos is the standard body for the mid and late game; Torva is bis all-round for the end game. For crush-only content Inquisitor's beats both. For 99 percent of players Bandos carries them until Torva.

Q: Can an Ironman get full melee bis without the Grand Exchange?

Yes. Bandos (Graardor), Primordial (Cerberus / CoX), torture (CoX), Ferocious (CoX), Avernic (CoX), and the Scythe (CoX / ToB) are all self-obtainable. Melee is one of the most Ironman-friendly max-combat styles.

Q: Which attack style should I use?

Slash for most enemies, crush (with Inquisitor's) for crush-weak bosses, and stab only for specific weaknesses. Always check the enemy's defensive weakness before a long grind.

Q: Are Barrows gloves good enough?

Barrows gloves are a strong, cheap all-round glove and fine for the mid game. Ferocious gloves are bis for strength and worth buying when you can afford them, but Barrows will not hold you back early.

Q: How much does a near-bis melee kit cost on the Grand Exchange?

Bandos + torture + primordial + berserker ring (i) + ferocious + avernic typically runs in the tens of millions of gold, while Torva and the Scythe are hundreds of millions combined. The mid-game core is affordable; the end-game is the gold sink.
