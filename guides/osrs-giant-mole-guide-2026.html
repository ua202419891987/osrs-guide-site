OSRS Giant Mole Guide 2026 — The Falador Lair Boss

Novice

The Giant Mole is the most beginner-friendly boss in OSRS — a level 230 boss in a safe instanced lair under Falador. The secret to fast kills is the Falador Hard Diary: it makes her claws and skins drop noted and adds a mole locator to your Falador shield. Pair that with her new 50% weakness to Earth spells (since 6 August 2025) and the Mole becomes a top-tier low-requirement money maker.

⏱️ Quick Summary — 30-Second Read

  • 📌 Falador Hard Diary is mandatory: noted mole claw/skin + a mole locator on your Falador shield (3/4). Without it you waste time digging for her.
  • 📌 Earth weakness: since 6 Aug 2025 she is 50% weak to Earth spells (was Water). Magic DPS got a big buff.
  • 📌 Protection: Protect from Melee works; she only uses melee and burrows. No typeless/prayer-immune gimmick.
  • 📌 Top loot: mole claw (always, ~6.5K), mole skin 1–3 (always, ~5.8K–17.4K), Baby mole pet 1/3,000, long bone 1/400.
  • 📌 Profit: ~984K GP/hr (Dharok's) to ~1.57M GP/hr (Twisted bow) per OSRS Wiki; 700K–1.0M realistic for most. Rates vary.

1. 🐀 Overview & Why Farm the Mole in 2026

The Giant Mole sits in a sweet spot for players who have finished the early game but want a safe, instanced, single-target boss with almost no risk. She lives in the Mole Lair under Falador Park (a safe instance — no PvP, no death risk). In 2026 she is more profitable than ever thanks to a combat update.

Burrow mechanic25% chance to burrow at 5%–50% HP (unless hit by a splash)
Protection prayerProtect from Melee fully blocks her hits
Slayer requirementNone — anyone can kill her
Average kill value~22,298 GP (23,685 on a Konar task)

🎯 The Three Big Draws

  • Zero risk, high consistency. One attack style, a fixed safe lair, and most kills take well under a minute.
  • Falador Diary synergy. Noted drops + a minimap locator turn a tedious dig-chase into a smooth 50–60 kph farm.
  • Earth-spell buff. Since Aug 2025 she is 50% weak to Earth, so even a modest mage setup hits hard.
💡 The One Upgrade That Wins the Farm

Before anything else, complete the Falador Hard Diary. It makes mole claws and skins drop noted (you never empty your inventory) and puts a mole locator on your Falador shield (3 or 4) so you always see where she burrowed. Without it, the Mole is a frustrating dig-chase; with it, she is a money printer.

3. 🗺️ How to Get There (Falador Diary)

The Mole Lair is entered by digging at the mound in Falador Park with a spade. The fastest repeatable route:

Step 1 — Reach Falador Park

Teleport with a Falador teleport tablet, the Explorer's ring (from Lumbridge/Draynor diaries), a Skills necklace (Crafting Guild), or just walk. The dig spot is the mole hill east of the park fountain.

Step 2 — Dig in

With a spade and a light source (the lair is dark), use the spade on the mound to drop into the safe Mole Lair instance. There is no instance fee — entry is free.

Step 3 — Use the locator

With the Falador Hard Diary done, equip your Falador shield (3 or 4) and the Mole appears as a yellow dot on your minimap. Chase and kill.

💡 Permanent Light Trick

If you have completed Making Friends with My Arm, light the Fire of Eternal Light in the lair once and you never need a lantern again. Huge convenience for long sessions.

4. ⛏️ The Fight — Burrow Mechanic & Locator

The Mole uses only melee (max ~11) and periodically burrows to a new spot in the lair. The fight is trivial to survive; the only "skill" is re-locating her quickly when she digs away.

③ Step-by-Step Kill

Phase 1 — Locate and engage

With the locator, run straight to the yellow dot and attack. Turn on Protect from Melee if you want zero damage, or just eat the ~11 hits.

Phase 2 — Burn it down

The Mole has very low defence. A Dharok's set at 1 HP with a Dwarven rock cake kills her in 2–3 hits. Magic (Earth spells, post-Aug 2025) and Melee stab weapons also work well.

Phase 3 — Re-locate on burrow

At 5%–50% HP she has a 25% chance to burrow (a splashing spell prevents it). Without the locator you Dig (spade) at each mound; with the locator you just run to the dot. Then repeat.

💡 Note on Cannons

The Mole is immune to the Dwarf multicannon — cannons deal no damage here. Your only DPS is your own style (Melee / Magic). Bring thralls (Arceuus) if you want passive help.

🏆 Recommended Default: Dharok's 1-HP Melee

Full Dharok's + Dwarven rock cake dropped to 1 HP + Protect from Melee. She dies in 2–3 hits and you take almost nothing. This is the OSRS Wiki's fastest method (~984K GP/hr). Magic (Earth) and Void setups are strong alternatives.

💡 Earth Spells Are Now Great

Since 6 August 2025 the Mole is 50% weak to Earth (she was weak to Water before). A player with moderate Magic can now kill her quickly with Earth spells — no need for top-tier Melee gear.

9. ⚠️ Common Mistakes (Myth-Busting)

❌ Myth 1: "Just kill her, diary isn't needed."

Inefficient. Without the Falador Hard Diary her claws/skins drop un-noted and you get no locator — you'll constantly bank and dig-chase. The Diary is the difference between 30 kph and 60 kph.

❌ Myth 2: "She's weak to Water spells."

Outdated. Since 6 August 2025 the Mole is 50% weak to Earth spells, not Water. Use Earth spells (or just Melee) for best DPS.

❌ Myth 3: "Bring a cannon."

Wrong. The Mole is immune to the Dwarf multicannon. Cannons deal no damage in the lair. Use your own Melee/Magic or Arceuus thralls.

❌ Myth 4: "You need Slayer to fight her."

No. The Mole has no Slayer requirement at all. Anyone with a spade and a light source can farm her. (Konar assigning her is optional.)

❌ Myth 5: "Protect from Magic is best."

The Mole uses only Melee. Protect from Melee (or just eating her ~11 hits) is what works. Protect from Magic does nothing here.

10. ❓ FAQ

Do I need Slayer to kill the Giant Mole?

No. The Mole has no Slayer requirement. You only need a spade, a light source, and (ideally) the Falador Hard Diary. Konar can assign her, but it is optional.

What is the best combat style for the Mole?

Melee (Dharok's 1 HP) is fastest, but since Aug 2025 Earth spells are also excellent thanks to her 50% Earth weakness. Stab weapons (Dragon sword) work too. She has very low defence.

Is the Mole safe? Can I lose my items?

Yes, it is completely safe. The Mole Lair is an instanced safe zone — no Wilderness, no PvP. If you die (very unlikely) you keep your items. Stay above 1 HP and the risk is essentially zero.

What does the Falador Hard Diary do for the Mole?

It makes mole claws and skins drop noted and adds a mole locator to your Falador shield (3/4) so you always see where she burrowed. It is the single biggest efficiency upgrade for this boss.

How many kills per hour can I get?

Average players manage ~35–45 kph; the OSRS Wiki cites up to ~50–60 kph with Dharok's + Hard Diary. Elite clue RNG can push a session well above 1M GP/hr.

11. 🏁 Final Tips

  • Diary first: finish the Falador Hard Diary before you farm — noted drops + locator are game-changing.
  • Earth, not Water: since Aug 2025 she is 50% weak to Earth spells.
  • Protect from Melee: she only hits with melee; prayer or just eat her ~11 max hit.
  • Permanent light: light the Fire of Eternal Light (Making Friends with My Arm) to skip the lantern.
  • Slayer bonus: take a Konar task for Brimstone keys (1/74).
  • Next boss: once the Mole is comfortable, step up to the King Black Dragon — the next safe instanced boss.
