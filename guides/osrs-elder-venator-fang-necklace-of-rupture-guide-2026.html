1. 🏹 Why the Necklace of Rupture Matters in 2026

For years the Amulet of Anguish has been the ranged amulet answer: a straightforward strength-and-accuracy boost that slotted into every ranged DPS setup without question. The Necklace of Rupture, introduced with Blood Moon Rises on August 30, 2026, is the first amulet to genuinely displace it on single-target Ranged content. That is a big deal, because the amulet slot is often the final upgrade a maxed ranged account chases.

What makes it notable is not that it exists, but that it is a crafted upgrade rather than a direct drop. You begin with the Elder Venator Fang — itself a Blood Moon Rises reward — and assemble the rest of the materials from the update's content. That design means the upgrade is accessible through play rather than a single lucky roll, which keeps it attainable for determined players while still demanding effort.

💡 The Amulet Slot Is the Last 1%

Most ranged accounts reach a point where weapon, armour, and prayer are all maxed and the only DPS left is the jewellery. The Necklace of Rupture is exactly that last percentage point. If you are already at the Anguish ceiling, this is the upgrade that finally moves the number again.

For the wider update context, our Blood Moon Rises guide covers every reward path, and our best-in-slot gear guide places the Rupture in the full ranged BiS stack so you can see what it replaces and what sits around it.

📜 Material Checklist

  • Supporting reagents: gathered from the update's activities — plan a mix of combat drops and skilling supplies.

The exact station and any intermediate items can shift, so always confirm the in-game interface before you commit valuable materials.

Step-by-Step Outline

⚙️ The Craft Path

  • Step 1: acquire the Elder Venator Fang from Blood Moon Rises content.
  • Step 2: collect the supporting reagents from the update's combat and skilling activities.
  • Step 3: reach the Crafting level required to attempt the assembly.

4. 📊 Stats & How It Compares to the Amulet of Anguish

The Necklace of Rupture's design goal is simple: beat the Amulet of Anguish on single-target Ranged DPS. The way it does that is by pushing Ranged accuracy and Ranged strength higher than the Anguish does, which translates into both more hits landing and harder hits when they do.

Direct Comparison With the Amulet of Anguish

The Amulet of Anguish is a pure Ranged strength-and-accuracy amulet, and it has been the safe pick for years because nothing else in the slot out-performed it. The Necklace of Rupture takes that same role and extends it: same slot, stronger numbers. That makes the swap a strict upgrade for any single-target Ranged encounter where you are already using Anguish.

📊 Rupture vs Anguish

  • Same slot: both occupy the amulet slot, so it is a direct one-for-one swap.
  • Ranged accuracy: the Rupture pulls ahead, meaning more hits connect.
  • Ranged strength: the Rupture also edges it, meaning harder hits on connect.
  • Best at single-target: the Rupture's advantage is largest where every hit counts.
  • Trade-off: the Rupture costs more to acquire, so budget accordingly.

The practical takeaway is that almost every Ranged PvMer who currently wears an Amulet of Anguish should eventually move to the Necklace of Rupture — the only real question is when the gold makes sense. 5. 🎯 Best PvM Uses & Where It Shines

Because the Rupture's advantage is single-target Ranged DPS, it shines anywhere you are already using a ranged setup against one enemy. The more the fight hinges on your personal DPS — rather than team output or mechanics — the more the amulet matters.

🐉 Where the Rupture Wins

  • Solo ranged bosses: every hit is yours, so the accuracy and strength edge compounds.
  • High-defence targets: the accuracy bump matters most where you would otherwise whiff.
  • Enrage or DPS-check phases: the extra strength shortens the fight.
  • Speedrunning: the last percentage of DPS is exactly what shaves seconds.

Where it matters less is content where the amulet slot is not your bottleneck — heavy mechanic fights where you barely attack, or team content where someone else is carrying DPS. There the gold might be better spent elsewhere, at least until you have surplus.

💡 Stack It With Good Habits

An amulet upgrade amplifies whatever DPS you already have, so clean play multiplies the gain. Our general PvM mechanics guide covers the positioning and timing that let you actually land the extra hits the Rupture buys you.

6. 🛡️ Build & Gear Integration

Slotting the Necklace of Rupture is the easy part — it goes where your Amulet of Anguish was. The real integration question is whether the rest of your build is strong enough that the amulet upgrade is the right next purchase versus a different slot.

⚙️ When to Prioritise the Rupture

  • Weapon and armour maxed: if those are done, the amulet is the natural next step.
  • Already on Anguish: the swap is a direct, frictionless upgrade.
  • Ranged is your main style: the investment pays off across every ranged boss.
  • Bank surplus: only buy the craft if it does not delay a bigger upgrade elsewhere.

If your weapon or armour is still below BiS, those slots usually return more DPS per GP than the amulet. Our gear upgrade priority guide ranks slots by return so you are not sinking gold into the Rupture while a cheaper slot would have helped more.

7. 💰 Market Position & Is It Worth the Gold

The Necklace of Rupture sits at the top of the ranged amulet market as the new best-in-slot for single-target content. Its price is driven by the material grind plus the Crafting gate, which means it is more accessible to players willing to farm than to those who only buy — but either way it costs more than the Anguish it replaces.

For a ranged main, the upgrade is worth it the moment the rest of the build is settled and the gold is spare. The DPS gain is real, permanent, and applies to every ranged boss you will ever do, which makes it one of the highest-value jewellery purchases in the game. For a player still filling bigger slots, it is a luxury to defer.

✅ Buy or Farm?

  • Farm it if you are already in Blood Moon Rises content and enjoy the grind.
  • Buy it if your time is worth more than the gold and you want the upgrade now.
  • Defer it if a weapon or armour slot still needs work first.

Long term, the Rupture should hold value well. Best-in-slot amulets do not get obsoleted quickly, and as more players finish their builds the demand stays steady. That makes it both a performance upgrade and a reasonable store of crafted value.

💡 It Ages Better Than Weapons

Weapons get rebalanced and replaced more often than jewellery. A best-in-slot amulet is a slow-moving target, so the Rupture is likely to remain the answer in its slot for a long time — which protects the gold you put into it.

8. ❓ Elder Venator Fang & Necklace of Rupture FAQs

Q: What is the Elder Venator Fang?

It is the base item for the Necklace of Rupture, obtained from Blood Moon Rises content (added August 30, 2026). You gather it and supporting reagents, then craft the amulet from it.

Q: What are the requirements to craft the Necklace of Rupture?

You need the Elder Venator Fang, the supporting reagents from the update, and a Crafting level around 70 to perform the assembly. Confirm the exact level on the in-game recipe panel, as it can shift with patches.

Q: How does the Necklace of Rupture compare to the Amulet of Anguish?

Both occupy the amulet slot, but the Rupture pushes Ranged accuracy and Ranged strength higher than the Anguish, making it a strict single-target upgrade for Ranged DPS.

Q: Is it the new ranged best-in-slot amulet?

For single-target Ranged content, yes — it overtakes the Amulet of Anguish, which had been the long-standing answer in that slot. Most Ranged PvMers will eventually swap to it.

Q: Where does the Rupture shine most?

Solo ranged bosses, high-defence targets where accuracy matters, DPS-check or enrage phases, and speedrunning — anywhere your personal Ranged DPS is the bottleneck.

Q: Should I buy it or farm it?

Farm it if you are already doing Blood Moon Rises content; buy it if your time is more valuable than the gold. Defer it if a weapon or armour slot still needs work first.

Q: Is the upgrade worth the gold?

For a ranged main with the rest of the build settled, yes — the DPS gain is real, permanent, and applies to every ranged boss. For players still filling bigger slots, it is a luxury to defer.

Q: Can I verify the exact recipe and stats?

Yes. Use the in-game crafting interface and combat panel as the source of truth, since exact reagent lists and the Crafting requirement can change with balance passes. Written guides are a starting point, not the final word.

Q: Will the Rupture stay relevant long term?

Best-in-slot amulets are slow-moving targets and rarely get obsoleted quickly, so it should hold both its performance edge and its market value for a long time compared to weapons.

Q: Does it help on multi-target content?

Its advantage is built around single-target Ranged DPS. On multi-target content the amulet still helps your hits, but the gap over the Anguish is most pronounced where every individual hit counts.
