🎣 OSRS Fishing 1-99 Training Guide 2026

Intermediate

Fishing is the perfect gateway gathering skill: it is relaxing, feeds Cooking directly, and turns a tidy profit at the high levels. This 1-99 guide covers the fastest route by tier — fly fishing, Aerial Fishing, Minnows, and the big money fish — with XP per hour tables and cost notes.

📅 Level: Intermediate — Gathering Skill · 13 min read · Updated July 2026

Published: July 20, 2026 · Last verified: July 2026

⏱️ Quick Verdict — 30-Second Read

  • 📌 Fastest 1-99: Fly (1-58) → Aerial Fishing (58-99) or Minnows (62-99). Expect ~45-60 hours.
  • 📌 Aerial Fishing (58+) is the best all-rounder — up to 50K XP/hr while also training Agility and Hunter.
  • 📌 Minnows (62+) give ~55K XP/hr but are click-intensive and bad for your wrists long-term.
  • 📌 Fishing is free to train — you only "spend" time; the fish you catch are pure value.
  • 📌 Big money: Anglerfish (82+) and Monkfish (62+) — cook them with our Cooking guide for double profit.

1. 🎒 Getting Started: Gear, Outfits & Quests

Fishing needs almost no gear, but two things make a big difference: the Angler's outfit and a couple of quest boosts.

Angler's outfit: Earned from the Fishing Trawler minigame, the full four-piece set grants +2.5% Fishing XP (not per piece). Prioritize this early — it speeds up every method for the rest of the skill.

💡 Quests That Save Hours

Sea Slug (1K XP) and Fish Slayer quests, plus Swan Song (which unlocks the Ranging Guild's Fishing Contest and is required for some spots) help. Most importantly, finish Animal Magnetism and Barbarian Training so you can use the Barbarian fishing spots.

Dwarf Cannon / bait: Most spots need no bait (fly fishing uses feathers; aerial uses no bait). Keep a stack of feathers handy from the GE — they are dirt cheap.

2. 🐟 Early Game (1-40): Shrimps to Fly Fishing

Start with Shrimps and Sardines (net/bait) until 5, then Herring to 10, and switch to Trout and Salmon fly fishing at a Barbarian Village or Shilo Village spot.

Fly fishing (20-40+): This is the classic early method. Sit at the River Lum or Barbarian Village with a fly fishing rod + feathers. It is ~20K XP/hr early and ramps up as your level climbs. It is also a great place to learn the rhythm of the skill.

🎯 Best Early Spots

  • 1-5: Shrimps (Port Sarim, Draynor).
  • 5-20: Herring / Sardines with bait.
  • 20-40: Fly fishing Trout/Salmon (Barbarian Village is F2P-friendly).
  • Quest skip: Sea Slug gives a fast head start to ~24.

By level 40 you should have a near-full Angler's outfit and be ready for the mid-game money and XP methods.

3. 🪶 Mid Game (43-62): Aerial Fishing & Trawler

This is where Fishing gets fun and efficient.

② Aerial Fishing (58+, but start at 43 with Molch)

At Molch (after Bone Voyage), you use a Cormorant's glove to catch fish mid-air. It trains Fishing, Hunter, and Agility simultaneously and hits 35-50K Fishing XP/hr. The downside: it requires 35 Hunter and 43 Agility to start, and you only really shine at 58+. It is the most well-rounded method in the game.

③ Fishing Trawler (mini-game, 15+)

The Fishing Trawler is the source of the Angler's outfit and gives decent XP (up to ~39K/hr at higher levels). It is slower than aerial but you are there early to farm the outfit anyway.

💡 Why Aerial Is the Sweet Spot

Because it trains three skills at once, Aerial Fishing is the best "value" grind from 58 to high 80s. You effectively get Agility and Hunter levels "for free" while pushing Fishing.

4. 🐠 Late Game (62-99): Minnows, Monkfish & Anglerfish

Three strong late methods, each with a different goal:

④ Minnows (62+)

Fishing Minnows at the Fishing Guild is the fastest raw XP — ~55K XP/hr. Downsides: extremely click-intensive, and the minnows convert to food you may not need. Best pure-speed option.

⑤ Monkfish (62+)

The Piscatoris fishing colony (after Swan Song) lets you catch Monkfish — ~38-45K XP/hr and excellent food that sells well. Great balance of XP and profit.

⑥ Anglerfish (82+)

At Port Piscarilius (after Client of Kourend + 100% Hosidius favour) you catch Anglerfish — ~40K XP/hr and the most valuable fish in the game. Many players push 82+ here for both XP and a strong money method.

🏆 Late-Game Decision

  1. Max speed: Minnows 62-99 (wrist fatigue warning).
  2. Balanced: Monkfish or Anglerfish 62/82-99 (XP + profit).
  3. Multi-skill: Aerial Fishing 58-99 (slower XP, free Agility/Hunter).

6. 💰 Fishing Money Making Notes

Fishing is one of the few skills where high-level training is the money making:

  • Anglerfish (82+): ~250-400K GP/hr raw, more if you cook them (see our Cooking guide).
  • Monkfish (62+): ~150-250K GP/hr; popular for PvM food.
  • Minnows (62+): ~200K GP/hr as food, but you may as well cook higher-value fish.
  • Dark crab (85+): High-level Wilderness food with good margins but PK risk.
💡 Double Your Profit

Catch high-value fish, then cook them yourself at the Hosidius Kitchen (with Cooking Gauntlets) to avoid burns. Raw + cooked Anglerfish combined is the strongest Fishing income loop in the game.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using the wrong rod and spot: Trout and salmon come from a fly fishing rod + feathers at a fly-fishing spot (Barbarian Village, Shilo Village, River Lum). Showing up with a small or big net instead wastes the trip.
  • Not getting the Angler's outfit early: The full four-piece set grants +2.5% Fishing XP (not per piece) and applies to every method. Farm it at the Fishing Trawler before you commit to long grinds.
  • Skipping Barbarian fishing: After Barbarian Training, Barbarian fishing trains Fishing, Strength, and Agility at once and is a strong 58-99 route that many players overlook for slower methods.
  • Overlooking Aerial Fishing's triple training: At Molch (after Bone Voyage) you train Fishing, Hunter, and Agility simultaneously. Chasing only Minnows for speed throws away free Agility and Hunter levels.
  • Never cooking the catch: High-level fish like Monkfish and Anglerfish are far more valuable cooked. Leaving them raw (or ignoring Hosidius Kitchen + Cooking Gauntlets) leaves a lot of GP on the table.
  • Sticking to weak methods too long: Fly fishing is great early but caps hard. Past 58-62 you should move to Aerial Fishing, Minnows, or Monkfish/Anglerfish — staying on trout/salmon to 99 wastes dozens of hours of XP and profit.

8. ❓ Fishing FAQs

What is the fastest way to 99 Fishing?

Fly fishing to ~58, then Minnows from 62 to 99 is the fastest route at roughly 45-55 hours. Aerial Fishing is ~10 hours slower but trains Agility and Hunter alongside.

Is the Angler's outfit worth getting?

Absolutely. The full set gives +2.5% Fishing XP and applies to every method. Farm it at the Fishing Trawler early — it pays for itself within a few levels.

Can F2P players train Fishing well?

Partially. F2P caps around level 40-50 (shrimps, sardines, herring, trout/salmon, lobsters). Lobsters are a decent F2P money method. Full efficiency needs membership.

Which fish makes the most money?

Anglerfish (82+) raw or cooked is the top earner, followed by Monkfish and Dark crab. Cooking them yourself roughly doubles the value versus selling raw.

Should I do Aerial Fishing or Minnows?

If you want pure speed and don't mind clicking, Minnows. If you want free Agility/Hunter levels and a calmer grind, Aerial Fishing from 58 to 99 is the smarter long-term play.
