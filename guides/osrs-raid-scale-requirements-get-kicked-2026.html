1. 🚪 Why You Keep Getting Kicked

Getting removed from a raid team stings, but it is almost never personal. Raid leaders optimise for one thing: a clean, fast, split-worthy clear. If your presence threatens that, you are out — regardless of how "close" your stats are.

There are four repeatable reasons players get kicked, and they map cleanly onto the three raids:

💡 The Four Kicks

① Scale mismatch — you applied to a 5-man CoX but the leader wanted a 3-man for a fatter split. ② No voice — ToB teams will not carry a silent runner. ③ Wrong role/KC — you claimed verzik but have 0 ToB KC. ④ Low invocation — you joined a 300+ ToA but only toggle 50, gutting the team's points. Our Raid Entry Requirements page lists the exact thresholds so you apply to the right team the first time.

The fix is not "get better gear" — it is understanding each raid's unspoken contract before you click apply. Read the three sections below and you will stop guessing.

2. 🏛️ CoX: Scale, Team Size & CM Reality

Chambers of Xeric is the most flexible raid in the game, and that flexibility is exactly why players misread it. CoX has no fixed team size — it scales from a solo speedrun to 100-player mass worlds. The leader picks a scale, and the scale dictates the loot split and the gear expectation.

① How CoX scale actually works

The raid's difficulty (and therefore its point value and purple rate) scales with team size and the personal bests of the raiders. A 2–3 man team faces a proportionally harder fight but splits the loose end and unique loot between fewer people. A 20-man mass is trivially easy but your individual share is thin. When a leader says "scaling a 4," they mean four people sharing the reward pool.

② Team-size tiers and what each expects

Pick the tier that matches your gear, not your ego

Use this as a rule of thumb when applying:

Team sizeSplit per personGear expectationBest for
1–2 manVery highNear-BiS, practicedSpeed / profit chasers
3–5 manHighStrong PvM gearMost LFG teams
6–10 manMediumEntry raid gear OKLearners / clans
15–100 manLowAnything goesMass worlds, fun

If you apply to a 3-man with budget gear, you will likely be replaced before Olm — not because you are bad, but because the split math no longer works for the other two. This is the single most common CoX kick.

③ Challenge Mode (CM) is a different contract

CoX Challenge Mode requires the whole team to have a recorded CM completion and tight, pre-drilled paths. Showing up to a CM with only normal-mode KC is an instant kick. Read the CoX loot & profit guide to see which scales actually pay before you commit.

3. 🩸 ToB: Mandatory Voice & Role Splits

Theatre of Blood is where most "I got kicked for no reason" stories are born — because the reason is almost always voice or roles. ToB is a coordinated, role-locked raid, and 99% of teams run it on Discord voice.

① No mic = no invite (usually)

ToB demands callouts: "bloat down," "nylocas left," "verzik purple," "tank swapped." A silent player forces three other people to guess, and guesswork wipes the raid. If a listing says "VC required" and you have no mic, do not apply — you will be removed on sight. This is the brutal but fair LFG门槛 of ToB.

② The five roles and why claiming the wrong one gets you kicked

Know your role before you load in

ToB splits into clear jobs. Claiming verzik with no KC is the fastest way to a kick:

  • Maiden — clears crabs, manages reds, calls nylos.
  • Bloat — times the down, tanks the slam.
  • Nylocas — wave clear, often the hardest DPS check.
  • Verzik P1/P2 — the DPS and movement phase.
  • Verzik P3 / tank — the endgame, reserved for experienced runners.

Be honest about your KC. A learner who says "I can do maiden and bloat, first ToB" gets invited far more often than a liar who claims verzik. See the Theatre of Blood guide for a full role breakdown.

③ Learner runs exist — use them

Many clans host "learner ToB" with reduced expectations and a teacher on voice. That is the correct on-ramp, not a 5-man speed team. Join a learner run, get your first 2–3 KC honestly, then apply to real teams.

4. ⚱️ ToA: Invocation & The Low-Split Math

Tombs of Amascut invented a new player complaint: "the split was too low." That is not a bug — it is the invocation system working as designed.

① Invocation sets the raid's point total

ToA difficulty is chosen by toggling invocations (300 = baseline, 500 = max, with the raid level shown as e.g. "ToA 450"). The total raid points are shared among the team at the end. A 4-man 450-invocation raid has a fixed point pool — and everyone is expected to contribute similar invocation levels.

② Why your split feels small

The "low split" is usually a mismatch, not theft

If you join a 500-invocation ToA but only toggle 150, the raid's final points are dragged down by your low contribution — yet you still take an equal share of the (smaller) pool. Teams hate this, and many will kick low-invocation applicants from high-invocation lobbies. Conversely, if you toggle 500 but the team averages 300, your split looks "thin" because the pool was small. Match your invocation to the lobby's stated level.

③ Soloing vs teaming

If splits frustrate you, solo ToA removes the argument entirely — every point is yours. It demands tighter mechanics, but it ends the "why is my split low" debate for good.

5. 🔎 Where to Actually Find Teams (LFG门槛)

The "wild" LFG (world 385 ToB, bus worlds, the in-game friends-chat spam) is the harshest environment and the worst place to learn. Get your teams from better sources.

① Clan guesting beats world 385

A clan with a raiding branch will guest you, teach you, and tolerate learner KC that open LFG never would. This single change ends most "I can't get into raids" problems. Our best clans & Discord guide shows how to pick one that actually raids.

② Discord LFG with stated requirements

Good LFG posts state scale, KC, gear, and voice rule up front: "ToA 450, 4-man, 150+ KC ea, VC." That transparency protects you — if you do not meet it, don't apply, and you will not get kicked.

③ Build your own team once you have KC

The moment you have ~5 ToB KC or a clean CoX PB, you become the host. Hosting flips the power dynamic: you set the scale, you pick the voices, and you never get kicked again. This is the real endgame of raid survival.

6. ✅ The 7-Step "Don't Get Kicked" Checklist

Run this before every raid application. Miss one and you are a kick risk.

Pre-apply checklist

  1. Read the scale. CoX team size and ToA invocation must match the lobby's stated numbers.
  2. Confirm voice. If "VC required," have a working mic — no exceptions.
  3. Claim only known roles. State your real KC for each role honestly.
  4. Meet the gear check. Cross-reference the boss gear checklist before applying.
  5. Match invocation. ToA: toggle within ~50 of the lobby's level.
  6. Have supplies. Bring your own food, pots, and(runes/spec weapons) — leeches get kicked.
  7. Show KC proof if asked. A screenshot of your rank beats a typed "trust me."
💡 When in doubt, guest a clan

Ninety percent of raid-kick pain disappears the moment you stop relying on open LFG and join a raiding clan. You learn faster, die cheaper, and stop begging for invites.

7. ❓ Raid Team FAQs

What team size is best for CoX if I want profit?

For pure GP/share, smaller is better: 2–3 man scales give each person a fat slice of the loot pool. But they demand near-BiS gear and clean Olm mechanics. If your gear is entry-level, a 6–10 man clan team pays less per run but lets you actually clear. Match scale to gear.

Can I do ToB without voice?

Technically the game allows it, but practically almost no LFG team will take you. ToB needs live callouts to survive the nylocas wave and verzik. Get a mic, or stick to learner clans where a teacher carries the calls. Silent ToB is a wipe waiting to happen.

Why was my ToA split so low even though I did my job?

Because ToA splits by total raid points, not by effort. If the lobby's average invocation was lower than yours (or you toggled low in a high-inv lobby), the shared pool was small and your equal share looks thin. Match your invocation to the stated raid level next time, or solo it.

How much KC do I need before applying to real teams?

Roughly: CoX ~50 PBs before small-scale, ToB ~5–10 learner KC before a real 5-man, ToA ~10 completions before a 400+ lobby. But the bigger gate is role honesty — claim what you have done, not what you hope to do.

Is hosting a raid hard?

Less hard than it feels. Once you have modest KC, post your own LFG: state scale, KC, gear, voice rule. You control who joins, set the pace, and never get kicked. Hosting is how most players escape the kick list permanently.

World 385 ToB keeps kicking me — what do I do?

Leave world 385. Open ToB LFG is the most toxic, highest-bar environment in the game and the worst place to learn. Join a clan learner run or a Discord LFG with stated requirements instead. See our clans guide.

Do I need max gear to not get kicked?

No — you need appropriate gear for the scale. A 10-man ToB welcomes entry raid gear; a 2-man CoX expects near-BiS. The kick happens when your gear mismatches the scale you applied to, not when it is "low" in absolute terms.
