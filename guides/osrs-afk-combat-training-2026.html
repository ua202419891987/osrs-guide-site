1. What Actually Makes a Method "AFK"

Before the tier list, understand what you are optimizing for. An AFK method is one where the game keeps progressing with minimal input from you — ideally minutes between clicks, not seconds. Three mechanics make that possible in OSRS.

① Auto-retaliate and long cooldowns

🎯 Why combat AFKs better than skilling

  • Auto-retaliate: Toggle it on and your character attacks back without a click. As long as a target is in reach, damage flows.
  • Slow attack speeds: A scimitar or whip hits every few seconds, not every frame, so the loop tolerates inattention.
  • Regenerating prayer: With the right setup, Protect prayers stay lit for minutes, so you survive without babysitting HP.
  • Stacked HP: High Hitpoints and good defence mean a few missed heals will not kill you.
💡 The real definition

"AFK" in OSRS is a spectrum. A method where you click every 30–60 seconds is "semi-AFK" and perfectly safe. A method that claims you can leave for 20 minutes is either lying or about to get you killed by a PKer. Train your attention budget to the method, not the marketing.

② AFK vs efficient — the trade-off

AFK training is almost always slower than active, optimized training. You trade XP rate for screen time. That is a fair trade when you are doing something else (watching a show, at work on a second monitor, commuting), but if you are staring at the screen anyway, an active method beats it. Pick AFK when your attention is the scarce resource, not your playtime.

2. Early Game AFK (Level 1–70)

The first seventy combat levels are where AFK shines hardest. The monsters are weak, the areas are safe, and the methods barely need you.

③ Sand crabs — the AFK default

🏖️ Why everyone starts here

  • Zero risk: Sand crabs are non-aggressive and in a safe zone; you cannot be attacked by other players there.
  • Fast respawn: They reset to their shell form and re-aggro quickly, so you keep a target without moving.
  • Level range: Comfortable from the low levels up through the high sixties for melee, and similarly for ranged with a bow.
  • The only catch: They de-aggro after a while, so you re-click every minute or two to reset them — that is the "AFK" cadence.

④ Chickens and cows for the absolute start

If you are below the level where sand crabs are efficient, chickens (for the very first levels) and cows (for a bit of early profit from hides) are free, safe, and forgiving. They are slower, but they teach the auto-retaliate loop with no downside.

⑤ Ammonite crabs — the step up

🦀 The mid-tier crab

  • Higher HP pools: Ammonite crabs have far more health than sand crabs, so each kill lasts longer and you click less often.
  • Still safe: Found in a safe area, so PKers cannot interrupt you.
  • Best window: Roughly the level seventies through the high eighties, where sand crabs get too weak to be worth it but you are not ready for aggressive content.
  • Caveat: They are popular, so worlds fill up — hop worlds rather than fight for a spot.
⚠️ Do not AFK aggressive monsters early

Any monster that attacks you on sight will eventually out-damage your passive healing if you stop clicking. Early AFK should be reserved for safe, passive targets like crabs. The moment a method requires you to manage food actively, it has stopped being AFK.

3. Mid Game AFK (Level 70–90)

Once you clear the crab stage, AFK options split by combat style and by whether you want XP only or XP plus profit.

⑥ Nightmare Zone (NMZ) — the AFK powerhouse

🌙 Why NMZ dominates this band

  • Absorption + Overload: The NMZ potion combo lets you stand in a crowd of bosses taking almost no real damage, then top up with one click every couple of minutes.
  • Melee, ranged, and magic all work: You can AFK any style here, which is rare.
  • XP rate: Strong for the level band, and the reward points can be spent on useful resources.
  • The AFK loop: Drink overload, drink absorptions, turn on auto-retaliate, check back every 1–2 minutes to re-dose. That is the entire routine.

⑦ Slayer with AFK tasks

Certain Slayer assignments are effectively AFK when done in safe spots or with cannon support — tasks made of high-HP, low-threat monsters let you auto-retaliate while the slayer points accumulate. The exact tasks shift with what your block list looks like, but the principle is constant: pair a safe, high-HP target with auto-retaliate and you have AFK Slayer.

⑧ Ranged and magic AFK

🏹 Style-specific notes

  • Ranged: A bow at crabs or at safe-spotted Slayer targets gives hands-off XP; bring enough arrows or bolts for the stretch.
  • Magic: Highest setup cost, but splashing is never AFK-friendly — use accurate spells on high-HP targets so every cast lands.
  • Defensive casting: Some magic setups let you tank while training, extending the safe AFK window.

4. Late Game & Endgame AFK

Past level 90, pure AFK XP gives way to AFK money making and AFK bossing. You will not set records, but you will make progress while barely present.

⑨ AFK bosses and activities

👑 Where endgame AFK lives

  • AFK-style boss loops: A handful of bosses have slow, predictable attack patterns where you can tank on prayer and re-click every so often — slower than active, but free of attention.
  • High-HP trash in safe content: Some endgame areas let you auto-retaliate on durable enemies with no PK risk.
  • Minigames with passive combat: A few repeatable activities reward you for simply surviving waves, which pairs naturally with AFK play.
  • The honest caveat: Every endgame AFK method is a fraction of the active XP or GP rate. Use it for the hours you would otherwise not play at all.
💡 Treat endgame AFK as "free" time, not "main" time

The players who get the most from AFK bossing are not replacing their active sessions with it — they are filling the dead hours (meetings, TV, travel) with it. Bank those hours and they add up to real progression over a month.

5. Gear & Prayer Setups for Safe AFK

The difference between safe AFK and a death screen is almost always gear and prayer, not skill.

⑩ Defensive gear first

🛡️ What to prioritize

  • Defence over offence: When you are not clicking, you want to take as few hits as possible. Defence bonuses matter more than DPS for AFK.
  • High Hitpoints: More HP is more buffer between your check-ins. Train it deliberately.
  • Damage reduction: Where available, gear and prayers that cut incoming damage extend the safe window dramatically.
  • Bring food you do not have to think about: Auto-eat settings or a comfortable food buffer mean a missed heal is an inconvenience, not a death.

⑪ Prayer is the safety net

Protect prayers (or their equivalents) are what let you survive minutes between clicks. The standard AFK loop is: turn on the relevant protect prayer, fight, and re-pot prayer every couple of minutes. If your prayer runs out and you are not looking, the next hits land fully — so keep a comfortable prayer reserve rather than cutting it close.

⚠️ Gear checks before you walk away

Auto-retaliate on? Protect prayer active? Enough food and prayer potions to cover the time you will be gone? A thirty-second pre-AFK checklist prevents most "I came back to a gravestone" moments.

6. AFK Money Making While Training

Some AFK combat methods pay you while they train you. The rates are not the best on the planet, but they are positive, which beats paying for training.

⑫ When AFK training is also profit

💰 The overlap zone

  • Drop and alch loops: Certain AFK kill methods let you high-alch the drops between attacks, turning idle combat into passive GP.
  • Loot that sells: High-HP monsters with tradeable drops give you both XP and a sellable stack.
  • Slayer points as currency: Even when the GP is modest, Slayer points unlock gear and resources that save you money elsewhere.
  • Reinvest the profit: AFK money is best spent on better defensive gear, which extends your next AFK session.

Be honest about rates: AFK money making is deliberately slower than dedicated money-making methods. If your goal is GP, do a dedicated method. If your goal is XP with a side of GP, AFK combat is the sweet spot. Our melee gear progression guide covers the defensive upgrades that make these loops safe.

7. AFK Training for Ironmen

Ironmen cannot buy supplies, so AFK training has extra value — it is a way to make progress without burning through a bank of potions you had to gather yourself.

⑬ Ironman-specific AFK logic

🔒 Self-sufficient AFK

  • Favor free or self-supplied methods: Crabs and safe-spot Slayer cost little, so they fit an Ironman bank better than potion-heavy bossing.
  • Bank the drops: Tradeable drops from AFK combat are a supply line, not just GP.
  • Pair with gathering: AFK combat fills the time between active gathering runs.
  • Plan around death: An Ironman death is a real loss; defensive setups matter more, not less.

8. The Traps That Get You Killed

Most AFK deaths are predictable. Here are the ones that catch experienced players.

⑭ The five AFK failure modes

☠️ Avoid these

  • PKer blind spots: Any non-safe AFK spot is a PKer magnet. If you are not in a safe zone, you are a target.
  • Prayer burnout: Walking away with low prayer is the classic "came back dead" cause.
  • Over-stacking aggro: Pulling more than you can tank while AFK ends in a pile of loot you cannot pick up.
  • Disconnects: A dropped connection leaves your character idle and vulnerable. Assume it will happen and keep buffers.
  • Randoms and interruptions: Some in-game interrupts need a click; if you are truly away, they stall your session at best.
💡 The one rule that prevents all of them

Never AFK longer than your safety buffer allows. If your gear and prayer can survive three minutes unattended, set a timer for two. The gap is your insurance.

9. AFK Combat FAQs

Q: Is AFK combat allowed, or is it botting?

Manual AFK — you are at the keyboard checking in periodically — is completely legitimate. Botting (a script playing for you) is against the rules. The line is whether a human is actually present and clicking. Stay on the human side.

Q: What is the single best AFK method overall?

There is no single best across all levels. Sand crabs own the early game, ammonite crabs the mid, and NMZ the level 70–90 band. Endgame AFK is situational. Match the method to your level, not to a generic "best" list.

Q: Can I really walk away from the screen?

Semi-AFK, yes — check in every minute or two. Fully walking away is only safe in genuinely safe, passive spots, and even then a disconnect can end it. Treat "walk away" as a risk, not a feature.

Q: Does AFK training hurt my account value?

No. XP is XP. The only cost is slower rates versus active training, which is a time trade, not a penalty. Many maxed players used AFK methods to fill their off-hours.

Q: How do I make AFK combat profitable?

Use methods with tradeable drops and consider high-alching between attacks. The GP is modest compared to dedicated money makers, but it is positive, and that is the point of AFK money making.
