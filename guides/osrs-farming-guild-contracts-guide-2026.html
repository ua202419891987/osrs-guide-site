OSRS Farming Guild Contracts Guide 2026 — Seed Packs, Hespori & Free XP

Published September 8, 2026  |  13 min read  |  By Yanchun Wu

The Farming Guild in Hosidius quietly pays you to farm: take a contract from Guildmaster Jane, grow exactly what she asks, and cash in a seed pack plus Farming XP every single run. Most players ignore contracts until level 85, which means they ignore thousands of free seeds and the entire Hespori boss. This guide explains the 45 / 65 / 85 contract tiers, what each seed pack tier actually drops, how to farm Hespori for anima seeds, and a repeatable routine that slots contracts into your existing herb and tree runs without wasting a single trip.

1. 🌾 Why Farming Guild Contracts Are the Best Daily Farming Activity

Most players treat Farming as a background skill: do tree runs twice a day, herb runs in between, and let the XP arrive in drips. Farming Guild contracts add a third layer that most guides skip — and it is the layer that pays for the other two.

A single contract costs you one crop cycle inside the guild. The reward is a seed pack that scales with your tier plus a chunk of Farming XP. Because the requested crop is always something you can grow right now, the only real cost is the seed you plant. Every completed contract therefore converts a cheap seed into a pack that frequently contains seeds worth several times more, and occasionally contains the Hespori seed that unlocks the guild's only boss fight.

💡 Why This Matters More in 2026

With herb and tree seed prices swinging harder after the farming updates of the last two years, seed packs are one of the few Farming mechanics whose value goes up when seed prices rise. Contract rewards are largely self-sustaining: the pack you earn usually contains the seeds you need to run the next contract.

For mains this is free XP and a small passive income. For Ironmen it is arguably the single most important Farming loop in the game — a reliable source of herb, tree and allotment seeds that does not depend on slayer drops, boss drops or the Grand Exchange.

2. 🔓 Unlock Requirements: Level 45 Farming & the Hosidius Guild

The Farming Guild sits in the north-east of Hosidius, part of the Kourend region. You do not need a quest to walk into the area, but you do need Farming levels to use the building:

🌱 Level Gates Inside the Guild

  • Level 45 Farming — enter the guild hall and access the main patch area (allotment, flower, herb and two tree patches)
  • Level 65 Farming — access the upper tier areas and receive medium-tier contracts from Guildmaster Jane
  • Level 85 Farming — access the hard-tier areas and receive hard contracts

Your contract tier is not chosen — it is tied to your Farming level. Guildmaster Jane will only offer you the highest tier you qualify for, and the crops she requests scale with that tier. You cannot ask for an easy contract at level 90; the game gives you hard contracts because that is where the best seed packs are.

💡 Getting There

The quickest route for most players is the Xeric's Talisman teleport to Hosidius, then a short run north. Alternatively use the Spirit Tree network if you have the Farming Guild teleport unlocked, or simply walk from the Kourend Castle lodestone after completing the relevant quest progression. Bring the exact seeds you need for your first contract so you can start immediately.

3. 🔁 How Contracts Work: Plant, Grow, Harvest, Claim

The contract loop is simple, but players skip it because they misread one step. Here is the exact flow:

① Ask Guildmaster Jane for a Contract

Guildmaster Jane stands inside the guild hall near the entrance. Talk to her and choose the contract option. She will name one crop — for example a specific herb, a specific allotment, or a specific tree — plus a quantity. That crop is your target.

② Grow the Requested Crop in the Guild's Own Patches

The contract only counts crops grown inside the Farming Guild. The guild has its own herb, allotment, flower and tree patches, so you plant the requested seed there, water if needed, and wait for it to finish growing. You can leave and come back — the patch keeps growing while you do anything else.

③ Harvest the Full Requested Amount

When the crop is ready, harvest it. You need to collect the full quantity Jane asked for. If a harvest comes up short (allotments and herbs can yield fewer than expected), you simply wait for the patch to regrow or plant again until the count is met. Checking the crop with Jane tells you exactly how many more you need.

④ Claim Your Reward and Repeat

Return to Guildmaster Jane with the full amount and select the reward option. You receive a seed pack and Farming XP based on the tier. Then immediately take the next contract. The whole loop takes under a minute of actual clicking once your patch timers are aligned.

💡 The One Rule Everyone Gets Wrong

You do not need to hand in the harvested produce. The game tracks the contract count the moment you harvest. If Jane still asks for more, you have not harvested enough yet — plant the same seed again in a free patch and repeat until the counter is satisfied.

4. 🎁 Contract Tiers & Seed Pack Rewards Explained

Seed packs come in three strength levels that mirror your contract tier. The crops requested and the seeds inside both get better as you climb:

🎒 What Each Tier Looks Like

  • Easy (45+ Farming): basic herbs, allotments and low-level trees. Packs contain low and mid tier seeds — useful early, cheap later.
  • Medium (65+ Farming): better herbs (including ranarr-tier), fruit trees and higher allotments. Medium packs are where the loop starts to profit.
  • Hard (85+ Farming): high-level herbs, palm/magic-class trees and the best allotments. Hard packs regularly contain rare tree seeds, high-level herb seeds and the valuable Hespori seed.

Because the pack contents randomize, one pack can feel underwhelming while the next pays for twenty contracts. Treat contracts as an average — over a week of daily runs, medium and hard tier players reliably come out ahead on both GP and Farming XP.

💡 Ironman Note

Seed packs are the backbone of a self-sufficient Ironman Farming setup. Doing one contract per day from level 45 builds a seed bank that covers herb runs, tree runs and Hespori attempts without ever touching a shop or a drop table.

5. 🌿 Hespori & Anima Seeds: The Boss That Grows in the Guild

Contracts are also the primary gateway to the Farming Guild's hidden boss. Hespori seeds drop from contract seed packs (most reliably from medium and hard packs). Once you have one, the fight is accessed through the guild's special Hespori patch:

① Plant the Hespori Seed

The Hespori patch is inside the guild. Plant the seed there — it requires a Farming level in the mid-60s to handle. The Hespori grows into a hostile plant that you fight at the patch itself, so no separate boss room or instance is involved.

② Fight the Hespori

The Hespori is an entry-level boss designed for mid-level accounts. It attacks with melee and ranged-style hits and periodically sends out a special attack that hits hard if you ignore it. Bring food and your best available gear; most players first kill it in the mid-60s to 70s combat stats without trouble. The fight takes a couple of minutes and costs almost nothing in supplies.

③ Collect Anima Seeds — the Real Prize

Hespori drops anima seeds: Attas, Iasor and Kronos. Plant one in an anima patch inside the guild and it permanently buffs your Farming for its entire life:

  • Attas seed: improves crop yield across your runs — more herbs, more allotment harvests.
  • Iasor seed: reduces the chance that patches get diseased, cutting your compost and protection costs.
  • Kronos seed: gives a chance for instant-growth on patches, effectively speeding up every tree and herb run.

💡 Which Anima Should You Plant?

Most efficiency-minded players rotate: Iasor while you are training (fewer diseased deaths, cheaper runs) and Kronos once your Farming is maxed or near-maxed (faster cycles on expensive trees). Attas shines for Ironmen who rely on yield over speed.

6. 🗓️ Slotting Contracts Into Your Existing Farm Runs

Contracts fail when players treat them as a separate chore. The fix is to make the contract part of your normal farm run circuit:

⏰ A Realistic Daily Routine

  • Morning: teleport to the guild, check your contract crop and your Hespori patch, harvest anything ready, plant the next contract seed.
  • Herb run: fold the guild's herb patch into your normal herb route so the contract herb grows alongside your money herbs.
  • Tree run: if the contract asks for a tree, do it in the same trip as your fruit tree run — one teleport, two jobs.
  • Evening: return to the guild, harvest the finished contract, claim the pack, and take the next contract so it grows overnight.

Because the guild patches are always available and never diseased when protected, the contract crop is the most predictable part of your Farming day. The key is always ending the session with a new contract already planted.

💡 Stack with Birdhouse & Seaweed

Treat contracts like birdhouse runs: a low-effort daily that pays out in seeds instead of nests. Players who already do seaweed and birdhouse runs can add the guild contract to the same reset cycle with almost no extra travel.

7. 🧠 9 Tips, Tricks & Common Mistakes

✅ Do This

  • Always take a new contract immediately after claiming — idle contract time is wasted seed potential.
  • Keep a spare seed of each common contract crop in your bank tab so a contract never stalls while you shop.
  • Plant the contract crop in the guild's own patch only — outside patches do not count.
  • Use ultracompost on expensive contract crops to guarantee the full yield and avoid replanting.
  • Save Hespori seeds until your farming level comfortably exceeds the patch requirement, then kill it the same day you plant.
  • Ironmen: open every pack the moment you earn it — the seeds inside fund your next contract and your herb runs.

❌ Avoid This

  • Ignoring contracts until 85 Farming — you leave hundreds of packs, and years of compounding anima value, on the table.
  • Letting a contract expire for days while you wait for a single patch — plant, protect, and move on.
  • Harvesting the requested crop and selling it before confirming the contract — you still need the count handed to Jane.
  • Forgetting the guild herb patch in your normal herb route — it is the cheapest contract XP you will ever earn.

💡 The Level 45 Starter Trick

At 45 Farming, grab every easy contract you can. Even the weakest seed packs feed your next tree run, and the daily XP compounds faster than most players expect. By 65 you will already have a bank tab of medium seeds waiting.

8. ❓ Farming Contracts FAQs

Q: What Farming level do I need to start Farming Guild contracts?

Level 45 Farming lets you enter the guild and take easy contracts. The contract tier upgrades to medium at 65 and hard at 85.

Q: Do I need a quest to access the Farming Guild?

You need access to Hosidius in Great Kourend, which comes through the standard Kourend quest and diary progression. Once Hosidius is reachable, the guild itself only asks for the Farming level gates described above.

Q: Can I cancel a contract I do not like?

Yes. Guildmaster Jane lets you end the current contract and start a fresh one. There is no direct penalty, but you lose the progress already invested, so only reroll when the crop is genuinely unaffordable or impossible for your level.

Q: Do outside herb patches count toward a guild contract?

No. Contract crops must be grown in the Farming Guild's own patches. Any other herb, allotment or tree patch does not count toward the requested amount.

Q: What are the rewards for completing a contract?

A seed pack scaled to your tier plus Farming XP. Higher tiers give stronger seed packs that more often contain rare herb, tree and Hespori seeds.

Q: How do I get a Hespori seed?

Hespori seeds drop from contract seed packs, most consistently at medium and hard tiers. Once obtained, plant it at the guild's Hespori patch and fight the boss there for anima seeds.

Q: Which anima seed should an Ironman plant?

Attas is the usual Ironman pick because improved yield stretches every herb and allotment harvest. If disease deaths become a problem, switch to Iasor until your compost and protection setup is solid.

Q: Are contracts worth doing for mains that already buy seeds?

Yes. Even if you never plant the rewards, seed packs resell, and the Farming XP from a one-minute daily loop beats almost any other low-effort skilling method at these levels.
