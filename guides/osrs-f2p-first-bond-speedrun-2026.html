OSRS F2P First Bond Speedrun 2026: Fastest Route to Your First Bond (20–25 Hours)

🛡️ Level: Beginner

A bond is the golden ticket of Old School RuneScape: redeem it for 14 days of membership, or sell it on the Grand Exchange for about 12.8M GP. Most players assume they must pay real money for their first one. You do not. Every F2P account can earn a bond entirely in free-to-play, and with a tight, optimized route you can do it in roughly 20–25 hours of focused play. This speedrun is built for beginners: no membership, no risky Wilderness methods beyond the safe Chaos Temple interior, and no obscure requirements. The route leans on the two highest GP/hr F2P methods — Wine of Zamorak and Ogress Warriors — backed by Yew cutting and a little F2P flipping to smooth the final stretch. If you are brand new to the game and want the wider beginner roadmap first, our New Player Guide 2026 lays out the first steps before you start grinding for a bond. 1. 🎒 What You Need Before You Start

This speedrun assumes a fresh, completely free-to-play account — no stats, no quest points, no GP. If you already have some progress, you can skip ahead. Here is the true starting line, and the discipline that keeps the run on schedule.

Account setup

Complete Tutorial Island, then claim the Stronghold of Security (10,000 GP, no requirements) — it is your starter capital and takes 10–15 minutes. Set your respawn point to Falador. Bank the starting coins; do not waste them on cosmetics or junk from the Grand Exchange. The Stronghold also teaches you the bank, the wilderness warning, and the report-abuse system, so treat it as your onboarding rather than a chore.

Skills you will build along the way

You do not need these upfront, but the route will raise them: Magic to 33 (for Telekinetic Grab → Wine of Zamorak), Attack/Strength/Defence to ~40 (for Ogress Warriors), Woodcutting to 60 (for Yews), and enough quest progress for Dragon Slayer I (unlocks Ogress Warriors and grants huge combat XP). None of this requires membership. If you want the full picture of which stats matter most early, our New Player Skill Priority Guide ranks every F2P skill by return on time.

Mindset: efficient, not casual

A "20–25 hour" speedrun means 20–25 hours of actual, focused grinding — not 20–25 hours of logged-in time with the game minimized. If you play casually, plan for 35–50 hours. The number is the floor for a player who switches methods the instant they unlock a better one. Treat the route like a shift: bank, run, repeat, with minimal idle time between trips.

💡 Buy Membership If You Can Afford It

Honest caveat: one month of membership (~$7.99) unlocks methods that earn 10x faster. If $8 is not a hardship, buy membership directly — your first month of P2P will easily cover future bonds. This speedrun is for players who want the F2P challenge or cannot spend real money. The point is choice, not pride.

2. 🎯 The Bond Target & Why Speed Matters

In August 2026 a bond trades for roughly 12.8M GP on the Grand Exchange. That is your finish line. Bond prices drift with the gold-inflation cycle and with real-world membership promotions, so track the live number before you commit — our Bond Price Surge Report explains the 2026 swings and how to time your sell or redeem. Here is why hitting the target fast matters.

The compounding effect

Every hour you spend at a low GP/hr method while a higher one is unlocked is GP lost forever. The speedrun's entire philosophy is: unlock the next method as early as possible, then pour hours into it. Wine of Zamorak at 400K–1.8M GP/hr crushes Hill Giants at 60K GP/hr — so the route gets you to 33 Magic fast, then lives there. 3. 🗺️ The Core F2P Bond Path (4 Phases)

Here is the bird's-eye plan. Times are estimates for an efficient player; your mileage will vary with luck and focus. The path is intentionally a loop: each phase unlocks the next, and Phase 4 simply stacks flipping on top of the Wine engine.

⏱️ The Four-Phase Route

  1. Phase 1 — Starter (0 → 100K GP): ~3–4 hours. Stronghold + cowhides/mining to build seed capital and early combat.
  2. Phase 2 — Unlock Wine (100K → 1M GP): ~6–8 hours. Train Magic to 33, start Wine of Zamorak, run Ogress Warriors alongside.
  3. Phase 3 — Grind (1M → 6M GP): ~7–9 hours. Pure Wine of Zamorak + Ogress Warriors, add Yew cutting as AFK filler.
  4. Phase 4 — Finish (6M → 12.8M GP): ~4–5 hours. Keep Wine grinding; deploy F2P flipping with your 1M+ surplus to accelerate the last leg.

Feed the loop left-to-right: starter capital → Wine engine → bond. Ogress Warriors and Yews reinforce the middle.

Total: ~20–26 hours. The single biggest lever is Phase 2 — getting to 33 Magic quickly. Below we break down exactly how, and which skill build makes that fastest.

4. 🧩 Recommended Skill Combinations

You cannot max every skill during a 25-hour bond run, so the order you train them decides whether you hit 12.8M in 20 hours or 40. The route is a combo, not four separate grinds — each skill unlocks the next earner. The priority order below is tuned for the shortest path to Wine of Zamorak.

Magic-first combo (recommended)

Push Magic to 33 as the very first training goal after your Stronghold seed capital. Magic is the gateway skill: Telekinetic Grab is the only F2P way to collect Wine of Zamorak, and it is also useful for the low-level enchantment/alchemy flips you will run in Phase 4. Train it by casting offensive spells on cows or chickens using air/fire runes — expect 2–3 hours. Once Wine is live, Magic keeps paying you every hour.

Combat + quest combo

Run Attack/Strength/Defence toward 40 in parallel, but do not grind them manually for long. Instead, complete the Dragon Slayer I quest line, which hands you a massive chunk of combat XP and unlocks Ogress Warriors at Corsair Cove. The quest also nudges your Magic higher, reinforcing the first combo. For the exact quest order that avoids dead ends, our Quest Requirement Wall Guide maps every prerequisite so you never waste a trip.

Woodcutting as idle fuel

Get Woodcutting to 60 only when you have downtime — while watching a show, between Wine teleports, or on a second account-monitoring session. Yews are your AFK filler, not your engine. Do not sacrifice active Wine hours to chop logs; treat Yews as the thing you do when you cannot click.

🎯 The Optimal Combo Order

  1. Stronghold (10K) → seed capital, no stats needed.
  2. Magic 33 → unlocks Wine of Zamorak (the engine).
  3. Dragon Slayer I → combat XP + Ogress Warriors unlock.
  4. Combat ~40 (mostly from the quest) → Ogress Warriors online.
  5. Woodcutting 60 (idle only) → Yew filler for the final stretch.

6. 🔍 Phase Breakdown — Method by Method

① Phase 1: 0 → 100K GP (Stronghold + Cowhides + Mining)

Claim the Stronghold of Security (10K GP). Then kill cows east of Lumbridge, collect cowhides, and tan them at Al Kharid (15K–25K GP/hr, zero requirements). Once you have ~30K, mine iron ore (15 Mining) for 30K–50K GP/hr while training Mining. Push combat on cows/goblins toward 20+ so later questing is smooth. Goal: 100K GP and ~20–30 combat stats in 3–4 hours.

② Phase 2: Get 33 Magic Fast, Then Wine of Zamorak

This is the critical unlock. To reach 33 Magic as F2P, cast Wind Strike / Confuse / Weaken on low-level monsters (goblins, chickens) using air runes — or, faster, buy fire runes and cast offensive spells on cows. Expect 2–3 hours of spellcasting to hit 33. As soon as Telekinetic Grab is available:

  • Teleport to the Chaos Temple in level 5 Wilderness (the temple building itself is safe; only the surrounding area is PvP).
  • Use Telekinetic Grab to steal Wine of Zamorak from the altar. Each wine sells for ~1,500–2,000 GP.
  • Collect 60–100 wines per hour → 100K–400K+ GP/hr. At high volume this is the best F2P method in the game.
⚠️ Wilderness Awareness

The Chaos Temple is in the Wilderness. The temple interior is not a PvP zone, but players wait outside. Always carry a one-click teleport (Varrock teleport tablet) and watch the minimap. If a skull appears, teleport immediately. Never bring more than you can afford to lose.

Run Wine of Zamorak constantly. In parallel, work toward Dragon Slayer I (requires 32 Smithing, 34 Magic recommended, plus a few low quests) — it grants massive combat XP and unlocks Ogress Warriors at Corsair Cove. By end of Phase 2 you should have ~1M GP, 33 Magic, and ~40 combat.

③ Phase 3: 1M → 6M GP (Wine + Ogress Warriors + Yews)

Now the grind begins in earnest:

  • Wine of Zamorak remains your primary earner — keep it running every hour you can focus.
  • Ogress Warriors (Corsair Cove) become your combat buffer: 60K–100K GP/hr from rune drops, herbs, and gems, while training combat for free.
  • Yew cutting at 60 Woodcutting is your AFK filler — 80K–120K GP/hr while you watch a show or between Wine trips.

Combined, a focused player adds ~500K–1M GP per active hour here. This phase is where most of your bond gets earned. The flipping techniques start helping once your bank passes 1M.

④ Phase 4: 6M → 12.8M GP (F2P Flipping Accelerator)

With 1M+ GP banked, add F2P flipping to the mix. Tradeable F2P items like nature runes, cannonballs, coal, and iron ore have healthy margins. Flip a few high-volume items between Wine runs — even 20K–80K GP/hr of passive flipping shortens the final stretch by several hours. Keep Wine of Zamorak as the backbone; flipping is the accelerator, not the engine. Use strict stop-losses so a bad flip never eats into your Wine progress. When your bank hits ~12.8M, buy a bond on the GE and redeem it (or hold it as 12.8M GP of value).

7. 🛡️ Gear & Inventory Setup (F2P)

You do not need expensive gear, but the right loadout saves hours. All of this is obtainable in F2P, and most of it is cheap on the Grand Exchange. For a full cheap-loadout plan, our Budget Gear Guide 2026 lists exact items and price targets.

Combat loadout for Ogress Warriors

Full Rune armor (buy with early GP), a Rune Scimitar (after 40 Attack), an Amulet of Power, and a shield. Bring food — trout/salmon from fishing or bought on the GE. Ogress Warriors hit hard but are slow; with rune gear you take minimal risk. Do not over-invest in a fancy weapon before your Wine engine is running — the scimitar is enough.

Wine of Zamorak loadout

Light gear (you teleport out, not fight). Carry law runes + air runes for Telekinetic Grab, a Varrock teleport tablet, and a few food items just in case. Weight does not matter here — speed of grabbing does. Keep your inventory open for wines; do not clog it with unnecessary supplies.

Inventory discipline

Keep a stack of teleport tablets always. For flipping, keep ~25% of your bank liquid so you can pounce on margins. Full gear and inventory planning is in the Budget Gear Guide 2026 linked above, and combat prep is covered in the New Player Skill Priority guide linked earlier.

💡 Spend Early GP on Unlocks, Not Cosmetics

Your first 100K should buy Rune armor and a scimitar for Ogress Warriors — not a fancy cape. Every GP reinvested into a higher GP/hr method comes back multiplied.

8. 🚫 Mistakes That Blow Your Time Budget

These are the reasons most "20 hour" bond attempts balloon to 60+. Avoid them, and the run stays on schedule.

Sticking to Hill Giants too long

Hill Giants (50K–80K GP/hr) feel safe, but the moment you have 33 Magic, Wine of Zamorak out-earns them 2–20x. Switch immediately. Loyalty to a comfortable method is the #1 time waster.

Skipping Dragon Slayer I

Dragon Slayer I is a long F2P quest, but the combat XP alone saves you hours of manual training, and it unlocks Ogress Warriors. Do it in Phase 2, not after you are stuck at 2M GP. The prerequisite maze is real, which is why the Quest Requirement Wall Guide is worth a read before you start.

Fear of the Wilderness

Wine of Zamorak is in the Wilderness, and fear keeps many players away — but the temple is safe and the risk is manageable with a teleport. Avoiding it costs you the best F2P method in the game. Learn the route, respect the minimap, and go.

Not flipping the last stretch

Sitting at 6M GP doing only Wine when you could be flipping 1M of surplus is leaving GP on the table. Flipping accelerates the final 6M→12.8M leg by several hours. Use the Advanced Flipping & Stop-Loss Guide once banked, with hard stop-losses so a bad flip never eats your Wine progress.

Burning out

Wine of Zamorak is click-intensive. If you hate it, rotate in Yew cutting and Ogress Warriors. A speedrun you quit at 8M GP is slower than a relaxed route you finish. Pace the 20–25 hours across a few sessions.

Over-buying gear early

Spending your first 500K on a cosmetic or a weapon you do not yet need starves the Wine engine of rune capital and delays the Ogress unlock. Buy only what directly raises GP/hr.

9. 🏔️ Ironman & Pre-Membership Prep

Two groups need a different mindset before starting this run: Ironmen, and players who plan to flip the bond into membership the moment they redeem it.

If you are an Ironman

Ironman accounts cannot trade with other players or use the Grand Exchange, so F2P flipping is dead and Wine of Zamorak becomes your only realistic bond path — but you also cannot sell the bond for GP, you can only redeem it for membership. That is fine: an Ironman's first bond is almost always self-earned and redeemed, never sold. Drop the flipping phase entirely, and lean harder on Wine plus Ogress Warriors. Expect the run to take a bit longer because you cannot buy runes in bulk cheaply the way a normal account can — you will craft or buy them at retail. Bank your own law/air runes via Runecrafting or the regular shops.

Pre-membership preparation

If your goal is to redeem the bond for 14 days of membership (the usual plan), use the F2P stretch to pre-stage work that pays off immediately in P2P:

  • Bank quest points — they carry over and unlock P2Pquest rewards faster.
  • Pre-train a combat style to 40+ so you can jump into member Slayer and bosses on day one.
  • Learn the GE — flipping in F2P teaches you the买卖 rhythm you will use for the far more liquid P2P economy.
  • Stack a small cash cushion — even 500K GP of leftover F2P earnings smooths your first member days before high-GP/hr methods open.

The moment membership activates, your earning ceiling jumps ~10x. The F2P bond you just ground is the key that opens it — treat the prep above as free compounding interest on that key.

10. ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I really get a bond in F2P with no membership?

Yes. Every method in this speedrun — Wine of Zamorak, Ogress Warriors, Yew cutting, and F2P flipping — is available on a free-to-play account. The finish line is ~12.8M GP, which buys a bond on the Grand Exchange. The only hard gate is 33 Magic for Wine, which any F2P account can reach.

Is 20–25 hours realistic for a beginner?

It is the floor for an efficient player who switches methods the instant they unlock them and is not afraid of the Wilderness Wine route. Casual players should budget 35–50 hours. Either way, it is achievable without spending real money. Track live bond price first via the Bond Price Surge Report so your target number is current.

What if I die at the Chaos Temple?

Death in the Wilderness drops your items where you fall, and another player can grab them. Mitigate by carrying only what you need (runes, teleport, minimal food) and teleporting the instant a skull appears. The wine itself is grabbed, not carried, so your exposure is low. Never bring your whole bank on a Wine trip.

Should I flip or just grind Wine of Zamorak?

Grind Wine as your backbone — it is the highest GP/hr F2P method. Add flipping only once you have 1M+ GP banked, as an accelerator for the final stretch. Flipping alone in F2P is slower than Wine; the two combined are fastest. Use the Advanced Flipping & Stop-Loss Guide so a bad flip never eats your Wine progress.

What should I do with the bond once I have it?

Redeem it for 14 days of membership to unlock the high-GP/hr P2P methods, or sell it for ~12.8M GP if you want to stay F2P with a fat bank. Most players redeem it — membership pays for itself within days. Ironmen can only redeem, never sell.

Can an Ironman do this speedrun?

Yes, but drop the flipping phase (no GE) and rely on Wine of Zamorak plus Ogress Warriors. You also can only redeem the bond, not sell it. Expect a slightly longer run because you cannot buy runes in bulk cheaply. The route is still fully F2P-legal for Ironmen.

Why not just buy membership with real money?

If $8 is not a hardship, buying membership directly is faster and unlocks 10x GP/hr methods immediately. This speedrun exists for players who want the F2P challenge or cannot spend real money. The honest answer: real-money membership is the most time-efficient path; the bond grind is the free path.

How much do the runes for Wine of Zamorak cost?

Telekinetic Grab costs 1 law rune + 1 air rune per cast. Law runes are the main cost (~100–200 GP each on the GE depending on the week). At 60–100 grabs per hour that is a few thousand GP/hr in runes — trivial against the 100K–400K+ GP/hr the wines return. Always buy laws in bulk to shave the margin.

What if Wine of Zamorak margins crash?

Wine prices dip when bots flood the market, but demand from High Alchemy and training is constant, so the floor is stable. If your local margin drops, shift hours to Ogress Warriors and Yews and check back. The four-method combo exists precisely so one soft market cannot stall your whole run.

Do I need the Stronghold of Security?

Not strictly, but its 10K GP is free starter capital and the safest first 15 minutes in the game. Skipping it just means a slower Phase 1. Claim it — there is no downside, and it teaches the bank and safety systems you will use all run.

11. 🎯 Final Tips — Hit 12.8M Faster

  • Unlock 33 Magic early. It is the single highest-leverage goal in the whole speedrun — Wine of Zamorak is your engine.
  • Do Dragon Slayer I in Phase 2. The combat XP and Ogress Warrior access are worth the quest time.
  • Never sit on a better method. The instant Wine unlocks, Hill Giants are done. Loyalty costs GP.
  • Keep a teleport tablet on you always. Wilderness safety is 90% preparation, 10% reaction.
  • Flip the surplus in Phase 4. 1M+ banked means flipping accelerates the last leg by hours.
  • Rotate methods to avoid burnout. Wine → Yews → Ogress Warriors → Wine keeps the 20–25 hours sustainable.
  • Reinvest every 100K into unlocks. Rune gear, scimitar, quest supplies — not cosmetics.
  • Redeem the bond for membership. P2P methods earn 10x faster; your first bond should open the door, not sit in the bank.
