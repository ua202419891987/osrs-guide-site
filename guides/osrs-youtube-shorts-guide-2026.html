How to grow your OSRS channel with Shorts — drive traffic to your site

Updated: May 2026 | By OSRS Guru

YouTube Shorts are the fastest way to grow an OSRS channel in 2026. This guide covers exactly how to make viral OSRS Shorts, including best topics, editing tips, and how to drive traffic to your website.

Why YouTube Shorts Work for OSRS

  • Massive reach: Shorts can get 10K-1M views (even with 0 subscribers)
  • Algorithm push: YouTube actively promotes Shorts to new viewers
  • Short attention: OSRS tips fit perfectly in 60 seconds
  • Traffic driver: Pin a comment with your website link

How to Make a Viral OSRS Short (Step-by-Step)

Step 1: Record Gameplay

  1. Use OBS Studio (free) to record OSRS gameplay
  2. Record in 1920x1080 (YouTube will crop to 9:16)
  3. Keep clips under 50 seconds (leave room for intro/outro)

Step 2: Edit for Mobile

  1. Use CapCut (free, mobile) or DaVinci Resolve (PC)
  2. Crop to 9:16 aspect ratio (vertical)
  3. Add captions (70% watch without sound)
  4. Add trending music (YouTube audio library = no copyright strike)

Step 3: Upload & Optimize

  1. Title: How to Make 3M GP/hr in OSRS (2026)
  2. Thumbnail: Bright, high contrast, big text
  3. Pin a comment: "Full guide: osrsguru.com/guides/[link]"

How to Drive Traffic to Your Website

Method 1: Pinned Comment (Safest)

  • After uploading, pin a comment: "Full written guide with tables: [link]"
  • This is allowed by YouTube TOS — very safe

Method 2: Description Link (Allowed)

  • Put your website link in the first line of description
  • YouTube allows this — just don't make the whole description a link farm

Method 3: End Screen (Advanced)

  • Add an end screen pointing to your website (needs 100+ subscribers)
  • Alternatively, link to your full-length video (which links to your site)

YouTube Shorts Algorithm Tips (2026)

  • Hook in first 3 seconds: "You won't believe this OSRS method..."
  • Use trending audio: YouTube audio library = no copyright issues
  • Post at peak times: 18:00-21:00 UTC (Mon-Thu)
  • Engage with comments: Reply to ALL comments in first 24 hours

Common Mistakes

  • Making horizontal videos: Shorts MUST be vertical (9:16)
  • No captions: 70% watch without sound — captions are essential
  • Too long: Keep it under 55 seconds (algorithm prefers shorter)
  • No CTA: Always tell viewers to "check the pinned comment"
