1. 🧭 Why the Lumby Elite Is Your First Elite

Adsterra ad slot — paste your Adsterra banner code here

There are 13 achievement diary regions in OSRS, each with an Elite tier. You do not need to do them in any particular order — but if you only finish one Elite this year, make it this one. The reason is simple: the Lumbridge & Draynor Elite rewards are utility perks that touch every activity you do afterward, not one-off boss shortcuts you visit twice.

Before you commit, read our Achievement Diary master guide for how tiers stack. The short version: finishing Elite keeps every lower-tier reward and adds the big ones on top. The Lumby Elite is also the cheapest Elite to access because it never forces you into a raid or a high-level boss.

① The rewards that pay off daily

Two perks alone justify the grind. First, fairy rings without a Dramen or Lunar staff — that is a permanent free inventory slot on every Slayer trip, farm run, and clue hunt for the rest of your account's life. Second, the 7th Slayer task block slot, which only unlocks at 250 quest points and is boosted by this diary. If you hate specific Slayer tasks, this is the single biggest quality-of-life unlock in the diary system.

② Why it beats harder region diaries early

Compare the effort: the Lumby Elite needs skilling and questing, full stop. The Wilderness Elite needs PK-safe bossing; the Kourend Elite needs the Chambers of Xeric; the Western Elite needs Corporess / Zulrah-adjacent content. None of that is required here. For a mid-to-high account that already quests, the Lumby Elite is the fastest route to an Elite-rooted reward set and the Quest Cape journey it forces is itself a completionist milestone.

③ What you keep from lower tiers

Completing the Elite does not replace the Easy/Medium/Hard rewards — you keep all of them. That includes the hard-tier 10% Tears of Guthix bonus, unlimited Falador cabbage-patch teleports, and the 15,000-XP lamp. The Elite simply layers the game-changing perks on top, which is why the Easy/Medium complete guide is worth doing first if you have not cleared the lower tiers.

2. 📋 Full Elite Task Checklist (6 Tasks)

The Elite tier is exactly six tasks. They look short on paper, but four of them are skill checks in the 70s–80s and one requires the Quest Cape. Here is the complete list with the exact gates and the gotchas that waste trips.

# Elite Task Hard Requirements Key Gotcha
1 Steal from a Dorgesh-Kaan rich chest 78 Thieving, Death to the Dorgeshuun, Lockpick Chest is in the west wing; bring food, the area is multi-way
2 Grapple a pylon on the Dorgesh-Kaan Agility Course 70 Agility, 70 Ranged, 70 Strength, Mith grapple + crossbow + light source Replaced the old Movario pickpocket on 10 Jul 2024 — do NOT look for Movario
3 Chop magic logs at the Mage Training Arena 75 Woodcutting, any axe Use the magic tree inside the Arena, not the one outside
4 Smith an Adamant platebody in Draynor sewer 88 Smithing, 5 Adamantite bars, Hammer Must be down the sewer; anvil is past the zombies
5 Craft 140+ Water runes at once 76 Runecraft (57 w/ 3 Raiments, 38 w/ 4), 28 essence, Water altar Essence only — no extracts; bring 28 to guarantee 140+
6 Perform the Quest Cape emote in the Wise Old Man's house All quests complete, Quest point cape House is in Draynor; equip the cape and use the emote

① Tasks 1–2: Thieving & the grapple

The rich chest (78 Thieving) is the easiest of the high-skill tasks if you already thieve — just complete Death to the Dorgeshuun first and bring a lockpick plus some food. The grapple course is the one people fail: it needs 70 Agility, 70 Ranged, and 70 Strength simultaneously, a Mith grapple, any crossbow, and a light source. As of the 10 July 2024 update this replaced the old Movario pickpocket, so ignore any older guide telling you to find Movario.

② Tasks 3–4: Woodcutting & Smithing

Chopping magic logs at the Mage Training Arena (75 Woodcutting) is trivial if you have the level — walk into the Arena and use your axe on the magic tree there. The Smithing task is the real gate: 88 Smithing to smith an Adamant platebody, and it must be done on the anvil down Draynor sewer, past the zombies. Bank your 5 Adamantite bars and hammer, run down, and smith it on the spot.

③ Tasks 5–6: Runecraft & the Quest Cape

Crafting 140+ Water runes needs 76 Runecraft (or 57 with three Raiments of the Eye pieces, 38 with four) and 28 essence at the Water altar — note extracts do not count, and you must use plain essence. The final task is the infamous one: perform the Quest Cape emote in the Wise Old Man's house in Draynor, which means you have completed every quest in the game. This is the bottleneck that decides whether the diary takes a weekend or a month.

3. 🚧 Skill & Quest Gates — The Real Wall

Most "I can't finish the Lumby Elite" stories are not about the tasks — they are about the gates. Here is the honest ladder and where players get stuck.

① The skill requirement ladder

Elite-tier skills required: 88 Smithing, 78 Thieving, 76 Runecraft, 75 Woodcutting, 70 Agility, 70 Ranged, 70 Strength. The lower tiers (which you must also clear) add 70 Crafting (amulet of power), 65 Farming (Belladonna), 63 Farming, 59 Runecraft, 52 Prayer, and others. Smithing 88 is usually the tallest single wall — plan a Giant's Foundry or blast-furnace push well in advance.

② The Quest Cape bottleneck

The Quest Cape emote task is the only one that can balloon from days to months. Every new quest Jagex releases raises the bar, and grandmaster quests such as Desert Treasure II and the Myreque finale are now required. Our Quest Cape guide tracks the current full list; our Optimal Quest Order is the fastest path to all of them. Do the questing early so the emote is the victory lap, not the blocker.

③ Prerequisite tiers you must clear first

You cannot attempt the Elite until Easy, Medium, and Hard are all complete — the diary interface gates it. The Hard tier alone wants 70 Crafting, 65 Farming, 63 Farming, 59 Runecraft, 52 Prayer, Tears of Guthix, Another Slice of H.A.M., and full Recipe for Disaster. If you are starting from scratch, treat the whole diary as a 4-stage project and bank the lamps from each tier as you go.

4. 🎒 Gear & Inventory Prep Sheet

Because the tasks are scattered (Dorgesh-Kaan, Mage Training Arena, Draynor sewer, Water altar, Draynor village), one well-packed trip saves hours of backtracking. Pack for all six at once.

① Must-bring items per task

  • Lockpick + food (rich chest, Thieving)
  • Mith grapple + any crossbow + light source (grapple course — a bullseye lantern or a lit mining helmet works)
  • Any axe (magic logs, 75 WC)
  • 5 Adamantite bars + Hammer (Adamant platebody, 88 Smithing)
  • 28 Rune/Pure/Daeyalt essence + Water tiara/talisman (140+ Water runes, 76 RC)
  • Quest point cape equipped (emote task)

② Teleport loadout

Build a teleport set that hits every location: a Dorgesh-Kaan teleport (or the train from Keldagrim after the quest), a Skills necklace (Mage Training Arena), a Draynor teleport (amulet of glory / explorer's ring), a Water altar run (or Abyss), and a Wise Old Man's house jog from Draynor bank. The Explorer's ring 3 (from the Hard tier) also gives unlimited Falador cabbage-patch teleports as a free travel node.

③ Boostable vs not

💡 Which levels you can stew up

Runecraft (76) is boostable with Raiments of the Eye — at 57 with three pieces or 38 with four you can still hit 140+ water runes, so don't panic if you're a few levels short. Smithing 88, Thieving 78, Woodcutting 75, and the 70/70/70 grapple stats are not boostable for the task check, so those must be trained outright. Plan the non-boostable grinds first.

The Explorer's ring 4 is the Elite reward, and it bundles six perks. Here is what each is worth to a real account.

① Fairy ring freedom (the inventory slot)

This is the headline perk. A free inventory slot on every fairy-ring trip compounds into thousands of extra resources banked over an account's lifetime — more brews on a Slayer trip, more seeds on a farm run, more clue items. No other Elite diary gives a permanent slot like this.

② The 7th Slayer block slot

Slayer is most players' longest grind. The 7th block (available at 250 quest points, which the diary effectively requires you to reach) lets you permanently shelve one more dead-weight task. Over a 1–99 Slayer journey that is a measurable XP and GP difference, not a rounding error.

③ Daily High Alchemy & the lamp

Thirty free High Alchemy casts per day (you still need 55 Magic and the items, but no nature runes) turns junk drops into steady GP with zero extra effort. The 50,000-XP antique lamp, usable in any skill at level 70+, is a clean one-time jump — drop it into your weakest 70+ skill or bank it for a future push.

6. 🗺️ Efficient 9-Step Completion Route

If your skills are already at the gates, the whole Elite can be done in a single focused session. If they are not, follow this order so the longest grinds start first.

① Phase A — Quest & prep (steps 1–3)

Step 1: Finish every quest for the Quest Cape (start this months early if needed). Step 2: Train the non-boostable skills — 88 Smithing, 78 Thieving, 75 Woodcutting, 70/70/70 Agility/Ranged/Strength. Step 3: Pack the inventory prep sheet from Section 4 so you never make a return trip.

② Phase B — Skilling tasks (steps 4–6)

Step 4: Smith the Adamant platebody in Draynor sewer. Step 5: Chop magic logs at the Mage Training Arena. Step 6: Craft 140+ Water runes at the Water altar (use Raiments of the Eye if boostable). These three are quick once skilled.

③ Phase C — Combat finish (steps 7–9)

Step 7: Steal from the Dorgesh-Kaan rich chest (78 Thieving + food). Step 8: Grapple the Dorgesh-Kaan pylon (70/70/70 + grapple). Step 9: Equip the Quest Cape and perform the emote in the Wise Old Man's house — claim Explorer's ring 4. Talk to Hatius Cosaintus if you ever lose the ring.

7. ❓ Lumby Elite FAQs

Do I have to complete Easy, Medium, and Hard before the Elite?

Yes. The diary interface gates the Elite tier behind full completion of the three lower tiers. The good news: you keep every lower-tier reward, so the Elite is purely additive. If you have not started, work up the tiers in order and bank the XP lamps as you go.

What is the hardest requirement in the Lumby Elite?

For most accounts it is a tie between 88 Smithing (the Adamant platebody task, not boostable) and the Quest Cape emote (requires every quest). Smithing you can train; the Quest Cape is a scheduling problem — start questing early using our Optimal Quest Order so it is done before you are ready for the emote.

Can I boost the Runecraft requirement for the Water rune task?

Yes. The task needs 140+ Water runes from 28 essence. At 76 Runecraft that is guaranteed; at 57 with three Raiments of the Eye pieces, or 38 with four pieces, it is still possible thanks to the set bonus. You cannot use extracts — plain essence only. Bring 28 to be safe.

Where exactly do I do the grapple task? I can't find Movario.

You don't — Movario was removed on 10 July 2024. The current task is to grapple a pylon on the Dorgesh-Kaan Agility Course, which needs 70 Agility, 70 Ranged, and 70 Strength, plus a Mith grapple, any crossbow, and a light source. Complete Death to the Dorgeshuun first for access.

Is the fairy-ring-no-staff perk really worth the whole grind?

For any account that Slayers, farms, or does clues regularly, yes. A permanent free inventory slot on every fairy-ring trip is the single most-used perk in the entire diary system. Add the 7th Slayer block and daily High Alchemy and the Elite pays for its own effort within weeks.

What happens to my rewards if I lose Explorer's ring 4?

Talk to Hatius Cosaintus (the Lumbridge & Draynor diary NPC) and he will replace it for free. The fairy-ring, Slayer block, and discount perks are account-wide and are not lost with the ring — only the ring's daily alch and run-energy functions need the item.

Should an Ironman do the Lumby Elite before other elites?

Almost always yes. Ironmen feel the inventory-slot and Slayer-block perks hardest because they cannot buy their way past slow grinds. The fairy-ring freedom alone saves thousands of banked resources. See our Achievement Diary beginner guide for the Ironman-specific route.
