1. What Is Varlamore (and Why It Matters)

Varlamore is a map expansion for OSRS located just south of the Kebos Lowlands. Released across three content drops between 2024 and 2025, it added an entire kingdom — a capital city, multiple biomes, new quests, bosses, skilling activities, and a transport network. For many players it is now the default mid-to-late game hub.

① Why players flock to Varlamore

🎯 The draw

  • Self-sufficient skilling: Hunter's Guild rumours, Mastering Mixology, and the Colossal Wyrm agility course changed several skill metas.
  • Group & solo bosses: Moons of Peril, Hueycoatl, and Doom of Mokhaiotl offer content for every account tier.
  • Teleport hub: Once unlocked, Varlamore is one of the most connected regions in the game.
  • Ironman-friendly: So much is obtainable in-region that it earned the nickname "Land of Ironman."
💡 How Varlamore compares to Kourend

If Great Kourend was OSRS's first "modern continent," Varlamore is its polished successor. Both are quest-gated regions packed with skilling and bosses, but Varlamore's Quetzal network and compact capital make day-to-day travel far smoother. Many players who outgrew Kourend content now treat Civitas illa Fortis as their default home bank and teleport hub, much like they once did with Hosidius or Shayzien.

2. How to Get There — Quests & the Quetzal Network

Access is gated behind a short quest chain, but the payoff is a fast, permanent travel system.

② Step 1 — Children of the Sun

This is a short introductory quest that unlocks Varlamore itself. After completing it, run to the East Varrock gate, where a Quetzal bird named Primio will fly you to Varlamore for the first time.

③ Step 2 — Twilight's Promise

Unlocks the real travel system

  • A teleport to Varlamore in your spellbook (requires 54 Magic).
  • Civitas illa Fortis teleport tabs.
  • The full Quetzal Transport Network across 14 locations.

④ The Quetzal Transport Network

DetailNotes
Total locations14 Quetzal landing spots around Varlamore.
Unlocked by Twilight's Promise8 locations (including Civitas illa Fortis, Hunter Guild, Quetzacalli Gorge, Sunset Coast, Tal Teklan, The Teomat, Aldarin, Auburnvale).
Buildable6 more (Cam Torum Entrance, Colossal Wyrm Remains, Fortis Colosseum, Kastori, Outer Fortis, Salvager Overlook).
Build cost per spot10 Quetzal feed + 3 Limestone brick + 4 Soft clay. Quetzal feed is bought on the GE or earned from Hunter's Rumours.

3. The Three-Part Rollout (2024–2025)

Varlamore shipped in three major updates, each adding a layer of content.

⑤ Timeline

ReleaseDateHeadline additions
Varlamore: Part OneMarch 20, 2024Civitas illa Fortis, Hunter's Guild, Moons of Peril, Fortis Colosseum.
Varlamore: The Rising Darkness (Part Two)September 25, 2024Hueycoatl group boss, Colossal Wyrm agility course, Mastering Mixology.
Varlamore: The Final Dawn (Part Three)July 23, 2025Doom of Mokhaiotl (Delve Boss), Vale Totems fletching minigame, more quests, a new slayer dungeon.
💡 Why the split matters

Because content arrived across three drops, older guides often cover only Part One. If you are returning in 2026, you now have the full region — including the Doom of Mokhaiotl delve fight added in Part Three.

Each release built directly on the last, so over roughly sixteen months Varlamore grew from a single capital city into a complete late-game hub with its own bosses, skills, and transport — a rare example of a region that kept expanding long after launch.

4. The Six Regions of Varlamore

Varlamore divides into six distinct areas, each with its own content.

All six are stitched together by the Quetzal transport network, so once you have built the key landing spots you can hop between them in seconds. This connectivity is what makes region-locked grinds — like Hunter's Guild rumours in Civitas illa Fortis or the Moons of Peril in the Tlati Rainforest — feel fast rather than tedious.

① Civitas illa Fortis

The capital city and your main hub — banks, shops, the Hunter's Guild entrance, and the Fortis Colosseum. Most travel funnels through here.

② Auburnvale

A residential town area with its own Quetzal stop and access to surrounding content.

③ Hailstorm Mountains

Mountainous terrain connecting routes and housing some of the region's wildlife and activities.

④ Tlati Rainforest

The lush rainforest biome — home to the Moons of Peril and the Ruins of Mokhaiotl (Doom's delve).

⑤ Aldarin

A southern city region with its own Quetzal stop and later-game content access.

⑥ Avium Savannah

The open savannah biome, tied into Hunter's Guild rumours and the region's hunting loop.

5. Part One Content — Hunter's Guild, Moons & Colosseum

⑥ Hunter's Guild

🏹 Hunter's Guild & Rumours

  • Take Hunter's Guild rumours from the guild in Civitas illa Fortis for repeatable, high-XP hunting tasks.
  • One of the best Hunter training methods in the game and a steady Quetzal feed source.

⑦ Moons of Peril

🌙 A 4-boss progression fight

The Moons of Peril is a popular mid-level boss encounter requiring completion of Children of the Sun and a recommended 70+ combat. It is a staple step-up boss before end-game PvM and a strong early money maker.

⑧ Fortis Colosseum

🏟️ Wave-based combat challenge

The Fortis Colosseum is a wave-survival style challenge in Civitas illa Fortis, testing your sustain and DPS under pressure for unique rewards.

💡 Why Part One content connects

Part One's additions are deliberately beginner-friendly. The Hunter's Guild scales its rumours to your level, the Moons of Peril teach mechanical bossing without punishing early mistakes, and the Fortis Colosseum rewards sustain over gear. Together they form a natural bridge from mid-level skilling into the bossing loop that the later Varlamore parts expand on, which is why most players experience the region in this order.

6. Part Two & Three — Hueycoatl, Doom, Mixology, Totems

⑨ Hueycoatl (Part Two)

🐉 Darkfrost group boss

  • A draconic group boss atop the Darkfrost, scalable solo to 20 players.
  • 60% weak to Earth; drops the Dragon hunter wand and Tome of earth.
  • See our full Hueycoatl boss guide.

⑩ Mastering Mixology (Part Two)

⚗️ Herblore activity

Mastering Mixology is a Herblore training activity offering an alternative, often AFK route to Herblore XP and supplies without grinding secondary ingredients the traditional way.

⑪ Colossal Wyrm agility course (Part Two)

🌀 High-tier agility

The Colossal Wyrm agility course is a strong late-game Agility training method introduced with Part Two.

⑫ Doom of Mokhaiotl (Part Three)

🔥 The Delve Boss

  • OSRS's first true Delve Boss, beneath the Tlati Rainforest at the Ruins of Mokhaiotl.
  • Scales deeper the further you push; demonbane is effectively mandatory past delve 5.
  • See our full Doom of Mokhaiotl guide.

⑬ Vale Totems (Part Three)

🏹 Fletching minigame

The Vale Totems fletching minigame, added in Part Three, provides a fresh Fletching training loop with its own rewards.

7. Why Varlamore Is the "Land of Ironman"

Varlamore earned its Ironman nickname because so much progression is obtainable in-region without the Grand Exchange or trading. An Ironman can train Hunter, Herblore, Agility, Fletching, and Slayer, then fight bosses for best-in-slot drops — all without leaving the kingdom.

🛠️ What an Ironman can self-supply here

  • Hunter: Guild rumours give both XP and the Quetzal feed needed to build transport.
  • Herblore: Mastering Mixology removes much of the secondary-farming grind.
  • Gear: Moons of Peril and Hueycoatl drops translate directly into mid-game upgrades with no GE required.
  • Agility & Fletching: The Colossal Wyrm course and Vale Totems cover two more skills in-region.

🎯 Ironman Starter Path in Varlamore

→ Step 1: Unlock the Quetzal network via Twilight's Promise — your travel backbone.
→ Step 2: Farm Hunter's Guild rumours for XP and Quetzal feed.
→ Step 3: Push Moons of Peril then Hueycoatl for gear upgrades.

8. Varlamore FAQs

Q: How do I unlock Varlamore?

Complete the short quest Children of the Sun, then take the Quetzal (Primio) from the East Varrock gate. Completing Twilight's Promise afterwards unlocks the spellbook teleport, Civitas illa Fortis tabs, and the full Quetzal network.

Q: When was Varlamore released?

In three parts: Part One on March 20, 2024; The Rising Darkness (Part Two) on September 25, 2024; and The Final Dawn (Part Three) on July 23, 2025.

Q: What is the capital of Varlamore?

Civitas illa Fortis — the central city housing banks, shops, the Hunter's Guild, and the Fortis Colosseum.

Q: What are the six regions of Varlamore?

Civitas illa Fortis, Auburnvale, Hailstorm Mountains, Tlati Rainforest, Aldarin, and Avium Savannah.

Q: Is Varlamore good for Ironmen?

Extremely. So much training and bossing is available in-region without the GE or trading that it is widely called the "Land of Ironman."

Q: What is the best money-making in Varlamore?

For most accounts, Hueycoatl (around 1.8 million GP/hr solo) and the Moons of Peril are the headline earners, with Doom of Mokhaiotl deep delves offering high upside for prepared accounts.

Q: Do I need to do all three parts' content?

No single quest gates it all, but progressing the Varlamore quest line unlocks transport and activities. Children of the Sun + Twilight's Promise are the minimum to move around freely; later quests open the delve boss and slayer dungeon.

Q: Where is the Doom of Mokhaiotl?

Beneath the Tlati Rainforest at the Ruins of Mokhaiotl, added in Part Three (The Final Dawn, July 2025).
