OSRS Ghorrock Teleport Guide 2026 — Level 96 Magic, Runes & the Real Route to the Frozen Waste Plateau

Published September 5, 2026  |  15 min read  |  By Yanchun Wu

Ghorrock Teleport is the highest-level teleport in Old School RuneScape — a level 96 Ancient Magicks spell that costs only 2 law and 8 water runes and drops you beside the ruined Zarosian fortress on the Frozen Waste Plateau. It is also one of the most misunderstood spells in the game: it is not on the Lunar spellbook, it does not cost blood runes, and you do not need 96 real Magic to use its tablet. This guide separates the facts from the myths, then shows you what the teleport is actually worth unlocking for.

1. The Ghorrock Confusion — Ancients vs Lunar

Search "ghorrock teleport" in any clan chat and the same argument repeats: someone insists it is a Lunar spell unlocked through Lunar Diplomacy and Dream Mentor, while someone else insists it is an Ancient Magicks spell from Desert Treasure. The second person is right — in OSRS, Ghorrock Teleport sits on the Ancient Magicks spellbook, not the Lunar book, and it is locked behind Desert Treasure I. The mix-up is so common it deserves clearing up before we touch a single rune.

The confusion comes from the Lunar spellbook's own far-north teleport, Ice Plateau Teleport at level 89 Magic, which lands on the same frozen region. Both spells serve players heading to the same north-west snowfield, but they are different spells on different books. When an old guide says "Lunar players just teleport to Ghorrock," it usually means Ice Plateau Teleport — and the two names get welded together in community memory. The game never mixes them up: Ghorrock is the ruins, Ice Plateau is the neighbouring snow, and each spellbook gets only one of them.

Ghorrock is also not a boss, raid or instance in OSRS. It is the ruined Zarosian fortress on the Frozen Waste Plateau, in the far north-west corner of the Wilderness — in lore a great Second Age settlement, in gameplay simply the landmark your teleport lands beside. Anyone expecting a monster will be disappointed: the value of the name is almost entirely the spell and the travel it unlocks.

💡 The one-sentence memory hook

Ghorrock = the level 96 Ancient teleport. Ice Plateau = the level 89 Lunar teleport. Same frozen neighbourhood, two different books — and the Lunar spell is the faster way to the Agility Course.

2. Unlock Requirements — Desert Treasure I vs the Lunar Quest Chain

Two different spells reach the same plateau, so "unlocking Ghorrock" has two honest answers. The spell actually named Ghorrock Teleport needs one quest but a brutal Magic level; the Lunar alternative asks for a longer quest chain at a lower level.

① For the real Ghorrock Teleport — Desert Treasure I

Ghorrock Teleport is an Ancient Magicks spell, and the whole Ancient book is unlocked by completing Desert Treasure I, the master quest across the Kharidian Desert where you fight the four elemental bosses. Without it there is no Ghorrock to cast — the spell simply will not exist on your book. The quest asks for roughly 50 Magic and supporting stats in the 50s, plus the ability to survive several punishing bosses; check the live OSRS Wiki for the exact current list before you plan the run.

② For the Lunar alternative — Lunar Diplomacy, then Dream Mentor

If your account lives on the Lunar spellbook — Vengeance for bossing, Plank Make for construction — the spell you want is Ice Plateau Teleport. Every Lunar spell is unlocked by Lunar Diplomacy, a quest with an unusual catch: its skill requirements are not boostable. Lokar Searunner will not even talk to you without the real levels of 65 Magic, 60 Mining, 55 Woodcutting, 61 Crafting, 49 Firemaking, 40 Defence and 5 Herblore, plus The Fremennik Trials, Lost City, Rune Mysteries and Shilo Village behind you.

Dream Mentor is the natural follow-up — it needs Lunar Diplomacy and 85 Combat — but it does not unlock any teleport. It rewards 10,000 Magic XP and the top tier of the Lunar book, most famously the Vengeance family, plus Heal Group and Spellbook Swap (check the live OSRS Wiki for the exact list). It enters Ghorrock talk through Spellbook Swap: at level 96 Lunar Magic you can swap to the Ancient book for a single cast and fire a real Ghorrock Teleport — an expensive party trick, but the one genuine link between Dream Mentor and "Ghorrock."

3. Spell Data — Level, Runes & XP

Here are the numbers that matter, from the current OSRS Wiki pages for the spell and its tablet — and read the runes carefully, because this is where most players misremember the spell.

🧊 Ghorrock Teleport — Official Stats

  • Spellbook: Ancient Magicks (requires Desert Treasure I)
  • Magic level required: 96 — the highest Magic requirement of any teleport in the game, tied with Spellbook Swap
  • Rune cost per cast: 2 law runes + 8 water runes — not blood, not astral
  • Experience per cast: 106 Magic XP
  • Destination: the Frozen Waste Plateau beside the ruins of Ghorrock, near the level 44 obelisk in the north-west Wilderness

If the rune cost surprises you, you are in good company. Several other high-level Ancient wilderness teleports genuinely burn blood runes, so players assume Ghorrock does too. The OSRS Wiki cost table is unambiguous: Ghorrock needs only law and water — cheap by deep-wild standards.

① The tablet — Ghorrock Teleport without the Magic level

The Ghorrock teleport tablet is a tradeable, stackable item that bypasses the 96 Magic requirement completely. Breaking it has no Magic level requirement at all — the only gate is Desert Treasure I on your account. Breaking gives no experience and shows the same wilderness warning as the spell, which you can toggle off through the Configure option.

📦 The Ghorrock Teleport Tablet

  • Use requirement: Desert Treasure I completed; no Magic level needed to break it
  • Making one: at the ancient lectern inside the Ancient Pyramid, 96 Magic on the Ancient spellbook, soft clay + 2 law + 8 water runes — 106 XP per tablet
  • Other sources: rare drops from Wilderness Slayer Cave monsters, and tradeable on the Grand Exchange (prices move — check the live GE graph)

The Lunar twin exists too: the Ice plateau teleport tablet, crafted on the Lunar lectern at level 89 with 3 law, 3 astral and 8 water runes, tradeable on the Grand Exchange. Between the two tablet families, any account with the right quest done can reach the frozen north without spending a Magic level on the journey.

4. What Ghorrock Gets You — Agility Course, Elite Diary & the Frozen North

A level 96 teleport needs strong reasons to exist. Ghorrock has three genuinely useful ones.

① The Wilderness Agility Course — the headline use

The OSRS Wiki transport guide for the Wilderness Agility Course ranks the Ice Plateau and Ghorrock teleports as the two fastest ways to reach it. From Ghorrock you run north-west, through the gates, then east; from Lunar Ice Plateau you simply run east. That matters because the course is the best raw Agility XP in OSRS when lapped correctly, and it pays Wilderness agility tickets plus — if you deposit the optional 150,000 GP into the dispenser — noted alchables, blighted items and a 1 in 40 medium clue chance per lap (1 in 20 with a ring of wealth (i)). The catch: it is singles-plus PvP territory and needs level 52 Agility, so it is an endgame spot rather than a casual one.

② The Elite Wilderness Diary task

Casting the Ghorrock Teleport spell is a specific requirement of the Elite Wilderness Diary, which turns a curiosity into a necessity for every diary cape chaser. Note the wording: the spell must be cast, so a bank tab of Ghorrock tablets will not satisfy the task. You need Desert Treasure I done and the level for it (or a valid boost).

③ Deep-Wilderness access and the level 44 obelisk

Ghorrock lands you right beside the level 44 obelisk, from which the wilderness obelisk network bounces you across the deep wild — handy for clue steps, wilderness Slayer tasks and scattered northern content — and it is the closest teleport to several frozen-plateau clue locations. It is not for blood runecrafting (the Blood Altar is in Morytania; see the OSRS Wiki for routes) or mainland Troll Country (use Trollheim Teleport). Keep it for the Wilderness and it will not disappoint.

💡 Route logic for ironmen and low-Magic accounts

Ironmen cannot buy tablets, so the ranking changes for them: the obelisk network is the realistic early option, the Lunar Ice Plateau spell is the goal once Lunar Diplomacy is done, and Ghorrock itself is a very late upgrade justified mainly by the Elite Wilderness Diary.

6. Is It Worth Training For? Boosts, Costs & a Verdict

The honest answer is that Ghorrock Teleport is rarely the reason anyone trains Magic — but it is a genuinely useful by-product of reaching the high 90s, and for some accounts it is a real unlock goal.

① Can you boost to cast it?

Partially — and this is where most guides go wrong. A standard Magic potion boost counts toward casting spells above your real level, so from base 92 a single dose reaches 96 and fires a real Ghorrock Teleport while the boost holds. Not every boost works this way — the OSRS Wiki temporary boost table notes that some, like the Kodai potion inside the Chambers of Xeric, do not allow higher-level spells — so read those notes first if your plan depends on a boost. And remember the reverse trap: Lunar Diplomacy's quest requirements cannot be boosted even though the teleport it unlocks can.

② The real cost picture

Per cast the spell is cheap — 2 law and 8 water runes, no blood, no astral. The expensive part is the level: from the low 90s to 96 is a real grind, and there is no tradeable unlock to buy. Most players simply arrive there through bossing, bursting or alching.

✅ Unlock it early if you tick any of these:

  • You chase the Elite Wilderness Diary or diary cape — the spell is a hard requirement
  • You grind the Wilderness Agility Course and want the fastest repeatable route to it
  • You are already 92+ Magic — a potion boost makes the cast available today

❌ Skip it for now if any of these fit:

  • You are below 92 Magic with no diary plans — the tablet does the same job without the grind
  • You have not completed Desert Treasure I — the whole Ancient book is locked regardless of level
  • You are an early-game ironman — the Lunar Ice Plateau spell at 89 serves you better

The one-line verdict: Ghorrock Teleport is a 96 Magic spell whose real value is the Elite Wilderness Diary requirement and top-tier Agility Course access — unlock it when either goal is yours, and let the tradeable tablet cover your travel needs until then.

7. Pro Tips & Safety

Treat the teleport like the wilderness tool it is, and it will serve you for years.

  • Respect the warning screen. Both the spell and the tablet warn you before entering the Wilderness. On a PvP-enabled world that is your last abort chance — the plateau sits at level 44+ Wilderness — so hop to a low-population world or bring minimal risk gear.
  • It is just a spell. Unlike a scroll unlock, Ghorrock Teleport never expires and costs only runes per use — there is zero downside to having it sit on your Ancient book.
  • Pair it with the Lunar route. For daily course runs, the Lunar Ice Plateau spell at 89 Magic is the faster habit; keep Ghorrock for the diary task and emergencies.
  • Know your tablets. The Ghorrock tablet needs Desert Treasure I; the Ice plateau tablet needs Lunar Diplomacy. Buy the one your quest list allows.
  • Check live GE prices. Tablet prices swing with wilderness updates and diary seasons, so never assume last week's price is this week's.

8. Ghorrock Teleport FAQs

Q: Is Ghorrock Teleport on the Lunar spellbook?

No. Ghorrock Teleport is an Ancient Magicks spell unlocked by Desert Treasure I. The Lunar equivalent is Ice Plateau Teleport at 89 Magic, which lands on the same frozen region — the two are constantly confused.

Q: What Magic level do I need for Ghorrock Teleport?

Level 96 is the real requirement listed on the spell. Because a standard Magic potion boost counts toward casting higher-level spells in OSRS, you can cast it from a base of 92 while boosted — but not every boost works that way, so check the OSRS Wiki temporary boost notes first.

Q: What runes does Ghorrock Teleport cost?

2 law runes and 8 water runes per cast, for 106 Magic XP. No blood runes and no astral runes. The blood-rune rumour comes from the other high-level Ancient wilderness teleports.

Q: Do I need Desert Treasure I to use it?

Yes. The spell only exists after Desert Treasure I, and the tablet requires the quest too — even though it has no Magic level requirement.

Q: Can I use the tablet without 96 Magic?

Yes. Breaking the tablet has no Magic level requirement. It is tradeable on the Grand Exchange and drops as a rare reward from Wilderness Slayer Cave monsters. Making one yourself needs 96 Magic on the Ancient spellbook at the Ancient Pyramid lectern.

Q: Is it a Wilderness diary requirement?

Yes — casting the Ghorrock Teleport spell is an Elite Wilderness Diary task. The wording matters: the spell itself must be cast, so breaking a tablet will not count.

Q: Is Ghorrock a boss or raid in OSRS?

No. Ghorrock is the ruined fortress at the Frozen Waste Plateau — a location, not an encounter. "Doing Ghorrock" in chat almost always means teleporting there for the Agility Course or a Wilderness clue step.

Q: Is Ghorrock useful for blood runecrafting?

No. The Blood Altar is in Morytania, reached through the Abyss or the mainland route around Slepe and Meiyerditch — not through the Wilderness. Ghorrock appears in blood-rune conversations only because of the mistaken idea that the spell costs blood runes.
