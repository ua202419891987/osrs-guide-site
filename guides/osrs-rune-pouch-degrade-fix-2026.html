1. 👜 First: Which "Rune Pouch" Do You Mean?

There are two completely different items players call a "rune pouch," and mixing them up is the root of most confusion. The rune pouch from the Enter the Abyss miniquest holds up to 16,000 of three rune types and never degrades — it is a permanent convenience item. The items that actually break are your essence pouches, the bags that hold rune / pure / daeyalt / guardian essence for Runecrafting.

When people say "my RC pouch keeps shattering," they mean the essence pouches (Small, Medium, Large, Giant, Colossal). Those are the only Runecrafting bags with a degradation timer. This guide is about those — because that is the problem that actually costs you essence and trips.

💡 Fast Disambiguation

If your bag holds runes (for casting spells) → it is the rune pouch and it will not break. If it holds essence (for making runes) → it is an essence pouch and it degrades. The fix below applies only to the second one.

2. 🔧 How Essence Pouch Degradation Works

Understanding the mechanic is what stops the panic. Essence pouches do not have a visible "charges" bar like a charged item — they degrade silently based on how much essence you push through them.

① It degrades when you FILL, not when you empty

The pouch only takes wear when you fill it with essence. Emptying it back out does nothing. Critically, the wear is per essence: a Giant pouch filled with 12 essence degrades ~12× faster than one filled with a single essence. So small, partial fills extend a pouch's life relative to stuffing it to the brim every time.

② Capacity drops in stages, then it disintegrates

As the hidden counter climbs, the pouch's max capacity steps down (e.g. a Large pouch goes 9 → 7 → 5 → 3 essence). If you keep using it without repair, the final stage is destruction — the pouch vanishes and you must get a new one from the Abyss or the Rewards Guardian. The 1–99 Runecrafting guide covers how to re-farm replacements quickly if one is lost.

③ You get a warning

Watch the inventory icon and chatbox

A degraded pouch turns a darker shade in your inventory, and the game prints a warning when it drops a capacity tier. Repair the moment you see it — a degraded pouch quietly halves your essence per trip, which is the real DPS (essence-per-hour) loss, not the eventual shatter.

4. 🛠️ 5 Ways to Repair a Degraded Pouch

You have options ranging from completely free to convenience-only. Pick by your account and risk tolerance.

① Dark Mage in the Abyss (free, any RC level)

Speak to the Dark Mage in the centre of the Abyss. He repairs all your pouches — including ones sitting in your bank — for free, instantly. To get there, talk to the Mage of Zamorak north of Edgeville (level 5 Wilderness) after the Enter the Abyss miniquest. Because it is a PvP zone, Abyss runecrafters just repair mid-trip; everyone else can leave pouches in the bank and only bring them out to repair.

② NPC Contact (Lunar spellbook)

If you have completed Lunar Diplomacy (67 Magic), cast NPC Contact, select the Dark Mage, and repair remotely — no Wilderness trip. It costs ~400–500 GP in runes per cast and fixes banked pouches too. Ideal for ZMI and Guardians of the Rift players.

③ Apprentice Cordelia (Guardians of the Rift)

Repair with abyssal pearls — but weigh the cost

Cordelia in the Temple of the Eye repairs all pouches for 1 abyssal pearl, after a one-time 25-pearl unlock. Convenient if you live at GotR, but pearls are usually better spent on the Runecraft outfit — most mains skip this and use NPC Contact or the Dark Mage instead.

④ Repair Rune Pouch (Livid Farm)

Unlocked at Livid Farm with 75 Magic and 70,000 produce points, this Lunar spell repairs a single Medium/Large/Giant pouch and boosts its durability by ~400% (a Giant then lasts ~50 fills instead of 10). At ~1,400 GP per cast it is a long-term convenience play for serious runecrafters.

⑤ Just replace it

If a pouch fully disintegrates, grab another: Abyssal monsters in the Abyss drop them, and the Rewards Guardian in Guardians of the Rift also hands them out. You do not "lose" permanently — you only lose the trip time to replace it.

5. 🚫 The "Need 99 Runecrafting" Myth, Busted

A persistent forum myth claims you must be 99 Runecrafting to stop a pouch shattering. That is false on every level. Let us be precise.

① No repair method requires 99

The Dark Mage repairs at any RC level — there is no minimum beyond owning the pouch. NPC Contact needs 67 Magic, not Runecrafting. Cordelia needs pearls, not levels. The only RC-gated pouches are the ones you can already use (Giant = 75, Colossal = 85); repair itself is level-free.

② Where the "99" confusion comes from

⚠️ Likely Source of the Myth

Players conflate two things: (a) the highest degradable pouch caps at 85 RC (Colossal), and (b) the Runecraft cape (99 RC) is one of the ways to prevent degradation. So "99 fixes pouches" is half-true — but you prevent, not repair, and you do not need 99 to repair. A level-50 with a Large pouch gets it fixed free by the Dark Mage today.

③ What actually stops the breaking

Prevention (Section 6) is gated by a cape, not 99 specifically — the Max cape variant works too, and the Abyssal lantern method needs 90 Firemaking, not Runecrafting. So you have prevention options long before 99. If a death ever costs you the pouch along with other gear, the death mechanics guide explains what is kept vs dropped.

6. 🛡️ 3 Ways to Stop Degradation Completely

Repairing is fine, but never degrading is better. Three verified methods:

① Wear a Runecraft cape or Max cape while filling

Since the 23 February 2017 update, pouches do not degrade at all while you are wearing a Runecraft cape, Max cape, or any Max cape variant and filling the pouch. Note: it does not repair an already-degraded pouch — it just freezes further wear. This is the single best fix for daily runecrafters, and the "99" in the myth refers to this cape.

② Lit Abyssal lantern in Guardians of the Rift

90 Firemaking = permanent no-degrade at GotR

While training at Guardians of the Rift, wielding an Abyssal lantern lit with Redwood logs (90 Firemaking) prevents pouch degradation entirely. No cape required. This is the go-to for mid-level players grinding GotR for the Colossal pouch without a Runecraft cape yet.

③ Fill partially and repair on a rhythm

If you have neither, simply fill pouches partially (less per-essence wear) and repair on a set rhythm — every Abyss trip, or every few GotR rounds via NPC Contact. The cheapest 99 Runecrafting guide builds a repair loop into the method so you never hit the destroy stage.

7. ❓ Rune Pouch FAQs

Does the rune pouch that holds runes degrade?

No. The Abyss-miniquest rune pouch (holds 3 rune types, 16k each) is permanent and never degrades. Only the essence pouches used for Runecrafting degrade. If your rune pouch "broke," you are almost certainly looking at an essence pouch.

Do I need 99 Runecrafting to repair my pouch?

No. The Dark Mage repairs all essence pouches for free at any Runecraft level. The 99 figure refers to the Runecraft cape, which prevents degradation while worn — but that is prevention, not repair, and it is optional.

Where is the Dark Mage and how do I reach him?

He is in the centre (inner ring) of the Abyss. Enter via the Mage of Zamorak north of Edgeville (level 5 Wilderness) after completing Enter the Abyss. Navigate the obstacles to the middle, talk to him, and all pouches — even banked ones — are repaired free.

How do I repair without going to the Wilderness?

Two safe options: the NPC Contact Lunar spell (67 Magic) contacts the Dark Mage remotely, or Apprentice Cordelia at Guardians of the Rift repairs for an abyssal pearl (after a 25-pearl unlock). Both fix banked pouches with no PvP risk.

My Giant pouch keeps breaking fast — why?

The Giant pouch degrades after only ~120 essence (~10 full uses), the shortest lifespan of any degradable pouch. Fill it partially to slow wear, repair it every Abyss trip, or use the Livid Farm Repair Rune Pouch spell to boost its durability ~400%.

Can I stop degradation entirely?

Yes. Wear a Runecraft cape / Max cape while filling (no degrade), or at Guardians of the Rift wield a Redwood-lit Abyssal lantern (90 Firemaking, no cape needed). Both make pouches effectively permanent while active.

What happens if I drop a degraded pouch with essence in it?

The essence inside is lost — you get a warning ("The contents of the pouch fell out as you dropped it!"). Never drop a filled pouch. If you must move it, empty it first. The charged-item loss guide covers similar "lost on drop/death" edge cases for other items.
