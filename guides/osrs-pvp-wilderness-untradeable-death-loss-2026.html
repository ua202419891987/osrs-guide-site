1. 💰 The >20 Wilderness Untradeable → GP Conversion

This is the rule that separates veterans from victims. Most "wilderness loot" guides list what drops; almost none explain why your untradeable sometimes turns into a pile of coins instead of landing in the PKer's inventory. Pair this with our Wilderness PvP Guide 2026 for escape routes and Death Mechanics Guide 2026 for the reclaim basics.

How the conversion works

When you die above Wilderness level 20 to another player, any untradeable item you would have lost (i.e., not in a protected slot) is not dropped as an item. Instead it is converted into coins equal to its Grand Exchange value, and those coins are handled by the normal loot rules. This was Jagex's answer to PKers hoarding high-value untradeables — the killer gets GP, not your one-of-a-kind gear.

Which untradeables convert vs which don't

The conversion applies to the broad class of untradeable gear on the "lost on death" list. Exceptions are the special items with their own rules — chiefly the rune pouch and looting bag, covered below. Tier 3 Trouver'd items (Infernal cape, Void) that are unlocked above level 20 are not converted — they are simply kept by the killer, which is why the lock from our Trouver Parchment Guide 2026 matters so much.

Do YOU get the GP or the killer?

In a PvP death the converted coins go to the killer (they are part of the drop). You receive nothing from the conversion — you simply avoid the worse outcome of the killer walking off with the physical untradeable. The silver lining: the killer's incentive is now GP, not item-collecting, which slightly raises the chance they leave your protected slots alone.

💡 The 20-Line Is a Hard Wall

Below Wilderness level 20, the conversion does not trigger — untradeables drop as items and can be kept by a non-skulled player. The moment you cross level 20, the GP rule switches on. Plan your risk band around that line.

2. 🎒 Rune Pouch Death Handling

The rune pouch is the single most misunderstood untradeable in the Wilderness. Players swear they "lost theirs to a PKer" — and while the runes inside can be lost, the pouch itself is almost always kept. If you rely on it for PKing or Revenants, read our How to Get Rune Pouch Guide so you know the source before risking it.

Pouch kept, runes dropped

On death — Wilderness or not, skulled or not — the rune pouch stays with you. What drops is the contents: the runes you stored inside spill to the floor (or to the killer in PvP). So you never lose the pouch; you only lose the loaded runes. Refill and go again.

Skulled vs unskulled

The pouch's "kept on death" property is independent of skull status. Whether you are unskulled (keep 3 + Protect Item) or skulled (keep 1 + Protect Item), the empty pouch remains yours. The only thing that changes is how many of your other items survive alongside it.

Recovering a lost rune pouch

Because the pouch is kept on death, you rarely need to recover it. If you somehow destroyed it (right-click "destroy") or dropped it and it despawned, the original source NPC re-issues it — you do not need to redo the Rune Mysteries content. Never panic-destroy a rune pouch; just re-talk to the NPC who gave it.

⚠️ The Real Rune Pouch Risk

It is not the pouch — it is the runes. A pouch loaded with 10,000 death runes and 5,000 blood runes is a fat pile of GP sitting in your inventory. PKers kill you for those runes, not the pouch. Keep your load lean.

3. 👜 Looting Bag Death Handling

The looting bag behaves nothing like the rune pouch, and this mismatch is exactly why players lose it. The bag's fate depends entirely on your skull status at the moment of death.

Unskulled keeps bag + contents

If you die unskulled in the Wilderness, your looting bag and everything inside it are treated as a normal kept/protected item. As long as it fits in your protected slots (3, or 4 with Protect Item), you walk away with the bag and its loot intact.

Skulled loses bag to killer

The moment you are skulled, your protected slots collapse to 1 (or 2 with Protect Item). The looting bag now competes with your weapon, cape, and gear for those slots. If it is not protected, the bag and all its contents go to the killer. This is the classic "I skulled for one KBD kill and lost my entire loot bag" story.

Looting bag + Trouver

The looting bag is itself an untradeable, but it is not on the Tier 3 combat list — it follows the skull rule above, not the 500K mangled repair. However, the contents (untradeable drops you stored) may individually convert to GP above level 20 if you are unskulled and they fall outside protection. The bag is your friend below 20; above 20, treat its contents as risky.

💡 Empty Before You Risk

Bank your looting bag contents before crossing into deep Wilderness. An empty bag is cheap to lose; a bag full of dragon bones, seeds, and untradeable drops is a PKer's jackpot. The Avoid PK World-Hop Guide 2026 shows how to bank safely.

4. 🛡️ Skull, Protect Item & Smite — the Three Forces

Three mechanics decide what an untradeable does on a Wilderness death. Most players understand the first two and get blindsided by the third. This is the combat-differentiated core of the guide.

Unskulled vs skulled keep rules

  • Unskulled: keep your 3 most valuable items; 4 with Protect Item prayer active.
  • Skulled: keep only your 1 most valuable item; 2 with Protect Item.
  • The 20-minute skull timer starts the moment you attack another player; dying while skulled shortens it to 5 minutes.

Why smite defeats Protect Item

Protect Item prayer adds one protected slot — but only while the prayer is active. A PKer running Smite drains your prayer points to zero, which drops Protect Item and removes that extra slot instantly. The result: even an unskulled player with Protect Item active loses a fourth item the moment their prayer hits zero. Never assume Protect Item saves you against a smiter.

Loot potential & risk scaling

"Loot potential" is the value a killer can extract from your death. It scales with Wilderness depth: deeper levels mean higher-value drops and the >20 GP conversion kicks in. A PKer at level 45 Wildy is hunting a very different payout than one at level 5. The practical lesson: the more untradeables you carry into deep Wildy, the bigger the target on your back.

5. 🎯 Practical Anti-Loss Loadouts (Combat-Differentiated)

This is the moat: competitors list the rules, we give you the loadouts. The right untradeable risk depends on what you are actually doing in the Wilderness. Here are field-tested configurations.

What to never bring above 20

  • Unlocked Tier 3 items (Infernal cape, Void, Blessed/Imbued rings) — above level 20 without a Trouver lock they are kept by the killer. Lock them or leave them.
  • Full looting bag of valuables — bank it first.
  • Charged endgame weapons you cannot replace cheaply — downgrade to a cheaper switch.

The "disposable untradeable" strategy

For Revenants and deep Wildy bosses, bring untradeables that are cheap to replace or that convert to GP harmlessly: a Fire cape (Tier 2, damaged not lost), Graceful (Tier 1, safe), and a Trouver'd Infernal cape (mangled, 500K repair). You accept a repair bill instead of a permanent loss. This is the exact playbook from the Trouver System Rework Guide 2026.

Bounty Hunter / PvP world differences

On dedicated PvP worlds (e.g., the Grand Exchange hot-spot worlds), the >20 Wilderness GP rule does not apply the same way — deaths there follow standard PvP drop rules, and Tier 3 items use the cheaper standard repair rather than the 500K mangled fee. The Wilderness Survival Beginner 2026 guide maps these safely. Know which world type you are on before you risk gear.

📋 3-Loadout Cheat Sheet

Low Wildy (0–19) PvM: Full setup + rune pouch (runes only risk) + empty looting bag. Mid Wildy (20–39): Trouver'd Infernal cape + Fire cape backup + locked rings. Deep Wildy (40+): Disposable Tier 2 only, no unlocked Tier 3, bank everything else.

6. 🏛️ Recovery After a Wildy PK

You died. Now what? The recovery path is far narrower than a normal death, and knowing the limits stops you from wasting a support ticket.

What Death's Office can / can't do

For a Wilderness PvP death, your dropped items went to the killer — not to a gravestone and not to Death's Office. There is nothing for Death to hold. The one exception is items that converted to GP above level 20: those coins belong to the killer too, so Death has nothing for you either. Death's Office only helps for non-PvP deaths (PvM, environmental) where a gravestone formed.

When Jagex won't help

Jagex does not reverse Wilderness PvP losses, ever. A ticket is only worthwhile if a documented bug or server disconnect caused the loss — and even then, evidence is required. The Charged Item Lost on Death Fix 2026 explains the ticket path for bug-related cases. For a straight PK, the only recovery is the next trip done smarter.

💡 Reset, Don't Rage

After a PK, bank, re-plan your loadout using Section 5, and return with disposable gear. The Wilderness tax is real, but a 500K repair or a few lost runes is survivable — a permanently lost Tier 3 is not.

7. ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Does my untradeable convert to GP above level 20 even if I'm unskulled?

Yes. The >20 conversion triggers on any PvP death regardless of skull status — it only depends on Wilderness depth and the item not being in a protected slot.

Is the rune pouch ever lost on a Wilderness death?

The pouch itself is kept on death in almost all cases; only the runes inside drop. You keep the pouch whether skulled or not.

Why did I lose my looting bag but keep my weapon?

You were likely skulled, collapsing your protected slots to 1–2. The bag lost the slot competition to your weapon. Stay unskulled, or protect the bag explicitly with Protect Item.

Does Protect Item save my Infernal cape from a smiter?

Only while prayer holds. A smiter drains your prayer to zero, dropping Protect Item and your extra slot. Against smiters, the Trouver lock (mangled, 500K repair) is your real safety, not the prayer.

Can I get my Wilderness-dropped untradeable back from Jagex?

No. PvP Wilderness losses are final and not reversed. Death's Office only applies to non-PvP deaths where a gravestone formed.

Are PvP worlds safer for untradeables than the Wilderness?

Different rules apply — Tier 3 items use the cheaper standard repair rather than the 500K mangled fee, and the >20 GP conversion does not apply. But you can still lose items to other players. "Safer" is relative.
