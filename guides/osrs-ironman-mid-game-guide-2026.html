⛏️ Ironman Mid-Game: Training, Stockpiles & Boss Prep (2026)

Updated August 19, 2026  |  18 min read  |  By Yanchun Wu

The Ironman mid-game — roughly base 60 to 80 — is where the account either accelerates or stalls. You are past the early scramble for basics, but not yet self-sufficient for endgame PvM. This guide tells you exactly what to train, what to stockpile (herbs, food, secondary materials), how to pivot into PvM, and which bosses should be your first kills.

📅 Updated: August 2026 — Ironman Mid-Game Guide · 17 min read
🛡️ Level: 60–80 base
🆕 NEW

The jump from early Ironman to a PvM-ready account is mostly about preparation, not raw levels. A mid Ironman who stockpiles correctly sails through boss prep; one who rushes levels with an empty bank hits a wall at every boss. If you are still in the first hours, read our Ironman Beginner Guide and Ironman Early-Game Guide first, then use this page to plan the bridge into Ironman Boss Progression.

💡 Quick Verdict

Train Slayer, Farming, and Herblore in parallel. Slayer gives you the GP and unlocks; Farming gives you the herbs; Herblore turns those herbs into the potions every boss demands. By base 70 with a few hundred of each key potion, a decent food pile, and 43 Prayer, you are ready for the safe first bosses (Giant Mole, KBD, Sarachnis, Bryophyta, Obor). Everything else is logistics.

1. 🎯 What "Mid-Game" Means for an Ironman

For a main account "mid-game" is vague. For an Ironman it is a precise, stressful plateau: you can do most content but cannot buy your way past any bottleneck. Self-sufficiency is the whole game, and the mid-game is where that philosophy bites hardest.

① The Mid-Game Window

We define the Ironman mid-game as base 60–80 in your core combat and gathering stats, with a handful of support skills (Herblore, Farming, Construction, Slayer) progressing alongside. You have left the "can I even kill this?" phase and entered the "do I have the supplies to do this repeatedly?" phase.

② Why It Feels Different From a Main

  • No safety net: a main buys potions, food, and gear. You make or farm all three.
  • Stockpiles matter: a single boss trip can eat 200 shark, 50 prayer potions, and a few hundred runes. You must have them banked.
  • Bottlenecks are personal: your weakest skill gates your strongest. Low Herblore? No overloads. Low Construction? No ornate pool, no fast restore.

③ The Mid-Game Mindset

Train for throughput, not just levels. Every herb run, every bird house, every Slayer task is building the war chest that lets you boss. The players who enjoy mid-game Ironman treat stockpiling as the content, not a chore before it.

2. 📈 Training Priorities (What to Level First)

Your levels are tools. Spend them on the tools that unlock more self-sufficiency. The priority order below maximizes what you can do next, not what is fastest to 99.

① The Core Priority List

Skill Target Why It's a Priority
Slayer70–85GP, unlock boss tasks, the backbone of mid-game income
Farming70–91Herb runs feed Herblore; 91 for ranarr/torstol
Herblore70–90Every potion you will ever need, made yourself

Agility60–80Shortcuts save hours; 80 for graceful & rooftops
Thieving55–90GP, gems, blackjacking, Master Farmers for seeds
Hunter60–80Chinchompas for Ranging; bird houses for nests
Magic75–94Bursting/barraging Slayer; teleports; Vorkath later
Ranged70–90Primary boss style for most mid-game content

② Why Slayer Leads

Slayer is the engine. It trains your combat stats, drops herbs and seeds (feeding Farming/Herblore), drops bars and gems (feeding Smithing/Crafting), and unlocks boss tasks (Grotesque Guardians at 75, Cerberus at 91, Smoke Devil at 93). A mid Ironman without Slayer is stuck; one with high Slayer is unstoppable. Our Slayer Beginner Guide covers the early tasks and the Block & Skip List keeps your task list efficient.

③ Support Skills You Can Slow-Roll

  • Runecrafting: do it in bursts (ZMI, then bloods) — needed but not urgent until late game.
  • Woodcutting/Fletching: keep a fletching stock for ranging, but don't obsess.
  • Cooking: just cook what you fish; aim 90 for anglerfish when you can.
  • Crafting: train alongside Slayer drops (gems, dragonhide) rather than grinding it.

3. 📦 Stockpiles: Herbs, Food & Secondary Materials

This is the heart of the mid-game. A stockpile is a buffer that lets you boss without interrupting a trip to go farm. Build these before you need them.

① The Three Stockpile Buckets

  • Herbs: raw ingredients for potions (cleaned and banked).
  • Food: sharks, anglerfish, karambwan, monkfish for healing.
  • Secondary materials: bars, logs, bones, runes, vials — everything else a boss or skill needs.

② Target Buffer Sizes

Item Type Comfortable Buffer Notes
Herbs (each common type)200–500Ranarr, toadflax, irit, avantoe, kwuarm, cadantine, lantadyme
Food (sharks+)1,000+More is always better; combats eat fast
Prayer potions300+43 prayer trips burn these constantly
Vials1,000+You will never have too many
Adamant/Rune bars500 eachSmithing, GP, gear upgrades
Bones (big/dragon)200+For hitting 43 / 70 / 77 Prayer

③ How to Build Stockpiles Passively

You do not "go farm stockpiles" as a separate activity. You accumulate them as a by-product:

  • Herb runs (Farming) every time the patches reset — 5–10 minutes, dozens of herbs.
  • Slayer drops — herbs, seeds, bars, gems all land in your loot.
  • Master Farmers (Thieving 38+) — the fastest seed source for herb runs.
  • Daily/bird houses (Hunter) — nests for compost and seeds.
  • GOTG / RC — runes you would otherwise buy.
💡 The "Don't Sell, Bank It" Rule

On a main, you sell herbs and bars for GP. On an Ironman, almost nothing is trash. A "useless" kwuarm today becomes a super combat potion tomorrow. Bank first, question later. The only things worth alching are genuinely redundant gear drops.

4. 💊 Herblore Prep (Potions You'll Need)

Herblore is the skill that turns your herb stockpile into boss-enabling consumables. Here are the potions a mid Ironman should always be able to make.

① The Essential Potion List

Prayer potionRanarr + Snape grass38Restore prayer for every boss

② Why 90 Herblore Is the Turning Point

At 90 Herblore you make Super Combat Potions, which replace the three super attack/strength/defence potions with one inventory slot. That single unlock frees a slot in every boss inventory and drastically improves trip length. Getting Herblore to 90 should be a top mid-game goal — and it is only possible if your Farming and Thieving are feeding you ranarr and torstol seeds.

③ Making Herblore Sustainable

  • Grow toadflax and ranarr in herb runs; both are common and high-value.
  • Get amylase from the Nightmare Zone or buy with points to cut stamina costs.
  • Use the Botanist's mask (or equivalent) to note cleansed herbs from farmers.
  • Make potions in bulk during "bank maintenance" sessions so you never boss dry.

The Ironman Self-Sufficient Skills Guide goes deeper on closing the Herblore loop without ever touching the GE.

5. 🍖 Food Stockpiles (Sharks, Anglerfish, Karambwan)

Food is the most-consumed resource in bossing. A mid Ironman should never start a boss trip wondering if they have enough to eat.

① Food Tiers by Healing

Food Heal Level to Cook Best Use
Lobster1240Early Slayer, safespotting
Monkfish1662Mid Slayer, cheap and common
Shark2080The mid-game standard boss food
Anglerfishup to 2290Best single-food heal, late mid goal
Karambwan1830 (fish)Combo-eat with shark for burst healing

② Sharks Are the Workhorse

Get Cooking to 80 and fish sharks (or cook raw sharks from Slayer drops) until you have 1,000+. Sharks are the default food for Giant Mole, KBD, Sarachnis, and most mid bosses. They are easy to mass-cook and easy to replace.

③ Karambwan Changes the Math

Karambwan are cooked separately (not on a range with other fish) and combo-eat with shark — eat a shark, then a karambwan in the same tick for 38 instant healing. This is the difference between surviving a big hit and dying. A mid Ironman should keep a few hundred karambwan banked for dangerous bosses (KBD, Vorkath prep, raids).

④ Anglerfish as a Late-Mid Goal

At 90 Cooking you can make anglerfish, which heal 21–22 and scale with your HP. They are the best single-food heal in the game and worth the grind once you are comfortable. Until then, sharks + karambwan cover everything.

6. 🔧 Secondary Materials (Bars, Logs, Bones)

Beyond herbs and food, a mid Ironman needs a quiet pile of "everything else." These are the items that quietly gate gear upgrades, POH builds, and prayer levels.

① Bars & Smithing Mats

  • Adamant & Rune bars: for smithing gear, GP (alching), and later Barrows repair. Mine and smelt in bulk.
  • Gold bars: jewelry (rings of dueling, games necklaces) for teleports — craft these constantly.
  • Silver bars: for enchanted symbols and god books later.
  • Coal: the real bottleneck for higher bars; blast mine or motherlode for it.

② Logs & Fletching Mats

  • Yew / Magic logs: ranged training and high-level fletching.
  • Redwood: for birdhouses and 99 Hunter efficient paths.
  • Flax → bowstrings: always needed; pick flax at Catherby in batches.
  • Feathers: from chickens or Aerial Fishing; needed for bolts and fly fishing.

③ Bones & Prayer Mats

Bone Use How to Get (Ironman)
Big bones43 PrayerHill Giants, ogres
Dragon bones70 / 80 PrayerSlayer, KBD, Adamant dragons
Ensouled heads43 Prayer, no altar neededReanimation (82 Magic) in safe spots
Infernal ashes72 Prayer (Ectofuntus)Superior implements, bosses

Hit 43 Prayer early via big bones on a gilded altar (build the POH chapel at 47 Construction) or ensouled heads if you have 82 Magic. Then push toward 70/77 for Piety/Rigour/Augury before serious bossing.

④ Misc "Always Useful"

  • Vials: bought from skilled crafters or dropped; you will use thousands.
  • Empty pots / jars: for Impling jars and bird houses.
  • Chaos / Death / Blood runes: from ZMI or bossing; never enough.
  • Pure essence: for your own RC; stock a few thousand.

7. 🔄 Transitioning to PvM

Stockpiles exist for one reason: PvM. The transition is less about stats and more about confidence and consumables. Here is how to make it smooth.

① Prerequisites Before Your First Boss

  • 43 Prayer (protect prayers) — non-negotiable.
  • 70+ combat stats (Attack, Strength, Defence, Ranged, Magic, Hitpoints).
  • A full food + potion buffer (see stockpile sections).
  • A POH with ornate pool + altar (infinite restore between trips).
  • Decent gear: at least rune / dragon, ideally Bandos-level by late mid.

② Picking Your First Style

Most mid Ironmen start with Ranged because the best early bosses (Giant Mole, KBD, Sarachnis, Zulrah later) are ranged-friendly and Ranging lets you safespot. Build a Ranged setup (blowpipe or rune crossbow + broad bolts) alongside your stockpiles. Magic (trident / ancients) is strong for bursting Slayer and a few bosses; Melee is for later, tankier content.

③ Learning the Loop

Bossing as an Ironman is a loop: restock → fight → bank → repeat. The ornate pool and a teleport network (ring of dueling, games necklace, Xeric's talisman) make the loop painless. Practice the loop on a safe boss (Giant Mole) before anything risky so the logistics become muscle memory.

⚠️ Don't Boss Dry

The fastest way to stall a mid Ironman is to empty your potion and food banks on a boss you are not efficient at. Always keep a buffer. If your prayer-potion count drops below 100, pause bossing and run herb runs until it recovers. Dry bossing is how accounts quit.

These are the bosses a mid Ironman (base 60–80) can realistically start. They are ordered by difficulty and by how much they give back to your account.

② Why These and Not Others

Grotesque Guardians (75 Slayer), Zulrah (75+), Vorkath (80+ ranged + elite diary), Cerberus (91 Slayer), and the raids are late mid or early late-game. They demand more levels, more gear, and bigger stockpiles. Start with the safe seven above; they build the bank and confidence for the harder bosses. The full ladder is in our Ironman Boss Progression Guide.

③ Dagannoth Kings Deserve a Spotlight

Dagannoth Kings are the quintessential Ironman boss. The three kings drop coins, herbs, seeds, battlestaves, and the dragon axe / warrior ring — exactly the self-sufficiency loot an Ironman craves. Supplies (good) resupply your Herblore, gold (Rex) funds alchs, and the pet (Supreme) is a milestone. Once you can comfortably kill all three, your mid-game flips into overdrive.

A good loadout is half the fight. Here is a template a mid Ironman should aim for, plus the inventory that makes trips long and safe.

② Standard Boss Inventory

  • 1–2 teleport (ring of dueling / ectophial) for emergencies.
  • 1–2 prayer potions (or more for long fights).
  • 1–2 super restores (restore stats + a bit of prayer).
  • 1 ranging potion (or super combat if melee).
  • Anti-venom / anti-fire as the boss demands.
  • Food filling the rest (sharks + karambwan).
  • Spec weapon (Dragon dagger / Dragon claws) for burst.

③ The POH Pre-Trip Routine

Before every boss session: ornate pool (full HP + stats), altar (full prayer), replenish any consumed potions from your stockpile, then teleport out. A maxed POH turns a 10-minute resupply into a 30-second step. This is why Construction is a mid-game priority, not a late one.

10. ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What base levels count as Ironman mid-game?

Roughly base 60 to 80 across your core combat and gathering stats, with support skills (Herblore, Farming, Construction, Slayer) climbing alongside. Below 60 you are early game; above 80 you are drifting into late game and raids prep.

Which skill should I prioritize first?

Slayer. It trains combat, drops herbs/seeds/bars/gems, and unlocks boss tasks. Pair it with Farming (herb runs) and Herblore (potions) so the three grow together. Construction to ~75 for the ornate pool is the next-most-important support skill.

How many potions and food should I stockpile?

A comfortable buffer is 300+ prayer potions, 1,000+ sharks, and 200–500 of each common herb. More is always safer. The rule is: never start a boss trip unless your buffers are full; if they are not, farm until they are.

What is the best first boss for a mid Ironman?

Giant Mole or Bryophyta. Both are weak, close to a bank, and need almost no gear. They teach the boss loop (restock → fight → bank) without punishing mistakes. Obor and KBD follow once you have anti-dragon shield and antifire.

Why is 90 Herblore such a big deal?

At 90 you make Super Combat Potions, which replace three potions with one inventory slot. That frees space in every boss inventory and lengthens trips dramatically. It is only reachable if your Farming and Thieving are feeding ranarr and torstol seeds — so it is the proof your stockpile loop works.

Do I need Construction before bossing?

Not strictly, but it is strongly recommended. A POH with an ornate pool and altar lets you fully restore HP, stats, and prayer in seconds between trips. Without it, every trip ends with a long bank-and-cook detour. Build it during mid-game; do not delay to late game.

Should I use Melee, Ranged, or Magic first?

Ranged is the best first bossing style for a mid Ironman — the early bosses (Mole, KBD, Sarachnis, Zulrah later) suit it and ranging lets you safespot. Build a blowpipe or rune crossbow setup. Magic is great for bursting Slayer; Melee comes later for tankier content.

How do I avoid stalling in the mid-game?

Keep stockpiling as a passive habit, not a separate grind. Run herb runs, do Slayer, thieve Master Farmers, and fill bird houses every day. Set one boss goal (clear Giant Mole, then KBD) so you always have direction. Pair this with the Optimal Quest Order so quests keep unlocking content alongside your levels.

When do I move from mid-game to late-game bosses?

Once you have base 80+ combat, 90+ Herblore, a full potion/food buffer, a BiS-ish ranged or melee setup, and comfortable kills at Dagannoth Kings and Sarachnis. That is the signal you are ready for Zulrah, Vorkath, Grotesque Guardians, and eventually the raids. The Boss Progression Guide maps that exact path.

Is the Gauntlet worth doing mid-game?

Yes. The Gauntlet has no stat requirements and rewards the best-in-slot weapon for its style (Crystal bow / staff, or the T1 version). A mid Ironman can learn the normal Gauntlet at base 70 and use the reward for the rest of the account. It is one of the highest-value free unlocks available in this stage.
