OSRS Twitter/X Promotion Guide 2026

How to grow your OSRS account on X (Twitter) — safely and effectively

Updated: May 2026 | By OSRS Guru

Twitter/X is a powerful platform for OSRS content. Done right, a single thread can bring 1,000-10,000 visitors to your site. This guide shows you exactly how to promote on Twitter/X in 2026 without getting suspended.

Why Twitter/X Works for OSRS

  • Real-time: OSRS updates trend instantly on Twitter
  • Community: OSRS Twitter is active & supportive
  • Viral potential: Good threads get retweeted by big accounts
  • No algorithm suppression: (Yet) — organic reach still works

What's Allowed vs. What Gets You Suspended

✅ Allowed (Safe)

  • Posting original guides with a link to your site
  • Sharing update news with your commentary
  • Replying to others with helpful advice + casual link
  • Using 3-5 hashtags per tweet (don't overdo it)

❌ Not Allowed (Suspension Risk)

  • Posting the same link repeatedly (spam)
  • Using too many hashtags (20+ looks like spam)
  • Buying followers: Gets your account suspended
  • Posting only links (no original content)

Safe Promotion Strategy (2026)

Step 1: Build Followers First

  1. Before promoting, spend 2-4 weeks posting helpful content
  2. Reply to big OSRS accounts with genuine advice
  3. Post screenshots of your progress (PvM, skilling)
  4. Get to 200+ followers before heavy promotion

Step 2: The "Value-First" Tweet Format

  1. Write a helpful thread (3-5 tweets) with real tips
  2. At the end of the thread, add: "Full guide: [link]"
  3. This way you're giving value first — algorithm likes it

Step 3: Engage With Comments

  1. After posting, reply to every comment within 12 hours
  2. More replies = more visibility in algorithm

Hashtags That Work for OSRS (2026)

Use 3-5 hashtags per tweet:

  • #OSRS (always use this)
  • #OldSchoolRuneScape (alternate)
  • #PvM (for boss guides)
  • #Ironman (for Ironman content)
  • #RuneScape (broader reach)

Warning: Don't use more than 5 hashtags — Twitter flags it as spam.

How to Track Results

  • Twitter Analytics: Check engagements (free built-in tool)
  • Click-throughs: Check Google Analytics (set up GA4 on your site)
  • Followers: Aim for 10-20 new followers per week

Expected traffic: 200-2,000 visitors per well-received thread

Cross-Posting: Twitter + Reddit + YouTube

Best workflow:

  1. Write guide on your site → Post thread on Twitter → Share on Reddit (value-first format) → Make YouTube short (1-min summary)
  2. This gives you 4x exposure from one piece of content
