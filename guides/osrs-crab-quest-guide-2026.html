1. 🦀 What Is Crab Quest and Why Sailors Care

Crab Quest is a September 2026 quest built around the Sailing skill and the coastal life that comes with it. The premise is small and charming: a fishing village has been overrun by crabs that are stealing bait and chewing through nets, and the locals need someone with a ship and a fishing background to sort it out. That premise is a vehicle for a quest that sits exactly where the Sailing curve starts to reward effort.

The reason experienced players point new sailors at this quest is its placement. At 40 Sailing you have cleared the clumsiest early voyages but not yet reached the efficient mid-game routes. Crab Quest hands you a meaningful XP lump right at that pivot, plus a tool that keeps giving afterwards. It is one of the cleaner "do this next" quests for anyone following a Sailing order.

💡 A Quest That Pays Back After the Cutscene

Unlike many story quests whose rewards are a one-time XP drop, Crab Quest gives you a crab pot you keep using. If you enjoy low-attention training, the pot alone justifies the trip, independent of the Sailing progress.

It also doubles as a gentle Fishing milestone. Because 30 Fishing is required, the quest naturally sits on the path of anyone training Fishing alongside Sailing, which is common given how often the two skills share a coastline.

Who should prioritise it? New sailors who have just crossed 40 Sailing get the most obvious value, but it is also a clean pickup for alts and skiller-style accounts that want a safe, no-combat quest with a lasting tool. If your goal is simply to keep Sailing momentum without burning out on the same voyage loop, this is the quest to slot in next.

2. 🎯 Requirements and Prerequisites Before You Start

The entry bar is two skills and nothing else. There is no quest wall in front of Crab Quest and no combat level gate, which keeps it accessible to accounts that have focused on Sailing and Fishing from the start.

⚙️ What You Need

  • 40 Sailing — the main gate, and the level the quest is designed to reward
  • 30 Fishing — enough to handle the crab-fishing tasks during the walkthrough
  • A registered ship — you need your own vessel for the voyage segments; the quest provides a starter route if you have one docked
  • Basic supplies — a few fishing bait, a rope, and some coins for a cart ride

If you are short on Sailing, the fastest way up is our Sailing 1–99 guide, which maps the most efficient early voyages. For Fishing, our Fishing 1–99 guide covers the methods that reach 30 quickly. Most players hit both naturally while levelling the two skills together.

💡 No Combat, No Stress

Crab Quest is safe content. You are never forced into a fight, so you can run it on a low-combat alt or an account that has ignored combat entirely. Pack food only if you feel like it; the quest does not demand it.

3. ⛵ How to Start: Getting Your Ship to the Village

You start Crab Quest at a coastal settlement that is reachable by foot but far more pleasant by ship. Because 40 Sailing is required, you already own or have access to a vessel, and the quest assumes you can sail it to the village dock.

🧭 Routes to the Start

  • By ship: plot a short voyage to the village dock; this also counts toward your Sailing activity for the session
  • By foot: walk from the nearest lodestone and follow the coastal path west to the docks
  • Quest giver: speak to the harbour master on the dock, who explains the crab problem and asks for your help

Talk to the harbour master to begin. The dialogue sets up the three problems the village faces: stolen bait, chewed nets, and a particularly large crab that has been spooking the smaller catch. Your tasks map to those three problems in order.

💡 Sail In, Do Not Walk

Even though you can walk, sailing to the start earns voyage progress and keeps your Sailing training ticking. Since the quest is a Sailing milestone, treat the journey as part of the grind rather than a detour.

4. 🪝 Full Quest Walkthrough Step by Step

The walkthrough is linear and the game tracks it in your quest journal. Follow the three task groups in the order the harbour master gives them.

Task 1: Recover the Stolen Bait

The crabs have dragged bait cages into a shallow cove. Sail your ship to the marked cove and use your rope to haul the cages back aboard. This is a short Sailing interaction: line up with the cage, secure it, and reel it in. No fighting is involved; the crabs scatter when the cage moves.

Task 2: Mend the Nets

Back at the village, the chewed nets need repair. Use your Fishing skill on the damaged net nodes to patch them. At 30 Fishing this is automatic; the quest simply checks your level and plays the mend animation. You recover a stack of usable bait as thanks, which feeds directly into the next task.

💡 Keep the Bait You Recover

The bait from the net repair is exactly what you need for the crab-fishing finale. Do not drop it. If you run short, buy a few more from the village shop before the last task rather than sailing away to restock.

Task 3: Land the Big Crab

The large crab is the quest's set piece. You do not fight it; you fish it. Equip a crab pot (the quest lends you one), bait it with the recovered bait, and drop it at the marked tile. After a short wait the crab is caught, the village is relieved, and the quest completes. The pot you used becomes a permanent reward.

✅ Quest Complete Checklist

  • Bait cages hauled from the cove by ship
  • Nets mended at the village using Fishing
  • Big crab caught with a baited crab pot
  • Permanent crab pot and teleport claimed

5. 🎁 Quest Rewards: XP, Crab Pot and Teleport

The reward screen is built around the two skills the quest tests. The headline items are the experience drops and the reusable crab pot, with a coastal teleport as a quality-of-life bonus.

🎁 What You Receive

  • Sailing experience — a solid lump that pushes a 40 Sailing account toward the next voyage tier
  • Fishing experience — a smaller drop that helps cement the 30 Fishing you already brought in
  • A reusable crab pot — the tool you used on the big crab, now yours to keep and use anywhere crabs are found
  • A coastal teleport — a quick way back to the village docks for future Sailing and Fishing trips
  • Quest point — counts toward your quest total and any diary or cape requirements

The crab pot is the reward that keeps working after the cutscene. Crabs are a low-attention catch, and having your own pot means you can passive-fish them whenever you are near a crab spot. For Ironmen in particular, a self-supplied crab pot is a small but real win.

💡 The Teleport Saves More Than Time

The coastal teleport is not just convenience. It lets you chain Sailing voyage resets with Fishing sessions at the same village, which is exactly the kind of loop that makes the two skills train efficiently together.

6. 🌊 How Crab Quest Fits the Sailing Grind

Sailing is a skill where momentum matters. The early levels feel slow because your ship is weak and your voyages are short, but each milestone unlocks faster, more profitable routes. Crab Quest sits at the 40 Sailing pivot, and understanding that placement helps you use it well.

The 40 Sailing Pivot

At 40 Sailing you can take voyages that were closed to you at 30, and the XP per hour starts to climb. The quest's XP drop lands you closer to that better tier, which means the next session after the quest is noticeably smoother than the one before it. Players who treat the quest as a training breakpoint rather than a box to tick get more from it.

Why It Beats Grinding the Gap

The awkward stretch between 35 and 45 Sailing is where many players stall, because the available voyages feel repetitive. Doing Crab Quest converts part of that stretch into a focused, story-driven session, which is easier to finish than another hour of the same loop. You still gain the Sailing XP; you just gain it with a purpose.

💡 Pair It With a Route Plan

After the quest, open our Sailing Wyrmscraig guide to see where 40-plus Sailing takes you. Wyrmscraig is the destination the whole Sailing curve points toward, and Crab Quest is a natural on-ramp to that longer journey.

The Fishing Synergy

Because the quest also rewards Fishing and gives a crab pot, it reinforces the habit of training the two skills together. Crab fishing is relaxed, needs little attention, and stacks neatly with Sailing voyage downtimes. If you want to monetise the loop, our Fishing money-making guide shows which catches pay.

The synergy matters more than it looks. Sailing voyages leave you with idle moments while the ship travels, and those moments are perfect for dropping a crab pot at a nearby spot. Over a long Sailing session, the Fishing XP from those idle windows is not trivial, and the crab pot from this quest is what makes the habit free to start.

7. 🧠 Efficiency Tips and Common Mistakes

✅ Do This

  • Sail to the start. The voyage counts as training and keeps your Sailing ticking.
  • Keep the recovered bait. You need it for the crab-fishing finale; do not drop it.
  • Use the teleport afterwards. Chain Sailing and Fishing loops at the same village.
  • Train Fishing alongside. The 30 Fishing gate is easy to extend into a real crab-fishing habit.
  • Plan the next voyage. Open your Sailing route list right after the XP drop to use the momentum.

❌ Avoid This

  • Walking and ignoring Sailing. You forfeit free voyage progress by foot.
  • Dropping the bait. The finale needs it; restocking means a detour.
  • Banking the crab pot. It is the reward; keep it for passive crab fishing.
  • Skipping the teleport. It is a genuine time saver for future trips.

💡 The Fastest Route Through the Quest

Get to 40 Sailing first, then do the whole quest in one sitting by ship. Recover bait, mend nets, and land the crab without leaving the village. Claim the pot and teleport, then immediately start the next voyage tier while the Sailing XP is fresh.

8. ❓ Crab Quest FAQs

Q: What stats do I need for Crab Quest?

40 Sailing and 30 Fishing. There is no combat requirement and no prior quest prerequisite, so any account with those two skills can start immediately.

Q: Is Crab Quest members only?

Yes. It is part of the September 2026 Sailing-era content drop and requires membership to start and to use the rewards afterwards.

Q: What do I get for finishing the quest?

Sailing and Fishing experience, a reusable crab pot, a coastal teleport back to the village, and a quest point.

Q: Do I have to fight the big crab?

No. You catch it with a baited crab pot rather than fighting it. The quest is safe content with no forced combat.

Q: How does the quest help my Sailing training?

The XP drop lands at the 40 Sailing pivot, pushing you toward faster voyages. It also gives a teleport that lets you chain Sailing and Fishing loops at the same village.

Q: Can I use the crab pot after the quest?

Yes. The pot is a permanent reward and works at any crab-fishing spot, making it a low-attention training and catching tool.

Q: What should I do right after Crab Quest?

Start the next Sailing voyage tier while the XP is fresh, and read our Sailing Wyrmscraig guide to see where 40-plus Sailing leads.

Q: Is the quest good for Ironman accounts?

Yes. The self-supplied crab pot and the safe, no-combat design make it a clean early milestone for the mode.

Q: Does the quest count toward a quest cape?

Yes. It awards a quest point like any standard quest, so it contributes to your quest total and related diary requirements.

Q: Where should I train Fishing alongside it?

Use the crab pot at nearby crab spots for low-attention gains, or follow our Fishing 1–99 guide for the broader route.
