Opening your bank after a game update and finding items missing is one of the most alarming moments in Old School RuneScape. The good news: Jagex keeps detailed server-side records, and when an item loss is caused by their systems rather than your actions, they do restore it. The bad news: most "missing item" reports turn out to be player error, which is not refundable. Our common bug fix guide covers quick client-side fixes, but a genuine bank/update loss needs a support ticket.

Before filing anything, take a breath and read this whole guide. Knowing the difference between a bug loss and a user loss saves you from a denied ticket and wasted weeks of waiting. If your items disappeared after a disconnect, also check our random disconnect fix guide — sometimes the items were never lost, just not loaded on your screen.

This is written for newer and returning players, so we explain every term plainly. If you already understand death mechanics, our death mechanics guide explains reclaim-from-Death's-Office separately, because that is a different recovery path from a bank bug or rollback.

1. 🔍 First: Confirm What Actually Happened

Jagex moderators will ask one question first: "Was this loss caused by our systems, or by your own actions?" Your ticket lives or dies on answering it honestly. Run through this checklist before you write anything.

Did you die and not reclaim in time?

If you died in PvM or the Wilderness and your gravestone expired, your items went to Death's Office — not a bug. Reclaim them there (see the death mechanics guide). No ticket needed.

Did you drop, trade, or alch it?

Double-check your trade history and recent drops. Many "missing" items were high-alched, dropped by accident, or moved to another tab/alt. Bank tags help you find misplaced items — our bank tags beginner guide explains how to organise so this never happens.

Did the client crash or show a blank bank?

A visual glitch is not a loss. Log out fully, restart the client, and check again. A blank or partial bank after a crash usually reloads correctly on a fresh login.

Was there a known update or server issue?

Check the official OSRS news / Twitter / Discord. If Jagex announced a rollback or a bug affecting banks, you are in restoration territory — and they may already be handling it globally.

💡 The 10-Minute Rule

Spend ten minutes ruling out user error before opening a ticket. A ticket filed for a self-caused loss is auto-denied and wastes the queue for players with real bug losses.

2. 📜 Jagex's Rollback & Compensation Policy

Jagex's stance is consistent: they restore items lost due to verified server-side faults, and they do not restore items lost to player actions or exploits. Understanding the two mechanisms — "rollback" and "compensation" — helps you set expectations.

What is a rollback?

A rollback reverts the game state to an earlier point in time. This happens rarely, usually after a major bug or data corruption, and it affects all players on the affected worlds — not just you. When Jagex rolls back, they are aware of it and typically post an announcement.

What is compensation?

Compensation is a targeted restoration of specific items to specific accounts after a verified bug. Unlike a rollback, it is individual: Jagex reviews your logs and returns exactly what the bug removed. This is the path most bug-loss tickets follow.

Why Jagex is strict about exploits

If a dupe or economy-breaking bug appears, Jagex patches it and then removes duplicated items rather than compensating. Players who gained from the exploit lose those items and are not made whole. This protects the wider economy.

⚠️ Don't "Test" a Bug for Proof

If you discover a repeatable bug that deletes or duplicates items, report it — do not exploit it to "show" Jagex. Using the bug to gain items turns a report into a ban-worthy offence.

4. 🚫 When Jagex Will NOT Restore

Just as important: know when to stop hoping. These losses are permanent by policy, and tickets for them are declined.

Your own mistakes

Dropped it, traded it away, high-alched it, or died and let the gravestone expire — these are player actions. The death-loss guide covers reclaim; mistakes do not get compensation.

Exploit / dupe gains

If you obtained items through a bug, Jagex removes them and will not restore your "original" stack. Abusing a dupe is a bannable offence.

Normal death (reclaimable but expired)

If the gravestone expired and items went to Death's Office, they are still reclaimable for a fee — but if you simply never reclaimed and they were eventually purged, that is on you.

Old losses beyond log retention

Trade and action logs are kept for a limited window. A bank "loss" you only notice months later, with no recorded fault, cannot be investigated.

💡 Honesty Saves Weeks

Admitting "I think I might have dropped it" up front is better than a false bug claim that gets flagged. False reports delay real cases and can earn a mute or warning.

5. 📸 Gathering Evidence Before You Ticket

A bug-loss ticket wins or loses on evidence. Jagex cannot "see" your missing items unless you show them. Collect these before writing.

Screenshot the current bank state

Open your bank, screenshot the full thing (or scroll through tabs). Show what is there so moderators see the gap. Note the exact missing items and quantities.

Record the date, time, and timezone

"After the July 30 update, around 20:00 UTC" is far better than "a few days ago". Jagex cross-references their update and incident logs by timestamp.

Note the world and activity

Which world were you on? What were you doing when items vanished (logging in, opening bank, after a disconnect)? Our mobile disconnect fix explains why backgrounding the app can desync your view.

Keep any error messages or clips

If the client showed an error, a "world unavailable" message, or a crash, capture it. A short clip of the bug reproducing is gold — but never exploit it repeatedly.

List items with approximate values

A clear bulleted list ("3x Torstol seed, 1x Abyssal whip, 4.2m cash") lets Jagex match it against server logs fast.

💡 Native Screenshots Beat Descriptions

Use the in-game screenshot key (default Print Screen / the camera button). Native shots carry metadata that helps verification more than a phone photo of your monitor.

6. 🪜 Step-by-Step Ticket Flow

Follow this exact order. It matches how Jagex's support queue routes cases.

Step 1 — Secure your account first

Change your password and re-enable your authenticator before anything else. If there is any chance of hijack, this stops further loss while the ticket is open.

Step 2 — Pick the right category

On the Jagex Support site, choose "Bugs" → "In-game item or progress lost". For hijack-related loss, use the account-recovery path instead — it routes to the security team.

Step 3 — Write a clear, factual report

Use the template below. Stick to facts: what you had, when it vanished, what you were doing, and the evidence attached.

Step 4 — Attach evidence, then submit once

Upload your screenshots/clip. Submit a single ticket. Opening duplicates slows everything down.

Step 5 — Wait and monitor your email

Responses range from a few days to a few weeks. Check the email tied to your account (including spam) for follow-up questions.

📋 Ticket Template (copy below)

Subject: Bank/update bug — items missing after [update date / rollback] Account display name: [your name] Account email: [your email] Date/time of loss (timezone): [YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM TZ] World: [number] What I was doing: [logging in / opening bank / after disconnect] Missing items (name + qty + approx value): - [item 1] - [item 2] What I observed: [The bank showed missing items after the update. I did not drop, trade, or die with them. Screenshots attached.] Request: Please check server-side logs for this account around the timestamp above and restore any items removed by the bug/update.

7. ⏳ What Happens After You Submit

Knowing the process stops you from panicking or spamming tickets.

Auto-acknowledgement

You get an email confirming receipt with a ticket number. Keep it — you'll reference it in any follow-up.

Human review

A moderator pulls your server-side logs: bank state, trades, drops, deaths, and login history around your timestamp. This is why accurate times matter so much.

Possible outcomes

  • Restored: Items returned to your bank (often within the same session or after a short delay).
  • Partial: Some items verified as bug-lost are returned; others show as player-action and are not.
  • Declined: Logs show the loss was your action or an exploit — you get a clear explanation.
  • Global fix: If it was a widespread rollback, Jagex restores everyone automatically without per-ticket action.
💡 Don't Keep Playing "To Test"

Once the ticket is in, avoid risky activity on that account. If a rollback is coming, your newer actions could complicate restoration.

8. ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

My bank looks empty after an update — is it gone?

Usually not. A blank or partial bank after a client crash is often a display glitch. Fully log out, restart the client, and check again before filing.

Will Jagex restore items I lost to a bug?

Yes, if the loss is verified as server-side (bug, rollback, or outage). You must provide evidence and accurate timestamps. User-error losses are not restored.

How long do trade/bank logs last?

Jagex retains action logs for a limited window. File promptly — a loss you notice months later with no fault recorded usually cannot be investigated.

Can I get items back if I abused a dupe?

No. Exploit gains are removed, not compensated, and repeated abuse is bannable. Report bugs; don't use them.

Do I need to be a member to get help?

No. Jagex support assists all account types. The recovery policy applies to F2P and P2P equally.

How fast will I get my items back?

It varies from a few days to a few weeks depending on queue and complexity. Global rollbacks are usually reversed within hours of the announcement.
