OSRS Best Magic Gear Progression 2026 — From Mystic Robes to Tumeken's Shadow

🆕 NEW
Published October 9, 2026  |  22 min read  |  By Yanchun Wu

Magic is the most accessible combat style to gear because so much of its power comes from a single weapon and one necklace. But the gap between a trident and Tumeken's Shadow is the largest DPS cliff in the game, and the wrong upgrade order can leave millions of gold sitting in a slot that barely helps. This 2026 progression walks every tier from mystic robes to the Shadow, ranked by damage per gold.

1. Why Magic Gear Order Is Unique

Magic damage is dominated by two things: the weapon's base spell or staff attack, and the magic damage bonus from jewelry and armor. Unlike melee, where every slot contributes a little, magic has a couple of enormous multipliers (the Shadow's special attack, the Occult necklace's flat 10 percent) and a lot of small ones. That means a small number of purchases do almost all the work.

This guide ranks upgrades by damage per gold so you never park millions in a marginal slot while a game-changing one waits.

① What "progression" means here

🎯 The mental model

  • Weapon first: Your staff or trident sets your floor; armor raises it only after the weapon is settled.
  • Occult is mandatory: The 10 percent magic damage necklace is the highest-value item in the whole style.
  • Ancestral is the body: Ancestral robes are bis for nearly all magic content and last you into the end game.
  • Ironman-aware: The best mid-game magic gear is self-obtainable, so you can reach near-bis without the Grand Exchange.
💡 The Shadow changes the math

Tumeken's Shadow has a special attack that roughly doubles its damage and its passive boosts magic by a flat amount. Against single-target bosses it is the strongest weapon in the game, but it is also one of the most expensive items in OSRS. Buy it last, and only once your Ancestral and jewelry are complete.

2. Early Game (1–50 Magic): Mystic and the First Staff

Early magic is cheap. Your weapon is a staff that provides infinite runes, and your armor is mystic robes. You are training and questing, not DPS racing.

② The starter loadout

③ The first real weapon: Trident of the Seas

✅ Your first power spike

The Trident of the Seas (charged with runes) is the first weapon that meaningfully raises your magic DPS and lets you boss efficiently. It is tradeable and relatively cheap, and it carries you through most mid-game content. Pair it with mystic robes and an Occult necklace if you can afford it, and you are already a competent magic attacker.

⚠️ Don't waste gold on a god staff

Saradomin, Guthix, and Zamorak staves are only marginally better than a basic staff for most content and cost far more. Skip them and go straight for a trident once you can afford it — the DPS jump is real and the rune cost is handled by the charge.

3. Mid Game (50–75): Trident, Occult, Ancestral Start

The mid game is where magic gets good. Two purchases define it: the Occult necklace and the start of Ancestral. Buy the Occult first — it is the single best value in the style.

④ The mid-game pillars

✨ The loadout that carries you for hundreds of hours

  • Occult necklace: A flat 10 percent magic damage boost. No other single item comes close in value.
  • Ancestral robe top + bottom: The bis magic body. Replaces mystic and adds a large magic bonus. Drops from the Chambers of Xeric or the Theatre of Blood for Ironmen.
  • Trident of the swamp: An upgrade over the seas trident with better speed and accuracy, self-obtainable from the Theatre of Blood or tradeable.
  • Tormented bracelet: A strong magic damage bracelet that rounds out the jewelry.

⑤ Supporting slots

🛡️ Fill these alongside the pillars

Eternal boots replace your mystic boots as the bis magic boots. Imbued zamorak cape beats the god cape for magic. Mage's book is the bis off-hand for magic accuracy. With Ancestral + Occult + Tormented + Eternal + trident you are already near the top of the magic ladder for most bosses.

💡 BIS-adjacent before BIS

You do not need Tumeken's Shadow to clear the Theatre of Blood, Tombs of Amascut, or the overwhelming majority of PvM. The Ancestral + trident core is enough for most of the game. Save the Shadow for when you are pushing the hardest single-target bosses where its special attack dominates.

4. Late Game (75–90): Ancestral, Tormented, Eternal

Once you have the mid-game core, the late game is about completing the jewelry and boots and unlocking Augury. None of these are cheap, but each is worth more than it costs.

⑥ The late-game damage items

BraceletTormented braceletBest magic damage bracelet; pairs with Occult.
BootsEternal bootsBest magic boots; a clear upgrade over mystic.

⑦ The free DPS: Augury

✅ The highest value "purchase" is a prayer

Augury (unlocked at 70 Prayer) adds a flat magic and defence bonus that outperforms most armor swaps and costs nothing but a few quests. If you have not unlocked it, do so before buying another gear piece — it is the most efficient magic DPS upgrade available, and it pairs with Rigour for ranged.

5. End-Game BIS: Tumeken's Shadow and the Full Max Setup

The full max magic setup is the strongest magic kit in the game and is built from high-level PvM. Here is the complete bis list.

⑧ The max magic loadout

👑 Best-in-slot magic (general single-target)

  • Weapon: Tumeken's Shadow (bis single-target) or Trident of the swamp (bis AoE / budget).
  • Body: Ancestral robe top.
  • Legs: Ancestral robe bottom.
  • Boots: Eternal boots.
  • Necklace: Occult necklace.
  • Ring: Ring of suffering (i) for defence, or Lightbearer for special attack economy.
  • Cape: Imbued zamorak cape.
  • Off-hand: Mage's book (or tome of fire for fire spells).
  • Bracelet: Tormented bracelet.
  • Head: Ancestral hat or Slayer helmet (i) on-task.
  • Prayer: Augury active.
💡 Shadow vs Trident

Against a single tough target the Shadow's special attack and flat boost make it the best weapon in the game. Against groups or when you want cheaper special attacks, the trident (or the Sanguinesti staff for lifesteal) remains excellent. Carry the Shadow for bosses, keep a trident for general use.

6. Budget Swaps and Ironman Self-Sufficiency

You do not need the full bis to be effective. These swaps keep you competitive for a fraction of the cost, and most are obtainable without the Grand Exchange.

⑨ Cheaper but strong

💰 Stretch your gold

  • Mystic robes instead of Ancestral early — a big chunk of the bonus for a tiny fraction of the price.
  • Amulet of fury / glory instead of Occult until you can afford the real thing (but buy Occult as soon as possible — it is worth it).
  • Climbing boots / wizard boots instead of Eternal for a long time.
  • God cape instead of the imbued zamorak cape early.
  • Barrows gloves are bis-friendly and tradeable, so skip the tormented grind early if needed.
⚠️ Ironman note

Every core magic piece is self-obtainable: trident (Theatre of Blood), Ancestral (CoX / ToB), Occult (CoX), Tormented (Cerberus / CoX), Eternal (CoX), and even the Shadow (CoX). An Ironman can assemble a near-bis magic kit without the Grand Exchange, which makes magic a strong first max-combat style for self-sufficient accounts.

7. The Exact Upgrade Order (Spend-Smart Path)

If you have a fixed budget, buy in this order. Each step is the highest damage-per-gold upgrade available at that point.

⑩ The list

🎯 Your magic upgrade roadmap

→ Step 1: Trident of the seas + mystic robes (bossing start).
→ Step 2: Unlock Augury (70 Prayer) — free DPS.
→ Step 3: Occult necklace (buy this before Ancestral — it is worth more).
→ Step 4: Ancestral robe top + bottom.
→ Step 5: Tormented bracelet, Eternal boots, then imbued zamorak cape.
→ Step 6: Trident of the swamp / Sanguinesti staff.
→ Step 7: Tumeken's Shadow (the final, biggest spike).
💡 Why Occult comes before Ancestral

The Occult necklace's flat 10 percent magic damage applies on top of everything and is cheaper than a single Ancestral piece. It adds more DPS per gold than the robe set's individual pieces for most players, so it should be your first "real" upgrade after the trident.

9. Common Magic Mistakes That Drain Your Gold

⑪ Ancestral before Occult and Tormented

A 1.5M Occult necklace and a 15M Tormented bracelet together beat a 30M Ancestral hat for raw multiplier, and cost far less. Buy jewelry first.

⑫ The Shadow before you can feed it

Tumeken's Shadow is best-in-slot, but the upfront cost is enormous and it needs high Magic to shine. If your bank is thin, Sanguinesti gives 80% of the value for a fraction of the price.

⑬ Ignoring Thralls

Arceuus Thralls (from the Necromancy-free summoning ritual) add free passive damage to every magic trip. Skipping them is leaving DPS on the table.

⑭ Wrong staff for the job

The Trident of the Swamp is often cheaper than the Trident of the Seas for slayer, yet players overpay for the "nicer" one. Match the weapon to the task.

⑮ Skipping Augury

Augury is a free ~20% magic boost from prayer. Like Rigour for ranged, it out-values most gear and costs only 77 Prayer.

⑯ Sitting on the Imbued Heart cooldown

The Imbued Heart is a temporary +magic boost on a long cooldown. Use it for big boss pushes, not for idle slayer — and never let it sit unused during a raid.

10. Magic Gear Progression FAQs

Q: Is Tumeken's Shadow worth it if I already have a trident?

Yes for single-target bosses. The Shadow's special attack and flat magic boost make it the strongest weapon in the game against tough targets. For AoE or cheaper special attacks the trident or Sanguinesti staff remain excellent, so keep both.

Q: What is the single best first upgrade after the trident?

The Occult necklace. It is a 10 percent flat magic damage boost, the highest value item in the entire style, and far cheaper than Ancestral. Buy it before any armor piece.

Q: Do I need Augury to raid?

Practically yes. Augury is expected at every high-level raid and it adds more DPS than most gear swaps. Unlock it (70 Prayer, a few quests) before you start chasing bis armor.

Q: Ancestral or Virtus?

Ancestral is the standard bis magic body for general use and has a higher magic bonus than Virtus. Virtus is a niche defensive alternative. For 99 percent of players Ancestral is the answer.

Q: Can an Ironman get full magic bis without the Grand Exchange?

Yes. Trident (ToB), Ancestral (CoX / ToB), Occult (CoX), Tormented (Cerberus / CoX), Eternal (CoX), and the Shadow (CoX) are all self-obtainable. Magic is one of the most Ironman-friendly max-combat styles.

Q: Should I use fire spells with the tome of fire?

The tome of fire boosts fire spell damage and is a strong budget option before Ancestral and the Shadow. Once you have the Shadow, the tome is typically outclassed, but it remains a cheap, chargeable choice for general slaying.

Q: What ring is bis for magic?

Ring of suffering (i) is bis for defence-heavy fights; Lightbearer is often used to stretch special attacks on weapons that consume them. Neither is a pure damage ring, so most players swap based on the fight rather than keeping one equipped always.

Q: How much does a near-bis magic kit cost on the Grand Exchange?

A trident + Ancestral + Occult + Tormented + Eternal kit typically runs in the tens of millions of gold, while Tumeken's Shadow alone is several hundred million. The mid-game core is affordable; the Shadow is the only true gold sink.
