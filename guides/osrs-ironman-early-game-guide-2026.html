Just started an Ironman account? This guide covers exactly what to do in your first 50 levels in 2026 — quest order, gear progression, and the most efficient route to mid-game.

First 10 Levels (The Tutorial)

  1. Complete Tutorial Island (choose Ironman mode at end)
  2. Go to Lumbridge — talk to Quest Guide (start all free quests)
  3. Do Cook's Assistant, Dernhelm, and Sheep Shearer (easy QP)
  4. Get 15 fishing & 15 cooking (fish shrimp, cook on range)

Tip: Train Attack/Defence on cows (Lumbridge fields) — they drop hides you can sell.

Levels 10-30 (The Mid-Early Game)

Quests to Do First

  1. Waterfall Quest — gives 2x 9,900 XP lamps (30 Attack/Strength/Defence)
  2. Tree Gnome Village — unlocks Gnome Agility (good XP)
  3. Fight Arena — 43,000 Combat XP (enough for 40+ Combat)
  4. Vampyre Slayer — 4 combat XP lamps

Gear Progression

  • Weapon: Iron scimitar → Steel scimitar → Green d'hide (ranged)
  • Armor: Iron → Steel → Green d'hide (ranged)
  • Food: Shrimp → Tilapia → Salmon

Levels 30-50 (The Late Early Game)

Key Quests

  1. Dragon Slayer 1 — rewards 18,650 Attack XP (unlocks Green d'hide)
  2. Lost City — unlocks Dragon longsword (best melee weapon until 60 Attack)
  3. Heroes' Quest — unlocks Dragon gear (need 55 Cooking, 53 Fishing, 25 Herblore, 45 Mining, 58 Smithing)

Skill Training (30-50)

  • Attack/Strength: Train on Hill Giants (Lumbridge Swamp) — drop keys for Obor
  • Fishing: Lobby (Lumbridge) until 40, then Lobsters (Musa Point)
  • Cooking: Cook all fish you catch (level Cooking alongside Fishing)

Ironman-Specific Must-Dos (Early Game)

  • Get 300K GP early: Sell cowhides, flax, or mining ore
  • Do Tree Gnome Village: Unlocks Gnome Agility (good XP, no requirements)
  • Start Fairy Tale I: Unlocks Fairy Rings (fast travel) — do early!
  • Get Explorer's Ring 2: From Lumbridge Diary (medium) — free run energy

Common Mistakes (Early Ironman)

  • Not banking everything: Ironman can't trade — keep all drops!
  • Doing quests out of order: Waterfall Quest first (huge XP)
  • Not getting Agility: Agility = run energy — essential for everything
  • Wasting GP on gear: Use free quest rewards (Dragon Slayer gives Dragon gear)

What's Next After 50?

  • Levels 50-70: Do Desert Treasure (unlocks Ancient Magicks), get Dragon boots
  • Levels 70-99: Barrows gloves (RFD quest), 80+ Combat for bossing
  • Quest order: Follow the "Optimal Quest Guide" on OSRS Wiki (ironman version)
