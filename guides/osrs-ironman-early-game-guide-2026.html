OSRS Ironman Early-Game Guide 2026 — Your First 10 Hours, the Self-Sufficient Way

Beginner

An Ironman account is locked out of trading and the Grand Exchange, so the first 10 hours feel completely different from a normal account. This gold-standard 2026 roadmap gives you the milestone checklist, tiered starter gear, verified GP/hr methods, the best early bosses (with real drop rates), and a live rare-drop expectancy calculator.

⏱️ Quick Summary — 30-Second Read

  • 📌 No Grand Exchange. Ironmen cannot buy or sell on the GE (bonds are the only exception), and cannot trade with other players. Everything you use must be gathered, crafted, or dropped — by you.
  • 📌 Quests are your XP. The first 10 hours should be heavy on questing: it hands you combat levels and unlocks (Falador, Morytania, Kourend) you cannot buy.
  • 📌 Gear tiers: Rune → Guthan's / Barrows → Barrows + Blessed → God wars gear. Progress is earned, not purchased.
  • 📌 First boss: Giant Mole (no requirements) — Baby mole pet 1/3,000, Dragon spear 1/10,738, elite clue 1/500.
  • 📌 Early GP/hr: ~150K–400K from cows/monks + nest farming; ~500K–1M once you can kill the Giant Mole.

1. 🛡️ Why Ironman Early Game Is Different

On a normal (main) account, the first week is usually: buy gear from the GE, skip content with gold, trade for supplies. On an Ironman that door is closed. Every weapon, every potion, every teleport tablet has to come from your own effort. The good news: OSRS is built for self-sufficiency, and the early game is where the mode feels best — every unlock is genuinely yours.

🎯 What Actually Changes

  • No economy shortcut. You cannot buy a Bond-funded bank; you grind your own GP (you can buy Bonds on the GE, but nothing else).
  • Quests matter more. Quest rewards (XP lamps, teleports, access to areas) replace the gear you would otherwise buy.
  • Collections over speed. A main rushes to end-game; an Ironman builds a toolkit (herbs, runes, birdhouses, farms) that pays off for hundreds of hours.
  • Death is permanent loss. Standard Ironmen keep 3 items on death; Ultimate Ironmen keep nothing — bank your loot often.
💡 Verified Ironman Restrictions (OSRS Wiki, July 2026)

Ironmen cannot trade with other players (except Shield of Arrav / Heroes' Quest quest items and Bonds), cannot use the Grand Exchange to buy or sell any item except Bonds, cannot pick up drops from another player's kills, cannot use Accept Aid, and cannot enter another player's POH. Group Ironmen may trade within their group. Hardcore Ironmen have one life; Ultimate Ironmen cannot bank at all.

2. 🚫 The #1 Rule: No Grand Exchange

This is the single fact new Ironmen get wrong most often, so it gets its own section. The Grand Exchange is a trading hub — and trading is exactly what Ironman mode forbids. When you try to buy or sell anything other than a Bond you will be stopped. Examining any item even shows its High Alchemy value instead of its GE price, a quiet reminder that the market is not yours.

💡 The Bond Loophole (Legit)

Because Bonds are the one GE exception, an Ironman can still fund membership with GP they earn solo (or with real money). Earning your first Bond from bosses like Vorkath later is a classic Ironman milestone — see the Bond Farming Guide.

🏆 Recommended First Real Goal: Barrows Gear

Once you have 60+ combat and Priest in Peril, Barrows is your first big gear spike. Each chest at 88%+ reward potential guarantees a Barrows item, and a specific piece sits around 1/102. Guthan's heals you (sustain), Dharok's hits hard, and Ahrim's is your first real Magic set. All of it is self-obtainable.

💡 High Alch Is Your "GE"

With no Grand Exchange, High Alchemy (55 Magic) is how you convert junk into GP. Train Magic on bones/alkarid flies early, then alch your Slayer and boss drops. It is slower than the GE but it is the Ironman economy.

🐹 The Giant Mole Is the Best First Boss

She has no Slayer or quest requirement, hits a max of 21, and is easily safespotted. Her mole claws and skins trade to Wyson the gardener for bird nests — your first renewable source of herb and tree seeds. With the Falador Hard Diary her drops become noted, stretching trips.

8. ⚠️ Common Mistakes (Myth-Busting)

❌ Myth 1: "I'll just buy gear from the GE."

Wrong. Ironmen cannot use the Grand Exchange for any item except Bonds. Plan to obtain gear through bosses, skilling, and shops. If you wanted the GE, you picked the wrong mode.

❌ Myth 2: "Trade a friend for supplies."

Wrong. Player-to-player trades are blocked (except the two quest items and Bonds). Your Group Ironman can share with group members only.

❌ Myth 3: "Skip quests, just train."

Inefficient. Quests hand you combat levels and unlocks you cannot purchase. Waterfall + Monkey Madness II alone are worth millions of GP in free XP.

❌ Myth 4: "Ultimate Ironman can use the bank."

No. Ultimate Ironmen cannot deposit or withdraw from a bank at all — only the looting bag and on-person storage. Bank constantly on a standard Ironman so a death is not catastrophic.

❌ Myth 5: "Hardcore deaths in minigames are safe."

Mostly true — but know which. Barbarian Assault, CoX, Castle Wars and Pest Control are safe deaths for Hardcore Ironmen, but a wildy or boss death still de-irons you. Learn the safe list before risking a life.

10. ❓ FAQ

Can an Ironman use the Grand Exchange at all?

Only to buy Bonds via a unique Ironman-only interface. You cannot buy or sell any other item, and you cannot sell Bonds either. Everything else must be sourced yourself.

What is the best first quest on an Ironman?

Waterfall Quest — it grants 13,750 XP split across Attack, Strength, Magic and Hitpoints with zero combat, instantly getting you to a useful level for free.

What is the best first boss for an Ironman?

The Giant Mole. No requirements, easy to safespot, and her claws/skins trade for bird nests that seed your herb and tree farms. Baby mole is a 1/3,000 pet.

Can I play with my friends if I'm an Ironman?

You can play alongside them, but you cannot trade. The exception is Group Ironman, where you and up to four group members can trade and share within the group.

Should I make an Ultimate Ironman for my first account?

Probably not. Ultimate Ironmen cannot bank, which makes the early game brutally slow. Standard Ironman is the recommended first mode; try UIM only if you enjoy the extra challenge.

11. 🏁 Final Tips

  • No GE, no problem: High Alch (55 Magic) is your economy. Bank often so a death costs little.
  • Quest first: Waterfall, Monkey Madness II, Priest in Peril, Fairytale II front-load your power.
  • One boss at a time: Giant Mole → Barrows → Obor/Bryophyta → then the boss roadmap.
  • Build the toolkit: birdhouses, herb runs, and shop runs pay off for hundreds of hours.
  • Next step: once the first 10 hours are done, follow the Ironman Boss Progression Guide for your mid-game order.
