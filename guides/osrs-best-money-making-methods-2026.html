Looking for the best money making methods in OSRS in 2026? This complete guide covers 15+ GP-making methods from low level (F2P) to end-game (Vorkath, CoX), with exact GP per hour and requirements.

Before You Start: 3 Golden Rules

  • GP/hr is a ceiling, not a promise. The numbers below assume near-perfect efficiency. Expect 60–70% on a normal day, and less while you're still learning the method.
  • Match the method to your account. A level-3 with 10 GP can't do Vorkath. Use the Quick Decision table to find what fits your stats and starting cash, then work up.
  • Consistency beats intensity. Earning 500K GP every day for a week outperforms one 3-hour grind that burns you out. Pick methods you can repeat without hating the game.

Low Level Methods (Combat 1-50)

1. Cowhide Collection (1.5-2M GP/hr)

  • Requirements: None
  • Method: Kill cows north of Lumbridge, collect cowhides
  • Profit: ~1,200 GP per cowhide, ~1,500 cowhides/hr

Profit: 1.5-2M GP/hr

2. Mining Iron Ore (1-1.5M GP/hr)

  • Requirements: 15 Mining
  • Location: Al Kharid Mine (3 iron rocks)
  • Profit: ~400 GP per iron ore, ~700 ore/hr

Profit: 1-1.5M GP/hr

3. Fishing Lobsters (1-1.2M GP/hr)

  • Requirements: 40 Fishing
  • Location: Musa Point (Karamja)
  • Profit: ~150 GP per lobster, ~200 lobsters/hr

Profit: 1-1.2M GP/hr

Mid Level Methods (Combat 50-80)

4. Slayer (Variable: 300K-2M/hr)

  • Requirements: 75+ Combat, started "Smoking Kills"
  • Method: Do Slayer tasks, focus on high-GP tasks (Gargoyles, Nechryaels)
  • Profit: Depends on task, 300K-2M GP/hr

Profit: 300K-2M GP/hr

5. Flipping Items (200K-2M GP/hr)

  • Requirements: 5-10M GP starting cash
  • Method: Buy low, sell high on GE. Use price checker tools.
  • Profit: Depends on market, 200K-2M GP/hr

Profit: 200K-2M GP/hr

6. Barrows (500K-1.2M/hr)

  • Requirements: 70+ Combat, 43 Prayer
  • Method: Kill Barrows brothers, loot chest
  • Profit: 100-300K GP per chest, 4-6 kils/hr

Profit: 500K-1.2M GP/hr

High Level Methods (Combat 80+)

7. Vorkath (2-4M GP/hr)

  • Requirements: 85 Ranged/Magic, 43+ Prayer, finished Dragon Slayer 2
  • Method: Solo Vorkath, use anti-venom + pray potions
  • Profit: 2-4M GP/hr (highest consistent GP/hr)

Profit: 2-4M GP/hr

2026 Update: Vorkath is still the most consistent high GP/hr method.

8. Zulrah (2-3M GP/hr)

  • Requirements: 75 Ranged/Magic, 43+ Prayer
  • Method: Learn all 4 rotations, use safespot
  • Profit: 2-3M GP/hr (learneable rotations)

Profit: 2-3M GP/hr

9. Chambers of Xeric (3-6M GP/hr)

  • Requirements: 85+ all combat stats, team recommended
  • Method: Raid CoX, aim for 10,000+ points
  • Profit: 3-6M GP/hr (team split)

Profit: 3-6M GP/hr

AFK Money Making (While Doing Other Things)

10. Gargoyles (600K-800K/hr)

  • Requirements: 75 Slayer
  • Method: Kill Gargoyles on Slayer task
  • Profit: 600K-800K GP/hr (semi-AFK)

11. Runecrafting (500K-1.5M GP/hr)

  • Requirements: 50+ Runecrafting (for Nature runes)
  • Method: Craft Nature runes at Altar
  • Profit: 500K-1.5M GP/hr (very click-intensive)

Tips for Maximizing Profit

  • Use GE price checker: Always check prices before committing
  • Consider bonds: If you have 10M+ GP, buy bonds instead of paying real money
  • Don't put all gold in one method: Diversify your income
  • Use price-checker websites: Like GE Tracker or OSRS Exchange

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What's the fastest way to my first bond (12.8M GP)? Flipping rares, Zulrah, or herb + bird-house runs on an established account. Brand-new accounts should start with F2P money makers and graduate up.
  • Why is my real GP/hr lower than the guide says? Rare drops, market competition, and death costs aren't in the base figure. Treat the numbers as a skilled-player average, not a floor.
  • Should I buy membership to make more gold? Usually yes — members methods (Barrows, Zulrah, CoX) earn 3–5× the best F2P options. One bond often pays for itself in a few days of focused grinding.
  • Is flipping safe for new players? Yes, but start with 1–2M GP and liquid items. Never invest your whole bank in one flip, and verify the margin with a price-checker before committing.
