OSRS Combat Achievements Advanced Guide 2026 — Elite, Master & Grandmaster Strategy

Updated August 19, 2026  |  15 min read  |  By Yanchun Wu

You cleared Easy and Medium, maybe a few Hard tasks — now the real wall begins. Elite, Master, and Grandmaster Combat Achievements demand precision, specific gear, and manufactured luck. This advanced guide shows how the tiers unlock and how to actually finish them.

🏆 OSRS Guru PvM Series · Advanced · 18 min read · Updated for 2026
🛡️ Level: Endgame

Combat Achievements (CAs) are the closest thing OSRS has to a bossing "report card". The first tiers are about showing up; the advanced tiers are about mastery. Elite, Master, and Grandmaster tasks force you to optimize gear, learn niche mechanics, and execute under pressure. This guide assumes you know the basics from our full Combat Achievements guide and want the advanced playbook: how tiers gate each other, which hard tasks are worth chasing, how to build a loadout that clears them, and the reward math that tells you where to stop. If you are still learning boss fundamentals, start with the PvM beginner guide before you burn attempts on timer-locked tasks.

The single mindset shift between Easy/Medium and Elite+ is that constraints, not bosses, become the enemy. A normal Vorkath kill is trivial for a geared player; a "kill Vorkath in under 90 seconds" task is a completely different fight that punishes every wasted tick. Advanced CAs are won in the planning phase — pre-pots, inventory layout, gear switches, and route memorization — far more than in raw reaction speed. Treat each task like a speedrun segment, not a normal kill, and your clear rate climbs dramatically.

⚡ Quick Verdict — The 30-Second Read

  • 📌 Advanced CAs test constraints (timers, no-prayer, restricted gear), not just your ability to kill the boss.
  • 📌 Tiers are gated: you must finish most of Elite before Master appears, and most of Master before Grandmaster.
  • 📌 Near-max stats + a BiS weapon (Fang / Bowfa / Shadow) are effectively required for Master and Grandmaster tasks.
  • 📌 Practice on high-density, friendly bosses first — Vorkath, Zulrah, Grotesque Guardians — to bank early wins.
  • 📌 For most players, Master is the smart stopping point: the Grandmaster grind mostly buys a cosmetic title.

1. 🎯 What Advanced Combat Achievements Actually Require

The jump from Medium to Elite is a different skill set, not just harder bosses. Easy and Medium tasks are completion-based; advanced tasks add constraints that punish sloppy play. Understanding the constraint taxonomy is the first step to clearing them, because most failed attempts are attempts at the wrong thing.

Common constraint families include speed kills (finish under a tight timer), no-damage or low-damage runs, no-prayer or limited-prayer challenges, spec-weapon-only kills, restricted gear (bronze-only, quest-required, or no-degrade setups), and flawless mechanical execution (perfect transitions, no hits taken). Each removes a safety net you relied on at lower tiers. A speed task strips your margin for error on DPS; a no-prayer task strips your margin for error on defense; a restricted-gear task strips both at once.

Advanced CAs reward preparation as much as execution. The player who pre-pots outside the instance, pre-arranges the exact inventory, and has studied the precise task wording clears tasks the better-geared but unprepared player cannot. When a task says "without leaving the boss room," it means exactly that — teleporting out to re-pot or grabbing a secondary from the bank invalidates the attempt. When it says "using only Ranged," your spec weapon and thralls must also be Ranged. The wording is a contract; read it literally and optimize for the constraint, not for the kill.

There is also a stamina and focus dimension most players underestimate. Advanced tasks are short, intense, and unforgiving — a single missed tick at the 80-second mark can fail a 90-second timer you had been on pace for. You cannot "grind" these the way you grind slayer; you have to execute cleanly. Build a pre-attempt routine (covered in Section 7) so that the mechanical execution becomes automatic and your attention stays on the constraint.

💡 Read the Task Text Literally

Many failed attempts come from misreading. "Without leaving the boss room" means exactly that. "Using only Ranged" includes your spec weapon and any summoned helper. Re-read the exact wording before every attempt so you optimize the right thing — the constraint, not the kill.

2. 🪜 Tier Unlocks — Elite, Master & Grandmaster Gates

CAs are tiered, and higher tiers only become visible and completable after you finish enough of the tier below. The system is intentionally a ladder, and understanding the gates prevents wasted effort on tasks you cannot even attempt yet.

The tier ladder

The tiers run Easy → Medium → Hard → Elite → Master → Grandmaster. You must complete a set number of tasks in each tier (typically the full lower tier or a large majority of it) to unlock the next. You cannot skip to Grandmaster without grinding the rungs beneath, and the in-game CA panel will simply hide the higher tier until you qualify. The diagram below shows the gating: each tier is locked until the one before it is substantially complete.

Why the gate matters for planning

Because you unlock upward, set a realistic stop point before you start. Clear Easy/Medium/Hard, push Elite, then Master, then decide if Grandmaster is worth it. Many players stop at Elite or Master because the reward jump to Grandmaster is small relative to the effort. Knowing your target up front stops you from over-investing in a cosmetic you do not care about.

Discovery tasks

Most tasks are visible once the tier unlocks, but a few are "discovery" tasks that appear only after a specific action — a particular kill condition, a niche method, or an unusual route. Watch the CA panel after unusual kills; you may unlock one you did not know existed. Our main CA guide lists the discovery set per boss so you can deliberately trigger them instead of waiting to get lucky.

Rewards scale, not linearly

Each tier grants a one-time reward plus progress toward permanent account-wide unlocks at Elite, Master, and Grandmaster — things like extra teleport options and small utility bonuses that help with the harder content above. The big permanent unlocks sit at the top. Know which reward you are grinding so you do not over-invest in a tier whose payoff you do not actually want. The table below summarizes the gate logic and what each tier tends to be worth.

Tier Unlocks When Typical Effort What You Get Worth It?
Easy From the start Low Intro perks, familiarity Yes — free
Medium Easy done Low–Moderate Small utility unlocks Yes
Hard Medium done Moderate Useful account perks Yes
Elite Hard done High Permanent perk + Master gate Yes for most
Master Elite done Very High Top permanent perk Yes — best stop point
Grandmaster Master done Extreme Title + cosmetics Only if you want the title
⚠️ Don't Chase GM First

Grandmaster tasks are among the hardest content for many bosses. Attempting them before Elite and Master build the muscle memory those tiers provide is inefficient. Climb the ladder — the no-prayer and timer habits you form at Elite are exactly what Grandmaster demands.

3. 🧠 High-Difficulty Task Strategy Frameworks

Advanced tasks fall into repeatable categories. Learn the framework for each and you can adapt to almost any task the panel throws at you, instead of re-learning from zero every time. Below are the four families and the exact approach for each.

Speed-kill framework

For timer tasks, every tick counts. Pre-pot outside the instance, enter with full Spec energy, use the best DPS rotation you can, and never waste a tick moving or mis-clicking. Bring the strongest weapon you can — a Fang, Bowfa, or Shadow — and optimize your gear for raw DPS, not sustain, because the fight should end before sustain matters. Practice at normal pace three times to learn the route, then push the timer. Record your best attempt's time so you know exactly how much slack you have.

No-prayer / low-prayer framework

These force perfect dodging. Learn each boss's attack telegraph — the animation, sound, or chatbox cue — so well that you avoid damage instead of absorbing it. Food becomes your buffer, so over-prepare inventory space and pre-brew so you are not caught with a red bar during a forced hit. The boss death-prevention guide covers the telegraph-reading habits that make these tasks feasible.

Restricted-gear framework

"Bronze-only" or "no upgraded gear" tasks test efficiency under a DPS handicap. Pick the boss with the weakest defence for your restricted weapon, and use every allowed buff — potions, altar, familiar, vengeance — to close the gap. These tasks are often about boss choice, not skill: a fast-killing low-defence boss turns a brutal handicap into a comfortable clear. Read the constraint, then pick the easiest legal target.

Flawless-execution framework

"No hits taken" or "perfect transition" tasks demand consistency over a full fight. Break the encounter into segments, master each segment, then chain them. Watch a speedrunner or a clean kill to spot the one repeating mistake you keep making — almost everyone has exactly one. The PvM beginner guide has the mechanical foundations these builds on.

🎯 The 3-Attempt Rule

  • Attempt 1: execute normally, note exactly where the timer or constraint broke.
  • Attempt 2: fix that one thing, re-measure the gap.
  • Attempt 3: optimize the opening and closing, then push for the clear.

Why three and not thirty? Because beyond three attempts fatigue and tilt take over, and your execution gets worse, not better. If you have not cleared in three clean, focused attempts, the blocker is preparation or gear — not "just one more try." Step away, review, and return. This single discipline saves more CA attempts than any weapon upgrade.

4. 🏝️ Best Bosses for Grinding Tiers Efficiently

Not all bosses are equal for CAs. Some have dense, achievable task lists; others have one brutal task that gates the whole tier. Prioritizing the friendly bosses is the difference between a weekend of clears and a month of frustration.

Highest task density per hour

Bosses like Vorkath, Zulrah, Grotesque Guardians, and the Kraken have approachable Hard/Elite tasks because their mechanics are well understood and repeatable, and their kill times are short enough to attempt a task many times per hour. They are the ideal early advanced targets. Our boss guide hub links to per-boss pages with exact task routes.

Avoid the bottleneck bosses early

Some bosses have a single Grandmaster-tier task so notorious it blocks completion for months (common examples are the solo end-game raids and certain high-enrage content). Do not start your advanced journey on the boss famous for its hardest task. Build momentum on friendly bosses first, bank the Elite and Master clears, and only tackle the notorious one once your mechanics and gear are proven.

Rotations, solo vs group, and stacking

Certain tasks rotate or boost on specific days — batch your attempts when they are active rather than forcing them off-cycle. For speed and flawless tasks, solo often gives full control; group content can ease pressure but adds coordination overhead. Pick based on the constraint. And critically: pick 2–3 bosses and clear their Elite tiers together, because the rhythm of similar mechanics carries over and you hit the Master unlock faster than hopping between unrelated fights.

Boss Task Density Difficulty to Clear Best For
Vorkath High Moderate Elite speed + no-prayer tasks
Zulrah High Moderate Elite timing tasks
Grotesque Guardians Medium–High Low–Moderate Early Elite wins
Kraken Medium Low Fast practice reps
Phantom Muspah Medium High Master mechanical tasks
Nex High Very High Master/GM for geared teams
💡 Stack Your Progress

Pick 2–3 bosses and clear their Elite tiers together. The rhythm of similar mechanics carries over, and you hit the Master unlock faster than hopping between unrelated bosses. Momentum is the cheapest multiplier in CA grinding.

5. ⚔️ Gear & Stats You Need Before Master/GM

Advanced tasks expose weak gear instantly. The DPS you "get away with" at Hard tier fails a 90-second timer at Elite, and the defense you patch with prayer at Medium fails a no-prayer task at Master. Close these gaps before attempting the top tiers.

Stats first, then gear

Max or near-max combat stats — 99 in your primary styles, 95+ Prayer, 90+ in supporting stats — remove RNG from damage rolls and make speed tasks feasible. Potions help, but the base must be there. A player at 85 Attack attempting a Fang speed task fights an uphill battle a 99 Attack player does not, because the max-hit and accuracy gaps compound over a short fight. If your stats are not there yet, the best-in-slot guide and the budget gear guide show the cheapest path to the thresholds.

Weapon priority

The weapon slot dominates DPS, and DPS is what most timer tasks measure. Melee: a Fang or Scythe. Ranged: a Bowfa or Twisted Bow. Magic: a Shadow or Sang. If you cannot afford BiS, the gear checklist shows budget switches that still clear most Elite tasks — but Master and Grandmaster usually need the real weapon. There is no substitute for raw max hit when the clock is the constraint.

Consumables, switches, and a restricted bank

Advanced tasks assume perfect consumable use: Super combats, ranging/magic pots, brews and restores for no-prayer tasks, and clean gear switches for style-locked tasks. Set your inventory identically each attempt so switching is automatic under pressure. Some tasks restrict gear to "quest-required" or "non-degradable" items — keep a dedicated restricted setup banked so you are never scrambling mid-session. The boss inventory prep guide walks through a repeatable loadout system.

Tier Combat Stats Weapon Consumables
Elite 90s in style, 80+ Prayer Strong switch or entry BiS Standard brews/restores
Master 99 primary, 90+ Prayer BiS or near-BiS Full pre-pot + switches
Grandmaster Maxed, 99 Prayer True BiS Perfect, memorized loadout
⚠️ Don't Attempt GM Under-Geared

Grandmaster tasks frequently assume near-BiS. Walking in with budget gear wastes attempts you could have spent climbing Elite and Master first. Hit the gear threshold, then attempt — your success rate multiplies, and you stop burning supplies on fights you were never going to clear.

6. 🎁 Rewards Worth the Grind (and Which to Skip)

CA rewards mix permanent perks, cosmetics, and a title. Spending your effort where it pays off is the difference between a satisfying completion and a burnout-driven grind you regret.

Permanent perks

The headline rewards are account-wide perks granted at Elite, Master, and Grandmaster — extra teleport headroom, small utility unlocks, and convenience bonuses that help with the harder content above and with everyday bossing. They are the main reason to climb past Hard, and they keep paying off long after the CA panel is closed.

Cosmetic and title rewards

Higher tiers grant capes, emblems, and the "Grandmaster" title. These are prestige, not power. If the title does not matter to you, stopping at Master saves enormous time for near-identical practical benefit — you keep the top permanent perk and skip the longest, most demanding grind in the game for a name tag.

What to skip

A few tasks offer tiny rewards for disproportionate effort — a brutal no-prayer clear on a boss you will never farm, or a speed task that needs a specific world and rotation. Unless you are a completionist, leave those and still claim the tier reward from the tasks you did complete. Quitting a single task is not quitting the game, and the tier reward does not require 100% of tasks.

Reward timing

Claim tier rewards the moment you hit the threshold — the permanent perks help with the harder tasks above, so sitting on an unlocked reward while grinding is leaving free power on the table. The achievement diary guide covers the same "claim early, use immediately" logic for the diary rewards that stack with CA perks.

💡 Optimize for the Perk, Not the Title

If the permanent perk unlocks at Master, ask whether the extra Grandmaster effort is worth a cosmetic. For most players, Master is the efficient stopping point — the GM grind rarely justifies a title you will show off for a week.

7. 🚫 Common Mistakes That Stall Your Progress

Almost everyone hits the same walls. Avoid these and your climb is faster and far less frustrating.

Mistake 1 — Attempting GM before Elite/Master

Skipping tiers robs you of the mechanics practice the ladder builds. Climb in order; the muscle memory for timers and no-prayer fights is the actual point, and Grandmaster assumes you already have it.

Mistake 2 — Under-preparing consumables

Forgetting a potion or mis-arranging the inventory wastes an attempt on a task where one missed tick fails you. Standardize your setup so the prep is automatic and your attention stays on the fight.

Mistake 3 — Rage-attempting the same task

After three failed tries, fatigue makes you worse. Walk away, review a guide or a replay, return fresh. Tilt is the silent CA killer — it turns a winnable task into a session-ending loop.

Mistake 4 — Wrong boss priority

Starting on a boss famous for one brutal task sinks morale and burns gear. Begin with high-density, friendly bosses to bank early wins and build the rhythm that carries into the hard content.

Mistake 5 — Ignoring rotations

Some tasks are only practical on active days. Forcing them off-cycle multiplies your attempt count for zero benefit. Track rotations and batch your attempts when the task is actually live.

📋 Pre-Attempt Checklist

  • Re-read the exact task text and constraint.
  • Confirm stats, weapon, and consumables meet the threshold.
  • Standardize inventory and gear switches.
  • Practice the opening segment once at a safe pace.
  • Attempt calm — stop after 3 fails, review, retry later.

8. 🛠️ Sample Elite & Master Task Walkthroughs

Theory is easier than execution, so here are three concrete walkthroughs showing how the frameworks above turn into a clear. These are templates — adapt the boss to whatever you are attempting.

Walkthrough A — Vorkath speed task (Elite)

Pre-pot outside with a Super combat and a ranging pot, enter with full Spec and a Dragon hunter crossbow or Blowpipe depending on your setup. Kill the spawn with the correct method, then unload Spec at the right window. The mistake most players make is wasting the first rotation on movement; instead, pre-position so your first attack lands on tick 1. Time your kills and keep a log — once you are consistently 5–10 seconds under the cap, the task is a formality. The gear checklist shows the exact switches that shave those seconds.

Walkthrough B — Grotesque Guardians no-prayer (Master)

This is a dodge task, not a DPS task. Learn the exact telegraph for the granite hammer slam and the flying phase, and pre-brew during the safe windows so your health bar is managed without prayer. The fight is long enough that one greedy eat loses you the task; instead, eat on a fixed schedule tied to the attack pattern. Most clears come from players who stopped trying to out-DPS the boss and started treating it as a rhythm game.

Walkthrough C — Restricted-gear kill (Elite/Master)

Pick the lowest-defence boss that satisfies the constraint, then stack every legal buff: altar for prayer restore, familiar for damage, Vengeance if allowed, and the strongest permitted weapon. The constraint is the boss choice — once you pick a target whose defence is low enough that your handicapped weapon still hits hard, the task becomes a normal kill wearing silly gear. Read the constraint, choose the easiest legal boss, then execute.

9. ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need max stats for Grandmaster tasks?

For most bosses, yes — near-max combat stats and high Prayer make tight timers and no-prayer tasks feasible. A few GM tasks are possible under-geared, but the attempt count is punishing and the supply cost adds up fast. Build stats and gear first; the gear checklist lays out the thresholds so you know when you are actually ready.

Can I complete Master tier with budget gear?

Often yes for the easier Master tasks, especially with a strong weapon. The hardest Master tasks and most Grandmaster tasks need near-BiS. Budget clears the ladder to Master; BiS clears GM. If you are between the two, push Master with what you have and treat GM as a later goal once the weapon slot is sorted.

Which tier reward is most worth it?

The permanent account-wide perks at Elite and Master are the practical prize. The Grandmaster title is prestige only. For most players, Master is the efficient stopping point — the GM grind rarely justifies a cosmetic name tag when the top perk is already yours at Master.

How do I find which tasks are active today?

The in-game CA panel shows availability and any rotation. Batch your attempts on active days rather than forcing them off-cycle. Our main CA guide explains the rotation system and which tasks to watch for, so you are not attempting something that cannot complete today.

Should I learn boss mechanics before CAs?

Absolutely. CAs assume fluent mechanics. If you are still learning a boss, start with the PvM beginner guide and the per-boss pages, then return once kills are comfortable. Attempting advanced tasks on an unlearned boss is pure frustration — you will fail the constraint and the kill.

What is the fastest way to unlock Master tier?

Clear Elite on 2–3 high-density bosses (Vorkath, Zulrah, Grotesque Guardians) rather than spreading thin. Similar mechanics carry over, so you build rhythm and hit the Elite completion threshold faster, which is what flips Master from hidden to available. Stacking progress beats hopping between unrelated bosses.

Why do I keep failing speed tasks by a few seconds?

Almost always wasted ticks: moving when you could attack, a slow first rotation, or a mis-timed Spec. Pre-pot outside, pre-position inside, and log your best times so you know your slack. The fix is almost never "more gear" — it is "stop wasting ticks." A clean route beats a slightly better weapon.

Are no-prayer tasks about gear or skill?

Mostly skill — specifically, telegraph reading. The boss hits just as hard; you simply cannot absorb it with prayer, so you must avoid it. Learn the attack cue, pre-brew on a fixed schedule during safe windows, and treat the fight as a rhythm game. The death-prevention guide builds exactly this habit.

Should I skip a task I cannot complete?

If a single task is blocking a tier reward you want and it is disproportionately hard for your account, yes — most tier rewards do not require 100% of tasks. Leave it, claim the tier reward from what you finished, and revisit later with better gear. Quitting one task is not quitting the game.

How do CAs relate to the death mechanics I should know?

Indirectly but usefully: advanced tasks push you into riskier no-prayer and flawless runs where a mistake can cost your items. Understanding death mechanics — what you keep on death, how gravestones work, and when to protect — keeps a failed attempt from becoming a bank-draining lesson. Know the stakes before you attempt the risky ones.

10. 🏆 Final Tips — Climb Smart, Not Just Hard

  • Climb in order. Elite → Master → GM builds the muscle memory the ladder teaches.
  • Read tasks literally. "No leaving the room" means no leaving — optimize the exact constraint.
  • Standardize every setup. Same inventory, switches, and pre-pot removes decision-making under pressure.
  • Stop after 3 fails. Review, rest, retry later. Tilt wastes more attempts than bad gear.
  • Know your stop point. Master often delivers the real reward; GM is mostly prestige.
  • Stack friendly bosses. Clear 2–3 Elite tiers together to hit Master faster.
  • Fix the constraint, not the gear. Most failures are preparation errors you can correct for free.
