1. 🎒 Requirements & Gear Floor

The first thing every new Doom runner asks is "what do I need to even enter?" The answer is more forgiving than most endgame bosses, and that is exactly why the gear floor surprises people. Doom sits under the Tlati Rainforest inside the Ruins of Mokhaiotl, and the only quest requirement to reach the fight is The Final Dawn. There is no hard combat level, no skill gate, and no diary wall in front of the boss lobby. You can walk in with a bare account if you have finished the quest.

That does not mean you should. The fight itself is a delve boss where each successful clear lets you sink deeper, and the deeper you go the more punishing the mechanics become. Our stance at OSRS Guru is that you should treat 80+ combat as the comfortable entry floor — below that, the rock throws, shockwaves and freeze-heavy burrowed phases will simply delete you before you learn the rotation. The practical gear benchmark we use is roughly the level of a Corrupted Gauntlet completion: if you can confidently clear the Corrupted Gauntlet, your accounts and instincts are in the right zip code for Doom.

The stat line we actually recommend

For anyone planning to collect the valuable unlocks and push past the early delves, we recommend building toward this baseline before you treat Doom as a regular money maker:

StatOur Recommended FloorWhy It Matters
Ranged90+Powers the Twisted bow / Zaryte crossbow damage and keeps your DPS competitive through long delves
Magic80+Runs Tumeken's shadow or the Eye of Ayak for the magic-style demonbane and burst damage
Prayer77Unlocks the protection prayers and augury / rigour swaps you need to survive the burrowed and shielded phases
Defence70Reduces chip damage from rock throws, acid blood and shockwaves so brews last longer

Notice we do not list a melee attack stat in the core floor. Melee only matters for the Charging phase, where you must interrupt the boss with a melee hit, and for that you can bring any decently accurate melee weapon as a swap rather than building the account around strength. The three stats that actually define your day-to-day damage are Ranged, Magic and Prayer, with Defence as the survivability cushion.

If you want the full phase-by-phase picture of why these stats line up the way they do, read our main Doom of Mokhaiotl guide and the delve tiers guide before you finalise your loadout. The gear below is built around those mechanics, not around abstract best-in-slot theory.

⚠️ Don't over-level, don't under-prepare

Doom has no skill wall, so the trap is the opposite of most bosses: players walk in at 70 combat expecting a quest boss and leave with nothing. Hit the 90/80/77/70 line first, then learn the fight. The gear is cheaper than the deaths.

2. 🏹 Ranged Setup

Ranged is the most popular style at Doom for a simple reason: the Twisted bow scales against high-HP targets, and Doom's bulk rewards exactly that scaling. For the vast majority of delves, a Twisted bow with a full Masori set and a Venator ring is the strongest, most reliable loadout you can bring.

Weapon: Twisted bow, or Zaryte crossbow

The Twisted bow is the best-in-slot ranged weapon here. Its accuracy and damage scale with the target's Magic level, and against a boss with this much health it outperforms almost everything else in the game. If you do not own a Twisted bow, the Zaryte crossbow is the strong, attainable alternative: it hits hard, it is accurate, and it lets you use the same ruby bolt (e) tricks many bosses are weak to. Either weapon pairs with the rest of the ranged setup below without changing your plan.

Armour: Masori, and why it wins

The Masori body, chaps and hat are the ranged armour of choice. Masori gives the best ranged accuracy and damage bonus of any ranged armour in the game while still keeping a respectable defence profile, which matters because Doom's chip damage is constant. You wear the full set for the accuracy and the damage; the defence is a bonus that keeps your brews relevant deeper into a delve.

Ring: Venator ring

The Venator ring is the off-style ring that rounds out the build. Its projectile bounces and accuracy bonus make it the default choice for a ranged Doom setup, and it is the ring we list in the quick summary for exactly that reason. Fill the remaining slots — neck, cape, boots, gloves, ammunition — with your standard best-in-slot ranged gear, and you have a loadout that will take you comfortably through the delve range where most players choose to bank their unlocks.

The ranged style is also the one most players use for the regular, non-shielded portions of the fight, because it lets you keep distance and react to rock throws and shockwaves without committing to melee. When the shielded phase arrives, you swap off this set entirely — see the demonbane section below for why.

3. 🔮 Magic Setup

Magic is the second full damage style at Doom, and it is the better choice for players who already own Tumeken's shadow or who want to farm the Eye of Ayak from Doom itself and use it immediately. The magic loadout mirrors the ranged one in discipline: best-in-slot gear, a strong powered staff, and the right ring.

Weapon: Tumeken's shadow, or the Eye of Ayak

The Tumeken's shadow is the best-in-slot magic weapon and the strongest magic option at Doom when you can afford it. Its flat magic damage boost makes every cast hit harder, and against a high-HP boss that compounds into a fast, clean kill. The Eye of Ayak is the standout alternative: it is a 3-tick powered staff with a demonbane effect, which means it does double duty as both your magic damage stick and a demonbane weapon for the shielded phase. Because the Eye of Ayak drops from Doom itself, it is the weapon that turns a deep delve into a self-funding loop — you can farm it and then use it.

Armour: Ancestral robes

The Ancestral hat, robe top and robe legs are the magic armour of choice. Ancestral gives the largest magic damage bonus of any robe set and pairs naturally with Tumeken's shadow for maximum output. As with Masori on the ranged side, the defence is a welcome side effect that helps you eat fewer brews during the longer delves.

Ring: Magus ring

The Magus ring is the magic counterpart to the Venator ring: it boosts your magic damage and accuracy and is the default ring for a magic Doom setup. With shadow (or Eye of Ayak), Ancestral and a Magus ring, your magic loadout matches the ranged loadout blow for blow in terms of planning, and the choice between the two styles usually comes down to which best-in-slot weapon you already own.

Magic is especially attractive if you plan to use the Eye of Ayak, because that staff covers your magic damage and your demonbane requirement at the same time. For the exact drop values and how the Eye of Ayak compares to the other Doom unlocks, see our Doom rewards and drops guide.

4. 😈 Demonbane Weapons

This is the section that separates Doom from every other boss, and it is the one new runners underestimate. Doom is, at its core, a demon, and during the shielded phase the boss becomes effectively immune to normal damage. Only demonbane weapons deal true damage during that window. On top of that, the shielded phase is immune to poison, immune to venom, and immune to thralls — so your standard DPS tricks simply do not work. You must bring a demonbane weapon, full stop.

The three demonbane styles

There are three demonbane weapons, one for each combat style, so you can match your demonbane swap to whatever main style you are already using:

WeaponStyleRole in the Fight
Dark demonbaneMeleeThe melee-style demonbane; also useful as your Charging-phase interrupt weapon
Light demonbaneRangedThe ranged-style demonbane; pairs with a Masori swap for ranged players
Flame demonbaneMagicThe magic-style demonbane; pairs with an Ancestral swap for magic players

Because the demonbane weapons come in all three styles, you do not need to learn a new attack style to handle the shielded phase — you keep your accuracy by swapping to the demonbane that matches your build. The key is that the swap exists and that you make it the moment the shielded phase begins, because every normal hit you land while the shield is up is wasted.

The Purging Staff and your thralls

The Purging Staff deserves its own line. It is the weapon that summons thralls, and thralls are a big part of your damage profile for the parts of the fight that are not shielded. Since the shielded phase blocks thralls, you want your thralls active going into the other phases so they are already chipping the boss when the fight opens. The Purging Staff is therefore not just a weapon — it is your thrall engine, and it is what keeps your effective DPS high without burning your own food.

The mechanic is non-negotiable: if you arrive without a demonbane weapon, the shielded phase will outlast your supplies and you will wipe. This is why the gear floor above assumes you are building toward a real demonbane swap rather than hoping to brute-force the shield with a Twisted bow. Our main Doom guide explains exactly when the shielded phase triggers so you can time the swap instead of panic-swapping mid-volley.

5. 🥗 Inventory & Consumables

Doom is a long, phase-heavy fight, which means your inventory is brew-heavy by design. You are not racing a clock; you are managing a resource war across multiple phases, freezes and a shielded window where only demonbane lands. The consumable plan below is what we run, and it is built around keeping you alive through the burrowed and shielded phases without running dry.

The core consumables

ItemPurpose
Saradomin brewsYour primary healing. Brews restore a large chunk of HP while dropping your other stats, which is exactly what you want when you are being frozen and chunked
Super restoresRecover the stats that brews drain, and top up Prayer between phases so your protection prayers stay live
Prayer potionsKeep your protection prayers active through the burrowed and shielded phases where a dropped prayer means a hit you cannot afford
Sanfew serumCure poison, venom and the various status effects in one dose so a single status does not snowball into a death
Regen potionPassive healing between phases so you recover HP without spending a brew, stretching your food deeper into the delve
Thrall runesThe runes needed to summon thralls from the Purging Staff — your free, continuous damage source

How the inventory actually plays

The loop is simple to describe and hard to execute: you brew up after a rock throw or a shockwave, you super restore to fix the stat drain and top Prayer, and you sip a prayer potion the moment your protection prayer flickers. The sanfew serum is your panic button for any status that lands, and the regen potion is the quiet workhorse that tops you off while you reposition for the next phase. The thrall runes are cast early, from the Purging Staff, so your thralls are already attacking before the first real damage window.

A typical split is mostly brews with super restores as the close second, a smaller stack of prayer potions, one or two sanfew serums, one regen potion, and the runes for your thralls. The exact count depends on how deep you go — deeper delves need more brews because the phases last longer and hit harder — so read the delve tiers guide to scale your inventory to your stop point. Our boss gear checklist also has a pre-trip consumable tick-box you can copy.

⚠️ Brews without restores is a death sentence

Never fill your inventory with brews and forget super restores. A brew drops your stats every sip, and without restores you will be hitting like a player with 1 Attack the moment the shielded phase starts. The 4:2 or 3:1 brew-to-restore ratio is the floor, not the ceiling.

6. ⚡ Gear Swaps & Presets

Doom is a swap-heavy fight. You are not in one setup for the whole kill — you move between your main damage style, your demonbane weapon for the shielded phase, and a melee weapon for the Charging phase where you must interrupt the boss. If you do these swaps by hand in the bank between deaths, you will waste half your session. The fix is presets, and it is the single biggest quality-of-life upgrade a new Doom runner can make.

What you are swapping between

At minimum you are managing four states during a deep delve:

  • Ranged damage set — Twisted bow or Zaryte crossbow with Masori and a Venator ring for the open phases.
  • Magic damage set — Tumeken's shadow or Eye of Ayak with Ancestral and a Magus ring if magic is your main style.
  • Demonbane set — the Dark, Light or Flame demonbane weapon (matched to your style) for the shielded phase where only true damage lands.
  • Melee interrupt set — any accurate melee weapon to break the Charging phase before it completes.

The discipline is to pre-build each of these as a saved preset so the only thing you do mid-fight is tap an F-key and keep your eyes on the boss. The demonbane swap in particular has to be instant, because the shielded phase does not wait for you to fumble through your bank.

Build the presets before you dive

Our PvM gear swap and preset guide covers the exact way to lay out presets in RuneLite and on the official client, including how to order them so your demonbane and melee swaps sit on the keys you can hit without looking. The short version: bind your main style to one key, your demonbane to the next, and your melee interrupt to a third, then drill the swaps on shallow delves until they are muscle memory. By the time you reach the delve where the shielded phase actually threatens your loot, the swap should be automatic.

Presets also save your inventory state, which means your brew-heavy consumable plan carries across deaths and re-dives without you re-packing every time. That alone is worth setting up before your first serious session.

7. 📱 Mobile Bossing

Doom is absolutely doable on mobile, but it is harder there than on desktop for one specific reason: the fight demands fast, accurate gear swaps, and swapping on a touchscreen is slower and easier to mis-tap than hitting an F-key. If you plan to run Doom from the official mobile client, the work is in the layout, not in the account.

Make the swaps touch-friendly

The first rule of mobile Doom is to set your presets so the demonbane and melee swaps are one tap away, not buried in a menu. The mobile bossing practice guide walks through the preset button placement, the action bar layout, and the camera settings that stop you from mis-clicking a rock throw while you try to swap weapons. The goal is to reduce the fight to a small number of large, reliable taps rather than a string of precise ones.

Reduce clutter, protect your prayer

On a small screen, information overload is what kills you. Hide the chat you do not need, enlarge the prayer book, and keep your brew and restore tiles in a fixed corner so you are never hunting for a potion while frozen. The shielded-phase demonbane swap is the moment mobile players lose the most runs, so practice it on shallow delves until the tap is instant. If you can land the demonbane swap cleanly on mobile, the rest of the fight is manageable — the boss does not move faster on mobile, only your hands do.

Mobile is also where the regen potion and sanfew serum earn their slot, because a clean status cure and passive healing cover for the slightly slower reaction time of a touchscreen. Treat your consumables as a larger safety margin on mobile than you would on desktop, and scale your delves a little shallower until the swaps feel natural.

Doom of Mokhaiotl Gear FAQs

Q: What skill requirements do I need to fight the Doom of Mokhaiotl?

There is no hard skill gate. The only requirement to reach the boss is completing the Final Dawn quest. The stats that matter are the ones that keep you alive and hitting — we recommend 90+ Ranged, 80+ Magic, 77 Prayer and 70 Defence before you treat Doom as a regular delve.

Q: What combat level should I have before attempting Doom?

We treat 80+ combat as the comfortable floor. Below that, the freeze-heavy burrowed phases and the rock throws will out-pace your supplies. The practical benchmark is roughly a Corrupted Gauntlet completion — if you can clear that reliably, your account and instincts are ready to learn Doom.

Q: Is the Twisted bow required, or is the Zaryte crossbow enough?

The Twisted bow is best-in-slot and scales hard against high-HP targets, but the Zaryte crossbow is a strong, attainable alternative that performs well at Doom. If you already own a Zaryte crossbow and Masori, you do not need to buy a Twisted bow to start collecting unlocks.

Q: Do I absolutely need a demonbane weapon?

Yes. During the shielded phase the boss is immune to normal damage, poison, venom and thralls, and only a demonbane weapon deals true damage. You can choose Dark, Light or Flame demonbane to match your main style, but you must bring one. The Eye of Ayak also counts as a demonbane weapon while being a strong magic staff.

Q: What Prayer level is recommended?

77 Prayer. That unlocks the protection prayers you need for the burrowed and shielded phases and the augury / rigour switches that keep your accuracy up. Below 77 you lose too much survivability and DPS to make deep delves worth the risk.

Q: What is the best inventory for Doom?

Brew-heavy: Saradomin brews as your main heal, super restores to fix the stat drain and top Prayer, prayer potions to keep protection prayers live, one or two sanfew serums for status cures, a regen potion for passive healing, and the runes to summon thralls from a Purging Staff. Scale the brew count up for deeper delves.

Q: Can I use magic instead of ranged?

Yes. Magic is a full alternative with Tumeken's shadow (or the Eye of Ayak) plus Ancestral robes and a Magus ring. The choice between ranged and magic usually comes down to which best-in-slot weapon you already own. Both styles need a matched demonbane swap for the shielded phase.

Q: Do I need a melee weapon at all?

You need a melee weapon for the Charging phase, where you must interrupt the boss with a melee hit. You do not need to build your account around melee — any accurate melee weapon as a swap is enough to break the charge. The Dark demonbane doubles as that melee option.

Q: How expensive is the gear floor, really?

The gear floor is closer to a Corrupted Gauntlet account than to a maxed TMW bidder. Masori and a Zaryte crossbow, or Ancestral and an Eye of Ayak, are the realistic entry points; the Twisted bow and Tumeken's shadow are upgrades, not requirements. The demonbane weapon is the one non-negotiable purchase, and the Eye of Ayak can be farmed from the boss itself.

Q: Is Doom viable on mobile?

Yes, but the gear swaps are the hard part on a touchscreen. Set your demonbane and melee presets as one-tap buttons, enlarge your prayer book, and keep brews and restores in a fixed corner. Practice the shielded-phase swap on shallow delves first, and run your delves a little shallower than on desktop until the taps are instant.
