📱 OSRS Mobile App Crashes: Bonds Shop, Loading & Freeze Fixes (2026)

Updated August 19, 2026  |  15 min read  |  By Yanchun Wu

The Old School RuneScape mobile app is convenient until it isn't — the bonds/membership store crashes on open, the loading screen freezes at a black or frozen frame, or the game dies the moment you switch apps and come back to a black screen. This guide gives you a platform-by-platform repair checklist that actually works, with the exact menu paths and the settings that stop the crashes for good.

📅 Updated: August 2026 — Mobile Troubleshooting · 14 min read
🛡️ Level: All
🆕 NEW

The OSRS Mobile app shares the same game servers as the desktop client, but it runs inside a sandboxed mobile shell that is far more sensitive to memory pressure, background app limits, and store-purchase handshakes. Most crashes are not your account — they are the app losing its footing on the device. If your store view closes the instant it opens, start with our Android crash & black screen guide and the iOS crash & black screen guide, because the store crash and the black-screen crash often share one root cause.

The fixes below are ordered from fastest to most thorough. Work top to bottom and test after each step — roughly 80% of crashes stop after the cache and background-limit fixes, without a full reinstall. Everything here is safe; none of it touches your account, your bank, or your membership status.

💡 Quick Verdict

If the app only crashes when you open the bonds/membership shop, it is a store handshake failure — fix it with a clean reinstall and a stable network, not by buying through the website. If it freezes on the loading screen, it is almost always corrupted cached assets — clear the app cache (Android) or offload and reinstall (iOS). If it black-screens when you return from another app, your OS is killing OSRS in the background — disable battery optimization and enable background app refresh. Match the symptom to the section and you will be back in Gielinor in minutes.

1. 🔍 Why the OSRS Mobile App Crashes

Before you start tapping menus, it helps to know what is actually happening. The OSRS Mobile app is a wrapper around a game engine that was originally built for desktop. On a phone it has to share limited RAM, CPU, and GPU time with the operating system and every other app. When any one of those resources runs out, the OS forcibly closes the app — that is a "crash," and the app gets no chance to save state. Understanding the trigger tells you which fix to apply first.

① The five crash triggers that cover 95% of cases

Almost every OSRS Mobile crash falls into one of five buckets. Identify yours and you have already cut the fix time in half:

  • Memory pressure: The device runs low on RAM, the OS kills the game. Common on phones with 2–3 GB RAM, or when many apps are open.
  • Corrupted cached assets: A half-downloaded texture, interface file, or cache chunk fails to load and the app freezes or closes. This is the #1 cause of loading-screen freezes.
  • Background app limits: iOS and Android both aggressively suspend or kill apps that are not on screen. OSRS is stateful, so returning to it after suspension often lands on a black screen.
  • Store handshake failure: Opening the bonds/membership shop triggers a secure purchase handshake with the App Store or Google Play. A flaky network, an outdated payment profile, or a stale login token makes that handshake crash the app.
  • Outdated app or OS: Running an old app version against a new game update, or an old OS missing a required security patch, produces crashes the moment the app touches new content.

The table below maps each symptom to its most likely trigger so you can jump straight to the right section.

Symptom Most Likely Trigger Go To
App closes the instant you open the bonds/membership shop Store handshake failure Section 2
Frozen on the loading bar / black screen at launch Corrupted cached assets Section 3
Black screen when returning from another app Background app limits Section 4
Random closes during long sessions Memory pressure Section 5 / 6
Crash after a game update Outdated app or OS Section 7

② How crashes differ between iOS and Android

iOS and Android kill background apps differently, which is why the "black screen on return" fix is not the same on both platforms. On iOS, the OS suspends apps in place and can terminate them silently when it needs memory; the fix is Background App Refresh plus keeping OSRS out of the automatic offload list. On Android, manufacturers add their own battery killers (Adaptive Battery, Battery Saver, per-app optimization) on top of stock Android, so the fix is disabling battery optimization for OSRS specifically. Android also lets you clear the app cache without losing your account, which iOS cannot do — iOS requires a full offload-and-reinstall. That single difference is why the Android guide has a dedicated cache step and the iOS guide leans on reinstall.

The GPU story matters too. iOS devices use a tightly controlled GPU stack, so driver issues are rare — most iOS crashes are memory or store related. Android runs on hundreds of GPU drivers from many vendors, so rendering crashes (white flash, artifact, then close) are more common there. If you see a rendering crash on Android, our mobile overheating & crash guide covers thermal and GPU driver throttling in detail.

③ Reading your crash — what the screen is telling you

You do not need a crash log to diagnose most issues. The screen state at the moment of failure is the clue:

  • Crash at the Jagex logo: The app loaded but the game cache is missing or corrupt → clear cache / reinstall.
  • Freeze at a specific % on the loading bar: One cache chunk keeps failing → clear cache, switch network, retry.
  • Instant close on a button tap (shop, bank, GE): UI element triggered a bad state → usually fixed by cache clear or app update.
  • Black screen after returning: OS terminated the suspended app → background limit fix.
  • Crash with a "OSRS keeps stopping" system dialog (Android): Unhandled exception → clear cache + data, then reinstall.
💡 Capture the moment

If a crash is reproducible, note the exact screen and the last action. That one sentence ("it closes when I tap the Bonds button on the membership tab") is what Jagex support needs, and it tells you immediately whether you are in Section 2 or Section 3.

2. 🛒 Bonds Shop / Membership Store Crash

This is the most-reported crash of 2026: you open the app, go to the membership or bonds store, and the app closes immediately — sometimes with no error, sometimes with a store popup that flashes and vanishes. The store view is the only part of OSRS Mobile that talks directly to Apple or Google's payment system, so when that handshake fails, the whole app goes down instead of just the store.

① Why the store view crashes (and why it is not your bank)

Opening the bonds/membership shop launches an in-app purchase (IAP) session. That session requires three things to line up: a signed-in store account, a valid payment method on file, and a stable network connection to the store's servers. If any one is stale — say your App Store password expired, or Google Play has a declined card cached — the handshake throws an exception the mobile app does not catch gracefully, and the OS kills it. Crucially, this has nothing to do with your in-game bank, your bonds already owned, or your membership. Buying bonds through the website instead is a safe temporary workaround, but it does not fix the app; the steps below do.

② Fix on iOS (App Store store crash)

Work through these in order and test the store after each:

🍎 iOS store-crash repair steps

  1. Verify your Apple ID session: Settings → [your name] → scroll down and confirm you are signed in. If "Media & Purchases" shows a prompt, tap it and re-authenticate.
  2. Check the payment method: Settings → [your name] → Payment & Shipping. Make sure a valid card is listed and not expired. A declined or expired card is the #1 iOS store crash cause.
  3. Force-close and relaunch: Swipe up from the bottom (or double-click home), find OSRS, swipe it away, then reopen.
  4. Update the app: App Store → your avatar → Available Updates → update Old School RuneScape if present.
  5. Offload and reinstall: Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Old School RuneScape → Offload App (keeps your data), then Reinstall App. This rebuilds the store framework cleanly.
  6. Sign out and back into the App Store: Settings → [your name] → Sign Out, reboot, sign back in, then try the store.

If the store still crashes after step 5, the issue is almost certainly the Apple ID payment profile — step 6 resolves it in the majority of remaining cases.

③ Fix on Android (Google Play store crash)

Android's store crash is usually a stale Google Play cache or a payment-profile mismatch:

🤖 Android store-crash repair steps

  1. Clear Play Store cache: Settings → Apps → Google Play Store → Storage → Clear Cache. Then Clear Data only if clearing cache alone does not help.
  2. Check the payment method: Google Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Payment methods. Remove any expired or declined card and add a working one.
  3. Update Play Services: Settings → Apps → Google Play Services → App details in store → Update. Outdated Play Services breaks IAP handshakes.
  4. Clear the OSRS app cache: Settings → Apps → Old School RuneScape → Storage → Clear Cache (keeps your login). See our Android cache corruption guide for the full 5-step routine.
  5. Reinstall OSRS: Uninstall from the Play Store, reboot the phone, reinstall. This is the definitive store fix on Android.

Buyers on Android should also confirm the account used to install OSRS is the same account signed into Google Play. Installing with one Google account and buying with another is a classic silent crash trigger.

3. ❄️ Loading Screen Freeze Fixes

A frozen loading screen — stuck on the Jagex logo, stuck at a percentage, or a black screen that never resolves — means the app cannot finish building its local game cache. The cache is a large set of files the app downloads and reads on launch; if one is half-written or corrupt, the loader loops or dies. This is the single most common freeze, and it is almost always fixable without losing your account.

① Why the loading screen freezes

On first launch after an update, or after the cache was interrupted (a call, a network drop, the phone sleeping), one cache chunk can be left incomplete. The loader tries to read it, fails a checksum, and either loops or crashes. Poor networks make this worse because the partial download is never validated. The fix is to remove the bad cache chunk and let the app rebuild it on a stable connection.

② Steps to unfreeze and rebuild the cache

🔄 Loading-freeze recovery (both platforms)

  1. Force-close the app (iOS: app switcher swipe; Android: Recent Apps → close OSRS).
  2. Switch to a stable network: prefer Wi-Fi over mobile data, or vice versa if Wi-Fi is the problem. A 5 GHz Wi-Fi near the router is ideal for the initial cache build.
  3. Clear the cache: iOS → Offload App then Reinstall (Section 5 step 5); Android → Settings → Apps → Old School RuneScape → Storage → Clear Cache.
  4. Relaunch and wait: The first load after a cache clear can take 5–15 minutes on slower connections. Keep the screen on and the phone plugged in.
  5. If it still freezes: Settings → enable "Low Power Mode" off, disable any VPN or ad-blocker that intercepts TLS, then repeat the cache clear.
⚠️ Do not clear "Data" on Android unless reinstalling

On Android, "Clear Cache" keeps your login and settings. "Clear Storage / Clear Data" removes your local login and forces a full re-download — safe for your account but slower. Only use Clear Data if Clear Cache plus a reinstall does not fix the freeze.

③ Preventing the freeze from coming back

Once the cache rebuilds cleanly, keep it that way:

  • Let the app finish loading completely before locking the screen or taking a call.
  • Keep at least 1.5 GB of free storage — the cache needs room to write temporary files.
  • Update the app the same day a game update drops; launching an old app against new server content is a freeze risk.
  • If you play on mobile data, allow OSRS unrestricted data (Android: Data usage → Unrestricted data; iOS: Mobile Data → enable for OSRS).

4. 📱 Background / Black Screen on Return

You are mid-bank, you switch to a browser or a messaging app to check a price, and when you come back OSRS is a black screen — or it reloads from zero and drops your session. This is not a crash in the app; it is the operating system reclaiming memory from a suspended app. OSRS holds a large in-memory game state, so it is one of the first apps the OS sacrifices. The fix is to tell your OS to leave OSRS alone in the background.

① Why the app dies in the background

Both iOS and Android use a least-recently-used model: when memory is tight, the OS terminates the app you have not looked at longest. OSRS is memory-hungry, so after a few minutes backgrounded — especially with other apps open — it gets killed. You then return to either a black screen (the dead process is still "there" visually) or a cold restart. The setting changes below raise OSRS's priority so the OS keeps it alive longer.

② iOS background settings

🍎 Keep OSRS alive in the background (iOS)

  1. Enable Background App Refresh: Settings → General → Background App Refresh → turn ON (and set to Wi-Fi & Cellular Data).
  2. Stop iOS from offloading the app: Settings → App Store → toggle OFF "Offload Unused Apps." This prevents iOS from silently removing OSRS when storage is low.
  3. Disable Low Power Mode while playing: Control Center → turn off the battery icon. Low Power Mode aggressively terminates background apps.
  4. Close other apps: App switcher → close memory-heavy apps (Safari with many tabs, video, maps) so OSRS is not the victim.
  5. Keep the screen on: Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → set to 5 minutes or "Never" while you play.

③ Android background settings

🤖 Keep OSRS alive in the background (Android)

  1. Disable battery optimization for OSRS: Settings → Apps → Old School RuneScape → Battery → Unrestricted (or "Don't optimize"). This is the single biggest fix.
  2. Turn off Adaptive Battery: Settings → Battery → Adaptive Battery → OFF. Adaptive Battery is the top cause of "black screen on return" on Android.
  3. Allow background data: Settings → Apps → OSRS → Mobile data & Wi-Fi → enable "Background data" and "Unrestricted data."
  4. Lock the app in recents: Recent Apps → tap the OSRS card menu (or long-press) → Lock / Pin. This stops the cleaner from swiping it.
  5. Avoid third-party "cleaner" apps: Disable any battery saver, RAM booster, or game turbo that auto-kills background apps.

After these changes, returning to OSRS should resume your session instead of showing black. If it still black-screens only after many minutes, that is normal OS behavior under heavy memory load — keep fewer apps open. See our background disconnect prevention guide for the network side of the same problem.

5. 🍎 iOS-Specific Crash Checklist

iOS is the more locked-down platform, which means fewer knobs but also fewer variables. Run this checklist when the generic steps above are not enough. Most iOS crashes are memory, store, or OS-version related.

① Update iOS and the app

An old iOS version missing a required security or graphics patch crashes OSRS on new content. Check: Settings → General → Software Update. Install any pending update, then update OSRS from the App Store. Jagex targets the current and previous iOS versions; if you are two majors behind, updating iOS alone often stops the crashes.

② Offload and reinstall (the iOS "cache clear")

iOS has no cache-clear button, so the equivalent is offload-then-reinstall, which preserves your documents and data while rebuilding the app binary and its frameworks:

  • Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Old School RuneScape.
  • Tap Offload App, confirm, wait for it to finish.
  • Tap Reinstall App (or reinstall from the App Store).
  • Log in — your account and settings return because they were preserved.

This is the iOS answer to Android's Clear Cache and fixes the majority of launch and store crashes that a normal relaunch does not.

③ Reset network settings (last iOS resort)

If crashes are network-tied — the app dies when fetching the store or on login — a corrupt Wi-Fi/Cellular profile can be the cause. Reset it: Settings → General → Transfer or Reset iPhone → Reset → Reset Network Settings. You will re-enter Wi-Fi passwords, but it clears stale TLS and DNS state that intermittently break the OSRS connection. This pairs well with our can't login fix guide when the crash happens at the login handshake.

6. 🤖 Android-Specific Crash Checklist

Android's openness is a double-edged sword: you can fix almost anything, but manufacturers pile on battery and memory managers that quietly kill OSRS. This checklist covers the Android-only steps that stop the crashes.

① Clear the cache (Android's fastest fix)

Unlike iOS, Android lets you wipe only the app cache without touching your account:

  • Settings → Apps → Old School RuneScape → Storage.
  • Tap Clear Cache (keeps login).
  • If crashes persist, tap Clear Storage (removes local login, full re-download).

This single step resolves the loading freeze and most "OSRS keeps stopping" dialogs. The dedicated Android black screen & cache guide walks the full 5-step version with storage and compatibility checks.

② Free up storage and RAM

Android crashes hard when internal storage drops below ~10% free. OSRS needs room for the cache and temporary files:

  • Settings → Storage → free at least 2 GB (delete old videos, offload photos to cloud).
  • Recent Apps → close everything except OSRS before a long session.
  • Avoid running OSRS on a phone with less than 3 GB RAM alongside other heavy apps.

③ Tame manufacturer battery and developer options

Beyond the background settings in Section 4, two Android-specific traps cause crashes:

  • Manufacturer killers: Samsung "Auto Optimize," Xiaomi "MIUI Optimization," OnePlus "OxygenOS Aggressive Doze" — find the brand's battery setting and set OSRS to "No restrictions."
  • Developer options: If you enabled Developer Options, turn OFF "Don't keep activities" and "Background process limit" set too low. Both silently kill OSRS in the background. Settings → System → Developer Options → reset these to default.
  • GPU rendering: If you see a white flash before the crash, force GPU rendering off: Developer Options → "Force GPU rendering" OFF, and "Disable HW overlays" OFF.

If crashes are heat-related rather than memory-related, our overheating & crash guide has the thermal throttling steps.

7. 🧰 Universal Fixes & Contacting Jagex

If platform-specific steps did not help, these apply to both iOS and Android and cover the cases where the problem is the install itself or your account state on Jagex's side.

① Clean reinstall (the definitive fix)

When nothing else works, a clean reinstall removes a corrupted app binary and forces a fresh cache build:

  1. Force-close OSRS.
  2. Uninstall it (iOS: long-press → Remove App → Delete App; Android: Settings → Apps → OSRS → Uninstall, or long-press → Uninstall).
  3. Reboot the phone — this clears lingering OS-level state.
  4. Reinstall from the official store (App Store or Google Play), not a third-party APK.
  5. Log in with your Jagex account and let the cache rebuild on Wi-Fi.

Your account, bank, and membership are stored on Jagex servers, not the app, so a reinstall never costs you progress. If you cannot log back in after reinstall, the issue is account-side — see our can't login guide.

② Is it the account or the device?

A quick way to tell: log into your Jagex account on a different phone, or on the desktop client. If OSRS runs fine elsewhere, the problem is your device (cache, OS, or hardware). If it crashes on every device at the same moment (for example, the same shop action), it is account or server side and Jagex needs to look. Also check the OSRS Mobile status page and the official Jagex channels for outage notices before assuming it is your phone.

③ Contacting Jagex support the right way

When you do contact support, give them the reproducible sentence and your device details so they can act fast:

  • Device + OS: e.g., "iPhone 13, iOS 18.4" or "Samsung Galaxy S22, Android 14."
  • App version: from the store page (e.g., 1.0.0.x).
  • Exact trigger: "crashes when I tap Bonds on the membership tab."
  • What you tried: cache clear, reinstall, network reset.
  • Crash ID if shown: some builds display a crash reference — include it.

Submit through the official Jagex Support site, never through social DMs. If the crash is tied to lag or FPS drops rather than a hard close, the lag & FPS drop guide is the better starting point.

8. ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Why does OSRS Mobile crash only when I open the bonds shop?

That is a store-handshake failure, not a game crash. Opening the bonds/membership shop starts an in-app purchase session with Apple or Google. A stale store login, an expired payment method, or a flaky network makes that handshake throw an uncaught exception and the OS closes the app. Fix the store account (Section 2), not the game. Buying bonds on the website works around it but does not fix the app.

Will clearing the cache delete my account or bank?

No. On Android, "Clear Cache" only removes temporary game files and keeps your login. On iOS there is no cache button, but offloading the app preserves your data too. Your bank, membership, and progress live on Jagex's servers, so a cache clear or reinstall never removes them. Only "Clear Storage / Clear Data" on Android removes the local login, and even that just makes you sign in again.

Why do I get a black screen when I return to the app?

The operating system terminated OSRS in the background to free memory. iOS and Android both do this, and OSRS is memory-hungry so it is an early victim. The fix is to stop the OS from killing it: enable Background App Refresh and disable Offload Unused Apps on iOS; disable battery optimization and Adaptive Battery on Android (Section 4). After that, returning resumes your session instead of showing black.

Is the loading-screen freeze a corrupted cache?

In almost every case, yes. The loading bar builds the local game cache; if one chunk is half-downloaded or corrupt, the loader loops or crashes. Clear the cache (Android) or offload-and-reinstall (iOS), switch to a stable Wi-Fi connection, and let the first load finish with the screen on. Keeping at least 1.5 GB free storage prevents the partial writes that cause the freeze.

Does a full reinstall fix everything?

A clean reinstall fixes the vast majority of crashes because it removes a corrupt app binary and forces a fresh cache. Reboot after uninstalling to clear OS state. It will not fix a store-handshake problem caused by an expired payment method, and it will not fix a server-side outage — but it is the right step when cache clears and settings changes do not help.

My Android phone says "OSRS keeps stopping." What now?

That is Android's crash dialog for an unhandled exception. Tap "App info" → Storage → Clear Cache, then Clear Storage if it continues. Next, disable battery optimization for OSRS and turn off Adaptive Battery. If it still stops, uninstall, reboot, and reinstall from Google Play. The full routine is in our Android black screen & cache guide.

Could this be my GPU or drivers?

On iOS, almost never — the GPU stack is locked down. On Android, a white flash or graphical artifact before the crash points to a GPU driver issue, more common on cheaper or older devices with outdated drivers. Try forcing GPU rendering off in Developer Options and lowering in-game graphics settings. Heat can also trigger GPU throttling crashes; the overheating guide covers that.

Should I buy bonds through the website instead?

You can, and it is safe — website bond purchases go to the same Jagex account. But it only avoids the symptom; the app store crash remains until you fix the store handshake (valid payment method, signed-in store account, updated Play Services or App Store). If you only ever buy on the website, you may never need the app store fix, but most players want the in-app convenience working.

Why does it crash only after a game update?

You are likely running an old app version against new server content. Jagex ships app updates alongside game updates; if your phone has auto-update off or is behind, the old app chokes on new assets. Update the app the same day a game update drops, and make sure your OS is reasonably current. The loading freeze after an update is the same corrupt-cache pattern described in Section 3.

How do I know if it is a Jagex server outage?

If OSRS crashes identically on every device and every network at the same action, or if the login itself fails for everyone, it is likely server or account side. Check the official OSRS Mobile status page and Jagex channels for outage posts before spending an hour on your phone. If other players are fine and only your device crashes, the problem is local — work Sections 4 through 7.
