1. Why an Organized Bank Saves You Real Hours

A messy bank is a silent GP drain. The three costs nobody counts:

  • Re-buying lost gear. You know you own a seers ring, but you cannot find it, so you buy another. That is pure GP loss.
  • Raid and boss trip delays. A 10-second scramble to find your blowpipe loadout becomes a missed raid spot or a wasted death.
  • Clue scroll and quest stalls. Needing one item and hunting three tabs for it breaks your flow and your focus.

Bank organization is the only QoL improvement in OSRS that is completely free, permanent and account-wide. It takes one evening to set up and pays back every single session after.

💡 The 60-Second Rule

If you cannot find any item in your bank within 60 seconds, your system is the problem — not your memory. This guide gives you a system that makes every item findable in under 10 seconds.

2. The 9-Tab Framework — A Layout That Works

Your bank supports up to 9 tabs (the main tab plus 8 you add yourself). The proven layout assigns each tab one job and never mixes them:

TabJobWhat lives here
1 — MainHot items you use dailyTeleport jewellery, cash stack, food for this session's content
2 — GearEquipment by slotMelee / ranged / magic gear, helmets to boots, organised slot-by-slot
3 — SuppliesCombat consumablesPotions, food, prayer potions, bolts/arrows, ammo
4 — Runes & TeleportsMagic stackAll runes, staves, teleport tablets, jewellery to charge
5 — SkillingTools + materialsPickaxe/axe/hatchet, ore, logs, herbs, seeds, farming gear
6 — Loot To SellYour profit pileBoss/slayer drops waiting for a GE run — clear it weekly
7 — Quest & UntradeablesProgress itemsQuest items, diary gear, graceful, fire/infernal capes, defenders
8 — Clues & PvM SetsActivity kitsClue-stash items, full PvM loadouts (see Section 5)
9 — Junk / MiscDecision pileEverything unclassified — review monthly, sell or drop what you never use

How to physically build it: open your bank, drag one item out to create a new tab slot at the top-right, rename the tab if you use RuneLite (right-click the tab icon), and stack related items together. Tab order matters more than perfection — the goal is that the item you need is always in the same place.

3. RuneLite Bank Tags — Unlimited Virtual Tabs

Real tabs have one hard limit: an item lives in exactly one tab. RuneLite's Bank Tags plugin removes that limit — it lets you tag any item with any number of labels and view each label as its own mini-tab inside the bank interface.

🏷️ How to Set Up Bank Tags

  • Enable the plugin: RuneLite sidebar → Plugin Hub/Settings → search "Bank Tags" → enable (it is a default RuneLite plugin — no download needed)
  • Tag items: while a bank tab is open, right-click any item → "Tag tab" (or use the tag editor) and type a name like ToA or Clue Med
  • View a tag: a new tab appears at the top of the bank showing every item carrying that tag
  • One item, many tags: your Dragon Warhammer can sit in Gear, Bossing and Wildy simultaneously

Bank Tags shine for content that shares items: your Vorkath setup uses gear that also appears in your ToA setup. With tags you never duplicate or move items — you just view the same stack through a different filter. Combined with Bank Tag Layouts (a separate RuneLite plugin), you can even arrange tagged items into a fixed grid that mimics your in-game inventory — so withdrawing your whole ToA loadout is one click.

💡 Tag by activity, not by item type

Real tabs organise by "what the item is". Tags organise by "what I do with it". Use tags for activities (Inferno, ToB, Farm run) and keep real tabs for broad categories — the two systems cover each other perfectly.

4. Placeholders & One-Click Re-Supply

Bank placeholders lock an item's slot so that when the stack runs out, the greyed-out slot stays in place instead of collapsing. This is the backbone of fast re-supply:

  • Enable: Options tab in the bank interface → toggle Bank placeholders on
  • Lock a slot: when the last item in a stack is removed (with placeholders on), the slot stays greyed out forever
  • Why it matters: your gear and potions never "jump" to a different slot, so muscle memory works — you withdraw the same position every time
  • Muscle-memory banking: with a fixed layout you can gear for Vorkath in under 10 seconds without even reading item names

Add this habit on top: after every boss trip, deposit-all, then withdraw-all for the next trip. The bank sorts itself because your slots never move.

5. Build PvM Set Tabs — Vorkath, ToA, Inferno

The single biggest time-saver for active players is a ready-made set for each piece of content you repeat. Here is the exact recipe:

🎒 Example: Vorkath Set Tag

  • Gear: DHCB or dragon crossbow + anti-dragon shield, Void/elite void or blessed d'hide, Pegasian boots, archer ring (i)
  • Consumables: 3× super antifire, 1× ranging potion, 2× prayer potion, food stack
  • Teleport: POH portal / lunar isle bank tele or a Drakan's medallion
  • Extras: rune pouch with crumble undead + house tabs

Build steps: ① open Bank Tags and create a tag named Vorkath; ② tag every item the trip needs; ③ withdraw everything tagged to confirm you have a full loadout; ④ save it as a layout with Bank Tag Layouts if you want one-click withdrawal in inventory order.

Do the same for any content you grind: Zulrah, ToA, CoX/ToB, GWD, Slayer tasks and Inferno attempts. The upfront 15 minutes per activity removes 100% of the "what did I bring last time" guesswork.

6. Common Bank Problems & Fixes

ProblemFix
"I can never find X"Give X a permanent home (tag + tab) and never let it migrate — placeholders enforce this
"My bank is completely full"Member banks hold roughly 800+ slots; full means clutter, not capacity — sell the Loot To Sell tab and junk tab first
"I keep re-buying gear I own"Search your bank by name before every purchase (bank search icon), and keep gear slots visible in the Gear tab

7. Sample Banks by Account Type

① Main / General PvM account

Follow the 9-tab framework as-is; spend extra effort on the PvM Set tag (Section 5) since mains cycle content fastest. Keep Loot To Sell slim and hit the GE weekly.

② Skiller / non-combat account

Your Skilling tab becomes the star. Sub-organise by skill: mining/smithing, woodcutting/fletching, farming/herblore, fishing/cooking. Quest & diary items matter most — keep the diary gear together so runs never stall.

③ Ironman account

Ironmen hoard by nature — the Junk tab is your discipline tool. Keep a strict "sell or drop to stay lean" monthly sweep, and use tags for daily content (birdhouse run, farm run, seaweed, stardust). Placeholders are non-negotiable for ironmen because every item is earned, not bought.

④ Boss-focused account

PvM Set tags are your entire bank philosophy. One tag per boss or raid, gear shared across tags, and a Supplies tab sized for heavy trips. You should be able to go from "bank open" to "teleporting to the boss" in under 15 seconds.

8. Bank Organization FAQs

Q: How many tabs can I have in my OSRS bank?

Up to 9 tabs in total — the main tab plus 8 that you create by dragging an item to the tab bar. RuneLite Bank Tags remove the limit virtually, letting you have as many named tag-views as you want.

Q: Are RuneLite Bank Tags legal / allowed?

Yes — Bank Tags is an official RuneLite plugin that only changes how the bank interface displays your items. It does not automate gameplay, so it is fully compliant with Jagex rules.

Q: What is the fastest way to organize a full bank?

Do it in three passes: ① sell or drop everything you never use (the 60-day rule); ② build the 9 tabs with broad categories; ③ enable placeholders and add Bank Tags for the content you repeat. One evening for a bank that stays clean for months.

Q: How do I lock a bank slot in OSRS?

Enable Bank placeholders in the bank Options tab. With it on, when the final item of a stack is withdrawn, a grey placeholder stays in that slot permanently — your layout never collapses.

Q: Where do quest items go?

Keep them in a dedicated Quest & Untradeables tab. Better: store quest gear in your POH costume room and clue items in STASH units — both free up real bank space.

Q: Can I use Bank Tags on mobile?

Bank Tags are a RuneLite desktop feature. Mobile OSRS has its own basic bank tabs (up to 9) but no tag system. Organise your base tabs for desktop and mobile compatibility — tags are a bonus layer on desktop only.

Q: What should I do with items worth less than 1K GP each?

Drop, alch or sell them in bulk. Low-value clutter is the number one cause of bank bloat — a 500 GP seed stack that sits for months is dead weight in a slot that could hold a real drop.

Q: How often should I reorganize?

With placeholders and a fixed tab framework, you should only need a 10-minute tidy once a month: clear the Loot To Sell tab, review Junk, and prune anything you no longer use. If you are reorganizing more often than that, your framework is too loose — tighten the tab jobs.

Q: Does an organized bank actually help me make GP?

Indirectly, yes. Faster banking means more trips per hour, fewer deaths from rushing, no re-buying lost items, and a clear Loot To Sell tab that actually gets sold instead of hoarded. Players routinely find 5-20M GP of forgotten sellable loot during a first cleanup.

Q: What is the best bank preset for bossing?

A per-boss tag with its full loadout (gear + potions + food + teleport), saved as a Bank Tag Layout for one-click withdrawal in inventory order. Build one per boss you grind and banking becomes a non-event.
