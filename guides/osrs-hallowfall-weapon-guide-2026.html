1. 🌟 Why Hallowfall Matters in 2026

Most melee weapons are built for one target. The Hallowfall is built for three. Its signature trait is a cleave that lands on up to two additional enemies standing in front of you, and that single property makes it the best melee weapon in the game whenever your foes are lined up in your facing arc. That is a narrow lane on paper, but in practice multi-target melee content is far more common than players expect.

The reason it matters this year specifically is the Wyrmscraig release. The Mad Angel arrives as a mid-game teacher boss, and she drops the Hallowfall as her unique. Because the boss is approachable, the weapon is obtainable without an endgame grind, which puts a genuinely best-in-slot multi-target tool within reach of the mid-game player for the first time in a long while.

💡 The Multi-Target Meta Is Quietly Huge

Players think of single-target DPS first, but group Slayer tasks, mob-heavy boss adds, and certain raid encounters all reward cleave. The Hallowfall turns those moments from "I wish I had AOE" into "this is exactly what I trained for." If you do any group content, this weapon deserves a slot in your bank.

2. 🏰 How to Obtain Hallowfall from the Mad Angel

The Hallowfall is the headline unique drop from the Mad Angel, the golem boss at the top of the Ardeaglais cathedral on Wyrmscraig. She arrived with the Wyrmscraig content on July 29, 2026, and the repeatable version unlocks after you complete the Fallen From Grace questline on the island.

📜 What You Need Before the Farm

  • Fallen From Grace completed: the repeatable Mad Angel fight unlocks only after this Wyrmscraig quest.
  • 62 Sailing: required for the quest, which is why Sailing players reach her naturally.
  • 75 Attack: the Hallowfall requires 75 Attack to wield.
  • 70 Strength: recommended so the cleave's splash damage is meaningful.
  • A golembane weapon for the farm: the Mad Angel is weak to golembane, so a granite hammer carries the fight.

Because she is a mid-level boss, the farm itself is cheap and repeatable. The bottleneck is the rare drop rate, not the difficulty, so the realistic plan is many short trips rather than one heroic push. Bank often, keep a teleport method, and use the Ardeaglais teleport drop to compress your kill-to-kill time once you have one.

3. ⚙️ Stats & the Multi-Target Cleave Mechanic

The Hallowfall is a two-handed sword. Its headline mechanic is that each standard hit also strikes up to two additional enemies positioned in the front-facing arc in front of you, dealing a portion of your max hit to each. The more enemies you line up, the more your single swing is worth.

How the Cleave Actually Behaves

The cleave triggers on your normal attacks, not just on a special, which is what makes it so spammable. You do not burn spec energy to splash — you simply keep hitting the front target and the splash lands automatically on whatever is beside it in your arc. Positioning is everything: if the extra enemies are behind you or to your side, the cleave misses them.

📊 Cleave Behaviour at a Glance

  • Trigger: standard melee hits, not a special attack.
  • Targets: up to two extra enemies in a front-facing arc.
  • Damage: a portion of your max hit per splashed enemy.
  • Scaling: more Strength and offensive gear raise both the main hit and the splash.
  • Best at three: with three stacked targets you are dealing triple effective damage per swing.

The discipline here is footwork. Because the cleave is directional, you must keep the pack in front of you. That means kiting mobs into a line, using walls or corners to funnel them, and resisting the urge to spin toward a stray add that would break your arc. Good Hallowfall players think like a cone, not like a point.

💡 Verify the Splash Percentage In-Game

The exact fraction of your max hit that splashes can shift with balance passes. Check the weapon tooltip and watch the combat log the first time you use it so you are building rotations on the real number rather than a remembered one. If a patch changed it, the log is where you will see it first.

4. 🎯 Best Scenarios: Group Slayer & Raids

The Hallowfall lives or dies by enemy count. Here is where it is unambiguously the best melee weapon in the game, and where you should always have it equipped.

🔥 Where Hallowfall Is King

  • Group Slayer tasks: any task where your team stacks mobs in front of one player.
  • Add-heavy boss phases: encounters that spawn waves you can funnel into your arc.
  • Corners and chokes: map geometry that forces enemies into a line.
  • Ironman AOE grinds: self-sufficient multi-kill farming without a team's help.

On a single target the Hallowfall is merely good — it no longer has the cleave advantage, so a purpose-built single-target weapon will out-DPS it. The skill is knowing when to swap. Bring the Hallowfall out the moment three enemies are in your arc, and swap back to a single-target weapon the moment the pack thins.

5. ⚔️ Hallowfall vs Same-Tier Melee Weapons

The fair comparison is not "is the Hallowfall the best melee weapon overall" — it is "is it the best when three targets are stacked." In that lane, nothing beats it. Against single targets, the picture is different, and understanding both keeps you from misusing it.

Vs Single-Target Power Weapons

Weapons built for one target — high-accuracy stab or slash options, or the Soulreaper axe's ramping burst — will out-DPS the Hallowfall on a lone enemy because they invest all their power into that one hit. The Hallowfall splits its identity across the cleave, so on a single target it is giving up some of its potential.

Vs Other Cleave or AOE Options

The Hallowfall's advantage over other multi-target melee tools is that its cleave is on the standard hit, not gated behind a special. That means sustained AOE rather than burst AOE, which is stronger over a long pack fight. Other options that only splash on spec lose DPS between specials; the Hallowfall does not.

📊 When to Reach for It

  • Three stacked enemies: Hallowfall, no contest.
  • Two enemies: still strong, often worth it.
  • One enemy: swap to a single-target weapon.
  • Short burst fight: a burst weapon may edge it out even on packs.

For the full weapon tier list beyond the Hallowfall, our best-in-slot gear guide ranks every melee option by role, and our Soulreaper axe guide shows the burst alternative you will be swapping against on single-target phases.

💡 Build a Swap, Not a Replacement

Treat the Hallowfall as one weapon in a rotation. The strongest players carry it for packs and a single-target weapon for bosses, swapping the instant the enemy count changes. Owning both is what makes you fast, not owning the flashiest one.

6. 🛡️ Build & Gear Recommendations

Because the Hallowfall is two-handed, your build philosophy is "maximise the front hit and keep the pack in your arc." You sacrifice an off-hand bonus, so your strength and accuracy investment has to be higher to compensate.

⚙️ Suggested Loadout

  • Weapon: Hallowfall (two-handed, so no off-hand).
  • Prayer: Piety or its equivalent for the Strength and accuracy boost that raises both main hit and splash.
  • Food: standard — the cleave kills packs so fast you usually need less than a single-target grind.
  • Potions: super combat or divine super combat to push max hit as high as possible.
  • Positioning: use terrain to funnel mobs; a defender or shield swap is only for emergency defence.

The single biggest upgrade to your Hallowfall DPS is not a gear slot — it is positioning practice. A player who perfectly lines up three enemies gets triple value from every swing, while a player with better gear but sloppy arcs gets single-target value. Drill the footwork and the weapon pays you back immediately.

7. 💰 Is It Worth It? Value Assessment

The Hallowfall is a rare drop from an approachable boss, which makes its value proposition unusual: the time cost is modest because the fight is easy, but the reward is genuinely best-in-slot in its lane. That is about as good a value as a weapon farm gets.

For any player who does group Slayer, mob-heavy bosses, or ironman AOE grinds, the Hallowfall is close to mandatory. It is not a luxury — it is the tool that makes those activities faster and safer. For a pure solo single-target bosser, it is skippable, because its cleave never gets to fire.

✅ Farm It If…

  • You do any group or multi-target content regularly.
  • You are already on Wyrmscraig for the Mad Angel or Sailing.
  • You run an ironman and value self-sufficient AOE farming.
  • You enjoy cheap, repeatable mid-game boss farms.

Skip it only if every boss you fight is a single target and you never group up. Otherwise the farm is cheap enough, and the payoff useful enough, that it belongs in your bank. Even as a tradeable rare, its utility in its lane keeps it relevant long after the initial hype fades.

💡 Long-Term Position

Multi-target melee content is not going away — if anything, group content keeps growing. A weapon that is best-in-slot at three stacked targets is a safe long-term hold, because that niche does not get obsoleted by single-target DPS creep.

8. ❓ Hallowfall FAQs

Q: Where does the Hallowfall drop from?

It is the unique drop from the Mad Angel boss on Wyrmscraig, added with the Wyrmscraig content on July 29, 2026. The repeatable fight unlocks after you complete the Fallen From Grace quest.

Q: What are the requirements to wield it?

It requires 75 Attack. 70 Strength is recommended so the cleave's splash damage is meaningful, and higher Strength raises both the main hit and the splash.

Q: How does the cleave work?

Each standard hit also strikes up to two extra enemies in a front-facing arc for a portion of your max hit. It triggers on normal attacks, not a special, so it is sustained AOE rather than burst AOE.

Q: Is the Hallowfall the best melee weapon in the game?

At three stacked targets, yes — no other melee weapon cleaves as efficiently because the splash is on the standard hit. On a single target it is merely good and a single-target weapon will out-DPS it.

Q: Does the cleave need a special attack?

No. The cleave is tied to your standard melee hits, which is exactly what makes it stronger than AOE options that only splash on spec. You keep splashing every swing.

Q: What is the best content for the Hallowfall?

Group Slayer tasks, add-heavy boss phases, corner-funneled mobs, and ironman AOE grinds. Anything where you can line up three enemies in your facing arc.

Q: How should I position for the cleave?

Keep the pack in front of you using walls, corners, and kiting so all targets sit in your arc. Spinning toward a stray add breaks the arc and wastes the splash.

Q: Is it worth farming if I mostly solo?

Only if you do multi-target solo content like ironman AOE grinds. If every boss you fight is a single target, the cleave never fires and a single-target weapon serves you better.

Q: Should I buy it or farm it?

The Mad Angel is an easy mid-game boss, so farming is cheap and repeatable. Farm it if you are already on Wyrmscraig; buy it only if you want it immediately and never plan to do the fight.

Q: Does the splash scale with my gear?

Yes. Anything that raises your max hit — Strength, potions, Piety, offensive gear — also raises the cleave splash, because the splash is a portion of your max hit per extra enemy.
