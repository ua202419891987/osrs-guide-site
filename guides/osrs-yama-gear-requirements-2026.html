OSRS Yama Gear Requirements 2026 — Recommended Setup & Budget Loadouts

Updated August 19, 2026  |  15 min read  |  By Yanchun Wu
🆕 NEW

Yama is one of Old School RuneScape's most demanding endgame bosses. This guide gives you the exact gear thresholds to fight each contract tier — from a sub-100M budget kit to a maxed BiS loadout — plus the stats, demonbane switches, prayer, and supplies you need before the first attempt.

🗡️ Boss Gear Series · Advanced · 24 min read · Updated for 2026
🛡️ Level: 90+

Yama is one of Old School RuneScape's most demanding endgame bosses, and walking in under-geared is the fastest way to burn through supplies and learn nothing. The good news: you do not need max BiS to fight Yama successfully in 2026. You need the right tier of gear for the contract difficulty you commit to, solid combat stats, and the correct demonbane switches. This guide lays out the exact gear thresholds — from a sub-100M budget setup to a maxed endgame loadout — plus the stats, prayer, and supplies you should have before your first attempt. We also compare the three loadout tiers head to head, explain how Yama's contract system scales your gear requirement, and show where a cheap demonbane switch outperforms expensive armour by a wide margin.

If you are still climbing toward endgame, our Gear Upgrade Priority Order shows the cheapest path to the gear below, and our Best-in-Slot Gear Guide 2026 covers the absolute max loadout in full. For the boss mechanics themselves, our Yama Deep Dive breaks the full fight down phase by phase. Pair this page with our Boss Guides hub for the broader PvM picture.

⚡ Quick Verdict — The 30-Second Read

  • 📌 You do not need max BiS to start. A ~45–95M budget melee kit clears the lowest contract tier if your stats are 80+.
  • 📌 Stats beat swag. 90+ in your chosen style plus Rigour/Augury outperform a fancy weapon on an under-leveled account.
  • 📌 A demonbane switch (Arclight or Scorching Bow) is the single cheapest DPS upgrade you can buy for a demon boss.
  • 📌 Match your gear to the contract tier, not to "Yama" in general — a kit that trivializes a low contract can get you killed on a high one.
  • 📌 Prayer scrolls (Rigour/Augury) give more DPS per GP than almost any single armour piece. Buy them before chasing the last BiS slot.
  • 📌 Fund the grind with a sustainable money maker so supply costs never gate your progression.

1. 🚪 What You Need Before Fighting Yama

Yama is approached through a contract system: you commit to a difficulty tier (similar in spirit to recollection/invocation scaling) that adjusts the boss's damage, mechanics, and reward payout. The gear you need scales directly with the contract you pick. A low contract is reachable on a tight budget; a high contract demands near-BiS defence and DPS. Understanding this relationship is the foundation of the entire gear plan below — the question is never "do I have enough gear for Yama," it is "do I have enough gear for the specific contract I am attempting."

Two questions to answer first

  • Which contract tier am I attempting? Be honest. Your first kills should be on the lowest viable tier while you learn the rotation. The rewards scale with difficulty, but so does the punishment for being under-prepared.
  • Can I survive the damage output? Yama hits hard and sustained. If your gear cannot keep you alive for the full fight, no amount of DPS matters — you will simply run out of brews before the kill.

How contract tiers affect your gear threshold

Each step up in contract difficulty increases Yama's damage output and adds or accelerates mechanics. Roughly speaking: the lowest tier is clearable on a budget melee kit with 80 combat stats; a mid tier wants a proper weapon (Bowfa or Scythe) and 90s; the top tier expects near-BiS defence, a mega-rare weapon, and tight prayer management. Match your gear to the tier rather than guessing — a kit that trivializes a low contract can get you killed at a high one, because the defensive floor you relied on simply no longer exists at higher damage numbers.

The diagram below shows how the three gear tiers in this guide map onto the contract ladder. Think of it as a staircase: each step up the gear investment unlocks the next one or two contract tiers. You should never try to skip steps — the gap between "budget" and "mid" gear is exactly the gap between a survivable low contract and a survivable mid contract.

Common pre-fight mistakes

  • Skipping the learning run. Watch a kill or attempt a low contract solo before committing supplies to a high one. The phase order matters more than your DPS on the first few attempts.
  • Under-leveled stats. Bringing 75s into a fight designed for 90s wastes the gear you did buy — you cannot hit the max hit or wear the best weapon effectively.
  • No escape plan. Always carry a teleport; a failed learning run should cost supplies, not your whole bank. Our Boss Inventory Prep Guide covers the escape-plan essentials.
  • Over-investing in armour before weapon. A better weapon raises your kill speed and thus your profit per trip far more than the last defence slot. See the priority order below.
💡 Learn on a Low Contract

The single biggest mistake is over-committing to a high contract with mid gear. Start low, learn the mechanics, then scale your contract up as your gear and confidence improve. Supplies saved on a clean low-contract learning run pay for the mid gear you will buy next.

2. 📈 Recommended Combat Stats & Quests

Gear is only half the equation. Before you attempt Yama at any serious contract, aim for these baseline stats. The "minimum" column is what you can scrape by with on the lowest tier; the "comfortable" column is what makes mid and high contracts realistic.

Stat Minimum Comfortable Why
Attack / Strength 80 90+ Unlocks the best melee weapons and max hits.
Ranged 80 90+ Needed for Bowfa / Tbow and demonbane ranged.
Magic 80 90+ Unlocks Sang staff / Shadow and ancients.
Defence 75 90+ Survivability against Yama's sustained hits.
Prayer 70 77+ (Rigour/Augury) Piety/Rigour/Augury are massive DPS and defence boosts.
Hitpoints 80 90+ More effective health = longer trips.

Beyond raw stats, your prayer scrolls matter as much as the numbers. Rigour and Augury each add roughly 25% damage and defence in their style — a larger, cheaper multiplier than almost any single armour upgrade. If you have the stats but not the scrolls, prioritize the scrolls before the last BiS piece. Our 1–99 Prayer Guide covers the fastest route to the levels you need, and Combat Achievements explain which tasks reward the prayer scrolls you want — work toward the relevant tiers on the side while you gear up.

Quest unlocks you should have first

Several unlocks gate the gear in this guide. Before sinking GP, confirm you have (or can get) the relevant quests done:

  • Mage Arena 2 — for the Augury and Rigour scrolls (and the Imbued Saradomin Cape as a free upgrade).
  • The Fremennik Exiles — for the Declaration of Dagannoth / access to relevant gear options.
  • Desert Treasure 2 — for the Ancient Sceptre and access to the magic power spikes used in the max setup.
  • Song of the Elves — for Prifddinas and the best ranged/magic skilling that funds your grind.
  • Dragon Slayer 2 — for the Dragon Hunter Lance option and a free combat milestone.

If you are short on any stat, train it efficiently using our Skill Training guides before sinking GP into gear you cannot yet use. A level 85 account wearing 2B GP of BiS still hits like a level 85 account — the weapon requirement gates the damage, not the price tag.

⚠️ Stats Gate the Weapon, Not the Wallet

The Scythe of Vitur requires 70 Attack, the Bow of Faerdhinen requires 70 Ranged, and Tumeken's Shadow requires 75 Magic plus the Ancient sceptre. If your stats are below those gates, the GP you spent is locked behind XP. Train first, buy second.

3. 💰 Budget Gear Setup (Under ~100M GP)

This setup is built for the lowest contract tier. It will not set speed records, but it will keep you alive and let you learn the fight. Prices are illustrative GE mid values for 2026 and drift with the market — treat them as a planning baseline, not a quote. The guiding principle of a budget kit is simple: maximize survivability and the weapon's max hit while spending almost nothing on slots you can fill for free.

Total: ~95M GP fully built (or ~45M using the cheapest options). For ranged, swap to Blowpipe + Armadyl; for magic, Trident of the Swamp + Ahrim's. This is a "survive and learn" kit — do not attempt high contracts with it. The whole point of the budget tier is to get you cheap kills on a low contract so you can fund the mid tier, not to compete on kill speed.

Ranged and magic budget variants

A pure-melee budget kit is the cheapest path, but if your melee stats are weak and your ranged or magic are higher, lean into the style you already own. A Blowpipe (free if you camp the boss that drops it) with Armadyl pieces (~20M for the body, ~15M legs) and an Anguish necklace (~15M) is a perfectly serviceable low-contract ranged kit for under 60M. On the magic side, a Trident of the Swamp (~7M) with Ahrim's (~12M for the set) and an Occult Necklace (~15M) does similar work. The demonbane weakness (covered below) means your melee or ranged switch does the heavy lifting during demon windows regardless of your primary style.

💡 Free Upgrades First

Before spending a coin, grab every free item: Fighter Torso, Fire Cape, Barrows Gloves, Neitiznot Faceguard, Dragon Defender, Ava's Assembler, and the Mage Arena 2 cape. They are listed in our Early Game Weapon & Armor Tier List, and they cut the real cost of a working Yama kit dramatically.

4. 🛡️ Mid-Tier Gear Setup (100M–500M GP)

Once you have a steady income from Yama and other bosses, this tier lets you push mid contracts comfortably. The priority is a strong weapon plus the defence to not get bursted during the high-damage phases. The mid tier is where Yama stops being a supply sink and becomes a money maker, because your kill speed crosses the threshold where drops and contract rewards outpace your brew cost.

Total: ~500M for a balanced melee-leaning kit (sub-Scythe). Add a Bow of Faerdhinen (~130M) if you prefer ranged, and a Sang staff for the magic phases. The single biggest mid-tier upgrade is the weapon: a Bow of Faerdhinen alone moves you from "budget" to "mid" more than any armour swap, because it raises your max hit and thus your damage per tick across the whole fight. The Avernic Defender is the second priority — its strength bonus is large for a shield slot and it is far cheaper than the armour it complements.

Where to spend first within the mid tier

If you cannot afford the whole mid kit at once, spend in this order: (1) weapon (Bowfa or save for Scythe), (2) Avernic Defender, (3) Ultor Ring, (4) Torva body, (5) Torva legs, (6) Torva helm. Defence slots at the bottom of that list are the least impactful on kill speed and the most impactful on "do I survive" — so they are a close call. The honest answer: buy the weapon and defender first for speed, then the defence for safety, then the ring for the last few percent of DPS. 5. 👑 Max / Endgame Gear Setup (500M+ GP)

For the highest contracts, you want near-absolute BiS. This is the setup our BiS Gear Guide details in full; the highlights below are organized by combat style so you can see exactly what "max" means for Yama.

Melee max

  • Head: Torva Full Helm (or the equivalent top-tier helm).
  • Body / Legs: Torva Platebody + Torva Platelegs.
  • Weapon: Scythe of Vitur for sustained multi-hit phases.
  • Shield: Avernic Defender.
  • Necklace: Amulet of Torture (or the best strength ammy you own).
  • Ring: Ultor Ring.
  • Boots / Gloves: Primordial Boots + Ferocious Gloves.
  • Cape: Infernal Cape.

Ranged max

  • Armour: Masori (f) set.
  • Weapon: Twisted Bow for the high-magic-phase and large hitboxes.
  • Shield: Twisted Buckler.
  • Ring / Boots: Venator Ring + Pegasian Boots.
  • Necklace: Necklace of Anguish.
  • Gloves: Zaryte Vambraces.

Magic max

  • Armour: Ancestral set.
  • Weapon: Tumeken's Shadow (or Sanguinesti Staff if you don't have Shadow).
  • Shield: Elidinis' Ward (f).
  • Ring / Bracelet: Magus Ring + Tormented Bracelet.
  • Boots / Necklace: Eternal Boots + Occult Necklace.

Shared requirements: Rigour + Augury prayers, a demonbane switch (see below), and a spec weapon such as Dragon Claws or a DWH/BGS for the right moments. You do not need all three styles maxed at once — most Yama kills are done in one or two styles with the others as switches. Pick the style you enjoy and max that first; our Yama Defence Stacking guide explains how to layer prayers, armour, and food so the max kit's defence actually shows up as longer trips.

🎯 Max-Kit Purchase Order

  1. Lock in the weapon for your primary style (Scythe / Twisted Bow / Tumeken's Shadow).
  2. Buy the defence set for that style (Torva / Masori / Ancestral).
  3. Add the prayer scrolls (Rigour/Augury) if not already owned.
  4. Fill the ring, boots, and gloves slots.
  5. Build the demonbane switch and a spec weapon.
  6. Only then expand into a second BiS style for switches.

6. 🔥 Demonbane & Key Mechanics

Yama is a demon, which means demonbane weapons deal bonus damage. Bringing the right demonbane switch is one of the highest-impact, lowest-cost upgrades you can make for this fight — and it is the part most under-geared players skip. The bonus applies on the demonbane weapon's hits, so you bring it as a switch for the high-damage windows rather than wearing it the whole fight.

Best demonbane options by style

  • Melee: Arclight (cheap, strong demonbane) is the budget MVP. At the high end, a powered demonbane variant extends the bonus further. Arclight alone is often under 5M and outperforms armour pieces ten times its price during demon windows.
  • Ranged: Scorching Bow (a demonbane-powered bow) gives strong ranged demon damage without a mega-rare. It is the ranged answer to Arclight.
  • Magic: Silverlight-line weapons and certain demonbane staves exist for the niche; for most players the Sang staff remains the practical all-rounder with a demonbane melee/ranged switch on the side.

Why a switch instead of a full demonbane loadout

You usually do not wear demonbane gear for the whole fight — you bring it as a switch for the high-damage windows where the bonus matters most, then return to your BiS DPS gear. This keeps your sustained damage high while still exploiting the demon weakness. The chart below shows the damage gap: a standard BiS hit versus the same hit with a demonbane switch during the vulnerable window. The switch is not a sidegrade — it is a step change in damage during the moments that decide the kill.

⚠️ Don't Over-Specialize

A pure demonbane setup often sacrifices too much defence or sustain. Use demonbane as a targeted switch, not your entire kit, unless you are specifically speed-running a low contract. The goal is a burst of bonus damage during the vulnerable window, then back to your survival gear.

7. 🎒 Supplies, Prayer & Inventory Tips

Gear gets you in the door; supplies keep you alive. For Yama, plan your inventory around the sustained damage profile and the prayer-drain phases. The most common reason a well-geared player fails a contract is not DPS — it is running out of brews because the inventory was built for a different boss.

Prayer and defence

Run Piety / Rigour / Augury (or the protect-prayer equivalent for the phase) the entire fight. The +25% damage and defence from Rigour/Augury is worth more than most gear swaps. If you do not have them yet, prioritize the prayer scrolls before chasing the last bits of BiS armour. Our Yama Phase 3 Prayer Pierce guide explains exactly how the late-fight prayer pressure works and why your prayer bonus gear matters more than your defence stat at that point.

Inventory template

  • Brews and restores in a ~3:1 ratio, adjusted to the contract's damage. Higher contracts want closer to 2:1 because the damage outpaces restore healing.
  • A couple of prayer potions (or a holy wrench + restore mix) to keep prayers up through the drain phases.
  • Your demonbane switch and a spec weapon in the spare weapon slot.
  • Blood runes if maging, plus a teleport out and a food buffer for learning runs.
  • One slot of a cheap food (sharks or better) as a panic heal that does not cost a brew charge.

Need a steady supply income? Our Money Making hub ranks the highest-GP/hr activities to fund your Yama trips, and our Boss Inventory Prep Guide gives a reusable inventory framework you can adapt per contract. The cost of a failed high contract is mostly supplies — fund the grind so a bad run stings instead of ending your progress.

💡 Brews Are Not Food

Brews heal a percentage but lower your stats; restores bring the stats back. Running pure brews without restores means your max hit drops exactly when you need it. Always pair them, and keep one slot of real food for the moment a brew would be wasted.

9. ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What gear do I need to start Yama at all?

A budget melee kit (~45–95M GP) with 80+ combat stats and Piety is enough for the lowest contract tier. Focus on survivability and learning the rotation before scaling up. You do not need a mega-rare or near-BiS to land your first kills — you need the right tier match and the demonbane switch.

Is the Scythe of Vitur required for Yama?

No. A Bow of Faerdhinen (~130M) or even Osmumten's Fang works for mid contracts. The Scythe helps at high contracts against large hitboxes and sustained multi-hits, but it is not a hard requirement to start. Save the 1B+ for the Scythe until the mid tier is already profitable for you.

Do I need a demonbane weapon?

Strongly recommended. Yama is a demon, so Arclight (melee) or a Scorching Bow (ranged) as a switch adds meaningful damage during key windows. It is one of the cheapest high-impact additions in the whole fight — often under 5M for an Arclight — and it outperforms armour pieces many times its price.

Which combat style is best for Yama?

It depends on your strongest BiS and the contract. Most players run melee or ranged as primary with a magic switch. Pick the style whose BiS you already own rather than forcing a style you haven't built, because the gear gap between styles is far larger than the stylistic DPS difference at the same investment level.

How much should I budget before my first attempt?

Realistically ~50M GP for a working budget kit plus a few million in supplies. You can earn the rest from Yama itself once you learn the fight on a low contract. Do not front-load 500M into a mid kit before you have proven you can survive the low contract — supplies are cheaper than regret.

What stats are most important if I can only train a few?

Pump your primary offensive style (Attack/Strength, Ranged, or Magic) to 90+, then Defence and Prayer to 77 for Rigour/Augury. Hitpoints to 90+ extends trips. The weapon stat gate is the hard wall — 90 in your style unlocks the best weapons and max hits, which matters more than any single defence slot.

Should I buy armour or the weapon first?

Weapon first, almost always. A better weapon raises your kill speed (and thus profit per trip) across every phase, while a defence slot mostly buys survivability you can compensate for with brews and practice. Our gear upgrade priority guide ranks each slot by GP-per-DPS so you spend in the right order.

Can I do Yama as a low-level ironman or with free gear only?

You can attempt the lowest contract with fully free gear (Fighter Torso, Fire Cape, Barrows Gloves, Neitiznot, Dragon Defender, Ava's) plus a whip and an Arclight, provided your stats are 80+. It will be slow and supply-hungry, but it is a legitimate on-ramp. The budget kit in Section 3 is exactly this path with a few paid upgrades to make it reliable.

Why does my max-hit feel low even with good gear?

Three usual culprits: missing prayer scrolls (Rigour/Augury add 25% damage), an under-leveled offensive stat (the weapon's max hit scales with your level), or no demonbane switch during demon windows. Fix those before buying more armour — they are cheaper and higher-impact than the next BiS piece.

How do I know which contract tier my gear can handle?

Start one tier below what you think you can do, run it clean three times, then step up. If you are burning brews faster than you can restore, or the fight ends in a teleport more than once in three attempts, drop a tier. 10. 🧭 Final Tips

  • Start on a low contract. Learn the mechanics with cheap gear before risking BiS.
  • Stats > swag. 90+ in your chosen style and Rigour/Augury outperform a fancy weapon on an under-leveled account.
  • Bring a demonbane switch. Arclight or Scorching Bow is the cheapest DPS you'll ever buy here.
  • Free gear first. Torso, Fire Cape, Barrows Gloves, Neitiznot, Ava's — grab them all before spending.
  • Don't buy Scythe before prayer scrolls. Augury/Rigour give more DPS per GP than almost any armour piece.
  • Weapon before armour. The weapon raises kill speed every phase; a defence slot mostly buys survivability you can brew around.
  • Match gear to contract. A kit that trivializes a low contract can get you killed on a high one.
  • Fund the grind. Use our money-making guides so supply costs never gate your progress.
  • Prep the inventory per contract. Brew:restore ratio and prayer gear change with the tier — don't reuse a boss-agnostic setup.
