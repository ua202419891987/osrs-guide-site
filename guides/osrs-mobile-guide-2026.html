📱 Playing OSRS on Mobile in 2026

Old School RuneScape runs natively on both iOS and Android through Jagex's official mobile client — the same live game, the same economy, the same saves as the desktop version. You don't manage a separate account; log in with your normal credentials and your bank, quests, and Grand Exchange offers follow you everywhere. In 2026 the mobile client is mature enough to handle bossing, raids, and even high-level PvM with the right settings, which is why so many players now split their grind between a phone and a laptop.

The catch is that a touchscreen is not a keyboard. Actions that are instant on desktop — weapon swaps, prayer flicks, inventory shuffling — take an extra tap on mobile, and that small delay is the difference between a clean kill and a death. The guides below are grouped by what you actually want to do on the go: get set up, learn the controls, tune performance, make some gold during a commute, or fix the inevitable crash. ❓ Mobile FAQ

Is mobile progress separate from my PC account?

No. The mobile app logs into the exact same character as the desktop client. Anything you do on your phone is reflected instantly when you next open OSRS on a computer, and vice versa. There is one shared bank, one Grand Exchange, and one quest log.

Can I really do bosses and raids on a phone?

Yes, with caveats. Zulrah, Vorkath, and even Chambers of Xeric are doable on mobile once you learn tap-hold and Menu Entry Swapper. The limiting factor is reaction speed on prayer switches — if a fight demands sub-second flicking, a keyboard will always be safer. Treat mobile as your farming, skilling, and steady-PvM device, and save tight mechanic fights for desktop.

Why does my phone overheat or crash during long sessions?

OSRS is light, but screen-on time, high brightness, and a warm room add up. Drop brightness to ~50%, enable the battery saver preset in Best Mobile Settings, close background apps, and take a 5-minute break every hour. If it still crashes, the troubleshooting guides above cover Android and iOS fixes specifically.

Does membership bought on mobile work everywhere?

Yes. A membership bought through the App Store or Google Play unlocks members content on every platform, including the desktop client. The only quirk is billing — subscriptions renew through the store you bought them on, not through the Jagex website. See the Mobile Membership guide for the step-by-step.
