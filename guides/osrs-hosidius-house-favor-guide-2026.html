1. What Hosidius Favour Was, and Why It Is Gone

Hosidius is one of the five cities of Great Kourend, added on 7 January 2016. It is the kingdom's agricultural heartland — it grows the food that feeds both the citizens of Kourend and the Shayzien Army, which is why it holds more land than any other house.

For eight years, progress in each Kourend city was tracked by a favour percentage — a reputation bar shown on the purple star icon inside your Quest List. You started at 0% with every house and ground toward 100%, unlocking content in stages.

That system is gone. Kourend Favour was removed on 10 January 2024, alongside the Children of the Sun quest, after the Varlamore Activities & Kourend Changes poll passed. Jagex's reasoning was that favour had become a mandatory, unpopular runway: you could not walk into the Farming Guild or the Woodcutting Guild and train, you first had to do hours of unrelated busywork.

When favour was removed, three things happened at once:

  • Some activities were deleted. Ploughing fields and the fertiliser hand-in to the Clerk no longer grant favour, because there is no favour to grant.
  • Some gates vanished. The Farming Guild, Woodcutting Guild, Tithe Farm, Vinery and the spirit tree patch near the Forthos Ruin are now gated only by their own skill level.
  • Some rewards moved to the Kourend & Kebos Diary. The Hosidius kitchen ranges and the disease-free farming patches now need the easy Kourend & Kebos Diary.

Any Hosidius favour guide written in 2023 or earlier is obsolete on the mechanics and still useful as a reward checklist — the rewards survived, only the gates changed.

2. The Real Historical Favour Thresholds

Almost every recycled Hosidius favour article lists the same clean ladder: 0%, 10%, 30%, 60%, 100%. That ladder never existed. The real unlock table had seven steps, and none of the middle ones were round numbers.

FavourWhat It UnlockedExtra Requirement
15%Steal from the Hosidius fruit stall in the farmer's market25 Thieving
35%Plant a spirit sapling in the spirit tree patch south-east of the Forthos Ruin83 Farming
50%The allotment, flower and herb patches south of Hosidius become permanently disease-freeNone
60%Enter the Farming Guild45 Farming
65%Plant grape seeds in the Vinery36 Farming
75%Enter the Woodcutting Guild60 Woodcutting
100%Play the Tithe Farm minigame34 Farming
100%Use the Hosidius kitchen ranges (additive 5% cooking success, 10% with the elite diary)None

Two details matter. First, 100% was the only tier requiring a full bar, and it held back the two things players wanted most: the Tithe Farm and the kitchen ranges. Second, 100% never meant 100% honest work — The Depths of Despair awarded a favour certificate worth a flat 10%, so the practical target was 90% plus one quest. That shortcut, plus a range sitting about six tiles from a bank chest with a flat bonus to your cooking success roll, is why Hosidius was the most-researched of the five houses.

3. How Favour Used to Be Earned

For the record, and for anyone cross-referencing an archived guide, these were the five Hosidius favour methods and their verified numbers.

Unlocked atMethodFavour Gained
0%Pushing a plough in the fields south of town0.2% per tile, roughly a 12% chance per tile
5%Handing sulphurous fertiliser to the Clerk0.1% per bucket, 2 Farming XP per bucket made
20%The Depths of Despair quest10% once, via certificate
45%Cooking and serving food in the Mess0.6% per dish served, 163 to 377 Cooking XP
65%Planting grape seeds in the Vinery0.8% per seed, 31.5 Farming XP on planting

Ploughing was the only 0% method and it was miserable: one tile at a time, a 12% chance per tile for 0.2% favour, and a plough that broke constantly and needed a hammer. Sulphurous fertiliser was the AFK shortcut — the Clerk pulled from your bank as well as your inventory, and from 5% you needed 950 buckets for a clean 100%, or 850 buckets to reach 90% plus one Depths of Despair completion for the last 10%.

From 45%, the Mess gave a flat 0.6% favour per dish with all ingredients provided. From 65%, planting grape seeds across all twelve Vinery patches gave 9.6% favour and at least 10,278 Farming XP per round. That table collapses a long grind into a short one: the two rewards that once demanded a 100% bar now cost a diary tier most accounts finish in an afternoon.

Why the easy Kourend & Kebos Diary is the real quest here

The easy tier is now the load-bearing requirement for Hosidius content, and its rewards are unusually generous:

  • Access to the Hosidius kitchen ranges — an additive 5% increased cooking success rate over standard ranges
  • The Hosidius farming patches south of the church never become diseased — allotment, flower and herb patches included
  • Rada's blessing 1 — three daily Kourend Woodland teleports, plus a 2% chance to catch two fish at once anywhere when equipped
  • An antique lamp worth 2,500 experience in any skill of at least level 30
  • Doubled Xeric's talisman drop rate from lizardmen, from 1/250 to 1/125

The disease-free patch line is the one people overlook. A permanently disease-free herb patch removes the need to babysit a crop, and it makes Hosidius one of the strongest farming regions in the game for a mid-level account. Our achievement diary beginner guide and the diary priority list both rate Kourend's easy tier highly for this reason.

Getting there is easy: fairy ring code AKR drops you at the Vinery, a skills necklace teleports to the Woodcutting Guild, a Xeric's talisman goes to Xeric's Glade, and Kharedst's memoirs reaches Hosidius directly after The Depths of Despair. See our transportation and teleport guide and the Kourend Castle route guide.

5. The Hosidius Kitchen in 2026

The kitchen is a small building in the north-west corner of Hosidius, just east of the Mess. Inside you get two cooking ranges, a bank chest and a water source — the whole reason the room is famous.

FeatureDetail
Entry requirementCompletion of the easy Kourend & Kebos Diary
Base bonusAdditive 5% increased chance of successfully cooking food vs a standard range
With elite diaryAdditive 10% instead of 5%
How the buff worksA flat +12 (easy) or +25 (elite) to the low and high values in the skilling success function, so the 5% is approximate rather than exact
Fastest teleportKharedst's memoirs or Book of the Dead to Lunch by the Lancalliums

Worked example: if a piece of food has an 80% chance to cook on a regular range, the Hosidius range pushes that to roughly 85% before the elite diary and roughly 90% after it. One caveat — if you are cooking food you already cannot burn, the kitchen gives less XP per hour than the Rogues' Den fire. It is a burn-prevention tool, not an XP-maximising one. 6. Tithe Farm in 2026

The Tithe Farm sits in the south-east corner of Hosidius. It used to be sealed behind a 100% favour bar; now it opens at 34 Farming and nothing else.

Take seeds from the table — one tier at a time, up to 10,000 — plant them across the patches, and water each seed three times: before minute 1, before minute 2 and before minute 3. Miss a watering and the plant wilts and dies. After three minutes it is fully grown and can be harvested for fruit, which you deposit into the sacks for Farming XP and points.

Seed TierFarming LevelBonus XP on the 75th Fruit
Golovanova seed341,500
Bologano seed543,500
Logavano seed745,750

Points are awarded at 1 point per 3 plants deposited, plus 2 bonus points for every 100 plants. Higher-tier seeds give more XP for identical effort, so always take the highest tier you can plant. Boosts count, but you must apply the boost before taking the seeds and before planting them.

RewardPointsWhy You Want It
Farmer's outfit set400 totalStrawhat 75, jacket or shirt 150, boro trousers 125, boots 50 — gives bonus Farming XP
Grape seed2 eachThe cheapest reliable Vinery seed source for an Ironman
Bologa's blessing (20)1 per stackConverts grapes into Zamorak's grapes

Every untradeable reward — outfit, farming tools and Auto-weed — costs 1,150 points, about 3,290 fruits if you always deposit in batches of 100. The 25x4 method takes roughly 10.5 hours. The cap is 16,000 points; anything past that still gives XP but no points, so spend before you hoard.

Leaving through the door resets your session entirely, so bank nothing and take nothing with you, though points and XP are kept. Bring your own spade, seed dibber and watering cans — six to eight cans is the usual recommendation.

7. Vinery, Grapes and Bologa's Blessing

The Vinery is a greenhouse in the south-east of Hosidius, and no favour is required — only 36 Farming. It holds twelve vine patches, a bank chest open to anyone, and a fairy ring nearby at code AKR.

DetailValue
Farming level36
Patches12
Items needed per patch1 grape seed, 1 saltpetre, seed dibber, gardening trowel
Growth time35 minutes
DiseaseNever — grape vines cannot become diseased
YieldAlways 5 or more grapes per patch
XP per seed31.5 on planting, 625 on checking the vine, 40 per grape harvested

The Vinery earns a permanent slot in your routine because it never fails: no disease roll, 35-minute growth, and at least 856.5 Farming XP per seed once you add the check-health bonus to a five-grape harvest.

Sulphurous fertiliser is the leftover of the old favour system and still has a job — it raises grape yield by 20%, and Zamorak's grape yield by roughly 60%. Make it by combining saltpetre with regular compost, the same recipe once handed to the Clerk for 0.1% favour each. Bologa's blessing is the upgrade path: holding blessings while harvesting converts each grape into a Zamorak's grape. The Tithe Farm shop sells a stack of 20 for 1 point, but the first purchase needs level 50 Prayer and a one-off 75,000 coin payment to Bologa while wearing a Zamorak-affiliated item. Blessings reduce your grape count per patch, so the trade is fewer but more valuable grapes.

8. Priority Order — What to Actually Do First

If you came here from an old favour guide looking for a grind order, this is the 2026 version of that advice.

OrderActionWhy
1Do the easy Kourend & Kebos Diary tasksUnlocks the kitchen ranges and makes the Hosidius patches disease-free — the two biggest rewards Hosidius offers
2Hit 34 Farming, then try the Tithe FarmFree Farming XP with no seed cost, plus a permanent source of grape seeds and Bologa's blessings
3Hit 36 Farming and start Vinery roundsTwelve disease-free patches on a 35-minute timer, with a bank chest on site
4Hit 45 Farming for the Farming GuildContracts, a closer herb patch and the guild's own disease-free patches
5Finish The Depths of DespairUnlocks Kharedst's memoirs, the fastest teleport into Hosidius
6Chase the elite Kourend & Kebos Diary laterDoubles the kitchen bonus to 10%, and the hard tier adds a 5% chance to save a harvest life at Hosidius and Farming Guild herb patches

The order matters less than it looks, because steps 2 through 4 are all gated by the same skill — you unlock them as you level Farming. The one thing worth doing early and deliberately is the diary, because it costs wall-clock time rather than XP.

What about the other four houses?

This is the part of the old meta players remember most, and the answer is simple: the trade-off is gone. There is no reputation bar to commit to, so the old "which house should I grind first" question has no strategic answer. Prioritise by reward instead: Arceuus for the spellbook, Hosidius for the kitchen and the patches, Lovakengj for the blast mine, Shayzien for the armour, Piscarilius for fishing and construction.

Because so much Hosidius content still ranks in search results, a few wrong ideas keep circulating.

TrapReality
Grinding ploughs for a bar that does not existThe most common mistake. Players follow a 2022 guide, push a plough, and notice nothing going up. Ploughs only give small Crafting and Farming XP drops now.
Buying 950 saltpetre and compost for a favour hand-inThe Clerk who took sulphurous fertiliser for favour is gone. Buy fertiliser only for its current use: a 20% grape yield boost at the Vinery.
Assuming 100% favour still gates the kitchenThe kitchen sits behind the easy Kourend & Kebos Diary, not a favour bar. Many players have put it off for years for no reason.
Reading the fruit stall as a favour rewardIt needs only 25 Thieving now, but the main-square stalls are watched by level 44 guard dogs. Use the stalls inside the building east of the market.
Trusting a 0/10/30/60/100 threshold listThat ladder never existed. The real thresholds were 15, 35, 50, 60, 65, 75 and 100.

The fruit stall is also known for strange fruits, which restore 30% run energy and cure poison and venom, and for golovanova fruit tops, an ingredient in botanical pies that boost Herblore by 4 levels.

⚠️ The one date to remember

Ten January 2024. If a Hosidius guide was published before that date, its favour mechanics are obsolete. Anything published after it that still tells you to grind favour is recycled.

10. Hosidius Favour FAQs

Q: Does Hosidius favour still exist in OSRS?

No. Kourend Favour was removed on 10 January 2024 with the Children of the Sun quest, after the Varlamore Activities & Kourend Changes poll passed. There is no favour interface in your Quest List and no activity that grants it. Every Hosidius reward still exists, gated by skill levels, quests and the Kourend & Kebos Diary instead.

Q: What were the actual Hosidius favour thresholds?

Seven tiers: 15% for the fruit stall, 35% for the spirit tree patch south-east of the Forthos Ruin, 50% to make the Hosidius allotment, flower and herb patches disease-free, 60% for the Farming Guild, 65% for grape planting at the Vinery, 75% for the Woodcutting Guild, and 100% for the Tithe Farm and the kitchen ranges. The widely copied 0/10/30/60/100 list is not the ladder the game used.

Q: How do I use the Hosidius kitchen ranges now?

Complete the easy tier of the Kourend & Kebos Diary. The ranges then give an additive 5% increased chance of successfully cooking food compared with a standard range, rising to 10% once you finish the elite tier. The building is in the north-west corner of Hosidius, just east of the Mess.

Q: Is it worth buying sulphurous fertiliser after the favour removal?

Only for grape yield. Made by combining saltpetre with regular compost, it increases grape yield at the Vinery by 20% and Zamorak's grape yield by roughly 60%. It has no favour use, because there is no favour.

Q: Should I still prioritise one house over the others?

No — that choice no longer exists. Prioritise by reward instead: Kourend & Kebos easy for the Hosidius kitchen and disease-free patches, then Arceuus for the spellbook.
