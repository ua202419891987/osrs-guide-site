🆓 Zero-Cost Quests: The Best Order for New Players (2026)

Updated August 19, 2026  |  15 min read  |  By Yanchun Wu

You do not need millions of GP or maxed stats to build a powerful OSRS account. Some of the highest-value content in the game is completely free — it costs nothing but a little walking. This guide ranks the best zero-cost quests, shows the exact order to do them, and explains the rewards that will carry your account for hundreds of hours.

📅 Updated: August 2026 — Beginner Quest Guide · 14 min read
🛡️ Level: 3+
🆕 NEW

Every new account starts the same way: broke, weak, and unsure what to click first. The fastest way to fix all three problems is questing. Unlike grinding, quests hand you huge XP lumps and permanent unlocks for free. If you are fresh off Tutorial Island, start with our New Player Guide 2026 for the big picture, then follow the optimized path in our Optimal Quest Order once you are ready to go deeper. This page focuses only on the quests that cost you absolutely nothing.

💡 Quick Verdict

Do every quest on this list before you grind a single skill. Waterfall Quest alone gives 27,500 total combat XP with zero requirements. Tree Gnome Village, Fight Arena, and Vampyre Slayer add another ~28,000 Attack XP for free. By the time you finish the free quest list you will have a level 40+ combat account, several teleports, and a handful of quest points — all without spending one GP.

1. 🎯 What "Zero-Cost" Means (and Why It Matters)

Before we list quests, we need to agree on what "zero-cost" actually means in OSRS. The game has a strict free-to-play (F2P) boundary, and even inside members content some quests ask you to bring supplies, teleport, or pay a small fee. This guide treats a quest as zero-cost when you can complete it on a fresh, broke account without buying anything from the Grand Exchange or farming GP first.

① The Definition We Use

A zero-cost quest meets all three tests below:

  • No GP purchase required. If a quest needs a spade, a bucket, or a pint of beer, those items are either given to you during the quest or are common ground-spawn drops you can grab for free.
  • No high stats required. We cap prerequisites at the very low levels a new account reaches naturally — think 10 Fishing, 10 Mining, or a few quest points earned from other free quests.
  • No membership-gated items. Free-to-play quests are preferred; members quests are included only when they ask for nothing but membership itself and a little walking.

This is stricter than most "easy quest" lists you will find, and that is the point. The whole value of this guide is that you can follow it on day one with an empty coin pouch.

② Why Free Quests Beat Grinding

New players often make the same mistake: they pick a skill and click it for hours. Grinding 40 Attack from scratch by training on cows takes roughly 25,000 XP and several hours of repetitive combat. Waterfall Quest gives you 27,500 Attack and Strength XP in about twenty minutes of walking and a couple of puzzle steps. The XP-per-minute difference is not close, and the quest also unlocks the Waterfall dungeon safe spot and a convenient teleport later on.

Quests also hand you things grinders never get: teleport tablets, shortcut unlocks, access to shops, and quest points that gate even bigger quests. Every quest point you earn is a key that opens another door. A brand-new account with 10+ quest points can start Dragon Slayer, the capstone F2P combat quest, far earlier than one that skipped questing.

③ What You Need Before Starting

Almost nothing. To begin you should:

  • Finish Tutorial Island so you have a basic toolkit and can bank.
  • Know how to read the quest journal (click the quest icon, or press the "Quest List" button under the stats tab).
  • Keep a few teleport options open — even the free Lumbridge home teleport saves time.
  • Train a couple of skills to level 10 if you plan to touch Knight's Sword (10 Mining, 10 Smithing) — both are trivial to reach.

That is it. No gold, no gear, no panic. Open the quest list and start clicking.

💡 The "Big Four" Combat Quests

If you remember nothing else, remember these four free combat quests: Waterfall Quest, Tree Gnome Village, Fight Arena, and Vampyre Slayer. Together they give roughly 42,000 Attack XP and 27,500 Strength XP with no requirements. That single push takes a level 3 account to about level 40 Attack and level 39 Strength instantly.

3. 🗺️ Recommended Quest Order (Step-by-Step)

This is the exact path we recommend for a totally fresh account. Follow it in order; each step sets up the next. The whole route can be done in an afternoon and leaves you with a strong, well-rounded character.

① Steps 1–5: The F2P Foundation

  1. Cook's Assistant — 300 Cooking XP, teaches you the quest interface. Takes two minutes.
  2. Sheep Shearer — 150 Crafting XP and 60 GP. Walk to the field west of Lumbridge and shear sheep.
  3. The Restless Ghost — 1,125 Prayer XP. Talk to the monk in the Lumbridge church, then exorcise the ghost in the basement.
  4. Imp Catcher — 875 Magic XP. Collect four beads from imps (they are everywhere) and return to Wizard Mizgog.
  5. Witch's Potion — 325 Magic XP. A short walk near Rimmington. Now your Magic is level 11+ for free.

After these five you have about 2,600 total XP across three skills and a feel for how quests work. You also have the Restless Ghost done, which matters later for members content.

② Steps 6–10: The Members Combat Push

Once you have membership (or if you are already a member), do these in order:

  1. Waterfall Quest — the crown jewel. No requirements, 27,500 combat XP. Teleport to Baxtorian Falls (north of Ardougne) and follow the dungeon.
  2. Tree Gnome Village — 11,450 Attack XP and the Gnome Glider network. Start at the Gnome Stronghold south of Ardougne.
  3. Fight Arena — 12,175 Attack XP plus 1,000 GP. Located near Yanille.
  4. Vampyre Slayer — 4,825 Attack XP. Talk to Morgan in Draynor.
  5. The Grand Tree — 7,900 Agility and 7,900 Magic XP. Continue the gnome storyline you opened in Tree Gnome Village.

At this point your Attack is around level 40, Strength near 39, and you have Agility and Magic started for free. That is a legitimate mid-level combat account built in under two hours.

③ Steps 11–15: Finishing the Free List

  1. The Knight's Sword — train Mining and Smithing to 10 first (a few minutes of mining tin/copper), then 12,725 Smithing XP.
  2. Recruitment Drive — needs 12 Quest Points; grab a couple of easy ones (Goblin Diplomacy, Witch's House) if short.
  3. Demon Slayer — 1,500 Attack XP, short and free.
  4. Gertrude's Cat — 1,525 Cooking XP and a kitten (a free, chill pet).
  5. Dragon Slayer — the F2P capstone. With 32 Quest Points banked, this grants 37,300 total combat XP and the rune platebody unlock.
⚠️ Dragon Slayer Is a Real Fight

Unlike the other quests here, Dragon Slayer ends with the Elvarg boss. You will need decent gear (a rune scimitar or better, food, and some combat level). With the free combat XP from steps 1–14 you should be around level 45–50 combat, which is enough with good food and Prayer. Do not attempt it at level 3.

5. 💡 How to Get the Most From Each Quest

Free quests are easy to waste if you rush them. A few habits multiply their value.

① Preparation: Do the Prereqs in Batches

Quests chain. Restless Ghost → Priest in Peril → Nature Spirit is a free three-quest arc that opens Morytania. Tree Gnome Village → Grand Tree is a two-quest arc that opens the gnome transport web. By knocking out prerequisites in groups you avoid backtracking and you always have the next quest available the moment you finish the current one. Before logging off, check which quest points you are one easy quest away from.

② Free Supplies: Grab What the Ground Gives You

Many "free" quests still ask for items. Instead of buying them, collect them for free:

  • Buckets & ropes: spawn at farms and near major banks (Lumbridge, Falador, Varrock).
  • Spades: toolbelt since 2019 — you already own one.
  • Beer / wine: sometimes needed (Pirate's Treasure needs a pint of rum you can buy for a few GP, or borrow from a table). Keep a couple hundred GP from Fight Arena in reserve.
  • Logs & bones: drop from the low-level monsters you pass while walking; bury the bones for free Prayer XP.

③ Common Pitfalls to Avoid

  • Skipping the quest journal: the journal tells you exactly where to go. Read it before every trip.
  • Grinding instead of questing: if a skill is below the requirement for a good quest, train it for five minutes rather than avoiding the quest entirely.
  • Forgetting quest points: Dragon Slayer needs 32 QP. If you ignore easy quests you will hit a wall. Track your QP count.
  • Dying in Dragon Slayer: bring food and Prayer. The Elvarg fight is free to attempt but painful if underprepared.

6. 🧭 Transitioning to Members (Free Path)

If you are F2P, the free quest list is smaller but still valuable. The moment you buy membership the doors fly open. Here is how to bridge the two.

① F2P Free Quests to Do First

On a free account, prioritize: Cook's Assistant, Sheep Shearer, Restless Ghost, Imp Catcher, Witch's Potion, Demon Slayer, Goblin Diplomacy, Witch's House, and finally Dragon Slayer (once you have 32 QP from the others). These give you combat XP, Prayer XP, Magic XP, a rune platebody unlock, and a stack of quest points — a strong launch pad.

② First Member Quests (Still Free)

The instant you become a member, run Waterfall Quest, Tree Gnome Village, Fight Arena, Vampyre Slayer, and The Grand Tree. None of them cost a coin. This is the single biggest "free power spike" in the game and it is available the moment your membership activates.

③ When to Buy Membership

You do not need membership to start, but you do need it to unlock the best free quests. The efficient play is: finish the F2P free list on a free account, then buy one month of membership and blast through the members free list in a single session. That sequence gives you a near-maxed-for-free account before you have paid for more than a single bond's worth of time. If you are saving for a bond, our Money Making hub lists F2P methods that fund the first membership from pure gameplay.

For players coming from a different starting point, the New Player Skill Priority guide explains which stats to train alongside questing so nothing bottlenecks you, and the Questing Beginner Guide covers the journal and quest-point system in depth.

7. ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the single best free quest in OSRS?

Waterfall Quest. It requires nothing, takes about 15–20 minutes, and awards 13,750 Attack XP and 13,750 Strength XP. No other single quest gives that much combat XP with zero requirements. Every new account should do it first thing after becoming a member.

Can a free-to-play account do any of these?

Yes. Cook's Assistant, Sheep Shearer, The Restless Ghost, Imp Catcher, Witch's Potion, Demon Slayer, Goblin Diplomacy, Witch's House, and Dragon Slayer are all F2P. The huge XP quests (Waterfall, Tree Gnome Village, Fight Arena, Vampyre Slayer, Grand Tree) are members-only, but they cost nothing beyond the membership itself.

How much combat XP can I get for free total?

Roughly 120,000+ total XP across Attack, Strength, Defence, Prayer, Magic, Agility, and Smithing if you do the entire free list (F2P + free members quests). That translates to a combat level in the high 50s from a level 3 start, with zero GP spent.

Do I need any items to start these quests?

Almost none. The toolbelt gives you a spade, tinderbox, and chisel. Buckets and ropes are free ground spawns. A few quests (Pirate's Treasure, some members arcs) want a small amount of GP for a drink or fee, but Fight Arena alone gives you 1,000 GP to cover that.

Why is Dragon Slayer listed as "free" if it's hard?

It costs no GP and has no stat requirements beyond 32 quest points, which the other free quests provide. The difficulty is the Elvarg boss fight, not a gold or level gate. We include it because a prepared free account can beat it and walk away with the rune platebody and ~37K combat XP.

Should I train skills or do quests first?

Do quests first whenever a quest gives XP in a skill you would otherwise grind. The only time to train before questing is when a quest requires a low stat (e.g., 10 Mining/Smithing for Knight's Sword) — and even then, five minutes of training unlocks a 12,725-XP reward, so the trade is wildly worth it.

What quest points do I need and why?

Quest points (QP) gate higher-tier quests. You need 12 QP for Recruitment Drive and 32 QP for Dragon Slayer. The easy free quests (Goblin Diplomacy, Witch's House, Cook's Assistant, etc.) build your QP count painlessly, so always keep an eye on the number in your quest journal.

Is the free quest path good for Ironman accounts too?

Yes — Ironmen cannot trade for supplies, so free quests are even more valuable to them. The same list applies, and Ironman-specific routing is covered in our Ironman Beginner Guide. Waterfall Quest and the gnome arc are staples of every Ironman's first day.

What should I do after the free quest list?

Move into the broader order in our Optimal Quest Order, start Slayer for combat income, and chase the Combat Achievements tiers that your new free levels unlock. You will also be close to the requirements for several money makers on our Money Making pages.

Are there any free quests I should avoid as a beginner?

Avoid long, combat-heavy, or puzzle-dense quests (Underground Pass, The Fremennik Trials, Legends' Quest) until you have levels and experience. They are "free" in GP but expensive in time and risk for a new account. Stick to the ranked list above until you are comfortable.
