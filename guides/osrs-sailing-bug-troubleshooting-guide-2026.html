OSRS Sailing Bug & Troubleshooting Guide 2026 — Stuck Boat, Cargo Loss, Plugin Fixes

1. 🚢 Boat Stuck at the Dock

The most common "bug" is not a bug at all — it is the gangplank. When you build or upgrade a ship at a port, the boat spawns with the gangplank down, and the game treats the deck as walkable ground. If you click the helm before stepping off, the helm often refuses because you are technically "docked" and the travel option is disabled.

How to fix it

  • Step off the ship. Walk down the gangplank to the dock tiles. This flips the boat into "moored" state.
  • Right-click the gangplank and select the board option to step back on. The helm should now respond.
  • If it still will not move, log out to the lobby and log back in. The dock state resets on reload.

Your cargo in the hold is safe the entire time — docking bugs never delete items. If you were mid-upgrade and the ship looks "broken," open the shipwright menu again; the upgrade usually completed server-side even if the model did not refresh. For a full walkthrough of building and upgrading your first vessel, see our 1-99 Sailing guide.

⚠️ The quick rule

If the helm ignores you, your problem is almost always that you are standing on the dock-loading tiles, not a server fault. Step off, re-board, and try again before filing a bug report.

2. 🌊 Boat Stuck at Sea

A ship that freezes mid-voyage — the helm spins, the world scrolls, but the boat will not change heading — is the second classic. It happens most often when you click the helm while the travel animation is already resolving, or when the client loses a packet during a region transition.

Recovery steps

  • Right-click the helm and pick Escape to cancel the current route.
  • Log out to the lobby and back in. This is the reliable fix; the boat reloads at its last valid position.
  • Re-plot the route from the helm once you are back.

The catch: a hard logout at sea can drop the cargo currently in your hold. Your bank is never touched, but anything loaded for delivery can be lost. If you were carrying high-value trade goods, reload at the nearest port and reclaim from the cargo office if the option appears. Players who primarily AFK-train on long routes should read our Sailing AFK training guide for safe route choices.

3. 🔌 Plugin & Client Crashes

Sailing pushes the old client renderer hard — large water planes, dynamic lighting on the hull, and the 117HD water shaders are the usual suspects. The symptom set is consistent: boats render invisible, the screen goes black when you board, or the client hard-crashes to desktop within seconds of entering a port.

What actually fixes it

  • Disable Legacy GPU in the GPU plugin settings, or switch the renderer to the new Official client renderer.
  • Disable 117HD temporarily. Many Sailing crashes are a 117HD water-reflection conflict that the team is still patching.
  • Update RuneLite — Sailing support shipped in a specific client build; an out-of-date client crashes on the new map chunks.
  • Lower the draw distance if you are on integrated graphics.

If the client crashes specifically when opening the ship-management interface, that is a known memory spike in the inventory model preview; closing other heavy plugins (Ground Items, GPU with high samples) frees enough headroom. The crashes are client-side and never affect your account or items.

4. 🎮 Helm Ignores Commands

Sometimes the helm accepts a click but the boat does nothing. Before assuming a bug, rule out the three "not a bug" causes:

  • Speed is not zero. The boat will not turn instantly at full sail. Drop to a low speed, then steer.
  • You are in shallow water. A boat on a sandbar or against a bank cannot change heading until it is in deep water.
  • You double-reversed. The helm has a brief lock after a hard reverse; a second reverse input is dropped.

If none of those apply and the helm genuinely eats inputs, click the helm twice with a one-second gap and re-select the destination from the map rather than the compass rose. That forces a fresh pathing request. This is the same root cause as the "stuck at sea" packet loss, so the logout-and-return fix in section 2 also works here.

5. 🏞️ Wyrmscraig Narrow River Jam

The Wyrmscraig river became infamous in July 2026 for a "Suez Canal" style traffic jam: boats stacking up at a sharp bend where the channel narrows, each waiting on the one ahead, until the whole stretch locks solid. It is not strictly a bug — it is the momentum model doing exactly what it was designed to do, badly, in a tight space.

How the river expects you to drive

  • You need forward momentum to turn. A boat at crawl speed cannot pivot. Keep a moderate speed into the bend.
  • Point into the bend early. Start the turn before the channel narrows, not at the apex.
  • Watch the NPC prompt. The river pilot NPC pops a hint when you enter the danger stretch — follow it rather than free-steering.
  • Do not stop. A dead-stop boat in the narrows blocks everyone behind it. If you must wait, pull to the wider pool before the bend.

If you are already jammed, the only clean exit is to reverse out to the wider pool and re-enter at the correct speed. Our Wyrmscraig guide maps the safe entry speed and the exact tile where the bend begins.

⚠️ Avoid the jam entirely

The river jam is worst during double-XP and community events. Route around Wyrmscraig at peak times, or accept a slightly longer path on open water where momentum steering is forgiving.

6. 📦 Cargo Hold & Death Penalty

The cargo hold is the single most misunderstood Sailing system, and most "I lost everything" posts come from not knowing the rules. Here is exactly what happens.

SituationWhat You KeepWhat You Lose
Capsize (boat destroyed)Your entire bank, worn gear, and inventoryThe cargo loaded in the hold, plus a reclaim fee at the port
Death on the boatBank and most inventoryA death pile spawns at the mooring — recoverable if you return
Ship facilities moved/removedBanked itemsCargo stored in that facility, if you ignore the warning

The key reassurance: your bank is never at risk from Sailing. Only the loose cargo in the hold is on the line. When you capsize, you respawn at the home port with a reclaim window; pay the fee and your hold contents (if any survived) return. A death pile left on the boat is recoverable at the mooring for a short window, exactly like a normal wilderness death pile.

The one trap is the facility-moved warning. If you store cargo in a ship facility and then move or scrap the ship, the game warns you that the contents will be destroyed. Confirm the move only after emptying the facility, or the cargo is gone with no reclaim. Our Group Ironman facilities bug-fix guide covers the GIM-specific version of this warning.

8. Sailing Bug FAQs

Q: My boat is stuck at the dock and will not move. What do I do?

Walk down the gangplank to the dock, then right-click the gangplank to re-board. The helm only responds once the boat is in a clean moored state. If it still will not move, log out to the lobby and back in to reset the dock state. Your cargo is safe the whole time.

Q: I logged out at sea and my cargo disappeared. Is it gone?

A hard logout at sea can drop the cargo currently in your hold. Your bank is never affected. Reload at the nearest port and check the cargo office reclaim option; some lost cargo is recoverable there for a short window.

Q: Why does my client crash every time I board a ship?

That is almost always a renderer conflict — Legacy GPU or 117HD struggling with Sailing's water shaders. Disable Legacy GPU, turn off 117HD temporarily, and update RuneLite to the Sailing-support build. The crash is client-side and never touches your account.

Q: The helm eats my clicks but the boat will not turn. Bug?

Often not. Check that your speed is not at zero, that you are in deep water (not a sandbar), and that you did not just reverse (the helm locks briefly). If those are fine, click the helm twice with a gap and re-pick the destination from the map to force a fresh pathing request.

Q: What is the Wyrmscraig narrow-river jam and how do I avoid it?

It is a momentum-model traffic jam at a sharp, narrow bend — boats stack because a stopped boat blocks everyone behind. Keep forward speed into the bend, turn early, follow the pilot NPC prompt, and never stop in the narrows. Route around it during double-XP events.

Q: Does Sailing death delete my bank?

No. Capsizing or dying on a boat only risks the loose cargo in the hold. Your bank, worn gear, and inventory are safe. A death pile on the boat is recoverable at the mooring for a short window.

Q: I moved my ship and the cargo in its facility vanished. Can I get it back?

Only if you ignored the move warning. When you move or scrap a ship with cargo stored in a facility, the game warns that contents will be destroyed. Once confirmed, there is no reclaim. Always empty the facility first.

Q: Lost Crates are not showing in my hold. Bug?

The crate exists server-side but the model failed to draw. Use Telekinetic Grab on the crate tile or simply relog; it will appear. This is a display glitch, not a lost item.

Q: Death's Coffer is locked after a Sailing death. Is my stuff stuck?

No — it is a display bug. Visit any banker once and the coffer re-syncs. The lock never reflects a real state change.

Q: Where do I report a Sailing bug that is not listed here?

Use the in-game bug report with a screenshot. Sailing bugs are triaged quickly because the skill is recent. Cosmetic and recoverable defects are patched in the weekly game updates.
