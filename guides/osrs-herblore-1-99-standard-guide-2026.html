OSRS Herblore 1-99 Standard Training Guide (2026)

Herblore turns humble herbs and secondaries into the potions that power almost every boss kill, Slayer task, and raid in Old School RuneScape. This 2026 standard guide walks you from your first cleaning of a Guam leaf to a bank full of Overloads — with every phase, XP rate, GP cost, quest shortcut, and herb-run strategy laid out step by step.

1. 🌿 What Is Herblore and Why Train It?

Herblore is an artisan skill focused entirely on potions. You clean grimy herbs, mix them with a vial of water to create unfinished potions, then add a secondary ingredient to finish each brew. Each finished potion grants a fixed amount of experience. Because the materials are consumed on use, Herblore is a buyable but costly skill — you are effectively trading Grand Exchange coins for experience points. Unlike a gathering skill such as Woodcutting or Fishing, you do not "earn" the raw materials by playing the skill itself; you supply them from Farming, Hunter, monster drops, or the market.

For brand-new players the single most important fact is this: Herblore is fully members-only. There is no free-to-play training path and no free-to-play potions. You must also complete the short Druidic Ritual quest before the skill is even unlocked on a members account. If you are still on a free-to-play world, treat Herblore as a goal to plan toward once you upgrade, and read our F2P to P2P membership guide for the transition.

Why experienced players grind Herblore to 99

  • PvM survival: Prayer potions, Super restores, and Saradomin brews are mandatory for bosses, Slayer tasks, and raids. Without them you simply cannot sustain long trips.
  • Damage buffs: Super attack, Super strength, Super defence, and Super combat potions raise your damage and accuracy by large margins, cutting kill times dramatically.
  • Overloads: At 90+ Herblore (after the Recipe for Disaster subquests) you unlock Overloads — the strongest combat potion in the game, boosting every combat stat at once.
  • Achievement Diaries and content: Many Achievement Diary tasks, elite clues, and high-level areas expect specific potions or a certain Herblore level.
  • Untradeable value: On an ironman, the only way to get potions is to make them, so Herblore directly gates your progression through the mid and late game.
💡 Members Only — Plan Around It

Because Herblore is members-only and buyable, most players train it in bursts whenever they have a coin buffer, rather than continuously. Pair it with another buyable artisan skill such as Fletching 1-99 when your bank can absorb the cost, and for the full skill list see our complete beginner skill training guide, which places Herblore alongside every other members skill in one roadmap.

The normal 1-99 experience total is 13,034,431 XP. To put that in perspective, a typical mid-tier potion grants around 100 to 180 XP, so you will make well over one hundred thousand potions on the road to 99. That is why method choice and material cost matter far more than click speed.

2. 🧪 How Herblore Works — Cleaning, Unfinished, Secondaries

Every potion in the game is assembled in the same three logical steps. Understanding them prevents wasted materials and helps you see why some potions are far more expensive per XP than others.

② Step 1 — Clean the herb

Use a grimy herb on your character (or simply click it in your inventory) to clean it. Cleaning grants a small amount of experience and is required before the herb can be mixed. In practice, most players skip the cleaning step entirely by buying already-clean herbs from the Grand Exchange, trading a trivial amount of XP for a massive saving of time.

③ Step 2 — Make the unfinished potion

Add a clean herb to a vial of water (or, for a handful of recipes, to coconut milk or a different base) to create an unfinished potion. This step gives a small experience tick and prepares the herb to receive its secondary.

④ Step 3 — Add the secondary ingredient

Add the secondary ingredient — eye of newt, limpwurt root, snape grass, red spiders' eggs, crushed nest, and so on — to the unfinished potion. This final step is where the bulk of the experience comes from, and it is also where most of the cost sits, because secondaries are often rarer than the herb itself.

💡 Buy Clean Herbs and Secondaries

For a standard 1-99 you almost always buy clean herbs and secondaries from the Grand Exchange rather than gathering them yourself. The time saved is worth far more than the small cleaning XP you skip. The only exception is ironmen and players who run their own herb farms, which we cover in the Farming section.

Make sure your inventory and bank are organised before a long session. A tidy setup with potions in one tab and materials in another lets you hold Make-X and fly through thousands of mixes, and simple bank-tag tricks make the loop painless even across many different potion types.

⚠️ Budget Before You Start Overloads

Do not begin Overloads until your bank can comfortably absorb the cost. A Prayer potion or brew route is far kinder to your GP if you are still mid-progression. Overloads are the fastest route from 90 to 99, but they are also the most expensive per XP in the entire skill.

5. ⚡ Fastest vs Profitable vs Ironman Routes

"Best" means different things depending on your account and your goals. Here are the three routes players actually use.

⑧ Fastest Route (Max XP/hr, Max Cost)

The fastest main route is straightforward: quest to ~40, then make the highest-XP potion you can at each bracket, and finish 90-99 with Overloads. This minimises hours (roughly 35 to 45 hours of active clicking) but costs the most — often 180 to 250M GP depending on Grand Exchange prices. It is the route for players who have a large bank and want the skill done.

⑨ Profitable / Cheapest Route (Slow, Low Cost)

The cheapest route leans on free quest XP, makes the lowest-cost-per-XP potions available, and grows its own herbs through Farming. By supplying materials yourself you can bring the total bill down toward 80 to 150M GP. Some players even turn a small profit by making potions during favourable price gaps, though Herblore is generally a GP sink. If budget is your constraint, our low-cost 1-99 Herblore guide goes deeper on squeezing every coin.

⑩ Ironman Route (Self-Sufficient)

Ironmen cannot buy materials from other players, so the entire skill becomes a logistics problem. You must farm herbs, collect secondaries from monsters and activities, and use shops (such as the Trollheim and Mor Ul Rek stores) for items that cannot be farmed. Herbiboar at Hunter 80+ is a major free herb source, and herb runs become a daily habit rather than an optional money-saver. Ironmen should also prioritise the ironman beginner roadmap so Herblore progression lines up with Farming and Hunter unlocks.

🎯 Which Route Should You Pick?

  • Main with a big bank: Fastest overload-finish route — done in days.
  • Main on a budget: Quest XP + grown herbs + brew route — cheapest per XP.
  • Ironman / Hardcore: Farming-first, self-supplied, accept a slower climb.
  • Returning player: Match your route to your current GP, not your ideal.

6. 🌱 Herb Runs, Farming Synergy & Cost Control

The single biggest lever on Herblore cost is Farming. A well-run herb farm supplies most of your Ranarr, Snapdragon, Toadflax, and Torstol needs for free once the seeds pay for themselves. This is why experienced players train Farming early and treat herb runs as a daily money-and-XP engine.

⑪ How to Cut the Bill

  • Grow your own herbs: Farming herb runs (Ranarr, Snapdragon, Toadflax, Torstol) supply most of your material needs for free after the seeds pay off. This is the single largest saving and the reason our 1-99 Farming profit guide is essential reading.
  • Master the herb run: A tight route of patches with teleports and ultracompost routinely yields millions per run. Our herb run mastery guide shows the optimal loop.
  • Herbiboar: At Hunter 80+ the Herbiboar minigame drops clean herbs and is a free Herblore material source for ironmen and mains alike.
  • Do the quest XP first: Free levels 1-40 save tens of millions before you brew a thing.
  • Watch Grand Exchange prices: Herb and secondary prices swing daily; buy in dips and avoid panic-buying during surges.
  • Fund with bossing: Use profits from the Money Making hub to pay for buyables rather than draining your main stash.
💡 Make Extras to Use

Prayer and Super restore potions are consumed by every boss trip. Making a few hundred extra during training is cheaper than buying them later at peak prices, and it means your Herblore session doubles as restocking your PvM supplies.

8. ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is Herblore members only?

Yes. There is no free-to-play Herblore training, and you must complete the Druidic Ritual quest to unlock the skill even as a member. If you are currently free-to-play, plan your membership transition first and treat Herblore as a members-only goal.

What is the cheapest way to 99 Herblore?

Do all the quest XP first (free to roughly level 40), then make the cheapest per-XP potions available, and grow your own herbs through Farming. Supplying your own materials can bring the bill down toward 80 to 150M GP versus 200M+ if you buy everything and finish with Overloads. Our low-cost guide covers the exact potion order for tight budgets.

How much does 99 Herblore cost?

A standard route costs roughly 100 to 200M GP; adding an Overload finish from 90 to 99 pushes it toward 150 to 250M depending on Grand Exchange prices at the time. Players who self-supply herbs through Farming sit at the low end of that range, while buyers using Overloads sit at the high end.

Can I make money with Herblore?

Rarely. Most potions cost more to make than they sell for, so Herblore is treated as a GP sink. The real "profit" is the combat performance the potions unlock. Some players flip certain potions during price gaps, but it is not a reliable money maker and should not be your primary income plan.

What are Overloads and when do I unlock them?

Overloads are the strongest combat potion in OSRS. They require level 90 Herblore and the completion of the Recipe for Disaster subquests (specifically the one that grants the Overload recipe). Each Overload grants 250 XP and boosts every combat stat at once. They are the fastest but most expensive route from 90 to 99.

Should ironmen buy or grow their Herblore materials?

Ironmen cannot buy from other players, so they must grow or collect everything. The practical answer is to farm herbs, use Herbiboar for clean herbs, and buy shop-limited secondaries from NPC stores. Farming and Hunter progression directly gate how fast your Herblore can climb, so train those skills alongside it.

How long does 99 Herblore take?

Time depends almost entirely on your GP, not your clicking speed. A buyer using Overloads can finish in roughly 35 to 45 hours of active mixing. A budget or ironman player making cheaper potions and growing herbs may take 60 to 100+ hours spread across many sessions, since much of the wait is Farming growth timers.

Why are Prayer potions so expensive to make?

Prayer potions use Ranarr weed, one of the most demanded herbs in the game because Prayer potions are consumed in nearly every PvM activity. High demand keeps Ranarr priced high, which is why the 38 to 45 bracket is where many players feel the cost jump for the first time. Making extras during training is cheaper than buying them later.

Do I need Herblore for bossing and Slayer?

Effectively yes. Prayer potions, Super restores, and Saradomin brews are expected on almost every serious boss trip and many high-level Slayer tasks. You can technically boss with shop-bought potions early on, but making your own at 63+ (Super restores) and 66+ (brews) is what makes sustained trips possible. Plan Herblore as a core part of your PvM prep.

🎯 Final Tips Before You Start

  • Complete Druidic Ritual and the full quest XP chain before spending a single GP.
  • Start a Farming herb-run habit early — it pays for Herblore later more than any other trick.
  • Use Make-X at a bank for smooth, fast mixing, and keep materials tagged in one bank tab.
  • Train Fletching alongside Herblore when you have a GP buffer — both are buyable artisan skills with simple loops.
  • Save Overloads for the 90-99 sprint once your bank is ready, not before.
  • Make a few hundred extra Prayer and Super restore potions during training — they become your PvM stockpile.
