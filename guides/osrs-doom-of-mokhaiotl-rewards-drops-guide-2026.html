1. 💎 Unique Unlocks by Delve

The defining feature of the Doom of Mokhaiotl is that it is a delve boss. You do not simply walk in and roll a full loot table. Instead, the boss sits at the bottom of a descending series of encounters, and the deeper you commit, the more of its unique loot becomes available to drop. Each successful clear lets you choose to bank your rewards and reset, or descend further for a better table at greater risk. This structure is what separates Doom from a traditional one-and-done raid boss, and it is the single most important thing to understand before you chase any specific item on this page.

The unique table is split across four unlock thresholds. You will not see the top-tier uniques until you have proven you can survive deep enough to reach them. The gating is strict: a shallow delve simply cannot drop what has not been unlocked yet, no matter how lucky you feel.

Delve DepthUnique UnlockedWhat It Is
Delve 2Mokhaiotl ClothA crafting material used to upgrade a tormented bracelet into confliction gauntlets
Delve 3Eye of AyakA 3-tick powered staff with a demonbane effect
Delve 4Avernic TreadsHybrid boots that can be combined with Cerberus boots for a BiS hybrid pair
Delve 6+Dom PetThe boss pet, a rare drop only available at delve 6 and deeper

This gating directly shapes your strategy. If you only want the cloth, you can stop at delve 2. If you want the Eye of Ayak you must reach delve 3, and the Avernic Treads require delve 4. The Dom pet is the deepest prize, locked behind delve 6. Because death, teleporting out, or claiming rewards all reset you back to delve 1, every deeper unique represents a real commitment of time and survival skill. Our Delve Tiers guide covers the exact risk-and-reward curve of going deeper, and our main Doom guide explains the mechanics you need to survive each layer.

One more point worth stating up front: the drop rate for these uniques continues to improve as you descend, but the table is what unlocks at the depths above. In other words, reaching delve 4 both unlocks Avernic Treads and gives you the best odds on every item below it. The deeper you go, the more efficiently you farm the whole table at once.

2. 👁️ Eye of Ayak

The Eye of Ayak is the signature weapon drop of the Doom of Mokhaiotl, unlocking at delve 3. It is a 3-tick powered staff, which places it in the same family as the other powered staves in the game — it requires no runes to cast its built-in spells, drawing instead from its own charges. For players who enjoy magic training, bossing, or slayer on demon-type targets, the Eye of Ayak occupies a comfortable middle ground between the cheaper powered staves and the top-end shadow.

Its headline feature is the demonbane effect. Against demons, the staff gains a substantial damage and accuracy bonus, making it one of the strongest magic options for any demonic encounter. Given that the Doom of Mokhaiotl itself is a demon-themed fight, the Eye of Ayak is not just a drop you sell — it is a drop that helps you clear the very boss it comes from, especially when paired with the right demonbane loadout. For the full setup, see our Gear & Inventory guide, which details magic and demonbane builds per delve depth.

At an estimated value of roughly 38.4M GP, the Eye of Ayak is the more accessible of the two big-ticket uniques from this boss. It is the item most casual and mid-level endgame players will target first, because it improves your magic dps on a wide range of content while still being cheap enough to actually buy and use rather than only chase. 3. 🥾 Avernic Treads

The Avernic Treads are the highest-value unique in the Doom of Mokhaiotl drop table, unlocking at delve 4. They are hybrid boots, meaning they provide a balanced defensive and offensive profile that works across multiple combat styles rather than locking you into one. For an account that wants flexibility — switching between magic, ranged, and melee without swapping an entire boot slot — hybrid gear is premium real estate, and the Avernic Treads sit at the top of that category.

Their real power comes from how they interact with existing best-in-slot boots. The Avernic Treads can be paired with Cerberus boots to upgrade into a best-in-slot hybrid pair. In practice this means the Treads are not a dead-end luxury item; they are a component in one of the strongest boot upgrades available, fusing the Avernic line's hybrid utility with the Cerberus line's raw power. For players chasing maximum account value, that upgrade path is a major long-term goal.

At an estimated 142.6M GP, the Avernic Treads are the loot that justifies deep delving for many players. Reaching delve 4 consistently is harder than reaching delve 3, which is exactly why the Treads command such a premium. If your goal is the Treads, you should read both the Gear & Inventory guide for a survivable delve-4 loadout and the Delve Tiers guide for when the deeper risk stops being worth it.

4. 🧵 Mokhaiotl Cloth & Confliction Gauntlets

The Mokhaiotl Cloth is the first unique to unlock, appearing at delve 2, and it is the quiet MVP of the Doom loot table. On its own the cloth looks unremarkable — a stackable material with no direct stat. Its value is entirely in what it builds: the cloth is used to convert a tormented bracelet into confliction gauntlets.

Confliction gauntlets are the all-style best-in-slot gloves in the game. Where most BiS glove options force you to pick a combat style (magic, ranged, or melee), the confliction gauntlets provide a strong bonus that applies across styles, making them the definitive glove upgrade for any serious PvM account. Because the tormented bracelet is itself a relatively accessible item and the cloth is the gating material, Doom of Mokhaiotl is the gateway to that upgrade.

StepItemResult
1Tormented braceletThe base item you already own or can obtain
2+ Mokhaiotl Cloth (delve 2+)The conversion material dropped by Doom
3Confliction GauntletsAll-style best-in-slot gloves

Because the cloth unlocks at delve 2, it is the most farmable unique on this page. You do not need to risk a deep delve to start collecting it, which makes Doom an attractive early target even for accounts that are not yet ready to push for the Eye of Ayak or Avernic Treads. If your account still needs its glove upgrade, this is the reason to learn the fight early. The deeper delve guides linked throughout this page explain how to scale safely once you want the bigger prizes.

5. 🐾 The Dom Pet

The Dom pet is the rarest and most prestigious drop from the Doom of Mokhaiotl, and it is locked behind the deepest threshold of all: delve 6 and beyond. Below delve 6 the pet simply cannot drop, no matter your luck. This makes it the true endgame flex of the boss — a signal that you have not only reached the deep delves but survived them often enough to roll the rare chance.

Like all boss pets, Dom is a cosmetic companion that follows you and shows other players you have conquered the content. There is no combat benefit, but for collectors and completionists the pet is frequently the real reason to keep delving past the point where the loot is already profitable. Because it only appears at delve 6+, farming it efficiently means you must already be comfortable at that depth — which loops back to gear, food, and demonbane preparation covered in our Gear & Inventory guide.

If the pet is your goal, treat the deeper uniques (Eye of Ayak, Avernic Treads, cloth) as welcome side income that offsets the cost of your pet grind. The profit-per-hour figures later in this guide assume you are cashing in those drops, which is exactly what keeps a pet hunt sustainable instead of a gold sink.

6. 📦 Common Drops & Quantity Multiplier

Beyond the uniques, the Doom of Mokhaiotl has a healthy common-drop table that forms the backbone of its sustained profit. The notable commons include demon tears, dragon platelegs, various runes, and seeds. These may not be headline items, but at volume they add up, and they are the reason even a dry streak on uniques is rarely a total loss.

The key mechanic for commons is the quantity multiplier. As you descend deeper, the number of common items you receive scales up. The formula the drop table uses is:

📐 Quantity Multiplier Formula

Qn = Q3 + trunc(Q3 × Mn)

Where Q3 is the base quantity at delve 3, Mn is the delve multiplier, and trunc() drops the decimal. The multiplier Mn keeps climbing with delve depth but caps at delve 9 — past that point, the per-kill quantity no longer increases, even though the unique drop rates may still be favourable.

In plain terms: going deeper makes each kill drop more runes, more seeds, more demon tears, and more dragon platelegs, up to a hard ceiling at delve 9. After delve 9 the quantity bonus is exhausted, so any further depth is purely about unique rates and the intrinsic challenge, not about stacking more commons. This is exactly why the Delve Tiers guide treats delve 9 as a meaningful inflection point for profit planning.

7. 💸 Profit Per Hour

So what does Doom of Mokhaiotl actually pay? The answer depends entirely on how deep you go, because depth controls both your unique drop rate and your common quantity multiplier. Two widely cited benchmarks come from the OSRS Wiki's money-making guide (MMG):

MethodEstimated Profit / Hour
Wiki MMG — Dive 1-8Approximately 9.4M GP/hr
Wiki MMG — Dive 1-16Approximately 12.6M GP/hr
OSRS Guru deep-delve estimateRoughly 12-23M GP/hr at deep delves

The Wiki's figures assume a specific dive range and a competent, consistent run. The Dive 1-8 method is the more accessible target and still clears roughly 9.4M GP per hour. Extending to Dive 1-16 pushes that to about 12.6M GP per hour. OSRS Guru's own deep-delve estimate lands in the 12-23M GP/hr band, reflecting that a well-geared account pushing the deeper, higher-multiplier delves can exceed the Wiki baseline substantially — though the upper end assumes good unique luck and near-optimal kills.

The other half of the equation is throughput. Doom of Mokhaiotl is not a high-kills-per-hour boss; the delve structure means each run takes meaningful time. A realistic expectation is roughly 6 kills per hour for a competent group or soloer working the deeper delves. That number is what turns the per-kill loot table into the hourly profit above. Fewer, deeper kills with better tables can out-earn many shallow kills, which is why depth — not raw kill count — drives Doom's profit.

To put Doom's hourly rate in perspective against other endgame content, our High Level Money Making guide and our Bossing Money Making guide both rank it among the stronger bossing options for accounts that can survive the deep delves. And for a broader look at what lucrative boss loot looks like across the game, our New Player Boss Survival guide is a useful companion read.

The practical takeaway: if you can comfortably reach delve 4 (for Avernic Treads) or at least delve 3 (for the Eye of Ayak) and sustain around 6 kills an hour, Doom is one of the better GP-per-hour bosses available. The risk is death resetting you to delve 1, so the profit figures assume you are claiming and banking intelligently rather than greedily over-diving. Doom of Mokhaiotl Drops FAQs

Q: Which uniques drop from Doom of Mokhaiotl, and at what delve?

The unique table is delve-gated. Mokhaiotl Cloth unlocks at delve 2, the Eye of Ayak at delve 3, Avernic Treads at delve 4, and the Dom pet at delve 6 and deeper. You cannot receive an item from a depth where it has not yet been unlocked, regardless of luck.

Q: How much is the Eye of Ayak worth?

The Eye of Ayak is estimated at roughly 38.4M GP on the Grand Exchange. It is a 3-tick powered staff with a demonbane effect, making it a strong, affordable magic option especially strong against demons.

Q: What are Avernic Treads used for?

Avernic Treads are hybrid boots that unlock at delve 4 and are estimated at roughly 142.6M GP. They can be paired with Cerberus boots to upgrade into a best-in-slot hybrid pair, which is their main long-term value.

Q: How do I get confliction gauntlets from Mokhaiotl Cloth?

Mokhaiotl Cloth (delve 2+) is used to convert a tormented bracelet into confliction gauntlets — the all-style best-in-slot gloves. You supply the tormented bracelet; the cloth is the gating conversion material dropped by Doom.

Q: When can I get the Dom pet?

The Dom pet only drops at delve 6 and deeper. Below delve 6 it cannot appear at all, so a pet hunt requires you to be consistently comfortable at that depth.

Q: How does the common-drop quantity multiplier work?

The quantity of common drops scales by the formula Qn = Q3 + trunc(Q3 × Mn), where Q3 is the base quantity at delve 3 and Mn is the delve multiplier. The multiplier keeps rising with depth but caps at delve 9.

Q: Does the drop rate keep improving past delve 9?

The common quantity multiplier caps at delve 9, so per-kill common counts stop growing there. Unique drop rates may still favour deeper delves, but the bulk commons bonus is exhausted at delve 9.

Q: What is the profit per hour at Doom of Mokhaiotl?

The Wiki MMG estimates about 9.4M GP/hr at Dive 1-8 and about 12.6M GP/hr at Dive 1-16. OSRS Guru's deep-delve estimate is roughly 12-23M GP/hr depending on depth and unique luck.

Q: How many kills per hour can I expect?

A realistic rate is roughly 6 kills per hour at the deeper delves. Because the delve structure makes each run time-consuming, depth and loot quality matter more than raw kill count for hourly profit.

Q: Is Doom worth grinding compared to other bosses?

For accounts that can survive delve 3-4 consistently, Doom is one of the stronger GP/hr bossing options thanks to the Eye of Ayak, Avernic Treads, confliction gauntlet material, and a scaling common table. Death resets you to delve 1, so profit depends on claiming rewards smartly rather than over-diving.
