OSRS Prayer Scrolls Guide 2026 — Deadeye & Mystic Vigour from the Royal Titans

🆕 NEW
Published September 12, 2026  |  13 min read  |  By Yanchun Wu

The Royal Titans dropped two of the most account-defining prayer upgrades in years: Deadeye and Mystic Vigour. Deadeye is a Ranged prayer that slots between Eagle Eye and Rigour, and Mystic Vigour is its Magic counterpart between Mystic Might and Augury. Both are untradeable, both are far easier to obtain than the CoX scrolls they lead toward, and Deadeye in particular turns the Corrupted Gauntlet Bow of Faerdhinen grind from a slog into a smooth ride. This guide breaks down the exact bonuses, how to farm both scrolls, and why they matter most for Ironmen.

1. 🗡️ Why the Prayer Scrolls Punch Above Their Weight

Old School RuneScape has always gated its best combat prayers behind rare drops. Rigour comes from Chambers of Xeric, Augury comes from Chambers of Xeric, and both sit at 74 and 77 Prayer respectively. For most of the game's history, that left a wide, awkward gap between the early-game prayers (Eagle Eye, Mystic Might) and the best-in-slot ones. The Royal Titans prayer scrolls close that gap.

Deadeye and Mystic Vigour are deliberately positioned as the "bridge" tiers. They are noticeably stronger than Eagle Eye and Mystic Might, but they stop short of Rigour and Augury. That placement is the whole point: they give mid-game accounts a real, meaningful DPS upgrade years before they ever see a Tumeken's Shadow or a Twisted Bow, and they do it through a boss that is far more accessible than a raid.

💡 The Smart Progression Move

If your account is hovering around 60–70 Prayer and you are weighing up what to grind next, the Royal Titans should jump the queue. Two bosses later you have a permanent Ranged and Magic prayer upgrade that you would otherwise wait hundreds of raid clears to replace. Our Ironman boss progression guide places the Titans right before the Corrupted Gauntlet for exactly this reason.

Both scrolls are untradeable. That is the single most important framing for this whole guide: there is no Grand Exchange price, no flip, and no shortcut. You unlock them by killing the Titans, and that is the only way.

One more framing point worth making: these prayers sit in a deliberate "middle" band of the prayer book. Eagle Eye and Mystic Might are the early-game tiers you unlock through quest points and modest Prayer levels. Rigour and Augury are the late-game tiers gated behind rare Chambers of Xeric drops and 74/77 Prayer. Deadeye and Mystic Vigour land squarely between them, both in stat value and in effort to obtain. That positioning is why they feel so good: they give you a near-endgame prayer bump at a mid-game account stage, and they do it through a boss most players can clear within their first session rather than after hundreds of raid clears.

2. 🏹 Deadeye: The Ranged Prayer, Stats and Unlock

Deadeye is the Ranged prayer scroll. It drops from Eldric the Ice King, one of the two Royal Titans. Once used, it unlocks the Deadeye prayer, which overrides Eagle Eye while active.

⚙️ Deadeye Stats

  • Ranged Attack: +18% (Eagle Eye gives +15%, Rigour gives +20%).
  • Ranged Strength: +18% (Eagle Eye gives +15%, Rigour gives +23%).
  • Defence: +5%.
  • Prayer requirement: 62.
  • Drain rate: 1 prayer point per 3 seconds.
  • Stacking: pairs with Steel Skin for an extra +15% Defence if you need it.

In plain terms, Deadeye is almost Rigour-lite. The ranged strength gap to Rigour is only 5%, and the accuracy gap is just 2%. For any content where you are not already at Rigour, Deadeye is the best Ranged prayer available — and since it only needs 62 Prayer, you can use it long before Rigour's 74. For Bow of Faerdhinen, Blowpipe, and Twisted Bow setups alike, that accuracy and strength bump is a real, measurable DPS gain.

💡 Deadeye Is the Corrupted Gauntlet Key

The single biggest winner from Deadeye is the Bow of Faerdhinen in the Corrupted Gauntlet. More accuracy means more hits land, and more strength means each hit hits harder — which means faster Hunllef kills and fewer supplies burned. Unlock Deadeye before you lock yourself in the Red Prison.

3. 🔮 Mystic Vigour: The Magic Prayer, Stats and Unlock

Mystic Vigour is the Magic prayer scroll. It drops from Branda the Fire Queen, the other Royal Titan. Once used, it unlocks Mystic Vigour, which overrides Mystic Might while active.

⚙️ Mystic Vigour Stats

  • Magic Attack: +18% (Mystic Might gives +15%, Augury gives +25%).
  • Magic Defence: +18% (Mystic Might gives +15%, Augury gives +25%).
  • Magic Damage: +3% (Mystic Might gives +2%, Augury gives +4%).
  • Defence: +5%.
  • Prayer requirement: 63.
  • Drain rate: 1 prayer point per 3 seconds.
  • Stacking: pairs with Steel Skin for an extra +15% Defence.

Mystic Vigour is the Magic equivalent of Deadeye: a near-Augury that you can use at 63 Prayer instead of 77. The damage gap to Augury is only 1%, which is tiny. Where Mystic Vigour shines most is magic-accuracy content — bursting Slayer tasks, the Barrows brothers, and anywhere your Magic Attack bonus is the bottleneck. If you are not yet at Augury, this is your best Magic prayer, full stop.

💡 When Mystic Vigour Matters Most

Magic accuracy is the name of the game for Mystic Vigour. On tasks where you cannon or burst with a powered staff, the extra accuracy and the +3% damage add up across thousands of casts. It is also a comfortable defensive prayer for magic-based bosses where you want the +18% Magic Defence.

4. 🔓 Farming Both Scrolls from the Royal Titans

Both scrolls are unique drops from the Royal Titans. Because each Titan drops a different scroll, the efficient play is to kill the duo and collect from the shared loot. If you have not learned the fight, our Royal Titans guide covers the elemental spawns, the contribution system, and the enrage slam you need to survive.

📜 Farming Notes

  • Deadeye: drops from Eldric the Ice King.
  • Mystic Vigour: drops from Branda the Fire Queen.
  • Both untradeable: you must obtain each on the account that will use it.
  • Kill volume: most players unlock both scrolls within a moderate number of kills — they are far more common than the Twinflame Staff.
  • Pair with a task: do the Titans on a Fire or Ice Giant Slayer task for bonus XP while you farm.

A practical tip: do not "save" the Titans for later. The scrolls are useful the moment you hit 62 and 63 Prayer, and the only thing blocking you is a few boss kills. Knock them out early, then let the prayers pay you back on every ranged and magic trip afterward.

💡 Use the Giantsoul Amulet for the Grind

The Royal Titans teleport on the Giantsoul Amulet (covered in our Giantsoul Amulet guide) is a free, unrestricted way to reset the fight between attempts. It costs no Glory charge and has no cooldown, which keeps your scroll farm efficient.

5. 🏆 Corrupted Gauntlet and Beyond: Where the DPS Shows

The Corrupted Gauntlet is the clearest example of why Deadeye is worth prioritizing. Inside the Red Prison you craft the Bow of Faerdhinen, and your DPS there determines how many supplies you burn and how clean your Hunllef kill is. Deadeye's +18% ranged accuracy and +18% ranged strength is a large, permanent multiplier on that DPS.

✅ Where the Scrolls Carry You

  • Corrupted Gauntlet: Deadeye makes Bowfa kills faster and safer — do the Titans first.
  • God Wars Dungeon: Deadeye and Mystic Vigour both help before Rigour/Augury.
  • Barrows: Mystic Vigour improves magic accuracy on the brothers.
  • Slayer bursting: Mystic Vigour's accuracy and +3% damage add up across long tasks.
  • Raids prep: both prayers smooth the road to Rigour/Augury content.

For a deeper look at the Gauntlet itself, our Corrupted Gauntlet guide and the advanced Gauntlet guide cover the full prep, the Hunllef phases, and the crystal armor you walk out with. Deadeye is the unlock that makes entering those guides less punishing.

💡 Don't Skip It for CG

Ironman route guides are near-unanimous: rush the Royal Titans before the Corrupted Gauntlet. The Deadeye prayer is the single biggest self-obtained DPS upgrade available to a pre-Rigour account, and it directly shortens the Bowfa grind that unlocks the rest of the mid-game.

6. 🛡️ Ironman Value and Self-Sufficiency

For Ironmen and Ultimate Ironmen, the prayer scrolls are even more valuable than for mains, for one simple reason: Rigour and Augury are rare Chambers of Xeric drops, and an Ironman cannot buy them from another player. Deadeye and Mystic Vigour are the best Ranged and Magic prayers an Ironman can realistically own for a very long time.

📊 Ironman Impact

  • Self-obtained: no raid drop required, just the Titans.
  • Permanent bridge: holds the ranged/magic prayer slot until Rigour/Augury arrive.
  • Account rocket: unlocks CG (via Deadeye) which unlocks Bowfa and crystal armor.
  • UIM safe: untradeable and worn/used, no bank pressure beyond the scroll item.

The cascade effect is the real story. Deadeye unlocks a stronger Corrupted Gauntlet, which unlocks the Bow of Faerdhinen and crystal armor, which unlocks a huge slice of the rest of the mid-game. For an Ironman, that one scroll is the keystone of an entire progression arc. Our best-in-slot gear guide shows where Bowfa and crystal armor sit in a long-term plan.

💡 Prioritize Deadeye First

If you only have time for one scroll, Deadeye usually wins for an Ironman because of the Corrupted Gauntlet payoff. Mystic Vigour is still excellent, but the CG unlock is the higher-leverage reward.

7. 💱 Trading, Pricing and Common Mistakes

"Trading" the prayer scrolls is a short conversation: you cannot. Both are untradeable, so there is no Grand Exchange listing, no margin, and no way to move one to an alt account. The practical "trading tips" are really about how to approach them as a self-obtained unlock.

✅ Do This

  • Unlock both on every account you care about. No alt can lend you one.
  • Do the Titans early. The prayers pay back on every ranged and magic trip after.
  • Hit 62 and 63 Prayer first. There is no point banking a scroll you cannot use yet.
  • Use the Giantsoul teleport. Free, unlimited resets keep the farm cheap.

❌ Avoid This

  • Waiting for Rigour/Augury instead. Those are rare; these are a few boss kills away.
  • Assuming you can buy a scroll. Untradeable — the GE will never have one.
  • Forgetting Steel Skin stacks. Pair either prayer with Steel Skin when you need the +15% Defence.
  • Skipping Mystic Vigour for a ranged-only account. You will want it the moment you burst Slayer.

💡 Verify In-Game Before You Commit

Prayer requirements and drain rates can shift with future updates. Before building a trip around Deadeye or Mystic Vigour, confirm the current numbers in your own client's prayer book — the values in this guide reflect the Royal Titans release and may be adjusted by Jagex over time.

8. ❓ Prayer Scrolls FAQs

Q: What are the OSRS prayer scrolls from the Royal Titans?

They are Deadeye (a Ranged prayer) and Mystic Vigour (a Magic prayer). Both are untradeable scrolls that unlock a new prayer when used.

Q: What stats does Deadeye give?

+18% Ranged Attack, +18% Ranged Strength, and +5% Defence. It requires 62 Prayer and drains 1 point per 3 seconds. It overrides Eagle Eye.

Q: What stats does Mystic Vigour give?

+18% Magic Attack, +18% Magic Defence, +3% Magic Damage, and +5% Defence. It requires 63 Prayer and drains 1 point per 3 seconds. It overrides Mystic Might.

Q: Which Titan drops which scroll?

Deadeye drops from Eldric the Ice King; Mystic Vigour drops from Branda the Fire Queen. Both are Royal Titans loot.

Q: Are the prayer scrolls tradeable?

No. Both are untradeable, so you must unlock each one on the account that will use it. There is no Grand Exchange price.

Q: Why is Deadeye good for the Corrupted Gauntlet?

It boosts Bow of Faerdhinen accuracy and strength by 18% each, which makes Hunllef kills faster and safer. Most Ironman routes do the Titans before the Gauntlet specifically for this.

Q: How do they compare to Rigour and Augury?

Deadeye is just below Rigour (+20/+23) and Mystic Vigour is just below Augury (+25/+25/+4). They are the bridge tier: almost as good, available far earlier, and untradeable.

Q: Should an Ironman prioritize Deadeye or Mystic Vigour?

Deadeye first, because it unlocks a stronger Corrupted Gauntlet and therefore the Bow of Faerdhinen. Mystic Vigour is still excellent and worth getting soon after.

Q: Can I stack these prayers with Steel Skin?

Yes. Deadeye and Mystic Vigour both add +5% Defence on their own and can be paired with Steel Skin for an extra +15% Defence when survivability matters more than a damage boost.

Q: Do the scrolls work with the setup from the boss gear checklist?

They work with any ranged or magic setup. Our boss gear checklist shows the weapon and armor pairings that benefit most from the accuracy and strength gains.
