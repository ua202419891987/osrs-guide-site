OSRS Mad Angel Combat Achievements Guide 2026 — All 9 Tasks Explained

Published September 12, 2026  |  13 min read  |  By Yanchun Wu

Jagex added a full Combat Achievement set for the Mad Angel once most of the playerbase had learned her mechanics, and the nine tasks span Medium all the way to Elite. Three of them are pure kill-count grind, but four are mechanic tests that force you to actually engage with the enrage phase instead of tanking through it. This guide breaks down every task, what it demands in practice, the order to attempt them in, and the small Wyrmscraig quality-of-life changes that arrived in the same update.

1. 🏆 Why the Mad Angel Achievements Are Worth Doing

Combat Achievement sets are usually added alongside a boss at launch. The Mad Angel was different: Jagex waited until most players had already learned her fight, then dropped a dedicated set built specifically around her mechanics. The result is a task list that reads like a checklist of the things people were getting wrong.

Look at the mechanic tasks and the pattern is obvious. Dancing Queen forces you to dodge the sword cleave to the correct side repeatedly. Smite Fight forces you to nail the lightning prayer timing three times in a row. Juggling Act forces you to keep attacking while catching the bouncing light ball. If your prayer switches have been sloppy or your positioning has been lazy, this set is Jagex telling you to clean it up.

💡 Why This Matters More in 2026

Combat Achievement points feed into tier rewards that affect everything from clue scroll rates to teleport unlocks. A boss with nine tasks is an unusually dense point source for a mid-level encounter — especially because three of the nine require nothing but repetition.

There is also a structural win. Mad Angel kills now count toward your total Combat Achievement boss kill tracker, so completionists no longer have to track her separately. If you are working toward a total-kill milestone, she is a genuinely efficient addition to your rotation.

2. 📋 The Full Task List: 9 Achievements, Medium to Elite

Here is the complete set as added in the August 26, 2026 update, grouped by tier:

🥉 Medium Tier

  • Mad Angel Adept — Kill her 25 times.
  • Dancing Queen — Kill her after landing 6 consecutive enhanced attacks by dodging her sweep during enrage.
  • Smite Fight — Kill her after 3 consecutive enhanced attacks from praying correctly against her smite during enrage.
  • Juggling Act — Kill her after attacking between every bounce of her blast attack during enrage.

🥈 Hard Tier

  • Mad Angel Veteran — Kill her 50 times.
  • Mad Angel Speed-Runner — Kill her 5 times within 10 minutes of entering the arena.
  • Perfect Mad Angel — Kill her without taking any avoidable damage.

🥇 Elite Tier

  • This Is Madness — Kill her 15 times without leaving the arena.
  • The Pen Is Mightier — Kill her without equipping a weapon.

Notice how the difficulty is distributed. Three tasks (Adept, Veteran, This Is Madness) are pure volume. Four tasks (Dancing Queen, Smite Fight, Juggling Act, Perfect Mad Angel) are execution tests. Two tasks (Speed-Runner, The Pen Is Mightier) are optimisation and endurance tests. That split tells you exactly how to sequence your attempts.

3. 🥉 Medium Tier Walkthrough: The Four Starter Tasks

Mad Angel Adept — Kill Her 25 Times

This is your foundation task. Nothing to optimise: just kill her 25 times. Because every other task in the set requires a kill to bank, you will make real progress here while you work on the mechanic tasks. Do not treat it as a separate grind — it completes itself.

Dancing Queen — 6 Consecutive Side-Dodges in Enrage

This task requires six consecutive enhanced attacks earned by dodging her sweep correctly during the enrage phase. Remember that the cleave repeats six times during enrage, so the task is essentially asking you to dodge all six cleaves to her side without a single mistake.

💡 Set Up the Arena First

The failure mode here is almost always environmental, not mechanical. If she is backed against a wall when the cleave starts, your dodge tiles may be unreachable and the streak breaks. Before you commit to the task, pull her into open ground so every dodge direction is available. Then hold the streak: side-dodge, hit, side-dodge, hit, six times over.

Smite Fight — 3 Correct Smite Prayers in Enrage

Her lightning smite becomes a three-strike sequence during enrage. This task asks you to pray correctly against all three strikes and then finish the kill. If you can time one smite, you can time three — the only difference is that the gaps shrink. The second strike lands roughly 4 ticks after the first and the third roughly 2 ticks after that.

If exact-tick timing is inconsistent for you, the reliable route is to 1-tick prayer flick Protect from Magic for the whole animation. It is more clicks, but it does not depend on hitting a precise tick — which is exactly what you want when the task demands three in a row.

Juggling Act — Attack Between Every Bounce

Her blast attack can be bounced back at her three times if you stand on the shadow tile each time. This task requires you to land an attack between every bounce and still finish the kill. It is a multitasking test: you are chasing the shadow tile while keeping your damage rotation running.

The practical approach is to move early. The shadow appears before the ball lands, so start walking toward the target tile the moment you see it, attack on arrival, and immediately look for the next shadow. Do not wait for the bounce to resolve before you move.

4. 🥈 Hard Tier Walkthrough: Veteran, Speed-Runner, Perfect

Mad Angel Veteran — Kill Her 50 Times

Pure volume, and by the time you are chasing Hard tier tasks you will already have a meaningful chunk of it from Adept. Keep a teleport back to the boss in your bank so travel time between kills stays minimal — the Ardeaglais teleport she drops is ideal.

Mad Angel Speed-Runner — 5 Kills in 10 Minutes

Five kills in ten minutes works out to roughly two minutes per kill including travel. That is demanding but very achievable with a proper golembane setup and clean mechanics, because her fight rewards accuracy windows rather than raw max hit.

⏱️ How to Actually Hit the Time

  • Pre-plan your banking. Do not bank between kills — bring enough supplies for all five.
  • Use the Ardeaglais teleport so the trip between kills is seconds, not a run.
  • Take every enhanced-attack window. Side-dodges and perfect smites are guaranteed damage, which is what compresses kill times.
  • Do not over-eat. Wasted attack ticks are the main thing that turns a two-minute kill into a three-minute one.

Perfect Mad Angel — No Avoidable Damage

This is the hardest of the three Hard tier tasks. You need a kill in which you take no avoidable damage — meaning no failed cleaves, no light-ball explosions, no unmitigated smites. Damage you cannot avoid (a standard melee hit that lands through your prayer timing) is a different matter; the task is about not getting hit by mechanics you had the tools to dodge.

Treat this as a discipline run rather than a damage run. Prioritise the correct response to every special over squeezing in extra attacks, keep Protect from Melee up at all times, and accept that the kill will be slower than usual. One clean run is all you need.

5. 🥇 Elite Tier: This Is Madness & The Pen Is Mightier

This Is Madness — 15 Kills Without Leaving the Arena

An endurance and supply-management challenge. You must complete 15 kills without leaving the arena, which means no mid-run banking. Plan your inventory around a long session: maximum food, efficient prayer restoration, and a clear idea of how many kills your supplies actually support before you commit.

💡 Budget Your Supplies Like a Trip, Not a Kill

Most players fail this task by treating each kill independently and running dry around kill nine or ten. Before you start, work out your per-kill consumption and multiply by fifteen. If your setup cannot sustain it, upgrade your prayer bonus or food quality before you attempt it rather than hoping the RNG is kind.

The Pen Is Mightier — Kill Her Without a Weapon

The genuine Elite gatekeeper. You must kill the Mad Angel without equipping a weapon. Armour, prayers, potions and food are all still available to you — only the weapon slot is off limits. On a boss most players are still learning, and against a golembane weakness you cannot exploit, this is a long, unforgiving fight.

Approach it the same way you approached Perfect Mad Angel: defence and mechanic discipline first, damage second. Expect the kill to take a long time, expect to make several attempts, and expect to be tested on the same three mechanics you have already learned — just with far less room for error because the fight drags on.

💡 Order Matters

Do not attempt the Elite tasks until you can clear her normal fight without thinking about the mechanics. Dancing Queen, Smite Fight and Juggling Act are the perfect warm-up: if you can bank all three, your enrage execution is already good enough for Elite.

6. 🛠️ Wyrmscraig QoL Changes That Shipped With the Update

The Combat Achievement set was not the only thing in the August 26, 2026 update. A handful of smaller changes arrived alongside it, and a few of them directly affect anyone farming the Mad Angel:

✅ What Changed

  • Mad Angel kills now count toward your Combat Achievement total boss kill tracker — no more tracking her separately.
  • The Jeweller's Chisel can be stored in the Magic Wardrobe in your Player-Owned House, freeing up a bank slot.
  • Right-clicking the Jeweller's Chisel now shows "Drop" instead of "Destroy" — a small change that saves anyone who has ever mis-clicked it.

Several unrelated issues were hotfixed the same day, and they are worth knowing about if you were mid-grind when the update landed. Nex access was temporarily disabled while Jagex investigated an update-related issue, then re-enabled once it was fixed. Mirrorback Araxytes at Araxxor were bugged and have since been hotfixed. An issue at the Doom of Mokhaiotl was hotfixed the same day. And completing a Port Task with a full inventory was failing to grant XP and the reward pouch — also fixed.

💡 If Something Felt Broken This Week

All of the above were confirmed resolved as of the update. If a run went sideways for you around that window, it was the patch, not your setup. Do not rebuild your gear strategy around a bug that has already been fixed.

7. 🧠 9 Tips to Clear the Whole Set Faster

✅ Do This

  • Stack the kill-count tasks. Adept, Veteran and This Is Madness all advance on the same kills — plan your sessions so one run counts toward several.
  • Pull her away from walls before any enrage mechanic task so your dodge tiles are always reachable.
  • Use 1-tick Protect from Magic flicking for Smite Fight if exact-tick timing is unreliable for you.
  • Move toward the shadow tile the moment it appears for Juggling Act — do not wait for the ball to land.
  • Treat Perfect Mad Angel as a discipline run, not a damage run. Slower is fine.
  • Budget supplies for fifteen kills before attempting This Is Madness.
  • Keep an Ardeaglais teleport in your bank so Speed-Runner attempts are not lost to travel time.

❌ Avoid This

  • Attempting Elite tasks before the Medium mechanic tasks. They are a built-in progression path — use them.
  • Panic-eating during mechanic runs. Every wasted tick is a mechanic you might miss.
  • Chasing damage upgrades instead of learning the tells. Accuracy windows matter more here than max hit.
  • Leaving the arena mid-session while working on This Is Madness — the counter resets your progress toward the streak.

💡 The One Rule That Fixes Most Failures

Nearly every failed attempt at these tasks traces back to the same cause: reacting too late because you were not watching her animation. Learn the tells before you learn the optimisations. Sword position means cleave, shadow tile means blast, wing flap means smite. Name the attack before it lands and the tasks become routine.

8. ❓ Mad Angel Combat Achievement FAQs

Q: How many Mad Angel Combat Achievements are there?

Nine, spanning Medium, Hard and Elite tiers. They were added in the August 26, 2026 update.

Q: Do I need to complete Fallen From Grace for these achievements?

Yes. The repeatable Mad Angel fight only becomes available after completing the Fallen From Grace quest on Wyrmscraig, and the achievements are tied to that encounter.

Q: What is the easiest Mad Angel achievement?

Mad Angel Adept — simply kill her 25 times. It also progresses alongside the other kill-count tasks, so it effectively completes itself.

Q: How do I earn Dancing Queen?

Land six consecutive enhanced attacks by dodging her sword sweep to the correct side during the enrage phase. Because the cleave repeats six times in enrage, the task requires a clean run through all six dodges.

Q: How do I complete Smite Fight?

Pray correctly against all three lightning smite strikes during the enrage phase, then finish the kill. The enrage smite is a three-strike sequence with shrinking gaps, so either learn the tick timing or 1-tick flick Protect from Magic throughout.

Q: What counts as avoidable damage for Perfect Mad Angel?

Damage from mechanics you had the tools to avoid — a failed cleave dodge, a light-ball explosion, an unmitigated smite. The task is about executing every mechanic correctly, not about avoiding damage you cannot prevent.

Q: Can I bank during This Is Madness?

No. The task requires 15 kills without leaving the arena, so plan your inventory around a long uninterrupted session before you start.

Q: How do I kill the Mad Angel without a weapon?

Everything except the weapon slot remains available — armour, prayers, potions and food. Because you cannot exploit her golembane weakness, expect a long fight and prioritise mechanic discipline over damage.

Q: Do Mad Angel kills count toward my total boss kill tracker now?

Yes. As of the August 26, 2026 update, Mad Angel kills count toward your Combat Achievement total boss kill tracker, so completionists no longer need to track her separately.

Q: What order should I attempt the tasks in?

Kill-count tasks first (Adept, Veteran), then the Medium mechanic tasks (Dancing Queen, Smite Fight, Juggling Act) to sharpen your enrage execution, then Speed-Runner and Perfect Mad Angel, and finally the Elite tasks.
