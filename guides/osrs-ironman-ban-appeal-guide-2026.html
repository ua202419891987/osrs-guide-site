1. 🛡️ Do Ironmen Get a Separate Appeal Channel?

Short answer: no. If you search "Ironman ban appeal" hoping for a special form, you will not find one. Jagex handles all account sanctions — main, Ironman, Hardcore, Group — through the same Ironman account system and the same Support Centre tickets. The "Ironman path" is not a different door; it is a different argument you make inside the normal appeal.

That distinction matters because it changes how you write the ticket. A main account appeals by explaining where GP came from. An Ironman appeals by explaining why it has no traded wealth at all — and why any items moving between your own characters are self-sufficiency, not RWT. Understanding this is the difference between a denied and a reversed case.

1.1 Why Ironman Bans Feel Different

Ironmen are supposed to be self-sufficient, so the moment a detection system sees "wealth entering an Ironman from outside," it assumes the worst: someone broke the mode, or the account is being used to funnel items. In reality, most of those transfers are between your own accounts — a skilling alt feeding your main, or a Group bank split. The system does not know the accounts are yours unless you tell it, with proof.

1.2 The Ironman Advantage You Forget

Here is the upside: Jagex can see your account is in Iron mode. That means there is no Grand Exchange trading history, no flip profits, no RWT-style cash injections — a clean Ironman ledger is the strongest possible evidence you did not buy gold. Use that. A normal appeal has to explain GP sources; yours can show there were none.

💡 Frame the Whole Appeal Around "Self-Sufficient"

From the first line, state the account is an Ironman and therefore cannot use the GE or receive traded wealth. That single fact pre-empts the most common false-positive (RWT) before a reviewer even digs into the logs.

2. 🔍 Most Common False Ban Reasons for Ironmen

Three patterns cause the overwhelming majority of wrongful Ironman actions. If you were banned, you were almost certainly caught by one of these.

2.1 Muling Mis-Detection

"Muling" is moving items/GP from one of your accounts to another. For a normal player that is fine; for an Ironman it is suspicious because the mode forbids receiving outside wealth. But plenty of legitimate Ironmen use a storage alt or a skillier helper and transfer items between characters they own. Jagex's automated trade monitor flags the transfer as RWT or account-sharing. The fix is to prove the accounts are the same person.

2.3 RWT False-Positives From "Trading"

Even Ironmen can be falsely flagged for RWT if they trade with a flagged player (e.g., a clanmate later banned for gold-selling) or receive a "gift" that system reads as payment. The False RWT Ban Appeal guide covers the general method; for Ironmen the twist is that any incoming wealth is automatically weird, so you must show it came from inside your own ecosystem.

🎯 The "Innocent Ironman" Checklist

  • You moved items between accounts you personally own
  • You are in a Group Ironman and split the team bank
  • You left GIM and kept your earned items
  • You traded a friend who was later banned for something unrelated
  • Your account has zero GE trading history

If two or more are true, you are squarely in false-positive territory.

3. 📂 Proving Your Ironman Identity in an Appeal

You cannot "show" a reviewer your Iron status directly, but you can point them at the facts that prove it. The goal is to make the reviewer say "oh, this is clearly a self-sufficient account" in the first 30 seconds.

3.1 What Jagex Can Already See

Jagex's backend knows your game mode, your.total XP, your item sources, and your trade history. A genuine Ironman shows: no GE transactions, items sourced from PvM/skilling, and transfers only to/from accounts on the same credentials. Your job is to narrate that ledger in plain English so the reviewer connects the dots fast.

3.3 What NOT To Say

⚠️ Avoid These Ironman Appeal Mistakes

Do not claim you "never traded" if you moved items between your alts — Jagex sees it. Do not argue the Ironman rules are unfair; argue that you followed them. And never claim a Group bank split was "loot from a friend" when it was your team — reviewers check group membership. Precision protects you.

4. 👥 Group Ironman & Shared-Bank Complications

Group Ironman (GIM) adds a layer normal Ironmen do not face: shared wealth and shared liability. One mate's mistake can ripple across the team.

4.1 When a Group Mate Gets Banned

If a teammate is actioned for RWT or botting, Jagex may review everyone who shared the group bank. Innocent mates often get caught in the sweep because wealth "flowed" from the bad account. Your appeal must separate your activity from theirs: you earned your share through group content, not from their rule-breaking.

4.2 Unlinking and Protecting the Rest of the Team

① Name the sanctioned mate explicitly

In your appeal, state which teammate was banned and that you had no part in their offence. Hiding the connection looks worse than naming it.

② Show your own contribution

List the bosses/activities you personally did to earn your bank share. Group content is legitimate; only the banned mate's source is the problem.

③ Consider leaving the group

If the team is beyond saving, leaving GIM and keeping your items is allowed — and it stops further association. State you have left or intend to.

🔒 Harden the Survivors

After any group scare, lock down the remaining accounts. Our Account Security guide shows how to enable 2FA and clean recoveries so a hijack can't be mistaken for your "real" play.

5. 📝 Step-by-Step Ironman Appeal Workflow

Follow this exact sequence. It is the same Support Centre path as any appeal, tuned for an Ironman's evidence.

① Read the Exact Offence

Screenshot the lockout. Note whether it says RWT, macroing, or "account sharing." The label decides your argument — see how RWT and macro differ in our False Macro Ban Appeal guide.

② Open the Ticket as the Banned Account

Sign into the Support Centre with the banned Ironman and choose to appeal a sanction. Reference the ban ID from the lockout screen.

③ Lead With "This Is an Ironman"

First sentence: state the mode and that it cannot use the GE or receive traded wealth. Pre-empt the RWT assumption immediately.

④ Explain the Transfer / Group Context

If muling: name your alts and say they are yours. If GIM: name the team and your personal contribution. Keep it factual.

⑤ Attach the Self-Sufficient Narrative

Summarize how you earned your wealth (bosses, skilling, collection log). Point to KC and XP as proof. Do not paste giant logs.

⑥ Wait — Do Not Spam

Reviews take days to a couple of weeks during ban waves. Duplicate tickets slow you down; one clear, consistent story is best.

⑦ If Denied, Assess Honestly

Ironman RWT/macro denials are usually final. If you genuinely did nothing wrong and have new evidence, submit once more; otherwise secure a clean new account and start the mode fresh.

⚠️ One Story Across Every Account

Because muling means Jagex looks at your whole account family, your appeal story must match across all your characters. Contradictions between what you say about your main and your alt are the fastest path to denial.

6. ❓ Ironman Ban FAQs

Is there a special Ironman support email or form?

No. Every Ironman appeal goes through the standard Jagex Support Centre like any other account. There is no "Ironman desk." The difference is the argument you make — proving self-sufficiency — not the doorway you enter.

I moved items between my own Ironman and my alt — is that bannable?

Moving items between accounts you own is allowed; it only becomes a problem if automation or a ToS breach is involved. False muling flags happen, but they are appealable by proving the accounts are yours and the transfers were personal.

My Group Ironman mate was banned — will I be too?

Not automatically. You can be reviewed if you shared the group bank, but innocent mates who earned their share through legitimate group content are reversible. Name the banned mate, show your own contribution, and separate yourself clearly.

Can Jagex see that my account is really an Ironman?

Yes. The backend records your game mode, GE usage (none, for Ironmen), and item sources. A genuine Ironman ledger — no traded wealth, self-earned items — is itself strong proof you did not buy gold or RWT.

What if my appeal is denied — is there any second chance?

For RWT/macro on an Ironman, a denial is generally final. You may submit once more with genuinely new evidence, but repeating the same facts rarely helps. The safe move is a fresh, clean account in the mode you want, with tighter security from day one.

🎯 What To Do Next

An Ironman false ban is scary, but your self-sufficient ledger is your best defence. Prove the mode, name your own accounts, and separate from any bad group mate — and a reviewer can reverse it.
