📅 Confirmed 2026 Sailing Update Timeline

Jagex announced the Sailing 2026 roadmap at the Winter Summit, outlining four seasonal updates:

SeasonUpdateStatus
Spring 2026Boat Combat rework — traditional stats apply at sea, new weapon hotpoints, enemy NPC ships✅ Released (March 2026)
Summer 2026The Red Reef quest (Tortugan Chapter 2), player-designed island, NPC ship combat expansion🔜 Upcoming
Fall 2026Barracuda Trial challenge, existing storyline sequel with Sailing mechanics🔜 Upcoming
Winter 2026Major new sea area — the biggest Sailing expansion of 2026🔜 Upcoming
BeyondLarger sea expansion — Jagex has confirmed an additional ocean area is planned beyond the 2026 roadmap, though no details have been announced yet❓ Announced

This is the furthest-out item on the official roadmap, and Jagex hasn't published any details about it yet — no level requirements, no named islands, no release window. What the teaser does tell you is that Sailing is being treated as a long-term, foundational skill, which means the Beyond expansion will very likely be the highest-level Sailing content in the game.

You can start preparing for it right now without any inside information. First, keep pushing your Sailing level. The Winter 2026 sea area is expected to require 80 or higher, and the Beyond expansion will likely sit above that, so treat every level as progress toward day-one access. Second, keep your ship current: maintain at least a Mahogany Hull and a solid cannon so you can handle tougher waters the moment they open. Third, stockpile ship-building materials. Jatoba, Ironwood, and Rosewood logs tend to be cheaper during quiet months, and new ships and upgrades almost always bring new Construction requirements, so holding a buffer of logs and nails saves you from paying premium prices on release day.

Fourth, stay tuned to official channels. Jagex typically runs polls and blog posts before major expansions, and the OSRS Wiki documents new content within hours of launch. Set a reminder to check the news post when the announcement finally lands, because early information often reveals the fastest training routes and the most efficient money-making methods before the market reacts. Finally, use the quieter months to clear backlog: finish Barracuda Trials and Port Tasks for extra Sailing XP, and complete any Tortugan quest content you've missed so you enter the new sea with the full story and all existing rewards behind you.

Treat the Beyond expansion like a marathon rather than a sprint. Consistent training now, a well-upgraded ship, and a healthy stockpile of materials mean you can enjoy the new content on day one instead of spending your first weeks catching up.

All information in this guide is sourced from official Jagex announcements. Details may change as development progresses, but the roadmap items listed above have all been officially confirmed.

⚓ Boat Combat Rework (Spring — Already Live)

Released in March 2026, the Boat Combat rework was the first major Sailing update of the year. It transformed ship-to-ship combat from a minigame-like system into a proper combat experience.

① What Changed

ChangeDetails
Traditional combat stats applyYour equipment and combat level now directly affect combat effectiveness on ships
Ranged gear affects cannonsRanged attack and Ranged Strength stats now correctly apply to ship cannons
New weapon hotpointsSloops received additional weapon mounting points for different weapon types
Enemy NPC shipsPatrolling enemy ships now appear at sea — attackable for loot
Dragon Cannon BarrelNew acquisition method added for this high-tier upgrade
New Port TasksProtect NPC ships from enemy attacks for rewards

② How This Affects You

If you haven't tried Boat Combat since March, return to it. The rework made naval combat viable for both training and profit. Your existing Ranged gear now powers your cannons, so gearing up is straightforward. The new Port Tasks offer a fresh way to earn Sailing XP and rewards.

💡 Dragon Cannon Barrel Update

With the new acquisition method added in the Boat Combat rework, obtaining a Dragon Cannon Barrel is now more accessible. Check the OSRS Wiki for current drop sources and methods.

📜 The Red Reef Quest (Summer 2026)

The Red Reef is the second quest in the Tortugan quest line, following the introductory Tortugan content from the Sailing launch. It is expected to release in Summer 2026.

① What's Confirmed

  • Part of the Tortugan quest line (Chapter 2)
  • Expands the lore of Gielinor's oceans
  • Expected to introduce new Sailing training methods
  • New quest rewards — Sailing XP and ocean-themed items are expected, though Jagex hasn't published the full list yet

Because Jagex hasn't published the full reward list yet, the smartest thing you can do is prepare your account so the rewards are useful the moment the quest drops. Start by making sure your Sailing level is above 60 and your ship is at least a Teak Hull, so any new requirements in the quest are already covered. Next, keep a small reserve of ship-building materials and a few hundred thousand GP on hand — new quest rewards are often tied to new Construction options or training methods, and having the resources ready lets you act before Grand Exchange prices react.

Looking at the first Tortugan quest as a baseline, you can reasonably expect a solid chunk of Sailing XP, some unique quest items tied to the ocean theme, and likely a reward that unlocks or upgrades a ship component — but treat that as an educated guess rather than a confirmed fact. To make the most of whatever the quest hands out, also finish any prerequisite Tortugan content and Sailing tutorial steps you've skipped, because quest chains in OSRS almost always assume you've completed the earlier chapters.

Finally, plan to check the OSRS Wiki and the official news post on launch day. Reward details, drop sources, and the fastest new training routes are usually documented within hours of a release, and players who act early tend to catch the best prices before the rest of the community catches on. With your Sailing level, ship, and resources already in place, you'll be in a strong position no matter what The Red Reef actually rewards.

② Preparation Tips

Based on the first Tortugan quest requirements, expect The Red Reef to require:

  • Sailing level: Likely 50-70 (prepare accordingly)
  • Ship upgrades: At least a Skiff with Teak Hull (31) recommended
  • Combat stats: Moderate combat levels for potential naval combat encounters

❓ Phase 2 FAQs

When is the Red Reef quest coming?

Jagex announced it for Summer 2026. As of June 2026, the exact release date has not been specified, but it's expected within the next few months.

What Sailing level do I need for Phase 2 content?

Based on announced content, aim for level 70+. The Red Reef quest, player island, and new sea area will likely require 50-80 Sailing. Higher levels give you day-one access to everything.

Is the Boat Combat rework worth coming back for?

Yes. The rework (live since March 2026) made naval combat much more engaging. Ranged gear now directly powers cannons, new NPC ships offer loot, and new Port Tasks provide additional training options. It's a significant improvement over the launch version.

What's the player-designed island about?

Jagex is creating an island based on community design input. Players will vote on its content, theme, and features. It's expected to include new skill resources, monsters, or a minigame.

Should I stockpile cannonballs?

Cannonball prices historically rise before combat-related updates. Stockpiling might be profitable, but only invest what you can afford to hold. The new NPC ship encounters coming in Summer 2026 will likely increase demand.

Will there be new Sailing bosses?

Not explicitly confirmed, but the Summer NPC ship expansion and Winter sea area are expected to introduce challenging encounters. The Barracuda Trial coming in Fall may also include combat elements.

Do I need Construction for Sailing content?

Yes. Construction is used to build ship components and facilities. Level 70+ Construction is recommended for Phase 2 readiness. The highest-tier upgrades require up to 87 Construction.

What about the new sea area in Winter?

The Winter 2026 sea expansion is described as the biggest Sailing update of the year. It will add a complete new sea region with new content, monsters, resources, and islands. Expect high-level requirements (likely 80+ Sailing).
