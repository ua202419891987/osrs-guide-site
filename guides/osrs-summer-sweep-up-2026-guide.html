OSRS Summer Sweep-Up 2026: Every Gear & PvM Change Explained

The first batch of Jagex's 2026 Summer Sweep-Up landed on 22 July. It reworks the Gauntlet prep phase, buffs the Sanguinesti Staff and Soulreaper Axe, and smooths out several boss mechanics. This guide breaks down every confirmed number and tells you exactly how to adjust your loadouts.

📅 Update Coverage — Based on the 22 July 2026 Game Update · 11 min read · Numbers verified against Jagex official sources

⏱️ Quick Summary — 30-Second Read

  • 📌 Sanguinesti Staff buffed: heal chance 1/5 (up from 1/6), +8 damage on proc (+1.6 avg/hit), +1 base max hit, charge cost 2 Blood runes (down from 3)
  • 📌 Soulreaper Axe reworked: +4 Strength bonus, stacks no longer drain HP, decay starts at 50 ticks (up from 20), special attack +12% accuracy per stack
  • 📌 Gauntlet prep rework: Tier 1 weapon now costs 0 Crystal Shards, shards auto-go to inventory, demi-boss range 16→10
  • 📌 Boss tweaks: Muspah won't swap forms until 4 hits; Gorillas won't swap prayers until 4 hits + 70 damage; Skotizo totems wait 1 minute to respawn; ToA Wardens now take Sang/Blood Fury lifesteal
  • 📌 CA timers tightened: Gauntlet Speed-chaser 4:45, Corrupted Speed-runner 6:05 — faster prep means stricter speed targets

1. Summer Sweep-Up 2026: Update Overview

The Summer Sweep-Up is Jagex's seasonal project for rebalancing and quality-of-life improvements across Old School RuneScape. The 2025 edition swept a broad range of content; the 2026 edition rolls out in three batches through summer. This guide covers the first batch, released on 22 July 2026, targeting gear, NPCs, and PvM encounters.

② What Shipped in Batch 1 (22 July 2026)

The headline changes are a long-requested Gauntlet prep rework, buffs to two popular weapons (the Sanguinesti Staff and Soulreaper Axe), and boss-mechanic adjustments that make Phantom Muspah, Demonic Gorillas, Skotizo, the Ice Demon, and the Tombs of Amascut Wardens feel less punishing. A wide range of other gear — Ghrazi Rapier, Blade of Saeldor, Pegasian Boots, Master Wand, Ancient Sceptre, Venator Bow, Shaman Mask, Rosewood Blowpipe, Tonalztics of Ralos — also got general buffs, and Inquisitor's armour was reworked.

💡 Why This Update Matters

Most of these are buffs and quality-of-life fixes, not nerfs to your damage output. That means your existing gear is now stronger or easier to use — and a few bosses that used to waste your time with prayer/style swapping are now far smoother to farm.

③ What's Still Coming (Batches 2 & 3)

Jagex confirmed the second batch focuses on Agility (Hallowed Sepulchre, Colossal Wyrm course, new shortcuts, mith grapple shortcuts at high level) and the third covers Hunter plus misc changes like Chivalry's prayer drain, bonfire UX, aerial fishing, and new items (Elemental amulets, Necklace of fangs). Those are not live yet — this guide documents only what shipped on 22 July 2026.

Source: Jagex Official — Summer Sweep-Up 2026 (June blog & 22 July Game Update), OSRS Wiki: Summer Sweep-Up 2026

2. The Gauntlet Prep Rework

The Gauntlet — both regular and Corrupted — has long been criticized for its repetitive, time-gated prep phase. Batch 1 directly targets that friction. Crystal management is faster, demi-bosses are more predictable, and the resource grind is more consistent.

① Guaranteed Demi-Bosses & Range Nerf

There is now a guaranteed random demi-boss spawn at the north, east, south, and west-most rooms from the centre — four demi-bosses with fixed locations, though which ones you face stays random. This removes the "bad prep" feel of awkwardly placed demi-bosses.

On top of positioning, demi-boss attack range was reduced from 16 to 10 — a real safety buff that lets you tag and retreat more reliably, cutting chip damage. Non-demi-boss enemies (wolves, scorpions, etc.) also got standardised defensive stats, so no mob is marginally better to kill than another.

Source: Jagex Official Game Update, 22 July 2026 — "Summer Sweep-Up Gear & PvM Changes"

② Crystal Shard & Crafting Changes

The biggest prep-speed win: weapon frames are now guaranteed from your first "low tier" NPC kill, so no more gambling for a frame. Crystal Shards are also auto-placed in your inventory and drop in a tighter quantity range, making resource income consistent.

Material stacking and crafting costs were adjusted as follows:

Phren Bark, Crystal Ore, and Linum Tirinum are now stackable, and — importantly — Tier 3 armour costs went up (to 100) on purpose: Jagex wants Tier 3 to stay a comfort-vs-speed trade-off, not a guaranteed must-have, so pushing down to Tier 1 prep for the fastest clears is still rewarded.

Source: Jagex Official Game Update, 22 July 2026 — Crystal Shard cost table & prep improvements

③ Combat Achievement Timer Adjustments

Because average completion times drop with faster prep, Jagex tightened several Gauntlet speed CAs. The new targets:

These are tighter, but they reflect the time you now save in prep — so they are realistic for anyone who already had close runs. If you were already near the old timers, you should clear the new ones comfortably.

Source: Jagex Official Game Update, 22 July 2026 — Gauntlet CA timer table

3. Weapon Changes: Sanguinesti Staff & Soulreaper Axe

Two of the most-used end-game weapons got attention. The Sanguinesti Staff is now a genuinely stronger alternative to the Shadow of Tumeken for players who don't own that megarare, and the Soulreaper Axe finally drops its hated self-damage.

① Sanguinesti Staff Buffs

The Sanguinesti Staff received a clean, numeric buff across the board:

StatNew (22 July 2026)Old
Heal chance on hit1 in 51 in 6
Bonus damage when heal procs+8 damage (+1.6 avg per hit)—
Base max hit+1—
Charge cost2 Blood runes/cast3

The heal-on-hit change is the headline: going from 1/6 to 1/5 means noticeably more sustain during long PvM trips, and the +8 damage whenever the heal procs averages out to roughly +1.6 damage per hit across a fight. Combined with the +1 base max hit and the cheaper 2 Blood rune charge cost (down from 3), the staff is both stronger and cheaper to charge. Jagex also refunds one Blood rune per charge on first login after the update (sent to inventory, then bank, then dropped if both are full).

🩸 Where This Shines

The Sanguinesti Staff's lifesteal now also works on the Tombs of Amascut Wardens (see Section 4), making it a much better ToA weapon than before. It remains below the Shadow of Tumeken in raw output, but for players without a Shadow it's now the clear best-in-slot magic option for many encounters.

Source: Jagex Official Game Update, 22 July 2026 — Sanguinesti Staff Tweaks

② Soulreaper Axe Changes

The Soulreaper Axe's rework removes its biggest downside — self-damage — and makes stacks easier to keep up:

StatNew (22 July 2026)Old
Strength bonus+4—
Stack limit5 (6% damage per stack)5 (6% per stack)
Stack decay delay50 ticks (up from 20)20 ticks
HP drain from stacksNoneDrained HP
Special attack accuracy+12% per stack (60% at max)+6% per stack
Special attack min hit+6% per stack (30% at max)—

The two changes players will feel most: stacks no longer drain your HP whatsoever (no more scrambling to eat between kills or during intermissions), and the decay delay doubled from 20 to 50 ticks, so you keep your 5-stack max damage bonus for far longer between kills. The +4 Strength bonus is a flat DPS gain, and the special attack now gains +12% accuracy per stack (60% at max) plus +6% minimum hit per stack (30% at max), making the spec much more reliable.

⚔️ Note on the Earlier Proposal

Jagex's June proposal briefly floated a different design (lower stack cap, higher per-stack damage). The shipped 22 July version is the one above — stack cap stays at 5, +6% per stack, no HP drain. Always trust the shipped numbers in this guide.

Source: Jagex Official Game Update, 22 July 2026 — Soulreaper Axe Changes

③ Other Notable Gear Buffs

Beyond the two headline weapons, Jagex listed general buffs to a long list of gear. Exact per-item numbers aren't all itemised in the shipped notes, but these were confirmed buffed: the Ghrazi Rapier, Blade of Saeldor, Pegasian Boots, Master Wand, Ancient Sceptre, Aquanite Hopper, Venator Bow, Shaman Mask, Rosewood Blowpipe, and Tonalztics of Ralos. A few have documented specifics:

  • Inquisitor's armour was reworked: the set bonus was redistributed to individual pieces, each piece's stats were increased, and the Inquisitor's Mace gained +7 Strength bonus and +7 Crush accuracy. The Great Helm, Hauberk, and Plateskirt each now grant a small individual Crush damage/accuracy bonus.
  • Tonalztics of Ralos special attack now reduces the opponent's Defence by 12.5% of its Magic level per hit (up from 10%) and grants +50% increased accuracy.
  • Burning Claws and the Eclipse Atlatl / Soulflame Horn (PvP) were nerfed; Belle's Folly drop rate was buffed. The Ice Demon demonbane vulnerability rose to 115% (see Section 4).

Source: Jagex Official — Summer Sweep-Up 2026 blog (Inquisitor/Tonalztics specifics) & 22 July 2026 Game Update (general buff list); summary confirmed by OSRS Wiki

4. Boss Mechanic Changes (Muspah, Gorillas, Skotizo, ToA)

Several bosses received "smoothing" changes that reduce tedious prayer/style swapping and dead time. None of these lower the bosses' full difficulty — they just remove artificial time sinks.

① Phantom Muspah

Phantom Muspah will no longer swap forms until you've dealt 4 hitsplats (even 0s count toward this). After the first swap, it additionally won't swap again until you've dealt at least 100 damage to its ranged form or 80 damage to its melee form. The Homing Spikes special attack duration was reduced to 34 ticks (down from 50), and the attack ends early if Muspah phases into its blue shield form. Net result: far less running around dodging spikes and more time dealing damage.

Source: Jagex Official Game Update, 22 July 2026 — Phantom Muspah tweaks

② Demonic Gorillas

Demonic Gorillas will no longer swap prayers until you've dealt damage at least 4 times AND dealt at least 70 damage with a style. Previously the swap timing could force frequent prayer flicking that felt random. Now you get a predictable window to keep one protect prayer up before the gorilla adapts, making kiting and DPS planning much cleaner.

Source: Jagex Official Game Update, 22 July 2026 — Demonic Gorillas tweaks

③ Skotizo & Ice Demon

Skotizo: Once a totem is destroyed, it won't respawn for another minute, and the arena has been reduced in size. That means less running between totems and a tighter, faster kill. Ice Demon: its demonbane vulnerability was increased to 115% (up from 100%), so Arclight / Silverlight / Darklight now hit noticeably harder — relevant if you use the Burning Claws / demonbane route at the Ice Demon in Chambers of Xeric.

Source: Jagex Official Game Update, 22 July 2026 — Skotizo & Ice Demon tweaks

④ Tombs of Amascut Wardens

The Wardens in the Tombs of Amascut are no longer immune to the life-leeching effects of the Sanguinesti Staff or the Amulet of Blood Fury. Jagex notes some non-standard damage calculations in P2 may look odd, but they should work as intended. This is a real buff to ToA magic setups — your Sanguinesti Staff now sustains you on the Wardens, which pairs directly with the staff buff covered in Section 3.

Source: Jagex Official Game Update, 22 July 2026 — Wardens in the Tombs of Amascut

5. How to Adjust Your Loadouts

You don't need to rebuild your account, but a few swaps will squeeze more value out of these changes. Here's where to act first.

① Magic Setups

If you own a Sanguinesti Staff, it's now strictly better and should be your default for ToA, Muspah (magic phase), and general PvM where the Shadow isn't required. The cheaper 2-Blood-rune charge also makes long trips more sustainable. Pair it with an Amulet of Blood Fury — its lifesteal now also works on the ToA Wardens, stacking with the staff's heal. The 1/5 heal and +1 max hit make the staff a much safer buy.

② Melee Setups

The Soulreaper Axe is now viable in content you previously avoided for self-damage — intermission bosses (Muspah, ToA Wardens, Nex phases) no longer punish lost stacks, since decay starts at 50 ticks and there's no HP drain. Keep 5 stacks up and enjoy the +4 Strength and better special. For crush content, Inquisitor's armour is stronger per-piece, so you don't need the full set + mace combo — mix with Oathplate or Bandos.

③ Prep Efficiency (Gauntlet & Slayer)

Gauntlet runners: build Tier 1 weapons for free (0 shards) and lean into Tier 1 prep for fastest clears, since Tier 3 got pricier on purpose. Let shards auto-stack and grab your frame from the first low-tier kill. For Slayer, the Gorilla and Muspah changes let you hold one protect prayer longer and waste less time.

🧰 Quick Loadout Checklist
  • Magers: equip Sanguinesti Staff + Blood Fury for ToA/Wardens sustain
  • Melee: Soulreaper Axe now safe for intermission bosses; keep 5 stacks
  • Crush content: Inquisitor pieces individually buffed — mix and match
  • Gauntlet: Tier 1 weapon is free; push Tier 1 prep for speed CAs

6. Who Should Care About This Update

Not every change affects every account equally. Here's who gets the most value.

① Speedrunners & CA Hunters

If you chase Gauntlet or Corrupted Gauntlet Combat Achievements, the prep rework is huge: free Tier 1 weapons, auto-inventory shards, and predictable demi-bosses shave real seconds. The tighter CA timers (4:45 / 3:45 / 7:05 / 6:05) are offset by those prep savings, so this is a net win for speedrunners.

② Mid-Game PvMers

The Sanguinesti Staff buff is aimed squarely at you. If you don't own a Shadow of Tumeken, the staff is now a legitimate best-in-slot magic weapon for most content, and the ToA Wardens lifesteal fix makes ToA less reliant on a Shadow. The Soulreaper Axe changes also make end-game melee more approachable without the self-damage tax.

③ Ironmen & Slayer Accounts

Ironmen benefit from the Gauntlet prep efficiency (faster CG weapon seed farming) and the boss smoothing changes that reduce food/dodge waste. Slayer accounts running Demonic Gorillas or Muspah tasks will notice less prayer flicking and shorter, cleaner kills. The stackable Phren Bark / Crystal Ore / Linum Tirinum also help bank management during prep grinds.

7. Summer Sweep-Up FAQs

What exactly is the Summer Sweep-Up 2026?

It's Jagex's seasonal rebalancing project for OSRS, split into three batches through summer 2026. Batch 1 (22 July) covers gear, NPCs, and PvM. Batch 2 targets Agility and Batch 3 targets Hunter plus miscellaneous QoL. This guide documents only the shipped 22 July changes.

How much was the Sanguinesti Staff buffed?

Heal chance went from 1/6 to 1/5, it deals +8 damage when the heal procs (+1.6 avg per hit), base max hit rose by +1, and charge cost dropped to 2 Blood runes per cast (from 3). You also get a 1 Blood rune per charge refund on first login.

Is the Soulreaper Axe actually better now?

Yes. It gains +4 Strength, stacks no longer drain HP, decay starts at 50 ticks (up from 20), and the special gains +12% accuracy per stack (60% at max) plus +6% minimum hit per stack (30% at max). It's now comfortable in intermission-heavy bosses.

What changed in the Gauntlet prep phase?

Tier 1 weapons now cost 0 Crystal Shards, shards auto-go to your inventory, weapon frames are guaranteed from your first low-tier kill, demi-bosses have fixed locations with range 16→10, and crafting costs were adjusted. Prep is faster and more consistent.

Did they make the Gauntlet speed CAs harder?

The timers got stricter to reflect faster prep: Gauntlet Speed-chaser 4:45 (was 5:00), Speed-runner 3:45 (was 4:00), Corrupted Speed-chaser 7:05 (was 7:30), Corrupted Speed-runner 6:05 (was 6:30). Because prep is much faster, these are achievable.

Are Demonic Gorillas easier after this update?

Smoother, not weaker. They won't swap prayers until you've dealt damage 4 times AND at least 70 damage with a style, giving a predictable window to hold one protect prayer and less awkward flicking — but overall difficulty is intact.

What changed with Phantom Muspah?

Muspah won't swap forms until 4 hitsplats (then needs 100 ranged or 80 melee damage before swapping again), and the Homing Spikes special dropped to 34 ticks (from 50) and ends early in blue-shield form. Less dodging, more DPS.

Does the Sanguinesti Staff now work on ToA Wardens?

Yes. The Wardens are no longer immune to the life-leech of the Sanguinesti Staff or Amulet of Blood Fury. With the staff's 1/5 heal buff, ToA magic setups sustain much better now.

What other gear got buffed?

Jagex confirmed general buffs to the Ghrazi Rapier, Blade of Saeldor, Pegasian Boots, Master Wand, Ancient Sceptre, Aquanite Hopper, Venator Bow, Shaman Mask, Rosewood Blowpipe, and Tonalztics of Ralos. Inquisitor's armour was reworked per-piece and Belle's Folly's drop rate improved. Burning Claws and the Eclipse Atlatl/Soulflame Horn (PvP) were nerfed.

Is this the full Summer Sweep-Up or is more coming?

More is coming. Batch 1 (this guide) is live as of 22 July 2026. Batch 2 (Agility) and Batch 3 (Hunter + misc, including Chivalry drain and new items) are planned but not yet shipped — we'll cover them when they go live.
