OSRS Best Ranged Gear Progression 2026 — From D'hide to Twisted Bow

🆕 NEW
Published October 9, 2026  |  23 min read  |  By Yanchun Wu

Ranged is the most gear-gated combat style in Old School RuneScape, and the upgrade order matters more than almost any other choice you make. Buy the wrong piece and you burn millions for a 1 percent gain; buy the right piece and your DPS jumps 15 percent overnight. This 2026 progression walks every tier from green dragonhide to the Twisted Bow, with the exact order that maximizes damage per gold spent.

1. Why Ranged Gear Order Matters More Than Other Styles

Ranged damage comes overwhelmingly from the weapon and its ammunition. A Twisted Bow with junk armor still out-damages a perfect Armadyl setup using a rune crossbow. That single fact reshapes how you should spend: in melee and magic the armor contributes more evenly, but in ranged the weapon is king, and the gap between tiers is enormous.

This guide is built around one principle — maximize damage per gold. We rank every slot by how much DPS it adds relative to its price, so you never waste a bond's worth of gold on a 0.5 percent upgrade while a 12 percent upgrade sits one quest away.

① What "progression" means here

🎯 The mental model

  • Tier by tier: Each section is a self-contained loadout you can assemble from the previous one by changing one or two slots.
  • GP-per-percent: We call out the upgrades that punch far above their cost (Anguish, Rigour, Pegasian) versus the traps (max cape early, unnecessarily augmented gear).
  • Ironman-aware: Where a piece is self-obtainable, we say so — ranged is unusually friendly to Ironmen because the best mid-game gear drops from bosses you can farm solo.
💡 One number to remember

A ranged attack roll is compared against the enemy's ranged defence, then a damage roll is scaled by your equipment bonus. Because the Twisted Bow's accuracy and strength scale with the target's magic level, it is not just "the best bow" — against high-magic targets it is in a different league. Buy it last only if you cannot afford it; it is the single biggest upgrade in the whole style.

2. Early Game (1–50 Ranged): D'hide and the First Real Weapon

From tutorial island to roughly level 50, your goal is cheap accuracy and the fastest training weapon you can wield. You are not chasing DPS yet — you are chasing XP and the gold to fund the mid game.

② The starter loadout

SlotBest cheap optionWhy
WeaponMaple shortbow → Magic shortbowFast, accurate, and free-ish from shops and drops.
BodyGreen / blue / red dragonhideLight ranged bonus, cheap, and tradeable.
LegsMatching d'hide chapsCompletes the ranged prayer bonus set.
AmmoAdamant / rune arrowsUse the best you can afford; ammo is your real early cost.
BootsLeather → SnakeskinMinor bonus; upgrade later.

③ The first real weapon: Rune Crossbow

✅ Your first power spike

The Rune Crossbow plus broad bolts (unlocked via the Ranging Guild) is the first weapon that meaningfully raises your damage and lets you tackle bosses like KBD and the Chaos Elemental. Pair it with Black dragonhide and you have a serviceable bossing kit for under a few hundred thousand gold. This is also the kit most players use to farm their first big drops.

⚠️ Don't over-spend on ammo early

Dragon arrows are a real upgrade but expensive. Until you are actually bossing for profit, rune or broad bolts are fine. The DPS gap is small compared with the cost, and you will burn through thousands of bolts an hour.

3. Mid Game (50–75): Blowpipe, MSBI, and Armadyl

This is where ranged transforms from "okay" to "best in the game for most content." Three purchases define the mid game, and they should come in this order.

④ The three mid-game pillars

🏹 The loadout that carries you for hundreds of hours

  • Toxic Blowpipe (with dragon darts): The single best all-rounder weapon in the game. Fast, accurate, and strong against everything that is not a high-magic boss. Self-obtainable from Zulrah for Ironmen.
  • Armadyl Chestplate + Chainskirt: The ranged damage body. Replaces your d'hide and adds a meaningful strength bonus. Drops from Kree'arra (God Wars Dungeon) or the Chambers of Xeric for Ironmen.
  • Masori / Ava's Accumulator → Assembler: The accumulator is cheap and mandatory; the assembler (from the Chambers of Xeric) is a huge quality-of-life and DPS upgrade later.

⑤ Supporting slots

🛡️ Fill these alongside the pillars

Necklace of Anguish is the best mid-game damage necklace and a top-three upgrade overall. Archers ring (then imbued) beats the fury for ranged by a wide margin. Pegasian boots replace your snake skin. Ava's assembler is the bis cape slot for ranged and saves darts. With blowpipe + Armadyl + Anguish + pegasian + archers ring (i) you are already near the top of the ranged ladder for most bosses.

💡 BIS-adjacent before BIS

Most players never "need" the Twisted Bow to do the majority of PvM. The blowpipe + Armadyl core clears Zulrah, Vorkath, Muspah, and the Moons of Peril comfortably. Save the Twisted Bow for when you are doing high-magic-content like the Theatre of Blood or Tombs of Amascut, where its scaling shines.

4. Late Game (75–90): Anguish, Pegasian, Assembler

Once you have the mid-game core, the late game is about squeezing the last percentages and unlocking Rigour. None of these are cheap, but each is worth more than it costs.

⑥ The late-game damage items

SlotUpgradeImpact
NecklaceNecklace of AnguishLargest single jewelry DPS bump; buy it early in this tier.
BootsPegasian bootsBest ranged boots; crafted from a pegasian visage drop.
RingArchers ring (i)Imbued version beats the unimbued by a clear margin.
CapeAva's assemblerBest ranged cape and saves darts — pays for itself.
Off-handTwisted buckler / bookSituational defence; the buckler is bis defensive off-hand.

⑦ The free DPS: Rigour

✅ The highest value "purchase" is a prayer

Rigour (unlocked at 70 Prayer) adds a flat ranged and defence bonus that outperforms most armor swaps and costs nothing but a few quests. If you have not unlocked it, do so before buying another gear piece — it is the most efficient ranged DPS upgrade available. Augury is the magic equivalent and worth getting at the same time.

5. End-Game BIS: Twisted Bow and the Full Max Setup

The full max ranged setup is the strongest ranged kit in the game and is built almost entirely from high-level PvM. Here is the complete bis list.

⑧ The max ranged loadout

👑 Best-in-slot ranged (per-attack, general use)

  • Weapon: Twisted Bow (bis against high-magic targets) or Toxic Blowpipe (bis against low-magic targets).
  • Body: Armadyl chestplate.
  • Legs: Armadyl chainskirt.
  • Boots: Pegasian boots.
  • Necklace: Necklace of Anguish.
  • Ring: Archers ring (i).
  • Cape: Ava's assembler.
  • Off-hand: Twisted buckler (defence) or book of law (switch).
  • Head: Slayer helmet (i) on-task, or Armadyl helmet off-task.
  • Gloves: Barrows gloves (or Zaryte vambraces for pure max).
  • Prayer: Rigour active.
💡 Twisted Bow vs Blowpipe

Against a low-magic target (say, a normal Slayer monster) the blowpipe still wins because the Twisted Bow's scaling works against it. Against a high-magic target (the vast majority of end-game bosses) the Twisted Bow is unmatched. Carry both and swap per fight — that is what max players do.

6. Budget Swaps and Ironman Self-Sufficiency

You do not need the full bis to be effective. These swaps keep you competitive for a fraction of the cost, and most are obtainable without the Grand Exchange.

⑨ Cheaper but strong

💰 Stretch your gold

  • God d'hide (black / blessed) instead of Armadyl early — a big chunk of the bonus for a tiny fraction of the price.
  • Fury or glory instead of Anguish until you can afford the real thing.
  • Climbing boots instead of Pegasian for a long time.
  • Ava's accumulator instead of assembler — cheap and nearly as good for DPS.
  • Barrows gloves are bis-friendly and tradeable, so skip the Zaryte grind early.
⚠️ Ironman note

Every core ranged piece is self-obtainable: blowpipe from Zulrah, Armadyl from Kree'arra or CoX, Anguish from CoX, Pegasian from Cerberus or CoX, assembler from CoX. An Ironman can assemble a near-bis ranged kit without touching the Grand Exchange, which is why ranged is the most popular first max-combat style for self-sufficient accounts.

7. The Exact Upgrade Order (Spend-Smart Path)

If you have a fixed budget, buy in this order. Each step is the highest damage-per-gold upgrade available at that point.

⑩ The list

🎯 Your ranged upgrade roadmap

→ Step 1: Rune Crossbow + broad bolts + black d'hide (bossing start).
→ Step 2: Unlock Rigour (70 Prayer) — free DPS.
→ Step 3: Toxic Blowpipe (dragon darts).
→ Step 4: Armadyl chest + chainskirt.
→ Step 5: Necklace of Anguish, then Archers ring (i), then Pegasian boots.
→ Step 6: Ava's assembler.
→ Step 7: Twisted Bow (the final, biggest spike).
💡 Why Twisted Bow is last on the list, not first

It is the biggest upgrade, but it is also the most expensive and the least necessary for the content that funds it. You will earn the Twisted Bow faster by bossing efficiently with the steps above than by saving for it from scratch. Buy the cheap DPS first, let it pay for the expensive DPS.

9. Common Ranged Mistakes That Drain Your Gold

Most ranged gold is lost not to bad buys but to wrong-order buys. The six most common traps:

⑪ Buying Armadyl before you own a Blowpipe

Armor adds a small flat bonus. A weak weapon caps your DPS no matter how good your chest is. Blowpipe first, always.

⑫ Cheap bolts at the boss

A Rune Crossbow hits dramatically harder with dragon bolts (or diamond (e) bolts) than with rune bolts. The bolt switch is often a bigger upgrade than the bow itself.

⑬ Skipping Rigour

Rigour is a free ~20% ranged boost from prayer. It out-values most single gear pieces and costs nothing but 74 Prayer. Turn it on before you spend a coin on armor.

⑭ The Crystal Helm greed

The crystal helm is technically best-in-slot, but the gap to an Armadyl helm is tiny relative to the grind. Skip it until everything else is finished.

⑮ The Venator Bow trap

Venator is brilliant at Akkha, Araxxor, and Chamber of Xeric, but near-useless in single-target. Do not buy it as your only bow — it does not replace the Blowpipe or Twisted Bow.

⑯ Twisting the Bow too early

The Twisted Bow scales with the enemy's Hitpoints. At low-HP slayer tasks it underperforms a Blowpipe badly. Save the 900M until you actually fight high-HP bosses.

10. Ranged Gear Progression FAQs

Q: Is the Twisted Bow worth it if I already have a blowpipe?

Yes, but only for high-magic-content. Against the Theatre of Blood, Tombs of Amascut, and similar bosses the Twisted Bow's scaling makes it the best weapon in the game. Against low-magic Slayer and most mid-game bosses the blowpipe is still competitive, so keep both.

Q: What is the single best first upgrade after the rune crossbow?

The Toxic Blowpipe. It is a larger, cheaper, and more versatile DPS jump than any armor piece and it carries you through the entire mid game. Buy it before Armadyl.

Q: Do I need Rigour to raid?

Practically yes. Rigour is expected at every high-level raid and it adds more DPS than most gear swaps. Unlock it (70 Prayer, a few quests) before you start chasing bis armor.

Q: Armadyl or Masori?

Armadyl is the standard bis body for general ranged DPS. Masori (fortunate) trades a little defence for more damage but is much rarer and more expensive. For 99 percent of players Armadyl is the answer; Masori is a min-max flex.

Q: Can an Ironman get full ranged bis without the Grand Exchange?

Yes. Blowpipe (Zulrah), Armadyl (Kree'arra / CoX), Anguish (CoX), Pegasian (Cerberus / CoX), assembler (CoX), and even the Twisted Bow (CoX) are all self-obtainable. Ranged is the most Ironman-friendly max-combat style.

Q: What ammo should I use with the blowpipe?

Dragon darts are bis. Addfang or scales are cheap to maintain via Zulrah. Do not waste money on ammo upgrades that barely move the needle until your weapon and armor are settled.

Q: Is the max cape worth it over Ava's assembler?

No for ranged. Ava's assembler is bis cape for ranged and also saves darts. The max cape is a defence and niche item, not a ranged damage upgrade, so keep the assembler equipped.

Q: How much does a near-bis ranged kit cost on the Grand Exchange?

A blowpipe + Armadyl + Anguish + pegasian + archers ring (i) + assembler kit typically runs in the low tens of millions of gold, while the Twisted Bow alone is several hundred million. The mid-game core is affordable; the end-game bow is the only true gold sink.
