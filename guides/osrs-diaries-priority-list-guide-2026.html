OSRS Diaries Priority List Guide 2026 — Which Achievement Diaries to Rush First

Published September 5, 2026  |  15 min read  |  By Yanchun Wu

There are 12 Achievement Diaries in Old School RuneScape, each with Easy, Medium, Hard and Elite tiers — 492 tasks in total. You will eventually want every reward, but the order you chase them decides how much free teleport access, passive GP and skilling efficiency you enjoy along the way. This guide ranks all 12 diaries by actual reward value, explains the Elite and Hard perks that matter most for daily play, and gives you a separate, no-nonsense priority order for Ironmen.

1. What Achievement Diaries Are (and Why Order Matters)

Achievement Diaries are sets of small, region-locked tasks you complete for a permanent reward from that area's taskmaster. Every one of the 12 regions splits into four tiers — Easy, Medium, Hard and Elite — and you must finish each lower tier before you can claim the next one's reward. Across all regions that is 492 tasks and 48 claimable rewards.

The tiers scale hard with your account. Easy tasks ask for skills up to level 40 (50 for Ironmen); Medium up to 65; Hard up to 75; Elite pushes into the 90s. That is exactly why the order you pursue them changes your day-to-day game so much: the Easy and Medium rewards are nearly free and you should grab them on autopilot, while the Hard and Elite perks — doubled resources, free teleports, passive income — are the things that save you hours every single week once unlocked.

Finish all 12 Elite diaries and you can buy the Achievement Diary cape from Twiggy O'Korn in Draynor Village for 99,000 coins. It is one of the most useful capes in the game because it lets you teleport to any completed diary taskmaster, but it is a long-term goal, not a starting point. This guide is about the road to it — specifically, which diary to do next.

💡 The core mindset

Diary rewards are permanent, account-wide buffs. Unlike a quest's one-time XP, a free teleport or a double-resource perk pays you back forever. The priority list below is simply "which permanent buff helps my account most, soonest."

3. How to Rank a Diary — What Makes a Reward Worth Grinding

Before the ranked list, here is the scoring logic so you can re-prioritise for your own account. Four factors decide a diary's place:

🎯 The Four Scoring Factors

  • Teleport value: does it unlock a teleport you will use weekly? Free, repeatable teleports beat one-time XP every time.
  • Passive GP: does it print money or save a repeatable cost (battlestaves, noted loot, double resources)?
  • Skilling efficiency: does it double a resource, speed up a furnace, or cut travel on content you already do?
  • Requirement cost: a low-skill Elite you can finish at 83 Slayer outranks one needing 96 Fishing. Cheap-to-get perks rank higher.

A diary can be "S-tier" for one account and "B-tier" for another. A pure Slayer account cares far more about Karamja (Duradel teleport) and Kourend (Konar synergy) than about Varrock mining perks. The list below is the generalist ranking; the Ironman section tunes it for self-sufficiency.

💡 Don't sleep on Easy and Medium

The Lumbridge & Draynor Medium Explorer's Ring 2 alone gives 30 free High Alchemy casts per day and run-energy restores — a permanent, account-wide QoL unlock for roughly an hour of tasks. Do every Easy and Medium diary during normal play.

4. S-Tier Diaries — The Ones You Rush

These four deliver the biggest, most universal, lowest-effort payoffs. If you do nothing else this month, do these.

① Ardougne — the teleport king

The Ardougne Cloak is the single best early teleport item in the game. From the first tier it teleports you to Ardougne, and when worn it also grants a free teleport to the Ardougne Monastery — which sits next to a fairy ring and a Spirit Tree, handing you the entire fast-travel network for almost no effort. The Elite (Cloak 4) adds a flat +10% pickpocket success chance everywhere, which is a massive Thieving/training and seed-farming multiplier. Highest requirement is only 94 Magic (boostable). Do Ardougne first, always.

② Lumbridge & Draynor — free alchs and infinite run

The Explorer's Ring is the quiet MVP. The Medium tier gives 30 free High Alchemy casts per day and run-energy restores; the Elite (Ring 4) tops out at 30 free High Alchemy casts per day with no runes at all plus an unlimited run-energy restore at the Lumbridge church. That is permanent, repeatable save on nature runes and stamina potions. Crucially, its Elite requirement is the lowest of all 12 (88 Smithing, boostable), so it is often your first Elite completed. Highest-value-per-hour, full stop.

③ Varrock — the daily GP printer

Varrock Armour 4 (Elite) lets you buy 120 battlestaves from Zaff every day for 7,000 coins each — a steady, resellable stack of value, plus a 10% chance to mine and smelt double ores/bars. The Medium tier also gives a chance at double ore and a Grand Exchange teleport toggle. For any account that touches the GE daily, Varrock is a top-three rush. Highest requirement: 95 Cooking (boostable).

④ Morytania — the Barrows multiplier

Morytania Legs 4 (Elite) grant 50% more runes from every Barrows chest — directly doubling your most reliable mid-game money rune income — plus a bonecrusher-style effect at the Slayer Tower and banking access at the Ectofuntus. If you farm Barrows or train Prayer through the Ectofuntus, this Elite is transformative. The catch is the 96 Fishing requirement (boostable), so it lands later — but it is never low-priority. The Hard tier already gives the 50% Barrows rune bonus on the way up.

5. A/B-Tier Diaries — Strong, But Goal-Dependent

These deliver excellent perks, but their value depends on the content you actually play. Rank them up or down for your own goals.

① Falador — the Mole farmer (Hard tier shines)

The Falador Shield 3 (Hard) is a sleeper hit: it gives a Giant Mole locator and automatically notes mole skins and claws, turning an annoying boss into a clean profit farm. The Elite (Shield 4) recharges your bonecrusher necklace daily and keeps the Falador tree patch from ever becoming diseased. Strong for farmers and skillers; less urgent for pure bossers.

② Karamja — the Slayer convenience (Elite)

Karamja Gloves 4 (Elite) grant a free teleport straight to Duradel (the best Slayer master), double tokkul from TzHaar fights, and access to the underground Shilo Village gem rocks. If Slayer is your main activity, Karamja jumps to S-tier. Otherwise it waits behind its 91 Runecraft requirement.

③ Kourend & Kebos — the self-sufficiency slot (Elite)

Rada's Blessing 4 (Elite) occupies your ammunition slot with no penalty, gives a +2 Prayer bonus, and grants a 10% chance to catch an extra fish anywhere in Gielinor — a quiet, account-wide fishing boost. It also unlocks the Lizardman Temple shortcut at 70 Agility for dragon warhammer farming. Excellent for bossers and skillers alike; its 95 Slayer requirement keeps it endgame.

④ Kandarin — the DPS and Agility perk (Hard)

Kandarin Headgear 3 (Hard) gives a +10% chance for bolt special attacks to proc (huge with Ruby Bolts (e)), a 10% boost to Marks of Grace on the Seers' Village course, and a Seers' teleport closer to the bank. The Elite headgear adds flax-to-bowstring efficiency. A great pickup for rangers and Agility trainers once you hit 90 Smithing.

⑤ Desert — the Nardah hub (Elite)

Desert Amulet 4 (Elite) gives unlimited Nardah teleports — each visit fully restores HP, Prayer and Special Attack — plus an 86 Agility shortcut into the Kalphite Lair. The Hard tier permanently attaches the Kalphite Lair ropes. Indispensable for Kalphite Queen farming and any desert bossing loop.

⑥ Fremennik — the Vorkath taxi (Elite)

Fremennik Sea Boots 4 (Elite) teleport you right by the Rellekka docks for fast Vorkath trips, note Dagannoth King bones, give a free lyre teleport, and deliver daily noted Adamant/Rune ore. With its 83 Slayer requirement it is one of the cheapest Elites to finish — a strong early Elite once you Slayer.

⑦ Western Provinces — the Zulrah unlock (Elite)

Western Banner 4 (Elite) grants unlimited Zulrah teleports and a daily Crystal impling spawn. If Zulrah is in your rotation, this is an S-tier diary for you. The 93 Slayer requirement is not boostable, so plan the levels.

⑧ Wilderness — the obelisk control (Elite)

Wilderness Sword 4 (Elite) lets you choose your destination on any Wilderness obelisk (no more random bounces) and teleports to the Edgeville Dungeon. Powerful for chinning and Wilderness content, but the 96 Magic requirement and PvP risk make it a last-priority Elite for most accounts.

6. The Ironman Priority Order

Ironmen cannot buy their way past anything, so the priority math shifts: self-sufficiency and resource multipliers beat pure teleports. Do every Hard diary as early as you can (the perks cost less than the Elites and pay off immediately), then chase the Elites that feed your own supplies.

⚒️ Ironman Diary Roadmap

  • Phase 1 — all Easy + Medium: same as every account; the Lumbridge ring and Varrock teleport are free QoL you cannot buy.
  • Phase 2 — all Hard diaries: Morytania Hard (Barrows runes), Fremennik Hard (noted DK bones), Kandarin Hard (bolt procs + Marks of Grace), Falador Hard (noted Mole loot), Wilderness Hard (noted dragon bones). These are your supply pipelines.
  • Phase 3 — Morytania Elite: 50% more Barrows runes is the single biggest Ironman unlock — it directly multiplies your rune, bolt and seed self-supply.
  • Phase 4 — Ardougne Elite: +10% pickpocket success supercharges seed and GP farming you cannot buy on the GE.
  • Phase 5 — Karamja & Kourend Elites: Duradel teleport + gem rocks, then Rada's Blessing fishing boost and Lizardman shortcut.
  • Phase 6 — the rest: Desert, Western, Fremennik, Lumbridge, Falador and Wilderness Elites as their 90s requirements land.

The throughline: an Ironman values anything that creates a resource it cannot trade for. Barrows runes (Morytania), noted bones (Fremennik/Wilderness), battlestaves it still buys from Zaff (Varrock), and fish (Kourend) all rank above teleports it could otherwise reach by foot. Varrock Armour 4's 120 daily battlestaves are still buyable from Zaff on an Ironman — a rare "free" tradeable supply worth grabbing the moment the diary is done.

💡 Ironman boost discipline

Most Elite requirements are boostable. A spicy stew (+5) or a skill potion lets an Ironman finish a diary a few levels early — useful when you are 1-4 levels short and the reward is a supply multiplier you want today.

Here is the generalist order that maximises reward-per-hour from a fresh-ish account to the diary cape.

Two rules of thumb close the loop: (1) always finish a region's lower tiers while you are in the area for the higher one — you cannot claim Elite without Easy/Medium/Hard done anyway; (2) let the wiki's diary tracker tell you which outstanding task is blocking a reward, then batch those tasks by region to save travel.

✅ You are prioritising well if…

  • You grabbed Ardougne, Lumbridge and Varrock early and use their perks weekly
  • Your Hard diaries are done in regions you farm (Morytania, Fremennik, Falador)
  • You finish a cheap Elite (Lumbridge/Fremennik) the moment its levels land

❌ You are leaving value on the table if…

  • You skip Easy/Medium diaries "because they are low tier" — the Lumbridge ring alone is worth it
  • You sit on 90s stats but never claim Morytania or Wilderness Elite for the multipliers
  • You buy nature runes for High Alchemy while owning an Explorer's Ring 4

8. Diaries Priority FAQs

Q: Which OSRS diary should I do first?

Ardougne. The Ardougne Cloak teleports you next to a fairy ring and a Spirit Tree, unlocking near-total fast travel for minutes of easy tasks. It is the highest-value, lowest-effort diary in the game and accelerates every other diary you complete after it.

Q: How many Achievement Diaries are there in OSRS?

There are 12 regions with diaries — Ardougne, Desert, Falador, Fremennik, Kandarin, Karamja, Kourend & Kebos, Lumbridge & Draynor, Morytania, Varrock, Western Provinces and Wilderness. Each has four tiers, for 48 rewards across 492 tasks total.

Q: What is the best diary reward for passive GP?

Varrock Armour 4 (Elite) is the cleanest GP engine: 120 battlestaves per day from Zaff at 7,000 coins each, resellable for profit. Morytania Legs 4 (50% more Barrows runes) and Falador Shield 3 (noted Giant Mole loot) are the other top money-makers.

Q: Which Elite diary has the lowest requirements?

Lumbridge & Draynor Elite, at 88 Smithing (boostable) — the cheapest Elite to finish, and it grants 30 free High Alchemy casts per day plus unlimited run-energy restore. Fremennik Elite (83 Slayer, boostable) is the lowest-skill Slayer-gated Elite.

Q: What is the best diary order for an Ironman?

All Easy and Medium first, then every Hard diary (Morytania, Fremennik, Kandarin, Falador, Wilderness) for supply pipelines, then Morytania Elite (Barrows runes), Ardougne Elite (pickpocketing), and Karamja/Kourend Elites. Ironmen prioritise resource multipliers over teleports.

Q: Do I need to complete diaries in order (Easy to Elite)?

You can complete tasks in any order, but you cannot claim a tier's reward until all lower tiers of that region are finished. So you will always claim Easy → Medium → Hard → Elite in sequence, even if you finished some Elite tasks early.

Q: Is the Achievement Diary cape worth getting?

Yes for efficiency-minded accounts. At 99,000 coins from Twiggy O'Korn in Draynor, it teleports you to any completed diary taskmaster and signals a fully completed diary log. It is a long-term goal requiring all 12 Elite diaries, not an early priority.

Q: Are diary requirements boostable for Ironmen?

Most are. Almost every Elite diary's headline skill is boostable (spicy stew, skill potion, or god banner), so an Ironman a few levels short can still finish. The exceptions include Western Provinces Elite (93 Slayer, not boostable) and a few specific individual tasks — check the wiki per task.
