1. ⚡ Overview & Why Runecrafting Got Good in 2026

Runecrafting is a Members skill where you carry Pure essence to altars and craft runes. It is a slow, click-heavy grind — but three things changed the calculus: the Guardians of the Rift minigame (free, engaging, unlocks the Raiments of the Eye outfit), Daeyalt essence (1.5× XP per essence), and the fact that high-level runes like Blood and Wrath are genuinely profitable. You no longer have to choose between "fast but bankrupt" and "cheap but miserable."

🎯 The Two Numbers That Matter

  • XP/hr — how fast you level. Lava runes win; GOTR and ZMI are close behind; Blood runes are slow but AFK.
  • GP/hr — Lava runes roughly break even, ZMI can profit at high levels, and Blood/Daeyalt/Wrath are clear money-makers.
💡 Raiments of the Eye Are the Keystone

Every high-level RC method is massively better with the Raiments of the Eye outfit (+60% extra runes, from GOTR) and the Colossal Pouch (85 RC, also from GOTR). Do GOTR to 77 first, then switch to Blood runes with Daeyalt for both profit and speed. This single decision cuts your 77–99 time roughly in half versus Blood runes alone.

6. 🔥 GOTR vs ZMI vs Bloods — Which Path?

🅰️ You want 99 as fast as possible

Lava runes from 23–99. Highest XP/hr (65–72K) but very click-intensive and a net ~8–9M GP to 99. Use Magic Imbue + Binding Necklace + Ring of Dueling. Not profitable, but the clock is shortest.

🅱️ You want the best balance (recommended)

GOTR to 77, then Blood + Daeyalt to 99. GOTR is free and gives you the Raiments of the Eye (+60% runes) and Colossal Pouch — both mandatory for efficient Blood runes. From 77 you both level and profit. This is the 2026 standard route.

🅲️ You want maximum GP

Blood + Daeyalt (77+) or Wrath (95+). Wrath runes (~410 GP each, DS2 required) and Daeyalt Blood runes are the top earners. Slower XP than lava, but you bank millions on the way to 99.

💡 Where ZMI fits

ZMI (Ourania) is the classic "no quest, just run" method. It is fine, but it is not the fastest (lava beats it) and not the most profitable (bloods beat it). Use ZMI only if you dislike GOTR or want a pure-run method before 27. Most players are better served by GOTR.

7. ⚠️ Common Mistakes (Myth-Busting)

❌ Myth 1: "ZMI is the fastest Runecrafting method."

Wrong. ZMI is ~45–55K XP/hr. Lava runes are faster (65–72K), and Blood + Daeyalt at 77+ beats both for XP/hour while also profiting. ZMI is a middle-ground, not the meta.

❌ Myth 2: "You need a Binding Necklace only for high levels."

Wrong. Any combination rune (Lava, Mud, Steam, etc.) has only a 50% success rate without a Binding Necklace. With it, 100%. On Lava runes that saves roughly half your Pure essence over 99 — about 5M GP. Always wear one.

❌ Myth 3: "Daeyalt essence is Ironman-only."

Wrong. Daeyalt gives 1.5× XP per essence and is the backbone of the best profit method (Blood runes at 77+). Any main account with Sins of the Father should mine it in Darkmeyer.

❌ Myth 4: "Runecrafting is always a money sink."

Outdated. From 77 (Blood) and 95 (Wrath) Runecrafting is a top GP skill — 800K–1.2M GP/hr. The "expensive grind" reputation is from the pre-Daeyalt era.

❌ Myth 5: "Skip GOTR, go straight to Lava."

You can, but you forfeit the Raiments of the Eye (+60% runes) and Colossal Pouch. Those two unlocks make every later method dramatically better. GOTR to 77 first pays for itself many times over.

8. ❓ FAQ

What is the fastest way to 99 Runecrafting?

Lava runes from 23–99 at 65–72K XP/hr, for roughly 150–200 hours total (including the early GOTR/quest levels). It is the highest XP/hr but costs ~8–9M GP net and is very click-intensive.

Is Guardians of the Rift worth it?

Yes — for almost everyone. It is free, engaging, and unlocks the Raiments of the Eye (+60% runes everywhere) and the Colossal Pouch. Do it to at least 77 before switching to profitable runes.

Do I need Daeyalt essence?

For the best 77–99 (Blood runes), yes. Daeyalt gives 1.5× XP per essence and is mined in Darkmeyer after Sins of the Father. Without it, Blood runes are slower (35–40K XP/hr) but still profitable.

How much does 1-99 Runecrafting cost?

Fastest (Lava): ~8–9M GP net. GOTR + Blood route: free to 77, then profitable. With Daeyalt you can finish 77–99 at a profit. Use the calculator in Section 5 for your exact level.

What level should I be before Blood runes?

77 Runecrafting and 100% Arceuus favour. You also want the Raiments of the Eye and a Colossal Pouch. Daeyalt essence (from Darkmeyer) is strongly recommended.

9. 🏁 Final Tips

  • Do GOTR first: the Raiments of the Eye (+60% runes) and Colossal Pouch are the foundation of every fast/profit method.
  • Bind that necklace: a Binding Necklace is non-negotiable for Lava runes — it halves your essence cost to 99.
  • Daeyalt at 77+: mine it in Darkmeyer; 1.5× XP turns Blood runes into both fast and profitable training.
  • Watch GE prices: Blood (~3.4K) and Wrath (~410) values fluctuate — verify before a long grind.
  • Next skill: the runes you make feed Magic 1-99 training (bursting/barraging) — your natural下一步.
