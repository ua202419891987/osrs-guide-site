1. 🔒 Why Late-Game Supplies Are the Real Ironman Boss

Early Ironman is hard because you lack stats. Mid-game is hard because you lack gear. But late game quietly becomes hard because you run out of consumables — and there is no shop to fix it. A maxed main can restock 500 brews in two minutes. You restock 500 brews by farming herbs for two weeks.

This is the single most common late-game Ironman complaint: "I ran out of brews mid-raid and now I'm stuck." The fix is to treat supplies as a production line, not an afterthought.

② 📊 The Three Supply Buckets

Split every consumable into one of three buckets so you know where the bottleneck lives:

BucketExamplesMain SourceRefill Speed
Combat ConsumablesBrews, prayer potions, food, restoresHerb/fishing/hunter farmsSlow (days)
Magic ConsumablesRunes, elemental spells, death/chaosRunecrafting, LMS, slayerMedium
Build / Boss SuppliesPlanks, mahogany, boss mats, fragsConstruction, COX/TOB, PvMSlow–Medium

③ 🎯 The Core Mindset: Stockpile Before You Spend

The biggest mistake late-game Ironmen make is bossing before they have a buffer. You should aim to keep a standing reserve so one dry streak never stops you:

🛡️ Suggested Standing Reserves (Lategame Iron)

  • 500+ Saradomin brews (4-dose) before heavy COX/TOB
  • 300+ prayer potions (or super restores) at all times
  • 1,000+ food (dark crabs / anglerfish mix)
  • 50k+ runes of your main spell type
  • 10k+ planks banked for construction grinds
💡 The Golden Rule of Ironman Supply

Never let a consumable hit zero before you start producing it. The moment you use your last 100 brews, the refill clock already started too late. Always farm while you still have a buffer, so the production line never stops.

④ 🚫 Beginner Supply Mistakes to Avoid

Even players who understand the "production line" idea still stall because of a few predictable habits. Watch for these four:

  • Banking supplies "for later": Hoarding 5,000 brews but never bossing means your effort isn't converting into progression. Supplies exist to be spent — use them to push content, then refill the buffer afterwards.
  • Ignoring Miscellania: It is the single most passive supply source in the game and costs almost no attention. Players who skip it regularly hit construction and herb walls that a maintained kingdom would have prevented.
  • Over-specializing too early: A brand-new Ironman who only farms herbs while ignoring food and runes will still stall at the raid. Balance the three buckets from day one instead of maxing one and neglecting the others.
  • Quitting the herb run because "prices are low": You don't sell herbs — you brew them. Grand Exchange price is irrelevant to a self-sufficient Iron; consistency matters far more than any single yield number.

The through-line is simple: treat supply as a habit, not a project. Fifteen minutes of farming today prevents a two-week stall next month, and the players who never fall behind are the ones who never stopped the production line.

2. 🧪 Brews & Prayer Potions: Farming Your Own Herbs

Herblore is the heart of Ironman self-sufficiency. Brews and prayer potions are the two items you will burn through fastest in end-game PvM, and both come from farmed herbs. Get this pipeline running early and never worry again.

② 🌱 The Daily Herb Run (Your Brew Pipeline)

A full herb run across all patches (with magic secateurs and ultracompost) is the backbone of supply. Prioritize the herbs that make your actual potions:

HerbMakesUsed ForNotes
Ranarr weedPrayer potionEvery boss tripPlant from seed; ~3.4 avg yield/patch
ToadflaxSaradomin brewTank builds, raidsBest brew herb per seed cost
SnapdragonSuper restoreLong trips, TOBAlso restore prayer + stats
AvantoeSuper combatMelee dps buffOptional but nice
KwuarmSuper antipoisonCOX / VorkathNeeded for venom/serp

① Build the 9-Patch Route

Hit all patches: Miscellania, Ardougne, Falador, Catherby, Port Phasmatys, Trollheim, Hosidius, Weiss, Harmony Island. Use Teleport to House (Fairy ring to Harmony), Skills necklace for Fishing Guild/Catherby, and Explorer's ring for Falador. One full run takes ~6 minutes and yields 25–40 herbs.

② Always Ultracompost + Attas Seed

Ultracompost nearly doubles yield vs compost. The Attas seed (Farming Guild) further boosts herb yield by ~20% while planted. With both, a Ranarr patch averages ~5 herbs instead of ~3 — that is the difference between a trickle and a flood.

③ ⚗️ Turning Herbs Into Brews & Potions

You need clean herbs + secondary ingredients + vials of water. The secondaries are where Ironmen get stuck, so plan them:

PotionHerbSecondaryHow an Iron Gets the Secondary
Saradomin brewToadflaxCrushed nestBird nests from woodcutting / Miscellania
Prayer potionRanarrSnape grassCollect on Waterbirth / Hobgoblin Isle
Super restoreSnapdragonRed spiders' eggsCatacombs / Slayer tower / Edgeville
Super combatAvantoeSuper attack+def+strMake the three supers first (torstol etc.)
💡 Vials Matter

Vials of water come from packing vials from empty vials at a water source (or buy empty vials with token — but you can't, you're Iron). Use the Humidify spell (Lunar) or fill at a well. Banking 10k empty vials early saves hours later. Also: do the Family Crest quest for the gauntlets swap if you mass alch — unrelated but frees herb-brew time.

Net result: a disciplined daily herb run produces roughly 1,000–1,500 toadflax per month → that is 1,000+ brews. Enough to sustain near-constant raid farming.

3. 🍖 Food Bottlenecks: Hunter, Fishing & Farming

Food is the silent supply drain. A TOB learner can eat 600+ food in a weekend. You cannot buy it, so you must produce it from Hunter, Fishing and Farming.

② 🦀 Best Ironman Foods Ranked

FoodHealSourceBest For
Dark crab27 (30 w/ bowl)Hunter (wilderness crabs)Best raw heal; wilderness risk
Anglerfishup to 22Fishing (LV 82, Piscarilius)Safe, bankable, raid food
Shark20Fishing (LV 76)Bulk filler food
Manta ray22Fishing (LV 81)Step up from shark
Karambwan18 + comboFishing (LV 65)Combo-eat with brews

③ 🎣 Setting Up Passive Food Production

① Dark Crabs (Best Heal, Best Effort)

At level 85 Hunter, catch dark crabs in the Wilderness (level 50+). They heal 27 each (30 in a bowl) — the highest reliable food in the game. Risk: PKers. Mitigation: bring only what you can lose, use a roaming teleport, and consider world 318 (lower risk off-peak). Many Ironmen keep 3–5k dark crabs banked.

② Anglerfish (Safe Bulk)

Anglerfish (Fishing 82, Piscarilius) need Anglerfishes from sandworms + fish from the fishing spot. They heal up to 22 and are the safest raid food because the spot is non-wilderness. Cook them on a range (Hosidius kitchen with 100% Hosidius favour = no burn) to avoid waste.

③ Farming: Fruit Trees & Bushes

Don't sleep on palm trees (coconut), papaya, and calquat for incidental food, plus sweetcorn / potatoes for cheap filler. A full fruit-tree run gives 7+ food items per tree per day essentially free. It is not your main line but it tops off the bank.

🎯 Food Combo Strategy

In raids, you rarely eat food alone — you combo-eat a brew then a karambwan/angler. So stock brews + karambwan together. Karambwan (Fishing 65, Karambwanji from Karamja) do not stack with other food in the same tick, which is exactly why they pair perfectly with brews. Keep ~500 karambwan banked.

4. 🔮 Runes & Magic Consumables: Never Buy, Always Make

Every spell — teleports, alchs, barrages, blood spells — eats runes. Ironmen source runes from three pipelines: Runecrafting, LMS, and Slayer.

② 🌀 Runecrafting: Your Rune Factory

At high Runecrafting you can make your own Blood, Soul, Wrath, and combination runes — the ones you actually burn in PvM:

Runes You NeedBest SourceIronman Note
Blood runesBlood altar (RC 77) / LMSEssential for blood barrage & Vorkath
Death runesDeath altar (RC 65) / slayerFor ancients & alchs
Nature runesNature altar (RC 44)High alch income = more supplies
Astral / Law / CosmicOurania / LMSTeleport fuel

① Ourania Altar (Low Effort, All Runes)

The Ourania Altar lets you craft a random rune from pure essence at any RC level. It is the laziest way to build a mixed rune pile. Pair with abyssal bracelet and a staff of the dead / rune pouch. Expect a few thousand assorted runes per hour.

③ 🏆 LMS & Slayer: Free Rune Drops

🎯 Where "Free" Runes Come From

Last Man Standing (LMS): Spend points on blood, death, and soul rune packs. A few LMS games per week keeps your ancients topped up with zero RC grinding.

Slayer: Tasks from Duradel / Konar drop runes constantly — especially from nechryael, spiritual creatures, and dust devils. Over a long slayer grind you accumulate 10k+ of each common rune passively.

Barrage training: Bursting/barraging Slayer tasks (nechryael, dust devils, maniacal monkeys) is the classic dual-purpose move — you train Magic and burn the runes you just crafted, while banking drops. Net cost is near zero.

💡 Rune Pouch & Staves

Always carry a rune pouch (Abyss miniquest) and the relevant staff (staff of fire for alchs, staff of water for teleports). This cuts inventory rune slots dramatically so you can carry more brews and food per trip.

5. 🏗️ Construction Materials & Boss Supplies

Two late-game drains don't fit the "potion/food/rune" buckets: construction materials (for POH, ports, and content unlocks) and boss-specific supplies (frags, scales, cores).

② 🪵 Construction: Planks From What You Have

MaterialUseIronman Source
PlankPOH rooms, furnitureSawmill (pay with GP you alch)
Mahogany plankHigh-end POHMahogany trees (VF,tree patch) → sawmill
Steel bar / limestoneMounted x-erc & altarsSmithing + mining
Gold leaf / marbleThrone, portal nexusGrand Exchange — BLOCKED for Iron

The Ironman catch: gold leaf, marble blocks and magic stone are normally bought on the GE — you cannot. You get them from temparl / shilo / kingdom (Miscellania) and from PvM drops (e.g., bounty, clue scrolls, and certain bosses). This is why a healthy Miscellania kingdom (25k+ coins/day, set to wood/stone) quietly supplies construction mats for months. Raising Kingdom favour to 100% and collecting daily is non-negotiable for late-game Ironmen.

③ 💎 Boss Supplies & Fragments

① Revenant / Wilderness Boss Fragments

Items like ancestral, pegs, and voidwaker components come from the Wilderness. Plan a dedicated wildcard trips setup and bank the fragments. Because you can't trade for the missing piece, you must farm the specific drop yourself — patience is the supply.

② Raid Supplies (COX / TOB / TOA)

Each raid consumes different mats: COX needs stamina/super restores + brews; TOB needs massive food + brews + restores; TOA needs brews, restores, and invocation-scaled supplies. The good news: raids drop the herbs, seeds, and secondaries you need to refill. Raid often enough and your supply line becomes self-funding.

③ Zulrah / Vorkath Scales & Bones

Zulrah drops scales (for scales on ranged gear and stamina) and herbs; Vorkath drops dragon bones + hides + alot of herbs. These bosses are your "supply printers" — killing them both progresses gear and refills your Herblore bank.

🎯 Miscellania Is a Supply Engine

  • Set workers to wood (mahogany) + fish + herbs for construction + food
  • Collect daily; never let favor drop below 75%
  • Over a year this yields thousands of planks and herbs you never "farmed" actively
  • This is the closest thing an Ironman has to passive income

6. ⚔️ Armor & Gear From Content (CG, Slayer, Fragments)

"Supplies" is not only consumables — gear is a supply too, because you can't buy replacements. Late-game armor comes from specific content, and running dry means a gear bottleneck.

② 🏹 The Gauntlet / Corrupted Gauntlet (CG)

The Corrupted Gauntlet is the premier Ironman armor source:

RewardDropWhy You Want It
Crystal armour setCG (enhanced)Best ranged armour pre-raid
Bow of faerdhinenCG (enhanced)Top-tier ranged weapon
Crystal helm/legs/bodyCGTier 70–80 ranged, repairable
Enhanced weapon seedCG (rare)Upgrades the bow

Strategy: Farm normal Gauntlet until the routine is automatic, then push Corrupted. Each kill gives a weapon seed / armour seed / crystal shard. You need multiple shards + seeds for a full set, so CG becomes a long but reliable gear-production line.

③ 🗡️ Slayer, Frags & Other Gear Drops

🎯 Gear That Drops, Not Bought

  • Slayer: Abyssal whip, warrior rings, black mask, NOX/ANC pieces from spiritual creatures — all from tasks
  • Fragments: Ancestral, pegs, scythe pieces from Wilderness bosses — farm the specific boss
  • Barrows: Tank gear (Karil's, Dharok's, Guthan's) — Guthan's set is itself a food-replacement sustain for some content
  • Clue scrolls: Ranged gear (armadyl), god books, and supplies — stack clues while bossing
  • COX: Twisted bow, ancestral, Scythe of Vitur — the end-game gear printer that also refills supplies
💡 Guthan's = Free Food

The Guthan's set (Barrows) heals you on hit. For some slayer tasks and low-intensity content it replaces food entirely — effectively a "supply" you wear. Worth grinding early as a sustain multiplier.

7. 🤝 GIM Cooperation & When to De-Bronze

Group Ironman (GIM) changes the supply math completely: you can share with groupmates. A well-organized GIM is the fastest supply path in the game. But there is a catch — de-bronzing.

② 🤝 The GIM Supply Multiplier

What You ShareHow It Helps
Herbs & secondariesOne farmer feeds the whole group's brews
Food (dark crabs, angler)Split a big catch across members
Boss drops / gearNeed-only loot; one Twisted bow helps the group
Construction matsCombine Miscellania + kingdom efforts

① Divide Labor by Strength

In a GIM, don't all farm herbs. One player specializes in Herblore/farming, another in hunter/fishing food, another in runecrafting/LMS. The group bank becomes a shared supply line — dramatically faster than any solo Iron.

③ 🥉 When to De-Bronze (Leave the Group)

De-bronzing converts your bronze GIM into a regular Ironman (you keep items but lose group trading). It is irreversible-ish (you can re-group only with a fresh group later). Consider de-bronzing when:

🎯 De-Bronze Decision Checklist

  • Group is inactive: Mates quit and you can't trade — you're effectively solo anyway
  • You hit a group bottleneck: A needed item depends on a mate who won't play
  • You want solo CG/raid pace: Group scheduling slows your personal progression
  • Supply sharing stopped helping: You out-produce the group and don't need it

Don't de-bronze if: your group is active, shares well, and you still rely on others for herbs/food. The supply multiplier usually beats solo until you're truly end-game self-sufficient.

💡 Hardcore & De-Iron

If you ever de-iron (turn main), you lose the challenge entirely — that is a separate, bigger decision. De-bronzing keeps you Iron, just solo. Weigh it carefully: the GIM supply engine is the strongest tool an Ironman has, so only leave when it has stopped working for you.

8. Ironman Supply FAQs

I keep running out of brews in the middle of TOB. What do I do?

You are bossing without a buffer. Stop raiding and run a focused herb cycle: plant toadflax across all 9 patches with ultracompost + Attas, collect snape grass and crushed nests, and brew in bulk. Aim for 500+ 4-dose brews banked before returning. Better: set a rule that you only raid while brews > 200, so the pipeline never empties.

What's the fastest way to get prayer potions as an Ironman?

Farm Ranarr weed (9-patch daily run) and collect snape grass from Waterbirth Island / Hobgoblin Peninsula. Each Ranarr + snape grass = one prayer potion (2-dose). A month of daily runs yields ~1,000+ Ranarr. Alternatively, super restores from Snapdragon are often better because they also restore stats and prayer during long trips.

Where do Ironmen get vials and secondaries if there's no GE?

Vials: buy empty vials from fly fishing shops / pack them with water (Humidify Lunar spell). Crushed nests: bird nests from woodcutting, Miscellania, and implings. Snape grass: collect nodes. Red spiders' eggs: Catacombs of Kourend / Slayer Tower. Most secondaries are collectible nodes or monster drops — never bought.

Is dark crab worth the Wilderness risk for food?

If you can play cautiously (low-pop worlds, roaming teleport, only bring what you can lose), yes — 27–30 heal is the best food in the game and dramatically cuts brew usage in raids. If PKing stresses you, anglerfish (safe, 22 heal) is the better ironman staple and you should mass those instead.

How do I get runes for barraging without Runecrafting grinds?

Three paths: (1) Ourania Altar for mixed runes at any RC level; (2) LMS points spent on blood/death/soul rune packs; (3) Slayer drops from nechryael, dust devils, spiritual creatures. Bursting/barraging those exactly tasks trains Magic while burning the runes you craft — net near-zero cost.

Can GIM really share supplies, and how do we organize it?

Yes — GIM group members trade freely within the group. Organize by specialization: one player runs Herblore/farming for the whole group's brews and potions, another hunts/fishes food, another does runecrafting/LMS. The shared group bank becomes a combined supply line far faster than solo Iron. Just keep Miscellania and kingdom favor high for passive planks/herbs.

When should I de-bronze my GIM?

Only when the group supply engine has stopped helping you: mates are inactive, you hit a group bottleneck you can't clear, or group scheduling slows your personal raids. If your group is active and still shares well, stay — the multiplier usually beats solo until you are fully end-game self-sufficient. De-bronzing is effectively permanent for that group.

How do I get construction materials like gold leaf if I can't use the GE?

Gold leaf, marble blocks and magic stone are normally GE-only, so Ironmen get them from Miscellania kingdom (set workers to wood/stone, 100% favor, collect daily), clue scrolls, and certain PvM drops. This is exactly why a well-maintained kingdom is non-negotiable — it is your passive construction supply for months.

What's the single best supply habit to build first?

The daily 9-patch herb run. It feeds brews, prayer potions, super restores, and super combats — the four consumables you burn in every late-game activity. Fifteen minutes a day, every day, compounds into a supply line that makes every other bottleneck easier. Start it the moment you have Farming ~32 and a decent seed stockpile.
