F2P Weapon Progression (By Attack Level)

Levels 1-10: Bronze/Iron

  • Melee: Bronze Scimitar → Iron Scimitar
  • Ranged: Bronze Crossbow + Bronze bolts
  • Magic: Staff of Air (free, everywhere)

Levels 10-40: Steel/Black

  • Melee: Steel Scimitar → Black Scimitar
  • Ranged: Iron Crossbow + Iron bolts
  • Magic: Staff of Fire (still best F2P option)

Levels 40-60: Mithril/Adamant

  • Melee: Mithril Scimitar → Adamant Scimitar
  • Ranged: Steel Crossbow + Steel bolts
  • Magic: Magic Staff (upgrade from Varrock shop)

Levels 60+: Dragon (Best F2P)

  • Melee: Dragon Scimitar (best F2P weapon, period)
  • Ranged: Rune Crossbow (requires 61 Ranged)
  • Magic: Still Magic Staff (no better F2P staves)

F2P Weapon Money Making (2026)

You can profit from F2P weapons by fletching your own ammo and selling excess gear:

  • Fletch Maplebows: ~200K GP/hr (fletch + string Maplebows, sell to shop or P2P players)
  • Sell Dragon Scimitars: If you get lucky with a drop (very rare), Dragon Scimitar sells for ~60K GP
  • Craft Bronze arrows: ~50K GP/hr (fletch Bronze arrows, sell to shop)

Total profit (F2P): ~50-200K GP/hr depending on method.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can F2P Train Ranged?

Yes! Maple Shortbow (30 Ranged) is free to use (fletch your own Maplebows). Bring Maple logs to the range, fletch into bows, string them (requires String Jewelry spell or balls of wool).

Is Dragon Scimitar Worth It?

YES. It's the best F2P melee weapon by far. If you can afford 60K GP, buy it immediately after hitting 60 Attack. It will last you until you become a Member.

What about Rune 2h Sword?

The Rune 2h Sword has a special attack (Smash) that deals 1.5x damage. Great for finishing off enemies, but Dragon Scimitar has better DPS overall.
