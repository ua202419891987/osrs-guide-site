1. What Is the Activity Adviser?

Adsterra ad slot — paste your Adsterra banner code here

The Activity Adviser is an in-game interface that recommends activities to you based on your current stats, quest progress, and recent play. In short, it answers the question every new and returning player eventually asks: "What should I do next?"

According to the OSRS Wiki, the Activity Adviser is an interface that recommends certain activities to players based on their current stats and quest progress. It first launched on 20 July 2022 as part of an A/B test, then became available to all accounts on 8 September 2022. As of the latest documentation it mainly suggests quests, with skills, minigames, and other categories expected to be added later. — Source: OSRS Wiki / Activity Adviser

① 🟠 How to Open the Activity Adviser

The Adviser is accessed by clicking the orange orb marked with a scroll just below the minimap (the circular map in the top-right of your game screen). If new players tell the Gielinor Guide on Tutorial Island that they are new, a related feature called Adventure Paths is also enabled, giving starter kits and milestone rewards. — Source: OSRS Wiki / Things to do (New player guide)

② 📂 Two Types of Suggestions

When you open the Adviser, content is split into two helpful categories:

🎯 Available Content vs Target Content

  • Available Content: Activities you can do right now because you already meet all the skill and quest requirements.
  • Target Content: Milestone activities you are close to but do not quite have the requirements for yet — great for showing you what to grind toward next.

The Quest section prioritises quests that unlock useful things like Fairy Ring teleports or progression equipment, because one of the most common early-game struggles is simply getting around the map.

③ 🧭 It Complements the Wiki, Not Replaces It

Jagex has stated the Activity Adviser is meant to complement the OSRS Wiki, not replace it. The Adviser highlights activities you might enjoy; the Wiki (and guides like this one) give you the deep detail. Use both together — let the Adviser pick a direction, then read a guide for the step-by-step. — Source: OSRS Wiki / Update: Activity Adviser Phase One

💡 Key Mindset

Suitable activities are based on a number of factors, not just the fastest XP route. The Adviser tries to point you toward content you'll enjoy — not a strict meta path. Play the game the way you like; treat the Adviser as a suggestion engine, not a chore list.

2. How the Activity Adviser Helps New & Returning Players

If you are brand new or coming back after a break, the single biggest wall is not knowing what to do. The Adviser directly attacks that problem.

① 🆕 For New Players: A Built-In Roadmap

New accounts face a maze of prerequisites — a quest locked behind another quest, locked behind a skill level, and so on. The Adviser automatically tallies your levels, quest status, and recent activity to surface quests you can start now and ones you're close to. This is especially valuable for teleport unlocks (Fairy Rings, memoriam teleports, etc.), which save hours of running around.

② 🔄 For Returning Players: Catch-Up Without Panic

Returning players often panic-sell banks or feel lost after months away. The Adviser shows you Target Content — what you're almost ready for — so you can ease back in with a clear, low-stress goal. Pair it with our Coming Back 2026 Guide for the broader "what changed while you were away" picture.

🛡️ Why Returning Players Love It

  • No more guessing which quest unlocks that one teleport you forgot
  • Surfaces quests that give progression gear (e.g., helms, god books, access to areas)
  • Reduces the "I have 1,500 total level and no idea what to grind" feeling

③ 🤝 Feedback Shapes the Tool

Because the Adviser is still being developed, Jagex actively asks for feedback — including a dedicated Q&A livestream and a feedback form. The more new and returning players comment, the better the suggestions get. If you are in the A/B test (see next section), your feedback genuinely influences the final design. — Source: OSRS Wiki / Q&A Summary 21/07/2022

3. The July 20, 2026 A/B Test Changes

The July 20, 2026 weekly game update brought the Activity Adviser back into the spotlight with a fresh round of A/B testing. Here is exactly what the update notes describe.

① 🔔 First-Time Login Pop-Up

Per the July 20 weekly update, players selected to be part of the A/B testing see a pop-up on their next login introducing the Activity Adviser. Importantly, the pop-up goes away automatically if you engage in combat — so it never blocks your gameplay, it just greets you once. — Source: Game Update Notifier / OSRS July 20 Weekly Game Update

⚠️ Note on the Date

The Activity Adviser originally launched as an A/B test on 20 July 2022 (per the OSRS Wiki). The July 20, 2026 weekly update appears to re-run / continue A/B testing and gather fresh feedback. Either way, the mechanic (a login pop-up that vanishes once you fight) is consistent with the original 2022 design. We cite the 2026 weekly update notes for the current test.

② 🧪 What "A/B Testing" Means for You

A/B testing simply means Jagex shows the feature to some players (the "test group") and compares their experience to everyone else. If you are selected, you get early access and a chance to shape the tool. If not, nothing changes for you yet — the Adviser remains available normally via the orange orb.

③ 💬 Share Your Thoughts

The update explicitly invited feedback from players who got early access, noting the team is "very keen to improve the new and returning player experience." A Twitch discussion was scheduled for the Thursday after the update (July 21) at 17:00 BST with the design team. If you were in the test, submitting even one line of feedback helps every future new player. — Source: Game Update Notifier / OSRS July 20 Weekly Game Update

4. PvP Arena Adjustments Explained

The same July 20 update included a set of PvP Arena feedback changes. These matter even for PvM-focused beginners because the Arena is one of the fastest ways to get an Imbue Scroll (more on that below). Here is what changed, with sources.

① 🏹 Bow of Faerdhinen Removed From the Loadout Chest

The update states: "The Bow of Faerdhinen has been removed from the loadout chest. Players have raised concerns about its power being disproportionate to other offerings like the Zaryte Crossbow." The team noted this is a change they'll watch closely and would revert if needed. — Source: Game Update Notifier / OSRS July 20 Weekly Game Update

Why does this make sense? The Bow of Faerdhinen ("Bowfa") is one of the most powerful ranged weapons in the game — the OSRS Wiki notes it provides a +106 Ranged Strength bonus and surpasses the Twisted Bow in raw Ranged Strength, producing its own ammunition. That level of power in a PvP Arena loadout (where everyone starts on equal footing) is exactly the kind of imbalance the change addresses. — Source: OSRS Wiki / Bow of Faerdhinen

🎯 What This Means In Practice

  • You can no longer pick up the Bowfa pre-loaded in the Arena — you'll use more standard Arena loadouts instead.
  • The Arena should feel more balanced between ranged, melee, and magic setups.
  • If you wanted Bowfa power for PvM, that's unchanged — this only affects the Arena loadout chest, not the open world.

② 💎 Imbue Scroll Price Cut to 200 Points

The update notes state the price of Imbue Scrolls was reduced from 500 Reward Points to 200, "so players can obtain more of them for use on the go," with a refund to players who already bought scrolls on their next login. — Source: Game Update Notifier / OSRS July 20 Weekly Game Update

This is independently confirmed by the OSRS Wiki's PvP Arena Rewards page, which lists the Scroll of imbuing at 200 points and records the reduction from 500 to 200 in its change log. So the current, wiki-verified price is 200 PvP Arena points. (Note: the Wiki dates the original 500→200 reduction to the 20 July 2022 update; the July 20, 2026 notes reaffirm the 200-point price and the refund.) — Source: OSRS Wiki / PvP Arena Rewards

💡 Why Imbue Scrolls Matter to Beginners

An Imbue Scroll lets you imbue gear without gaining combat XP — perfect for skillers, pures, and anyone who wants a Slayer Helmet (i) or ring imbue without levelling combat. At 200 points, it's roughly 2–3 hours of Arena matches even if you lose, making it the fastest imbue method in the game.

③ 🔧 Other PvP Arena Tweaks

The July 20 update also shipped several quality-of-life and balance changes to the Arena:

🛠️ Full List of Arena Changes

  • Rune Pouch configure option: You can now set the types of Runes you take into the PvP Arena via the "Configure" option on the Rune Pouch. (The Spellbook still shows spells needing runes from items like the Tome of Fire.)
  • Copy opponent's loadout: A new button lets you copy your opponent's loadout in unranked duels — handy for trying new setups.
  • ELO / Rank Points rebalance: The ELO system was "disproportionately affecting players with larger rank gaps," so Rank Points now scale differently in uneven fights — beating weaker opponents gives fewer points, losing to stronger opponents loses fewer, beating stronger opponents gains more, and losing to weaker opponents loses more.

All of the above are reported in the July 20, 2026 weekly game update notes. — Source: Game Update Notifier / OSRS July 20 Weekly Game Update

5. What This Means for New Players

You might be thinking: "I'm a beginner, why should I care about the Activity Adviser or the PvP Arena?" Here's the practical breakdown.

① 🧭 Less Aimless Grinding

The Adviser turns "I don't know what to do" into "the game is literally telling me three good options." For a new player, that is worth more than any single gear upgrade. It steers you toward teleport-unlocking quests early, which pays off forever in travel time.

② ⚔️ The Arena Is a Beginner-Friendly Imbue Source

Even if you never plan to PvP seriously, the Emir's Arena (PvP Arena) is the fastest way to get an Imbue Scroll. At the new 200-point price, a few casual matches get you a Slayer Helmet (i) or an imbued ring with zero combat XP — a huge boost to your Slayer efficiency. You earn points for participating, wins or losses both count.

③ 🚫 Bowfa Removal = Fairer First Fights

If you do try the Arena, you'll no longer get instantly out-ranged by someone who happened to load a Bowfa. The playing field is more even, which is exactly what a new PvP-curious player wants. The change also signals Jagex is actively tuning the Arena for balance — a good sign if you're thinking about dipping into PvP later.

💡 Don't Overtthink It

If you're a pure PvM player, none of these changes hurt you. The Adviser helps you find content; the Arena changes only affect the minigame; and open-world Bowfa power is untouched. Read on for the one action that actually benefits you: the cheap Imbue Scroll.

6. How to Use These Changes (Action Plan)

Here is a simple, beginner-first checklist to turn this update into progress on your account.

① 🟠 Step 1: Open the Activity Adviser Today

Click the orange scroll orb below your minimap. Skim the Available Content list and pick one quest that unlocks a teleport or useful item. Knock it out, then check Target Content to see what you're close to. Repeat weekly.

② 💎 Step 2: Grab an Imbue Scroll From the Arena

Travel to Al Kharid and enter the Emir's Arena. Queue for 1v1 fights, play a few (you get points even losing), and once you hit 200 PvP Arena points, buy a Scroll of Imbuing from the reward shop. Use it on your Black Mask / Slayer Helmet to make a Slayer Helmet (i) — or save it for a ring imbue. — Price verified: OSRS Wiki / PvP Arena Rewards (200 points)

🎯 Quick Arena Reference

Location: Al Kharid, Emir's Arena · Cost: 200 PvP Arena points per Scroll of Imbuing · Why: Imbue gear with no combat XP gain · Best for: Slayer Helmet (i), Berserker Ring (i), Salve Amulet (i)

③ 🔄 Step 3: Combine With Your Returning-Player Plan

7. FAQs

What exactly is the OSRS Activity Adviser?

It's an in-game interface (the orange scroll orb below the minimap) that recommends activities — currently quests — based on your stats, quest progress, and recent play. It shows what you can do now (Available Content) and what you're close to (Target Content). Source: OSRS Wiki / Activity Adviser.

How do I know if I'm in the July 20 A/B test?

If you're selected, you'll see a pop-up the next time you log in. The pop-up disappears automatically once you engage in combat, so it never blocks you. If you don't see it, you're not in the test group this round — the Adviser is still available normally via the orb. Source: Game Update Notifier / July 20 update.

Did the Activity Adviser just launch in 2026?

No. It originally launched as an A/B test on 20 July 2022 and opened to all players on 8 September 2022. The July 20, 2026 weekly update re-runs / continues A/B testing and gathers fresh feedback. The core tool is the same one released in 2022. Source: OSRS Wiki / Activity Adviser.

Why was the Bow of Faerdhinen removed from the PvP Arena?

Per the July 20 update, players raised concerns that its power was disproportionate to other Arena loadout options like the Zaryte Crossbow. The Bowfa is one of the strongest ranged weapons in the game (+106 Ranged Strength, surpassing the Twisted Bow), so in an equal-footing Arena itcreated an imbalance. The change only affects the Arena loadout chest, not open-world PvM. Source: Game Update Notifier & OSRS Wiki / Bow of Faerdhinen.

How much does an Imbue Scroll cost now?

The Scroll of Imbuing costs 200 PvP Arena points, down from the original 500. This current price is confirmed on the OSRS Wiki's PvP Arena Rewards page. The July 20, 2026 update reaffirmed the 200-point price and promised refunds to players who already bought scrolls. Source: OSRS Wiki / PvP Arena Rewards.

Do I need to win Arena fights to get the Imbue Scroll?

No. You earn PvP Arena points for participating — both wins and losses award points (losses simply award fewer). At 200 points per scroll, a few casual matches, even if you lose most of them, get you there. It's widely considered the fastest imbue method in the game.

Is the PvP Arena safe for a low-level player?

The Emir's Arena is a controlled minigame with its own loadouts and risk rules — you're not in the Wilderness, so you don't risk your bank. As a beginner you can dip in just to farm Imbue Scroll points without the danger of real PvP death. Just don't take valuable gear you aren't willing to use in the provided loadouts.

What can I imbue with the Scroll of Imbuing?

You can imbue Slayer Helmets, rings (e.g., Berserker Ring, Warrior Ring), the Salve Amulet, and similar items — the same items covered by Nightmare Zone and Soul Wars imbuing. A big advantage: imbuing via the scroll grants no combat XP, which is ideal for pures and skillers. Source: OSRS Wiki / Imbuing.

Will the Bowfa change affect my PvM bossing?

No. The removal only applies to the PvP Arena loadout chest. Outside the Arena, the Bow of Faerdhinen works exactly as before for Slayer and PvM. If you already own or plan to use one, nothing changes for open-world content.

Should I change my playstyle because of this update?

For most beginners and returning players, just two actions matter: (1) use the Activity Adviser orb to find your next quest, and (2) consider a few Arena matches for a cheap Imbue Scroll. Everything else is Arena-specific balance you can ignore until you're PvP-curious.
