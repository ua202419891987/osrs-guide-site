OSRS Sailing Nerf Survival Guide 2026: Bounty XP Cut 25% — What to Train Instead

Updated August 19, 2026  |  15 min read  |  By Yanchun Wu

On April 1, 2026 Jagex shipped the first big Sailing balance pass: Bounty Task XP was cut by 25%, cannons were rebalanced, and the Spring Boat Combat rework landed. If your 1–99 plan leaned on Bounty Tasks, this is your pivot map. Pair it with our Sailing Training Guide for the full method list and the 1–99 route.

⛵ Level: Advanced
⚓ Advanced — Sailing · 12 min read · Updated July 30, 2026

  • 📌 Bounty Tasks lost 25% XP on April 1, 2026 — they are no longer the lazy middle-ground method.
  • 📌 Deep Sea Trawling was doubled and Port Tasks buffed 10–15% — those are your new Bounty replacements.
  • 📌 Barracuda Trials are still king for raw XP (80K–200K+/hr) if you can click.

1. 🔄 What Changed on April 1, 2026

Sailing's first major balance pass arrived as part of the Spring Boat Combat rework and the wider 2026 roadmap. The headline number players panic about is the Bounty Task XP reduction — but the patch also buffed several methods, so the net effect is "shift, don't quit." Our Phase 2 prep guide covers the longer-term roadmap; this page is the immediate damage-control version.

① Bounty Task XP −25%

The single biggest nerf: Bounty Task experience was reduced by 25%. Bounty Tasks were the comfortable "medium intensity" middle option — more engaging than AFK Salvaging, less click-heavy than Trials. At −25% they drop below Port Tasks on the efficiency curve for most players, which is exactly the behaviour Jagex intended when it buffed Port Tasks in parallel.

② The Spring Boat Combat rework context

This nerf did not happen in a vacuum. The Sailing Into 2026 roadmap put Boat Combat first because the Sailing Opinion Poll showed cannons felt expensive, slow, and weak. The April 1 changes rebalanced cannons (slower attack, bigger hits, Ranged-gear scaling) and shipped the auto-retaliate setting. The Bounty cut is the XP-side of the same "make every method earn its slot" philosophy.

③ What got buffed (so you can pivot)

Two methods you should immediately move toward: Deep Sea Trawling XP was roughly doubled (rope net 4→7, linen 5→9, hemp 6→11, cotton 8→15 XP; gather rate 4→3 ticks; baited shoals now correctly add 20%), and Port Tasks (Courier & Bounty) were buffed 10–15% XP with better GP via loot sacks. Salvaging stayed beloved and untouched. The takeaway: the game gave you replacements the same day it took Bounty away.

2. 🪤 Why Bounty Was the Trap

Understanding why Bounty got cut tells you which future methods are safe — and which are next.

① Bounty's old position

Before April 1, Bounty Tasks sat in a sweet spot: decent XP, decent GP, and low click intensity. That made them the default "I'll just do Bounty" method for players who didn't want to sweat Trials or fall asleep at Salvaging. When a method is both easy and efficient, Jagex treats it as a balance failure — and corrects it. The same logic earlier buffed Port Tasks to pull players off pure Bounty reliance.

② Who got hit hardest

The players who built a whole 70→99 plan around Bounty lost the most. Ironmen and casuals who valued the low-intensity GP+XB combo are the real casualties, because their alternative (Salvaging) is pure AFK with weaker GP, and Trials demand attention they may not want to give. If that is you, the AFK Sailing guide shows how to soften the blow with the buffed Trawling.

3. ⛵ The 4 Alternative Training Methods

Here is where your Bounty hours should go. Numbers below reflect post-April-1 rates (Trawling/Port buffed, Bounty nerfed).

Method XP/hr (post-nerf) Intensity Verdict
Barracuda Trials 80K–200K+ High (clicky) Still the XP ceiling
Deep Sea Trawling ~Doubled vs old Semi-AFK Best Bounty replacement
Port Tasks (Courier/Bounty) ~15K–70K (+10–15%) Medium Now beats old Bounty
Shipwreck Salvaging 30K–50K AFK Safe fallback, untouched

① Deep Sea Trawling (the Bounty killer)

This is your primary pivot. With XP roughly doubled and the gather rate dropped to 3 ticks, Trawling went from a meme to a legitimate semi-AFK trainer. Build the trawling net facility on your ship, sail to a shoal, and let it run. Baited shoals now correctly add 20% catch, so always bait when available. It is the closest thing to "old Bounty but better."

② Barracuda Trials (still the ceiling)

If you can handle the click intensity, Barracuda Trials remain the highest XP in Sailing at 80K–200K+/hr and were not touched by the nerf. The April 1 patch only reduced Bounty XP; Trials are still the optimal road to 99 for active players. Use Wind Motes (Wind Catcher facility at 53) and the Crystal Extractor (73) for passive boosts. The 1–99 guide has the exact Trial setup.

③ Port Tasks buffed 10–15%

Courier and Bounty Port Tasks received a 10–15% Sailing XP boost plus loot sacks that improve GP/hr. Post-nerf, standard Port Tasks now out-value the old Bounty XP rate for medium-intensity players. They are longer on average (less travel between tasks), which actually helps efficiency. Run them from Port Sarim or Port Roberts depending on your level.

④ Shipwreck Salvaging (the safe fallback)

Salvaging was explicitly left alone because players love it. At 30K–50K XP/hr it is pure AFK and remains the default for players who want Sailing trained while they do something else. It will not get you to 99 fast, but it is the method least likely to be nerfed again — Jagex has signalled it wants Salvaging to stay the chill option.

4. 🔫 Cannon & Combat Rebalance — Adapt Your Boat

The nerf was not only about XP. Boat combat changed in ways that affect how you build and gear your ship for PvM and Bounty-adjacent sea creatures.

① Cannons now scale with Ranged gear

The biggest combat shift: Sailing cannons now scale with your worn Ranged accuracy and strength, and Ranged prayers/void now apply in cannon combat. Previously cannons were a flat "fourth combat skill" that ignored your gear — now your equipment matters. Translate that: wear your best Ranged gear (void, armadyl, or pegs) on the boat, and protect/rigour prayers actually help. Cannons also hit slower but harder per shot, improving cannonball efficiency.

② The Sailing cannonball economy got cheaper

Counter-intuitively, the cannon economy improved. Sailing cannonballs are now a separate type produced with a new mould that yields 8 cannonballs per metal bar and does not require the Dwarf Cannon quest. Drops from Sailing content increased, shops restock faster, and you can unnote bars at the Ancient Furnace via Isles. So the "cannons are too expensive" complaint was answered — your per-hour cannonball cost fell even as combat got rebalanced.

③ Auto-retaliate & smarter crew

The April 1 update added an auto-retaliate setting for cannons when your boat is attacked, plus "Fire at Will" crew behaviour and better targeting logic. For players who found boat combat too click-intensive, this is the real quality-of-life win — your crew now acts more like land auto-retaliate. Use it on solo spawns (Vampyre Krakens, Great White Sharks) that were mapped for easier bullying.

5. 💰 Money Making After the Nerf

A 25% Bounty XP cut also nudges Bounty GP/hr down, because you complete fewer tasks per hour for the same effort. Here is the post-nerf money picture.

① Bounty GP/hr shift

Bounty Tasks still drop Sailing-specific loot and remain a GP source, but the XP cut means you are spending more time per XP — so if your goal was GP, the opportunity cost rose. Many players will now run Bounty only for its unique drops (Dragon Sheets from Orcas, Krakens) rather than as a training method.

② Best Sailing money post-nerf

For pure GP, the winners are Salvaging (steady, AFK, no nerf) and high-level sea-creature PvM now that cannons scale with gear (better DPS = faster kills = more drops). The Sailing money guide breaks down exact GP/hr per method, and Skilling Money Post-Sailing shows how the new skill feeds other money makers.

6. 🗓️ Your 7-Day Pivot Plan

If you were mid-Bounty-grind when the nerf hit, don't keep bleeding XP. Here is the reset.

① Week 1 — Re-route and rebuild

Day 1–2: Switch your active method to Deep Sea Trawling; build/upgrade the trawling net facility and bait every shoal. Day 3–4: If you can click, move to Barracuda Trials for the XP ceiling — set up Wind Motes and the Crystal Extractor. Day 5–7: Run buffed Port Tasks for variety and GP; equip Ranged gear for any boat combat so cannons pull their weight.

② Week 2+ — Lock the efficient route

Commit to Trials (active) or Trawling (semi-AFK) as your primary, with Salvaging as the burn-out relief. Ignore Bounty as a trainer; use it only for targeted unique drops. Re-check our Best Quest Order After Sailing so your post-99 plan (Fractured Archive raid at 50, Frost Dragons at 87) stays on track despite the rate changes.

7. ❓ Sailing Nerf FAQs

Exactly what got nerfed on April 1, 2026?

The headline nerf was Bounty Task experience, cut by 25%. Alongside it, cannons were rebalanced (slower but harder-hitting, now scaling with Ranged gear) and an auto-retaliate setting was added. Deep Sea Trawling and Port Tasks were buffed in the same window, so it is a rebalance, not a pure downgrade.

Is Bounty Task still worth doing at all?

For training, no — at −25% XP it sits below buffed Port Tasks and untouched Trials/Salvaging on efficiency. For money, only run Bounty if you are chasing its unique drops (Dragon Sheets, Kraken loot). Treat it as a targeted drop method, not a leveling method.

What is the new best AFK Sailing method after the nerf?

Deep Sea Trawling, now that its XP was doubled and the gather rate dropped to 3 ticks. It is semi-AFK, baitable, and outperforms the old Bounty rate. Pure-AFK players can still fall back on Shipwreck Salvaging, which was not changed.

Do I need to change my cannon setup?

Yes, gear-wise. Because cannons now scale with your worn Ranged accuracy/strength and Ranged prayers apply, wear your best Ranged armour and use Rigour/Protective prayers on the boat. The cannonball economy also got cheaper (8 per bar, no Dwarf Cannon quest), so your per-hour cost dropped even though combat was rebalanced.

Did the 99 Sailing time increase a lot?

Only if you stubbornly kept doing Bounty. Switching to Trials (80K–200K+/hr, untouched) or doubled Trawling keeps your 1–99 timeline essentially the same. The nerf punishes inertia, not the skill — adapt and your ETA barely moves.

Will Salvaging get nerfed next?

Unlikely soon. Jagex has repeatedly signalled it wants Salvaging to remain the chill AFK option and explicitly left it alone while buffing Trawling and Port Tasks. If anything gets touched later, it would be the still-dominant Trials, not Salvaging.

Where should I train Sailing right after the Pandemonium miniquest?

Follow the standard curve: Port Tasks to ~15 for the Skiff + Salvaging Hook, then Trawling/Trials from 30. The nerf does not change the early game — it only reshuffles the 30+ methods. See our Sailing Training Guide for the full level-by-level path.
