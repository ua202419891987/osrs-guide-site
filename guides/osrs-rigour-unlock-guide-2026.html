OSRS Rigour Unlock Guide 2026 — Requirements, Cost & Is It Worth Buying?

Published September 4, 2026  |  13 min read  |  By Yanchun Wu

Rigour is the single biggest ranged DPS unlock in OSRS — +20% ranged attack, +23% ranged strength and +25% defence on every shot you take. But it costs around 20M GP and demands 74 Prayer and 70 Defence. This guide gives you the exact unlock path, the real numbers, and a straight answer on when Rigour is worth buying and when your GP is better spent elsewhere.

1. Why Rigour Is the Best Ranged Upgrade You Can Buy

Every ranged player hits the same wall around the mid-game: your damage plateaus and the only way forward looks like buying a 100M+ weapon. Rigour breaks that wall for a fraction of the price. It is a forgotten prayer — one of three locked combat prayers added to Old School RuneScape in January 2017 — and once active it applies to every single ranged attack you make:

🏹 What Rigour Actually Gives You

  • +20% Ranged Attack — you hit more often, so less damage is wasted on splashes
  • +23% Ranged Strength — every successful hit deals up to 23% more damage
  • +25% Defence — you take noticeably less damage while the prayer is on
  • Drain rate: 1 prayer point every 1.5 seconds (40 points per minute) — manageable with prayer potions

Compare that to the free alternative, Eagle Eye, which only gives +15% ranged attack and damage. Rigour is a strict upgrade on every line — more accuracy, more damage, and a defensive bonus Eagle Eye does not have at all. For anyone who fights with a bow, crossbow or blowpipe, Rigour is effectively a permanent equipment upgrade that never takes an inventory slot.

💡 The Golden Rule of Rigour

Rigour multiplies whatever ranged gear and level you already own. The stronger your base setup, the bigger the absolute DPS gain — which is why it shines most at Vorkath, Zulrah, raids and the Inferno, and matters least for casual Slayer with a magic shortbow.

2. Rigour Requirements — 74 Prayer & 70 Defence Explained

There are two separate gates here, and players constantly confuse them:

GateLevelWhat it means
Using the prayer74 Prayer + 70 DefenceYou cannot activate Rigour below these levels
Reading the scrollNoneYou can read the Dexterous Prayer Scroll at any level — but the unlock is wasted until you meet the use requirements

① Prayer 74 — the expensive gate

Prayer is one of the fastest skills to train and one of the most GP-hungry. The practical routes from 70 to 74 (or 60 to 74 if you are behind):

  • Gilded Altar with Dragon Bones (POH): roughly 350% XP with both burners lit — the standard method, costs a few million GP for the level range
  • Superior Dragon Bones: faster but roughly twice the cost per bone; use only if you value time over GP
  • Ensouled Dragon Heads / Reanimation: slower but partly self-funded through Arceuus spellbook Magic XP
  • Pest Control: the cheapest route but very slow — only worth it if you are training the skill entirely on a budget
💡 Don't burn 74 Prayer before you own the scroll

The scroll itself has no level requirement, but Rigour does. If you read the scroll at 60 Prayer you simply cannot turn the prayer on until you reach 74 — the unlock is not lost, it just sits there. Train Prayer first, buy the scroll after.

② Defence 70 — the easy gate

Defence 70 is a level almost every account passes naturally through questing and Slayer. If you are still below it, Barrows runs, quest experience rewards (like Dragon Slayer II and Song of the Elves) and Slayer tasks will carry you there with no dedicated training session needed.

3. How to Unlock Rigour — The Dexterous Prayer Scroll

Rigour is not taught by a quest NPC. It is unlocked by reading the Dexterous Prayer Scroll, a rare reward from the Chambers of Xeric (Raids 1). Once read, the scroll is consumed and Rigour is permanently added to your prayer book on that account — including on every account build that meets the level gates.

① Route A — Earn it from Chambers of Xeric

The Dexterous Prayer Scroll is one of the raid's rare unique rewards (rollable from the Ancient Chest alongside items like the Twisted Bow and Kodai Insignia). The realistic picture for a learner:

  • Recommended stats: 80+ Ranged and the melee stats to hold your own in rooms like Tekton and Vasa Nistirio
  • Realistic dry streak: uniques are rare — many players run dozens of raids before a scroll lands their way
  • Best team route: join a learner-friendly clan chat or Discord (e.g. "We Do Raids" style communities) rather than soloing your first completions
  • Upside: the raid itself is profitable — even a dry streak pays for supplies through split loot

② Route B — Buy it on the Grand Exchange

The scroll is fully tradeable, which makes Rigour a pure GP purchase for most players. The catch is the price tag:

💰 Dexterous Prayer Scroll — What It Costs

  • Grand Exchange price: roughly 19-20M GP in recent trading (check the live GE graph — it moves with raid meta and update hype)
  • Ironman note: iron accounts cannot buy it — the scroll must be earned from CoX, which makes Rigour a genuine late-game grind on an iron
  • One-time cost: you buy the scroll once and the unlock is permanent for that account

③ The exact steps to unlock (whichever route)

Once the scroll is in your inventory:

1. Right-click the Dexterous Prayer Scroll and select Read (or left-click — it is read-only).
2. Confirm "Learn Rigour" in the dialogue — the scroll is consumed.
3. Open your Prayer book and you will see Rigour under the Ranged prayers, ready to activate at 74 Prayer / 70 Defence.

💡 Read it at a bank

Reading the scroll consumes it with no take-back. If you bought it on the GE and are not yet 74 Prayer / 70 Defence, you can safely hold the scroll in the bank — it never expires — but only read it once you can actually use the prayer.

4. Rigour vs Augury vs Piety — The Three Forgotten Prayers

Rigour is one of three "forgotten prayers" released together, each unlocked by its own scroll and each boosting one combat style. Here is the full picture so you can prioritise correctly:

PrayerUnlock ScrollBoostLevelsBest For
PietyLearned free from the King's Ransom quest+20% Attack / +23% Strength / +25% Defence70 Prayer / 70 DefenceMelee bossing & Slayer
RigourDexterous Prayer Scroll+20% Ranged Atk / +23% Ranged Str / +25% Def74 Prayer / 70 DefenceRanged bossing, raids, Inferno
AuguryArcane Prayer Scroll+25% Magic Atk / +25% Magic Dmg / +25% Def77 Prayer / 70 MagicMagic bossing & bursting

Rigour sits at 74 Prayer, three levels below Augury's 77 — which makes it the cheapest forgotten prayer to actually use, on top of being the most impactful for the majority of endgame PvM where ranged is king (Zulrah, Vorkath, raids, GWD). For most accounts the priority order is: Rigour first, Piety comes free from questing, Augury last unless you main magic.

5. Where Rigour Changes Your DPS — Boss & Raid Checklist

Rigour is not equally valuable everywhere. It matters most where ranged accuracy and damage are already your bottleneck:

🎯 Rigour Tier List — Where It Earns Its Keep

  • Must-have: Inferno (Zuk/Jad waves), Chambers of Xeric, Theatre of Blood, Vorkath, Zulrah, Leviathan, Muspah
  • Strong value: God Wars Dungeon (Zilyana, Graardor ranged roles), ToA ranged rooms, Phantom Muspah, most 90+ Slayer bosses
  • Nice-to-have: casual Slayer with broad arrows, Sarachnis, early PvM where food is the real constraint
  • Low value: anything you already one-shot or where you only use magic/melee

The reason is simple math: at high Ranged levels with good gear, +20% accuracy and +23% damage convert into a 10-15% total DPS increase on most bosses — often the single cheapest DPS-per-GP upgrade between 100M and 1B total gear value.

💡 The prayer-potion pairing

Rigour drains 40 prayer points per minute — roughly one full prayer potion (4) every 4-5 minutes of continuous combat. Always bring 2-3 prayer potions (or super restores) on boss trips where you plan to keep Rigour active the whole kill.

6. Is Rigour Worth ~20M GP? A Decision Checklist

This is the question everyone actually asks. The honest answer depends on your account stage, not on your bank value. Run through this checklist:

✅ Buy Rigour now if you tick ANY two of these:

  • You ranged boss for 5+ hours a week (Vorkath/Zulrah/raids)
  • You are 90+ Ranged with a blowpipe, dragon/armadyl crossbow or bowfa-style setup
  • You are learning the Inferno or pushing ToB/CoX completions
  • Your next best DPS upgrade costs 50M+ (e.g. saving toward a Tbow or Shadow)

❌ Skip Rigour for now if you match any of these:

  • You are below 80 Ranged — train the level first, the scroll will still be there
  • You mostly melee (Slayer, GWD melee roles) — gear upgrades beat a ranged prayer you rarely use
  • 20M is more than 10% of your bank — Rigour should never bankrupt you; a DPS weapon upgrade often matters more at low budgets
  • You are an ironman below the CoX gear/stats threshold — grind stats first, chase the scroll later

The one-line verdict: if you ranged boss regularly at 90+ Ranged, Rigour is the best ~20M you can spend in the game. If you do not, buy it when your bank comfortably covers it — it is a permanent upgrade, not a consumable, so there is no rush and no wrong time as long as you can actually use it.

7. What to Use Before You Own Rigour

Until the scroll is yours, you are not playing at a handicap — you are playing on the standard prayer book like every player did for the first eight years of OSRS:

  • Eagle Eye: +15% ranged attack and damage at 44 Prayer — the budget stand-in and still excellent for mid-game Slayer
  • Hawk Eye / Sharp Eye: the lower-level steps (26 and 15 Prayer) for early quest bosses
  • Combination note: you can stack Eagle Eye with your Ranged potion, Slayer helm (on task) and void set bonuses — all multiplicative layers you already have before Rigour arrives

When you finally add Rigour on top of those, every layer multiplies — which is exactly why the jump feels so dramatic the first time you turn it on at Vorkath or in the Inferno.

8. Rigour FAQs

Q: Can I unlock Rigour before 74 Prayer?

Yes — reading the Dexterous Prayer Scroll has no level requirement. However you cannot activate Rigour until you have 74 Prayer and 70 Defence, so it is smartest to read the scroll only once you meet both gates.

Q: Is Rigour a one-time purchase or do I need to pay again?

One-time. Reading the scroll permanently unlocks Rigour on that account. The only repeat cost is prayer potions to keep it active during combat.

Q: What is the Dexterous Prayer Scroll price in 2026?

The scroll typically trades around 19-20M GP on the Grand Exchange, but the price moves with raid meta, updates and supply. Check the live GE graph before buying, and never pay far above the current offer price.

Q: Do I need to complete any quest to unlock Rigour?

No quest is required for Rigour itself. You only need the Dexterous Prayer Scroll plus the 74 Prayer / 70 Defence levels. (The related melee prayer Piety does come from the King's Ransom quest chain — a separate unlock.)

Q: Rigour or Augury first?

For almost every account: Rigour first. It unlocks at 74 Prayer (vs 77 for Augury), costs less, and ranged is the dominant style in most endgame PvM. Only mage mains should prioritise the Arcane Prayer Scroll / Augury.

Q: Does Rigour work in PvP?

Yes — Rigour is fully active in PvP and is a staple of ranged PvP builds (NH bridding and pure ranged fights). The +20% accuracy and +23% damage apply to your attacks on other players.

Q: Can an ironman buy the Dexterous Prayer Scroll?

No. Ironman accounts cannot trade for it — the scroll must drop from your own Chambers of Xeric completions. Budget for a long grind and treat it as a late-game iron milestone.

Q: What level should my Ranged be before I buy Rigour?

Around 90 Ranged is the sweet spot where Rigour's percentage boosts translate into meaningful DPS. Below 80 Ranged, the same 20M is usually better spent on gear or a stronger weapon, because percentage boosts scale with your base damage.

Q: How much prayer does Rigour drain?

Rigour drains 40 prayer points per minute — 1 point every 1.5 seconds. That is roughly one prayer potion (4-dose) per 4-5 minutes of continuous use, so always bring restores on boss trips.

Q: Where does Rigour drop from?

The Dexterous Prayer Scroll is a rare unique reward from the Chambers of Xeric (Raids 1) — rolled from the Ancient Chest at the end of the raid. It is also tradeable, so most players simply buy it.
