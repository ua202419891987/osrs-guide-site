OSRS Giantsoul Amulet Guide 2026 — Royal Titans Teleport Item, Charges & Uses

🆕 NEW
Published September 12, 2026  |  13 min read  |  By Yanchun Wu

It carries zero combat or prayer bonuses, but its teleport network is genuinely one of the best in the game for mid-level Slayer, Treasure Trails, and air-orb running. This guide covers how to farm it, how to charge it, every destination, and how it stacks up against the other utility amulets competing for your neck slot.

1. 🗡️ Why the Giantsoul Amulet Earns a Bank Slot in 2026

Most Royal Titans rewards are flashy: the Twinflame Staff, the Deadeye and Mystic Vigour prayer scrolls, and the Desiccated pages. The Giantsoul Amulet is the quiet one. It does not hit harder, it does not boost your prayer, and it will never appear on a best-in-slot gear list. What it does is save you steps — and in Old School RuneScape, steps are time, and time is either XP or GP.

The amulet teleports you to three places that are awkward to reach otherwise: Bryophyta's lair (just south of the Edgeville Dungeon, next to a Vannaka shortcut), Obor's lair on the Hill Giant plateau, and the Royal Titans arena inside the Asgarnia Ice Dungeon. None of those are "endgame" destinations, but all three sit on top of grinds that the average account revisits for years: low-level Slayer tasks, clue scrolls, giant kills for diary steps, and the endless loop of charging air orbs.

💡 The Real Value Is the Vannaka Shortcut

The Bryophyta teleport lands you a short walk from an agility pipe that drops you straight into the Edgeville Dungeon by Vannaka. That single shortcut turns the amulet into the fastest repeatable route to a Slayer master in the game, which is why Slayers and clue chasers keep it equipped long after they have outgrown the bosses it teleports to.

If you came here expecting a prayer amulet, here is the correction up front: the Giantsoul Amulet gives zero prayer bonus and zero combat bonuses. The prayer upgrades from the Royal Titans are the Deadeye and Mystic Vigour scrolls, covered separately in our Prayer Scrolls guide. The amulet is purely a travel tool — but a very good one.

2. 🔓 How to Get the Giantsoul Amulet from the Royal Titans

The amulet is an untradeable drop from the Royal Titans, the duo boss released with the Royal Titans update. You fight Branda the Fire Queen and Eldric the Ice King together inside the Asgarnia Ice Dungeon, and the amulet can drop from looting either Titan. If you have not done the fight yet, our Royal Titans guide walks through the mechanics, the elemental spawns, and the contribution system that controls your loot rolls.

📜 Drop Basics

  • Source: loot from Branda the Fire Queen or Eldric the Ice King (Royal Titans).
  • Drop chance: roughly 1/16 from the Titans — it is one of the more common unique drops, so most players see one within a handful of kills.
  • Tradeable: no. It is an untradeable reward, so mains and Ironmen acquire it the same way.
  • Collection log: it is the most commonly completed Royal Titans log entry, which tells you how often it shows up.

Because the drop is common, you should not "farm for it" the way you would a rare weapon. Kill the Titans for the Twinflame Staff or the prayer scrolls, and treat the amulet as a near-guaranteed bonus. Once you have one, any further copies are still useful — see the Ironman section for what to do with duplicates.

💡 Get It Early, Use It Forever

The amulet has no stat requirement to wear and no quest gate beyond reaching the Titans. As soon as you can clear the fight, grab one. The teleport network pays for itself on your very next Slayer trip, and it never becomes obsolete the way a weapon does.

3. 🔋 Charging It: Big Bones, Law Runes and 16,000 Teleports

Out of the box the amulet is uncharged and cannot teleport. You charge it by using one set of big bones (noted bones work) and one law rune on it. Each charge is one teleport, and the amulet stores up to 16,000 charges at once.

⚙️ Charging at a Glance

  • Per teleport: 1 set of big bones + 1 law rune.
  • Max stored: 16,000 charges.
  • Noted bones: yes, you can use noted big bones, which makes bulk charging trivial.
  • Cost: a few hundred GP per teleport at current Grand Exchange prices — cheap enough to use constantly.
  • Can you uncharge it? No. Unlike Xeric's talisman and the Pendant of Ates, the Giantsoul Amulet cannot be drained back to recover the bones and runes.

The "you cannot uncharge it" point matters for inventory planning. Once bones and a law rune are sunk into the amulet, they are gone — but since a full 16,000-charge load costs only a few million GP at most and lasts an extremely long time, this is rarely a real concern. Top it up in bulk whenever you are at a bank and forget about it.

💡 Bulk-Charge From Your Slayer Drops

Big bones are among the most common drops in the game. If you are already on a Slayer task that hits giants or other big-bone droppers, dump the noted bones straight into the amulet between trips. You will almost never need to buy bones specifically for it, and the law runes are pocket change next to a real rune tab.

4. 🧭 Teleport Destinations and Everyday Uses

The amulet offers three teleport destinations, accessed by right-clicking it in your inventory (Rub) or selecting a worn option while equipped. Each one opens a different grind.

① Bryophyta's Lair — The Slayer and Clue Hub

Bryophyta is a low-level boss just outside the Edgeville Dungeon. The teleport lands you a short distance from an agility pipe that requires 51 Agility and drops you right next to Vannaka. That makes the amulet the fastest route in the game to:

  • Slayer: grabbing and turning in Vannaka tasks without running from a guild or a portal.
  • Treasure Trails: an Easy clue step (talk to Vannaka), a Medium step (kill a Hill Giant for a key — Obor's teleport helps here too), a Hard step (dig at the Moss Giant in Varrock Sewers), and an Elite step (talk to Vannaka).
  • Air orbs: from the Edgeville Dungeon it is a short run to the Air Obelisk for charged-air-orb crafting.

② Obor's Lair — Hill Giant Boss and Diary Steps

Obor is the Hill Giant boss on the plateau north of Edgeville. His teleport is handy for the Medium clue key step mentioned above and for players grinding Obor specifically for the giant key drops and diary completions.

③ Branda and Eldric's Lair — The Titans Themselves

Naturally, the amulet takes you to the Royal Titans arena. If you are still farming the Twinflame Staff or the prayer scrolls, this is a free, unrestricted teleport to the fight — no Glory charge spent, no quest requirement beyond having unlocked the area.

💡 One Amulet, Three Grinds

Because all three destinations feed into long-term account progression (Slayer points, clue scrolls, diary steps, and boss unlocks), the amulet stays relevant from a fresh member account all the way to a maxed main. Very few teleport items cover that much repeated content.

5. ⚖️ Giantsoul Amulet vs Other Utility Amulets

The neck slot is crowded with teleport and utility items, so it is worth seeing where the Giantsoul Amulet actually wins. None of these compete on combat stats — the amulet has none — so this is purely about travel convenience.

📊 How It Compares

  • Xeric's talisman: teleports to Chambers of Xeric entrances and gives a skip. Stronger for raiders, but it does nothing for Vannaka or giant bosses. The Giantsoul Amulet wins for Slayer and clues.
  • Pendant of Ates: teleports to the Ates compound in Varlamore. Useful for Varlamore content, but again no Vannaka access. The two serve different regions.
  • Digsite pendant: great for the Digsite and Fossil Island, not for giant bosses or Slayer masters.
  • Skills necklace / Combat bracelet: bank and guild teleports, but no direct Vannaka or boss-lair drops.

The honest verdict: the Giantsoul Amulet is a specialist item. If your account lives in Varlamore or at raids, something else fits better. But if you run Vannaka Slayer, do clue scrolls, or kill giants for diaries, the Bryophyta teleport plus the Vannaka pipe is unmatched, and that is exactly the niche this amulet owns.

💡 Equip It, Don't Bank It

Because it has no stats, you might be tempted to leave it in the bank. Don't. Keep it equipped while skilling or Slaying — the worn teleport options mean you never waste an inventory slot, and you can Rub it to Vannaka the moment a task is done.

6. 🛡️ Ironman and UIM Notes: Duplicates, Death Retention

Ironmen and Ultimate Ironmen get two extra reasons to care about the Giantsoul Amulet, both tied to its untradeable nature.

Duplicate Amulets and the Odd Old Man

Since the drop is common, you will eventually loot a second (or third) amulet. Duplicates can be exchanged with the Odd Old Man for 30 noted big bones each. That is a small but steady bone source — handy for prayer training or for feeding back into more amulets you charge for alts. Our Ironman beginner guide covers where bones fit into an early game plan.

Death Retention at the Titans

If you die while fighting the Royal Titans, the amulet is always retained as an additional inventory item. That means even on a risky Learner kill, you keep the amulet and get fast access to your gravestone just outside the tunnel entrance. For Ultimate Ironmen, who cannot use the Grand Exchange and rely on self-sourced teleports, that retention is a quiet quality-of-life win.

✅ Ironman Quick Reference

  • Self-source only: must be dropped by the Titans; cannot be bought.
  • Duplicate value: 30 noted big bones each from the Odd Old Man.
  • UIM safe: retained on death at the Titans, so it never blocks your gravestone run.
  • Charge supply: noted big bones from Slayer make charging free in practice.

7. 🧠 Pro Tips and Common Mistakes

✅ Do This

  • Keep it equipped while Slaying. The worn Rub option sends you to Vannaka without eating an inventory slot.
  • Bulk-charge with noted bones. Fill it to 16,000 once and forget about it for months.
  • Use the Bryophyta teleport for clue steps. Four clue tiers reference Vannaka or Hill/Moss Giants — one amulet covers them all.
  • Pair it with the 51 Agility pipe. The real power is the Vannaka shortcut, not the teleport itself.
  • Turn in duplicates. The Odd Old Man conversion is free bones you are already walking past.

❌ Avoid This

  • Expecting a prayer or combat bonus. It has none. Don't equip it expecting DPS.
  • Assuming you can uncharge it. The bones and law rune are consumed for good.
  • Leaving it in the bank. An unequipped teleport item does nothing for you.
  • Buying big bones just to charge it. Your Slayer drops already supply more than enough.

💡 The Fastest Way to Test Its Value

Do one Vannaka Slayer task with the amulet equipped and the 51 Agility pipe open. Time the round trip from bank to Vannaka to kill to bank. Then imagine doing that fifty times without the teleport. That gap is the entire argument for the item.

8. ❓ Giantsoul Amulet FAQs

Q: Is the Giantsoul Amulet a prayer amulet?

No. It provides zero prayer bonus and zero combat bonuses. It is purely a teleport amulet. The Royal Titans prayer upgrades are the separate Deadeye and Mystic Vigour scrolls.

Q: Where do I get the Giantsoul Amulet?

It drops untradeably from looting either Branda the Fire Queen or Eldric the Ice King in the Royal Titans fight, at roughly a 1/16 chance.

Q: How do I charge the Giantsoul Amulet?

Use one set of big bones (noted works) and one law rune on it. Each charge is one teleport, and it holds up to 16,000 charges.

Q: Can I uncharge it to get the bones back?

No. Unlike Xeric's talisman and the Pendant of Ates, the Giantsoul Amulet cannot be drained. The bones and law rune are consumed.

Q: Where does the Giantsoul Amulet teleport?

To Bryophyta's lair, Obor's lair, and the Royal Titans arena. The Bryophyta teleport is the standout because of its nearby Vannaka agility shortcut.

Q: What is the best use for the Giantsoul Amulet?

Fast repeatable access to Vannaka for Slayer, several Treasure Trail steps, giant-boss diary tasks, and air-orb running via the Edgeville Dungeon.

Q: What do I do with duplicate Giantsoul Amulets?

Exchange them with the Odd Old Man for 30 noted big bones each. It is a small but free bone source.

Q: Does the Giantsoul Amulet survive death?

Yes — if you die at the Royal Titans, it is retained as an additional inventory item, giving you quick access to your gravestone outside the tunnel.

Q: Is the Giantsoul Amulet worth it for maxed mains?

For players who still do Vannaka Slayer, clue scrolls, or giant diaries, yes. For pure raiders or Varlamore-focused accounts, a different teleport item may fit better.

Q: How does it compare to Xeric's talisman?

Xeric's talisman wins for Chambers of Xeric access; the Giantsoul Amulet wins for Vannaka, giant bosses, and clue steps. They serve different regions.
