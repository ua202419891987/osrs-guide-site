1. 🔍 Symptom → Side Map (Read This First)

The biggest mistake OSRS players make is "fixing their PC" when the problem is in Jagex's data center — or rebooting the router when the login server is literally offline. Before you touch a setting, match your symptom to a side. The whole point of this guide is to stop that waste. If you came from our Mobile Can't Log In fix, the logic is the same: confirm where the failure lives before you act.

The two sides are completely different and need opposite fixes. If your problem is "can't get in at all" rather than "kicked mid-session," the Mobile Can't Log In fix is the right page; this guide is for the "already playing, then disconnected" case:

💡 The Core Rule

If other players in the same world are also dropping, it is Jagex-side. If you alone drop while everyone else is fine, it is your connection. That single question decides everything that follows.

2. 🖥️ Server-Side Causes Jagex Controls

These are problems you cannot fix on your end. Trying to "repair" them locally just wastes time. Here is what's actually happening on Jagex's side and how to confirm it.

② Scheduled weekly game update (Wednesdays)

Old School RuneScape deploys a game update every Wednesday. The live game is taken down briefly, then brought back. If you're online when the update hits, you get disconnected with no personal warning — it's a global reset, not a ban, not a hack. See §5 for the exact timing.

③ Unscheduled downtime & "World Unavailable"

Individual worlds are taken offline for restarts, DDoS mitigation, or hardware issues. When a single world goes down you'll see "World Unavailable" or "The world is currently full / unavailable." This is normal and rotates — just pick a different world. A full-service outage (all worlds) is rarer and will be announced on @JagexSupport.

④ DDoS attacks & lobby instability

OSRS occasionally suffers DDoS attacks that cause mass random disconnects, "failed to connect to server," or inability to leave the lobby. These are Jagex-side and usually resolve within minutes to a couple of hours. During a DDoS, no local fix helps — the only move is to wait and watch the status feed.

🚨 Don't Get Re-scammed During Outages

Scammers swarm every outage, DMing "verify your account to prevent disconnect loss." Jagex will never message you about disconnects. Our Account Security Guide covers the full anti-phishing checklist so you don't lose your account while chasing a connection fix.

3. 📡 Local-Side Causes You Control

If the symptom is yours alone, the fault is almost always between your device and Jagex's servers. These are ranked by how often they're the real culprit.

⑤ Wi-Fi congestion & packet loss

The #1 real-world cause of "random disconnect mid-fight." Wi-Fi drops single packets constantly; OSRS is forgiving of lag but a burst of packet loss severs the session. Signs: disconnects spike near microwave use, when someone starts a download, or when you move far from the router. Fix: use 5 GHz over 2.4 GHz, sit closer, or plug in Ethernet on desktop.

⑥ Mobile data handoff & sleep kills

On OSRS Mobile, walking between cell towers or letting the screen sleep mid-session quietly kills the socket. The app may not even show an error — you just "are out" when you return. Keep the screen awake and stay on one connection type.

⑦ Router / modem needs a reboot

Consumer routers leak memory and drop long-lived TCP sessions after days of uptime. A 30-second power cycle clears it. If you disconnect at roughly the same interval every time (e.g., every 20 minutes), the router's NAT/state table is the suspect.

⑧ Background apps stealing bandwidth

Cloud backups, game launchers, streaming, or a Windows/phone update can saturate your upload pipe. OSRS needs a steady outbound heartbeat; if upload chokes, the server times you out. Pause heavy uploads while bossing.

💡 Quick Local Test

Open a constant ping to a stable host (e.g., ping -t 8.8.8.8 on Windows) while you play. If the ping shows "Request timed out" at the exact moment you disconnect, your connection — not Jagex — is the problem. That single test ends 80% of arguments.

4. 🌳 The 60-Second Diagnostic Tree

Follow this in order. Stop at the first branch that matches. This is the fast version of everything above, built so you spend zero time fixing the wrong side.

Step 1 — Is it everyone or just you?

  • Open @JagexSupport and r/2007scape. Hundreds of "disconnect" posts at once? → Server-side, stop here, wait.
  • Nobody else complaining? → Go to Step 2.

Step 2 — One world or all worlds?

  • Only one world says "World Unavailable" → That world is down. Hop worlds, done.
  • Every world fails → go to Step 3.

Step 3 — Does it happen on a second device / network?

  • Try the official client on a different connection (e.g., phone hotspot). If it's stable there → your home network is the fault (§3).
  • Still drops on a clean connection → your account may be flagged or your ISP is route-blocking (see §7 and our VPN Login Blocked fix).

Step 4 — Is it time-of-day patterned?

  • Same time every Wednesday → scheduled update (§5), expected.
  • Same interval every session → router NAT timeout (§3, step 7), reboot + enable a keep-alive.

🎯 The 60-Second Verdict

Everyone + all worlds = Jagex, wait. Just you + one world = hop. Just you + all worlds = your network. Patterned timing = scheduled or router. That's the entire decision tree in four questions.

5. 🗓️ The Silent Wednesday Maintenance Logout

This is the disconnect that confuses the most players because it looks like a hack or a ban. Every Wednesday, Jagex takes the live game down for the weekly update. If you're logged in, you are simply disconnected — no email, no warning beyond the in-game countdown that many players miss.

⑨ When it happens

  • The game update typically deploys around 10:30–11:00 UTC on Wednesdays (subject to change; check the in-game notice board).
  • Servers go offline, then come back. Total downtime is usually 5–30 minutes.
  • If you log in during the update window you may be bounced repeatedly — wait until the world list is back to normal.

⑩ It's not a ban, not a hack

A Wednesday kick affects every single player online at that moment. If your friends also got booted, you're fine. The Jagex Account FAQ confirms scheduled maintenance never triggers a ban. Just wait for the "Worlds are now available" message and re-log.

💡 Avoid Losing a Kill

If you're deep in a boss when the Wednesday countdown shows, bank or teleport out before the shutdown. Dying in the final 60 seconds of maintenance is a completely avoidable "lost voidwaker / lost synapse" story. Set a calendar reminder for the weekly update time.

6. 🌍 Single "World Unavailable" vs Full Outage

Players constantly confuse these two, then waste an hour "fixing" their PC for a problem that's one world away. Here's the clean distinction.

⑪ One world says "World Unavailable"

  • The world is restarting, full, or temporarily removed. This is normal and frequent.
  • Action: open the world list, pick another (ideally a less populated one for PvM). No local fix needed.
  • If a world you love is "missing" for more than a day, it may have been merged or retired — check the news page.

⑫ Every world fails + status confirms outage

  • Full login server outage or major DDoS. Nothing you do locally matters.
  • Action: step away, follow @JagexSupport, retry in 10–15 minutes.
  • Don't spam login — it can rate-limit your IP and slow your return once servers are back.

7. 🛡️ VPN, Firewall & ISP Blocks

If the diagnostic tree in §4 points at "your connection but not your router," the cause is usually one of these three.

⑬ VPNs that break OSRS

Some VPN exit nodes are shared by bot farms and get IP-banned by Jagex, producing random "Connection lost" or instant disconnects. If you run a VPN while playing, disable it and retry. Our VPN Login Blocked fix walks through whitelisting and safe provider choices.

⑭ Firewall / antivirus interference

  • A third-party firewall or "gaming optimizer" can silently drop OSRS's socket after a few minutes.
  • Add the Jagex Launcher / Old School client to your firewall's allow-list (both inbound and outbound).
  • On corporate or school networks, the admin may block game ports entirely — you'll need a different network.

⑮ ISP routing problems

Occasionally your internet provider's route to Jagex's servers is flaky (common with mobile tethering and some rural ISPs). A different connection type or a reputable gaming VPN with a clean residential IP often stabilizes it. This is the one case where a VPN actually helps a disconnect problem.

🔋last Resort Before You Rage-Quit

If all else fails, reinstall the official client / clean-reinstall mobile to rule out a corrupted local file, then test on a phone hotspot. If the hotspot is stable, your home ISP/router is 100% the cause — not Jagex.

8. ❓ FAQ — Disconnects & World Errors

Why do I keep getting randomly disconnected in the middle of bosses?

Almost always local packet loss (Wi-Fi congestion, router NAT timeout, or upload saturation from a background backup). Run a constant ping test while you play — if "Request timed out" lines up with your disconnects, it's your network, and the §4 tree will point you to the fix.

What does "World Unavailable" actually mean?

It means that specific world is down, full, or being restarted — not that your account or game is broken. Pick a different world from the list. If every world shows it, that's a full outage (server-side), and you should wait for an official all-clear.

Is the silent Wednesday logout a ban or a hack?

No. It's the weekly game-update shutdown that boots everyone online at once. It's expected, affects all players, and never results in a ban. Wait for the "Worlds are available" message and re-log. See §5.

Can a VPN cause random disconnects?

Yes — VPN exit nodes shared with bot farms get IP-banned by Jagex, causing instant or repeated disconnects. Disable the VPN first. If you need one for routing, use a provider with clean residential IPs; details in our VPN Login Blocked fix.

How do I prove it's Jagex and not me?

Two checks: (1) Are other players disconnecting at the same time (status feed / Reddit)? (2) Does a different device or network stay stable while yours drops? If yes to either, it's server-side or your local network respectively. The 60-second tree makes this unambiguous.
