OSRS Mortimer Slayer Master Guide 2026 — Task Choice, Mortifiers & Wyrmscraig Unlock

🆕 NEW
Published September 12, 2026  |  15 min read  |  By Yanchun Wu

Mortimer is the highest-level Slayer Master in Old School RuneScape, locked behind Wyrmscraig and the Fallen From Grace quest. He does not hand you a single assignment like every other master. He offers you a genuine choice of tasks, layers each one with unlockable modifiers called Mortifiers, and assigns only monsters that have a superior variant. This guide covers his unlock requirements, the two-then-three task choice system, every Mortifier, the punishing 100-point skip tax, his shared task pool, and exactly who should be grinding him for the Imbued Heart and Eternal Gem.

1. 🗡️ Why Mortimer Changes How You Play Slayer

Slayer has always been a skill of adaptation. You take whatever the master hands you, you build your block list around the monsters you hate, and you skip the rest with Turael when the streak is not worth protecting. Mortimer flips that loop on its head. Released with the Wyrmscraig update, he is a skeleton Slayer Master who presents you with a small menu of assignments instead of a single roll, and then sweetens each option with a modifier you can weigh against the others.

That single design choice rebalances the entire conversation around efficiency. On a normal master you are chasing one "best" task and treating everything else as a speed bump. With Mortimer you are comparing three offers at once: one might pay more Slayer points, another might be longer for better XP, and a third might quietly boost your superior unique chance. The fight is no longer against the RNG — it is against your own prioritization. For players who enjoy theorycrafting their Slayer session instead of autopiloting it, that is a genuine breath of fresh air.

💡 He Is a Choice Master, Not a Replacement

Jagex has been clear that Mortimer is not meant to replace Duradel, Konar or Nieve. He is an alternative playstyle for players who want agency over their assignments and who are specifically hunting the superior Slayer table. If your goal is a points streak, the traditional masters are still the faster route. If your goal is an Imbued Heart, Mortimer is now one of the strongest options in the game.

2. 🔓 Unlock Requirements & Getting to Wyrmscraig Cavern

Mortimer sits in Wyrmscraig Cavern, which you only reach during the Fallen From Grace quest. You do not need to fully complete the quest to talk to him — partial completion that grants cavern access is enough — but you do need the island itself unlocked first. That means Sailing is effectively a prerequisite, because docking at Wyrmscraig requires a ship and the relevant voyage.

📜 Mortimer Access Requirements

  • Combat level 100 and Slayer level 70 — the standard unlock path.
  • Or 99 Slayer at any Combat level — show him the Slayer cape and he unlocks immediately.
  • Partial completion of Fallen From Grace — you need the Wyrmscraig Cavern opened, not the whole quest finished.
  • Wyrmscraig reached by ship — plan your Sailing progression before you chase him.

If you are still climbing toward the Sailing level needed to dock at Wyrmscraig, the fastest route is laid out in our Wyrmscraig Sailing guide. For most accounts the realistic gate is not the 70 Slayer — it is getting the island online in the first place.

Getting to Mortimer Fast

🧭 Three Reliable Routes

  • Slayer ring teleport — the "Teleport" option drops you at the Wyrmscraig Cavern entrance, the single most reliable way to reach him.
  • Astral Contact (Lunar spellbook) — you can take a new assignment from Mortimer remotely without leaving your slayer spot, which is huge for trip efficiency.
  • Teleport to Boat — if you dock a ship inside the cavern with a greater teleport focus, you can blink straight to it.

💡 Unlock the Slayer Cape Shortcut Early

If you are already 99 Slayer, Mortimer is unlocked the moment you can reach the cavern. That makes him a natural first stop on a maxed Slayer account the second Wyrmscraig opens, because you skip the 100 Combat / 70 Slayer gate entirely.

3. 🎯 The Task Choice System: Pick Your Assignment

When you ask Mortimer for work, he does not assign one task. He offers a choice. You start with two candidate assignments, and after you complete enough tasks for him a third option opens up, giving you even more control over what you grind. Each creature can only appear once per prompt, so you will never be asked to pick between two identical offers — you cannot be offered Crawling Hands twice in the same window.

You can, however, get the same monster back to back across different prompts even without the Slayer cape proc. The RNG is per-prompt, not per-session, which means a creature you just finished can legitimately show up again next time.

⚙️ How the Choice Behaves

  • Two options at first — pick one and the other disappears.
  • Three options after enough completed tasks — more flexibility once you have proven yourself to him.
  • No duplicates in one prompt — each offered creature is unique to that window.
  • Streak is separate — like Krystilia, Mortimer tracks his own streak independently of normal masters.

One quirk matters a lot for planning. If Mortimer has already offered you a choice, you cannot take a task from any other Slayer Master until you resolve it. Try, and you get the message that Mortimer has provided you with a task choice and you must go to him to pick. This stops you from shopping around for a better offer elsewhere after seeing his menu — the choice he gives is the choice you work with.

💡 Read the Whole Panel Before Clicking

The modifier on each task is shown up front. A longer task with a quantity bonus is not automatically worse than a short one with a points bonus — it depends on whether you are chasing XP, points, or superior drops that session. Decide your goal before you walk up so the choice is fast and deliberate rather than a panic click.

4. 🔧 Mortifiers Explained: What Each Modifier Does

Every task Mortimer offers carries at least one modifier, referred to in his dialogue as a Mortifier. These are not random fluff — they are the reason his assignments feel worthwhile even when the creature itself is not your favorite. You unlock more modifier types as you complete tasks for him, and early on you only see the basics.

🎁 Mortifier Unlock Thresholds

  • 0 tasks: Slayer Point Modifier — the task awards bonus Slayer reward points on completion.
  • 0 tasks: Task Quantity Modifier — adjusts how many creatures you are assigned, scaling with the monster's tier.
  • 25 tasks: Clue Scroll Modifier — improves clue scroll drop rates during the task.
  • 50 tasks: Slayer XP Modifier — boosts Slayer XP gained from the creatures.
  • 75 tasks: Superior Drop Rate Modifier — increases your chance to hit the superior unique table.
  • 100 tasks: Third Task Choice — unlocks the third assignment option in his menu.

The Slayer Point and Task Quantity modifiers are available from the very first assignment, which is why even an early Mortimer task has something to compare. The Superior Drop Rate modifier is the one superior hunters care about most: it raises your odds of landing the unique table on a superior spawn rather than just increasing how often superiors appear. That distinction matters, because more superiors alone would inflate Slayer XP on normally AFK tasks, whereas better unique odds directly helps the Imbued Heart grind without disrupting the XP curve.

💡 Stack the Right Modifier to Your Goal

Chasing the Imbued Heart? Weight toward tasks carrying the Superior Drop Rate modifier once it is unlocked. Chasing points for a big unlock? Take the Slayer Point modifier. The whole point of Mortimer is that you get to optimize the assignment to the session instead of hoping RNG hands you the perfect task on a single master.

5. 💸 Skip & Block Costs: Plan Your Two Slots

Mortimer's flexibility comes with a stiff price. Because you can simply decline his offers and pick a better one, Jagex made skipping and blocking far more expensive than on a normal master. If you are the kind of player who skips constantly, he will drain your Slayer points faster than any other master in the game.

⚠️ The Tax Breakdown

  • Skip cost: 100 Slayer points, versus the usual 30 on a standard master.
  • Block slots: only two (six on normal masters, seven with the Elite Lumbridge & Draynor Diary).
  • Block cost: 120 Slayer points per slot.
  • No Turael reset: you cannot wipe a Mortimer task by taking a replacement from Turael.
  • Shared stores: storing a Mortimer task uses one of your normal store slots, which are shared across all masters.

The two block slots are the part players underestimate most. On Duradel you might block six creatures you never want to see. On Mortimer you get two, so your block list has to be surgical: block the tasks that genuinely wreck your motivation or your GP per hour, not every mildly annoying monster. Everything else, you either skip (expensive) or finish.

The shared task store is a trap for the careless. If you are holding an undesirable Mortimer task in storage and you also pick up an undesirable task from another master, you are forced to complete one of the two before you can free the slot. Plan your storage around that interaction so you never lock yourself out of a useful master.

💡 Skip Rarely, Block Wisely

Because a skip costs 100 points, treat it as a last resort. The two-block strategy that works best is to block your single worst GP task and your single most hated XP task, then accept everything else and use the choice system to steer toward the good offers. Mortimer rewards players who commit to a plan, not players who reroll constantly.

6. 📜 Mortimer's Task Pool & Shared Masters

Mortimer's defining trait is that he assigns only creatures with a superior variant. That instantly makes him the most concentrated superior-hunting master in the game, because every single task he can give you has a superior you can chase. For Imbued Heart and Eternal Gem farmers, that focus is the entire appeal.

He is also the only Slayer Master who can assign Venators directly. Venators are Vampyrium pack beasts that otherwise only appear on a Vampyre task after the Blood Moon Rises quest, so Mortimer giving them out by name is a unique route into that content. On top of that, he shares several tasks that used to feel exclusive to one master: Hydras (historically Konar), Infernal Mages (Vannaka) and Rockslugs (Mazchna). If you wanted those creatures without their original master, Mortimer is now a legitimate alternative.

🐾 Notable Shared & Exclusive Tasks

  • Venators — exclusive to Mortimer; require Blood Moon Rises completion to fight.
  • Hydras — shared with Konar's assignment pool.
  • Infernal Mages — shared with Vannaka's assignment pool.
  • Rockslugs — shared with Mazchna's assignment pool.
  • All superiors — every creature in his pool has a superior variant by design.

Because Mortimer is built around superiors, you can also complete his tasks at the corresponding boss when applicable. A creature that has a boss variant can be finished there, which gives you flexibility if you would rather fight the boss version than the slayer version. That interplay is worth understanding before you commit to him as your main master.

💡 Pair Him With a Venator Plan

If you want Venators specifically, Mortimer is the cleanest assignment route. Our Venator / Vampyrium Slayer guide covers the gear, the vampyrebane weapons and the Blood Moon Rises unlock in full, so you can line up the task and the trip in one plan.

7. 👥 Who Should (and Shouldn't) Use Mortimer

Mortimer is a destination master, not a starter master. He rewards accounts that already have Slayer progression, Wyrmscraig access, and a clear goal for their assignments. Used correctly he is one of the best masters in the game; used blindly he is a point-draining trap.

✅ Use Mortimer If You...

  • Want superior uniques — the Imbued Heart and Eternal Gem grinds are his headline use case.
  • Value choice over autopilot — you enjoy weighing XP against points against drop rate each session.
  • Skip rarely — you can commit to a plan and finish what you pick.
  • Are already at Wyrmscraig — you finished Fallen From Grace and want to use the cavern.
  • Are 99 Slayer — instant unlock the moment you reach the island.

❌ Avoid Mortimer If You...

  • Are low Slayer — below 70 Slayer you cannot unlock him on the standard path at all.
  • Skip constantly — the 100-point tax will bankrupt your points economy.
  • Want a points streak — traditional masters are faster and cheaper for streak grinding.
  • Lack Wyrmscraig — no island access means no Mortimer, full stop.

💡 A Practical Weekly Routine

Unlock Wyrmscraig and Mortimer, set a two-slot block list around your worst tasks, and prefer assignments whose modifier matches your session goal. Track superior unique progress separately from raw Slayer XP so you can see whether the choice system is actually paying off. Once the third option unlocks, re-evaluate which creatures you were avoiding — the extra choice often flips a previously skipped monster into a keeper.

8. ❓ Mortimer Slayer Master FAQs

Q: How do I unlock Mortimer?

Reach Wyrmscraig Cavern during the Fallen From Grace quest, then meet either 100 Combat and 70 Slayer or 99 Slayer at any Combat level. Partial quest completion that opens the cavern is enough; you do not need the whole quest finished.

Q: Where is Mortimer located?

In Wyrmscraig Cavern. The fastest way there is the Slayer ring teleport to the cavern entrance, or the Lunar spell Astral Contact to take an assignment remotely.

Q: How many tasks does Mortimer offer?

He offers two assignments at first, and a third option unlocks after you complete enough tasks for him. Each creature appears only once per prompt, so you never choose between duplicates.

Q: What are Mortifiers?

Mortifiers are unlockable modifiers attached to every task he offers. They can boost Slayer points, adjust task quantity, improve clue rates, add Slayer XP, or raise your superior unique drop chance, and they unlock as you complete tasks for him.

Q: Why is skipping so expensive with Mortimer?

Because he lets you pick between assignments, the skip tax is raised to 100 Slayer points and Turael cannot reset his tasks. The design pushes you to commit to a choice rather than reroll constantly.

Q: How many block slots does Mortimer have?

Only two, each costing 120 Slayer points. That is far fewer than the six (or seven with the Elite Lumbridge & Draynor Diary) on a normal master, so your block list must be carefully chosen.

Q: Does Mortimer assign Venators?

Yes. He is the only Slayer Master who can assign Venators directly. You still need to complete the Blood Moon Rises quest before you can fight them, but he is the cleanest route into that task.

Q: Is Mortimer good for an Imbued Heart grind?

Extremely. Every creature he assigns has a superior variant, and his Superior Drop Rate modifier specifically improves your chance to hit the superior unique table. For superior hunters he is one of the best masters available.

Q: Can I store a Mortimer task and take another master's task?

Stores are shared with the normal Slayer system, so a stored Mortimer task uses one of your regular slots. If you hold an undesirable Mortimer task and pick up another undesirable task elsewhere, you must finish one of the two to free the slot.

Q: Does Mortimer replace Duradel?

No. He is a specialist master for choice-driven, superior-focused Slayer. Players chasing a points streak or a specific efficient task will often still prefer traditional masters, using Mortimer when superior hunting is the priority.
