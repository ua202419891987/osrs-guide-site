1. 📉 Why the 2026 Nerf Watch Matters

OSRS runs on an iron law: when one method dominates the money-making tier list, it becomes a nerf target. The 2026 content cycle leaned hard into this. After the launch of Sailing and the sweeping economy reviews, Jagex shipped more balance passes in the first seven months of 2026 than in the previous two years combined. If you were a one-method grinder, you felt it.

This page exists because nerf news is scattered across blog posts, hidden update threads, and dead Reddit threads by the time you need it. We centralise every 2026 change in one calendar, rate its real impact, and point you at the replacement. The point is not to complain — it is to move your GP before the next patch. See how last cycle played out in our Mid-Year Review.

💡 The Mindset That Saves Millions

A nerf does not delete value — it moves it. When the top method drops 30%, the second-best method usually rises 30% as players rotate in. The winners are the players who already had a tier-2 method trained and ready. That is the entire game of the Nerf Watch.

2. 🧊 January–March 2026: Early-Year Sweeps

The year opened with quiet but meaningful trims. These were not headline nerfs — they were the "slow the inflation" type that compounds over a season.

① January: Herblore secondary sink adjustment

Jagex increased the Grand Exchange tax on a cluster of high-volume secondaries (snape grass, ranarr weed) from 1% to 2%, nudging margin flippers. The direct GP/hr hit on GE flipping was small (~6–8%), but it signalled intent: the economy team was watching velocity.

② February: Shifting Sailing rewards

Two Sailing money makers launched in late 2025 (cannonball bounties, naval salvage) were earning above the target band. February's patch cut their hourly yield by ~18% and rebalanced the crew wage payout. Players who had trained Sailing early still profited — just less absurdly. Our Sailing Money Guide was updated the same week.

③ March: Slayer Point rework ripple

The Slayer rework lowered points on low-weight tasks and boosted them on high-weight bosses, indirectly nerfing the "block everything easy, farm points" strategy. Net effect: point-farming via junk tasks dropped ~25%, pushing players toward real boss Slayer.

3. 🌞 April–June 2026: The Summer Sweep-Up

This was the heavy period. The "Summer Sweep-Up" promised in the 2026 roadmap arrived in waves, and it is the reason this tracker exists.

① April: Boss loot table compression

Several 2025 bosses (Royal Titans, Phantom Muspah alts, and the Chasm content) had outlier drop rates compressed toward the mean. Unique drop rates rose slightly while consistent alchables fell. High-volume runners saw GP/hr dip 12–20%.

② May: The Farming & Herb run normalization

Herb runs had quietly become one of the best AFK GP methods in the game. May cut seed yield variance (fewer ultra-rare snaps) and trimmed the tokkul-exchange loop. Hourly profit fell ~22% but remained solid — it stayed on the tier list, just lower.

③ June: Summer Sweep-Up main pass

The headline June update hit three over-performing methods at once: (a) a Skilling pet-related boost was removed from Wintertodt, (b) the Tempoross scaling bonus for groups was flattened, and (c) a popular F2P-to-members flip route was closed. Combined, these reallocated an estimated 40M+ GP/day back into the broader economy. Full detail in our Summer Sweep-Up Guide.

⚠️ If You Ignored June

Anyone still running the pre-June Tempoross group bonus or the closed flip route lost money and efficiency for a full month. The fix was free: pivot to the replacement methods listed in the Impact Table below. The lesson is the point of this whole page.

4. 🔥 July 2026 Live: Current Active Nerfs

As of July 30, 2026, these changes are live and currently shaping the meta. This is the section to check first each time you log in.

① Yama unique tax (July 9)

The Chasm boss's most valuable alchables saw a 15% quantity reduction per kill, shaving ~10% off raw GP/hr for solos while leaving the unique chase unchanged.

② Sailing phase-2 bounty rebalance (July 17)

Phase-2 naval bounties were over-performing versus the dev target. The July 17 hotfix trimmed top-tier bounty payouts by ~14% but buffed mid-tier contracts, flattening the curve.

③ GE 2% tax expansion (July 24)

The 2% tax applied in January expanded to a wider secondary basket. Margin flippers should re-model their stacks — see our GE 2% Tax Explained piece for the exact affected items.

6. 🛡️ How to Adapt: 5 Moves to Protect Your Income

You cannot stop the nerfs. You can stop them from hurting. Do these five things and a 2026 patch becomes a non-event for your bank.

① Diversify across two skill types

Never let one method be >50% of your income. Pair a Skilling earner with a Bossing earner so when one gets trimmed, the other absorbs the shock. Our tier list ranks both sides.

② Read the dev blog before it hits live

Jagex always pre-announces balance passes. The Mid-Year Meta Shift analysis shows how to read a blog post and predict the cut 2 weeks early.

③ Keep a trained "tier-2" method ready

The replacement method should already be unlocked and practised, not something you learn the day after the nerf. Train it during downtimes.

④ Bank before the patch deadline

If a flip route or buy limit is closing, front-load your volume in the final week. Many players earned a month of profit in the last 72 hours before a route died.

⑤ Re-model after every hotfix

Re-run your numbers within 24 hours of any patch

A 14% trim is invisible if you keep grinding on autopilot. Set a calendar reminder: after every update, re-calculate GP/hr for your top 3 methods and re-rank them. The players who do this are the ones still climbing while others complain on the forums.

7. ⚔️ Bosses & Content That Got Touched

Beyond raw GP, several bosses changed in ways that affect how you approach them. Knowing these prevents wasted trips.

① Chasm / Yama content

The July 9 alchable trim means Yama is now a unique chase boss more than a steady GP boss. Bring a team to split and keep trip volume high. The glyph one-shot mechanic is unchanged — covered in our dedicated Yama guide.

② Royal Titans & Phantom Muspah

Loot compression made these marginally less efficient but far more stable — they are now "safe" mid-tier earners rather than spike plays. Good for account progression.

③ Wintertodt & Tempoross

Both lost bonus scaling. They remain excellent for low-to-mid accounts and FM/Firemaking training, but stop treating them as top-tier GP. See What Changed in 2026 for the returning-player view.

9. ❓ Nerf Watch FAQs

How many OSRS nerfs shipped in 2026?

At least 12 distinct balance passes through July 30, 2026, spanning Skilling, Bossing, Sailing, and the Grand Exchange. The heaviest concentration was the June Summer Sweep-Up, which bundled three method nerfs into one update.

Which 2026 nerf hurt players the most?

The closed F2P-to-members flip route (June) was the only true 100% kill — it went to zero overnight. For raw GP moved, the Tempoross group-bonus flattening reallocated the most economy-wide value.

Are nerfs always bad for the game?

No. Most 2026 nerfs targeted outlier methods that were crowding out content. By trimming the top 5–10%, Jagex keeps dozens of methods viable. The losers are one-method players; the winners are diversified ones — which is the whole thesis of this tracker.

How do I know a nerf is coming before it hits?

Read the developer blogs. Jagex pre-announces balance passes weeks ahead. Our Mid-Year Meta Shift guide shows the exact tells to watch for.

What should I do the day a method I use gets nerfed?

Re-model your top 3 methods within 24 hours, pivot the trimmed one to its replacement (see the Impact Table), and keep grinding — the GP simply moved sideways. Do not quit the game over a 15% trim.

Where can I see the full replacement list?

The Impact Table in Section 5 maps every nerfed method to its best live replacement, and the 2026 Tier List ranks the replacements by current GP/hr.

Will Sailing methods get nerfed again?

Likely. Sailing is new and Jagex is actively tuning it — the July 17 phase-2 hotfix proves they watch it closely. Keep a non-Sailing earner trained so a Sailing trim never hurts.
