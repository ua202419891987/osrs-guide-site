❓ Membership FAQ

Did membership prices really go up in 2026?

Yes. In most regions the monthly rate rose from $11.99 to $14.99, the 6-month and yearly plans rose proportionally, and bonds climbed from $6.99 to $9.99. Exact figures vary by country due to regional pricing, so check the Price Increase guide for your local currency.

Is membership still worth it after the hike?

For active players, almost certainly. Members unlocks 15 exclusive skills, 200+ quests, the entire members world map, and every high-value boss. If you play more than ~8–10 hours a week, that content easily outweighs $14.99. If you're a casual or returning player unsure you'll stick around, start with "Is It Worth It?" before spending.

Bond or subscription — which is cheaper?

A subscription is cheaper and more predictable if you pay with real money. A bond costs more per month but can be bought with in-game GP (around 12.8M GP), making membership effectively free for players who can farm that gold. The Bond vs Subscription guide breaks down every plan side by side.

What do I actually lose by staying free-to-play?

Free-to-play keeps the core game but locks most skills past level 20–50, roughly a third of quests, the entire members map, and nearly all profitable bosses. You can still train, socialize, and even earn a bond — but progression slows hard. The F2P to P2P guide shows the smoothest point to upgrade.
