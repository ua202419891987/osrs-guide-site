Combat Achievements for Beginners: Where to Start (Easy & Medium) 2026

Combat Achievements (CAs) look like an end-game wall, but the first two tiers — Easy and Medium — are built for accounts exactly like yours. This guide is the filter: which tasks are actually doable at a low level, how to sort them by your gear and goals, what rewards to grab first, and a roadmap that takes you from combat 40 to your first boss without the burnout.

📅 Stage 1.4 — Part of the Combat Progression Series · 16 min read · Updated August 2026
🛡️ Level: 40+
🆕 NEW

Combat Achievements are a bank of repeatable combat challenges split into five tiers. Most new players freeze at the tier list and quit before earning a single point. You don't need that. Start with our full Combat Achievements overview if you want the complete tier breakdown, but if you are still building your account, follow the new player progression guide mindset: do the easy wins, bank the points, and let the rewards compound.

💡 Quick Verdict

Easy + Medium together hand you enough points for the Gold reward tier (5,000 points) with margin to spare, and Gold is the sweet spot for a beginner: it unlocks the useful account-wide perks without demanding maxed gear. The only mistake is trying to brute-force Hard/Elite tasks early. Filter by what your account can already do, knock out the low-hanging fruit, and the higher tiers open on their own later.

1. 🎯 What Combat Achievements Are (and Why Start Low)

Combat Achievements are a collection of optional combat challenges tracked in the Achievements tab (the sword icon next to your Quest list). Each completed task grants points; points fill reward tiers; tiers unlock permanent account perks from the Combat Achievement Gallery in the Warriors' Guild. None of it is required to play — but the low-tier perks are genuinely useful, and the point total is a neat badge of progression.

① The Five Tiers at a Glance

CAs are split into Easy, Medium, Hard, Elite, and Master. The point thresholds are fixed and the same for every account:

TierPoints to ReachRealistic for a Beginner?
EasyStarts at 0 (first milestone is Bronze)✅ Yes — almost all of it
MediumMostly within the first 5,000 points✅ Yes — most of it with effort
HardBuilds past 5,000🟡 Later — needs better gear
EliteBuilds past 10,000🔴 End-game only
MasterTop tier, 20,000+ range🔴 Maxed mains only

The key point: you do not need to clear a whole tier to benefit. You need points, and Easy + Medium are where the points are cheapest for a low-level account.

② How Points and Reward Tiers Work

Every task has a point value. Easy tasks are worth roughly 5–20 points each; Medium tasks run about 15–50 points. Reward tiers are unlocked at cumulative point totals, and each tier lets you claim a reward from the Gallery. The thresholds beginners care about:

Reward TierCumulative PointsWhy a Beginner Cares
Bronze1,000First permanent unlock, trivial from Easy tasks
Silver2,500Another unlock; still all Easy + a few Medium
Gold5,000⭐ The beginner target — strong perks
Runeforged10,000Needs Hard tasks; come back later
Dragon15,000End-game
Mythical20,000Completionist

③ Why Easy + Medium First Is the Right Call

The higher tiers punish you with requirements like 90+ combat stats, BiS gear, and no-hit solo kills of brutal bosses. Easy and Medium tasks, by contrast, are tuned for accounts still training. Many only ask you to do something you were going to do anyway — kill a boss once, survive a phase, use a specific style. Knocking these out early gives you free rewards and a point buffer that makes the scary tiers feel optional rather than mandatory.

A useful habit: open the Achievements tab before every boss or Slayer trip and read the tasks for that monster. You will often find a one-line challenge — "kill it using only Ranged" or "finish without eating" — that you can satisfy during a trip you were already taking. Those free points are the entire reason Easy/Medium feel effortless: you are not grinding CAs, you are spotting the ones your normal play already completes.

💡 Mindset Shift

Treat CAs like a side-quest tracker, not a checklist. Every time you fight a boss or train a style, glance at its CA tab. If a task says "kill it using only Ranged," you just earned points for playing normally. The players who "finish" CAs are usually the ones who never tried to sprint them.

2. 🟢 Easy Tier: The Tasks Actually Worth Doing

Easy tasks are your bread and butter. Most only need a membership, a weapon, and a willingness to read the task text. Below is the filtering logic: keep the tasks your account can already do, and park the rest.

① Filter: "Can I Do This With Current Gear?"

Before touching a task, ask one question — do I already own the gear to attempt it, or will I need to grind for it? If the answer is "grind," skip it for now. Easy tasks that pass the filter for a combat-40–70 account typically include:

  • Entry boss clears: Kill Scurrius, the Giant Mole, or a Barrows brother at least once. These are accessible with mid-tier gear.
  • Style-specific kills: Defeat a boss using only Melee, only Ranged, or only Magic. Pick the style your best gear supports.
  • Survival tasks: Finish a fight without leaving the arena, or without eating a specific food type.
  • Slayer-flavored tasks: Several Easy CAs overlap with normal Slayer — do the Slayer task, tick the CA box for free.

② The Best Easy Tasks by Content Type

Not all Easy tasks are equal. The table ranks them by how little setup they need. "Setup" means gear or stats you must acquire first.

③ Easy Quick Wins You Can Finish This Week

If you want 1,000 points (Bronze) fast, run this loop: do your daily Scurrius kill (10), knock out a Giant Mole trip (10), clear one Barrows tunnel (10–20), and grab every Slayer-overlap task your block list allows (5–15 each). Five or six tasks get you to Bronze with zero gear upgrades. For the full task-by-task walkthrough, our Easy Combat Achievements walkthrough lists every Easy task with exact steps.

🎯 Bronze-in-a-Week Plan

  1. Day 1–2: Scurrius kill + Giant Mole kill (≈20 pts)
  2. Day 3–4: One full Barrows run, all six brothers (≈60–120 pts)
  3. Day 5–7: Slayer tasks that overlap CA categories (≈50–100 pts)
  4. Fill gaps with any remaining style-lockedEasy kills
  5. Total: comfortably past 1,000 → claim Bronze

3. 🟡 Medium Tier: Best Bang-for-Buck Tasks

Medium tasks are where beginners either get stuck or get their biggest payoff. They demand a bit more — a specific kill method, a tighter rotation, or a slightly scary boss — but the point values are high enough that a handful of Medium tasks out-earn a dozen Easy ones.

① Which Medium Tasks to Do First

Prioritize Medium tasks that reuse bosses you already fight. If you are already doing Slayer or money-making at a boss, the Medium CA for that boss is "free" relative to the trip you'd take anyway. Good early Medium targets:

  • Barrows flawless brother: Kill a brother without taking damage from it. Easier than it sounds with Protect prayers.
  • Scurrius no-food: Clear Scurrius without eating — very doable once you know the pattern.
  • Spec-weapon kills: Finish a boss using only special attacks for the last chunk of HP.
  • Speed kills: Defeat a boss under a time threshold — rewards efficient play, not gear.

② Medium vs Easy: Where Your Points Come From

The chart below shows roughly how points accumulate. Easy tasks are numerous but small; Medium tasks are fewer but fat. A balanced beginner grabs both.

③ Medium Tasks to Park (For Now)

Some Medium tasks secretly require end-game-adjacent gear — a specific magic level for a no-prayer kill, or a DPS check that needs Barrows/God gear. If a Medium task needs an upgrade you don't have, park it. You'll return after the mid-game gear progression or normal bossing. Forcing these early just burns food and motivation.

4. 🏆 Reward Priority: What to Unlock First

Points are meaningless until you spend them at the Combat Achievement Gallery in the Warriors' Guild. Each tier lets you claim a reward; pick the ones that help a low-level account most.

① The Reward System (Gallery)

The Gallery sits in the Warriors' Guild. You open the reward interface, see your current tier, and claim one reward per tier (some tiers offer a small choice). Rewards are permanent account-wide buffs — they are not consumed. The beginner-relevant ones improve quality-of-life: extra teleport charges, prayer restoration options, and combat conveniences that save time on every future trip.

Claiming is instant and free — walk to the totem in the Guild, click the tier you have reached, and pick (where offered). There is no downside and no expiry, so the only failure mode is forgetting to claim. Set a reminder: the moment your point total crosses 1,000, 2,500, or 5,000, open the Gallery. Players routinely sit on 4,900 points with Gold unclaimed for weeks, leaving a permanent perk unused.

② Best Beginner Rewards (Priority Order)

TierReward Type to PrioritizeWhy First
BronzeTeleport / travel unlockSaves bank trips; compounds daily
SilverPrayer / restore convenienceLonger boss trips without banking
GoldBest account-wide perk available⭐ Biggest beginner payoff

Concrete example of the compounding effect: a teleport reward you grab at Bronze saves you thirty seconds per trip. Over a thousand trips that is hours of real playtime returned to you — for a task you finished in your first week. That is why the low tiers matter more than their small point values suggest.

③ What NOT to Rush

Don't burn motivation chasing a single high-tier reward. The Master-tier cosmetics and elite perks are years away for most accounts, and they don't change your day-to-day grind. Spend your energy reaching Gold (5,000 points) — that is the line where beginner value peaks. Past Gold, the next meaningful jump is Runeforged (10,000), which needs Hard tasks you'll naturally accumulate while bossing later.

⚠️ Don't Claim the Wrong Reward

Some tiers let you pick between rewards. Read both before claiming — a permanent choice can't be undone. When torn, pick the travel/teleport or prayer convenience option; those help every account type. Cosmetic-only choices are safe to defer.

5. 🔍 The Filter Method: Sort Tasks by Your Account

This is the differentiator. The site already has a CA overview and an Easy walkthrough; this section is the method for deciding what to do on your specific account, today. Use the three filters below in order.

① Filter 1 — Combat Level & Stats

Your combat level and base stats cap what's realistic. Rule of thumb:

  • Combat 40–60: Entry bosses (Scurrius), Giant Mole, basic Barrows. Skip anything mentioning "no prayer" or "speed."
  • Combat 60–80: Add Medium Barrows flawless, Scurrius no-food, style-locked kills on accessible bosses.
  • Combat 80+: Start peeking at Hard tasks you can reach with Barrows/God gear.

② Filter 2 — Gear You Already Own

Cross-reference every task against your bank. If a task needs a weapon or prayer level you lack, it moves to "parked." Example: a "Ranged-only boss kill" is free if you own a Rune Crossbow + Broad bolts; otherwise it waits until your skill priority plan funds the ranged kit.

You Own…Do These CA Tasks Now
Magic shortbow / basic mage setMagic-only kills on low-defense bosses
Rune Crossbow + boltsRanged-only kills, safe-spot tasks
Full Guthan's / BarrowsFlawless / no-food tasks on weak bosses
43+ PrayerAll Protect-prayer survival tasks

③ Filter 3 — Your Goal (Money / PvM / Slayer)

Finally, sort by what you're already doing so CAs ride along for free:

  • Money-making focus: Do CAs on the bosses you already farm (Giant Mole, Barrows). Zero extra trips.
  • PvM focus: Chase style-locked and no-food tasks on bosses you're learning — they force clean mechanics.
  • Slayer focus: Filter the CA list to Slayer-overlap tasks and clear them during normal assignments via the Slayer beginner guide.
💡 The 10-Second Filter

Open the CA tab. For each task ask: (1) Can my stats do it? (2) Do I own the gear? (3) Does it overlap my current grind? If all three are yes → do it this week. If any is no → park it. You'll revisit parked tasks automatically as your account grows.

6. 🗺️ Account-Progress Roadmap (40 → First Boss)

Here is the staged plan that turns a fresh combat-40 account into one with Gold-tier CAs and a first real boss under its belt. It mirrors the natural new player progression.

① Stage A — Combat 40 to 60 (Bronze → Silver)

Goal: 2,500 points. Actions: Scurrius kills, Giant Mole trips, Barrows brother clears, Slayer-overlap tasks. You'll hit Silver without leaving mid-tier gear. Use this stage to learn Protect prayers — they make every later CA easier.

② Stage B — Combat 60 to 80 (Silver → Gold)

Goal: 5,000 points. Actions: Medium Barrows flawless runs, Scurrius no-food, style-locked kills on bosses you can now reach, and your first "speed kill" attempts. By the end of this stage you claim Gold — the beginner finish line.

③ Stage C — Past Gold (Parked Tasks Auto-Unlock)

Once you're bossing regularly (Vorkath, Zulrah, GWD), the Hard-tier tasks you parked earlier become reachable. You don't grind them — they complete as a byproduct of normal progression. This is the whole point of filtering: the work you deferred pays off on its own.

A40–60Bronze → SilverScurrius, Mole, Barrows, Slayer CAs
B60–80Silver → GoldMedium flawless, no-food, style-locks
C80+Gold → RuneforgedBossing byproduct, Hard tasks

7. 🚫 Common Beginner Mistakes

These are the traps that make people quit CAs at combat 50. Avoid all three.

① Mistake: Skipping Easy to "Save Time"

Some players jump straight at Hard tasks for big points and get wiped repeatedly. Easy tasks are cheap points and free rewards. Skipping them trades guaranteed progress for frustration. Always clear Easy first — it's the floor under everything else.

② Mistake: Wrong Task Order

Doing tasks in the order they appear in the tab, instead of by your account, wastes trips. A "no-prayer Vorkath" task at combat 60 is a wall; a "Scurrius kill" is a warm-up. Order by the filter method (Section 5), not by the list. Your point total will be identical, but your sanity won't.

③ Mistake: Ignoring the Rewards

Players hoard points and never visit the Gallery. Points do nothing until claimed. The moment you cross 1,000, go claim Bronze. That teleport or prayer perk helps every future hour. For the advanced reward choices later, our advanced CA guide breaks down the elite picks.

💡 One Rule to Remember

Easy first, filter by account, claim at every tier. Do those three things and you'll hit Gold before you realize it — no maxed gear, no burnout, no spreadsheet.

8. ❓ Combat Achievements FAQs

What combat level should I be before starting CAs?

There's no hard minimum, but combat 40 is a comfortable floor because you can reach Scurrius, the Giant Mole, and basic Barrows, which cover most Easy tasks. Below that you'll mostly be limited to Slayer-overlap tasks. Start whenever you have 43 Prayer and a mid-tier weapon.

Do I need membership to do Combat Achievements?

Yes. All CA content (Scurrius, Barrows, boss tasks, the Gallery in the Warriors' Guild) is members-only. F2P accounts cannot earn CA points. If you're pre-membership, focus on the new player guide first.

How many points do I need for the Gold tier?

Gold requires 5,000 cumulative points. Easy tasks (≈5–20 each) plus a handful of Medium tasks (≈15–50 each) get there comfortably. Most players hit Gold somewhere in the combat 70–80 range without grinding a single Hard task.

Can I finish Easy and Medium without maxed gear?

Yes — that's the entire premise of this guide. The tasks are tuned for accounts still training. You'll need decent mid-tier gear (Barrows or God wars-level for the tougher Medium ones) but nowhere near BiS. The "no-prayer" and tight speed Medium tasks are the only ones that may wait.

What's the best first boss for a CA beginner?

Scurrius. It's the intended entry-level boss, has its own Easy and Medium tasks, and teaches boss mechanics (prayer, positioning, eat timing) without punishing you hard. The Giant Mole is the close second. Both appear constantly in Easy/Medium CA lists.

Should I do CAs or just keep training?

Both — they overlap. CAs are best treated as a tracker you consult while doing what you'd do anyway (Slayer, bossing, training). You rarely need dedicated CA trips at the low tiers. That's why the filter method in Section 5 works: it rides on your existing grind.

Do CA rewards expire or get consumed?

No. Rewards claimed at the Gallery are permanent account-wide unlocks. You claim one per tier (some tiers offer a choice), and it stays forever. That's why claiming at every tier matters — each is a permanent upgrade to your account.

How do I see which tasks I've completed?

Open the Achievements tab (sword icon, next to the Quest list) and select the Combat Achievements sub-tab. It shows every task, your completion status, and your current point total and tier. Check it before and after each boss trip.

What if a task feels impossible right now?

Park it. The filter method exists precisely for this. Every "impossible" task becomes reachable later as your stats and gear improve — usually as a byproduct of normal bossing. Forcing it early only wastes food and motivation. Revisit after Stage C.
