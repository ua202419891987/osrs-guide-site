Overview: Why Train Thieving?

Thieving is one of the fastest skills in OSRS — with the right methods, you can hit 99 in 30-40 hours. It's also highly profitable at high levels, with Vyres bringing in 700K+ GP/hr from Blood Shards and Elves providing consistent crystal shard income.

The Rogues' Outfit – DOUBLE All Pickpocket Loot!

Before you do any serious Thieving training, get the Rogues' Outfit first! This full set (5 pieces) provides a 100% chance to receive double loot from all pickpocketing, stalls, and chests. This literally doubles your GP/hr, seeds/hr, and blood shard rates.

How to Get the Rogues' Outfit

  1. Complete The Feud quest (requires 30 Thieving) — this unlocks the Rogues' Den
  2. Enter the Rogues' Den in Burthorpe (trapdoor in the Toad & Chicken pub basement)
  3. Navigate the maze — walls, traps, and guards. Level 50 Thieving + 50 Agility recommended
  4. At the end, open the Wall Safe (requires lockpick)
  5. Each safe opening has a chance to give one piece of the outfit
  6. Repeat until you collect all 5 pieces: Mask, Top, Trousers, Gloves, Boots

Average time to full set: 1-2 hours. 100% worth it — the double loot effect pays for itself dozens of times over on the way to 99.

Method 1: Blackjacking – FASTEST XP (270K+ XP/hr)

Blackjacking is the fastest Thieving XP in the game but also the most click-intensive. It involves luring a specific NPC (Menaphite Thug or Bandit), knocking them out, and double-pickpocketing them before they wake up.

Requirements

  • Level 45 Thieving (for Menaphite Thugs)
  • Blackjack (buy from Ali Morrisane after The Feud quest, or from Pollnivneach pub)
  • Access to Pollnivneach (requires The Feud quest progression)

How to Blackjack (Step-by-Step)

  1. Go to Pollnivneach (magic carpet from Shantay Pass)
  2. Find a Menaphite Thug (level 45-55) or Bandit (level 55-65)
  3. Right-click "Knock-out" with your blackjack equipped
  4. While the NPC is stunned, pickpocket twice rapidly before they wake
  5. If they wake and attack, knock them out again and repeat

Pro technique: After knock-out → pickpocket → pickpocket → knock-out. With perfect rhythm, this loops indefinitely and yields the highest XP rates in the game.

Time to 99: ~45-50 hours. Warning: This method causes wrist strain — take breaks! Use Ardougne Knights for longer, comfortable sessions.

Method 2: Ardougne Knights – BEST AFK (250K XP/hr, 5+ Min AFK)

Ardougne Knights are the meta pickpocketing method for most players. With a "splasher" (another account or friend casting a spell on the Knight), the Knight becomes permanently trapped in one spot — allowing you to click in one place for 5+ minutes without moving.

Requirements

  • Level 55 Thieving (to pickpocket Knights of Ardougne)
  • Rogues' Outfit (doubles coin drops)
  • Dodgy Necklaces (highly recommended! 25% chance to avoid stun)
  • Ardougne Medium Diary (+10% pickpocket success rate in Ardougne!)

How the "Splash Method" Works

  1. Go to Ardougne Market (east side, near the Gem Stall)
  2. Find the Knight — he patrols near the market stalls
  3. Have a "splasher" (another player or alt) cast a combat spell on the Knight with -65 Magic attack bonus to ensure they always splash (miss)
  4. Lure the Knight into the house north of the market (2-tile corridor) — he can't move while being splashed
  5. Once locked in place, spam-click pickpocket for as long as the splasher is active
  6. You'll need a new Dodgy Necklace every ~40-60 successful pickpockets

Many players host open splash worlds — check popular Thieving worlds (World 302, World 374) and simply walk up to a splashed Knight.

Method 3: Pyramid Plunder – Balanced XP + Loot

Pyramid Plunder is a Thieving minigame in Sophanem. You raid a pyramid, looting urns, chests, and sarcophagi for XP and valuable artifacts — including the rare Sceptre of the Gods.

Requirements

  • Level 21+ Thieving (each room requires higher level)
  • Completion of Icthlarin's Little Helper (grants access to Sophanem)
  • Food + Anti-Poison recommended (snakes and scarab swarms damage you)

How Pyramid Plunder Works

  1. Enter the pyramid and speak to the Guardian Mummy
  2. Each room has urns, chests, and a golden sarcophagus — search them for XP and loot
  3. Higher rooms = higher XP rates. At level 91, you can access Room 8 for the best XP
  4. You have 5 minutes per run before being kicked out
  5. Artifacts (stone, gold, etc.) can be sold to Simon Templeton for GP

Sceptre of the Gods

The Sceptre of the Gods (1/1000 chance from golden sarcophagus in Room 8+) is one of the most valuable Thieving items. It functions as a teleport to Pyramid Plunder and has a chance to auto-loot — looting urns or chests without clicking. With it, XP/hr jumps to 280K+ in Room 8.

Method 4: Master Farmers – Best Seed Source

Master Farmers are the best source of Ranarr seeds in the game, making them essential for Ironman accounts (Prayer potions) and profitable for mains. At level 71+ Thieving with the Rogues' Outfit, you get 2 seeds per successful pickpocket — double the profit.

Best Location: Hosidius Farming Guild

Use the Master Farmer in the Hosidius Farming Guild (requires 45 Farming + 60% Hosidius favour). Trap him in the north-east corner of the building — he can't move, you can click repeatedly. With the Seed Box (from Tithe Farm), seeds go straight into the box — no banking needed.

Method 5: Elves – High-Level Profit Pickpocketing

Pickpocketing Elves in Prifddinas is a top-tier profit method. Elves drop coins directly (280-350 GP per pickpocket) and have a 1/1024 chance to drop Crystal Teleport Seeds (value ~18K GP) and Enhanced Crystal Teleport Seeds (value ~3.2M GP!).

Requirements

  • Level 85 Thieving (Prifddinas elves)
  • Completion of Song of the Elves (grandmaster quest)
  • Rogues' Outfit + Dodgy Necklaces
  • Shadow Veil spell (Arceuus spellbook, level 47 Magic) — 15% better pickpocket success for 45 seconds

Method 6: Vyres – Blood Shard Hunting (700K+ GP/hr)

Pickpocketing Vyres in Darkmeyer is the most profitable Thieving method in the game, driven entirely by the Blood Shard drop (1/5,000 chance, worth ~7M GP). This is a must-do for any account that's completed Sins of the Father.

Requirements

  • Level 82 Thieving (Vyres)
  • Completion of Sins of the Father
  • Vyrewatch outfit from Darkmeyer shop (100% auto-pickpocket!)
  • Rogues' Outfit (for double coin drops)
  • Dodgy Necklaces

Ironman Thieving Strategy

Thieving is arguably the most important skill for Ironman accounts. It provides GP (Ardougne Knights, Elves), seeds (Master Farmers), Blood Shards (Vyres), and even crystal equipment charges (Elves).

Optimal Ironman Path

  1. 1-38: Quests (Fight Arena, The Feud) + Men/Women → Farmers
  2. 38-55: Master Farmers in Hosidius for Ranarr seeds — start your Prayer potion stockpile early!
  3. 55-85: Ardougne Knights with splash method — your main GP source. Every 50 GP coin drop is essential for kingdom management and shop buying.
  4. 85-99: Elves (Prifddinas) for consistent GP + Crystal Shards for armor, or Vyres for Blood Shards

Priorities: Rogues' Outfit → Ardougne Medium Diary → Dodgy Necklaces → Shadow Veil (if using Arceuus). Each boosts your efficiency significantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What's the fastest way to 99 Thieving?

Blackjacking Bandits at 250K-280K XP/hr. Total time: ~45-50 hours. But it's extremely click-intensive. For a slightly slower but far more comfortable approach, use Ardougne Knights with a splasher (250K XP/hr, 5+ min AFK).

Q: How do I find a splasher for Ardougne Knights?

Go to Ardougne Market, World 302 or 374. Look for the house north of the market stalls — there's almost always a splasher active. If not, ask in the Thieving Discord or try less populated worlds where you can set up your own with a friend or alt.

Q: Is Rogues' Outfit really worth it?

Absolutely, 100% yes. It doubles ALL pickpocket loot — coins, seeds, Blood Shards, Crystal Teleport Seeds, everything. Not getting it before training Thieving is like doing Slayer without a black mask. Get it ASAP after The Feud quest.

Q: What are Dodgy Necklaces and where do I get them?

Dodgy Necklaces provide a 25% chance to avoid being stunned when a pickpocket fails. They break after ~40-60 uses. Buy them from the GE (~1-2K GP each) or craft them (10 Crafting, Opal + Gold Bar). Budget ~100-200K GP for necklaces on the way to 99.

Q: Should I do Pyramid Plunder or Knights for XP?

Knights are better for pure XP/hr (250K vs 220K at Pyramid Plunder) and AFK (5+ min vs active gameplay). Pyramid Plunder's advantage is the Sceptre of the Gods — once you have it, XP/hr jumps to 280K+. Many players do Pyramid Plunder until they get the Sceptre, then switch to Knights.

Q: How many Ranarr seeds can I get per hour from Master Farmers?

At level 71+ Thieving with Rogues' Outfit: ~15-25 Ranarr seeds per hour. At level 85+: ~25-35 per hour. At 30K GP per seed, that's 750K-1M GP/hr equivalent — making Master Farmers the best Thieving profit method on paper (though seed prices fluctuate).

Q: Can you AFK Thieving to 99?

Yes, with Ardougne Knights and a splasher. Once the Knight is locked in place, you click one spot repeatedly for 5+ minutes. With Dodgy Necklaces, you rarely get stunned. This is the closest Thieving gets to "AFK" and is the most popular meta method for 99.

Conclusion

Thieving is a fast, profitable, and versatile skill that rewards preparation. Get the Rogues' Outfit, Ardougne Medium Diary, and Dodgy Necklaces before you start, and your journey to 99 will be smooth and profitable.

Recommended path for mains: Quests to 37 → Blackjack Bandits 45-55 (fast early XP) → Ardougne Knights (splash) 55-85 → Elves 85-99 (500K+ GP/hr). Total: ~55-60 hours, ~25M GP profit.

Recommended path for Ironman: Quests → Master Farmers 38-55 (stock Ranarrs) → Knights 55-85 (GP) → Mix of Elves/Vyres 85-99 depending on whether you need Crystal Shards or Blood Shards more.
