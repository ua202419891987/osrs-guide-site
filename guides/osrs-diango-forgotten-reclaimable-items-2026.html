1. 🧸 Who Is Diango and Where to Find Him

Diango is the cheerful NPC who runs Diango's Toy Store in Draynor Village, just north of the bank and near the market. For most of OSRS history he has been the go-to character for holiday and event cosmetics — the silly, festive, mostly untradeable outfit pieces handed out during annual events. If you are a returning player who last logged in during a Christmas or Halloween event, the odds are good that Diango is holding something you forgot you owned. New to the game? Start with our New Player Guide to learn how to get around Draynor and the rest of Gielinor.

Diango does three things, and players confuse them constantly:

  1. Distributes the current year's free holiday items during the event window.
  2. Reclaims holiday/event items you previously earned but later lost, dropped, or destroyed.
  3. Stores your holiday cosmetics in "Diango's memory" so they don't take up bank space.

Only the second and third matter if you are hunting for a forgotten item. The default right-click option on Diango opens a chat where he lists everything tied to your account. Good bank and inventory habits matter too — but Diango is the safety net most players never use.

💡 Teleport Shortcut

Draynor is one of the easiest villages to reach. Use a Draynor teleport, the Explorers' ring, a Wilderness sword, or simply walk from Lumbridge. Diango is a 15-second run from the bank.

2. 🎄 What Diango Stores: The Full Categories

Diango's reclaimable inventory is organized around the three big annual events plus a handful of one-off promotions. Here are the categories, with the kinds of items you will find in each.

Christmas / Winter event items

This is the largest group. Free Christmas cosmetics Diango can reclaim include the Santa costume pieces (hat, jacket, pants, gloves, boots), reindeer hat, Christmas tree (small & large), festive jumpers, snow globes, Christmas scarves, and various sacks of presents from yearly events. These are untradeable event rewards — not the tradeable "Santa hat" rare.

Halloween event items

The reclaimable Halloween cosmetics are the skeleton costume, zombie costume, ghost costume, werewolf set, and ghoulish set, plus assorted event novelties. Note: the tradeable "Halloween masks" (skeleton/zombie/ghost/demon masks that are rares) are NOT from Diango — those come from the Grand Exchange.

Easter event items

Diango reclaims the Easter ring, bunny ears, Easter baskets, and various cosmetic eggs handed out during Easter events. These are purely cosmetic fun, perfect for flexing during a holiday.

Toys & misc novelties

A long list of simple toys — yo-yos, spinning plates, mouse toys, balloons, kites, and similar gag items — are also reclaimable. Many were originally event or promotion giveaways.

One-off event & promotion items

Certain quest or promotion rewards (for example the Diango clothing set and a few anniversary items) are reclaimable as well. Availability depends on whether the specific item was ever tied to your account through an event.

⚠️ Diango vs. the Grand Exchange

The single biggest mistake: assuming Diango will replace an item you bought from another player. He only reclaims items your account originally received from an event or promotion. Bought rares are a GE problem, not a Diango problem.

3. 📋 The Reclaimable Items List (Free vs Paid vs Once)

Not every reclaim works the same way. Diango applies three tiers, and knowing them saves you confusion at the counter.

Reclaim Type How It Works Examples
Free — Unlimited Most holiday cosmetics can be reclaimed as many times as you lose them, at no cost. Santa costume pieces, bunny ears, skeleton costume
Free — Once Some promotional items can only be reclaimed a single time after the original is lost. Certain anniversary / promo novelties
Paid A few items cost a small GP fee to replace, or were originally purchased from Diango's shop. Some toy-store stock items
Not reclaimable Tradeable rares and a handful of discontinued promos cannot be replaced by Diango. Partyhats, Santa hat rare, Halloween masks, pumpkins

The practical rule: if it is untradeable and came from an event, Diango almost certainly has your back. If it is tradeable and valuable, it came from the GE and Diango will shrug. When in doubt, just talk to him — his dialogue lists exactly what your account is eligible to reclaim.

4. 🧠 Diango's Memory: Free Bank Space

Over years of playing, holiday cosmetics pile up. A single dedicated collector can fill dozens of bank slots with Santa hats (the cosmetic ones), trees, and costumes. Diango solves this with Diango's memory — a separate storage for holiday items.

How the memory storage works

  • Right-click Diango and choose the storage option to deposit holiday cosmetics.
  • The items leave your bank but stay tied to your account.
  • Withdraw them anytime, in any quantity, for free.
  • This is perfect for Achievement Diary players who want a tidy bank but still need their festive gear for clue scrolls or photos.

Using the memory is also the safest way to "never lose" a cosmetic. Once stored, an item cannot be dropped, lost on death, or destroyed by accident. For players doing clue scrolls that occasionally want a specific holiday piece, keep the memory organized so the right item is one click away.

💡 Memory vs. Reclaim

Storage (memory) is for items you still have but want out of your bank. Reclaim is for items you lost. Both are free, both are with the same NPC — just pick the right dialogue option.

5. 🚫 Permanent Loss Exceptions — What You Can't Get Back

This is the part every "Diango will save me" player needs to hear. A small set of items are genuinely gone forever if you lose them, and Diango cannot help.

Tradeable holiday rares

The big-value rares — partyhats (red, green, blue, yellow, purple, white), Santa hat, Halloween masks (skeleton/zombie/ghost/demon), pumpkin, and Easter egg — are tradeable and bought from other players. They are not event handouts, so Diango will not replace them. Lose one and you must re-buy it on the GE.

Discontinued / real-world promotion items

A few cosmetics were tied to real-world promotions, conventions, or long-discontinued giveaways. Depending on the item, these may not be reclaimable through Diango and are effectively account-locked once lost. If you own one, treat it as irreplaceable.

Items destroyed with a hard confirm

Most holiday cosmetics use a reclaimable "destroy" option, but a handful of one-time promo items warn that destruction is permanent. Always read the destroy confirmation text — if it says "this cannot be reclaimed," believe it.

⚠️ Wilderness Death Does Not Bypass Diango

If you lose a reclaimable holiday item in the Wilderness, Diango can still reclaim the original event copy for you — because eligibility is based on your account having earned it, not on the specific physical item. The PKer keeps the dropped one, but you can get your own back free. Tradeable rares, however, are still gone.

6. 🪜 Step-by-Step: How to Reclaim a Lost Item

Reclaiming takes under a minute. Follow these steps:

  1. Travel to Draynor Village and find Diango north of the bank.
  2. Right-click Diango and choose the "Reclaim" / "Lost items" / "Holiday items" dialogue (wording varies by item type).
  3. Browse the list he shows — it only displays items your account is eligible to reclaim.
  4. Select the item you want and confirm. Free items appear directly in your inventory; paid ones ask for GP first.
  5. Bank it or store it in Diango's memory so it never gets "lost" again.

If an item you expect is missing from his list, it almost always means one of two things: the item was a tradeable rare (GE only), or your account never originally received it from an event. There is no hidden menu — what he shows is what you can get.

7. 🌟 5 Tips for Returning Players

  1. Check Diango before the GE. New returns often waste millions buying a cosmetic Diango would give free.
  2. Store, don't hoard. Move holiday items into Diango's memory to free bank space for daily and weekly activities.
  3. Screenshot your rares. Tradeable rares have no reclaim — document what you own for insurance and trading.
  4. Do the annual events. Each Christmas/Halloween/Easter you log in for the free item expands what Diango can later reclaim for you.
  5. Use cosmetics for clues. Some clue scroll steps need specific holiday gear — keep a set accessible.

8. ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Diango in OSRS?

In Draynor Village, at Diango's Toy Store north of the bank. He is the holiday/event cosmetic NPC.

Can Diango give me back a partyhat or Santa hat?

No. Those are tradeable rares bought from other players on the GE. Diango only reclaims untradeable event/promotion items your account earned.

Is Diango's memory storage free?

Yes. Depositing and withdrawing holiday cosmetics in Diango's memory is free and unlimited.

What if I lost a holiday item in the Wilderness?

Diango can still reclaim the original event copy for you, because eligibility is per-account. The PKer keeps the dropped one, but you get your own back free.

Are there items Diango can NEVER replace?

Yes — tradeable rares and a few discontinued real-world promotion items are permanent losses if destroyed or lost. Always read the destroy confirmation text.
