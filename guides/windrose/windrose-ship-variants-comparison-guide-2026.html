Windrose Ship Variants Comparison 2026

How Variants Work: Three Flavors Per Tier

Each hull tier in Windrose ships in three variants with distinctly different stat profiles rather than different skins:

  • Stock — the balanced baseline. Middle HP, middle speed, middle cargo.
  • Brethren — the tank and hauler. More HP and more cargo, slower.
  • Blackbeard — the raider. Faster, often heavier guns, noticeably less HP and cargo.

That trade is consistent across tiers: Brethren buys survivability and capacity with speed, Blackbeard buys speed and guns with survivability, Stock takes neither extreme. Which one is correct depends entirely on how long you plan to keep the ship.

The deciding question is not "which is best" — it is "how long will I sail this?" A variant that is wrong for a hull you keep for two hours can be perfect for a hull you keep for twenty.

Ketch Variants — Full Numbers

The Ketch is the tier where the spread is easiest to see, because it is the hull almost every player builds first.

VariantHPSpeedCargoCannonsRole
Stock Ketch50,00019 knots283 × 12lbBalanced
Brethren Ketch65,00017 knots363 × 12lbTank / hauler
Blackbeard Ketch35,00021 knots203 × 12–24lbSpeed / fighter

Read that table carefully, because the swing is violent. The Brethren Ketch has 65,000 HP against the Blackbeard Ketch's 35,000 — nearly double — while the Blackbeard is 4 knots faster and carries just over half the cargo.

Note also that all three Ketch variants are limited to three cannons. The Blackbeard's edge is that its battery can mount up to 24lb guns where the others top out at 12lb — more punch per shot, but still only three of them.

Caliber vs. count: three heavy guns and three light guns are not the same ship. Heavier caliber means more damage per volley, which rewards accurate, close-range gunnery; more guns means a wider spread and more forgiving aim. If you are still learning to lead your shots, the wider battery is the kinder teacher — another reason the three-gun Blackbeard Ketch is a poor learning hull despite its paper DPS.

The cargo column deserves a second look too. Twenty slots on the Blackbeard Ketch against thirty-six on the Brethren is not a minor difference — it means roughly half as much material moved per trip, at the exact stage of the game where you are hauling constantly to build your first real base.

Reading the Numbers: HP, Speed & Cargo

What each stat actually buys you

  • HP — how long you survive sustained fire. This is the stat that decides whether a bad engagement is a retreat or a lost ship.
  • Speed (knots) — whether you get to choose your fights. Faster means you escape losing engagements and close on winning ones.
  • Cargo — how much a single profitable run carries. This is an economy stat, and it compounds over dozens of trips.
  • Cannons — both count and caliber. Three 12lb guns and three 24lb guns are very different batteries.

The trap is reading speed as a combat stat. Speed is an agency stat: it lets you decline fights. HP is the stat that keeps you alive in fights you cannot decline. On a ship you are still learning, HP is worth more than knots.

Why the same profile flips value between tiers

The interesting part of the variant system is that no profile is permanently best. The identical "fast and fragile" trade reads completely differently depending on what sits underneath it:

SituationBest statWhy
You are still learning the shipHPMistakes cost HP, not speed
You sail soloSpeedNobody else is there to bail you out of a bad engagement
You are hauling for economyCargoCompounds over dozens of runs
You fight committed fleet battlesHPNeither side can disengage, so the bigger pool wins
You will replace the hull soonHPSpeed on a short-lived ship has a short shelf life

That table is the whole recommendation engine. A Ketch is "still learning" plus "replace soon" — both rows say HP, so Stock wins. A Brigantine is "solo" plus "keep for a long time" — both rows say speed and guns, so Blackbeard wins.

Best Ketch Variant: Stock

Take the Stock Ketch. Two reasons, and they compound:

  • The Blackbeard Ketch's health pool is too small. At 35,000 HP it has the least survivability of any Ketch, and the Ketch tier is where you are still learning naval combat. It punishes exactly the mistakes a new captain makes.
  • You are going to replace it soon anyway. The Ketch is a transitional hull on the way to the Brigantine, so the Blackbeard's speed advantage has a short shelf life while its fragility is felt every single fight.
The Brethren Ketch is the trap pick. 65,000 HP and 36 cargo look like free wins, but 17 knots is the slowest option in the tier — you cannot disengage, and you will spend your Ketch phase watching fights you cannot escape. Save the tank profile for tiers where you actually keep the ship.

Stock sits in the middle on every axis: 50,000 HP is enough to survive a mistake, 19 knots is enough to leave a bad fight, and 28 cargo is enough to keep your economy moving while you push toward the Brigantine.

Best Brig Variant: Blackbeard

Take the Blackbeard Brigantine. This is the tier where the Blackbeard profile finally pays off, because the hull underneath is stout enough to survive the glass-cannon trade.

The Blackbeard Brigantine runs at 22 knots and carries 6 × 24lb guns. That combination — enough speed to pick every engagement, and six heavy guns instead of three light ones — makes it the most versatile mid-game ship in Windrose. You can chase, you can leave, and you can out-shoot most of what you meet.

It is also the correct economic choice, because the Brigantine is the hull you will sail for a long stretch. Unlike the Ketch, this is not a transitional purchase — speed and guns both pay dividends over many sessions. Full unlock requirements are in our Brigantine Unlock Guide.

Best Frigate Variant: Blackbeard (Brethren for Fleet Fights)

Default to the Blackbeard Frigate. It mounts 12 × 36lb guns — a battery large enough to end engagements before sustained enemy damage ever gets rolling. Damage that arrives early is damage you never have to tank, which is why the glass-cannon math works at this tier and not at the Ketch tier.

Take the Brethren Frigate if you fight in groups. Its health pool is close to double the other Frigate variants, which changes the shape of coordinated fights entirely: in a sustained team engagement where both sides are committed and neither can disengage, raw HP is the stat that decides the outcome, and the Brethren's speed penalty stops mattering when you are not the one choosing the range.

Simple rule: solo or small-group roaming → Blackbeard. Committed fleet engagements where you expect to trade fire for a long time → Brethren.

Getting to the Frigate at all requires second-biome materials — Tarred Planks and Tarred Fabric. There is no way to skip the tier, so plan that supply line while you are still sailing the Brigantine. For the naval combat layer itself, see our Combat & Naval Guide and Ship PvP Combat Guide.

The Ship Disassembly Space Trap

Here is a mistake that costs real materials and is easy to avoid. Disassembling a ship returns resources — but only if you have room to receive them.

If your inventory is full and there is no chest nearby, the refunded resources are lost. A patch added protection for this case, but players still report losing returns when they dismantle a hull with a stuffed pack and nothing to dump into. Do not test it on a Brigantine.
1

Empty your inventory first

Before you disassemble anything, clear your pack. Assume the refund will be large — a hull returns a lot of material at once.

2

Place a chest next to the Wharf

A storage container within reach is your safety net if the refund exceeds your pack. Build one before you need it, not while the prompt is open.

3

Dismantle at the Wharf with the ship docked

Ship operations happen at the Wharf — the same station that builds hulls, manages your fleet and repairs damage. The Shipwright's Workshop only handles the four equipment slots on a ship you already own; see our Wharf vs Shipwright's Workshop Guide if you have the two mixed up. Recall the ship with Esc then K if it is anchored elsewhere.

Treat disassembly like a high-value trade, not like pressing delete. Thirty seconds of inventory management protects a refund that took hours to accumulate.

FAQ

What is the best Ketch variant?

Stock. 50,000 HP / 19 knots / 28 cargo balances survivability and speed. The Blackbeard Ketch's 35,000 HP is too fragile for the tier where you are still learning naval combat.

Isn't the Brethren Ketch better with 65,000 HP?

Not for most players. It is the slowest Ketch at 17 knots, so you cannot disengage from bad fights, and you replace the Ketch soon anyway. Save the tank profile for the Frigate.

What are the exact Ketch variant stats?

Stock: 50,000 HP / 19 knots / 28 cargo / 3 × 12lb. Brethren: 65,000 / 17 / 36 / 3 × 12lb. Blackbeard: 35,000 / 21 / 20 / 3 × 12–24lb.

Which Brig variant should I build?

Blackbeard. At 22 knots with 6 × 24lb guns it is the most versatile mid-game ship — enough speed to choose engagements and enough guns to win them.

Which Frigate variant should I build?

Blackbeard by default (12 × 36lb ends fights before sustained damage forms). Pick Brethren if you fight committed fleet battles, where its near-double HP pool decides long engagements.

Do variant choices carry over between tiers?

No — each tier is a separate build with its own variant choice. That is exactly why picking for the hull's expected lifespan matters.

Can I skip to the Frigate?

No. The Frigate needs second-biome materials — Tarred Planks and Tarred Fabric — so you must progress through the Brigantine first.

I lost materials when disassembling a ship. Why?

Ship disassembly refunds resources only if you have inventory space or a nearby chest to hold them. Empty your pack and place a chest at the Wharf before dismantling.

Where do I disassemble or build a ship?

At the Wharf, with the ship docked. Recall it with Esc then K if it is anchored elsewhere.

Is speed or HP more important?

On hulls you will keep, speed buys the ability to choose fights; on hulls you are still learning, HP buys the ability to survive the fights you misjudge. That is why Stock beats Blackbeard on the Ketch and the reverse is true later.
