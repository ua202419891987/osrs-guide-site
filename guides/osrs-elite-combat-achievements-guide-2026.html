1. What the Elite Tier Is & Why It Matters

Combat Achievements (CAs) launched on 21 July 2021 as a tier-based set of optional combat challenges. Think of them as Achievement Diaries for bossing: instead of "teleport here and talk to that NPC," you are asked to kill bosses a set number of times, under a time limit, or without taking a specific hit. The system is split into six tiers of rising difficulty, and every completed task pays points you can spend toward reward unlocks.

Here is the current shape of the whole system, using the live OSRS Wiki task counts and point values:

Two numbers matter for an Elite hunter. First, the Elite tier alone is 164 tasks worth 656 points — more tasks than any other single tier. Second, you do not need to finish all 164 to claim the Elite reward set. You need 1,075 total points from any combination of tiers, and because lower tiers are far easier, most players arrive at 1,075 with a healthy mix of Easy, Medium, Hard and a solid slab of Elite tasks. That is the key mindset: Elite is the grind, but the reward threshold is reachable without a single "Perfect" task if your other tiers are strong.

💡 The one-sentence memory hook

Elite = 164 tasks, 4 points each, 656 points in the tier, but you only need 1,075 cumulative points (across all tiers) to claim the Ghommal's hilt 4 reward set.

2. Elite Requirements — Stats, Quests & Gear

Unlike Easy and Medium, the Elite tier is not something you stumble into through normal play. Jagex's own recommended profile is base 90 combat stats, and several mandatory gates are hard quest locks. You can read the in-game tracker at any point, but actually attempting the tasks needs the following on your account.

① Skill gates

  • 95 Slayer — mandatory and not boostable. This single level gates a huge slice of Elite content, so it is usually the real bottleneck.
  • 94 Magic — for Ice Barrage and Vengeance (used in tasks like Demonic Rebound). Boostable, so a potion helps.
  • 85 Mining — to one-down Het's Seal in Tombs of Amascut. Boostable.
  • 65 Farming — to fight Hespori. Boostable.

② Quest gates

The Elite quest wall is long. The non-negotiable unlocks include Song of the Elves, Desert Treasure II — The Fallen Empire, Dragon Slayer II, The Fremennik Exiles, Monkey Madness II, Perilous Moons, Secrets of the North, The Heart of Darkness (Amoxliatl), A Kingdom Divided, Haunted Mine and While Guthix Sleeps. Newer bosses add more: The Final Dawn, Beneath Cursed Sands, The Blood Moon Rises (Maggot King) and The Ides of Milk (Brutus) all open Elite tasks too.

③ Item gates

Several tasks assume you own specific tools: a demonbane weapon (Arclight / Scorching Bow), a crystal halberd or dragon halberd, Hespori seeds, rune thrownaxes, a salve amulet, and (for one task) Verac's flail — though that flail task can also be done with thralls, so it is not a hard requirement.

💡 Don't bottleneck on 95 Slayer

If you are 90 Slayer with strong gear, you can still clear dozens of Elite tasks — but the moment a target is a Slayer-locked boss (Cerberus, Abyssal Sire, many more), 95 is the wall. Plan your Slayer grind before, not during, your Elite push.

3. The Elite Reward — Ghommal's Hilt 4 & the 1,075-Point Threshold

Rewards are claimed from Ghommal (or Laidee Gnonock) by the Warriors' Guild entrance in Burthorpe. Unlike Achievement Diaries, you do not need to finish a lower tier before claiming a higher one — you only need the points. The Grandmaster set is the lone exception, which demands every currently available task. When new tasks ship, thresholds can rise, and reward sets you already own stay usable at their lower-tier limits until you catch up.

🏆 What the Elite tier completion unlocks

  • Ghommal's hilt 4 — 3 daily teleports to Mor Ul Rek (the TzHaar Fight Caves / Inferno hub) plus unlimited God Wars Dungeon entrance teleports. This is the headline prize and a genuine quality-of-life upgrade for any endgame PvMer.
  • Antique lamp (50,000 XP) — a combat-skill lamp, the largest in the tier ladder.
  • Slayer helmet recolor (Purple) — a premium cosmetic variant of the slayer helm.
  • Resource pack — a drop of useful PvM supplies (food, potions and similar).

Beyond the tier set, the cumulative point system also hands out account-wide perks at milestone totals — the kind of unlocks players often lump in when they talk about "Elite rewards." Examples include the ornate gravestone (a longer-protection gravestone) and Demon's bane (a chargeable item that damages demons), both tied to specific point totals rather than to finishing the Elite tier itself. Because the Elite grind alone contributes 656 points, reaching 1,075 sweeps you past several of those milestones — but exact thresholds shift when Jagex adds tasks, so always confirm the live numbers on the OSRS Wiki before you plan around a specific unlock.

💡 Hilt 4 vs the lower hilts

Ghommal's hilt 1–3 teleport only to God Wars Dungeon (with daily charges at the low tiers). Hilt 4 is the first to add Mor Ul Rek teleports — which is exactly why Elite is the tier most Inferno and Fight Caves runners actually push for.

4. Real Elite Tasks, Broken Down by Type

Every Elite task is tagged with a type, and learning the type tells you how to approach it. The Wiki lists five: Kill count, Mechanical, Perfection, Restriction and Speed (plus a Stamina subtype for repeat kills without leaving). Here are real Elite task names and their exact requirements, pulled from the live task table.

① Perfection — no damage, no death

  • Perfect Sire — Kill the Abyssal Sire without taking damage from the external tentacles, miasma pools, explosion, or Sire while not praying the appropriate protection prayer.
  • Anti-Bite Mechanics (Cerberus) — Kill Cerberus without taking any melee damage.
  • Unrequired Antifire (Cerberus) — Kill Cerberus without taking damage from any lava pools.

② Mechanical — use the boss's own mechanic

  • Demonic Rebound (Abyssal Sire) — Use the Vengeance spell to reflect the damage from the Abyssal Sire's explosion back to him.
  • Ghost Buster (Cerberus) — Kill Cerberus after successfully negating 6 or more attacks from Summoned Souls.
  • Respiratory Runner (Abyssal Sire) — Kill the Abyssal Sire after only stunning him once.
  • Dancing with Statues (Chambers of Xeric) — Receive kill-credit for a Stone Guardian without taking damage from falling rocks.

③ Speed — beat the clock

  • Amoxliatl Speed-Chaser — Kill Amoxliatl in less than 30 seconds.
  • Araxxor Speed-Trialist — Kill Araxxor 4 times in 10:00.
  • Brutus Speed-Trialist — Kill Brutus in less than 2 seconds.

④ Restriction — fight under a self-imposed limit

  • Without Ralos' Light (Amoxliatl) — Kill Amoxliatl without losing any prayer points.
  • Relaxxor (Araxxor) — Kill Araxxor after destroying six eggs.

⑤ Kill count — the quiet point farmers

  • Cerberus Veteran — Kill Cerberus 75 times.
  • Araxxor Veteran — Kill Araxxor 25 times.
  • Callisto Veteran — Kill Callisto 20 times.
💡 "Perfect" tasks are the real Elite wall

Kill-count and most restriction tasks are mechanical. The Perfection tasks — no damage from a specific source across a whole fight — are where hundreds of attempts go. Batch them once your gear and prayer-flicking are sharp, not before.

5. Boss-by-Boss Elite Highlights

The Elite tier spreads across almost every endgame boss and the three raids. A few deserve special mention because they are either high-value or surprisingly approachable.

① Abyssal Sire — the mechanical playground

The Sire alone carries four Elite tasks in the list above (Perfect Sire, Demonic Rebound, Respiratory Runner, Abyssal Veteran). If you enjoy the fight, it is one of the most efficient single bosses to farm for Elite points — Demonic Rebound in particular is a clean, learnable trick once you time Vengeance to the explosion.

② Amoxliatl — fast and frost-friendly

Amoxliatl's Speed-Chaser (<30s) and Without Ralos' Light (no prayer loss) are two of the more gear-forgiving Elite tasks because the boss is short and the mechanics are simple. A competent ranged or melee setup clears both with practice, making Amoxliatl a great early Elite target.

③ Araxxor — the 2024 boss with real Elite teeth

Araxxor brought a fresh set of Elite tasks — Araxxor Speed-Trialist (4 kills in 10:00), Relaxxor (six eggs) and Araxxor Veteran (25 kills). The speed task rewards pathing efficiency, while Relaxxor is a clean restriction challenge. If you already camp Araxxor for profit, these come almost for free.

④ Cerberus — the Slayer-locked staple

Cerberus is a point mine: Veteran (75 kills), Ghost Buster (negate 6+ soul attacks), Anti-Bite Mechanics and Unrequired Antifire are all Elite. The catch is 95 Slayer, which is exactly why this boss is the poster child for the skill gate in section 2.

⑤ The raids — Chambers of Xeric, Theatre of Blood, Tombs of Amascut

All three raids contribute Elite tasks, from CoX's Dancing with Statues to Theatre of Blood completion and ToA mechanics. Flawless Olm hand kills (the "Perfect Olm's Left Claw" / "Right Claw" style tasks) and clean raid runs are where team coordination meets the Elite tier — and where a lot of the 1,075 points come from for raid mains.

💡 New bosses mean new Elite tasks

Jagex keeps adding Elite tasks alongside new content — recent bosses like the Royal Titans, Araxxor, Hueycoatl and Yama all carry their own CA tasks. The task roster grows with every update, so treat the live OSRS Wiki table as the source of truth rather than any fixed list.

6. Recommended Gear & Stats Progression

You do not need maxed stats to start Elite, but you do need a near-endgame kit for the hard tasks. The progression that works for most accounts:

The single biggest multipliers are the forgotten prayers: Rigour (74 Prayer / 70 Defence, +20% ranged accuracy and +23% ranged strength) and Augury (77 Prayer / 70 Magic, +25% magic accuracy and damage). At Elite-tier bosses where DPS is the difference between a speed task and a reset, those prayers are not optional. Pair them with a full inventory of brews and restores, a Rune Pouch with the right spells (Vengeance for Demonic Rebound), and a Salve amulet where undead are involved.

✅ You are ready for Elite when:

  • You are 90+ base combat with 95 Slayer done or close
  • You own Rigour (or Augury for magic bosses) and the gear above
  • You can already solo most mid-tier bosses without dying
  • Your quest log is effectively maxed for PvM content

❌ Wait before pushing Elite if:

  • You are below 95 Slayer — the gate will block a third of the tier anyway
  • You still die to normal-mode bosses — fix mechanics before restrictions
  • You lack Rigour — speed tasks will frustrate you without the DPS

7. Strategy — How to Clear the Elite Tier Efficiently

The mistake most players make is jumping straight at the flashy Perfection tasks. The efficient path is the opposite: bank the easy points first, then spend your "attempt" budget only on the tasks that genuinely need it.

  • Start with kill counts. Cerberus Veteran, Araxxor Veteran, Callisto Veteran and the rest are AFK-able over time and need zero skill — they quietly push your point total toward 1,075 while you learn the boss.
  • Batch by type. Do every "no prayer loss" task in one session, every speed task in another. Context switching between restriction styles wastes more attempts than it saves.
  • Use the in-game tracker and RuneLite. The CA tracker filters by boss and tier; RuneLite's plugin shows incomplete tasks at the boss so you never forget a live one.
  • Lean on Slayer. Many Elite targets are also Slayer tasks — camping them for points doubles as progress toward 95 Slayer and the boss kill-count tasks at once.
  • Save Perfection for last. Perfect Sire, Anti-Bite Mechanics and the raid "Perfect" tasks demand sharp flicking. Tackle them once your kit and muscle memory are proven, not as a warm-up.

The one-line verdict: Elite is a marathon, not a sprint. Farm kill counts, batch by task type, keep Slayer running, and let the 1,075-point threshold arrive as a side effect of becoming a better PvMer — then claim Ghommal's hilt 4 and enjoy Mor Ul Rek on demand.

8. Elite Combat Achievements FAQs

Q: How many Elite Combat Achievement tasks are there?

The Elite tier currently holds 164 tasks, each worth 4 points — 656 points from the tier alone. The full system spans 646 tasks across all six tiers. Because Jagex adds tasks with new content, always check the live OSRS Wiki for the current count.

Q: How many points do I need to claim the Elite reward?

You claim the Elite reward set at 1,075 total Combat Achievement points. Those points can come from any mix of tiers — you do not have to finish every Elite task. Lower tiers are easier, so most players reach 1,075 with a blend of Easy, Medium, Hard and a solid chunk of Elite.

Q: What does the Elite tier reward give me?

The Elite completion reward is Ghommal's hilt 4 (3 daily Mor Ul Rek teleports plus unlimited God Wars Dungeon teleports), a 50,000 XP antique lamp, a purple slayer helmet recolor, and a resource pack of PvM supplies. The hilt 4 Mor Ul Rek teleport is the headline prize for Inferno and Fight Caves runners.

Q: What are the hardest requirements for Elite tasks?

The real wall is 95 Slayer, which is not boostable and gates a large share of Elite bosses. On top of that you need 94 Magic (boostable, for Vengeance / Ice Barrage), 85 Mining (boostable, for ToA) and the long quest chain — Song of the Elves, Desert Treasure II, Dragon Slayer II, Fremennik Exiles and more.

Q: Is "Perfect Sire" a real Elite task?

Yes. Perfect Sire is a genuine Elite Perfection task: kill the Abyssal Sire without taking damage from the external tentacles, miasma pools, explosion, or Sire while not praying the right protection prayer. The Abyssal Sire also has Demonic Rebound and Respiratory Runner as Elite tasks, making it one of the most efficient single bosses to farm.

Q: Do I need max stats for Elite?

No. The recommended profile is base 90 combat stats, and you can clear most kill-count and restriction tasks well before max. The tasks that truly need near-best gear are the speed and Perfection tasks, where DPS and flicking separate a clear from a reset.

Q: Are there new Elite tasks in 2026?

Jagex keeps adding Elite tasks alongside new bosses and raids — recent additions cover bosses like the Royal Titans, Araxxor, Hueycoatl and Yama. The roster is not static, so the live OSRS Wiki task table is the only authoritative source for what is currently in the tier.

Q: What is the difference between the Elite reward and Demon's bane / ornate gravestone?

Ghommal's hilt 4, the lamp, the purple slayer helm recolor and the resource pack are the Elite tier-completion rewards, claimed at 1,075 points. Demon's bane and the ornate gravestone are separate account-wide perks unlocked at cumulative point milestones in the CA system — the Elite grind pushes you past several of them, but they are not part of the tier set itself. Confirm exact thresholds on the live Wiki.

Final Tips — Avoiding the Classic Elite Traps

  • Don't chase Perfection first. Kill-count and restriction tasks are free points — bank them before you burn attempts on no-damage runs.
  • Keep 95 Slayer moving. It is the one gate you cannot boost, and it silently blocks a third of the tier. Train it alongside your boss grinds.
  • Own Rigour before speed tasks. Without the +20% ranged accuracy and +23% strength, speed tasks become gear checks you will fail.
  • Claim at 1,075, not at "all Elite done." The threshold is cumulative — grab Ghommal's hilt 4 the moment you cross it and use the teleport while you finish the rest.
  • Watch for new tasks. When Jagex ships a boss, new Elite tasks ship with it — sometimes easier than what you are stuck on. Check the Wiki after every update.
