OSRS Mobile & Desktop Progress Sync 2026 — Cloud Save & Cross-Device Play Explained

Updated August 19, 2026  |  15 min read  |  By Yanchun Wu
🛡️ Level: 1+

One of the first questions new and returning players ask is simple: "If I train on my phone during the commute, will my bank and levels be there when I log in on my computer at home?" The short answer is yes. Old School RuneScape does not store your progress on your device — it stores everything on Jagex's servers, tied to your single account. This beginner-friendly guide explains exactly how progress sync works between the mobile app and the desktop client, what transfers instantly, what does not carry over, and how to switch devices without ever losing a single item. If you are still choosing how to play, our OSRS Mobile Guide 2026 covers setup and device requirements, while our New Player Guide explains the early game. The good news is that cross-device play is not a feature you unlock — it is the default behavior of the entire game, available from the moment you create an account.

⏱️ Quick Summary — 30-Second Read

  • 📌 There is no "cloud save button" — sync is automatic because your character lives on Jagex's servers, not your device.
  • 📌 One account, every platform. Bank, skills, quests, Grand Exchange offers, and friends list are identical on mobile and desktop.
  • 📌 Never run two sessions on one account at once. The newer login boots the older one and can drop you mid-action.
  • 📌 Interface layouts and plugin settings do not sync between RuneLite (desktop) and the official mobile client.
  • 📌 Log out fully before switching to avoid a "world unavailable" desync and to protect your items.

1. 🔄 How Progress Sync Actually Works

Old School RuneScape is a live, server-authoritative game. That technical phrase just means one thing for you: the real version of your character — your bank, your XP, your Quest log, your Grand Exchange offers, even your run energy — is kept on Jagex's game servers at all times. Your phone and your computer are only "windows" that display that server-side character. When you chop an oak log on mobile, the server records the log and the Woodcutting XP. When you later open the desktop client, it simply asks the server, "show me this account," and the server streams the current, up-to-date state to your screen. Nothing about your progress ever physically lived on the phone or the laptop; those machines are terminals, not storage.

This is why there is no manual "upload to cloud" or "download save" button anywhere in the client. Sync is not a feature you toggle — it is the foundation the whole game is built on. The moment an action completes, it is saved server-side. You do not need Wi-Fi at the end of a session to "keep" your progress; the progress was never on your device to begin with. The official mobile guide confirms the app logs into the exact same character as the desktop client, and the same is true for the Steam client, the Jagex Launcher, and the official website's Old School page. There is exactly one character, and every doorway leads to it.

The practical takeaway: you can train Farming on your phone at lunch, log out, open your laptop that evening, and your herb patches, bank, and GP will all be exactly where you left them. There is no separate "mobile character" and no transfer step. The diagram below shows the mental model that prevents almost every sync confusion — two screens, one source of truth.

💡 Think of Devices as TVs, Not Hard Drives

If the mental model helps: your account is the broadcast, and your phone, tablet, and PC are just different TVs showing the same channel. Changing the TV does not change the show. Unplug one and the broadcast keeps playing; plug in another and it shows you exactly where things are.

2. 📦 What Syncs Automatically (and What Doesn't)

Almost everything that matters syncs perfectly. But a few things are stored locally on each device and will not follow you across platforms. Knowing the difference stops confusion before it starts, and it explains why your bank looks identical on both devices while your carefully arranged hotkeys do not.

✅ Always synced (server-side)

  • Bank contents and value — every item, every stack, every untradeable.
  • Skills and XP — including total level and combat level.
  • Quest progress — started, finished, and quest points.
  • Grand Exchange offers — buy/sell orders and collected items.
  • Inventory, equipment, and worn gear — what you are holding carries over exactly.
  • Friends, ignores, and clan chat — your social lists are account-wide.
  • Diaries, unlocks, and teleports — account-wide progress like fairy rings and quest unlocks.
  • Membership status — active on every device at once.

⚠️ Not synced (stored per device)

  • Interface layout and window position. The custom panels you arrange in RuneLite on desktop do not appear on mobile, and vice versa.
  • Plugin settings. RuneLite plugins (ground markers, tile indicators, banker tags) live on your desktop client, not the mobile app. Our Bank & Inventory guide shows how to stay organised without relying on desktop-only plugins.
  • Chat settings and private chat colour. These are client preferences saved locally.
  • Camera angle and zoom. Each client remembers its own view; logging in fresh on a new device starts at a default camera.
  • Mobile-specific settings like tap-and-hold delay, FPS cap, and brightness presets stay on the phone.

In short: your character is one and the same everywhere; your client preferences are local to each device. Losing a custom layout when you switch is normal and harmless, and the table below makes the split explicit so you always know what to expect.

Data Type Stored Where Carries to Other Device? What To Do
Bank, skills, quests, GE offers Jagex servers Yes — identical everywhere Nothing; automatic
Interface layout & plugin config Local client No Reapply on the new device
Chat colours & camera presets Local client No Re-set per device
Membership & friends list Jagex servers Yes — account-wide Nothing; automatic

3. 🔁 How to Switch Devices Safely

Switching from mobile to desktop (or back) is routine, but doing it sloppily can get you stuck in a "world unavailable" loop or, worse, logged out mid-action and exposed. Follow this clean routine. The steps are ordered on purpose — most players skip step 1 and 2, and that is exactly where the problems come from.

Step 1 — Finish what you are doing

Bank your loot, empty your inventory of anything valuable you want protected, and stand somewhere safe (a bank or a non-Wilderness spot). Do not switch while standing in the Wilderness or mid-fight. Because the server is the source of truth, the only thing you can "lose" by switching is the state of an action that was still in progress — so let actions finish first.

Step 2 — Log out, don't just close

Use the in-game logout button (or the mobile app's logout) rather than force-closing the app or closing the laptop lid. A proper logout tells the server to release your session cleanly. Closing the app abruptly can leave a "lingering" session that blocks your next login for a few seconds and triggers the dreaded world-unavailable message.

Step 3 — Open the other device and log in

Launch the desktop client or mobile app, enter your credentials, and your character loads with everything intact. Because the server already saved your last state, there is nothing to "transfer." The load takes a moment while the client streams your full character, but the data was never at risk.

Step 4 — Re-apply local preferences

Accept that your layout and plugin settings are fresh. Re-open the panels you use or re-toggle your mobile tap-hold settings. This takes thirty seconds and is expected — it is not a sign anything went wrong. The flow diagram below shows the routine at a glance.

Get into the rhythm of fully logging out on one device before logging into the next. It is the single habit that prevents nearly every cross-device problem in this guide, and it costs you nothing but a tap.

4. 🚫 Why You Must Never Double-Login

This is the most important safety rule for cross-device play. You cannot be logged into the same account on two devices simultaneously. OSRS enforces a single active session per account. The moment you log in on a second device, the server boots the first session. This is not a bug you can work around — it is a hard rule built into the game to protect your account integrity.

What actually happens

Suppose your phone is still "logged in" because you only closed the app instead of logging out, and you then open the desktop client. The desktop login succeeds and force-disconnects the phone. Usually this is harmless — but if the phone was mid-action (e.g., auto-retaliate fighting a monster, or standing in a dangerous area), the forced disconnect can drop you at a bad moment. In the Wilderness, a surprise disconnect is exactly how players lose expensive gear, because you cannot react to an attacker while the session is collapsing.

Backgrounding is not logging out

On mobile, switching to another app or locking your screen does not log you out — it keeps the session alive (and drains battery). If you then log in on desktop, the mobile session gets booted. Our mobile guide explains battery and background behaviour in detail. The fix is simple: always tap logout before you walk away from the phone. Treat the green "logged in" state as something you must end deliberately, never something that ends when you stop looking at the screen.

⚠️ Never "Test" by Logging In Twice

Some players think they can run two clients to trade between them or watch both screens. The server will kick one session, and you risk losing items if the kicked session was holding valuable loot. One account, one session, always.

5. 🛠️ Troubleshooting Sync Problems

Because sync is automatic, most "my stuff didn't save" scares are actually display glitches or user error. Here is how to diagnose the common ones before you panic or file a ticket.

"My bank looks different / items missing after switching"

First, rule out a visual glitch. Fully log out and back in — a stale view sometimes shows an old bank state. If items are genuinely missing, check whether you left them on the ground or in a Grand Exchange sell offer on the other device. Remember the GE and ground drops are server-side too, so an offer you placed on mobile is still pending on desktop until it completes, and a drop on the floor belongs to that world, not your account.

"World unavailable" when I try to log in on the second device

This usually means your previous session has not fully released. Wait 10–30 seconds, then try again. Make sure you logged out (not just closed) on the first device. A stuck session almost always clears within a minute; hammering the login button only extends the lockout window.

"My Grand Exchange offer isn't there"

GE offers are account-wide, but there is a brief delay when a session is mid-sync. Open the Grand Exchange interface and wait a moment; the offer or its collected items appear. If an offer finished while you were away, the items sit in the GE collection box — collect them from any device, exactly as you left them.

"My layout/plugins didn't come with me"

That is expected, not a bug. Interface and plugin settings are per-device, as covered in Section 2. Nothing is lost; you just reapply your preferences. The Bank & Inventory guide has a clean setup you can recreate in seconds.

If a genuine loss occurred after an update rather than a switch, our Common Bug Fix guide and the item-recovery guide cover what Jagex restores automatically and what you must request.

6. 🔐 Keeping Your Shared Account Secure

A single account used on multiple devices is also a single point of failure. Every device that can log in can drain your bank if it is compromised. Protect the account, not just the devices — because the character is shared, a hole in any one device is a hole in all of them.

Enable the Jagex Authenticator on every device

The Jagex Launcher and mobile app support the official authenticator. Set it up once and it protects logins from all devices. If you ever lose access, the recovery path is documented in our support pack. Never disable 2FA "just to make logging in faster" — that is how hijackers empty banks, and a compromised account is far more painful than a six-digit code.

Use a password manager, not a shared note

Typing your password on a phone keyboard invites shoulder-surfs and keyloggers from dodgy app stores. A reputable password manager autofills securely. Never reuse your OSRS password elsewhere, and never paste it into a site that is not the official jagex.com login.

Log out of public or borrowed devices

If you ever log in on a friend's phone or a library computer, log out and, if possible, revoke the session afterward. An unattended saved login on a shared device is a free bank to whoever picks it up next. The same applies to the desktop client on a shared family computer.

Watch for phishing across platforms

Phishing links work the same on mobile and desktop. The "double your GP" and fake "account locked" messages aim to steal your credentials. Our Beginner Money Making guide includes a scam-avoidance section — the rules apply everywhere you play, and the mobile browser is an easy place to tap a bad link by mistake.

💡 One Account Means One Weak Link

Because progress is shared, a compromise on your phone compromises your desktop wealth too. Secure the account once, properly, and every device benefits. The strongest chain is only as safe as its weakest login.

7. 📱 Mobile vs Desktop: Which Should You Play On?

Because your character is identical everywhere, the real question is not "which device has my progress" but "which device suits the task." Each platform has strengths, and smart players move between them during the day. The table below compares the two on the things that actually affect your grind.

Aspect Mobile (Phone / Tablet) Desktop (PC / Laptop)
Best for Passive / AFK skills, bankstanding, commuting, short sessions Intensive bossing, tick-perfect activities, many plugins
Plugins (RuneLite) Not available — official client only Full plugin suite, ground markers, overlays
Input precision Tap / virtual joystick, slightly slower for complex routes Mouse + keyboard, fastest for precise movement
Battery / power Drained by long sessions; keep charged Unlimited while plugged in
Multitasking Easy to play while travelling or watching TV Easy to alt-tab to wikis and calculators
Your progress 100% identical to desktop 100% identical to mobile

The takeaway is not "pick one." It is "use both." Train Herblore or do birdhouse runs on your phone during a commute, then settle at your desk for a Nightmare or Vorkath session where plugins and precise input matter. The shared character means the GP you make on the train is waiting in your bank when you sit down to boss. For the money-making side of this, the Beginner Money Making guide and the GE Flipping guide both work perfectly from either device — flipping in particular is ideal on mobile because it needs almost no mechanical input.

If you want to grow into bossing on desktop, the Slayer Money Making guide and the Best Money Making Methods guide rank the activities that benefit most from a real keyboard. The point is that the device is just a doorway — the bank on the other side is the same regardless of which one you walk through.

8. ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is my OSRS progress stored in the cloud?

Yes — effectively. Your character lives on Jagex's servers, so your bank, levels, quests, and Grand Exchange offers are identical on mobile and desktop. There is no manual cloud-save button because saving is continuous and automatic from the moment an action completes.

Do I have a separate mobile character?

No. The mobile app and the desktop client both log into the same account, so there is one shared character. Anything you do on your phone appears on your computer and vice versa — there is no transfer step because there is only ever one character.

Why did my interface layout not carry over?

Interface arrangements, plugin settings, and chat preferences are saved locally on each device, not on the server. Your character syncs everywhere; your client preferences do not. This is normal and nothing is lost — you simply reapply your layout on the new device.

Can I be logged in on my phone and PC at the same time?

No. OSRS allows only one active session per account. Logging in on a second device boots the first. Always log out fully on one device before logging into the other to avoid surprise disconnects, especially in dangerous areas.

Will my Grand Exchange offer survive switching devices?

Yes. GE buy and sell orders are account-wide and persist on the server. You can place an offer on mobile and collect the result on desktop once it completes — just open the GE interface and wait a moment for it to display.

Do I need Wi-Fi at the end of a session to keep my progress?

No. Progress is saved on the server the instant an action finishes, not when you disconnect. You can lose signal, close the app, or shut down the laptop and your character is already safe on Jagex's servers. The device was never the storage.

Will my membership work on both devices?

Yes. Membership is tied to your account, not your device, so a membership bought anywhere applies on mobile, desktop, Steam, and the Jagex Launcher simultaneously. You never repurchase for a second device.

Can I use RuneLite plugins on mobile?

No. RuneLite and its plugins run only on the desktop client. The official mobile app is a separate, plugin-free client. Your plugin settings therefore cannot follow you to the phone, which is why Section 2 lists them under "not synced." Plan mobile sessions around content that does not need overlays.

What happens if I get disconnected while switching devices?

A clean disconnect just drops you to the login screen; your progress is untouched because it was always server-side. The only real risk is being disconnected mid-action in a dangerous spot — which is why Step 1 of the safe-switch routine tells you to finish up and stand safe before you log out.

Is it safe to play on public Wi-Fi on mobile?

The connection itself carries your game traffic, not your password in plain text, but public networks are riskier for phishing and session snooping. Use the official app, keep your authenticator on, and avoid tapping login links from messages. The account-security steps in Section 6 apply everywhere you connect.

9. ✅ Final Tips — Sync Without Stress

  • Treat progress as automatic. You never need to "save" or "upload" — the server already has it.
  • Log out, don't just close. A clean logout prevents world-unavailable loops and surprise disconnects.
  • One session at a time. Never run the same account on two devices; the second login kicks the first.
  • Reapply local settings after switching. Layout and plugins are per-device — expect to re-set them, it is not a loss.
  • Keep membership active. A membership bought anywhere works on every platform, including the desktop client.
  • Secure the account once. The Jagex Authenticator and a unique password protect all your devices at once.
  • Never switch mid-Wilderness. Finish and bank before moving devices to avoid a disconnect that costs gear.
  • Use both devices. Passive skills on the phone, intensive content on desktop — the same bank rewards both.
