Long-Tail Keywords (The Secret Weapon)

Long-tail keywords are 5+ word phrases. They have lower volume but much higher conversion (people who search them really want the answer).

Examples of Long-Tail Keywords That Rank

  • "OSRS how to get dragon defender after 70 attack" (volume: 300/mo, competition: zero)
  • "OSRS ironman f2p money making with no stats" (volume: 200/mo, competition: zero)
  • "OSRS how to beat Zulrah for beginners rotation" (volume: 500/mo, competition: low)

Pro tip: Use AnswerThePublic.com — type "OSRS how to" and see every question players are asking. Each question = one long-tail keyword.

Keyword Difficulty: How to Tell if You Can Rank

Signs a Keyword is EASY to Rank For

  • Top 10 results have low DR (Domain Rating): Check with Ahrefs free toolbar
  • Top results are forums/Reddit: Means no one has written a proper guide
  • Keywords with "2026" in them: Most content is outdated — fresh content wins
  • "How to" keywords: Tutorial intent = low competition

Signs a Keyword is HARD to Rank For

  • Top results are OSRS Wiki / IGN / big sites: Don't bother
  • Keyword has 10K+ searches/month: Too competitive for new sites
  • Short keywords: "OSRS guide" = impossible. "OSRS [specific] guide" = possible

2026 Keyword Strategy: What's Diferent Now

  • Google loves "2026" in titles: Players want updated content — always include the year
  • "For beginners" keywords convert well: New players are your best audience
  • Mobile-first indexing: Your content must be readable on phones (short paragraphs, bullet points)
  • Featured snippets: Structure content with tables/lists to win position zero

Common Keyword Mistakes

  • Targeting "OSRS guide": Impossible to rank for (competition = OSRS Wiki)
  • Not updating old keywords: "OSRS guide 2023" will lose to "OSRS guide 2026"
  • Keyword stuffing: Using the same keyword 50 times = Google penalty
  • Ignoring search intent: "OSRS gold" = buying intent. "OSRS how to" = tutorial intent. Diferent!
