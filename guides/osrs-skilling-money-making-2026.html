1. 🔨 Overview & Why Skill for GP in 2026

Skilling money is the anti-bossing strategy: no protection prayers, no instances, no PK risk. You train a skill and the GP shows up as a byproduct. In 2026 the economy rewards high-tier processed goods — runes, leather, planks, bars — because demand from bossers, crafters, and ironmen is constant.

All GP/hr figures below come from the OSRS Wiki Money Making Guide, computed from cached July 2026 Grand Exchange prices and documented methods. Because prices move daily, treat them as verified baselines and re-check live before a long grind.

💡 The Skilling Advantage

Bossing can out-pay skilling at the very top, but skilling has zero downside risk and doubles as XP. A 90 RuneCrafter earns ~1.97M/hr and levels up. That combination is why skilling is the backbone of every long-term ironman and main account.

3. 🟥 Runecrafting — The King of Skilling GP

Runecrafting is historically the most profitable skill, and 2026 is no exception. Two methods dominate:

🩸 Blood Runes (77+ Runecraft) — ~1.97M/hr via the Abyss

Mine dense essence, chisel into dark essence fragments, craft at the Blood Altar (or via the Abyss). At 77 you get 1 blood rune per fragment; the blood essence effect adds ~50% expected bonus. The Abyss route (~1.97M/hr) out-earns the Arceuus route (~842K/hr at max) because it is faster. Needs 100% Arceuus favour for Arceuus, a chisel, pouches, and Graceful.

👻 Soul Runes (90+ Runecraft) — ~1.05M/hr

Crafted at the True Soul Altar after charging it with pure essence. Slower pace than bloods but a higher per-rune value (~310–360 GP each in 2026). Best with the full Raiments of the Eye set and a colossal pouch. ~1.05M/hr after tax per the Wiki.

💡 Boost Your Runes

Wear the Raiments of the Eye set (+10% runes per trip), use the largest pouch you have (colossal at 85+), and run the Arceuus essence mine efficiently. These stack to add hundreds of K/hr.

5. ⚒️ Smithing — Blast Furnace Runite

At 85 Smithing, smelting runite bars at the Blast Furnace pays ~2.6M/hr — one of the highest skilling rates in the game. You need:

  • 85 Smithing (for runite bars) and Ice gloves (or a Smithing gauntlet) to handle hot bars.
  • A coal bag and a steady supply of runite ore + coal (8 coal per runite bar, or use the ore multiplier with the coal bag).
  • Blast Furnace is in Keldagrim; the Foreman fee is waived at 60 Smithing.
💡 Bar Choice Scales With Level

Below 85, adamantite bars (~2.44M/hr at 70) and gold bars with Goldsmith gauntlets (~1M/hr) are solid. Runite is the ceiling — and it also trains Smithing fast, so you are paying for XP too.

9. ⚠️ Common Mistakes (Myth-Busting)

❌ Myth 1: "Skilling can't compete with bossing."

False. Blast Furnace runite (~2.6M) and blood runes (~1.97M) out-earn most solo bosses and carry zero death risk. Skilling is the better risk-adjusted earner for many accounts.

❌ Myth 2: "The GE tax is 1%."

Outdated. The Grand Exchange tax was raised from 1% to 2% on 29 May 2025. Any guide still quoting 1% is pre-2025. Always subtract 2% from your sell side (capped at 5M per item).

❌ Myth 3: "Tanning is the best easy money."

Context-dependent. Tanning green dragonhide is ~311K/hr — fine as a zero-req method, but far below Runecrafting or Blast Furnace once you have the levels. Use tanning as a starter, then graduate.

❌ Myth 4: "Black chins are safe like red chins."

Dangerous. Black chinchompas are in the Wilderness. The ~2.5M/hr assumes you are not PK'd. Red chins give ~1.8M/hr with no risk — often the smarter long-term choice.

10. ❓ FAQ

What is the absolute best skilling GP/hr with no requirements?

Tanning green dragonhide (~311K/hr) is the top zero-requirement skilling method — you only need GP to buy hides. From there, level Runecrafting and Smithing to unlock the million-per-hour tiers.

Is Runecrafting worth the grind to 77/90?

Yes for money. Blood runes at 77 pay ~1.97M/hr and train the skill; soul runes at 90 add ~1.05M/hr. The XP is slow, but the GP ceiling is among the highest in the game.

How much starting capital do I need?

Tanning needs ~5M for a bulk hide buy. Runecrafting needs essence capital (pure essence is cheap, ~9 GP). Blast Furnace needs ore + coal (~2M+ per hour of bars). More capital = bigger volume = more GP/hr.

Does the 2% GE tax hurt skilling?

A little — it is already netted out of the Wiki figures. On a 2M/hr method that is ~40K/hr in tax. It matters most on high-volume, low-margin methods like tanning, where you must buy low and sell high to stay profitable.

Which skill should I train first for money?

If starting fresh: get 77 Runecrafting (blood runes) and 85 Smithing (Blast Furnace) — those two unlock the strongest steady earners. Pair with farming runs for passive income between grinds.

11. 🏁 Final Tips

  • Pick the highest tier your stats allow — runite > adamantite > gold at Blast Furnace; blood > soul at RC.
  • Stack your boosts — pouches, Raiments of the Eye, Graceful all add K/hr for free.
  • Re-check live GE prices before committing capital; margins move daily.
  • Avoid the Wilderness unless you accept the PK risk (black chins).
  • Combine with passive income: farming runs and GE flipping fill the downtime between skilling grinds.
🤝 Skilling Compounds

Unlike bossing loot, skilling XP is permanent. Every hour you spend earning GP also brings you closer to 99 — the ultimate long-term bank boost.
