1. 🌳 Overview & Why Train Woodcutting in 2026

Woodcutting is the quintessential AFK skill — minimal attention, steady XP, and (with the right trees) solid GP. Three reasons it stays popular:

🎯 The Three Big Draws

  • True AFK: Redwoods let you go 3–5 minutes between clicks — the most relaxed 99 in the game.
  • Passive GP: Yew, Magic, and Redwood logs sell for steady income while you train.
  • Fletching feed: Your logs power Fletching (and Firemaking), making it a core gathering skill.
💡 The One Rule That Wins the Grind

Get into the Woodcutting Guild (60 WC + 75% Hosidius favour) and wear the Lumberjack outfit. The +7 invisible level and +2.5% XP apply to every tree — they are free XP you leave on the table without them.

8. ⚠️ Common Mistakes (Myth-Busting)

❌ Myth 1: "Willows are the best XP."

Willows are fine early but Teaks (35+) are far faster (90k–235k vs ~50k XP/hr). Move to teaks as soon as you hit 35.

❌ Myth 2: "Bank your teak logs for profit."

Teaks are trained by dropping logs (shift-drop) for max XP. Banking them kills your rate. Save banking for Yew/Magic/Redwood if you want GP.

❌ Myth 3: "Redwoods are the fastest."

Redwoods are the most AFK, not the fastest (60k–80k XP/hr). Teaks and Sulliusceps are quicker; redwoods are for relax sessions.

❌ Myth 4: "You need a high level for the Guild."

You need 60 WC + 75% Hosidius favour — the favour is the gate, not a high level. Get favour early and the Guild boosts you from 60.

❌ Myth 5: "Any axe is fine."

Axe tier drives ~90% of your rate. Rune → Dragon → Crystal is a massive upgrade. Never chop with a rune axe at 90.

10. ❓ FAQ

What's the fastest way to 99 Woodcutting?

Teaks from 35 (up to 235k XP/hr with tick manipulation / Forestry), then Sulliusceps (65+) and Redwoods (90+). Pure AFK, Redwoods alone take ~120–130 hours.

Do I need the Woodcutting Guild?

For Redwoods and Magic trees, yes — they're inside the Guild (60 WC + 75% Hosidius favour). The +7 invisible level also boosts every other tree you chop there.

Which axe is best?

Crystal axe (71 WC, after Song of the Elves) is best, followed by Dragon axe (61, with its +3 special). Below that, Rune axe (41). Axe tier dominates your XP rate.

Is Woodcutting profitable?

Yes, if you chop Yew, Magic, Redwood, or Sulliuscep and bank the logs. Teaks (dropped) trade GP for speed. Forestry Felling Axe Handles are the best side income.

What are Sulliusceps?

Mushroom "trees" on Fossil Island (unlocked by Bone Voyage, 65 WC). They give ~80k–110k XP/hr and ~200k–400k GP/hr — the best balance of XP and profit in the skill.

11. 🏁 Final Tips

  • Guild + Lumberjack: +7 level and +2.5% XP apply to every tree — get both early.
  • Best axe always: Crystal > Dragon > Rune. Don't chop under-axed.
  • Teaks to 65: fastest raw XP (drop logs). Sulliusceps then balance XP + GP.
  • Redwoods for AFK: 90+ in the Guild — most relaxed 99 in the game.
  • Forestry worlds: world 444 for teak boosts; farm Felling Axe Handles for GP.
  • Next skill: turn logs into Fletching XP, or grow them via Farming.
