1. 🔒 Why the Old Game Center Login Is Dead

If you started playing Old School RuneScape on an iPhone years ago, you almost certainly logged in through Apple's Game Center — tap the icon, confirm your Apple ID, and you were in. That method predates the Jagex account migration, and it is now retired. The app no longer accepts a bare Game Center sign-in; it expects a Jagex account instead. That mismatch is why so many long-time iOS players see "account not linked," an endless spinner, or a hard lockout after an update.

This is a different problem from a normal login failure, which our Mobile Can't Log In fix covers. Here the credentials technically exist, but the binding between Apple's Game Center and your character was severed during migration. The good news: your character was migrated too, and the link can be rebuilt.

💡 The Key Mental Model

Think of Game Center as an old front door that Jagex bricked up. Your character moved through the back door into a Jagex account. You don't need the old door — you need the new key (your Jagex account email + password + 2FA). Recover that and the lock disappears.

2. 🔗 Recover the Game Center → Jagex Link

Do this on a browser first (phone or PC). The app cannot rebuild the link by itself once it's broken — you must re-establish it through the Jagex account system, then carry it into mobile.

② Step-by-step link recovery

Step 1 — Find the Jagex account your character was migrated to

  • Go to the official Jagex account sign-in page.
  • Try the email you used when you originally set up or migrated the account. If it recognizes the email and asks for a password + 2FA, that's your Jagex account.
  • If you never set one up, use "Recover your account" with your character name and original creation details — Jagex will route you to the correct Jagex account.

Step 2 — Confirm the character is inside that Jagex account

  • After signing in on the web, open "Manage accounts / characters." Your old Game Center character should be listed there as one of the linked characters.
  • If it's there, the link is effectively rebuilt at the server level — the app just needs to use the Jagex account login, not Game Center.

Step 3 — Log into mobile with the Jagex account

  • Open OSRS Mobile and choose email + password (the Jagex route), NOT the Apple/Game Center button.
  • Enter the same email/password you used on the web, plus your 2FA code.
  • You are now in your previously-Game-Center-locked character. The old Game Center door is irrelevant.
🚨 Don't Create a Second Jagex Account by Mistake

The #1 way players permanently "lose" a Game Center character is making a brand-new Jagex account and linking the wrong email. Always recover the existing migrated account (Step 1) rather than signing up fresh. If you're unsure which email holds the character, use account recovery instead of creating another.

3. 🔄 Switch Between Multiple Accounts on One Phone

This is the single strongest pain point for serious mobile players: you have a main, an iron, and a skilling alt, and you want them all on one device without a 10-minute dance every time. The answer is structural, not a hidden setting.

③ The rule: one Jagex account per character

Jagex accounts are containers. A single Jagex account can hold multiple characters (your main + your iron can live under one login), but if your characters were created separately (e.g., one via Game Center, one via email), they likely sit under different Jagex accounts. To switch, you switch Jagex accounts, not just characters.

④ Switching flow on mobile

  1. Open the in-app menu → Log out (or "Switch account" if your client version shows it).
  2. On the login screen, tap the correct method for the next account: email+password for a Jagex account, or the matching Apple/Google/Steam button for a linked one.
  3. Enter that account's credentials + 2FA. You're now on the other character.
  4. If both characters are under the same Jagex account, use the in-app character picker instead — no full logout needed.

🎯 Pro Setup for Alt-Junkies

Consolidate every character you own into one Jagex account using the migration tool. Then mobile switching is a one-tap character picker, never a credential shuffle. The Jagex Account FAQ explains the hard limit (usually up to 3–5 characters per Jagex account depending on age/status), so plan your grouping before you migrate.

⑤ Avoid the "wrong account" trap

If tapping Apple keeps dumping you on the same character, it means that Apple ID is bound to that specific Jagex account. To reach a different character under a different Jagex account, you must log fully out and use that account's login method. The app remembers the last method — clear it deliberately each switch.

4. ♻️ Reinstall Wiped Local Login — Restore It

A clean reinstall (often done after the standard login troubleshooting) deletes the app's locally cached "remember me" token. Players panic, thinking the account is gone. It isn't — the character lives on Jagex's servers.

⑥ What a reinstall actually deletes

DataStored WhereLost on Reinstall?
Your character, levels, bank, GPJagex serversNo
Local "remember me" tokenPhone app cacheYes (expected)
Game Center binding stateApp + Jagex serversApp part yes; server link survives
Your settings / layoutsPhone app dataYes (reconfigure after)

⑦ Restore local login after reinstall (iOS)

  1. Reinstall OSRS from the App Store (not an iCloud backup — that can re-copy a broken cache).
  2. Open the app and choose the Jagex account (email + password) route, not Game Center.
  3. Enter credentials + 2FA. The server recognises you instantly; the "lost" login is just re-created.
  4. Re-enable "remember me" if offered, and re-set your layouts.

⑧ Restore local login after reinstall (Android)

  1. Uninstall, restart the phone, reinstall from Google Play.
  2. Log in with the correct method (email, Google, or Steam — match how the account was made).
  3. If Google/Game Center was the old route, it now goes through the Jagex account email — see §2.
💡 The "Lost Login" Is Just a Missing Key, Not a Lost House

Reinstalling only throws away the key sitting on your phone. The house (your account) is untouched on Jagex's servers. Re-authenticate once and you're home. This is exactly why a reinstall is safe even when it feels scary mid-migration.

5. 📱 One Jagex Account per Character (Best Practice)

If you manage several accounts on mobile long-term, adopt this structure to never hit the Game Center lock again.

⑨ Consolidate where you can

  • Group characters you actively swap between into a single Jagex account (up to the per-account limit). Mobile then offers an in-app character picker — instant switching, no logout.
  • Keep strictly separate projects (e.g., a main you'd never risk near an alt) in their own Jagex accounts, and switch via full logout as in §3.

⑩ Use a password manager, not memory

Multi-account players fail switches because they typo or mix up which email owns which character. Store each Jagex account email + a note of which characters it holds in a password manager. The Account Security Guide covers manager setup and why 2FA backup codes matter here specifically.

🔋 Membership Follows the Account, Not the Device

If you subscribe through iOS, the membership is tied to the Jagex account you bought it on. Switching characters under that same account keeps membership active; switching to a different Jagex account without its own membership will show F2P. Our Mobile Membership Guide breaks down per-account billing so you don't accidentally strand an alt.

6. ⚠️ Migration Traps That Lock You Out

These are the silent mistakes that turn a recoverable Game Center account into a "lost forever" one. Avoid all of them.

⑪ Trap 1 — New Jagex account instead of recovery

Signing up fresh creates an empty account with no character. Always recover the migrated one. If in doubt, use the recovery form with your character name.

⑫ Trap 2 — Assuming Game Center still works

Tapping the Apple/Game Center button expecting the old flow fails post-migration. Use the Jagex email route. The Apple button now only matters if your Jagex account itself is linked to "Sign in with Apple" — a different thing from the legacy Game Center login.

⑬ Trap 3 — 2FA on the wrong email

Players enable 2FA on email A, then try to log in the character that lives under email B. The code "never works" because it's for a different Jagex account. Match the 2FA app entry to the exact email you're logging in with.

⑭ Trap 4 — Scam "account switcher" apps

Third-party "OSRS account switcher" tools ask for your credentials and are a leading cause of hijacks. Jagex's own client + Jagex account system is the only safe switcher. Anything else is a phishing front.

🚨 Recovery Is Your Safety Net

If every path above fails, the official account recovery process uses creation details only you know. Start it from a browser with your character name, original email, and any historical transaction info. It's the legitimate way back in — never pay a "recovery service" DM.

7. ❓ FAQ — Game Center & Account Switching

My old Game Center login just spins forever — is my account gone?

No. The legacy Game Center route is retired; your character was migrated into a Jagex account. Recover that Jagex account on the web (or via account recovery), then log into mobile with email + password + 2FA. See §2.

Can I have two OSRS accounts on one phone?

Yes. If both characters share one Jagex account, use the in-app character picker. If they're under different Jagex accounts, log out and log into the other with its own credentials. Consolidating into one Jagex account makes switching a single tap — details in §3.

I reinstalled the app and now it won't remember me — did I lose the character?

No. A reinstall only deletes the local "remember me" token; your character, bank, and GP are server-side. Just re-authenticate with the Jagex account email + 2FA and the login is restored. See §4.

Tapping Apple keeps logging me into the same character — why?

Because that Apple ID is bound to that specific Jagex account. To reach a different character under a different Jagex account, fully log out and use that account's login method. The app caches the last method, so clear it deliberately each switch.

Should I keep Game Center at all, or fully move to a Jagex account?

Move fully to a Jagex account. Game Center login is deprecated for OSRS; the Jagex account is the supported, recoverable, multi-character path and the only one that gets new security features. The migration guide walks through it safely.
