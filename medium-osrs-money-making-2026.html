💰 OSRS Medium Money Making Guide 2026 — 10 Best Mid-Level GP Methods

Published: July 2026 10 Methods Req: 50–80 stats Updated for 2026

You have outgrown the free-to-play grind: your stats are in the 50s and 70s, you own a few million GP, and you are comfortable with combat. But you are not yet ready to solo Vorkath or raid the Chambers of Xeric. This is the "medium" tier — and it is where OSRS starts to feel profitable. The methods below earn roughly 150k to 900k GP per hour using realistic, mid-game setups.

Every method lists its requirements, expected GP/hr, and the gear you actually need. Numbers are ballpark figures on a healthy 2026 economy and will drift with the Grand Exchange — treat them as planning ranges, not guarantees. For absolute beginner methods, see our full money making hub.

1. Giants' Foundry — best pure GP/hour

Unlocked after the Sleeping Giants quest (needs 20 Smithing, 20 Mining, 20 Crafting to start), Giants' Foundry rewards you for forging swords from a molten tank. It is one of the most efficient Smithing training methods and one of the best mid-game money makers. At 80+ Smithing you can consistently clear the highest-XP sword route, banking 400k–700k GP/hr plus Smithing XP.

  • Bring ice gloves (or run to the nearby bank) and a coal bag pre-filled.
  • Use the preset deposit to speed up each cycle — every second saved is GP earned.
  • Smithing XP here is so good that the GP is almost a bonus while you train toward 99.

2. Zalcano — low-effort PvM profits

Zalcano is a solo boss in the Elder God Wars area requiring 70 Mining and 70 Smithing, plus a pickaxe and a hammer. You chip away at the shielded boss, mine the tephra she drops, and smelt it into a bar to finish her off. Yields run around 500k–900k GP/hr from ores, seeds, and the occasional valuable drop. Use a crystal pickaxe (or at least a rune pickaxe) for speed. It is a great bridge into "real" bossing because the fight is mechanical rather than mechanically punishing.

3. Blast Furnace — Smithers' classic

Requires 60 Smithing for most bars (gold bars are viable from 40 with goldsmithing gauntlets). Buy ores from the Grand Exchange, smelt them at the BF in Keldagrim, and sell the bars back for a tidy margin. Expect 300k–600k GP/hr. Essentials: a coal bag, ice gloves (or a bucket of water), and stamina potions to keep running. Many players run adamantite or runite bars for the highest margins.

4. Gargoyles (Slayer) — profitable Slayer tasks

At 75 Slayer you unlock Gargoyles, which drop the Granite maul (a 300k+ weapon) and stack up steady alchables. With a rock hammer / rock thrownhammer to finish them, expect 300k–500k GP/hr while also progressing Slayer toward the profitable 85+ tasks. Block or skip low-value tasks and extend Gargoyles when you can. The Slayer helmet (i) and a decent crush weapon (like an Abyssal bludgeon or Barrelchest anchor) speed kills noticeably.

5. Barrows — gear drops and clue scrolls

With around 70 Combat and reasonable gear (e.g. Iban's staff or a trident, plus prayer potions), the six Barrows brothers are very doable. Per completion you get a chance at unique Barrows equipment (each worth 50k–3M) plus a clue scroll. A full run pays 250k–500k GP/hr, more with valuable uniques. Use the standard safe-spotting routes and bring prayer potions — the brothers hit hard if you let prayer drop.

6. Wintertodt — Firemaking + loot

Requires only 50 Firemaking. At the Wintertodt prison you chop roots, fletch them into kindling, and feed the brazier while dodging attacks. Rewards include the Pyromancer outfit (a Firemaking XP boost), herbs, seeds, gems, and clue scrolls — worth 200k–350k GP/hr alongside fast Firemaking XP. Bring a hammer, axe, tinderbox, and knives, plus food. Higher Woodcutting and Fletching speed your supply generation.

7. Tempoross — relaxed Fishing income

Tempoross is a no-requirement minigame, but 55+ Fishing makes it smooth. You fill a boat with water, fish, and attack the boss. Loot includes Big bones, seaweed spores, clue scrolls, and the reward pool's valuable fish. Expect 150k–300k GP/hr plus Fishing XP — a chill, low-risk earner for mid-levels. Bring a harpon / fishing rod (optional), and use the temporary boost from fish barrels.

8. Mahogany Homes — Construction that pays

At 50 Construction you can take Carpenter contracts around Gielinor. Unlike conventional Construction (which costs GP), Mahogany Homes pays you while granting XP. Budget 150k–250k GP/hr plus a steady path to 99. Use the build-and-remove strategy on oak/mahogany planks and always carry a saw, hammer, and plank sack.

9. Herb Runs — passive Farming gold

With access to the nine herb patches (Falador, Hosidius, Trollheim/Weiss, and the three Fossil Island patches), plant ranarr, snapdragon, or toadflax with ultracompost. A single run every ~80 minutes clears 150k–400k GP. Use the Explorer's ring (3) or Teleport tablets to hit patches fast, and a magic secateurs for a yield boost. Herb runs pair perfectly with bird house runs and tree runs as a daily routine.

10. Bird House Runs — ironman-friendly Hunter

Requires just 5 Hunter, 15 Construction, and 30 Woodcutting (for oak logs). Set bird houses on Fossil Island, empty them ~50 minutes later for feathers, eggs, and nests (including rings and seeds). Each run earns 100k–150k GP and is almost entirely passive — ideal to stack alongside herb and tree runs. Bring a teasing stick and pre-built empty bird houses.

How to pick the right method

  • Training a skill anyway? Do Giants' Foundry, Blast Furnace, Wintertodt, or Mahogany Homes — you get XP and GP together.
  • Want to learn PvM? Start with Zalcano and Barrows; both teach boss mechanics safely.
  • Short on time? Herb and bird house runs take minutes and pay passively between longer sessions.
  • Watch the GE. Flip between methods based on which bars, herbs, or seeds are spiking that week.

FAQ

What stats count as "medium" level in OSRS?

Roughly 50–80 in your key stats, a few million GP, and entry-level members gear. You are past the tutorial but not maxed.

Which method is the safest for new PvMers?

Zalcano — it has a clear, repeatable pattern, no prayer-draining prayer switches, and a safe minigame-style arena.

Do I need membership?

Yes. All ten methods require a members' world; free-to-play players should start with our F2P guides.
